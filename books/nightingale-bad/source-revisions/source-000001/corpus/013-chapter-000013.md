# Chapter 12

Chrollo jumps into the interrupted flow of Tserracnich's lecture. He gets Juno's attention by leaning over
to her to ask about her statement necklace and matching earrings. Juno brightens immediately and
takes the opportunity to finally talk about herself. Chrollo isn't as tall or broad as Tserracnich, but he's far
more handsome and engaging.


Wife-swapping.

The attention of the other people in their gaggle is split as they glance between Chrollo and Juno's light

conversation and the tension between Kurapika and Tserracnich. Tserracnich's no more interested in

Juno talking about what her sister makes for craft fairs now than he was ten seconds ago so his
attention stays fixed on Kurapika.

"… Rude bitches like you always have a short shelf-life.  I wonder how long  it'll take you to degrade."

Tserracnich replies with the tone of a casual remark.

The only indication that Chrollo’s still listening to their conversation is the subtle twitch of his fingers
against his spoon. Kurapika’s mouth tightens as he suppresses the need to scream in Tserracnich's face.

As if being a bitch is anywhere near the level of Tserracnich's sins. Kurapika's fear and his rage are a

tangled mess that's growing by the second - threatening to spill out of every pore.


Kurapika turns his nose up at Tserracnich but otherwise stays very still, like if he moves too much he'll
break the surface tension that's holding his blood-red hatred together.


"Your dominance is obviously low-class," Kurapika replies hotly.


He knows that Tserracnich is the richest person in this room and proud of it. Maybe this way, he can

goad Tserracnich into thinking he's of the same ilk.


Tserracnich laughs. "If you only bare your neck for blue-blood it's no wonder you haven't been tamed.
Being paired up with that and all."

Tserracnich jerks his head towards Chrollo. For the first time, Kurapika wonders what Tserracnich knows

of the thief. Chrollo's been watching Tserracnich because they're hunting him and Kurapika had assumed
that Tserracnich wouldn't care about anyone else enough to notice him. But what if that's wrong?


Deciding it won't change things, Kurapika shakes off the idea. It could also be that Tserracnich merely

assumes everyone is a peasant compared to him and has no real knowledge of Chrollo as an individual.

Kurapika steps away from the group rather than giving Tserracnich a response and dumps his untouched
ice-cream into a trashcan. Taking a moment to himself, Kurapika breathes in deeply and tries to decide
what to do next. He definitely has Tserracnich's attention, that much is clear, but how can he ensure that
Tserracnich will ask for him as a partner… Perhaps he could directly challenge Tserracnich's assumption
that he could tame Kurapika, but maybe that would read as too flirtatious and desperate.

A hand materializes from beyond Kurapika's consciousness. He nearly jumps out of his skin and turns to

see Chrollo looming over his shoulder.

"It's going well," Chrollo assures him. "I left him with Juno and a few others but he's still staring at you."

Kurapika swallows but he doesn't turn to check. He'll just have to trust Chrollo.

"I'll try to keep people off you two as much as possible. But trust me - you're irresistibly vicious."

Kurapika's brow twitches and he closes his eyes. He's  starting to worry he's  trusting  Chrollo's
masochistic streak too much.

"… Don't drool on me," He mutters.

"No promises," Chrollo replies.

Kurapika can't help smiling to himself. He really is going to miss Chrollo.

He smacks  Chrollo's hand  off  like he  can't stand his touch and stalks away. Though  Chrollo's

encouragement helped, Kurapika decides he's going to take a quick bathroom break to try Melody's

breathing techniques one more time before returning to his performance. Near the door to the hallway is
a set of all-gender bathrooms and Kurapika quickly picks one to duck into.


Inside are two stalls and one is occupied but Kurapika doesn't linger on that. He goes to the sink and

wets a paper towel, pressing it to the back of his neck to try and cool some of his simmering rage while

taking care not to get his scent patches wet. He looks at himself in the mirror and is relieved by how
normal he appears. His face is a little flushed from the adrenaline but he looks more clear-minded than

he feels.


Behind him, the stall opens and an alpha Kurapika doesn't recognize steps out. He gives Kurapika a wide
birth as he goes to wash his hands for far too little time. His body language makes it seem like he's

worried about how lingering around an omega in private might be interpreted. It's both thoughtful and a

little offensive.

The alpha rushes out as Kurapika fixes his bangs and tucks the strands of hair that frame his face
behind his ears to show off the nice rubies dangling from them - the most expensive thing he owns and a

gift from Izunavi. He hopes they'll pass for a casual choice and seeing them is another reminder for him
to stay strong.

The door to the bathroom swings open again and glancing towards it is more of an automatic reaction

than a decision. Seeing who it is, Kurapika freezes with his hands still in his hair. Tserracnich locks eyes
with him and Kurapika is not naive enough to think that this is a coincidence.

"Your alpha was just whining to my omega about how you're losing interest in him…" Tserracnich drawls.


Whether that's a lie Chrollo is telling or a lie Tserracnich is making up to drive a wedge between them,
this is good. It's an easy path forward. But Kurapika can't find the words to capitalize it as Tserracnich
prowls towards him. Trapped in Tserracnich's gaze, Kurapika feels frozen in time.

"Of course you would. That man barely counts as an alpha anyway," Tserracnich continues, since an

omega's words barely matter to him anyway.

Tserracnich joins Kurapika in the mirror. Looking at their shared reflection makes Kurapika aware that his

hands are still hovering on either side of his face. Nothing about his shock is a passable performance at
all. Slowly, Kurapika lowers his hands to grip the edge of the sink. The sight of Tserracnich standing over

his shoulder makes hair on the back of Kurapika's neck bristle. His vision narrows and he becomes
achingly aware of every shallow breath.

It's a nightmare and Kurapika is terrified that if he looks down into the sink he'll see Pario's head, floating

in a pool of bleach with his brown hair clogging the drain.

"So you'll leave him, naturally. It's just biology. That's why it doesn't matter how I speak to you, nor you to

me."

Tserracnich moves a hand up to trace Kurapika's jaw. Kurapika's knees buckle as a dizzying wave of
nausea nearly makes him sick. A cold sweat rushes over him and he's forced to acknowledge that his
original plan to match with and seduce Tserracnich on his first try would have never worked. He can't

pretend. He really, really can't.

Tserracnich either doesn't notice or doesn't care about the discomfort that's seized Kurapika's body. He
just keeps speaking at him. Kurapika wonders if Tserracnich spoke to his victims in the same way. Or

perhaps he never spoke to them at all and merely instructed the other members of the exploitation ring
on what to do using this tone.

"You have a rather unique face. The shape of your eyes is quite striking…" Tserracnich remarks with a

semblance of thoughtfulness. "I wonder what lineage you are a princess of. Good genetics, and you
seem healthy and bright… Not a bad candidate to carry my down-line."

Tserracnich lowers his hand to trail his fingers down the yellow omega lanyard. He lets the cord slide
through his fingers for a few centimeters before tightening his grip to tug on it.


Every time Kurapika's ever gone alone into a room with Chrollo, Babimyna always reminds Kurapika that

he can call for him.


Kurapika sees red. He's not the one who's at risk of being another one of Tserracnich's victims. He's not
the one who is going to need to call for help.


In one silent motion, Kurapika seizes Tserracnich's wrist and ducks out from under his lanyard. Through

the haze of adrenaline, Kurapika has never executed a faster take-down in his  life by kicking out

Tserracnich's knees. Tserracnich hisses through his teeth in pain as he drops. Although Tserracnich's
caught off-guard but he has nearly as much reason to keep quiet as Kurapika does.

He grabs the front of Kurapika's sweater to pull him down. Kurapika's feet slip on the tile as he's yanked
forward faster than he expected because Tserracnich is strong. The moment he's on the floor with
Tserracnich, the alpha grabs his jaw and digs his fingers into the joints hard enough to nearly make

Kurapika yelp in pain.

"You stupid - "

Kurapika strikes up with his palm, hitting the bottom of Tserracnich's nose. Blood bursts over Kurapika's
hand but he's more concerned with making sure he covers Tserracnich's mouth to muffle him. Beneath

Kurapika's palm, slick with blood, he can feel Tserracnich's lips pulling back like the alpha's about to try

and sink his fangs into Kurapika's wrist.

Even a broken nose is not enough to make Tserracnich lose his grip on Kurapika's jaw, but it does loosen.
Years of training omegas with Izunavi is enough for Kurapika's body to escape on it's own. He grabs

Tserracnich's thumb and uses it as leverage to wrench the man's hand off his face.

As he pries Tserracnich's hand open, Kurapika's lanyard falls back into his possession.

Before Tserracnich can bite him, Kurapika slips away. He dives behind the alpha and latches onto his
back, locking his legs around Tserracnich's waist to keep him from turning on Kurapika. In the same

instant, he wraps his lanyard around Tserracnich's neck to choke him.

Tserracnich's full weight falls back onto Kurapika, knocking the air out of his lungs, but Kurapika doesn't

let up. The weight of the heavily-muscled alpha makes it hard to breathe, but Kurapika is doing everything
he can to make damn sure that Tserracnich is in a much, much worse position.

The cord of the lanyard is too narrow for Tserracnich to get a hold on it as Kurapika pulls it into the

column of his throat. Kurapika keeps his elbows tucked in close to his body, not giving Tserracnich one

centimeter of leverage while Tserracnich thrashes on top of Kurapika. Tserracnich's struggle makes them
slide across the tiles further under the sink. The back of Kurapika's head hits the plumbing but he barely

feels it.

"… How could you," Kurapika whispers to the underside of the porcelain, hot, enraged tears stinging the
corners of his eyes.


"How could you hurt so many people - so many children - and not care. And think you had a right. You
think you had a right because… because nothing. Just shit you made up because you wanted to, you…"

It doesn't matter  if Tserracnich understands what Kurapika is talking about.  It doesn't matter what

Tserracnich has to say for himself or if he's even able to listen. Let Tserracnich being the one spoken at
rather than to for a change.

"I hate you, I hate you, I hate you…" Kurapika hisses out with every thin breath he manages to take while

denying Tserracnich even one gasp.

Kurapika feels no pain in his fingers as the cord digs into them from his relentless, white-knuckle grip.
Tserracnich's struggle weakens as he begins to lose consciousness.


Kurapika feels no satisfaction as Tserracnich goes still and his jaw stops trying to gulp down air. All

Kurapika wants to do is wail out his agony and cry, but he can't. Kurapika can't cry or scream, or stop

trying to pull the cord of his omega lanyard through this monstrous alpha's throat.

The bathroom door opens and Kurapika doesn't care to look to see who steps in. It doesn't even strike

him as odd that they don't scream until a familiar pair of pale hands are dragging him out from under the
sink by his shoulders.

"Kurapika," Chrollo's urgent voice finds it's way through the dull ringing in Kurapika's ears before he's able

to focus on his face.

Somehow, Chrollo's managed to coax Kurapika into letting go of the cord and sitting upright. The yellow
flashes in Kurapika's peripheral vision as Chrollo drapes it over his shoulders.

"Kurapika. You need to leave quickly, alright? You aren't feeling well." Chrollo whispers.

Kurapika looks from Chrollo's calm face to Tserracnich's body, then back to Chrollo. Chrollo is quickly

unknotting the tie for his waistband and pulling the cord fully free from his pants. Kurapika watches,

barely comprehending what Chrollo  is doing as he matches  it to the deep, red bruising around
Tserracnich's neck.

"Good enough," Chrollo mutters then turns his gaze back to Kurapika.

Chrollo takes Kurapika's hands, cupping them as he's done so many times before. He gives them one

grounding squeeze before opening Kurapika's hands to smear Tserracnich's blood over his knuckles.
Looking up into Chrollo's eyes, Kurapika finds a familiar and comforting stillness.

"'So farewell hope, and with hope farewell fear'," Chrollo whispers.

Kurapika is able to recognize it's a quote from the text that he used send that first secret message to

Chrollo's people, but Kurapika cannot parse the meaning.

"Now wash your hands and go home."

Wordlessly, Kurapika sways to his feet and rinses the remaining blood from his hands. He winces at the

sound of a dull thud behind him as Chrollo strikes Tserracnich's motionless face, bloodying it further.
Chrollo has plenty of good reason to take his own vengeance on Tserracnich, but seeing it doesn't stop

the sense of vertigo any more than Kurapika's own revenge did.


The world is a blur. It's surreal to step out into the party, carrying on like nothing in the world has

changed. Like what Kurapika just did doesn't matter. But he knows that this sense of limbo won't last.


Kurapika walks past the guard who's closest to the bathroom and he's wrapped up in conversation with
Juno. That seems like something Chrollo would do - leaving the two of them distract each other when he

realized that Kurapika was taking too long after Tserracnich followed him into the bathroom.


Kurapika makes his way to the doors that lead back into the hallway. He passes Kaiser who gives him a
long look that makes Kurapika feel deeply unwell but he keeps walking. One of the guards stationed at

the exit door is Babimyna.


Babimyna is surprised at the sudden approach and nearly stumbles back as Kurapika latches onto his

arm.

"I… I'm not feeling well," Kurapika whispers to him. "I need to leave - please."


Kurapika isn't sure what he looks like, but he feels faint and disheveled. Judging from Babimyna's face,
he doesn't look like himself at all.

"Of course. Let me…" Babimyna looks up at the second guard stationed at the door.


They exchange a nod and Babimyna quickly brings Kurapika out of the room. Kurapika clings to the

support of Babimyna's arm as they walk, actually leaning into it. He's watching his feet at his walks and
every step he takes feels too loud.

"You look... You're so pale," Babimyna says, shaken. "Let me take you to a doctor. We have one nearby…"

Kurapika shakes his head no.

"No, I just need to get out of here. I…" The yellow swaying from Kurapika's neck presents itself as a tool

once more.

It says "Caution. Be careful with me," it says "I'm weak," it says "Save me". It's a weapon and he'll try to use
it one more time.

"I need to see a specialist. You don't have an omega care provider here, do you?"

Babimyna hesitates, then shakes his head. He's clearly uncertain, but he doesn't know any better and he's

too good of a man to question an omega about their own needs.

"I don't think so. I'm sorry, I'll call you a cab while you go through the check-out." Babimyna says.

"Thank you," Kurapika allows the genuine relief to come through.

He passes through the security gate in a blur soon Babimyna is helping him into the back seat of a taxi.

It isn't until the taxi makes the turn onto Kurapika's street that he wonders what they're going to catch

Chrollo doing to Tserracnich's body.

They're going to catch Chrollo.

Kurapika runs up to his apartment and dry-heaves into the toilet.





The next day, Kurapika is standing in his bathroom, staring at the developing bruises left on his jaw from
Tserracnich's fingers when receives a call from the counselor's office. It's Eliza on the phone, telling him

they'll have to cancel his upcoming appointments. She's doing her best to sound normal, but  it's

obviously a mask. He wants to hang up the phone, but that would be too strange.

"… Oh. Is everything okay?" Kurapika asks, almost robotically.


Kurapika wants to stop performing but if he does then Chrollo covering for him will be worthless. Despite

"Um, yeah. It's just…"

There are muffled voices of other people in the office speaking.


"I can't say. I'm sorry." Eliza says instead. "But we'll call you back to schedule another therapy session and

"Alright. I'll see you later," Kurapika says, knowing he is not going anywhere near that place again.

He hangs up the phone and steps away from the mirror.


The Paradise Lost quote Chrollo whispered to Kurapika lingers in the back of his mind. Maybe it was
whatever passes for Chrollo’s strange style of comfort. But Kurapika thinks, hopes, that it was a final

It's dangerous for Kurapika to seek them out for so many reasons. He doesn't have anything to offer

That night, Kurapika decides to take the risk and return to 13th Street Printing Company. The alley is as

He knocks again and even tries speaking Chrollo's final words to him. He listens through the door and

Turning away to go home, there's a familiar knot in his throat a heat behind his eyes. It swells with every
step and he keeps trying to swallow it down with short, tight breaths. There's no reason for it - no reason

at all.


Stop it.

Kurapika's vision blurs as he fumbles with his keys to get into his apartment. Mortifyingly, tears shake
free from his lashes.

No matter how many times Kurapika wishes he didn't cry so easily, he sobs into his pillow until he falls
asleep. He dreams of walking into his bathroom and finding Tserracnich's body sprawled across the
floor, his empty eyes staring up at the yellow ceiling lights.




Kurapika is back to going through the motions at work. During his bathroom breaks he finds himself

avoiding looking in the mirror and does his business as fast as possible. The bartender on his shift asks

For once, he's glad Melody doesn't have a show with them tonight. He doesn't feel ready to accept her

warmth.


Instead of thinking of himself, Kurapika thinks of Pario, running into the arms of his foster parents with

His shift ends before the sun rises. Kurapika steps out into the dark and rainy early morning. Despite the

Chrollo didn't have to step in and cover for Kurapika at all. That was never in their deal and Kurapika…
Kurapika knows that Chrollo had plans of his own. Kurapika wonders if those will still be possible now.

He hadn't really let himself think about whether it would be right or wrong to aid Chrollo before. Now, all

It's Kaiser, standing in a thick, black wool coat without a speck of rain on him and his hair perfectly

Kurapika's heart pounds. There's no reason for Kaiser to approach him outside of a therapy session and

in.

Kurapika may have utterly destroyed every ounce of good will Chrollo had in the prison, but he still won't

"You were surprisingly impatient," Kaiser says.

Kurapika doesn't speak. Whatever Kaiser thinks, he might not have any real evidence yet. He's probably

got a wire so Kurapika has to be careful not to say anything that gives him evidence.

"What?" Kurapika asks softly.

Kaiser blinks. He does not appear interested in Kurapika's attempt to play dumb.

"I can't promise  I'll be able to protect you  if they decide to take the investigation beyond Chrollo's
confession. Do with that as you will - but please put in a good word for me with Ms. Melody anyway."

Kaiser says.


With no further explanation, Kaiser turns away, taking his umbrella with him. Kurapika watches the sharp

silhouette of Kaiser's frame walk across the street and turn out of view.


Melody had a contact inside the prison.

Kurapika stares at the lid of his coffee mug, dumbfounded by how much Kaiser just admitted.

The guy Melody was texting… Said she hadn't made up her mind about him…

Melody's contact always had to be someone with access to confidential records and some sway over the

And what Melody had said about the guy she was testing…

Kurapika’s mug slips from his fingers and drops to the sidewalk, spilling coffee and steam into the rain.

Against his will, Kurapika is forced to imagine Kaiser's face next to Chrollo’s in the corner of his bedroom,

No. Absolutely not. Not even in twenty-something years!

Kurapika buries his face in his hands. He wants to berate Melody for her taste in men, but he can't.
Kurapika’s in a glass house and Melody could easily hit a high note that shatters it.





Another two days of tense monotony pass and Kurapika isn't sure what to do with himself. It feels like he

"I can't tell you unless you come with us."

Kurapika closes his eyes, taking a deep breath to try and clear his head to be reasonable. There is

He doesn't know if he is under any serious scrutiny since Chrollo was caught red-handed, apparently

"Who's your family? The Spiders?" Kurapika deserves to know if Chrollo expects him to even consider this

Chrollo’s lips curl into a little smile.

"… You caught me."


It's just as Kurapika suspected. A few weeks ago, that answer would have been frightening. Now it's

another weight off his shoulders to know he wasn't just being paranoid.


The Spiders are a dangerous group and probably even more capable than the rumors suggested with

He fists Chrollo’s coat and grits his teeth. Kurapika is used to thinking himself in circles, but what he

"Do not hide important things like that from me again."


Chrollo brings Kurapika to the window and points to the convenience store across the street. A van is
parked and there's the silhouette of a large man leaning against  it. It's the sort of sight that would
activate any omega's fight or flight response.

"You have my word. Come with me and I will treat you like family."

"Woah. You really smell like the boss." The big alpha says bluntly.

That word bludgeons Kurapika, but he's careful not to let it show. Like water going down a drain pipe, his

"boss."

"Don't just stand out there. Bring him in." The pink-haired woman calls out.

Boss.

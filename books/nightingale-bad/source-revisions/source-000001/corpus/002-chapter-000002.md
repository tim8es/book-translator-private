# Chapter 1

The screening process had been even more involved than Kurapika thought it would be. It started out as
a simple form, then Kurapika was called for an interview which was practically a minor psych evaluation
and now… This.

Kurapika sits in a creaky plastic chair with his hands curled in his lap and his back perfectly straight,
staring at the white "scent test" barrier. It reminds Kurapika of a bank tellers station because of the

counter top with a divot in the center that's just big enough for a hand to pass through. The only

difference is that there isn't a clear window to the other side. Kurapika won't be able to see who sits
across from him and neither will they. He'll have to rely on his other senses to pass judgment.

For the last few months Kurapika has been playing his cards carefully. His application to the program

was a blend of selective truths and lies. His highborn education: a lie, his eclectic studies: the truth, his
passion for collecting rare antiques: another lie, his ambitious and independent personality: the truth,

and his reason for signing up for the program: the biggest lie of them all. Then he rehearsed his interview

with Melody dozens of times to make sure it would be convincing.

Every move he's made has hopefully led to him being paired up with the correct alpha. The infamous
Tserracnich.

Yorknew City has many prisons but finding out which held Tserracnich was easy enough. What was
much harder was finding out that he was recently cleared as eligible for the omega therapy program.

The program is meant to address the mental-health crisis that has plagued alpha prisons for decades.
Separating prisons by secondary gender is mostly to protect beta and omega convicts from alphas but

the results of locking a bunch of alphas up together  - including the guards  - has created a uniquely

hostile environment. For alpha convicts, even the non-violent offenders, healing is nearly impossible and
rates of recidivism are staggering. It creates an ouroboros effect that only worsens alpha reputations

across the board.


So the omega therapy program was created to pair approved alpha convicts up with omega volunteers

as a way for the convicts to practice proper socialization and provide respite from the constant need to
compete for dominance with everyone around them. Not to mention the convenient fact that giving

hopeless alphas something to lose makes them easier to control. An alpha can be removed from the
program for bad behavior so they'll have to be good if they want to keep their access to their therapy

omega. It's still an experimental concept. However, thanks to an abundance of caution, the trials so far

have been promising.


As a general concept, Kurapika would say he's in favor of restorative justice  - another truth on his
application. It's just that some people don't deserve the chance to grow or heal. Some people should just

die. Kurapika didn't mention that on his application.

Kurapika’s pulse jumps as he hears a door opening on the other side of the barrier. There's the sound of

chains dragging across the shelf as the faceless convict sits down. Just on the other side of the wall is
an alpha that they determined Kurapika would be compatible with based on his tailored application. It
could be Tserracnich. It should be Tserracnich if Kurapika’s built the correct profile based on everything
he knows about the man.

Omega volunteers aren't given any specific details about the prisoners because having preconceived

notions could damage potential relationships prematurely. Plus, the alphas in the program would

struggle to imagine the good person they could be around their omega if the knowledge that all of their
sins are on display is looming over them. It's hard for anyone to reinvent themselves with someone who

knows their past. Kurapika understands that as well as anyone else.

But some people can't change - they don't deserve a chance to change.

The alpha prison guard standing to his left leans over to whisper to him.

"It's alright," she says, sensing his unease despite the scent-blocking patches he's wearing. "You can
leave anytime."

Kurapika nods. He won't leave, even if the most disgusting alpha he can imagine is sitting right across

from him.

On the prison side are more scraping and clicking noises as the alpha is cuffed to the table, preventing

him from reaching more than a few centimeters beyond the hole. Supposedly, only alphas who are
considered to be low-risk to omegas are approved for the program. However, Kurapika knows that the

system isn't perfect because Tserracnich was approved. The system can't be perfect because Kurapika

was approved.

He's pretty sure Melody is going to tease him forever about the answers they had to workshop for his
psych evaluation.

"All clear," the alpha guard on the prison side calls to them. "You can take off your patch when you're

ready."


Kurapika takes a slow, calming breath and peels off one of the scent-blocking patches on his wrist.
There's trepidation in his scent, but that's to be expected given the circumstances. However, he isn't so
afraid that his scent is drowned in sour fear. Kurapika’s real pheromones are still palpable. His scent

can't possibly be a lie.

He reaches into the opening, his palm up as a sign of invitation and his fingers relaxed. Much like a bank
tellers window, the opening has a metal basin that's cold against Kurapika’s knuckles. Easy to clean

between visits, he expects.

The faceless alpha's fingers brush against his and Kurapika has to swallow the urge to jump out of his
seat. Despite expecting to be touched, it's hard for him to stay calm when he can't see it coming.

The alpha’s hands are steady and gentle as he drags the tip of a finger down Kurapika’s middle finger to
his palm, not touching his exposed scent gland just yet. The alpha’s hands feel exploratory and curious,
nothing like the selfish nature Kurapika was expecting Tserracnich to display. But if Tserracnich is also
trying to play a long, slow game, then Kurapika can't expect him to be on his worst, most honest behavior.

One hand cradles the back of Kurapika’s, cushioning him against the metal basin. Against the back of
Kurapika’s hand, the alpha’s palm is worn. Kurapika closes his eyes to map the calluses in his mind. The
toughest areas of skin are consistent with someone whose spent a lot of time practicing with a weapon -
perhaps a knife. There is also an odd callus on his thumb that Kurapika doesn't know what to make of…
He didn't think Tserracnich was the type to get his hands dirty with fighting, but perhaps these calluses

were formed from wielding instruments of torture which Kurapika isn't very familiar with.

Finally, the alpha presses his thumb to Kurapika’s sensitive gland. His fingers reflexively twitch at the
touch and his eyes flicker behind his eyelids. The alpha massages his gland for a moment then the hand
disappears. The alpha must be taking in the scent of Kurapika’s pheromones lingering on his thumb.

Then, the alpha's hand settles next to Kurapika’s in the basin. His palm is up, inviting Kurapika to

investigate his pheromones in turn. Kurapika opens his eyes and looks down to see light skin which is a
good sign. Tserracnich is pale. Just barely peeking through the opening is the alpha’s wrist where the

metal cuff is digging into his skin just below his scent gland.

Much like the alpha did, Kurapika approaches him slowly. He slides his fingers down the man's palm and
it looks just like he pictured. The alpha’s fingers are long, his nails are clean, and the blue veins on the
inside of his wrist are surprisingly prominent… Kurapika runs his fingers along them until he touches the

man's scent glad where he feels his pulse throb.

Once he has the alpha's pheromones on his fingers, Kurapika brings them up to his nose to take in the

scent. He braces himself to conceal his disgust. He's expecting to be disgusted.

Kuapika is not disgusted. The alpha’s pheromones smell…

… Kurapika supposes that Tserracnich wouldn't have been as successful of a monster as he was if he
was overtly repugnant. Being able to pass as charming was critical to Tserracnich's operation.

Kurapika's other hand is still resting on the table and the alpha reaches for it. He brushes their fingers

together in a playful manner than makes Kurapika stiffen.


"Alright, time's up." The guard on Kurapika's side says.


Abruptly, the alpha's hands are pulled back over to his side of the barrier. Kurapika sticks the scent-
blocking patch back onto his wrist.


Kurapika is escorted out of the room. The guard has him sit on a bench in the hall outside the
psychologist's office and hands him a survey to fill out. Kurapika answers each question with neutrally-

positive answers about the experience while deep in thought about his actual feelings. He doesn't have
nearly enough information to be sure that alpha was his target. At the same time, he doesn't want to be

too cagey and risk losing a potential opening. Secretly, he had hoped his instincts would be good enough
to just know he was sitting across from a monster. But such a thing just isn't realistic. If it was that

obvious, the list of Tserracnich's victims would have been far shorter. Those who were lured in were no

less omega than Kurapika is.


From there, it's a two week waiting period while the psychologists and his potential inmate match sort
things out. Kurapika's done all he can for now.

While walking to his shift at the bar, Kurapika gets a call from the Yorknew Prison Mental Health

Department. His eye widen at the sight of the familiar number and he steps aside to huddle by a lamp-
post away from the usual urban river of evening foot traffic to answer the call.

He schedules his first face-to-face meeting with his assigned convict alpha.





Exactly one week after the phone call, Kurapika is sitting in the designated visitation room. Waiting.

The room is small and windowless. There's a framed poster on the wall of a generic field of flowers.

Kurapika is sitting on a cushioned, grey, pleather chair across from an identical chair that's arranged at a

three-quarters angle towards his own. Between the chairs is a little round table with a few index cards of
bland icebreaker questions. In the corner of the room is a fake potted plant and attached to the ceiling

above it is a security camera.

It's early enough in the morning to be well before when he needs to go to work but that also means he's
tired from being up all night. Although, if he had taken the night off of work, he doesn't think it would

have helped. Kurapika knows his nervous energy would have kept him up so it's for the best that he was

at least doing something productive with his time. He watches the clock that's hanging across from the
poster until the door opens and a guard leads his assigned alpha in. Kurapika steals himself, determined

to conceal any emotions as he slowly turns to see the alpha.

The alpha is mostly obscured by the guard's shoulders but the glimpse of dark hair Kurapika catches

tells him everything he needs to know. This isn't Tserracnich.

A sickening wave of disappointment and relief washes over Kurapika, leaving him feeling cold as
adrenaline drains from his veins. Getting paired up with the wrong alpha is a setback. Kurapika will have
to rethink his strategy… But admittedly, he's glad to know that he didn't find Tserracnich's pheromones
pleasant.

Once they're through the doorway, the guard steps aside so Kurapika can have a better look at this alpha.

He's shorter than the guard and his size his further de-emphasized by his reserved posture. He has

shaggy, black hair that frames a spade-tipped cross tattoo on his forehead. Beneath his bangs are wide,
deep-set, black eyes that strike Kurapika as distinctly focused. The lower half of the alpha's face is

partially obscured by the muzzle on him, which is a standard cautionary measure. Through the narrow
bars of the muzzle, Kurapika can make our the alpha’s straight nose and pale lips.

There's something familiar about this alpha’s face but Kurapika's sure they've never met. While he was
building his profile of Tserracnich, Kurapika poured over newspaper articles and internet posts, trying to

capture every little detail. In his mind, he combs through all the research he did on Tserracnich, trying to
match this man's face to one of Tserracnich's known accomplices. He imagines each mugshot one by

one but none of them match this alpha. During his investigation, he also glossed over a lot of other
recently convicted criminals by proxy, so perhaps this alpha was just in a report he skimmed.


The guard gestures for the alpha to have a seat in the empty chair and he does so. The guard looks

between Kurapika and the alpha without making any move to remove the alpha's handcuffs. After a beat

of silence where Kurapika and his new partner size each other up, the guard clears his throat. He speaks
only to be Kurapika.


"Would you be more comfortable with me in the room? Or should I step out?" He asks.

"You can go," Kurapika says.


He's not happy, but it's isn't out of concern for his own safety.


The guard nods. "I'll be right outside."


Once the guard has left, the alpha sinks into his chair to lounge. The pleather creaks.

"You look like you have a lot on your mind." The alpha says.

His voice is low and smooth and his tone is casual, like he's chatting with someone in a café.

"Why don't we start with introductions? My name is Chrollo."

Kurapika folds his hands in his lap. He feels as though he's being intensely observed and it isn't because

of the security camera. He wants to avert his gaze to hide from the feeling but refuses to appear so
submissive. He forces himself to meet Chrollo’s eyes.

"My name is Kurapika. It's nice to meet you."

Kurapika is speaking politely but even to him it sounds entirely fake. It's hard to care about all of his

rehearsals when he's sitting across from the wrong man.

"Kurapika," Chrollo echos. "I can't help but get the feeling you're disappointed."

Kurapika bites the inside of his cheek.

"I think I'm just still getting used to the new environment," Kurapika deflects.

It's a common stereotype that omegas struggle with feeling unsafe in new places so he could use that to
his advantage.

Chrollo hums and rests his elbows on the arms of his chair. His hands are together, fingers steepled.

"… I see. That's similar to what the psychologist's office was saying about you."

That makes Kurapika’s brows twitch. He was intentionally trying to throw the psychologist off so

whatever they said to Chrollo was probably bullshit. Still, Kurapika wants to know what it was. He didn't
even know the psychologist was discussing his profile with his potential alpha match. Especially when
Kurapika didn't hear a word about any of the inmates in the program.

Chrollo stares fixed on Kurapika’s face. It feels like his eyes are swallowing up Kurapika’s every fleeting

expression.

"You can just ask me, you know." Chrollo continues as if he's reading Kurapika’s mind.

A hot flash of irritation spikes through Kurapika but he shoves it down. He reminds himself of his goal
and thinks of Melody's soothing performance at the bar last night to calm down.

"… Do you know why they paired us together?" Kurapika asks.


Although he wants the gossip for what the psychologist said about him, that's not as important as

knowing where he went wrong. Kurapika knows that he can request a new alpha at any time but it would

be suspicious if he did that too soon. Or worse, too often if he gets paired up with the wrong person
again.

Chrollo tilts his head as though he finds the question curious.

"I think there are a few reasons. For starters, I requested you."

Hidden in his lap, Kurapika's hands ball into fists as he fights to mediate his temper again. This random

alpha criminal didn't know he was fucking Kurapika over. Chrollo didn't know but now Kurapika blames
him anyway.

"I didn't know we could request specific people." Kurapika says, voice tight.

Chrollo shrugs. "Not really. It isn't like I knew all the applicants but I did scent test with a few omegas and

out of those options I liked you best."

There's a thick lump in Kurapika’s throat that he swallows. He has so many questions. Why didn't he get
to test multiple alphas? Why did Chrollo get to test multiple omegas?  If he had the chance, could

Kurapika have picked Tserracnich out of the lineup?

"Do you know why they let you test with multiple omegas?" Kurapika presses.

If Chrollo is bothered by being interrogated about the prison's mental health program instead of getting

to do a little speed-dating, he doesn't show it.

"It's probably because of my 'even temperament' and 'calm presence,'" Chrollo says, obviously quoting
whichever therapist or psychologist was working with him. "They consider me to be very low-risk to

omegas, and I have to agree. I'm not interested in dominating omegas just for the sake of it."

Chrollo looks up and down Kurapika’s body slowly.

"So they had me scent test with a few omegas who they thought were 'sensitive.'"

It's a brutal shot right to Kurapika’s ego. He knew he was a little tense but he didn't think he was
portraying sensitive.

"I…" Kurapika finally breaks eye-contact to glance at the soulless flower print on the wall. "I appreciate the

concern but I'm not sensitive."


Chrollo leans forward, resting his elbows on his knees to be closer to Kurapika.

"I can tell you're tense but you're not afraid of me," Chrollo lowers his voice even more so it will be difficult

for the camera to pick up.


Suddenly Kurapika becomes very aware that Chrollo's not facing the camera, meaning not even the
shape of what Chrollo says can be monitored.


"No,  I still think you're disappointed about something, and you're awfully curious about the selection
process… Looking for someone? A former lover?"

The suggestion is so disgusting that Kurapika’s real, visceral reaction flashes across his face before he

can control it.


"No," He whispers back.


"Not a lover, hm?"

Chrollo rubs his fingers together in thought. Kurapika recalls the calluses he felt on Chrollo’s hand vividly.

"You also didn't hesitate to dismiss the guard. I don't get the sense that you're so afraid of alphas that
you can't stand to be in a room with more than one. So perhaps… It was because he's a cop?" Chrollo
proposes, a crooked smile flickering behind his muzzle. "Please stop me if I'm getting carried away but

I'm starting to wonder what sort of sweet, darling little therapy omega I've got."

Kurapika is more careful not to react this time but he knows it's too late. Being closed off now is just as
suspicious… But what does Chrollo really have? Only pure speculation. Kurapika isn't busted yet.

He shoots Chrollo a flat, unimpressed look.

"Are these fantasies of yours why you picked me in particular?" Kurapika challenges.

"No," Chrollo shakes his head and straightens up again. "I just liked your scent the most and I thought you

had lovely hands."

"…"

It's so simple that Kurapika is tempted to call it a lie after all the speculation Chrollo just did. Then again,

why lie about something so simple? Feeling a headache coming on, Kurapika lets his head drop to the
side and rubs his temple with his index finger.

Now he's wondering why such a relaxed, though strange, alpha signed up for a therapy companion at all.

But Kurapika can't ask because it would be only natural for Chrollo to ask the same of him. At this point,
he doubts Chrollo would buy the answer Kurapika gave on the application form.

Swiftly changing topics, Chrollo reaches for the index cards on the table between them. He flips through

a few but Kurapika can tell his interest in them isn't real. Not like his risky interest in Kurapika.

"Shall we try playing by the program's rules now?" Chrollo suggests.

He stops on one card and slips it from the pile, holding it out so Kurapika can see it.

"Here's an easy one. Coffee or tea?"


Kurapika stares at Chrollo from the corner of his eye. He doesn't answer immediately, wondering  if

Chrollo is somehow going to spin this into a verbal trap too. The silence hangs undisturbed. This alpha
really is calm. So calm it's weird.


"Coffee."

As soon as the word has left his mouth he recalls the strong notes of coffee in this alpha’s pheromones.
His cheeks feel warm against his knuckles. He hadn't allowed himself to form an opinion on Chrollo’s

scent when there was still a chance he was smelling Tserracnich. Now that he knows he wasn't enjoying
Tserracnich's pheromones, it's harder to dismiss the fact that they were genuinely compatible during the

scent test.

Chrollo rolls the card between his fingers then lets it go, allowing it to flutter to the floor carelessly.

"I pick coffee too. Next question…"

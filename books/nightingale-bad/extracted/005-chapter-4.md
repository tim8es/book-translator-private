# Chapter 4

Later that week, Kurapika's manager asks him to work a double-shift at the bar to cover for one of his co-

workers. He pulls his hair back into a tie and rolls up his sleeves to keep up with the workload through
the crowds and the heat. The whole time, he's thinking about Chrollo.

At first, Kurapika thought that Chrollo signing up with the program was odd since he doesn't appear to

have any issues related to dominance. Then, Kurapika learned a bit more about the perks of the program
beyond having a therapy companion which would be more than enough incentive for every alpha in the

prison to try to get cleared and matched. Now, Chrollo's words from their last meeting add a new

element. If Chrollo truly believes that the majority of omegas volunteering are crazy - that they just want
subjects for think pieces or partners to keep under their thumb - would dealing with an omega like that

be worth the perks?


Maybe. Maybe books and coffee and getting to talk to someone nice on the outside are good enough
when you're sentenced to be locked up for over a decade. Kurapika could believe that.


Or maybe Chrollo is really looking for something more. Something he thinks he'll have a better chance at

getting by leveraging an omega who has something to hide rather than omega who just wants to delude

themselves into a love affair.


Kurapika doesn't make it home until the sun comes up. He collapses onto his futon for a few hours of
sleep then wakes with a fresh determination. He decides to arrive for his next appointment early to do a

little more investigating on his partner.

"You're early," Babimyna observes once Kurapika passes through security.

"Yes," Kurapika agrees blandly. "I'd like to wait in the office until it's time for my appointment."

Babimyna shrugs and stops outside the office to wait. He gives Squala a nod of acknowledgement while

Kurapika gives him a wave and steps inside. He passes Squala's desk and goes straight to Eliza who
perks up when she sees him.

"Good morning, Kurapika! How are you doing?" Eliza says.

"I'm doing well," Kurapika replies. He can feel Squala's attention on them.

Kurapika crosses his arms over his chest and hunches a bit to make himself appear smaller and more

meek than he is. He lowers his voice to whisper to Eliza like they're friends.

"I was just curious about something… but I was worried about being rude by asking." Kurapika whispers.

Immediately interested, Eliza leans in to listen carefully. Kurapika continues.

"Do the others who visit Chrollo not bring him things?" Kurapika asks.

"Others?" Eliza looks confused. Good.

Kurapika pretends to look confused as well. He's intentionally creating a misunderstanding in hopes that
Eliza will accidentally say more than she should when she explains.

"Each alpha only gets assigned one…"

"Oh, no,  I know he only has one therapy omega." Kurapika continues, wringing his hands together
awkwardly and pinching his brows to mimic concern for his partner. "I just meant… are only therapy
omegas allowed to bring things for the prisoners? If Chrollo’s been wanting something as simple as a
book, wouldn't his family…"

Eliza's eyes brighten up once she realizes what Kurapika’s 'misunderstanding' is.


"Ah, I thought you were asking about other therapy omegas!" She laughs a bit and Kurapika chuckles with

her.


"No, no, other visitors will bring things like photos or letters once they're cleared, but Chrollo hasn't had
any visitors as long as I've known him. Not until you. I can see why he'd be fond of you."

There it is. Happy with the information he's gotten, Kurapika quickly steers the conversation away from

Eliza revealing some of Chrollo's private information towards a juicer topic. One that will hopefully keep

Eliza from wondering if she's said too much.


Kurapika brings his hand up to curl it around his face shyly.

"You really think he's fond of me? We get along great but I don't know if it's just…"


Eliza slaps her hands on her desk, leaning over it with a big smile.

"Definitely! Listen, omega to omega, he's been talking to me about you too." Eliza gives him a wink. "But
don't tell him I told you."

Kurapika smiles and nods. The omega code of honor is real. He didn't expect Eliza to give him even more
information than he was fishing for so easily. Chrollo must be doing the same thing when he has to
come here to talk to his therapist. There's no way for Kurapika to know what Eliza's told Chrollo, but he’s

been careful to keep his persona consistent around her.

"Ah, that's good. I just… I know I can be a little shy, so…"

Kurapika lowers his voice and ducks his head to touch his hair, mimicking the body language he uses in
the room when he whispers things to Chrollo that he wants to hide from the camera.

"Don't worry about a thing. You are so cute," Eliza encourages him just like the girls at the bar do with

their friends. "I can totally see you being his type."

Kurapika cups his hand over his mouth to whisper to Eliza even more softly. He knows Squala is still

trying to eavesdrop.

"Omega to omega… I think that," Kurapika points towards the guard desk and then to Eliza. "… Y'know?"

Chrollo said that he'd tell Eliza not to date cops and Kurapika doesn't actually disagree. But it would be
nice if Eliza and Squala were more preoccupied with each other than with what's going on in the therapy

visitation rooms.

Eliza follows the motion of Kurapika’s hand and her eyes flicker in recognition.

"… Really?" She whispers.

Kurapika nods.

"It's just a feeling I get."

While Eliza babbles about her interactions with Squala, flustered and looking for hints she may have
missed, Kurapika reminds himself of the simple facts he has to use against Chrollo.

He's in  jail for a big robbery and the stolen goods were never recovered. Chrollo most likely had

accomplices that he didn't have a falling out with or he probably would have snitched for a lighter

sentence. Thanks to Eliza, Kurapika now knows those accomplices are smart enough to stay away from
Chrollo, which leaves him all alone. Then, Chrollo decided to get involved in the therapy program, which

he knew would get him access to one random omega from the outside who Chrollo believed was likely to

be emotionally compromised. Either Chrollo was really bored with serving his sentence legitimately, or he
is looking for a way to contact his people.

If Kurapika’s right, then he’s willing to get his hands a little dirtier if it gets him a shot at Tserracnich.
Working with some thieves is trivial on Kurapika’s conscious compared to the weight of Tserracnich's
continued existence.


Kurapika clears his throat and decides to take the leap, asking the most important question.

"Um, Eliza. I know it's for safety but I was wondering if… Chrollo and I could get a little more privacy? I

really don't think Chrollo would do anything bad to me."


Eliza straightens up.

"O-Oh, okay! I mean, I can't give you permission for that but it does happen. Mated pairs have visitation

completely unrelated to our program - not that you have to be mates - but I'm just saying that we know

how things are sometimes. Let me send an email. You'll have to do an application and a consultation for

a private room."

Eliza's manicured nails fly across her keyboard as she types up a message.

"… But on the down-low, we don't really watch. Y'know. Unless it's a prisoner who's kinda a yellow alert
already, but your partner is just Chrollo, so."

Eliza does a wiggly motion with her hand, showing that no one in the program is worried about him.

"I don't think you'll have any problems getting approved for next time."

It's time for his appointment so Kurapika stops gossiping with Eliza. He walks past Squala like he wasn't
just using him to stir the pot in the office and returns to Babimyna in the hall, giving him his usual shy
smile.

"Everything still okay?" Babimyna asks.

Kurapika nods. "Yeah, I just like chatting with Eliza. She's so sweet."

Babimyna shrugs, accepting that easily. Eliza's the popular type and one more omega wanting to be

friends with her is normal. Kurapika is so good at being normal.

Babimyna and Kurapika continue down the hall to his usual room.

Once Kurapika’s in the room, he grabs the icebreaker cards off the table and starts laying them out in a

grid randomly. He won't reveal his hand to Chrollo until they're alone so for these appointments so
Kurapika will make due as he has been.

When Chrollo enters the room, Kurapika’s standing over the table, the array of cards clearly on display.

Chrollo raises a brow as he approaches, stopping on the opposite side of the table.

"What's this?" He asks.


"I think we should play a game," Kurapika says. "So we can get closer."


Just one more game and then we'll be close enough.

Chrollo’s eyes flicker over the cards, likely looking for a pattern.

"… Alright, what are the rules?"


Kurapika gives Chrollo a dry smile.

"I think you'll get it." We've already been playing. "We take turns picking cards to ask each other questions.
If one of us doesn't want to answer, we can pass and set it aside. For each one you do answer, that's a

point."


Info for info, just like before.

Chrollo hums in thought as he takes in the utterly useless questions on the table.


"And what are the points for?" Chrollo asks.


"I'll bring you something next time." Kurapika says.

"For how many points?"


"Any."


Chrollo and Kurapika stare at each other for a beat of silence. Kurapika implores Chrollo to get it with his

eyes.


We're killing time until our next appointment and getting new things to say about each other.

Chrollo chuckles.

"Alright, if that's what you want. Mind if  I go first?" He plucks a card off the table and passes  it to
Kurapika.

"What season would you be?"

Kurapika looks down at the card and sighs. The problem with this plan is that he has to answer these

stupid questions too.

"I don't know… fall?"

Not that it's matters but Kurapika thinks that a burst of color right before everything shrivels up suits him
fine.

"Really? I think you're more of a spring." Chrollo says.

Kurapika closes his eyes so he won’t roll them at Chrollo on camera. He can think of half a dozen cheap

reasons an alpha would compare a younger omega to spring.


What an asshole.

Not that it matters.

Kurapika sets the card aside and picks one for Chrollo.


"What celebrity or character was your childhood crush?" Kurapika asks.


"The Red Power Cleaner. Red's my favorite color."

Chrollo answers so seriously that Kurapika almost laughs. That is the sort of logic children use… But he's

had a lot of practice at not laughing at the silly things kids say.


"I like red too," It's exactly how Kurapika would respond to a child.


Chrollo puts the card in his pile. One point was all Kurapika said he needed but hopefully they can keep
putting on this show, even if everything Chrollo says from now on is a lie.


Chrollo picks another card.


"If you were a school teacher, what would you teach?" He reads.


Kurapika heart stops. He hadn't bothered to check the cards because he didn't think that any of them
would affect him. He knows the question is innocent, but the words bring a flood of memories that slam
into the shaky dam surrounding Kurapika’s mind. He shuts his eyes and holds his breath like he's been

plunged underwater. Despite Kurapika telling himself that it's a normal question and he can just say

anything, the words won't come out. It doesn't matter but it feels like it does.

"… Pass." Kurapika hates himself for being weak.

Chrollo’s brows raise in surprise.

"… Alright."

Kurapika opens his eyes but avoids Chrollo’s gaze. He puts the card in the pass pile and to his surprise
Chrollo picks another card.

"Then how about this one for me. Do you like rainy days? Yes, I love the rain. How about you?"

Kurapika takes a steadying breath and nods.

"I like the rain too."





The next time Chrollo and Kurapika meet, it's raining.

Thanks to that icebreaker game, Kurapika had a lot of pointless things to say about Chrollo during his
evaluation and he's sure Chrollo was able to do the same. Kurapika had to fill out a lot of paperwork with

a witness, saying that he was of sound mind and understood the risks and all that.

Kurapika rushes out of his cab, holding the hood of his raincoat in place to avoid getting soaked on the
way to the guard's check-in. He goes through the front gates and tries to contain his nervous energy.

Kurapika isn't naive enough to believe they'll have real privacy but being able to get the heat off their
backs until Kurapika can accomplish his goal will be enough. More than being alone with Chrollo,

Kurapika is nervous about the item Chrollo asked for in exchange for playing that card game.

Babimyna escorts Kurapika to a different visitor's room a little further down the hall. Kurapika doesn't
bother to fight down his blush. Anyone would be embarrassed if they were being so obviously brought to
a separate space that announces their relationship status. But Babimyna remains professional and

reminds Kurapika that he can call out if he needs anything.


Stepping into this room is like stepping into another timeline. Kurapika takes in the new space as
removes his raincoat. It's nearly identical to the first room but just different enough to feel off. The little
table and chairs are the same and the soulless print on the wall feels like a picture of the same flower
field taken at a different angle. The main difference is the addition of a little, gray loveseat. Kurapika
shudders at the sight of it.


Taking a cursory glance, Kurapika can't see any obvious surveillance. But camera feeds exist and don't

whenever its convenient for the system. He'll just have to make sure not to raise any suspicion until the

job is done.


Even though he hates the sight of it, Kurapika sits on the couch to wait.


Several minutes  later, Chrollo is brought into the room. He's  still cuffed and muzzled. During the
consultation, Kurapika was told that they'd be kept on unless he requests access to a key. He's fine with

handling Chrollo like an animal until it's more convenient to do otherwise.

"Imagine my surprised when I was called into a private room consultation." Chrollo says the moment the

door is shut behind him.


"You weren't surprised," Kurapika counters. "And I gave you plenty to talk about."


Chrollo hums in agreement and approaches the loveseat. He sits with his cuffed hands resting in his lap
and leans back into the cushions while Kurapika remains stiff and upright. Kurapika crosses his arms
over his chest as the couch shifts under Chrollo’s weight. His eyes flicker to Kurapika’s crossed arms
pointedly but Kurapika keeps them folded. Chrollo smells like the rain.

"You brought me my prize?"

Chrollo opens his hands expectantly.

Kurapika slowly unfurls his arms. He tugs his left sleeve up to reveal the bandana tied around his wrist.

He pulls on one of the ends until the thin material unravels. He still can't believe Chrollo wants something
like this. Kurapika holds it out pinched between between his thumb and forefinger like he’s repulsed by it.
He lets it go and it flutters down into Chrollo’s open hands.

Chrollo closes his hands around the fabric and it disappears with a little sleight-of-hand.

"Much appreciated. Even some of the alphas who aren't approved to go into the private rooms with their

omegas are getting tokens of affection."

"I see…"

Reflexively, Kurapika covers his un-blocked wrist with his hand. Chrollo has a point, though. The more
genuine they make their attraction seem, the less suspicion they'll draw. Slowly, he pulls his hand away
from his wrist and folds his fingers together in his lap primly.

With that exchange out of the way, there’s no reason to put off his next move. Kurapika takes a deep

breath and braces himself for their next exchange. Without any preamble, Kurapika tips his hand just
enough to reveal he's looked into Chrollo's background.


"You have accomplices on the outside from that heist, right?" Kurapika whispers.

The words feel too big to fit in the small space between them, but Kurapika drives them in like a wedge.
Chrollo’s face remains completely blank.


"No," He lies.


Kurapika is sure it's a lie, so he keeps pushing try and rattle Chrollo enough to crack his poker face.


"You must be loyal to them. That's why you haven't had any visitors in years, right?"

There’s another stretch of silence that grows wider the more Kurapika drives his challenge between
them. Chrollo slowly reaches across the gap from his lap to Kurapika’s. From an outsider's perspective,
the way Chrollo wraps his cuffed hands around Kurapika’s to envelope them might look tender. Kurapika

knows it's a warning but he won't back down. He keeps his hands still and counts his steady breaths.


"Tell me who they are and I can get in touch with them for you." Kurapika continues.

Chrollo’s hands clamp down around Kurapika’s tightly. His grip is strong enough to make Kurapika's
fingers ache. He bites the inside of his cheek so he won't flinch when Chrollo applies pressure to his

thumb - knowing it would take only a bit more force to break it.

Kurapika meets Chrollo’s dark eyes, refusing to show fear. Chrollo wouldn't threaten to destroy both of

their chances like this unless it was something he really cared about. Kurapika is on the right track.

"… You give me a name first. Who are you after?" Chrollo replies, his voice lower and more dangerous
than Kurapika’s ever heard.

Kurapika swallows the chill that tone sends through him. This is it. The tipping point for both of them.

"….. Tserracnich."

The name falls out if his mouth and lands between them with a heavy thud. Whatever the name means

to him, it makes Chrollo loosen his grip, but Kurapika is too tense to relax as the pressure eases off his
fingers.

"Do you… know him?" Kurapika asks.

"… I'm familiar with his work."

Kurapika’s jaw tightens, fearing the worst. There are many monsters who are fans of Tserracnich's.

"What's that supposed to mean?" He hisses through his teeth.

Chrollo pulls his hands back, surrendering their eye-contact by dropping his gaze first.

"Tserracnich's network sprawled far beyond him. He's got more victims than the ones he was sentenced

for."

Kurapika’s fury vanishes in and instant and his jaw relaxes. His carefully measured breaths break

formation into real, pained gasps.

"… Yes…." He agrees. "So you… you know someone?"


Chrollo nods and looks over at the shitty print on the wall.

"Knew someone. I think. I can't prove it." Chrollo says softly.

The words hit the wound in Kurapika’s heart that's raw and festering. Chrollo doesn't need to prove that
to Kurapika. Moving with his emotions and not his reason, Kurapika places his hands on Chrollo’s
forearm and leans in closer to him, wanting to find a mirror for his own pain in Chrollo’s hidden face.


"Who-?"


"Tserracnich got approval for the therapy program?"

Chrollo’s words are harsh, cutting through the thick haze of Kurapika pain like an icy wind cutting through
thick humidity. The coldness in Chrollo’s voice shakes Kurapika out of his vulnerability and makes him

focus on his real goal. He isn't actually here to do group-therapy with another person who Tserracnich

has caused misery.


He pulls away from Chrollo and crosses his arms over his chest, curling into himself as he looks away
from Chrollo to the blank wall on the opposite side of the room. This is the second time he’s shown his

weakness to Chrollo. He needs to get it together.

"From what I've been able to dig up, that seems to be the case." Kurapika replies, his voice having been

dried out by the cruel reality stretching out ahead of them. "Unless he shows his true colors and does
something to get kicked out, I…"


"He probably won't. Tserracnich's spent decades being careful as a free man and as a convict." Chrollo

says.

Kurapika nods although he doesn’t know if Chrollo is looking.

Chrollo is probably right about Tserracnich. All at once, he feels the leftover aching in his feet from his
double-shift anew and his eyes feel heavy. He is so tired.

Kurapika’s shoulders slump and he sinks deeper into the couch.

"Then I'm sure you can understand why I want to get him alone."

Chrollo chuckles. It's hollow and so far away that it echos in Kurapika’s head.

"I do."

"Help me get to Tserracnich and I'll help you get in touch with your people. I don't care what you want to

say to them. I suspect you wanted to charm some delusional omega into helping you anyway."

"You were right. We've got a deal."

Kurapika feels Chrollo’s knuckles bump against his bicep. He looks over his shoulder to see the alpha
offering his hand. Kurapika turns his shoulders towards Chrollo once more and firmly grasps his hand to
shake on it.

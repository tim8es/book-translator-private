# Chapter 3

A thin mat cushions Kurapika’s shoulders as Izunavi's weight bares down on him. Heavily calloused

hands pin Kurpapika's wrists above his head and the scent of stale cigarettes covers Izunavi's
pheromones. The position is comfortable in it's familiarity.


Kurapika looks away from the older alpha to the crowd of omegas watching just a few steps away.


"You can use your hips like this." Izunavi explains.


Kurapika shifts his hips slowly, digging his heels into the mat. The omegas watch carefully as Izunavi's

balance is disrupted, giving Kurapika the opportunity to break free.

"See how he gets leverage here," Izunavi lets go of Kurapika’s wrists to reach back and tap his bare ankle.


Kurapika keeps his hands  in place, waiting for Izunavi to grab them again so they can slowly

demonstrate the move a few more times.


Then, they demonstrate the maneuver at a more realistic speed. To show the omegas how to really put
up a fight, Kurapika puts everything he has into bucking Izunavi off and the old alpha falls heavily onto

one shoulder as Kurapika scrambles to his feet. From there, Izunavi and Kurapika explain that the
omegas have a lot more options to protect themselves.

"- but if you can manage  it, running away to get to safety is always better than dragging out a fight."

Izunavi reminds them.

With the demonstration over, the omega students split into teams to practice together.

A few times a month, Kurapika assists Izunavi in teaching these omega self-defense classes in their
local community center. Kurapika’s known the man for years. Long before his haphazardly pieced
together life came apart at the seams. But in this room it's almost like nothing has changed about
Kurapika as he roams the floor, watching and giving feedback to this crop of nervous but determined
students

The class ends at eight, so Kurapika and Izunavi walk to the nearest corner store for a late dinner. They

grab a few sandwiches then sit on the curb to eat together. They don't talk much but Izunavi asks how

his new job at the bar is going and Kurapika asks Izunavi about his three other jobs that he works in
addition to teaching self-defense.

Izunavi doesn't know that Kurapika is volunteering in the prison. As far as Izunavi knows, Kurapika is
moving on and slowly recovering from the horrors that drove him to seek the prison out in the first place.

Kurapika doesn't plan on telling him. It would only disappoint the man. Izunavi doesn't need to know that
Kurapika can't move on from what happened.





Kurapika brings volume three of Horror Angel to the security desk for his next appointment. The guard at
the desk flips through the slightly worn pages and his eyes don't even linger on the occasional coffee
stains. He hands the book back to Kurapika and gestures to him to pass through the gate. Kurapika’s
usual escort is waiting for him, hands folded behind his back. He's a tall, broad alpha with a strong jaw

and curly, light hair that's cropped onto the top of his head.


Kurapika walks down the hall, holding the book to his chest. The electricity of the overhead lights buzzes
and Kurapika's soft, flat shoes are barely audible on the linoleum next to the heavy, steel-toed boots of
his escort. As they pass the psychologist's office, Kurapika’s eyes dart over to see Squala on the morning

shift again, slumped over a disposable coffee cup. Halfway down the hall, a low voice above his head
cuts through the dead air, nearly startling Kurapika.


"Babimyna."


Kurapika looks up at the guard who's face remains stiff.


"What?"


"My name is Babimyna," the guard says again. "I just thought I should introduce myself to you."

"… Babimyna." He says the guard's name slowly for the first time. "I'm Kurapika."


He introduces himself in turn to be polite. Though he's sure his escort knows his name already.


They reach the visitation room and stop just outside the door. Babimyna clears his throat a bit awkwardly.


"Still want me to stay outside?"

Kurapika nods.

"There haven't been any issues." Kurapika assures him.

He wonders if Babimyna is concerned since Kurapika went to the psychologist's office yesterday. But

rather than questioning him, Babimyna just nods in agreement.

"Right. If you need anything, just shout."

Babimyna opens the door and holds it for Kurapika. Kurapika nods in acknowledgement and walks in,
getting a hint of the alpha's pheromones as he passes the length of his outstretched arm. Babimyna's

scent is clean and inoffensive.

Kurapika has a seat and Babimyna closes the door to give him a semblance of solitude while he waits
for Chrollo. Kurapika holds the book in his lap, hidden just out of sight under the table. A few minutes

pass and the door opens again to let Chrollo inside. As soon as Chrollo is sitting close enough to hear

Kurapika speak in a low voice, Kurapika gets straight to the point.

"Squala's at the desk again, so no one is listening to us now." Kurapika whispers.

Chrollo’s brows raise. He leans over the table, eyes sharpening with tangible focus rather than the open,
passive perception Kurapika usually gets from him.

"How do you know that?" Chrollo whispers back.

"We're just one feed of at least a dozen."


Chrollo stares at Kurapika, urging him to elaborate. He's incredibly  still and despite his lack of

expression, Kurapika can sense his growing intensity.


"I saw it on his screen." Kurapika explains.


Surprise makes Chrollo's brows twitch, undeniable judgment slipping through the cracks of his stony
facade.

"You looked at his screen in front of him?"

"He didn't notice," Kurapika hisses. "I'm pretty sure he's more interested in Eliza than me."


Chrollo's intensity softens around the edges into something more like curiosity.

"Ah, that makes sense. I hadn't noticed… I'll have to remind her not to date cops." Chrollo says.


Kurapika lifts the book he's holding in his lap and places it on the table between them. Chrollo stares at

it. Kurapika is sure he's surprising the alpha a lot today.


"I think we have a lot to offer each other." Kurapika says softly, pushing the book towards Chrollo.

Chrollo places his hand on the cover to accept it and Kurapika's hand shies away from his, remembering
their first and only touch.

"… So you'll finally tell me what it is you want?" Chrollo asks.


Kurapika takes a steady breath, reminding himself of what to say before the words come out.


"I want information about how to navigate more effectively in the therapy program."

"Why?"

"Because you were right, I am looking for someone." Kurapika admits that much.

"Who and for what?"

Kurapika rests his hands on the table and laces his fingers together.

"I can't say."

Chrollo turns the book over in his hand.

"… Incomplete information from you will mean incomplete dedication from me." Chrollo hums.

Kurapika hesitates. "… That'll have to do for now. Will you just give me some information in exchange for
whatever I'm allowed to bring you?"

It's a gamble. According to Eliza, omegas will often bring their alphas things for nothing other than the

companionship itself. Not to mention the fact that Eliza made it clear that Chrollo is capable of choosing
to be charming. If he were to ask for a different omega, he could probably get a lot out of them without

the extra trouble Kurapika brings.

A few beats of silence pass as Chrollo considers his options. There no way for Kurapika to know what's
going on inside Chrollo's head as he idly traces a finger along the spine of the book. Eventually, Chrollo
breaks the silence with his decision.


"Alright, then here's some information for you that'll make us even for now: You aren't the only crazy

omega in the program." Chrollo says.


Kurapika's brows raise as he's taken aback. Chrollo doesn't know Kurapika's reason for being here, so he
thinks it's a bit much that Chrollo's already calling him crazy. He hasn't even broken any rules yet… except

for a few, little lies.


Rather than challenge Chrollo's interpretation of both him and the other omega volunteers, Kurapika is

more interested in understanding it.


"What do you mean?" Kurapika asks.


"Come on, just think about it," Chrollo leans in with a slight smile playing on the corners of his lips. "What
sort of omega would volunteer for this? I mean, really? Omegas would only come here if they're writing a

thesis or they're desperate for an alpha that they have almost total control over. Maybe some even want

both."


Kurapika turns Chrollo's interpretation of the program over in his mind. It's true that if Kurapika didn't
have ulterior motives, he wouldn't volunteer for something like this out of good will towards alpha

prisoners. When he thinks of working for a good cause, he thinks of the omega self-defense classes and

his old career.

"Now you're a rare case that I haven't figured out yet." Chrollo continues. "But, my point is, they already
think you're weird."


Chrollo's thumb twitches up, a barely-noticeable acknowledgement of their overseers.

"They're expecting you to ask for questionable exceptions. Do us both a favor and take advantage of it."

Kurapika raises a skeptical brow and lowers his chin, speaking even quieter and more rushed.

"I'm not going to ask them to do something as suspicious as assign me to the prisoner or my choice just

because you think-"

Chrollo shakes his head no so Kurapika stops, giving him a chance to explain. He hold his hand out to
Kurapika.

"Take off your scent patch and give me your wrist."

Kurapika stiffens and pulls his hands into his chest protectively.

"What?" Kurapika breathes.

Chrollo doesn't move a muscle. Not even a twitch. He doesn’t blink and continued to stare at Kurapika

expectantly.

Being stared at like that sends a chill through Kurapika. He hesitates a moment longer, then steels his

nerves. He pushes himself up from his seat and takes the two steps around the table to Chrollo's side.
He's very aware that this much movement could have gotten Squala's attention but as long as nothing

too dramatic happens, they won't be interrupted. By closing the gap between them, they can whisper
more effectively and it'll give Kurapika an excuse to keep his head down and hide his face.

He stands over the alpha, a hand wrapped protectively over his wrist as he mentally prepares to expose a

bit more of himself to Chrollo than he planned to. Sharing scents is allowed by the program so he's sure
other pairs must do it on occasion…


Chrollo sits beneath him perfectly still. He's simply watching Kurapika, waiting for him. The act shouldn't
feel as significant as it does. Kurapika knows he's already shared his scent with Chrollo, but doing it face-

to-face makes it feel so much more personal.


Before signing up for this program, Kurapika had only ever shared his uninhibited scent with close

friends and a few children in need of comfort. Still, he reminds himself that it doesn't have to mean
anything. He slowly digs his nail under the adhesive of the patch.


He peels off his scent blocking patch and slowly lowers his arm, offering his hand to Chrollo. He holds

his breath as he waits for Chrollo to take it, his heart pounding in his ears. Mercifully, Chrollo doesn't
make him wait for long.

Chrollo raises his cuffed hands to cradle the back of Kurapika's hand just as gently as he did the first

time they explored each other. With his hands cupped as though he's holding water, Chrollo curls his

thumb over the curve of Kurapika's palm to caress the valley between his pinky and thumb. He traces
Kurapika's prominent fate line and glances up to his face once more.

Kurapika can't make out what Chrollo sees in him before he bows his head to press his face to Kurapika’s

palm. The muzzle keeps a few centimeters between them, preventing Kurapika from feeling the true
shape of Chrollo's face. The thin metal bars separating them are cold on Kurapika’s skin but he doesn't

pull away. Slowly, Kurapika relaxes as he comes to realize that like this, Chrollo also has an excuse to
hide his face from the camera. It's strange but Kurapika can rationalize it.

Chrollo’s breath flutters over his skin as he slowly moves his face to get as close to the scent gland at
Kurapika's wrist as possible. A low, pleased sound rumbles in Chrollo’s throat and Kurapika’s heart skips

a beat. The unsteady hold Kurapika has on his nerves shakes.

Chrollo’s hands glide up to explore Kurapika’s forearm and the light touch gives Kurapika goosebumps.
While Chrollo’s face is hidden in Kurapika’s hands, Chrollo finally whispers what exception he wants

Kurapika to take advantage of.

"Tell them we're courting," Chrollo's deep voice is barely audible.

Kurapika stiffens, shivering as the breathy words spill over his wrist. Instantly, the tentative calm he had
is blown away, leaving him feeling cold and nauseous. Kurapika can barely conceal his shock at Chrollo's

request. It's not that he trusted Chrollo, but during their last meetings it didn't seem like this was what he

was after. Kurapika had mentally prepared himself for the possibility that he'd need to do something like
that to get Tserracnich to let his guard down with him. He hadn't thought he's need to do that just to get

information on the program without the guarantee he'd even be able to get close to Tserracnich.

Kurapika swallows. When he finds his voice it's brittle and dry.

"You want me to-?"

Chrollo shakes his head no, disguising the motion as rubbing his face on Kurapika’s arm like an

affectionate cat.

"No. Calm down. They'll give us more privacy if you tell them that. We're not the first pair to get cozy…"

Kurapika resists the urge to glance at the camera, his face burning. The notion that the program's safety
measures would be more lax if they think the omegas want to get intimate with felons is absurd. It's so

absurd that Kurapika would think Chrollo is lying if he hadn't had that strange conversation with the
counselor.

Chrollo lifts his big eyes to Kurapika’s face but keeps his chin down.

"But isn't it… too soon?" Kurapika murmurs.


"I said there were crazier omegas than you, right? The other inmates talk. Some like to brag."


Kurapika is skeptical about that. It seems like Chrollo is telling the truth, but those other inmates might

not be. They could be a bunch of knot-heads who are just eager for a chance to brag about getting an

omega now that it's a least possible for them to do so.

"Fine, tell them whatever you want. Just get us close enough to network and we can see where this goes.
Maybe we can find a few more reasons to trust each other." Chrollo whispers.


The notion of trust turns in Kurapika's mind. He's come to see trust as a strange thing. Like a crystal,

trust has a complex shape that looks different from every angle Kurapika views it from. It distorts the
light of truth as it bounces through it's jagged structure. Much like a crystal, trust can also be worn down,

reshaped under pressure and heat, or shattered entirely. Still, trust remains valuable, and Kurapika can't

help but crave it.


Although this alpha's intentions are mysterious, Kurapika can't help but feel there's something undeniably
sincere about him. It's that sincerity that trips Kurapika up. Before he even realizes he's doing it, he asks

a question that he shouldn't.

"… Why did you sign up?" Kurapika breathes.

He wishes Chrollo didn't hear him.

"You first."


Of course.

Kurapika presses his lips together tightly. They both know he won't say. Through the muzzle, Kurapika
catches a flash of white teeth as Chrollo smiles. He lifts his face from Kurapika’s hands and lets go of

Kurapika's. Then, Chrollo holds his cuffed hands up to Kurapika in offering.

"Do you want a turn? I am not an unfair man."

Kurapika sighs, his shoulders slumping a little bit.

"No, but give it to me anyway if we're going to do this."

"Ouch."

Kurapika takes hold of Chrollo’s hands and looks down at his palms. Using his thumb, he brushes one of
Chrollo’s cuffs out of the way so he can rub his scent gland. He refuses to lower his head to take in the
alpha's scent, able to smell it just fine with what distance remains between them.

This time, he lets himself think about Chrollo's pleasant scent a little more. There are warm, roasted

notes that remind him of coffee and something rich and earthy like leather. There's something more to it
though. A faintly sweet and herbaceous note that Kurapika can't put a name to.

Before he can get too wrapped up in it, Kurapika moves his thumb down to rub Chrollo’s fingers. He finds

that callous he couldn't identify the cause of from their scent test.


"What's this from?" Kurapika asks.

Chrollo flexes his fingers under the touch.


"Lock picking. I used to be too heavy-handed when I was starting out but I promise that my touch is
much more refined these days."


"So you're a thief," Kurapika ignores the innuendo and pretends he doesn't know about the bank robbery
for now.


"I have a few charges but that's how I see myself."


Kurapika moves away from Chrollo's thumb to trace straight down his palm and all the way down his
middle finger.


"Do you have any skills you take pride in?" Chrollo asks.

Kurapika stills his hand and closes his eyes. He takes a deep breath.

"… I just work in a shitty bar," he mumbles.


"That's not what I asked."

Kurapika knows he's keeping his eyes closed for too long. He needs to look at Chrollo and try to finally
get a read on his enigmatic face. Instead, he trails his fingers back down to Chrollo's wrist and lets the
weight of his palm rest in Chrollo's.

"I… I help teach omega self-defense classes on the side," He admits.

Kurapika still doesn't open his eyes to check Chrollo's reaction.

"That's noble of you," Chrollo replies and Kurapika can't read anything in his tone at all. "Have you ever

had to take down an alpha before?"

Kurapika's been in a few confrontations in his  life. Mostly,  it's been  little things when he was in
community college and a few bad shifts at the bar. Usually, just a bit of force and a whole lot of
confidence is all that's necessary to get a dumb alpha to back off. He's never had to hurt anyone badly

before.

"… Not yet."

# Chapter 2

The evening after meeting Chrollo, Kurapika settles into the methodical task of wiping down tables while

Melody performs her set on stage. Weeknights are lethargic, which Kurapika is grateful for. The patrons
who come at midnight on a Wednesday are halfway into a stupor when they arrive.


Watching his hand move in circles over the scuffed table he's cleaning helps Kurapika think more clearly.

He needs to slow down and consider his options carefully.

After his shift, Kurapika looks into Chrollo's past. Though he didn't get a last name, Chrollo's first name is
unusual enough to yield results. According to public records, Chrollo's in his thirties and has a few

charges related to robbing a bank using a dangerous weapon, although it appears no one was killed
during the altercation. Even so, he was given a heavy sentence of twenty-five years…

Looking deeper into the case, Kurapika can't find any indication that the contents of the bank's vault were
ever recovered, which means Chrollo either somehow spent it all, or he had accomplices who were never

caught. Kurapika is willing to bet that has something to do with how aggressive Chrollo's sentencing
was. There's always more going on than what a person is formally charged with. Still, Kurapika doesn't
find the story to be particularly moving. If Chrollo’s primary vice is being a thief who refused to snitch

that lines up with what he said about being low-risk. Compared to how evil Tserracnich is, Chrollo might
as well be a saint.

Although Chrollo was too observant for comfort, he also seemed to be very forthcoming.  If his

relationship with the people running the therapy program is half as good as Chrollo acts like  it  is,

Kurapika could get a lot of valuable information from him.





He doesn't cancel his reoccurring appointment to meet Chrollo. The mental-health facility is separate
from the concrete block which houses the prisoners but it's still on the prison's campus. So, Kurapika has

to pass through the front gate and security check-in like everyone else, but he has special badge that

marks him as a therapy omega. He can tell the security staff view him differently from other visitors,
even though they're not supposed to.


This time, Kurapika is carrying a travel mug of coffee from home to compliment the bags he's got under

his eyes. He's not sure how lax they'll be about bringing in a drink from outside, but it's innocent enough
that he shouldn't face much backlash if it's the wrong move.

The guard at the check-in desk opens Kurapika’s mug to run a wooden stirring stick through the dark

liquid, making sure there's nothing hiding inside. The restrictions on what therapy omegas can have are

lighter because there's a real effort being made to create a gentler environment for the program.
Kurapika also suspects that lingering biases against an omega’s capacity for violence are covertly at play.

The coffee could have poison. If Kurapika was paired with was the correct alpha it would have been

poison. Consequences be damned.


Kurapika takes his coffee back without comment and goes to wait in the therapy visitation room,
nodding in recognition to his familiar escort. He sits in the same chair as last week, holding the mug in

his lap with both hands. A few minutes later, the door is opened and Chrollo is allowed in. Like last time,

Kurapika waves the guard away so they can be alone.


"I knew I smelled something good in the hall." Chrollo remarks as he takes a seat.


He gestures to the mug with his cuffed hands.

"Did you bring that to taunt me or gain my favor?"

Kurapika eyes the muzzle over Chrollo’s mouth, keeping it locked up. Though he doesn't particularly care

to free this man, Kurapika doesn't think Chrollo is some rabid beast either. Kurapika sets the mug on the
table between them and gives Chrollo a slightly awkward smile.


"We're friends now, aren't we?"

Surprise flashes through Chrollo’s eyes then he places his hands over the muzzle, hiding his clear

amusement.

"That sort of act really doesn't suit you."

A muscle twitches under Kurapika’s eye and without thinking he snatches the mug back and drinks from
it. He can feel the weight Chrollo's eyes on him as he swallows the hot, bitter drink.

"Xi-Yu University?" Chrollo remarks, observing the logo plainly visible on the side of the mug. "Did you

study there?"

Kurapika lowers the mug, his face calm once more. Xi-Yu University is a prestigious school in upper
Yorknew City that's known for its elite ties to the world of business and finance. Kurapika just got this
mug to match the story he made up on his application. He studied the school with the intention of

impressing that highborn beast Tserracnich during conversation.

"Yes," Kurapika lies without inflection.

He dangles the easy lie like a lure. As expected, Chrollo sees through it.

"Come on, you're not even trying anymore." Chrollo replies.

Kurapika shifts his chair to fully turn his body to Chrollo. He rests his elbows on the table, leaning over it
towards the alpha. Chrollo's brow twitches but he doesn't change his posture to mirror Kurapika’s. In this

position, Kurapika is aware his face is in full view of the camera above that ugly, fake plant.

"Are you educated, Chrollo?" Kurapika asks, letting the man's name roll off his tongue for the first time.

There's a gleam of interest in Chrollo’s hollow eyes. He sees the lure that Kurapika’s waving in front of his

face.


"Not formally," Chrollo replies. It's likely the truth. "But I've read up on a few subjects. Mostly in the
humanities."


Kurapika nods, taking that in as a possible subject to engage Chrollo with.


"That's good. Education is important for its own sake."


Chrollo's eyes close as he chuckles, lashes casting shadows on his cheeks from the harsh overhead

lighting.


"That sounds like an earnest belief. Now I know there's no way you went to a business school. It probably

wasn't even your major wherever you actually studied. What a strange thing to lie about." Chrollo says.

Chrollo is speaking softly again so only Kurapika’s lies have a chance of being recorded and Chrollo’s
acknowledgement of the truth is hidden.

"Maybe you're just a pathological liar and came here for some easy validation. I wouldn't really mind
that… But I'd love to have the privacy to speak with you more plainly."


Kurapika swallows. On top of knowing a lot about the therapy program, Chrollo is clever and savvy. He
would make a great ally even though Kurapika can't risk telling him the whole truth.

"… One of the great things about Xi-Yu University was the networking opportunities."

"…"

Kurapika is speaking clearly so he believes that the surveillance will pick up on his "normal" half of the
conversation and hopefully lead anyone listening into filling in the blanks of what Chrollo is saying to be
equally mundane. But the non-sequitur is so obvious that Chrollo should be able to understand there's a

hidden message in his words.

Networking… let us connect for real. I want to speak more plainly with you too.

Chrollo finally mirrors Kurapika’s body language, leaning into the conversation. His cuffs clunk against

the tabletop as he rests his hands on it.

"Even though, as an omega, some of the people there thought I was just trying to find someone to marry."
Kurapika continues.


I want something I'm not supposed to want. I'm not here to act like a proper omega.

"… Is that right? Must be frustrating to not be taken seriously."

Chrollo engages with Kurapika’s lie and responds at a normal speaking volume so Kurapika knows he

must've gotten the gist of it.

Kurapika nods earnestly. As he thinks of what to say next, he licks his lips unconsciously, searching for
the right words. He's sure there must be a way for alphas and omegas to have more privacy. But how?

"So, you aren't married?" Chrollo asks.

Kurapika pauses. What could the coded message in that be? In trying to figure out the clue, an awkward

amount of silence passes between them.

"… No," Kurapika answers hastily.


It's the truth and it matches what's on his application. Maybe Chrollo is asking if Kurapika already has an

accomplice. He does, but he would never say anything that could implicate Melody. Kurapika presses his
lips together in thought then decides to continue with this line of dialogue. If he's interpreting Chrollo

correctly, Chrollo might be asking if he's open to a partnership.

"… But, ah. I'm open to the possibility. For the right… alpha."

Kurapika’s cheeks grow warmer with each embarrassing word that leaves his mouth. He bites down the

urge to cringe at himself. It's a miracle that Chrollo doesn't laugh in his face.

"… Right. Whoever you take an interest in would be very lucky." Chrollo’s response is playful but measured.


There's amusement twinkling in his eyes like light catching on something at the bottom of a well.
Kurapika wonders if Chrollo is flashing a lure of his own.

Kurapika clears his throat and sips his coffee again. This time, he reaches for the index cards of ice
breakers to flip through them. Maybe there's something in them he could use.


"What was the last book you read?" Kurapika reads from a card.

That should give Chrollo some flexibility in his answer. If Chrollo is as well-read as he claims, he could
pick some title or genre that will give him a clue about what he wants in exchange for this partnership.

"The second volume of the Horror Angel series. "

"…"

The response is so instantaneous Kurapika doubts there's any secret message in it.

"Have you ever heard of it? It's about-"

Kurapika takes a deep breath. "Yes, I know it. I've read the latest volume."

Chrollo gives Kurapika a charming smile.

"Will you bring it for me? I've been requesting it through the prison's library for months without any luck."

There it is. Now Kurapika knows they can start trading. He gives Chrollo the same pretty smile that he

gives to patrons at the bar. The smile that almost touches his eyes.

"Okay, I'll try."

It will be good to start testing the limits of what he can get past the front desk. Kurapika flips through a
few more cards. "Cats or dogs? How do you like your eggs?" Wastes of time. Kurapika taps the cards

against the table to even them out before setting the pile down.

"I think you'd be surprised what they'd let you do." Chrollo adds. "And you should try to talk to Eliza if she's

available."


Kurapika nods. Eliza. She must be the one who runs her mouth to Chrollo just a bit too much.




After the session, Chrollo is escorted back to the cell block and Kurapika notifies the guard outside the
visitor's room that he'd like to go to the on-site psychologist's office before leaving. The guard nods and
gestures to the office that's just down the hall. It’s not even out of this guard's view so he doesn’t bother

calling a separate escort for Kurapika.

By the hallway benches is the a guard for the to the office. He's stationed behind a desk with a pair of
monitors. The guard is leaning back in his chair and flipping through a magazine that Kurapika can't see

the cover for. Beyond the guard desk is a receptionist - a petite woman who Kurapika can barely see

behind her computer.

Kurapika fixes his hair as he walks past the guard, his visitor's pass dangling over his navel on the end of
a yellow lanyard that signals he's an omega. Only when he's right in front of the desk does the guard look
up. The man looks at Kurapika's badge first and his face second. So inattentive… Kurapika wonders if this
guard is always on the morning shift. He glances at the guard's name tag to take note of it. Squala.

Kurapika keeps his chin down and greets the guard politely as he continues walking to the receptionist. It
seems that once he’s been cleared at the entrance to the prison, the guards inside won't bother frisking
him between locations. Unless he gives them a reason to be suspicious, that is.


The receptionist looks up once Kurapika reaches her desk. The woman has long, dark hair with a bright

yellow lanyard peeking through the strands, meaning she's also an omega, but her badge is clearly
marked as member of the staff along with her name: Eliza.

Kurapika’s careful not to show his surprise. He was sure that the person Chrollo was getting his details

from would be a doctor or therapist. Sensitive information must pass through this woman's hands.

Under his bangs, Kurapika allows his eyes to sweep across her desk, scanning the space. There are piles
of folders filled with papers and a cup of pens with silly charms attached to the ends. Along with the
paperwork, the desk is full of little trinkets. The sort of silly things found in gift-shops. Either Eliza has

more time to travel than the average receptionist or she's popular among her co-workers.

Just like Squala, Eliza's eyes flicker from Kurapika's badge to his face.

"Good morning, Kurapika!" She greets him with a chipper smile after having read his name. "Are you here
for your alpha appointment?"

Since this wasn't his first appointment with his alpha, there was no need for Kurapika to check in with the

staff for the therapy program after clearing security. The fact that she's asked that question means

there's a communication gap between security at the front and here.

"Oh, no, I just finished it." Kurapika says. "I was wondering if someone here was available to talk about it."

Eliza nods and starts moving her mouse, clicking around.

"Let me see who's available…"

Eliza eyes flicker in recognition as she reads something on her monitor.


"Oh! You're paired with Chrollo! How's that been? Um, if you don't mind telling me, that is." She asks,
visibly eager in the way a friend would be but trying to tone it down for her job.


Behind Kurapika, he can hear the leather of the guard's chair creek.


"It's been going well," Kurapika replies, not making any attempt to lower his voice. "I'm not coming to

complain about him, I just wanted to ask if I could bring him something."


Now that Eliza knows Kurapika isn't in emotional distress, she opens up right away.


"Ah, yes. Omega companions bring things in for their partners all the time. It's a real perk for the alphas."

Eliza says. "But it does depend on the thing. It has to be cleared and all." She adds quickly.


"Right, that makes sense." Kurapika nods.


Eliza leans over her desk so Kurapika matches her. She lowers her voice and whispers to Kurapika poorly.


"Not that I have favorites or anything, but I think you got really lucky." She says with a twinkle in her eye.

Eliza is definitely the sort of person to try and make friends with anyone and anything. Kurapika doesn't
understand that sort of thing, but he doesn't disparage her for it either.

"You really think so?" Kurapika whispers back.


Eliza nods.

"I think a lot of the inmates are good on the inside and all… But Chrollo just makes it easy, you know?"


Kurapika thinks of the mind games Chrollo has been trying to play with him. Being with Chrollo these last

two appointments has been like someone running their hands over worn fabric, intentionally searching
for and picking at loose threads. At the very least this means that Eliza  isn't listening to their

conversations, not that he would have expected a receptionist to be allowed to do that.

"Right…"

Kurapika can feel eyes on them. He looks back to see the guard Squala staring. Once he’s been caught,
Squala blushes and quickly turns away to pretend to be watching his monitors. From this angle, Kurapika

can see that Squala has the security camera footage open. The monitors have at least a dozen feeds

split between them. Kurapika doesn't have time to examine the feeds without drawing attention but
within three seconds he can spot footage of the inside of the visitation room he and Chrollo were just in

as welll as the hall outside the room where the other guard is posted.

Kurapika turns back to Eliza and sees there's a faint blush on her cheeks too.

"A-Anyway!" She clears her throat. "The only person who's free right now is a counselor, but if all you want
to talk about is policy, he should be able to help. Is that okay?" Eliza says.

"That would be great, thanks." Kurapika replies.

Kurapika steps back into a little chair that's in the corner of the room. It seems like he and Chrollo aren't

being heavily monitored in real time, especially not their words since Squala  isn't listening with

headphones. With the way he's sitting, Kurapika thinks Squala is only likely to notice big movements on
those  little feeds, such as a scuffle. The feed could be being transmitted to other computers and
Kurapika thinks it's safe to assume the recordings are stored somewhere but… He and Chrollo should

have a little breathing room.

After a few minutes, the counselor calls Kurapika into his office. The counselor is a tall man with long,
white hair. He's wearing a butter yellow sweater and has a calm demeanor to match. He's either a beta or

blocking his pheromones heavily.


The counselor gestures for Kurapika to have a seat in front of his desk. Unlike Eliza, this man keeps him
desk clear. Aside from his computer, there's onto a box of tissues placed next to his name plate: Kastro.

"Hello, Kurapika, I don't believe we've had the pleasure of meeting."


Kastro introduces himself politely.


"Thank you for volunteering and welcome to the program. How have your appointments been going?"

Kastro asks, taking a seat across from Kurapika.

"It was nice. I was nervous at first." Kurapika keeps his voice soft as he speaks.

If they already think he's sensitive then he might as well use it to his advantage.

"I think we're warming up to each other. We like the same book series."

Kastro gives Kurapika a mild but encouraging smile. "That's great."

"… But he hasn't read the latest one yet. Is it alright if I bring it in to lend it to him?" Kurapika asks.

"That should be alright. We want your appointments together to be as comfortable as possible… Of

course, we still have to balance things like safety and the prison's existing policies. The security check-in
will have to inspect any items you bring and they'll have the final say about what's allowed." Kastro says.

That confirms what Eliza said. Kurapika wonders if there's a way he could use the words in the book to

create a cipher that Chrollo would understand but the guards wouldn't notice. That should be possible
and he thinks that Chrollo would be smart enough for it. The question remains, how much information

should he give to Chrollo? He doesn't know Chrollo's opinion on Tserracnich, if he knows of the man at

all. He also doesn't have any leverage over Chrollo yet. Kurapika should share as little as possible.

Noticing Kurapika lost in thought, the counselor speaks up again.

"You know… It's very common for omegas in the program to become fond of their alphas."

Kurapika’s thoughts on creating a cipher screech to a halt. A sickening chill rushes down Kurapika’s spine
knowing Tserracnich is one of the alphas lurking in the program.

"There's nothing wrong with… well," Kastro reconsiders his words before continuing.

"Your feelings about Chrollo are always valid." He says, like he’s worried about making too many

assumptions. "That's why my colleagues and I are here to speak to you both regularly. Everyone here just
wants you to be safe and happy."

Kurapika keeps his face neutral, pretending not to notice what the counselor is implying. He doesn't care
if bringing Chrollo gifts makes it look like he’s being swept off his feet by that convict. But the way Kastro
is talking almost makes it sound like he's… expecting it.

"I appreciate you looking out for me," Kurapika replies mildly.


After a bit more smalltalk, Kurapika takes his leave for the day.


Kurapika spends another night on his feet at the bar, turning over his options for what to do next. At the
end of his long shift, he plucks the third copy of Horror Angel off his bookshelf and places it next to his

keys, waiting to be delivered to Chrollo.

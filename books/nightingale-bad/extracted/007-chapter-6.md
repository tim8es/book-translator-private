# Chapter 6

At the security check-in desk, Kurapika adjusts the sleeves of his cardigan, making a show of trying and
failing to conceal the ends of a fresh strip of fabric tied around his wrist. The security guard glances at

Kurapika's wrist but he doesn't say anything. In response to the man's prying eyes, Kurapika ducks his
head in a poor attempt at hiding, hunching his shoulders like he wants to be even smaller.

Good. Let the guard think that Kurapika’s secret is that he's bringing Chrollo another demure  little

handkerchief of his scent.


Inside the private room, Kurapika waits for Chrollo by the table. When Chrollo enters the room, he's also

got something for Kurapika. His copy of Horror Angel: Volume Three. Chrollo sets the book on the table
and since Kurapika is standing, Chrollo stays on his feet. He regards Kurapika with a questioning tilt of

his head.


Without a word, Kurapika rolls up one of his sleeves to reveal the strip of fabric tied around his wrist. He
tugs it free and the fabric unravels, falling to the table along with the slip of paper it was concealing. The
actual secret. Immediately, Chrollo's eyes lock onto the note. He rounds the table to Kurapika’s side and

snatches the note off the table. Kurapika doesn't try to keep it from him, allowing the alpha to stride past
him and so he can read it with his back turned and shoulders hunched - hiding it from prying eyes. It’s

pointless, of course. Kurapika looked at the note the second he was alone with it, but it's also written in
some sort of code that he wasn't able to decipher. Still, one thing sticks in his mind. That strange,

asterisk-type symbol he saw on the door-frame of that run-down building.

He watches Chrollo's back as he reads the short note several times. Most likely, Chrollo is memorizing it.

After a few moments, Chrollo's shoulders rise and fall with a deep breath. He folds the small note up
even smaller and passes it back to Kurapika.


"Get rid of this," he says.

Kurapika takes the note. Glancing back at the table, he notices Chrollo’s swift hands had swiped up the

strip of fabric he used to hide the note originally. He sighs but doesn't want to comment on that,
preferring to not think of why Chrollo took it. He tucks the note into the waistband of his underwear since

no one's strip-searching him.

Chrollo turns to face him. He looks Kurapika in the eyes and says:

"I really enjoyed the book. I'd love to hear what you thought of the reveal."

Normally, Kurapika would love to talk about the book series he's invested in. But the irrelevant statement
following Chrollo essentially giving him an order strikes a nerve. Kurapika’s jaw tightens as sudden anger
ignites inside him and all of the anxiety he felt about tracking down Chrollo's people and getting trapped

in a dark room with them acts as fuel. His face twists into a sneer.

Is this seriously all Chrollo has for him? Returning his paperback copy of a book?

Without breaking eye-contact, Kurapika shifts his foot in between Chrollo's. He hooks it around the inside
of Chrollo’s left ankle and at the same time shoves his shoulder - taking his leg out from under him and

knocking him off-balance. A grunt is forced out of Chrollo as he drops backwards into the loveseat.

Before he can recover, Kurapika grabs him by the muzzle, his fingers slipping through the bars so they're

centimeters from his lips. He holds on tight, letting the narrow bars of the cage dig into his skin, and
yanks Chrollo’s face up to take hold of his gaze with a burning glare.

"Don't forget that I don't work for you." Kurapika snaps. "What do you have for me?"

Chrollo's breath caresses Kurapika’s fingers as he looks up, eyes wide without the faintest trace of

hostility. Chrollo is so calm he's not even the type of alpha to get angry when he's challenged.

"I've got information," Chrollo offers right away. "Tserracnich's a high-profile prisoner so he's kept

separate from most of us, but I know who Tserracnich's omega is."


Kurapika stays silent, waiting for Chrollo to continue without easing up even a millimeter.


"She's a young omega in her early twenties named Juno. Tserracnich likes her but I think he's getting
bored with her fast."

Chrollo’s mouth twitches just under Kurapika’s fingers and his breath hitches in barely concealed

amusement.


"She's blonde but I don't think it's natural so I think you've got a chance at taking her spot. You're as
enticing as any blonde, baby-sitting cheerleader is to a murderous madman."

Kurapika huffs and rolls his eyes, finding that detail ridiculous. From his investigations on Tserracnich,

the man didn't have a preferred hair color among his victims.

"I'll try and figure out what about Juno isn't satisfying Tserracnich and you can start dropping hints that

you're growing bored with me for the same reasons. Maybe I don't know enough about wine-tasting for

you or something." Chrollo continues.


"Wine-tasting?" Kurapika replies doubtfully.

Chrollo shrugs. "Tserracnich is disgustingly rich and truthfully I don't have a very refined pallet. I'll let you

know when I get something better to work with."


Kurapika holds Chrollo's serene gaze for a few more beats then eases back with reluctant acceptance.
It's not nothing so Kurapika slips the key to the muzzle out of his sleeve.

"Good enough. Because I don't think you're a mindless animal, I'm going to unlock you so you can drink

coffee like a human being for a few minutes."

Chrollo’s eyes flicker from the lingering sneer on Kurapika’s face to the key that's pinched between his
forefinger and thumb.

Shoving Chrollo around a little has helped soothe Kurapika’s nerves but he can’t help the way his heart

pounds. Since Kurapika believes that alphas can and should be held responsible for their behavior, he

must also believe that alphas are capable of being more than mindless brutes. Chrollo isn't an exception
to this. Though Kurapika's able to rationally tell himself that Chrollo wouldn't choose to attack him in this
way, the possibility remains. If Kurapika’s judgment is wrong, then his life could change very, very fast.

Kurapika pulls on the muzzle to make Chrollo bow his head, exposing the lock on the back of his neck.
He's willing to accept the risk.


At least Chrollo isn't stupid or ugly.

The lock clicks open and Kurapika carefully eases the muzzle off his face, pausing only to run his fingers
over the back of Chrollo’s ears to ease the straps off them. Closer than ever, Kurapika can see that

Chrollo has piercing holes in his ears. Between the piercings and the tattoo, Kurapika can't help
wondering how Chrollo used to dress himself when he had the choice.

Chrollo doesn't make any sudden movements and slowly lifts his head. Chrollo doesn't lunge for him. He

doesn't even look at Kurapika just yet. Instead, he's more preoccupied with rubbing the red indents left on

the bridge of his nose and the backs of his ears left by the weight of the muzzle. Kurapika pushes down
the urge to wince in sympathy. He hadn't considered that, but looking at the muzzle in his hands it was

obviously designed without comfort in mind - just cost and durability.


He turns his back to the alpha and steps away to drop the muzzle and key on the table. He exchanges
them for his travel mug which he passes to Chrollo. Chrollo accepts it and flicks the top open to breathe

in the fragrant steam before taking a long sip of the drink. He hums deeply in satisfaction.


"This is good. You make it at home?" Chrollo asks.

Kurapika crosses his arms tightly and hums in affirmation, "French press."


"I'm sure it's not much of a compliment to say it's better than what I get in the mess hall, but this is a real

treat. Thank you."


Kurapika sits on the loveseat next to Chrollo, keeping his arms crossed so his uncovered wrist is tucked
in his armpit.

"I thought you said you didn't have a refined pallet," Kurapika says tightly.

"I don't. That's how bad it is."


Amusement plays at the corners of Kurapika's mouth and he quickly lifts his hands to comb through his

hair, trying to hide his fond expression from Chrollo's watchful eyes.

"… Well, I don't know much about wine either. Bussing tables at the bar hasn't taught me a thing about it."
Kurapika replies.

"Then I can tell you some wine-lingo to use and you tell me how you manage to keep the drunk alphas off

you."

"A broom works pretty well." Kurapika deadpans.

Chrollo chuckles and Kurapika doesn't even realized he's uncrossed his arms to talk with his hands until
he notices Chrollo tracking them with his eyes. Kurapika flushes as he becomes aware of his exposed

wrist. Chrollo momentarily locks eyes with him.

"I bet. Dish rags are good for batting flies off too." Chrollo responds to Kurapika’s words rather than

comment about his body.

Kurapika drops his hands into his lap, warmth lingering on his cheeks, but he doesn't bother to cross his
arms again.

The corners of Chrollo's eyes crinkle and he tilts the mug towards Kurapika.

"… I'm glad I can partake in communion with you." Chrollo says.

Kurapika eyes the mug. "… What do you mean?"

"I know you're well-read enough to know what I mean."

Kurapika won't try to deny that. In literature, every meal or drink shared with another is an act of
communion. He slowly reaches out and wraps his fingers around the mug, fitting them between Chrollo's
to take it from him. He brings the mug up to his lips and fits them over the damp imprint Chrollo left

behind to take a drink of the coffee. It's still hot enough for Kurapika to feel the warmth rushing down his

throat and spreading through his chest.


He lowers his chin and passes the mug back to Chrollo. Suddenly, Chrollo seems much closer despite
Kurapika not feeling him move. Maybe it's just in his head. Chrollo places both of his hands on the mug,
covering Kurapika’s so they're holding it together. Kurapika freezes and stares at their hands, not sure

what to make of the contact.


"I know you don't work for me," It almost sounds like an apology for earlier. "I've been thinking a lot about
why you've come here."


Kurapika's pounding heart replaces the relaxed warmth that was settling in with a surge of adrenaline.
Kurapika doesn't try to resist it. It's an important reminder. He shouldn't have gotten distracted in the first
place. He doesn't respond but he listens with his whole body. All his senses are trained on Chrollo.
Chrollo’s voice is just as expressive as Kurapika’s closed-off face.


"Even if he was arrested right after whatever it was he did to set you off, I know from experience that due

process can take a very long time. Even for cases far more straightforward than Tserracnich's mess."

Chrollo fits his fingers in between Kurapika’s pale knuckles.

"I'm sure being made to sit and wait must've been agony for someone as determined as you. And then, in

the end, I have no doubt that he was only charged for a tiny fraction of his crimes and allowed to live."


Kurapika takes a shaky breath, remembering the cold stillness he felt during the months of gathering
evidence and then the lengthy trail before a verdict was reached. He remembers the outrage he felt after

he heard Tserracnich's sentencing. It shook him out of the the way he had hunkered down and shut
himself off from feeling anything. It was then he was set ablaze with the heat of a new purpose.


"I think you're more of a spring."

Kurapika blinks himself out of the surge of memories brought on by Chrollo's words. His eyes are burning.

"You're mistaken about something,"

Kurapika replies, still unable to lift his gaze from their joined hands. He can feel Chrollo watching him,
taking in more than Kurapika wants him to like always. It coaxes the truth of Kurapika’s feelings out of

the darkest parts of him. He had thought that only Tserracnich would see the ugliness inside Kurapika in
his final moments. Now, one more person might get to see it.

"I don't want him to be executed by the government. I want to be the one to kill him," He whispers.

Kurapika's never said it out loud to anyone before. Melody knows that he's coming here to seek revenge,

but saying it cleanly, laying out his ugly hatred - it's new. It's a heavy feeling he's never dumped out in the
open. Kurapika isn't here because he wants justice as a victim, he wants revenge.

Chrollo lifts Kurapika’s mug from their hands to set it on the floor by the arm of the couch. He keeps one
hand on Kurapika's and brings it up to kiss his knuckles. The smoldering heat of Kurapika’s hatred gets
mixed up with the heat of Chrollo’s lips touching his skin for the first time. Kurpakia's breath gets stuck in
his throat and he shuts his eyes so he won't have to see the way Chrollo looks at him. With his lips still

pressed to Kurapika, Chrollo speaks, matching the softness of Kurapika's confession with one of his own.

"I've killed. They just didn't get me for it."


Chrollo whispers.


Kurapika stiffens, shock replacing the severity of his vitriol.


"But I know you won't tell. My secrets are as safe as yours."

Kurapika's face tightens as he tries to block out the implicit truth in Chrollo's words. When he finds his

voice, it's shaky.


"I haven't done anything illegal yet. If we betrayed each other, they couldn't arrest me." Kurapika whispers
back, his lips barely moving.


"Sure, but I'll get them to look more closely into your fake background and they'd blacklist you just for the

possibility that I'm telling the truth. You'd lose your only chance. I know that your revenge matters more
to you than getting caught or you wouldn't be here at all."


Chrollo's lips move away from Kurapika's knuckles. He turns Kurapika's hand over and takes his time
kissing each fingertip and then his palm.


"Consider my confession a show of trust between us."

Kurapika should be worried about details like who  it was Chrollo killed. He should care how or why

Chrollo did it. He should be worried about just how good Chrollo is at masking how dangerous he is. It's
risky to stay close to Chrollo when Kurapika has no idea  if he did  it in self-defense, or as a cold

calculation, or simply for pleasure. But Kurapika only has one thing he really wants to know.

"… What was it like?" He asks quietly, wrapping his free arm around himself for comfort. He opens his
eyes to stare at the framed print over Chrollo’s shoulder with the same feeling he gets looking out the
bus window. "… Killing someone."

"I thought it would be harder than it was," Chrollo answers. "I've always heard that sort of thing changes a

person forever and that it damages their soul. I expected to feel a torrent of agony, but that feeling never
came."

Chrollo encircles Kurapika's naked wrist with his thieving, and apparently murderous, fingers.

"But I don't know how you'll feel when it's your turn. I don't think there's anything you can do to prepare

yourself for it."

"I am prepared," Kurapika says too automatically for it to be genuine.

"Alright." Chrollo's reply is just as automatic. If Chrollo is bothered by Kurapika's response, he doesn't
show it. He accepts Kurapika's answer at face value.

Kurapika's eyes drift from the field of flowers to take in the sight of Chrollo holding his hand to his face.

Kurapika hasn't acknowledged the way Chrollo's been speaking to him since they met, brushing it off as

double-speak or teasing. It's harder to ignore the way Chrollo is touching him. Kurapika has to accept
that he should take Chrollo at face-value too.

"… I liked the reveal," Kurapika says, bringing them back to the book as a form of peace offering.

With half of his face tucked against Kurapika's wrist, Chrollo's small smile is obscured.

"I liked it too. It was a little cliche but - "


"- But it was set up well."


Chrollo nods, "Exactly."


Chrollo lowers their hands to let them rest in his lap. His hold on Kurapika is light enough that it would be

easy for Kurapika to pull away if he wanted to.


Although Kurapika didn't mean to let this visit become a book-club meeting, it happens extremely fast.

Getting to talk about something normal for once brings Kurapika out of his body into his imagination. It's
a relief.





"You're in a good mood."


Melody's tone is as pleasant as her smile. She's sitting at the bar with a glass of water between her

folded hands. Kurapika is on the other side of the bar, counting the money in the register so they can

close up for the night. He isn't smiling.

"What?" Kurapika's rhythm doesn't fluctuate as he quickly thumbs bills onto the counter.

"You heard me," Melody doesn't let up. "Peace looks lovely on you."


Kurapika frowns, his body thoughtlessly trying to prove her wrong.


"Any peace I feel is only temporary until I've done what I need to do." He says.


"Mm, maybe, but  if we don't appreciate the things in life that are temporary, then there wouldn't be

anything to appreciate at all." Melody says.

Melody is too wise. It's both wonderful and frustrating. She sees through him just as easily as Chrollo
does and Kurapika wonders what the alpha convict and the beta musician could possibly have in

common to give them that skill.

"Fine," Kurapika concedes and moves onto the coins. "Any word from your contact?"

It was thanks to some mysterious connection Melody has that he was able to learn that Tserracnich was
in the Omega Therapy Program in the first place. He's not completely reliant on Chrollo, despite what the

alpha might think. However, because Melody is so wise, she hasn't revealed her contact to Kurapika just

as he hasn't revealed Melody to Chrollo.

"Not much, but there are talks inside the program about alternative therapy methods planned for the next
few months." Melody says.

"Such as?"

"Nothing ground-breaking. Just more gentle socialization and trying out healthy coping activities."

Kurapika scoffs.

"If you think only the innocent deserve a gentle rehabilitation experience, then you should give  it an

earnest try for yourself." Melody calls him out without hesitation.

Kurapika flattens out his scornful expression.


"No, I don't need that. And I don't think it would be helpful anyway."


Melody hums a few notes. They both know that Kurapika just said two completely different things.


"In any case, the plans for the program aren't set in stone, so I think you could put in some suggestions
for socialization methods that you think would be helpful to you." Melody finishes, sipping her water.


"I got it, thanks Melody."

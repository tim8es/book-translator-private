## **Chapter 25: Epilogue**

### Chapter Summary

Ilya’s self-worth was achievement-based, which was another thing he had learned about

himself. He and Shane had this one in common, though. They had both agreed that there

was nothing wrong with this, even when they used it to self-sabotage themselves. Like

when Shane wasn’t allowed to eat his Snickers after a bad game or when Ilya set

unreasonable emotional targets for himself so the reward felt sweeter. You know. Stuff

like that.

Yay, therapy.

## Chapter End Notes

And we're DONE. And thus the longest piece of fanfiction I've ever written is complete

(which, to be honest, probably means it needs a really good edit).

Thank you so much to everyone who has read, liked, commented and shared with

friends. I appreciate it so much and I have loved reading your comments and thoughts

and suspicions (omg you guys are so suspicious). It's been a real joy to me.

I have nothing else really to add here in terms of authors notes. OR DO I.

On the 'bromance' - Ilya invites his good friend Shane Hollander to lots of the Cens

occasions. 'Hollander was in town to see his parents,' becomes his frequent refrain and

Bood has to stare fixedly at the ceiling as if he has no idea. The rest of the Cens are

hockey bros so they think nothing of it. Well. Harris thinks PLENTY of it but he stays

completely silent on the subject until one day he heads upstairs to use the other

bathroom at Bood's and walks in on Ilya and Shane making out. He is dragged into the

Hollanov Fan Club. He, in fact, becomes its treasurer.

The Metros become super peeved to see Shane at all these social occasions so in return

invite Ilya to their things. This causes Hayden and J.J. no end of stress because in the

comfort of his own team, Shane is more relaxed and liable to have a drink or two and

suddenly can't keep his hands off Ilya which Ilya does NOTHING to help.

On the woking of the MLH - The Code gets a big heaping help when Dallas Kent is

revealed to be a rapist. Toronto HAVE to clean up their act so they pull out all the stops,

including voluntarily enforcing The Code (Jesus why did I call it this) so then it's

Montreal, Ottawa, Vancouver and Toronto all one big inclusive family. And naturally,

when Scott Hunter comes out by kissing Kip on the Ice, New York join and all the other Works inspired by this one

New York teams and now its reached the US and not just being a weird Canada thing

and Crowell is REALLY pissed. Now he has to take it seriously so through gritted teeth

he adopts the whooole thing for MLH to try and make a good news story. Except who is

front page for all of this? That's right. It's Shane and Alex and Petrov who were the

'inspiration'. Suck it Crowell.

On the Cens - Troy and Luca still go to Ottawa, don't worry, the Metros don't poach

everybody. And Harris is there. Eventually, Ilya's shitty head coach retires and Stewie

takes the job. They win their first cup.

On Ilya/Shane - Ilya proposes on a random Tuesday in the summer whilst they're at the

lake. He uses electric candles because have you ever tried to keep candles lit with a

slight breeze? Hard work.

When do Shane and Ilya come out as a couple? Valentine's Day 2019, after Ilya gets his

PR. No one is surprised. Comeau wins the betting pool and cries at the wedding. They

have the candles at their wedding on the tables. There are chairs.

On Petrov - Ah, Petrov. Mishka. I did try to get them to fuck. I actually began this story

with the intention but, alas, it didn't work. It was Wrong. Every time I wrote something,

Shane would literally wheedle out of it? And Petrov was so besotted with Shane he just

wanted to do right by him? Painful. Not Sexy. Much better as gay mentor, which is the

role he takes on happily. He's notably absent in this epilogue because - being a

workaholic - he doesn't just stay in Europe for 6 months, it becomes a year, and his

relationship with his ex is rekindled.

Btw, some headcanon, Petrov 7 is actually Petrov and his six close friends from

boarding school, which includes Alex. They had formed a little clique at school - rich

kids who didn't fit the 'norm'. Also Petrov doesn't smile because he got hate-crimed in

his twenties and it broke his jaw in several places. He's never been happy with his smile

since. I know. The trauma. He has a lot of therapy. Weird to have head-canon that you

don't actually write into a story but hey ho.

No idea what I'll do with my spare time after this. Guess I'll have to write something

else.

# Chapter Two

At the quay Pavel handed his own scarf to Nina; she had left hers at the inn. The eastbound boat had not arrived, but the westbound one had.

Nina opened the envelope. Apart from a small dried leaf it was empty; there was no money inside. She put it away without a word.

The river took the last of the light, and for a moment the town seemed to hold its breath. 'We can wait,' she said. It sounded less like a promise than a question.

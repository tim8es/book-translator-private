*Again.*

Jason?

They remembered.

<!-- bt-block:000152 -->

<!-- bt-block:000154 -->
Jason, though—Tim glances around for him and spots him near the medbay entry, his back turned. Tim can’t tell if it’s his attempt to give them privacy or if he’s acting as guard.

<!-- bt-block:000155 -->

<!-- bt-block:000159 -->

Oh.

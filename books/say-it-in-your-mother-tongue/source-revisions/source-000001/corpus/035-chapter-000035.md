Chapter 33: leave you here all alone

<!-- bt-block:000002 -->
Chapter Notes

<!-- bt-block:000003 -->
**This is a Multi-POV ensemble story — chapters may focus on one pairing or move between multiple.**

<!-- bt-block:000004 -->
The only thing that still makes sense about his life, is the car.

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
Nico takes his right foot off the gas pedal, slowing the car enough to be driving under the speed limit. He yawns, having slept maybe fifty minutes if you *heavily* round it up, as the sign informing him that he’s indeed reached the factory appears. Turning right, he pulls into the parking lot.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
*Good.*

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
From the looks of it, none of Sebastian’s cars are parked there — hopefully *yet*, possibly *anymore*. Nico nods to himself, satisfied with the fact that he won’t have to deal with any type of confrontation just yet, especially not the one he can feel coming his way from Sebastian.

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
The factory is quiet.

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
A receptionist greets him, smiling in a way that would send shivers down the spine of any German person ever — and not the pleasant kind of shivers. She buzzes him in without a single comment about the fact that managed to forget his keycard again, calling after him to wish him a good day.

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
*Doubt it will be.*

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
Nico moves through the halls like a shadow, fast and unseen. No one gives him a single weird look as he hides in *his* garage, busying himself with checking the car, inspecting what the mechanics have been working on since his last time there. He hums, pleased with the fact that they repaired the mirror he’s been complaining about from the very first moment behind the wheel.

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
He only feels the shift in the air once he absolutely has nowhere to hide.

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
One of the mechanics behind a nearby corner says something in broken Spanish, his smile audible in his voice. Having obviously gotten a response which Nico doesn’t overhear, the mechanic says, “Yeah, he’s already here.”

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
*Scheiße.*

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
Grabbing the first thing he can, Nico pretends to examine a can of brake cleaner as Gabriel walks into his line of sight.

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
Something inside Nico, somewhere deep within his soul, settles the moment his eyes make contact with Gabriel’s face the same way his cold, trembling fingers would if he had just a little bit of courage left in his body. He scans it, searching for something — *anything* — still clutching the spray can in his hands like a lifeline. “Good morning,” he says, offering Gabriel a soft smile he knows won’t be reciprocated.

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
Gabriel doesn’t spare him a single look as he walks past with a deadpan, *“Morning.”*

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
The warmth that *always* surrounds Gabriel is nowhere to be found, Nico realizes with lips parted in a mixture of surprise and unease. He takes a step towards the *boy* without really meaning to, his hands stilling on the can of brake cleaner.

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
*Say something. Sag etwas. Say something. Sag—*

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
“Did Seb bring you?” Nico breathes out the first thing his panicked mind could think of. *Stupid*, he calls himself mentally, cringing at how dumb he must seem.

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
Looking into his face, but not into his eyes, Gabriel nods. He forces out a heavy, “Yes.”

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
“Okay. Good,” Nico says, his voice breathy and a little lost, as he returns the spray can onto the shelf he took it from — immediately regretting his decision to do so as he fidgets with the strap of his wristwatch.

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
*Schau mich an, bitte. Just look me in the eyes, Gabi.*

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
“How was the drive?” Nico asks. “Were there a lot of cars?”

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
*Ich hab auf dich gewartet, weißt du das? I actually waited the whole night for you.*

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
“Fine,” Gabriel huffs, his voice rough.

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
*Kept thinking that you’d come back and I’d tell you everything and you’d… you’d kiss me. Isn’t that fucking pathetic? I’m almost forty and all I could think about was kissing my teammate.*

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
He looks away from Nicolas, his head hanging low on his shoulders as if he’s deciding something entirely too heavy. “Not a lot, was still early.”

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
*You need to tell him the truth, Nicolas.*

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
“I, *um*, I waited. For you, I mean. The… the whole night.”

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
Gabriel stops then, his whole body going stiff. He looks over his shoulder, but he doesn’t meet Nico’s eyes, stabbing his gaze into the German’s chest instead. “You didn’t have to,” he breathes out, his voice trembling just enough to be noticeable. He closes his eyes for a moment before adding, “Seb told you am not coming back.”

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
“I was hoping—”

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
*I was hoping you’d change your mind.*

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
Stepping away, no longer looking at Nico, Gabriel exhales loudly. His voice is steady when he admits, “me too, Nicolas. Guess we were both wrong.”

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
The garage’s obscenely loud cacophony of metal-on-metal noises mixed with the screeching of power tools stops, as if even the building felt the weight of their confrontation — which isn’t surprising, given that it’s the first one *actually* honest on both ends. The muffled voices coming from somewhere past the walls disappear, replaced by the sound of heartbeat and two sets of labored breath.

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
Gabriel places his right hand on the wall. He doesn’t lean on it, he just touches the cold surface like it’s the last stable thing in his *universe*. “Seb offered to come pick with me the stuff from hotel,” he says, much quieter than before, with closed eyes. “In afternoon,” he continues after a second, his composure gone. His breathing is shallow and way too fast, as if even speaking takes *too much* strength (which he doesn’t have).

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
*Oh.*

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
Swallowing loudly, Nico nods even if he’s aware that Gabriel can’t see him. “Alright,” he pushes out finally. “Should… *uh*, I’m probably not going to be there. When you come,” aware that his voice is too loud, Nico doesn’t have it in him to control it. “But that’s better for you two anyways, right?”

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
Gabriel digs the nails of his left hand into his palm, pushing into the soft meat hard enough to leave marks before he allows himself to respond. “Right,” he repeats, defeated. Fighting the need to look over his shoulder again, Gabriel just shakes his head as he leaves Nico’s side of the garage with a sigh.

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
And Nico, no matter how much he wants to follow him, wants to beg him to stay, just stands there, lips parted, eyes wide, confident that his oldest friend just *stole* Gabriel from him.

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
**=** **♡** **=**

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
“Aren’t you *too cute* to be sitting here all alone?”

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
Lance chuckles without looking up, typing on his phone. He’s sitting on one of the lounge chairs — the type reserved for sunny beaches in the Caribbean and not some closed off waiting area by simulators in a factory located in the middle of England. “Are you flirting with me?” he asks, fluttering his eyelashes at Fernando in faux innocence.

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
*“Sí, hermoso,”* Fernando purrs as he leans closer to Lance, caressing his cheek gently.

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
Not realizing he’s actually doing it, Lance pushes his cheek into Fernando’s hand, rubbing it like a cat. “But you should know something about me,” he breathes out, finally meeting Fernando’s deep eyes.

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
“Tell me, then,” the Spaniard demands, his voice low.

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
*“Yo tengo un novio,”* Lance says with the softest of smiles, his cheeks an adorable shade of pink.

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
Fernando licks his lips. He smiles at Lance, bright and wide, before bending down. Their faces are so close that their breaths become one, mingling with one another in a way they know *all too well*. “Do you now, *cariño?”* he asks quietly, only intending for one set of ears to overhear.

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
*“Oui,”* Lance nods. “I do.”

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
“That’s okay,” Fernando says, inching even closer to Lance’s face — as close as he can without actually pressing their lips together. “I have one too.”

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
Lance bites his lip, lowering his gaze for a breath, before meeting Fernando’s eyes again. “What’s he like?” he questions as the blush on his cheeks deepens slightly. “Is he pretty?”

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
“The prettiest,” Fernando responds with a nod. “And he’s *super* smart, too.”

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
“Really?” Lance breathes out, shuffling in his seat slightly. *“Wow,* he sounds awesome,” he adds with a chuckle. “Where is he now? I’d love to meet him.”

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
Fernando shrugs, offering Lance a casual smirk. “No idea, *pretty boy,”* the words roll from his tongue smooth and flirty. “Where’s your man?” Fernando asks, making a spectacle out of looking around them like a lighthouse keeper, “must be a terribly big fool to leave you here all alone.”

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->
Shaking his head, Lance objects, “no, he’s actually really nice. And he trusts me a lot, you know?”

<!-- bt-block:000056 -->
“Does he?”

<!-- bt-block:000057 -->
*“Oui,”* Lance responds with a single nod. “That’s why he can leave me alone while he works.”

<!-- bt-block:000058 -->
*“Ah,* that’s where you got me wrong, *cariño,”* Fernando says, before he lowers his voice just enough so that *only* Lance could possibly hear his next words, “I *do* trust you. But I am also *un hombre egoísta*. A *very* selfish man. I’d keep you all to myself, *querido*, if it was up to me,” with his right index finger, he taps the middle of his chest as the word *me* leaves his lips.

<!-- bt-block:000059 -->
Lance giggles.

<!-- bt-block:000060 -->
“So how about I take you out?” Fernando suggests honestly. He looks into Lance’s face, meeting his eyes with a soft smile, as he continues, “just you, me, bottle of great Spanish wine? What do you say?”

<!-- bt-block:000061 -->
With a titter, Lance breathes out, “but what would *mi novio* say?”

<!-- bt-block:000062 -->
“Have fun?” Fernando offers with a snigger. “I think he’d want you to go, *pretty boy.”*

<!-- bt-block:000063 -->
“Okay then,” Lance says, nodding, “it’s a date, then.” He unlocks his phone again, which has been vibrating in his hand every three to five seconds the whole time, and laughs loudly when he sees Esteban’s messages.

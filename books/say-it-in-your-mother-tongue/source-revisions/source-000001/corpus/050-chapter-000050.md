<!-- bt-block:000004 -->
Lance looks at the message again and sighs.

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
He hesitates to lift his right hand, closed into a tight fist, deciding on... *everything*. With another sigh, he knocks.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
*“Tu peux entrer,”* a voice from inside the room beckons him to enter.

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
Lance exhales all of the air from his lungs, breathing out until the point of pain, before he presses the door handle and pushes the door open. He scans the room with his eyes and his heart, hidden behind its cage of ribs, aches with a simple realization. The room that has served as Lawrence’s office for over two decades is the same as it’s always been — white walls, wooden bookshelves, a cabinet with glass doors full of bottles of expensive alcohol he receives as gifts and party favors, a computer and a landline that’s seen better days. Behind the huge desk, that surely doesn’t need to be *this* big, is a framed family photo taken on Lance’s tenth birthday.

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
*“Salut, papa,”* he says.

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
Lawrence looks up from a tablet that’s lying on the desk and once he makes eye contact with his son, he smiles. “Ah, good. I was starting to worry you did not receive my message,” he tells him before locking the tablet and pushing it further away on the table, making space in front of himself to rest his arms there.

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
With a nod, Lance closes the door behind him. “No, I just needed to, *uh*… finish… something,” he lies, and he knows that Lawrence knows it’s a lie, but he cannot bring himself to care. “What did you want to talk about?”

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
There’s a hum in the air between them, the noise of Lawrence’s too powerful computer mixing with the ever-present noise of this house that seems to never really go away properly, no matter how many people have come to check what could be causing it. Lawrence cocks his head to one side as he asks, “Is there anything you’d like to tell me?”

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
Few years ago, *hell,* even few weeks ago, Lance would be a stuttering mess trying to explain away any *mistakes* and *missteps* he could have made recently that his father would somehow learn about, no matter how hard he’d try to hide them away. He knows what reaction his father wants from him.

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
“I have no idea what you’re talking about, *papa,”* Lance says with a smile on his lips, soft and *almost* genuine. “Is everything alright?”

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
Lawrence hums under his breath, low and angry. He doesn’t look at his son when he tells him, “You seem to be forgetting people love to tell me things. Especially things that could bring *bad attention* to *my* business.”

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
*Yeah, it’s always about* your *business, papa.*

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
Wordlessly, Lance just stares into his father’s eyes.

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
*It’s never about anything else, is it, papa?*

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
He can see his father’s eyes become narrower, his eyebrows traveling down slightly. Lawrence takes a deep breath before he stands up, with his hands on the table. His voice is low when he lets out, “Do you understand what *this* could mean for you?”

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
Lance doesn’t say a word, only continues looking into his father’s eyes, like a cat trying to pick a fight.

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
Shaking his head, Lawrence looks away — and it takes everything in Lance not to feel victorious in their *little* encounter — and mutters something under his breath. He walks away from his table, hands clasped behind his back, moving across the room to his son without sparing him another look.

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
*Accuse me of sleeping with Fernando, papa.*

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
Lance licks his lips, unable to wipe the smile off his lips.

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
*Tell me that I’m a fucking disgrace, papa.*

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
He straightens his posture, his feet shoulder-width apart, giving off the vibe of effortless power and *bottomless* confidence.

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
*Say it, papa.*

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
“Are you gay?”

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
Lance cannot stop himself from barking out in laughter.

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
Lawrence’s voice is rougher and so very loud when he asks again, “Are you gay?” It sounds less like a question the second time.

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
“No,” Lance tells him truthfully.

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
Lawrence nods, his lips in a tight line. “Okay,” he breathes out after a few quiet seconds.

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
“Is that it?” Lance asks, cocking his head to one side. “Like… are we done? Is that all?”

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
A beat of silence, or maybe several, passes when neither of them moves a muscle. The quiet falls over them with heavy finality, and when Lawrence clears his throat without following it up with anything afterwards, Lance decides the conversation is over.

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
He exhales, exasperated and *oh so tired,* shaking his head a little before he decides to move. He turns away from his father, *spinning* on his heel, and takes a swift step towards the doors leading into the hallway. He doesn’t say anything before he reaches the door completely. With one hand on the handle, he turns back to Lawrence partially, breathing out, “Was that all? Do you really have no other questions?”

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
Biting his tongue, Lawrence shakes his head in disappointment before he asks, with a single nod, “Are you straight?”

China comes faster than either of them expected.

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
Nico is pacing the hallway in front of *Gabi’s* hotel room nervously, thinking of something smart (or at least *not stupid)* to say for the millionth time, but failing miserably. He sighs, closing his eyes for a second, before he decides to *finally* knock on the door — even if he doesn’t have a clue of what to say to his *teammate.*

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
“A second!” familiar voice shouts from inside the room.

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
Nodding — with nobody around to see — Nico shoves his hands into his pockets.

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
*I’m not nervous. Ich bin nicht nervös. I’m not nervous.*

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
Gabriel opens the door, gripping a towel in one hand, rubbing it over his wet hair. “Yeah? Oh, hey,” he says with a wide smile, moving out of the way to let Nico come inside the room.

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
“Hey,” Nico breathes out, his voice a little *too shaky* for his liking. “I, *uh*, I just wanted to… yeah, wanted to see you, I guess.”

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
Still smiling, Gabi throws the moist towel onto an empty chair. “Did you miss us?” he asks.

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
They are standing almost three meters apart, like they’re scared to get close to one another, Nico with hands in his pockets, Gabi empty handed, fidgeting with his fingers.

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
*“Us?”* Nico repeats, finally breaking the silent spell that was cast over them.

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
Gabi nods joyfully, moving past Nico fast towards the place where Nico guesses a bed stands. When he returns, he’s holding the stuffed bunny Nico won for him in the arcade — well, *technically,* Nico didn’t necessarily *win* it; in reality, he asked the employee (who was looking after the place) if he could buy the plushie because they did not have enough tickets to actually win it. “Yeah, *us,”* Gabi says with a wide smile, clutching *Bunny the bunny* to his chest.

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
Nico chuckles, and it’s a genuine sound that soothes something behind his ribs. “Yeah,” he breathes out, “I missed you.”

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
Nodding once, Gabi admits softly, “I missed you too.” He hesitates for a second before adding, “It was a little weird not having you pick me up.”

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
“Yeah,” Nico lets out, “not my fault you *decided* to come on time.”

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
Gabi giggles, still clutching Bunny in his hands. *“You* came late. How’s that *my* fault?”

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
Nico shakes his head, stepping closer to his *teammate*. “It’s your fault that you didn’t come after me,” he tells him, his voice casual and obviously not upset as he adds, “if you did, I’d pick you up.”

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
“Ah,” Gabi shakes his head as well, *“you* came late and *I* had to hitch a ride with the admin of Sauber socials. She kept asking me about my opinion about TikToks we could film. I hated that.”

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
*He’s so pretty. How is he so pretty?*

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
“I promise to pick you up in Japan,” Nico laughs.

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->
Gabi nods, looking away for a second. “So, *uh,* wanna watch a movie?” He bites his lower lip as he meets Nico’s eyes again, blushing lightly when he admits, “I downloaded like fifty we could watch. Even the one about Lauda and Hunt.”

<!-- bt-block:000056 -->
“Sure,” Nico says with a smile. He watches Gabi for a moment, his heartbeat speeding up a little too much.

<!-- bt-block:000057 -->
*Do I even deserve someone like this?*

<!-- bt-block:000058 -->
“But maybe not Rush, please,” he adds after staying quiet for way too long, letting out a soft chuckle.

<!-- bt-block:000059 -->
Gabi cocks his head to one side, asking, “Why not? Don’t like Lauda?”

<!-- bt-block:000060 -->
“No, on the contrary,” Nico laughs, “I like him *too* much to enjoy the film.”

<!-- bt-block:000061 -->
“And do you like *me?”* Gabi blurts out, his voice timid and quiet. “I… I mean—”

<!-- bt-block:000062 -->
“Of course I do,” Nico breathes out, not letting Gabi finish his sentence, “I thought I made that pretty obvious by now.”

<!-- bt-block:000063 -->
“Then ask me.” He hesitates for a seconds before adding, *“Please.”*

<!-- bt-block:000064 -->
Nico chuckles, shaking his head. “You won’t let it go, will you?” He watches as Gabi shakes his head. “Alright,” he says, closing his eyes as he asks, “Gabriel, do you want to—”

<!-- bt-block:000065 -->
“Yes,” Gabi blurts out. “Yes.”

<!-- bt-block:000066 -->
“You don’t know what I wanted to say,” Nico laughs. There’s less than half meter of space between them left and Nico can no longer tell himself that *Gabi’s* just confused, that *Gabi* doesn’t feel the same, that it’s a bad idea for the plethora of reasons he thought of over the weeks. He reaches for the hand Gabi isn’t holding Bunny with, wrapping his fingers around it. “I wanted to ask… do you want to date me? You know, be my boyfriend?”

<!-- bt-block:000067 -->
Gabi nods, telling Nico, “I already said yes, *old man.”*

<!-- bt-block:000068 -->
“Okay, good,” Nico tells him with a smile. “Want to watch something then?”

<!-- bt-block:000069 -->
Pulling Nico towards the bed, Gabi starts listing the movies he has on his laptop, ready to watch. “Lord of the Rings, the first three Saw movies, I think there’s Final Destination too. Oh, and I also have *The Bob’s Burgers Movie*, if you wanted to see that.”

<!-- bt-block:000070 -->
“Isn’t that a cartoon?” he asks, sitting down on the bed.

<!-- bt-block:000071 -->
“Yeah,” Gabi nods once, “a freaking good one. Especially the movie.”

<!-- bt-block:000072 -->
Nico gives him a confused look, his eyebrows low and furrowed together, before asking, “You really want to watch that?”

<!-- bt-block:000073 -->
“It’s either that or Rush,” Gabi tells him, putting on a fake pout. “You pick.”

<!-- bt-block:000074 -->
“Put on the cartoon,” Nico blurts out. “They don’t race in it, do they?”

<!-- bt-block:000075 -->
“No, they sell burgers and—”

<!-- bt-block:000076 -->
Nico doesn’t let him finish, telling him instead, “No spoilers. If they don’t race, I’m down.”

<!-- bt-block:000077 -->
“You’ll love it,” Gabi promises, turning on the movie on his laptop connected to the huge television on the wall, before he climbs into bed next to Nico, resting his head on Nico’s shoulder before breathing out the word, *“boyfriend.”*

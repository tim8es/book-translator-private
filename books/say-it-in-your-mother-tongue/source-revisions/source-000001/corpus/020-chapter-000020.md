Neither of them knows how it happened, but they are *all* having lunch together.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
Liam is shuffling sweet potato fries into his mouth like he might perish any second, with his Red Bull cap backwards on his head, as he occasionally nods to something Checo is saying. “Yeah, it’s *rad*,” he says, or more precisely *tries to say* over the handful of fries in his mouth.

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
Not really paying attention, GP sends another message to his wife, telling her how much he loves her, before settling his phone down to *finally* start eating. His steak has already gone cold a little, having arrived at the table about fifteen minutes ago.

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
Christian nudges Max’s foot under the table. “You okay?” he mouths before taking a sip of his third cup of coffee.

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
Max just nods, but he knows Christian isn’t buying it. “I’m fine,” he says with a smile, hoping no one else caught the moment. He’s *not* fine.

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
The food on his plate looks wrong, the restaurant is too full, GP is chewing too loudly, his clothes keep touching him in all the wrong places, and Checo hasn’t looked at him in over twelve minutes. Max pushes a bit of his chicken around on the plate, staring at it like it offended him, when a warm palm touches his thigh.

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
“We can order you something else,” Checo suggests in a hushed tone. He’s leaning closer to Max, so he doesn’t have to shout over all the noise of this place, “if you don’t like it, it’s okay.”

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
Shaking his head, Max cracks a soft smile. “It’s fine,” he lies, still pushing the same piece of meat around without even tasting it.

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
“*Leoncito*,” Checo breathes out, finally looking at Max properly. Their eyes meet as if by accident, and Checo cannot help but smile. “Do you want my pasta? It’s nothing special, *pero sabe bien.*”

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
Max looks down at Checo’s plate before looking back up, into his eyes. He gives him a soft nod. “Yeah.”

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
Checo’s smile widens. “Here,” he says before pushing his meal towards Max. He reaches for Max’s plate, taking his fork as well. “I hope you like.”

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
Without giving it a single thought, Max grabs Checo’s fork and starts eating — shoving the penne covered in tomato sauce into his mouth like a hungry kid. There is something endearing about the way he eats, leaning both elbows on the table. “Good,” he pushes out between bites, smiling, before shoving more pasta into his mouth.

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
Nodding, Checo puts the piece of meat Max has been pushing around into his mouth with Max’s fork. His left hand is still on Max’s thigh when he returns his attention to Liam. “And did Rich say something about the car?”

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
“Are you two dating?”

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
Checo pauses with his hand halfway to his mouth. “What?” he asks, arching his eyebrows. “No?”

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
Liam nods. “Sure looks like it,” he chuckles, finally finished with his fries. He takes a sip of his zero-sugar soda before adding, “you literally just switched plates. And forks.”

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
Checking if Max noticed what Liam said, Checo relaxes a bit when he realizes he’s too preoccupied with his pasta to care. There is something prickly about the way he asks, “So what?”

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
“Nothing,” Liam shrugs, “it’s just interesting. That’s all.”

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
Checo opens his mouth to say something, when GP jumps into the conversation. “I’d love to have your problems, *mamma mia,*” he says, rolling his eyes. “All of you have enough shit to focus on, don’t you?”

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
There is a hush of voices from a table nearby.

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
With a fork still in his mouth, Max asks, “What? Did I do something?”

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
At which, Christian cannot help but laugh.

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
Lance has been quiet since his dad asked him to come to his office.

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
He’s sitting in a too-soft chair, bouncing his right leg like a bored schoolboy, and his eyes are barely hanging open. Lance doesn’t try to pretend that he’s happy to be there, doesn’t bother putting on the same show he would for a journalist.

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
Lawrence notices immediately. He places his cigar into a glass ashtray before asking, “Are you okay?”

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
“Yeah,” Lance answers, and it is not *totally* a lie. “Just a bit tired, *papa*.”

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
“Want me to drive you to London?”

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
“No, I’m staying with Nando for a bit,” Lance smiles as he says it. He’s putting on a show and they both know it. His picture-perfect smile doesn’t crack as he adds, “Less commuting.”

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
Lawrence nods, making a little *hm* sound. “That’s smart,” he says after maybe twenty seconds of silence. “Saving money and the planet, aren’t you?”

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
Shifting in his seat, Lance knows his father is trying to *ask him something* without actually asking. “*Oui*,” he agrees, “and time, *papa*.”

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
*Ask me if we’re fucking, papa.*

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
*“And time,”* Lawrence repeats before standing up. He walks from his table towards the window, placing his right hand onto the frame. As he’s looking out, he asks, “Should I take it as… *teambuilding?”*

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
*Do other drivers also do this type of teambuilding?*

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
Lance stands up as well. “We are close, yes,” he answers, trying to sound as neutral as possible. His heart is beating too fast for his liking. “I think we’re friends.”

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
*But then again, friends don’t usually know how the other one moans when having an orgasm, do they, papa?*

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
Lawrence turns to face him again. “Good friends?”

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
*I want to marry him, papa.*

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
Nodding, Lance meets his eyes. “The best of friends,” he tells his father confidently, before adding, “I’d ask Fernando to be my child’s godfather, if I had one, *papa*.”

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
*But I’d rather have him be the father of my child, papa.*

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
Walking back to his table, Lawrence hums. “*Magnifique*,” he exhales as he claps his hands together. “It’s every team’s dream to have their drivers be *best friends*, isn’t it?”

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
*Ask me what’s really on your mind, papa. Ask me if I let Fernando Alonso touch me.*

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
“I wouldn’t know,” Lance shuffles on his feet, standing straighter than necessary, like he’s standing in front of a judge and not his father. “I’ve only ever been a proper part of this one, *papa*.”

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
Lawrence sits back down, intertwining his fingers before he lets his joint hands rest on the table. He has to look up, now, to meet his son’s eyes. “You know you can tell me anything, right?”

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
*You know I can’t, papa. You made that pretty clear.*

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
*“Oui, papa,”* Lance nods with a soft smile. “I know.”

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
“Good,” Lawrence puts the cigar back between his lips, inhaling the smoke, before he says, “I’m glad we’ve had this talk. You’re a good kid.”

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
*I’m not a child anymore, papa. When will you see that?*

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
“Me too, *papa*,” Lance breathes out, folding his arms behind him like a soldier. “Can I go? I think Gary wanted to talk about strategy with me.”

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
Lawrence smiles wide, showing off his teeth. “Ah, of course,” he waves towards the door, gesturing that he’s free to leave. When Lance reaches the door, his father clears his throat and says, with even brighter smile, “Lance? Keep *this* in mind, will you?”

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->
Lance nods, suddenly at loss for words.

<!-- bt-block:000056 -->
“Keep making me proud.”

<!-- bt-block:000057 -->
With that, the door *hisses* shut behind him, swallowing anything he might have been preparing to say in response. Lance turns left, moving fast past everyone — engineers, strategists, a man carrying a bouquet of yellow roses. Picking up the speed, he’s basically jogging towards the staircase leading to the empty office floor.

<!-- bt-block:000058 -->
*Stupid*.

<!-- bt-block:000059 -->
He takes the stairs two at a time.

<!-- bt-block:000060 -->
*Of course he knows.*

<!-- bt-block:000061 -->
Stumbling down the empty hallway, he searches for the door numbered 742. He pushes the door open, almost falling inside, before he shuts it closed with barely enough force.

<!-- bt-block:000062 -->
He’s been using this very office for a while, running to hide behind the empty desk every time his father makes him cry.

<!-- bt-block:000063 -->
Lance turns the key, locking the door, before he walks towards the table. Falling to his knees, he crawls under it, hugging his knees and hiding his face between them as the first tears start rolling down his cheeks.

<!-- bt-block:000064 -->
*It’s not fair*.

<!-- bt-block:000065 -->
When his phone gently vibrates in his pocket, he only hugs his legs tighter. His back tenses up with each quiet sob. He’s learnt how to cry silently a long time ago.

<!-- bt-block:000066 -->
*It shouldn’t be this hard.*

<!-- bt-block:000067 -->
His phone vibrates again, then twice more, but Lance doesn’t have it in himself to check. His fingers are trembling too much to even try and unlock his phone, let alone use it.

<!-- bt-block:000068 -->
*Why do I never get to be happy?*

<!-- bt-block:000069 -->
As his phone vibrates again, another noise rips through the room.

<!-- bt-block:000070 -->
A knock.

<!-- bt-block:000071 -->
Lance curls up even more into a ball.

<!-- bt-block:000072 -->
Another knock.

<!-- bt-block:000073 -->
There are more tears streaming down his face, but Lance isn’t making a single sound.

<!-- bt-block:000074 -->
*Go away.*

<!-- bt-block:000075 -->
Another knock.

<!-- bt-block:000076 -->
*GO AWAY!*

<!-- bt-block:000077 -->
Two much gentler knocks followed by even gentler voice. “*Lancito*?”

<!-- bt-block:000078 -->
*Please, go away.*

<!-- bt-block:000079 -->
He crawls out from under the desk, wiping the tears from one half of his face as he slowly walks to the door. “Yeah?” he asks, his voice hoarse.

<!-- bt-block:000080 -->
“Can I come in?”

<!-- bt-block:000081 -->
*No*.

<!-- bt-block:000082 -->
Lance reaches for the key, turning it swiftly to allow Fernando to enter. He steps away from the door, wrapping his arms around himself in a poor attempt at a self-hug.

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
Everything looks bigger from the pitlane.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
Fernando sips water from his Aston Martin branded cup, biting down hard on the flexible tubing used as a straw, while he watches the race continue without him. “Come on,” he says under his breath, his eyes pinned to the *other* dark green car on track. “Come on, Lance. Bring it home.”

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
Gary, Lance’s race engineer, offers Fernando his headset with raised eyebrows and a soft smile on his lips, without actually asking a single question.

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
He grabs it, pulls it over his head and says, breathy, “Lance, keep going. You’re doing so well.”

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
“Fernando?”

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
*“Sí,”* the Spaniard exhales, “I’m here. Hülkenberg is a second behind. Don’t let him pass.”

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
“Trying,” Lance groans back without any actual poison in his voice. How much to Albon?”

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
Fernando looks at Gary who mouths the number of meters at him. He repeats it into the microphone. “Keep pushing,” he adds, “you have a lap left.” And with that, Fernando returns the headset to Gary, moving away from the engineer while watching the race on the ever-present monitors.

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
Everything happens in slow motion, yet so fucking fast Fernando can *barely* register it.

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
Norris’ McLaren zooms through the finish line, followed closely by Max’s Red Bull. Russell and Antonelli finish within two seconds of each other, and another two seconds later, Albon brings home fifth place. And then —

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
“You’re sixth!” he shouts at Lance as the man climbs out of his car, arms already around Fernando’s neck before his feet even touch the ground properly. “You were amazing!”

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
Lance fiddles with his chin strap, though his fingers are too shaky to undo it — and so Fernando takes over for him, *unclicking* the clasp with ease and two decades of experience.

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
Once free of his carbon-fiber and Kevlar headgear, Lance laughs, throwing his head back. “I’m sixth,” he repeats, a little breathless and entirely happy.

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
“Good job, mate,” Lewis says, clapping Lance on the shoulder.

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
“Thanks,” Lance responds with a smile, offering his right hand to dap up the Brit.

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
Lewis spreads his arms instead, obviously hinting at wanting a hug, not just a dap.

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
Only barely suppressing a growl, Fernando steps away from his *teammate,* allowing *Hamilton* to give him a celebratory two-armed hug that is entirely *too much* in his opinion. Shooting daggers from his eyes, he stares at the back of *Hamilton’s* head, observing the interaction like a hawk.

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
“You were flying, man,” Lewis tells Lance before he loosens his arms, letting the Canadian move from his embrace. “Bloody brilliant.”

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
Lance chuckles, shaking his head a little as he says, “It was the car, I swear.”

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
“That was all you, mate,” Lewis objects with a wink, clapping his shoulder again. He turns to look at Fernando — who doesn’t bother to put on a fake smile for the seven-time world champion — and tells him, “Pity you had to retire the car, *hm?”*

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
Fernando gives him a blank look as he breathes out, in a heavy accent, “Pity you left Mercedes, *hm?* Given your finish.”

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
Lance barks out in laughter, fake-coughing immediately to cover it, hiding his face behind his hands.

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
“Okay,” Lewis breathes out, confused about Fernando’s *very sudden* and *very unexpected* hostility. “Touched a nerve there, I guess,” he mutters under his breath, shooting Lance a look that screams *begging for help*. With raised eyebrows, he says, “Anyways, I better get a move on. Good job.” He flicks his eyes back to Fernando as he adds, “Sorry for your DNF.”

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
Fernando furrows his eyebrows as they watch *Hamilton* walk towards George and Kimi, hugging the rookie driver in a familiar way.

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
It is then that Lance slaps Fernando’s upper arm, unable to hide his laughter anymore. “What the fuck was that?” he asks, giving his *teammate* a played up dirty look. “You gonna scare away all my friends like that?”

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
“No,” Fernando groans, relaxing his eyebrows again before he continues, “just *Hamilton.”*

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
Lance shakes his head, still laughing. “Possessive asshole,” he says under his breath, making sure it’s loud enough for Fernando to hear.

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
“Thought you knew that about me,” Fernando growls, his voice low and drawn out. He leans into Lance’s personal space again, grabbing the pull tab of his race suit and pulling down the slider of his zipper, as he says, “I don’t like people touching what’s mine. Especially not *Hamilton.”*

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
**=** **♡** **=**

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
Max sniffles, wiping his face into the long sleeve of his champagne covered overalls.

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
“First podium of the season,” Christian tells him excitedly when Max gets off the podium. He shakes Max’s hand again, for the third time already, telling him something that Max doesn’t hear over the loudness of his own heartbeat. Christian waves a hand in front of Max’s face, his voice a little panicked when he asks, “Are you okay? Max. *Max?* Hey, are you alright?”

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
Nodding, Max groans, “Yeah, *fine.* I need to go.”

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
*Not here.*

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
Not waiting for his *boss* to say anything else, Max walks past the two-dozen people dressed in Red Bull uniforms, weaving through them the same way he would zigzag between other drivers’ formulas on track. He scans the crowd, searching for Checo’s face.

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
He bites his lower lip, drawing a pain-filled groan out of his own throat.

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
*He’s not here.*

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
Max is searching for the Mexican frantically, flicking his eyes from person to person in the hopes that it’ll be Checo.

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
*“Oi,* Max,” someone with a Spanish accent calls his way.

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
Glancing over fast, Max groans when he realizes it’s not Checo — meeting Carlos’ face instead. “What?”

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
“Ain’t you just a ray of sunshine today,” Carlos rolls his eyes, but comes closer to the Dutchman, lowering his voice as he tells him, “I have a message for you.”

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
Max mutters something under his breath that sounds suspiciously close to *fuck off* before asking, “What message?”

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
Carlos smirks, looking around them conspiratorially to make sure no one is listening to their conversation. “I was supposed to tell you this,” he says, clearing his throat, *“Once off the podium, come back to me. You know where you’ll find me.”*

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
Blinking wordlessly a few times, Max licks his lips before breathing out, “Who told you that?”

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
Cocking his head to one side, Carlos purses his lips, letting the moment draw out for a second. “I think you know,” he says, wiggling his eyebrows up and down.

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
“Right,” Max nods. He keeps standing there for a brief moment, the gears in his head visibly turning, before he breathes out, *“Verdomme,”* and begins running towards one of the drivers’ exits. He pushes someone — he guesses Charles — out of the way, ushering a quick *sorry* without a single look back.

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
The hotel they’re staying in is barely three minutes away on foot, but the adrenaline in Max’s veins makes him close the distance in less than ninety seconds. He doesn’t greet the receptionist, only nods in the general direction of the front desk without making sure anyone is even standing there.

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
Running up the stairs, Max bumps into another guest, who calls out his name. Max doesn’t react to it, his mind focused on two things only: putting one foot in front of the other *and* the number of their suite.

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
He reaches the second to last floor, turning right into the short hall he remembers the room being in. There are only two suites on this side of the floor, theirs being the one numbered 711.

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
Slapping his pockets, Max realizes he left everything in the paddock — his phone, his wallet, even his room key — the only thing he brought being *himself*. He breathes out, raises his fist and knocks three times, light enough to be dismissed but loud enough to be audible.

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
Exactly four seconds go by without anything happening.

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->
Max looks around — whether to make sure no one is there or hoping someone would appear, he himself doesn’t quite know. He doesn’t really have time to figure it out, though, because the door suddenly opens.

<!-- bt-block:000056 -->
*“Hola, campeón,”* Checo tells him with wide smile that shows off his teeth. “Wanna come in?”

<!-- bt-block:000057 -->
Furrowing his eyebrows, Max breathes out a slightly confused, “Carlos said—”

<!-- bt-block:000058 -->
Checo chuckles, nodding once. *“Sí,* I asked him to,” he tells Max. Reaching for the front of Max’s overalls with his right hand, Checo grips the fabric tightly and pulls Max into their hotel suite.

<!-- bt-block:000059 -->
Max stumbles inside, staying upright thanks to his fast reflexes and the wall he leans his hand on. “What’s happening?” he asks, laughing nervously.

<!-- bt-block:000060 -->
“You got second,” Checo tells Max, running the palms of his hands over his chest. He looks up to meet Max’s eyes, licking his lips.

<!-- bt-block:000061 -->
Wordlessly, Max just nods in agreement, trying his best not to look upset with the fact that he didn’t win.

<!-- bt-block:000062 -->
Checo undoes the Velcro by Max’s neck, pulling the slider down the zipper at the front of his overalls, exposing the hot skin of his chest before he continues by saying, “I’ll make it up to you now.”

<!-- bt-block:000063 -->
“How?” Max breathes out, his voice shaky and a little gone. He’s looking into Checo’s eyes and there is hunger in them he doesn’t quite recognize, mixed with the deliciously familiar affection Checo always seems to look at him with.

<!-- bt-block:000064 -->
Checo’s hands are cool on Max’s body as the Mexican runs them over the exposed glistening skin. He slides one of his hands behind Max’s neck, pulling their faces together into a kiss so hot it could melt all the icecaps of Antarctica. “Let *me* get silver now,” he tells Max when they part again, sliding his hands down Max’s sides as he adds, “Because I wanna make you finish first tonight.”

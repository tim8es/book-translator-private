<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
The office building is quiet at this time of day.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
With most of the workers already gone, Lance walks around looking for something to do before Fernando finishes in the simulator. Yesterday, he spoke with the nice receptionist for forty minutes before Fernando came to “pick him up” and took him home. Today, however, Lance wants nothing else but his peace and quiet.

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
“*Yo*, hot stuff, come here often?”

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
Lance laughs, strolling nonchalantly towards the man. “Not often enough,” he purrs, placing a hand on Esteban’s chest, “since I missed *you* over here.”

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
“Well, better notice me late than never, *darling*,” Esteban snakes his hand around Lance’s waist, pulling him closer. “Do you believe in love at first sight, or should I walk around one more time?”

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
They both burst out laughing, hugging each other in a friendly manner before pulling apart.

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
“What are you even doing here?” Lance asks, leaning one shoulder against the wall. “Aren’t you supposed to be in France, *Frenchie*?”

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
“You know how it goes,” Esteban shrugs, shoving his hands into his pockets. “Took the family for a holiday, ended up in here.”

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
Lance rolls his eyes. “What a *lovely holiday*. Why not go somewhere sunny and *less England-y*?”

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
“We have some family nearby, so parents were all excited to go say hello and shit,” Esteban pulls out his phone to check the time. “I might have kinda lied about needing to see you. For work and all.”

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
“Ah, don’t make me part of your lies,” Lance slaps his shoulder maybe a bit too harshly. “Last time you did that your mama called me to ask about my surgery. *Before* I had any.”

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
“And I already apologized for it like seven times by now,” Esteban throws his arms up. “I need to go pick them up in thirty minutes anyways.”

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
Lance nods. “Where’s your family from? Do I know them?”

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
Shaking his head, Esteban says, “I doubt it. They live in this tiny village by Manchester.”

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
“*Manchester?* That’s like three hours away!” Lance basically shouts at his friend. “How do you plan to be there in thirty minutes?”

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
Esteban shrugs nonchalantly. “Oh, I don’t. I’ll just call them that I’m stuck in traffic.”

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
“*Stupide*,” Lance rolls his eyes again. “What a blessing it must be to have you as a son.”

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
“Your *papa* could say the same thing,” Esteban shoots back. He looks around, checking if anyone’s listening to them, before lowering his voice to say, “at least I don’t sleep with my father’s employee.”

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
Straightening up, Lance gives him a dirty look. “*Monsieur Ocon*, I have no idea what you’re talking about,” he states, but the worried look in his eyes betrays him. Without Esteban saying anything else, Lance sighs and adds, “We didn’t sleep together.”

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
“*Yet*,” Esteban clarifies, “you mean you haven’t *yet*.”

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
Lance nods with a deep sigh. “Yeah,” he admits, running a hand through his hair. “And at this rate, we never will! Every time it looks like, *you know*, like it might happen, something goes wrong.” Wrapping his arms around his own torso, Lance hugs himself tightly. “Last time, someone triggered the fucking fire alarm in my apartment building. Have you ever had firefighters *rescue you* while actively trying to get laid? Because I have!”

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
Esteban fights the need to laugh, biting his inner cheek. “I’m sorry, man,” he manages to say before cracking up. “Were you at least dressed?”

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
“Yeah,” Lance spits out, “but that’s not the point!”

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
Nodding, Esteban pretends to be serious. “Yikes,” he finally says. “That was number three or four?”

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
“*Five*. If it happens again, I’ll join a fucking monastery and become a nun.”

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
“Aren’t you Jewish?”

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
Lance nods, once. “I am also a man. But I’m so frustrated at this point that I might as well become a fucking nun.”

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
“That sucks,” Esteban claps his arm. “I better leave, though, what if it’s contagious?”

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
“Go, you *traitor*,” Lance frowns at him, cracking up after a few seconds. “Drive safely. If you die, I’ll kill you.”

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
Esteban salutes Lance with a wide smile. “Yes, sir,” he laughs, leaning closer to add, “and you better finally fuck the guy, or you’ll both be even more insufferable than you already fucking are.”

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
Lance slaps his arm again with a loud, “Esteban!”

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
And with that, Esteban leaves. He can’t even be out of the building yet when he texts Lance.

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
Max keeps trying *very hard* to act like his usual self, all jerky movements and too many self-deprecating jokes, but Checo can see right through it. He’s avoiding *something*. And whatever that *something* is, Checo needs to deal with it.

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
They are *out*, just strolling around a park close to their Airbnb, and Checo cannot help but talk. It starts innocently enough, with Max asking about a statue they encountered — *“What is that supposed to be?”* — and Checo chuckling as he gives him a university course level of monologue about the metal piece.

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
“And if you look here,” Checo points to a sharp edge near the bottom of the statue, “you can actually see the artist’s vision. He went for sharpness, which… *of course he did*. The official title is something like *‘And it is near’* but most people would know it as the piece about… *capitalism*. How it cuts us into pieces and skins us alive and steals our… sense of self. I’ve actually met the author, kind of a weird little fella. He told me about his newest piece called *capitalism 2.0*. It’s a zombie eating itself because, well, *you get it*. Zombies make us into capitalism. Wait. *No*. Capitalism makes us into zombies.”

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
Max laughs. “You got me,” he admits through chuckles. “Like I’d have actually believed you!”

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
Checo smiles back at him, filing the sight in front of him into a hidden part of his mind, under the term *Happy Max*. He nods with, “What gave it away?”

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
“No way you met this fella,” Max shakes his head. He points at a little plaque with » *Modernist’s dream*, Markus de Ville, 1905 « written on it. “I doubt Markus over here is still alive.”

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
“Ah,” Checo sighs. He claps Max on the back as he admits, “I did not notice that.”

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
“We can’t all be perfect,” Max jokes back, his voice and face relaxed in a way Checo hasn’t seen in a while.

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
Checo doesn’t stop himself from breathing out, *“Except for you.”*

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
Max goes bright red, his cheeks hot and blushing, and he coughs. He shakes his head, unable to find the words to object, but still needing to make it known that *he* isn’t perfect.

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
They start walking again, talking about everything and nothing all at once, when Checo gestures towards a small building. “I’ll just be a second,” he tells Max.

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
“Take your time,” Max nods back. “I’ll sit here,” he adds, pointing at a bench not far from the public toilets.

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
Not saying anything else, they part ways. Max sits down on the bench, instinctively pulling out his phone to distract himself from the need to run away. There is a new message waiting for him. It’s from Lando.

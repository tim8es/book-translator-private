<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
Radiohead is blasting through the motorhome, accompanied by *Gabi’s* singing.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
Nico leans back, noticing the coldness of the wall even through his shirt, and just sighs.

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
*“I don't care if it hurts, I wanna have control. I want a perfect body. I want a perfect soul,”* the Brazilian’s voice echoes through the space. With only a pair of shorts on, Gabriel walks out of the shared bathroom, the volume of his voice dropping slightly as he reaches Nico, singing with Thom Yorke, *“I want you to notice when I'm not around. You're so fuckin' special. I wish I was special.”* He breathes in, preparing to scream-sing the chorus when Nico stops him.

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
“Can you imagine making out to this song?” he asks Gabi, a little annoyed with himself for not saying what he *actually* wanted to say.

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
Gabriel laughs, stealing one of the *many* black-and-green shirts hanging over the seat of a barstool. Nico, it turns out, is quite messy during race weekends, especially when he doesn’t do *that* well in quali (it gets even worse when he DNFs the actual race, however, as Gabi had the *privilege* to find out). Dressed in a polo that’s only a slightly bigger than the one issued to him, he breathes out an amused, “What?”

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
“Isn’t that the lyrics?” Nico thinks aloud, “Something like, *we’d make out in your car to Radiohead,* no?”

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
“No idea,” Gabi shakes his head, laughing, “you sure that’s a real song?”

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
Nico shoots a blank stare his way, pulling out his phone to Google it. *“Hah,* I was right. Almost. She sings, *we’d make out in your Mustang to Radiohead,* but yeah. It’s real.”

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
“Who the fuck sings that?” Gabriel grabs Nico’s phone out of his hand, checking the search results. “Katy Perry. Okay. Didn’t know you’re a middle-aged woman.”

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
“It was really popular,” Nico groans, stealing his own phone back, “like I also know some fucking… Justing Bieber songs. Doesn’t make me a teenage girl, though.”

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
Gabi lifts his hands in faux defense gesture. “Alright, *dude,”* he laughs, adding, “whatever you need to tell yourself.” He’s still laughing as he walks to his own phone to skip the ending of *Creep* and play another song on the playlist. He hums as Bo Burnham’s *Welcome to the Internet* starts playing, lowering the volume a little.

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
*Ask him.*

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
“Are you hungry?” Nico breathes out.

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
*Not that!*

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
Shooting Nico a smile, Gabi nods. “Starving,” he admits. “I could eat the whole fridge.”

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
“Let’s go get you some food, then,” Nico smiles at him back, his palms a little sweatier than the weather asks for. He exhales deeply, watching his *boyfriend* move around the motorhome with familiarity and ease that makes Nico’s heart skip a beat from time to time.

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
*I have to ask him. I just have to.*

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
“What would you like to eat? *Do-you-want-to-stay-with-me-before-Silverstone?* Would you like pizza?” he asks, entirely too fast, his words blurring together into a *mush*.

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
Gabi stops while putting a sock on his right foot, looking up to meet Nico’s eyes. “What?”

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
Licking his lips, Nico nods and blurts out again, “What would you like for food? *Do-you-wanna-stay-with-me-before-Silverstone?* How about pizza?”

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
A beat silence passes between them. Gabi’s eyebrows furrow together, then relax, before he furrows them again, his eyes narrow and suspicious. “See now,” he breathes out, his hands still stuck mid-pulling on the sock, “what was the thing in the middle?”

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
Nico shrugs, trying to seem nonchalant. “Pizza?”

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
“I love pizza,” Gabi nods, abandoning his sock that’s only halfway on, “but that’s not what I meant. What did you say about Silverstone?”

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
“Well,” Nico shrugs again, no longer able to look directly at Gabi, instead flicking his eyes towards the ceiling, “I mean… *yeah.* We’re in Austria, I live in Germany, so it, *uh*, seems… *reasonable*. It’s closer and, you know, faster than if you went home.”

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
*Please say yes.* *Bitte sag ja. Please say yes.*

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
Gabi stands up — one foot entirely sockless, the other halfway — and steps closer to Nicolas. His voice is low when he asks, “And are you asking me as my very nice teammate, *or,”* he pauses as he reaches with his right hand to Nico’s left cheek, gently guiding him to meet his eyes again, “or are you asking as *my boyfriend?”*

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
*Both. Both, Gabi.*

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
“Which one,” Nico breathes out quietly, flicking his eyes from one of Gabi’s eyes to the other nervously, “which one would *you* prefer to be asking?”

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
Chuckling, Gabi throws his arms around Nico’s neck. “Which one will kiss me goodnight?” he asks, rubbing their noses together.

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
Nico snakes his arms around Gabi’s waist, pulling them closer together, before he breathes out, “Both of them could,” with a smile.

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
“And could one of them kiss me *now?”*

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
*Ja. Ja. Ja. Ja. Ja.*

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
Shaking his head lightly, Nico closes his eyes when he tells him, *“Nein.”* He looks back into Gabi’s eyes and adds, “we agreed we wouldn’t… *do that*… in the paddock.”

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
Gabi pouts, putting on his best *puppy eyed* expression. *“Please,”* he whines, “it’s like we’re just teammates who go on dates now.”

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
“Isn’t that what dating *is?”* Nico asks, his eyebrows high on his forehead. “I mean… We’re teammates and we go on dates, so… Isn’t it?”

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
Frowning, Gabi lets go of Nico’s neck — he remains *very* close to Nico, though — and folds his arms on his chest. “Even Oscar acts more *boyfriend-y* with Lando than we do. And they’re *not even dating!”* he groans with a played but angry expression. “You haven’t even kissed me yet.”

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
“I kissed you,” Nico protests, cocking his head to one side, “multiple times.”

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
“On the cheek!” Gabi groans, throwing his arms around as he exclaims, “that doesn’t count.”

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
Nico pokes the inside of his cheek with his tongue, thinking. He puts on his best persuasive smile as he offers, “If you come home with me, I will kiss you.”

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
Furrowing his eyebrows, Gabi nods, breathing out, “On the lips?”

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
*“Ja,* on the lips,” Nico nods once, “I promise.”

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
“Alright,” Gabi says, wiggling out of Nico’s hold. He finally finishes putting on the sock, balancing on one foot to do so, before he walks to the short hall to his bedroom.

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
“Where are you going?” Nico breathes out, confused.

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
“Packing. We’re going right now,” Gabi shoots back over his shoulder, grinning as he adds, “so you don’t change your mind.”

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
*What’s taking zo lang?*

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
He’s chewing on a piece of gum he’s found in his pocket, trying not to think about how long it had to have been there — the last he remembers buying gum, he was in the store with Penny who refused to leave without getting the pinkest package of sweets he’s ever seen; he managed to bribe her, though, with gum which seemed like the more reasonable treat to buy.

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
The gum in his mouth managed to lose *all* its flavor in less than ninety seconds, but he refuses to spit it out, still chewing on it like his life depends on keeping it in his mouth, while he’s waiting for Checo to do… whatever it is he said he needed to do.

With a sigh, Max locks his phone. He gazes out of the car’s window, his eyes taking in the shapes of the letters that make up the sign above the main entrance to the store.

<!-- bt-block:000077 -->
*He’s not going to propose to me, is he?*

<!-- bt-block:000078 -->
He bites his nails, tapping his foot nervously.

<!-- bt-block:000079 -->
*No, he’s not. That would be crazy.*

<!-- bt-block:000080 -->
Nodding, Max sighs as his body relaxes.

<!-- bt-block:000081 -->
*But what if he is?*

<!-- bt-block:000082 -->
He breathes out, excited *and* horrified equally.

<!-- bt-block:000083 -->
*I mean, we really are* *the type of people to get married on whim. But would Checo really propose to me now?*

<!-- bt-block:000084 -->
Max unlocks his phone to see if Checo didn’t text him since the last time he’s checked it. He didn’t, Max notes, before looking back outside. There is movement behind the shop’s window, but the glass is cloudy enough to obscure the person so no one could recognize them from the outside.

<!-- bt-block:000085 -->
*Nee. Of course not.*

<!-- bt-block:000086 -->
For a couple of seconds, Max just stares, his brain empty, before a *troubling* thought sneaks back into his mind.

<!-- bt-block:000087 -->
*But like… is it really that unlikely? I mean, yeah, it is, but like… We already live together anyway. And his mama did say she can’t wait for the summer holiday because the whole family will be together for more than an afternoon again.*

<!-- bt-block:000088 -->
Max bites his nails, breathing out strongly enough to fog up the car’s window. With a sigh, he shakes his head. “He won’t,” Max tells himself strongly, ready to finish the *conversation.*

<!-- bt-block:000089 -->
*What would I tell him if he really did ask me?*

<!-- bt-block:000090 -->
He licks his lips, fidgeting with the phone case again. With it off, he lets out a soft hum.

<!-- bt-block:000091 -->
*I’d say yes, of course.*

<!-- bt-block:000092 -->
Max nods again, biting his lower lip as he forces the case back onto his phone, agreeing with *himself*.

<!-- bt-block:000093 -->
*Well, unless he’d ask me in some stupid way, like that one video… what was it? Something like, “Will you let me hit it forever?” Yeah, no. I’d say no if he asked like that. I’d tell him to* rot op.

<!-- bt-block:000094 -->
Pulling the case off again, Max notices it’s much easier to do so than it used to be half an hour ago. He chuckles to himself, moving it around in his hands with a sort of pleasant rhythm.

<!-- bt-block:000095 -->
*Goed dat Checo is not like that. He looks like the type of guy to ask very traditionally, a hundred percent! He’d for sure use the phrase, “will you do me the honor,” or something really close to that.*

<!-- bt-block:000096 -->
Max lets out a sigh — not one of despair or anger or even annoyance, that is, — a *dreamy* one, similar to those you’d expect from a rom-com’s protagonist and *not* from Max *fucking* Verstappen.

<!-- bt-block:000097 -->
*He’s just… he’s just so nice and sweet. He would maybe make a joke about selling his soul to me, or something like that.*

<!-- bt-block:000098 -->
*Checo doesn’t even have to ask me for my soul. He already has it.*

<!-- bt-block:000099 -->
Nodding to himself, Max smiles, soft and genuine.

<!-- bt-block:000100 -->
*He could have anything he asks for — my soul, my life, everything I own. It’s worthless without him anyways.*

<!-- bt-block:000101 -->
Someone walks past the inside of the shop window again, pulling Max out of his thoughts. He stares at the door, furrowing his eyebrows before he relaxes them again with a smile.

<!-- bt-block:000102 -->
*If Checo asks me to marry him, I will say yes. I will say yes and I will tell him just how much I love him. And I will also—*

<!-- bt-block:000103 -->
A familiar voice interrupts Max’s thoughts with, “Sorry Max, they lost the—”

<!-- bt-block:000104 -->
“I love you,” Max blurts out, glancing at Checo with wide eyes. “Do you wanna get married?”

<!-- bt-block:000105 -->
Checo stops mid-move, his lips parted, having Max’s words push a surprised *ah* from his chest. “What?” he breathes out, shaking his head in confusion (and not disagreement). “What did—”

<!-- bt-block:000106 -->
“I love you,” Max says again, shuffling in his seat so he’s facing Checo properly. “I love you,” he repeats, louder and more confident. “I, *uh*, I’ve been thinking.”

<!-- bt-block:000107 -->
“Dangerous,” Checo chuckles, his face gentle. He reaches for Max’s left hand, wrapping both of his around it with tenderness Max has already come to expect over the months. “What… thinking about what?”

<!-- bt-block:000108 -->
*“Ik hou van je,”* Max says in Dutch, adding immediately, “I love you.” He takes a deep breath. “Like… a lot. And I don’t know why I didn’t tell you sooner.”

<!-- bt-block:000109 -->
“It’s okay,” Checo shrugs, telling Max with a big smile, “It was worth the wait.”

<!-- bt-block:000110 -->
“And I wanna have this forever,” he breathes out, his voice a little shaky as he continues, “This. You, *us.* You know?” he pauses, waiting for Checo to nod. Once he does, Max continues, “So if you wanna… *yeah*. My answer is yes. We could get married.”

<!-- bt-block:000111 -->
Checo exhales all his breath at once in a huff. “Oh,” he lets out, “did you just propose to me?”

<!-- bt-block:000112 -->
*Oh no.*

<!-- bt-block:000113 -->
“I, *uh,* I think I did, yeah,” Max admits, his words trembling slightly. “But we… we don’t… we don’t have to. It was just… just wanted to say it, you know? I mean—”

<!-- bt-block:000114 -->
*Verdomme, stop. Stop talking. Don’t ruin this even more, please, just shut up!*

<!-- bt-block:000115 -->
“Max,” Checo pulls him out of his anxious loop, “look at me.”

<!-- bt-block:000116 -->
With a single nod, Max meets Checo’s warm eyes. “Sorry.”

<!-- bt-block:000117 -->
“No, no sorry,” Checo insists, offering Max a soft smile. “But you stole my line, you know?”

<!-- bt-block:000118 -->
“Pardon?”

<!-- bt-block:000119 -->
*“Sí,”* Checo chuckles, “you stole my line. I wanted to be the one to ask you to marry me and you just beat me to it.”

<!-- bt-block:000120 -->
Blinking wordlessly at Checo, Max tilts his head confusedly to one side. “What?”

<!-- bt-block:000121 -->
“I had it planned, you know?” Checo nods once, still smiling wide. “To make it special and all.”

<!-- bt-block:000122 -->
*“Oh,”* Max breathes out, a little sad, but mostly disappointed. “I’m sorry.”

<!-- bt-block:000123 -->
“Oh, don’t be. I will still do it,” Checo winks at him, kissing the hand he’s holding between his palms. “And I’m not telling you *when* I’m going to do it,” he laughs, “so you can be *properly* surprised.”

<!-- bt-block:000124 -->
Checo leans closer, kissing Max lightly.

<!-- bt-block:000125 -->
Once their lips part, Max asks, his cheeks flushed, “So, you weren’t in there buying an engagement ring now?”

<!-- bt-block:000126 -->
Laughing, Checo pulls a small box from his pocket. “No, *baby,”* he tells him as he opens it, showing off a pair of delicate golden hoops, “I ordered *mama* earrings for birthday.”

<!-- bt-block:000127 -->
Max chuckles, leaning closer to Checo to connect their lips once again. He moves away from Checo for a breath, just long enough to ask him, “And what would *mama* like from her future son-in-law for birthday?” before he kisses him again.

<!-- bt-block:000128 -->
Unable to stop laughing into the kiss, Checo is the one to break it this time. “A wedding invitation,” he tells Max. “And maybe a necklace to go with the hoops.”

<!-- bt-block:000131 -->
Fernando looks from the front door, smile on his lips as he asks, “That’s it?”

<!-- bt-block:000132 -->
Nodding, Lance walks towards the house. “Yeah,” he says, his voice low.

<!-- bt-block:000133 -->
When he steps inside the house — located in the Swiss Alps, overlooking a small river and fields covered in small brown dots he knows to be cows — he notices that Fernando has already started opening the boxes, taking out things and setting them down onto surfaces around the living room area.

<!-- bt-block:000134 -->
Lance places the last box onto the coffee table with a sigh. He’s deep in his thoughts when he overhears Fernando laughing from one room over. “What’s so funny?” he asks, moving towards the source of laughter.

<!-- bt-block:000135 -->
In the room Fernando declared as *their office*, Fernando is standing by a bookshelf covered in more racing trophies than books, holding something in his hands. “You kept it?” Fernando asks Lance, glancing at him over his shoulder.

<!-- bt-block:000136 -->
Lance checks what the Spaniard has in his hands, stepping closer. He frowns once his eyes meet a light-colored racing glove signed in black sharpie. “We don’t have to display that,” he groans, trying to grab it from Fernando’s hand. “Give it.”

<!-- bt-block:000137 -->
“No,” Fernando shoots back, placing *his* glove down onto one of the many shelves. “Should be displayed. It’s signed.”

<!-- bt-block:000138 -->
Rolling his eyes at him, Lance smiles when he sees the glove Fernando signed for him *too many* years ago placed there, next to Fernando’s karting trophy. “I think it’s… it’s the most valuable thing I own, actually.”

<!-- bt-block:000139 -->
Fernando shakes his head, turning to face Lance properly. “I don’t think it is, *cariño,”* he breathes out, caressing Lance’s cheek with the warm palm of his right hand. “You have a polaroid selfie with Lauda that he actually autographed for you.”

<!-- bt-block:000140 -->
“Yeah,” Lance nods, “but I’m not dating Niki, am I?” He chuckles, pausing for a breath, before continuing, *“This,”* he gestures towards the glove, “is from the day I fell in love with you. Not many people have something like that.”

<!-- bt-block:000141 -->
“I don’t even have something like that,” Fernando says with a faux frown, before putting on a genuine smile. “Seems unfair.”

<!-- bt-block:000142 -->

<!-- bt-block:000143 -->
Nodding once, Fernando stands up on his toes to be able to kiss Lance — it’s more of a peck than anything else. “We still have the rest of our lives to collect memories, *princesa,”* he breathes out, gentle and so totally in love it heals something deep in Lance’s bones.

<!-- bt-block:000144 -->
“If I didn’t know any better, I’d think you’re getting all soft on me, *mon chéri,”* Lance whispers over Fernando’s lips, kissing him back.

<!-- bt-block:000145 -->
Fernando laughs, pulling away from Lance to tell him, “You’re ruining my *tough guy* reputation, *Lancito.”* Their eyes meet again in a collision of affection and warmth before he adds, “I love it.”

<!-- bt-block:000146 -->
“Glad I could help,” Lance winks at him. He exhales, his breath trembling a little as he asks, no longer able to look into Fernando’s face, “Are you sure it’s okay? All my stuff here?”

<!-- bt-block:000147 -->
Cocking his head to one side, Fernando furrows his eyebrows in confusion. “You moved in with me, *cariño.* Of course it’s okay,” he tells him, his voice low but steady. “I want your *everything* here.”

<!-- bt-block:000148 -->
Tight-lipped, Lance nods, but it’s obvious he’s still not quite sure.

<!-- bt-block:000149 -->

They move through the living room, past kitchen, past doors leading into the bathroom and a guestroom, before Fernando stops, hand on the handle of the very last door. He opens it, moving inside *their* bedroom, with Lance following him. “You see that?” he asks Lance.

<!-- bt-block:000150 -->
“What?”

<!-- bt-block:000151 -->
“This,” Fernando gestures around the room, smiling. “This is *our* bedroom. *Our* bed. *Your* clothes live in *our* closet. This is *our* house. You know that, right?”

<!-- bt-block:000152 -->
Lance bites his lower lip, nodding. “Yeah.”

<!-- bt-block:000153 -->
“Yeah,” Fernando repeats.

<!-- bt-block:000154 -->
“Okay,” Lance nods again.

<!-- bt-block:000155 -->
Fernando smiles at his *partner,* giving him a loving smile before telling him, his voice firm, “But if you ever wear shoes around the house, I will kick you out.”

Chapter 36: married and without a kidney

<!-- bt-block:000004 -->
A black car — one that Lance doesn’t recognize — stops in front of him.

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
Fernando steps out with a soft smile on his lips, dressed in a dark suit and a cream-colored shirt with the top button unbuttoned. “Hey,” he greets Lance, pulling him into an instant embrace.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
*He’s so handsome.*

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
“Why are you dressed like that?” Lance asks into Fernando’s neck, letting himself be hugged tight and hugging back even tighter. “I should go change!”

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
“No, *cariño,* don’t,” Fernando says gently as he lets go of Lance, only keeping one hand on his arm. “I like this.”

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
*This* means a pair of dark blue jeans — one that Lance wants to pretend he just *threw on* even if they are brand new from one of the super obnoxious expensive stores close to his apartment — and a white hoodie with black drawstring — the aglets of which Lance has been chewing on while waiting for Fernando to pick him up for their *date*.

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
*How did I get so lucky?*

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
Lance lowers his eyes as he looks at Fernando’s dress shoes, grimacing like they personally insulted him. “You’re wearing *loafers*. I’m wearing fucking vans. I’ll go change.”

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
With a grin, Fernando moves his hand over Lance’s arm, sliding into his palm. Their fingers intertwine almost on instinct the second they touch. “You look good,” Fernando breathes out, low and affectionate, before he takes a step back towards the car, pulling Lance’s hand as he does so. *“Vamos.* We have a place to be.”

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
Shaking his head, Lance follows Fernando to the car, pretty sure it’s borrowed from *a friend of a friend* of which Fernando seems to have hundreds, maybe thousands, all over the world. “Nice car. More the type for Norris to be driving than you, isn’t it?”

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
*What’s gotten into you?*

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
“Only the best for you, *querido,”* Fernando tells him as he opens the passenger door. “Please.”

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
*You know you don’t have to do* all this *for me, right?*

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
“If anyone told me that *Fernando Alonso,”* Lance starts, looking from his seat *up* to meet Fernando’s eyes, “considers *McLaren Senna* to be *the best*, I don’t think I’d believe that.”

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
Before the door even finishes closing, Fernando is already in the driver’s seat, seatbelt clicked in. “What would you believe? Valhalla or Valkyrie?” Fernando asks with a grin.

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
Lance shakes his head again. “No,” he admits, feeling even more out of place *inside* the car. “Maybe Peugeot 205 GTi. Or Ferrari 250 Testa Rossa,” he says, nodding along, before adding, “maybe even Alpine A110. That feels like more your style.”

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
*“Ah,”* Fernando breathes out, slowly rolling the car into an empty street, only looking at Lance out of the corner of his eye, “are you calling me old, *cariño?”*

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
“I’m calling you distinguished,” Lance protests with a grin. He lets his hands drop into his lap before he adds, “Someone who appreciates the classics.”

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
Fernando nods, smiling. “You aren’t wrong,” he says, gently holding the steering wheel with both hands as he drives them towards a sign that explains where to drive to leave London.

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
“I rarely am,” Lance laughs, looking straight ahead into the *bright* evening, illuminated by too many streetlights and signs, “yet people are always surprised.”

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
“Being confident *te sienta bien*. You should be like that more often.”

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
Lance shakes his head, looking out of the car. “Where are we going?”

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
“Can’t say, it’s *una sorpresa,”* Fernando says with a heavy Spanish accent. With his left hand, he lets go of the steering wheel, placing it on Lance’s thigh instead. “A good surprise. Promise.”

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
“Okay,” Lance nods, covering Fernando’s hand with his own. He bites his lower lip, feeling like a teenager getting taken out on a date for the very first time. Hoping the car is dark enough not to show his blush, he asks, his voice cracking on the first word, *“So*… does this car have like… Spotify or something?”

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
Fernando laughs. “I’d hope that a car worth one point five million pounds has something to play music,” he says with a snigger, gesturing to the built-in touchscreen in the middle console. “You can look.”

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
It takes Lance six minutes to figure out how to connect his phone to the car and another two to realize that he doesn’t know what playlist to play. He looks at Fernando, phone in hand, and asks, “What… what should I play?”

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
*Why am I so nervous?*

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
Fernando looks away from the new but narrow road, meeting Lance’s eyes for a brief moment. “Whatever you like, *cariño.* I know what songs you usually play.”

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
“Yeah,” Lance nods, “but what do *you* want to listen to?”

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
Licking his lips, Fernando nods, a smile pulling the corners of his mouth *up*. “You can play Madonna or Buddy Holly or… the theme song of MASH. You can play *anything*.”

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
Lance rolls his eyes. “Okay,” he basically groans, choosing the first song that came to mind.

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
As the opening chords turn into the first verse and a female voice sings, *“Can you hear the drums, Fernando?”* unable to stop himself, Lance looks at Fernando with a shit-eating grin.

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
Fernando barks out in laughter. “Didn’t expect *that* one.”

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
“It’s a classic,” Lance suggests with a chuckle, “and you said you like those.”

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
“It really is, *sí,”* Fernando nods, “but… I don’t really want some Swedish woman saying my name like that. Could you… *you know,* skip it?”

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
Lance nods, pressing *next* without thinking, without *checking* if it’s safe. His face goes bright red the second the realization dawns on him. “I’m sorry,” he blurts out as Foreigner’s *I Want to Know What Love Is* plays out from the speakers, “I’ll skip that.”

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
“No, wait,” Fernando grabs his hand, stilling it in air before he can press *next* again. “I like this one. Is good.”

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
*It’s stupid.*

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
“It’s cheesy,” Lance protests, *too* aware of the heat in his cheeks. Watching as Fernando turns left, forcing the sportscar to enter a small-town road, Lance bites his lower lip. “It’s only a little better than like… *Careless Whisper.”*

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
Fernando chuckles, finally letting go of Lance’s hand. “I like cheesy,” he shrugs, giving Lance an innocent smile. “And this one is a classic, no?”

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
“It can be stupid and a classic at the same time,” Lance rolls his eyes, but deep down — in the little place behind his ribs, right under his heart, where he hides *all* his wildest dreams and hopes — it soothes something in him. He sighs but doesn’t skip the song. He admits, barely audible, “I kinda like the song, too.”

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
“See?” Fernando breathes out, touching his thigh again, his steady fingers light on Lance’s leg. “Is a good song.”

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
Lance doesn’t look at Fernando again until the song ends. Much safe *Enjoy the Silence* by Depeche Mode starts playing when Lance asks, “Will you really not tell me where we’re going?”

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
Fernando chuckles. “No,” he shakes his head, “surprise.”

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
“I don’t like not knowing,” Lance admits, his voice quiet and honest.

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
“You trust me, *no?”* Fernando breathes out. He looks at Lance, who just nods in agreement. “I’m not taking you to a bad place. Like a dentist. Is a fun surprise.”

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
“I know,” Lance draws out the word *know* much longer than necessary, “but *still*. You’re wearing a suit, we’re in a car worth a million pounds and you don’t want to tell me where we’re going. Either you’re going to propose to me or sell me for organs. There’s no other option.”

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
Fernando barks out in laughter. “You’re funny, *cariño,”* he says, looking at Lance briefly. “I promise is not like that, okay? No organs.”

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
“And proposing?” Lance asks, his eyebrows high on his forehead. “Are you gonna propose?”

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
“No,” Fernando shakes his head. “No proposal.”

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
Lance relaxes into his seat with an amused, “Good,” looking around them into the dark. “But if I wake up married *and* without a kidney, I’ll bite you.”

<!-- bt-block:000142 -->

<!-- bt-block:000143 -->

Chapter 12: not the love of your life

<!-- bt-block:000002 -->
Chapter Notes

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
The darkness is generous.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
It is generous, and all-encompassing, and Max cannot help himself but claw onto the bedsheets in panic.

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
*Cold*.

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
His eyes shoot open and he turns to check the space next to him.

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
*Empty*.

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
He looks around the room, frantic and scared, and seeing that he can’t see *anything*, he jumps from bed with straight legs.

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
*Gone*.

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
The door into the lounge is cracked open. He pushes it to open fully, stepping into the unlit living room on shaky legs. “Checo?” he calls out, too loud to be a whisper, but too quiet to be a shout — and just desperate enough to make his own chest hurt.

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
He doesn’t get a response, not immediately that is, but he hears the unmistakable sound of a toilet flushing coming from the hallway.

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
*Oh*.

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
Shame, like a layer of cold sweat, clings to Max’s skin. He runs a hand over his neck, scrubbing the stench of embarrassment off with his nails, but it doesn’t work. Max rubs a hand over his cheeks.

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
Checo leaves the bathroom with a yawn. Before he can say anything, Max is already muttering an apology.

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
“I’m sorry, I thought… I don’t *know*. But you were gone —”

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
“Max,” Checo says trying to stop Max’s rambling, “*Maxie.*”

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
“— and I just got —”

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
“*Baby*, listen,” Checo tries again. “Don’t say sorry, okay? No bad happened, okay?” his voice is steady and calm and soft like a blanket. “Should I wake you up next time?”

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
*Baby. He called me baby. Checo called me baby.*

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
Max shakes his head. “No,” he says, but it sounds more like a question. He doesn’t meet Checo’s eyes when he speaks again, much quieter this time, “*yeah*.”

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
“*Soy* an old man, okay? I need to pee during the night,” Checo offers with a soft smile that Max cannot see, before taking Max’s hand into his own and lightly pulling them towards the bedroom. “We sleep now. Is too early for being awake.”

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
“I’m sorry,” Max says again as they lie back down.

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
Checo makes a noncommittal sound. “No sorry,” he yawns, wrapping an arm around Max.

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
They are cuddled together, properly this time, with Max wrapped in tanned arms and warmth of something more than just a friendly body next to him. One of his hands is squished between them, but the other is resting directly over Checo’s heart again.

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
*Warm.*

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
Not really thinking about it, Checo kisses the top of Max’s head. “G’dnight,” he mutters into his hair, hugging him tighter.

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
*Safe.*

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
Max lets out a shaky breath. “Night.”

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
*He called me baby.*

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
As the minutes pass, Checo’s breathing comes with an occasional quiet snore and his embrace becomes limper. Max finds himself still awake, playing the word over and over again in his head, unable to force his brain to shut up. His heart starts beating faster as the realization becomes obvious —

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
*Verdomme, am I in love with Checo?*

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
**=** **♡** **=**

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
A loud *ding-ding-dong* followed by the crunchiest piece of announcement booms through the open space.

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
Nico looks at the arrivals sign again, checking if he’s even waiting for the correct plane. He is. He *knows* he is. But that doesn’t mean he won’t check the flight information in two minutes again, for the seventeenth time in the past forty minutes.

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
*Calm down, Jesus Christus. You’re waiting for your teammate, not the love of your life.*

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
No one seems to be paying him much attention, which is both surprising and soothing at once. The woman sitting on one of the *I-am-here-to-pick-up-someone* benches yawns and lazily turns the page of her book. She hasn’t looked at anyone the whole time, focusing only on her book and her watch.

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
*How can you be so calm?*

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
Nico starts pacing — *again*. His smartwatch tells him that he managed to get all his daily steps in, but Nico just sighs and slaps the watch with his palm. Mentally, it takes him everything not to start screaming and cursing up a storm that the stupid plane still hasn’t landed yet, but he just hides it behind a typically neutral German expression and a cup of terrible automat coffee.

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
The man scrolling through his phone laughs aloud and everyone looks at him for a brief moment, disgusted by the lack of common sense and decency necessary to make such a loud noise in such a public space. Nico certainly looks at him with the intensity of a pissed off *bayerische* grandma.

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
A young couple walks past them talking in a mixture of broken German and even *broken-er* Spanish.

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
*Ah, look at us! We’re so happy!*

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
Nico finds himself cringing at the overly happy sight, sipping his coffee as if it could bring him the sanity he so desperately seeks.

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
Another terrible announcement rings out.

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
It’s the same in every airport, no matter how luxurious or brand new it might be. Mumbling, terrible speakers, someone with the worst accent ever speaking 80 words per second.

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
Nico rolls his eyes. He squishes the empty cup and throws it into a trashcan, that is otherwise filled with half-full water bottles and a few wrappers from nearby McDonalds. When he turns around to walk back to *his* spot in front of the arrivals sign, he lets out a surprised *oh*.

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
The woman is no longer reading. She’s shoving her book back into her purse, smiling at Nico like they’re lifelong friends. “Finally,” she says in a heavy accent Nico cannot place. “It’s here.”

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
“Oh, *that* was ours?” Nico asks, trying to sound impartial but failing. Watching the woman nod, he cracks a soft smile. “Finally.”

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
She also smiles. “Who’re you picking up?”

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
The question makes Nico pause. What would he describe Gabriel as to someone who doesn’t know either of them?

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
*My coworker.*

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
*Just a guy I know.*

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
*This kid that I have feelings for.*

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->
*A Brazilian Formula One driver, who is my teammate. I race in F1 for a living, by the way.*

<!-- bt-block:000056 -->
“A friend,” he decides on, no matter how much he tries to tell himself it’s a lie.

<!-- bt-block:000057 -->
“Ah, that’s *schön*,” the woman says, standing up from her seat. “My daughter is bringing home her partner for the first time. Don’t know who’s more nervous, me or them,” she laughs, and it settles something deep inside Nico’s chest. “You from around here?”

<!-- bt-block:000058 -->
“No,” Nico shakes his head, “we have to drive to Zürich today.”

<!-- bt-block:000059 -->
“Good luck,” the woman says, nodding. She looks towards the guarded exit and has to spot someone she knows based on the way she starts walking towards them with a great smile. She turns to Nico, now with about five meters between them, and shouts, “Drive safe! And tell your friend hello!”

<!-- bt-block:000060 -->
“I will,” is all Nico manages to say before the woman gets pulled into a tight hug by a twenty-something looking girl.

<!-- bt-block:000061 -->
He watches as the woman’s daughter pushes another girl towards her mum, smiling wide and gesturing like crazy. *That’s why she was nervous*. The woman just pulls her possibly-future-daughter-in-law into a hug and Nico cannot help but smile.

<!-- bt-block:000062 -->
Geneva airport is small, smaller than he usually travels from, but there is something nostalgic about it — even if Nico has never personally used it before. He keeps staring down the arrivals gate and his heartbeat speeds up, slowly becoming *too fast* for him to be waiting for *just* his friend.

<!-- bt-block:000063 -->
Nico exhales through his mouth in an attempt to calm down, but deep down, he knows it’s pointless. He shoves his hands into the pockets of his skinny jeans, suddenly hating the decision to wait *here* and not in the car. He looks over his shoulder towards the parking lot.

<!-- bt-block:000064 -->
*I could still go back. I could wait in the car. It would be—*

<!-- bt-block:000065 -->
He turns his head just in time to see a messy head of dark hair appear from behind a group of similarly dressed old guys.

<!-- bt-block:000066 -->
*Scheiße.*

<!-- bt-block:000067 -->
Gabriel is there, rolling a suitcase with the elegance of quirky love interest from an early 00s romantic movie, and Nico cannot help but watch as he walks past everyone else. He has headphones around his neck, his black zip-up hoodie is open and falling from one of his shoulders, revealing the green shirt he’s wearing. He looks messy and tired from the flight, but there is something undeniably *him* about all of it. Gabriel yawns, covering his mouth with his hand, before *finally* looking up.

<!-- bt-block:000068 -->
*A friend*.

<!-- bt-block:000069 -->
He lights up like the sun when he sees Nico. Gabriel picks up his pace, basically jogging, and Nico’s mouth goes dry.

<!-- bt-block:000070 -->
*Too late.*

<!-- bt-block:000071 -->
They are maybe three meters apart when Gabriel suddenly stops. A faint blush covers his face as he lets go of his suitcase, not looking back when it falls to the ground. He gives Nico one of his softest smiles, bright and honest and so fragile that Nico wants to wrap him up in bubble wrap to protect him from danger — and especially from *himself*.

<!-- bt-block:000072 -->
“Hi,” Gabriel breathes out and his voice is quiet, uncharacteristically so. His eyes are fixed on Nico’s face, as if he’s trying to burn all of it into memory.

<!-- bt-block:000073 -->
*Looking in your eyes, I'm coming home.*

<!-- bt-block:000074 -->
Nico exhales a breath he didn’t know he was holding before forcing himself to speak. “*Hi*,” he repeats.

<!-- bt-block:000075 -->
And it’s the only permission Gabriel needs. He launches himself at Nico, wrapping his arms around Nico’s neck in a crushing hug that forces all the air from Nico’s lungs. “I missed you,” he admits, giggling and way too happy to be seeing his almost forty years old teammate.

<!-- bt-block:000076 -->
*Just get away from me.*

<!-- bt-block:000077 -->
Nico hugs him back. “I—” His arms pull the warm body closer, into an embrace neither of them should be allowing to happen for this long.

<!-- bt-block:000078 -->
*Please, just stop touching me.*

<!-- bt-block:000079 -->
And it’s Nico who breaks the contact first. “You took your time.”

<!-- bt-block:000080 -->
He doesn’t dare to look into Gabriel’s face as he does so, afraid of what he might see in the molten chocolate of the Brazilian’s eyes. Whether he’s more scared of rejection, or being allowed *more,* he doesn’t know.

<!-- bt-block:000081 -->
Gabriel’s expression tightens for a second, but soon enough, he’s back to his usual self. He rips off his hoodie, pushes it into Nico’s hands with, “*Hold this*,” and spins around. “Look at this, *old man*!” he announces joyfully, pointing his thumbs at the white text printed on neon green fabric of his shirt.

<!-- bt-block:000082 -->
There it is, printed in big bold letters, the number 27 with one word above it. HÜLKENBERG.

<!-- bt-block:000083 -->
Gabriel laughs. “I have your merch, *old man*,” he says as he grabs his hoodie back from Nico’s hands. “Bet I got it before you,” he adds, slapping Nico’s shoulder in a friendly manner, before reaching for his suitcase.

<!-- bt-block:000084 -->
Nico’s hands itch with the need to grab Gabriel’s wrist, to force him to spin again and show him the letters on his back one more time. His breath stutters out of him, uneven and wrong, like it doesn’t belong to him anymore.

<!-- bt-block:000085 -->
*He’s wearing my name.*

<!-- bt-block:000086 -->
Not stopping himself in time, Nico reaches out with shaky fingers. He *almost* says it, *almost* begs Gabriel to let him see again, let him touch and make sure he’s not imagining it. But he doesn’t, ripping his gaze off Gabriel. “Let’s go,” Nico forces himself to say instead, already moving towards the exit, already leaving the idea of Gabriel ever being *his* behind.

He wakes up on the couch again.

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
Yawning, Max sits up, ultimately making the blanket slide onto the floor. He rubs his eyes until all that remains is just blackness and stars, yawning once again.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
*What time is it?*

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
He pats the space where his head was lying, searching for his phone. When he does, the screen shows 11.49 in bold white lettering. Max groans, throwing the phone back onto the sofa.

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
His back cracks as he stands up, exhaustion and pain clinging to his every step as he toddles into the tiny kitchen in his *temporary* apartment. Opening the fridge out of habit, he looks inside while scratching his naked torso. Nothing. When did he even eat last time?

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
An annoying *ding* from the living room informs him that a new message has arrived.

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
Blinking the tired out of his eyes, he grabs a can of Red Bull and shoots it back like a shot of tequila, grimacing as the carbonated drink travels down his throat. He crushes the empty can with his hand, not bothering to throw it out, just tosses it onto the counter with a grunt.

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
Another *ding*.

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
“Fuck *off*,” Max groans. Looking around, he searches for anything to busy himself with, but the notification eats at his brain until he sighs and returns for his phone. “Whatcha fucking want from me?”

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->

Six minutes and seventeen seconds later, a soft knock on the door echoes through the bare-walled apartment.

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
“You have keys,” Max groans, loud enough for the other person to hear. He grimaces at how pained he sounds. *Pussy*, he curses himself, closing his eyes.

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
A rustle of keys in the keyhole, a *click*, a whine of hinges. *Thud*. A shoe hitting the floor, then another. A sigh followed by the softest, “*Hey*.”

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
He still doesn’t trust himself to open his eyes, so he opens his mouth instead. “I’m fine,” he says, even if it sounds like an obvious lie.

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
“I know,” Checo breathes out, suddenly much closer than before.

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
Max’s head is hanging low, his forearms resting on his thighs, his fingers intertwined — for uninformed eyes, he’d look like he’s deep in prayer.

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
Checo knows better, though. He steps even closer, the bag with food completely forgotten. With steady hands, and unsteady heartbeat, he touches Max’s face. It’s gentle, familiar, utterly innocent.

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
Max leans into the warmth of calloused hands maybe a little too eagerly for his own liking, but he doesn’t care. It feels good. It tingles and tickles and embarrassingly makes him tear up like he’s a little kid.

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
A second hand touches his face, but doesn’t stay there. It travels *up*, past his temple and ear, into his unkept hair. It tangles itself there, pulling a little before moving to pet his hair. “You don’t have to say anything,” Checo says, his voice quiet but firm. “I get it, okay?”

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
A non-committal *hm* escapes Max’s lips. He pushes his head forward, into Checo’s space, leaning into his hands, into the warmth. It’s comforting — enough, or maybe *too much* — allowing Max’s eyes to let the tears spill onto his cheeks.

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
At least five minutes pass before either of them makes a noise.

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
“Do you want me to say things?” Checo asks, still caressing Max — his face, his hair, *his soul*.

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
“No,” Max groans, shaking his head the tiniest amount. He exhales through his nose, focusing on the feeling to will the new wave of tears away. “*Ja*,” he sounds hoarse, choking back a sniffle.

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
Checo runs his hand through Max’s hair, combing it, as he breathes out, “You’re so brave.” His voice is soft, vibrating low in his throat.

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
With a barely concealed whine, Max shakes his head in disagreement.

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
“You are so brave,” Checo repeats, even more gentle than before. “And I am so very proud of you.”

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
The body under his hands trembles; a choked back sob forces Max’s back muscles to flex and relax in an unsteady rhythm. Checo doesn’t stop petting Max, softly rubbing at his cheek in small circles, combing his dark hair that’s usually kept much shorter than it is now. His hands are warm and familiar, holding the pieces of Max’s soul together without being asked to.

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
As another sob ripples from Max’s lips, Checo falls to his knees. With both hands on Max’s face now, holding his head as he caresses the pink, wet cheeks with his thumbs, Checo says, “I’ve never been so proud to be someone’s teammate.” He smiles up at Max. “And I’ve never been happier to be someone’s *friend*.”

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
Max lifts his gaze, looking at Checo through his wet eyelashes. His lower lip is trembling. The muscles in his jaw tense as he shakes his head. “No,” he mutters, his voice hoarse. “*Weak*.”

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
“*No digas tonterías*,” Checo’s tone carries no real poison, though it doesn’t allow any protests. “Stop it. You’re not weak.”

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
Max shakes his head again, the tears still rolling down his cheeks. He inhales sharply — the way kids do when crying makes it hard for them to speak. “Why,” he breathes out, shakily, “am I *never* enough?”

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
The raw honesty punches the air from Checo’s lungs. His hands go still, his lips part, his Adam’s apple bobs as he swallows. The words hang in the air between them, dangerous and prickly, snaking their way around Max’s throat and into Checo’s heart.

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->
Max’s gaze jumps from one of Checo’s eyes to the other, searching for an answer with blurry vision and shaky breath. “*I’m sorry*,” he whispers, lowering his gaze to the floor between their feet.

<!-- bt-block:000056 -->
“But you are,” Checo finally says as he returns his hands on Max’s cheeks. He’s holding the Dutchman in place, forcing him to look up again. The tips of his fingers are suddenly cold on Max’s skin, as if all the blood from them traveled elsewhere — right into Checo’s reddening cheeks. “You are enough. *For me*.”

<!-- bt-block:000057 -->
Time goes still around them, their breaths mingling into one.

<!-- bt-block:000058 -->
It’s Checo who breaks the silence first. “*Eres todo para mí*.”

<!-- bt-block:000059 -->
Six months into their first years as teammates, Checo made a comment about Max behind his back, in Spanish — confident his Dutch teammate wouldn’t understand that he just called him a dumbass (*tonto del culo*). To his surprise, Max actually understood Spanish *to a certain level*.

<!-- bt-block:000060 -->
Right now, though, Max’s eyebrows knit together in confusion. “I am… *all*… for you?” he asks, voice hoarser and quieter than before.

<!-- bt-block:000061 -->
Checo can see the gears in Max’s mind work, finally clicking together.

<!-- bt-block:000062 -->
“*Oh*.”

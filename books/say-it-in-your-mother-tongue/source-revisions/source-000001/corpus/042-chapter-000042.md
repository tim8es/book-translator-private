<!-- bt-block:000031 -->

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
Nico doesn’t even have time to think of a response before Gabriel tells her, in a cheerful, heavily accented voice, “Oh, it was lovely. Me and my husband here both agree that we *have to* come back here someday. We loved it here *a ton!”*

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
Her eyes widen, becoming huge, but she doesn’t get to say anything because Gabriel is already walking away, mischievous smile on his lips and wheeled suitcase pulled effortlessly behind him.

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
Nico shoots the receptionist an apologetic look, but he doesn’t even try to explain *the truth* to her, following behind the *boy* with his own suitcase in hand and both of their backpacks slung over shoulders. “Why’d you say that?” Nico laughs as they walk towards his car. “She looked like she’s about to have a heart attack.”

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->

“What?”

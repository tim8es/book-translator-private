<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
“May I come in?” Fernando asks gently, his voice quiet, like he already knows.

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
*Please, don’t ask.*

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
Fernando leaves space between them, his arms hanging down his body as if defeated by the sight in front of him. “Can I touch you?” he breathes out, his voice trembling with something too heavy for it to carry.

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
*Yes, please.*

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
Lance looks at his shoes, the worn-out sneakers he’s had for ages that he refuses to get rid of because they mean too much to him, as he nods without making a sound.

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
After a beat, Fernando steps closer. He’s not moving fast, slowly — so slowly that it’s barely considered movement at all — he raises one hand to touch Lance’s face. He cups his cheek, rubbing soothing circles with his thumb under Lance’s eye. “I will hug you. Is that okay?”

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
But Lance doesn’t answer.

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
He lets his body fall towards Fernando’s chest, hiding his face there. He doesn’t bother muffling his cries, inhaling sharply as a sob rips through his throat.

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
Fernando wraps his arms around Lance, pulling him even closer, like that could protect him. Like that could undo *everything*. “*Ahora te tengo, Lancito,*” he whispers into Lance’s hair, running hands over his back. He’s smoothing out the fabric of his shirt as he does so, but the wrinkles on his soul remain, *still*.

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
Lance grips Fernando’s shirt tightly — so tightly in fact that he might have already ripped it — but neither of them seems to care or *even* *notice*. With a painful sob, he pushes his face deeper into the warmth of Fernando’s body, hiding his face there. His mouth is open, his nose scrunched up, his tears streaming down his cheeks into the fabric. Another painful groan, but it’s no longer *just* a sob. This one… is a barely whispered, “*Why?*”

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
Without meaning to, Fernando sighs. His voice is hoarse when he mutters, “I wish I knew, *Lancito*. I wish I knew.”

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
They stand like that, with Lance bent and hidden in Fernando’s embrace, for a long time. Long after Lance’s throat goes dry, and Fernando’s palms start feeling raw from rubbing the shirt. There are tears in the corners of Fernando’s eyes that he cannot wipe away, but that doesn’t matter. Nothing matters. Only Lance. Only the crying boy in his arms and the bruise on his heart that Fernando doesn’t know how to heal.

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->

<!-- bt-block:000056 -->

<!-- bt-block:000057 -->

<!-- bt-block:000059 -->

<!-- bt-block:000060 -->

<!-- bt-block:000061 -->

<!-- bt-block:000098 -->

<!-- bt-block:000099 -->
*If you kissed me right now, I’d not protest. Do you know that?*

<!-- bt-block:000100 -->

<!-- bt-block:000101 -->

<!-- bt-block:000102 -->
Max licks his lips.

<!-- bt-block:000103 -->
His eyes travel from Checo’s — *down,* towards his lips — and he swallows. There’s barely a handspan of space between them, their breaths already mingling together. All it’d take is *just* moving his head, *just* surging up, *just* one decision to move to crash their lips together. All it’d take—

<!-- bt-block:000104 -->
*Please*.

<!-- bt-block:000105 -->

<!-- bt-block:000106 -->
*Wait.*

<!-- bt-block:000107 -->
His breathing is fast, unsteady and shaky, when he sits back on his heels, letting go of Max — of the body *under* him, so warm and willing and entirely *not his to touch*.

<!-- bt-block:000108 -->
*Oh.*

<!-- bt-block:000109 -->
Max nods to himself, sitting up on the bed with something too close to disappointment obvious on his face. But he doesn’t say anything. Not when he stands up. Not when he picks up the sleep shorts from his suitcase. Not when he returns to the bathroom. Not when he sighs, no longer confused about his feelings.

Chapter 29: come back when you know

<!-- bt-block:000002 -->
Chapter Notes

<!-- bt-block:000003 -->
**This is a Multi-POV ensemble story — chapters may focus on one pairing or move between multiple.**

<!-- bt-block:000004 -->
See the end of the chapter for <!-- bt-inline:000001:start -->more notes<!-- bt-inline:000001:end -->

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
The Audi *zooms* past a sign with the number 120 on it.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
Nico stares straight ahead, his eyes locked on the motorway with deadly precision, gripping the steering wheel like it’s its fault that Gabriel left. He finds himself reaching for the gearshift *again*, even though this RSQ8 is his third *fully* automatic car, resting his hand on the gear selector instead.

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
He’s speeding, he knows that he is, but there is not a single cell in his body that *cares* right now.

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
The number on the speedometer keeps climbing — over 140, over 150, *over 160*.

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
Nico doesn’t slow down.

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
He’s *zigzagging* between cars, dodging them like he’s driving on a circuit, and not a busy road. The little tablet that came with the car shows that only about ten kilometers remain until he reaches his destination.

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
The car speeds up *again*, this time reaching 172 kilometers per hour.

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
Nico grips the steering wheel tighter.

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
The sign above the road tells him he needs to *get off* the motorway on the next exit.

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
In complete silence, Nico turns sharp right, only leaving his blinker on for one *blink*. And then he’s *off*. The road is narrow now, curving to the right before its two halves merge and it’s forced to curve sharply to the left.

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
With a groan coming from somewhere deep in his chest, Nico slows down. Still breaking the law, he lowers the speed *just enough* not to endanger other drivers as he takes the calm, out-of-town roads leading to Sebastian’s farmhouse.

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
Out of the corner of his eye, he can see the pastries moving on the passenger seat.

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
*I’m coming for you*.

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
He doesn’t have to check the navigation to know less than a kilometer is left, the house itself already visible on the horizon. The dirt road leading to the property is winding up, and Nico takes it as slow as possible.

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
The second the car stops, Nico is jumping out, the pastries back in his hand.

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
He doesn’t expect Sebastian to be already on his way to, *for sure*, give him an earful, no matter *how deserved* it might be. “How the fuck are you here already?” Sebastian barks at him, stage-whisper like.

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
Nico just shrugs. “Took the car,” he states plainly.

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
Sebastian gives him a measured look. “It’s over forty kilometers,” he says like they both need the reminder. “And as far as I know, you didn’t come in a fucking helicopter.”

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
Avoiding looking into his eyes, Nico shrugs again with a nonchalant, “I’m German. We’re extremely efficient.”

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
“Nicolas,” exasperated, Sebastian exhales, “you’d have to go like… *really fast.* Like we’re talking *gunning it* at 160 the whole time to get here.” His voice is firm and even, like he already knows the answer, when he continues, “I refuse to believe that anyone would be stupid enough to do that on *Schweizer* *Autobahnen*, not even *you*.”

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
“What do you want me say?” Nico offers casually, even if there ain’t a casual bone in his body. “I’m already *hier*, aren’t I? Too late to go slow now.”

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
Sebastian shakes his head. “You’re not gonna smile like this when you get a nasty fine in the mail,” he groans, rolling his eyes for a good measure. He clears his throat. “What did I do wrong to deserve friends like this?” he asks towards the gray sky, not expecting an answer.

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
“Being a menace in F1,” Nico answers anyways with a cheeky smile. When Sebastian turns to face him again, he doesn’t let him get a single word out before asking, “Where is he?”

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
“In the stable,” Sebastain nods towards a new looking building not far from them. “I didn’t tell him you were coming.”

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
“Good,” Nico says with a nod. “Wouldn’t want you to.”

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
He starts walking towards the stable, his steps steadier in his running shoes than the heartbeat in his chest. Almost halfway there, he looks over his shoulder, noticing that Sebastian isn’t following him. “What?”

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
“Nothing,” Sebastian shakes his head, tight-lipped. “Just… if you start shouting at each other, take it outside. *Wind of Pressure* doesn’t like it.”

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
“Who?”

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
“The horse,” Sebastian clarifies like it’s stupid that Nico even feels the need to ask. “She doesn’t like it.”

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
Nico nods. “Okay, sure. Whatever you say.”

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
*Well, let’s hope he doesn’t start screaming at me.*

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
Inhaling deeply, Nico can smell freshly cut grass mixed with something *homely*, a scent long forgotten that only the deepest part of his soul still seems to remember. He places a hand on the wooden doorframe, breathing out.

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
*Gott gibt mir Kraft, here I go.*

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
As Nico steps inside the stable, his resolve *dissolving*, he forces a quiet, *“Hey,”* to come out.

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
He watches as Gabriel turns to look at him — wide eyes, parted lips, *confusion* written across his face. His hand, with which he’s been petting *Wind of Pressure*, stills in the air mid-movement. “Nico?” he breathes out shakily.

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
Nico just nods. “Yeah,” he says, stepping closer into the quiet place Gabriel seems to share with the horse. Unsure what *more* to add, Nico repeats, even softer now, *“Hey.”*

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
“What are you doing here?” Gabriel asks, his voice tight in his throat.

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
She immediately picks up on his uneasiness, neighing loudly.

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
Gabriel pets her between the ears. *“Easy, girl,”* he whispers towards the horse, letting his hand linger in a spot she seems to like. “It’s okay, *shh.”*

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
“You’re good at this,” Nico comments, almost absent-mindedly. He moves closer to Gabriel again, since the horse doesn’t glare at him anymore. “Do you like horses?”

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
“I like *her,”* Gabriel tells him plainly. “She’s not rude to me.”

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
Nico licks his lips, nodding. “Yeah, why would she be?” he exhales, immediately annoyed with himself for doing that. “I mean… *you’re nice.* Why would anyone be rude to you?”

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
Gabriel huffs with a humorless expression. “I don’t know, Nicolas,” he says before meeting Nico’s gaze — his eyes are full of something that is entirely too close to pain for Nico’s liking, “why would anyone be rude to me?”

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
*Point taken.*

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
“I, *uh,* I wanted to,” Nico starts, but his voice gets stuck in his throat, unsure how to continue. “*um,* how're you doing?”

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
*Wind of Pressure* neighs again.

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
With a soft, almost apologetic smile, Gabriel pets her head again. “I know, *menina*. Is okay,” he tells her, caressing her mane. Looking over his shoulder, he gives Nico a blank stare. “You really come all this way to ask that?”

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
*I came all this way to apologize, Gabriel.*

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
Nico doesn’t answer, only tilts his head with a blank expression. He knows he has never looked more German.

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
*“Eu não entendo você,”* Gabriel admits before returning his gaze to *Wind of Pressure*. “Why are you here?”

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->
*Because I need you to come back with me.*

<!-- bt-block:000056 -->
“I want,” Nico starts, his voice uncertain as he looks at the horse. She’s giving him a dirty look, Nico’s sure of it, even if he doesn’t understand horses at all. “I mean, *I wanted to*… it’s *just* that— I got you these,” he stutters out and holds out his hand that is still gripping the plastic bag with pastries he’s bought what feels like a lifetime ago.

<!-- bt-block:000057 -->
Slowly, Gabriel moves away from *Wind of Pressure*, trying not to spook her out with any sudden motion. He backs out of her pen, closing a wooden gate behind himself with a soft *clunk* as the lock clicks into place. Gabriel turns to look at Nico, *finally*, looking him up and down, before his eyes shift to the bag. “What is that?”

<!-- bt-block:000058 -->
“Pains un chocolat. And some donuts,” Nico tries to say as casually as possible, failing miserably. His voice cracks on the word *chocolat* like he’s thirteen and not slowly pushing forty. “For breakfast.”

<!-- bt-block:000059 -->
With his eyebrows high on his forehead, Gabriel exhales a confused, *“Você o quê?”* He steps closer to Nico, eyeing the bag — and the man himself — suspiciously. “Why?”

<!-- bt-block:000060 -->
*I don’t know*.

<!-- bt-block:000061 -->
“For breakfast,” Nico repeats. “I thought… you probably didn’t eat so, *uh*, it felt… *appropriate*.”

<!-- bt-block:000062 -->
Gabriel laughs. It’s ironic and sharp and it clings to Nico’s skin like a layer of sweat. *“Appropriate,”* Gabriel repeats after him, shaking his head. “Donuts felt appropriate.”

<!-- bt-block:000063 -->
*“Ja,”* Nico nods. “They felt like a good choice. In the store.”

<!-- bt-block:000064 -->
“Okay,” Gabriel says, moving *even closer* to Nico, leaving only an armlength of space between them. He looks straight into Nico’s eyes, determined and a little defiant, before stating, with a deadpan expression, “I already *took* breakfast with Seb.”

<!-- bt-block:000065 -->
Nico nods, thin-lipped. “I… *figured*,” he breathes out. “It’s just… I mean… *yeah*. But like, you should still take it.” He lifts his hand, *straight-up* offering the bag to Gabriel.

<!-- bt-block:000066 -->
Looking at the bad, then at Nico’s face again, Gabriel shakes his head. “You don’t get to fix it with food.”

<!-- bt-block:000067 -->
“I know.”

<!-- bt-block:000068 -->
*Wind of Pressure* grunts, stomping with her left front leg.

<!-- bt-block:000069 -->
Gabriel glances at her with a smile. “*Ssh,*” he breathes out towards her. The second he looks back at Nico, his smile is gone. “Is not fair,” he says, throwing his arms up. “I thought… maybe would be different after the night. Maybe would like me more. But you just… would be better if you just tell me you don’t like me. Would hurt less, the truth.”

<!-- bt-block:000070 -->
*I* do *like you, Gabriel. I really do.*

<!-- bt-block:000071 -->
“The truth,” Nico exhales, screwing his eyes shut. “The truth is that I do like you, Gabriel,” he admits, his voice shaking only a little less than his hands. “And I, *uh*, I fucked up. I was, *um*… I was really rude to you and… and I’m sorry.”

<!-- bt-block:000072 -->
Gabriel shakes his head in disbelief. “Yeah, *well*, I don’t… allow that. *No*, how you say? *Accept*. I don’t accept,” he says with a thick accent.

<!-- bt-block:000073 -->
“What?” Nico breathes out, eyebrows knitted together.

<!-- bt-block:000074 -->
*“Sim,”* Gabriel nods, folding his arms on his chest. “I don’t accept,” he repeats calmly. “You don’t get to fix everything with one sorry and… and a pink donut! Small words, small gestures, small… *everything.”*

<!-- bt-block:000075 -->
“I know,” Nico says again, closing his eyes for a second too long to *just* be a blink. “Don’t you think I know that?”

<!-- bt-block:000076 -->
Gabriel licks his lips, no longer looking directly at Nicolas. “Say, then,” he requests, his breathing shallow and entirely too fast, “what do you want from me? What do I say?”

<!-- bt-block:000077 -->
*Say that you accept. Tell me it’s okay and that you love me and want to go back with me. Tell me I didn’t fuck up too badly.*

<!-- bt-block:000078 -->
Nico says none of that. Instead, he breathes out, “I don’t know.”

<!-- bt-block:000079 -->
Gabriel nods once. “When you know,” he tells the German, his voice even and slightly cold, “come back. Before then?” he pauses for a second. His eyes are teary when he looks back at Nico, his words full of nothing but pain, “Goodbye, Nicolas.”

<!-- bt-block:000080 -->
Swallowing loudly, Nico nods. *“Na dann,”* he breathes out, unsure what to do with his hands. He steps from Gabriel, moving blindly backwards from the boy. “Goodbye then, *Gabriel*,” he manages, unable to offer a single argument against that. He turns on his heel and walks towards the exit. He’s almost out when he stops, looking over his shoulder again. Nico gives Gabriel a painful thin-lipped smile before he hangs the bag with pastries on a nail sticking from the wall.

<!-- bt-block:000081 -->
*Say something. Say something. Say something. Say—*

<!-- bt-block:000082 -->
“How long will you wait?”

<!-- bt-block:000083 -->
He doesn’t see Gabriel shrug. “For you?” he exhales, taken aback slightly. Gabriel shakes his head when he admits the painful truth. *“Talvez para sempre.”*

<!-- bt-block:000084 -->
Nico nods, as if he understood, before he walks out of the stable, *Wind of Pressure* neighing once more behind him. He doesn’t stop when Sebastian looks at him with questioning expression, he doesn’t stop when the first tear rolls from his eye, he doesn’t stop until he’s by his car, unlocking it. He climbs inside without saying a word, starting the engine before he even puts the seatbelt on, reversing *just* enough to be able to turn without hitting anything. He doesn’t look at Sebastian again.

<!-- bt-block:000085 -->
The Audi speeds down the dirt-road, gravel crunching under the tires, as the man turns on the radio. Switching it to memory card, he puts on a playlist he’d never admit to like out loud. As the first song eases in, a soft rock classic *Everybody Hurts* by REM, his tears *kick in* as if on instinct.

<!-- bt-block:000086 -->
And Nico doesn’t know that it means *maybe forever*.

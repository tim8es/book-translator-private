Chapter 31: a blow of mercy

<!-- bt-block:000002 -->
Chapter Notes

<!-- bt-block:000003 -->
**This is a Multi-POV ensemble story — chapters may focus on one pairing or move between multiple.**

<!-- bt-block:000004 -->
His night was cold, long and entirely sleepless.

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
Nico gets up from *their* bed for the seventeenth time in the last hour, checking the time on his watch. He pulls on a black sweater as the numbers on the watch go from 6.22 to 6.23 with a groan. He takes out another cigarette, no longer lying to himself about it being his last, and lights it on fire before stepping onto the balcony with only his socks on. It disappears into his lungs too fast for his liking, forcing him to light another one to deal with the weight of *everything*.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
Without actually meaning to do so, he takes out his phone, unlocking it with practiced fingers. He opens his messages.

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
The last message that arrived was from Sebastian.

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
**Seb Vettel**<!-- bt-media:000001 -->Gabriel asked me for a ride to the factory tomorrow. We’ll be there at 8.

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
Nico didn’t bother responding to it, or even opening it, not because he doesn’t have anything to respond, but because he has *too much* to say and not enough will to actually do so. With a sigh, he shoves the phone back into his pocket.

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
With cold fingers — that he swears aren’t trembling — he takes out another cigarette, lighting it up and taking a drag. He can feel a single tear escape his eyes as he exhales.

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
The cigarette might not be his last, but this season… it possibly is.

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
**=** **♡** **=**

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
His coffee has gone cold over twenty minutes ago.

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
Fernando yawns, taking a sip of the disgusting brown-ish liquid everyone around the factory pretends is coffee, not hiding his grimace. As he sets the cup down, he looks at Lawrence again.

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
He’s sitting at his table, looking at his huge screen, clicking at something every few seconds. His gray suit is tidy and well-fitting, but it still doesn’t help him much — there is something *messy* and strange about the way he behaves. He inhales sharply, as if in surprise with whatever is on his screen.

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
“Something wrong?” Fernando asks casually, forcing his lips into a nonchalant smile.

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
“No, nothing,” Lawrence responds too fast, like he’s trying to convince himself as well as Fernando. “Not on my end. And you?”

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
Fernando nods, still putting on a show of a happy employee who could not possibly be in love with his boss’ son. “Everything’s great,” he claims with a smile.

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
“Good,” Lawrence breathes out with a nod.

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
“Good,” Fernando repeats in the same carefully rehearsed tone of voice as before.

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
There is stillness between them, something heavy and rigid and entirely too personal that has nothing to do with racing. Neither of them *says* it, but both can feel it — this *thing* wrapping itself around them like a noose.

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
Lawrence breaks the quiet first. “You and… *my son,”* he says, his voice tight, “I’ve heard… you’ve grown closer, *oui?”*

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
With a smile that’s anything but genuine, Fernando nods. *“Sí,”* he admits, “we’ve started sharing rides to the factory more.”

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
*“Hm,”* Lawrence breathes out, nodding without looking directly at Fernando, “he has told me you two… *share* a space. *Your* space, to be exact.”

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
Fernando takes another sip of his coffee, taking his time to answer. “That is true, *sí,”* he says, his smile still in place. He speaks his next words carefully, almost like he’s prepared this answer a long time ago, “It’s just easier this way. Lance has someone to drive him, and I don’t have to bother doing my own laundry.”

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
“Is that so?” Lawrence asks, still not looking at Fernando.

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
*“Sí.”*

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
For the first time since Fernando sat down, Lawrence decides to actually spare him a look. “He could have asked for a company car.”

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
“He could have, yes.”

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
Lawrence nods, folding his arms on the table. He stares at Fernando with an unreadable expression. “And your laundry… you could just get it cleaned. Professionally.”

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
Fernando meets Lawrence’s eyes, holding the eye contact as he asks, “and are you telling me this as my boss or as… *his father?”*

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
“Which one would you prefer to be telling you this, Fernando?”

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
“Neither,” the Spaniard admits with an uncomfortable chuckle, “but if I had to choose… probably my boss.”

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
Lawrence nods again. “Then I’m telling you as his father.”

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
Inhaling sharply, Fernando taps his fingers on the table. “Well then, *Mr. Strulovitch,”* he starts, rolling the name on his tongue like he’s weighing it — or his options. “Let me respond as your son’s *friend*.”

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
Exhaling an amused huff, Lawrence raises his eyebrows in question as he asks, “Is that what you are?”

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
Before he gets a chance to respond, Fernando’s phone *dings* loudly as a new message arrives.

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
“Deal with it,” Lawrence nods towards the phone laying face down on the table, *“we* can continue our discussion later.”

“Tell my son to focus on his simulator work instead of *bothering* my other driver,” Lawrence’s voice cuts through the quiet of the room.

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
Fernando whips his head to look over at him. With his phone already locked — out of habit, he’d claim, even if they both know that’s not why — he lets out a sigh. “It’s Chris. We were talking *racing*, actually.”

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
Lawrence measures him with his eyes, giving him a once over. “Chris got you smiling like that?” he asks with one eyebrow raised. “You mean *cold fish* Chris Cronin? Are you sure we’re both talking about the same man?”

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->
The phone on the table *dings* again, informing both of them that a new message has arrived.

<!-- bt-block:000056 -->
“Oh, but he can be funny,” Fernando nods, unconvincingly, putting on a performance of nonchalance before adding, “when he wants to.”

<!-- bt-block:000057 -->
Fernando watches as Lawrence pushes himself away from his table, standing up in one swift motion. Seemingly without even realizing, he smooths out his suit jacket and buttons it again. He moves around the table in three steady steps, leaning one hand on the desktop casually. Every gesture of his sends out a clear message.

<!-- bt-block:000058 -->
*Lawrence Stroll is a businessman, first and foremost, before he’s anything else.*

<!-- bt-block:000059 -->
“I think you’re reading too into it,” Fernando states plainly before looking away. He licks his lips, not because he’s nervous, but to wipe a smile off them.

<!-- bt-block:000060 -->
Another *ding*.

<!-- bt-block:000061 -->
Noncommittally, Lawrence hums. “Do you?” he breathes out as he takes another step closer to Fernando, forcing the openness of his giant office to close up around them slightly.

<!-- bt-block:000062 -->
Fernando nods. He’s still sitting, playing his role as the definition of indifference and lackadaisicalness perfectly, even if he wasn’t handed the script for this *show*. He bites out a simple, unshakable, *“Yes.”* And with that, he stands up as well.

<!-- bt-block:000063 -->
“I think you’re… not reading into it enough,” Lawrence delivers his words like a blow of mercy — *coup de grâce* — though the sharpness of his voice is anything but merciful. He stares down at the Spanish driver, scanning his face it might reveal all the universe’s secrets — and if not that, then at least Fernando’s.

<!-- bt-block:000064 -->
With another *ding*, Fernando breaks their eye contact to look at his phone. He doesn’t unlock it to check who’s messaging him, only shoves the device into his pocket to quiet it. “I’m better at math than reading,” he says without amusement. “Always have been.”

<!-- bt-block:000065 -->
“Even before your retirement?” Lawrence asks in a tone of voice that *could* be considered gentle if it did not belong to *him*.

<!-- bt-block:000066 -->
A soft, honest chuckle escapes Fernando’s lips. *“Sí*, even before then,” he breathes out, calmer than ever. “Even in Ferrari.”

<!-- bt-block:000067 -->
Lawrence nods. “You are a smart man, Fernando,” he states, almost like it’s meant to be a compliment, before he turns away from him, carefully sliding his hands into the pockets of his suit jacket. He gazes out of the window, not actually seeing what’s happening behind the glass, only staring at the reflection of Fernando. Lawrence sighs, lowering his head as he turns back to look at the Spaniard. His face is drawn into something that’s *sort of* close to concern, but his eyes… they are full of something that feels dangerously close to anger.

<!-- bt-block:000068 -->
“Thank you, *boss.”*

<!-- bt-block:000069 -->
Lawrence nods again, shrugging off the response. “You are a smart man, Fernando,” he repeats, his voice even more careful than before. “Is it worth it? *Your career,* I mean. Does it feel,” he pauses — if for dramatic effect, or because he’s searching for the correct words, neither of them knows for sure, “this might very well be your last season. Does it feel worth it?”

<!-- bt-block:000070 -->
Swallowing the bitter taste in his mouth, Fernando nods with a tight smile. “Ah, so this is from where the wind is blowing,” he laughs, shaking his head. “Contract negotiations. You could have just said so.”

<!-- bt-block:000071 -->
“No,” Lawrence objects, “I was just saying… sometimes, when we do what we think is best,” he pauses for a breath, “we actually do the exact opposite.”

<!-- bt-block:000072 -->
Fernando can feel the right corner of his mouth lift in a smirk. “I don’t think I’m doing *the best,”* he breathes out firmly. “I don’t know what *best* is. Do you?”

<!-- bt-block:000073 -->
“Perhaps I don’t,” Lawrence pushes out through gritted teeth, “But I know what *best* isn’t.”

<!-- bt-block:000074 -->
Tilting his head slightly, Fernando’s eyes widen entirely on purpose. “And?” he breathes out with faux genuineness. “What is it not?”

<!-- bt-block:000075 -->
Lawrence looks him over, closing his eyes slowly. “I think you should leave now,” he states, turning on his heel before walking back to his table. He grips the backrest of his chair, digging his nails into the soft fabric, as he watches Fernando way too cheerfully gather his things. He remains quiet until Fernando reaches the door. “Sure, keep doing what you’re doing. I can’t tell you to stop. But this time next year, I’ll make sure to ask you again if you *still* think this all,” he gestures around them with his left hand, “was fucking worth it.”

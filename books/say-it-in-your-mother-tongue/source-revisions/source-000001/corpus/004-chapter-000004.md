Chapter 2: contractually obligated to be fine

Chapter Notes

<!-- bt-block:000004 -->
Something in the garage is humming.

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
Nico looks around, annoyed, for the source of the sound. “Fucking —” he growls, throwing his clipboard onto the closest table. With a twitch in his right eye, he walks past a few mechanics who have been doing god-knows-what for the last twenty minutes, searching for the thing turning his favorite song of engine purr and hydraulic whines into a cacophony making his teeth itch.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
“Oi, Nico,” a way too chipper voice rings out.

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
*Oh, for fuck’s sake,* Nico thinks as his eye twitches again. *Another fucking source of annoying noise*.

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
He turns around, a stern expression sitting strongly on his face. “What do you want?”

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
“Rude,” Gabriel casually laughs, clapping his German teammate on the shoulder. “Can I not talk to my favorite *colega de equipe* just because?”

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
“It is never *just because* with you,” Nico states, plain and seemingly unable to experience normal human emotions. “What do you want, Bortoleto?”

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
Shrugging, Gabriel gives Nico another bright smile. “I just wanted to share my, *uh*, *anotações* about the braking. I printed out the *telemetria* for you, here,” he says as he pulls out two folded pieces of paper and shoves them into Nico’s hand. “The green highlighter is when I brake heavy, the pink when I engine brake. Use it. Or don’t.”

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
Not waiting for a response, Gabriel turns around and strides away from his confused teammate with too much energy.

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
*What?*

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
Nico parts his lips. “Wait,” he calls after Gabriel, who stops and turns to face him immediately. “Why are you helping me?”

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
Gabriel shrugs. “No *motivo*. Just felt like it.”

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
Raising one eyebrow, Nico looks at him suspiciously. “We don’t hold each other’s hand, *kid*. Why are you doing this? Why now?”

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
“*Old man*, we are P19 and P20. If we don’t help each other, no one else will,” Gabriel says like it’s the most reasonable thing *ever* — which, to be fair, it kind of is when you drive for Sauber.

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
Nico opens his mouth, then closes it again without making a *peep*.

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
Understanding that the conversation is over, Gabriel spins on his heel and steps out into the short hallway leading away from Hülkenberg’s side of the garage while whistling a tune he made up.

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
Still holding the papers, Nico watches Gabriel walk away with a puzzled look plastered on his face. He swallows loudly, tightening his grip of the telemetry, as he forces himself to look *anywhere but Gabriel*. He scratches his head, making eye contact with the printed data.

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
*What the actual fuck has just happened?*

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
The rain hasn’t stopped in five hours.

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
Max rubs his face with his fingers, before shoving his hands back into the pockets of his Red Bull branded dark blue hoodie. The window he’s been staring out of gets more and more foggy with each breath he takes, but he can still see how the puddles outside get fuller as more time passes.

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
There is a radio turned on in one of the rooms around here, judging by the fact that for the last forty minutes — or however long he’s been glued in one spot — he could hear music. Originally, he thought it was Liam playing something from his speaker, but none of the songs sound even remotely like the stuff his new teammate usually listens to. The first song he could name of the top of his head was *American Pie* by Don McLean. And right now, it’s Johnny Cash’s *Hurt*. Whoever is playing it, they are really *going through it*.

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
Stretching, Max yawns. Few tears escape his eyes as he does so.

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
He hears a door *whine* open and *thud* shut but doesn’t bother looking over his shoulder to check who decided to join him in his makeshift observatory. That is until he hears a familiar voice.

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
“*I have successfully made contact with the Prime Minister of Kamino*.”

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
Max turns around.

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
And there *he* stands. Checo. Wrapped in a long brown coat, dripping rain water onto the floor, with a bright smile plastered on his face. The hood frames his head, falling a little too low — covering his entire forehead, reaching over his eyebrows all the way to his eyes. With arms in front of him, he adds, “*They are using a bounty hunter named Jango Fett to create a clone army.*” The coat makes him look shorter than he actually is.

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
“Are you really quoting Obi-Wan Kenobi at me right now?” Max asks with a wide smile. The tone of his voice betrays him, though, allowing the barely concealed melancholy to slip through.

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
“I would quote Anakin at you, but the only quote of his I can remember is the whole, ‘*oh-mah-god Padmé, I love you so much*’ thing,” Checo laughs, shrugging off the wet coat. He lets it fall onto ground without care. “And like I love you, *amigo*, but not *that* much.” He walks closer to Max, pulling him into a tight hug. “You should probably say it back, dude,” he says, grinning, “or I’ll feel like a major loser who just got rejected.”

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
Max laughs — really laughs — with his mouth wide open, tilting his head back. As Checo lets go of him, he pokes Checo’s ribs with a jokey, “Oh, you might have to remind of that one, because I don’t think I remember.”

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
The Mexican clears his throat, spreading his arms in a theatrical manner. His voice is clear and much deeper than normally as he says, “*From the moment I met you, all those years ago, not a day has gone by when I haven’t thought of you. And now that I’m with you again… I’m in agony. The closer I get to you, the worse it gets!*”

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
Max grabs Checo’s hand. “Oh my,” he breathes out, blinking at his friend like a school-girl in love. “I never knew! You should have said something! I love you too,” he pretends to confess, one hand over his heart and the other still holding Checo’s. “Now kiss me, *Romeo*.”

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
“Anakin,” Checo corrects him, laughing.

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
“Yeah, tomato, *potato*,” Max jokes, butchering the phrase ‘*tomato, tomato*’ (*to-may-to, to-mah-to*) on purpose.

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
A moment passes with neither of them saying anything. The silence between them isn’t empty, but it’s not charged either; instead, it’s filled with the chords of R.E.M.’s *Everybody Hurts*.

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
Checo steps closer to Max. He has to look up slightly to meet his eyes. “You alright?” he asks, voice quiet and steady.

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
Max watches a drop of rainwater slowly travel from Checo’s forehead down around his nose, only stopping when it reaches a tiny wrinkle above his lips. “Of course,” Max nods once, putting on another picture perfect smile. “I am actually contractually obligated to be fine.”

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
Sighing, Checo lowers his gaze before making eye contact again. “And if you weren’t?”

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
Max takes a step back, turning away from his ex-teammate as he does so. His breath is uneven and ragged, as if it’s taking everything in his power to force himself to continue, to take another breath, to stay on his side of the barricade. “I’m fine,” he states again, knowing that Checo isn’t buying it. “How are you? *Unemployed* life treating you well?”

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
When they first met, Checo and him, Max was not happy. He would tell you that himself, adding a non-committal ‘*of course*,’ followed surely by a comment about how many teammates he’s had until then and only ‘*really became friends with one of them*’ who didn’t want to race by his side after a few years anyways.

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
Checo stands next to him, looking out onto the concrete covered in puddles the size of Mexico, and doesn’t respond. He bumps Max’s bicep with his shoulder. The contact is fleeting, but the warmth of it lingers between them longer than it should. “*Cariño*, you know we’re still friends, right? Even if no longer teammates.”

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
Huffing, Max mutters something too quietly for Checo to catch it.

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
“What?” he asks, not really expecting Max to repeat himself. And he’s right — Max just holds his arms on his chest, a gesture somewhere between a self-hug and a holding up a shield. “I won’t let you be all *huff and puff*. Speak to me.”

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
It takes Max two minutes before he even considers saying *anything*, and it takes him another ninety seconds to decide how honest he wants to be. He looks at Checo, this short-person-shaped source of light and happiness, and he just breathes out, defeated. “Kelly… Kelly broke up with me.”

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
Checo immediately turns to him, looking over his face, searching for *anything* that would tell him that he just misheard Max. “What? When? *What?*”

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
Max breathes out through his nose, pushing his sleeve up to check the watch on his left wrist. He watches the seconds hand, waiting. “It has been exactly five hours, seven minutes and thirty seconds… *now*.”

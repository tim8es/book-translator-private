It has been almost six hours since Gabriel left.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
Nico didn’t mean to keep track — at least he keeps telling himself that — but he put on Pink Floyd’s *The Wall* a few minutes after being left alone in the suite and since then, the album has managed to play *all the way through* four times.

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
He pretends to check his emails, refreshing the page over and over again, while staring at the clock in the right corner of his laptop screen. The time slowed down, dragging now like a snail covered in molasses, to the point where Nico might actually be going mad.

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
*Where are you?*

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
Checking his phone again — with no new messages — he stands up from the table and walks to one of the windows. He looks out, into the cold Zürich evening, and sighs.

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
*I’m such an imbecile.*

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
He walks to a second window overlooking the same street, opening it before leaning out the most he can. He searches for the familiar face among the few people wandering outside. No luck.

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
*I should’ve gone with him.*

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
Nico closes the window again. The room is colder now, and he sighs again, regret and shame filling his veins in equal measures.

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
*Oh fuck, finally*.

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
Turning to see his teammate, Nico exhales a shaky breath. Gabriel is standing there, his face red from wind, hoodie zipped up all the way to his neck. He looks… tired. More tired than when he left. And more cold as well.

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
“Hey,” Nico breathes out gently, not trusting himself to say much more.

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
“Hey,” Gabriel says back, shutting the door behind him way too harshly. He walks past Nico towards the wardrobe he threw his suitcase into, opening it and pulling out a set of clean clothes. Not saying anything else, he starts walking towards the bathroom, but Nico doesn’t let him.

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
He moves in front of Gabriel, stopping him in his tracks. “Are you okay?”

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
*Where the fuck have you been this long?*

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
Gabriel searches his face with a confused look in his eyes. “Never been better, Nicolas,” he breathes out in a tone of voice Nico’s never heard from him before. “What about you?”

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
*Where have you been?*

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
“I’m quite okay myself, thank you,” Nico shoots back, his voice dripping with something dangerously close to anger. “Had fun?”

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->

<!-- bt-block:000077 -->
His hand hurts a little, but Fernando cannot bring himself to stop petting Lance’s hair.

<!-- bt-block:000078 -->
Sitting on the living room couch, Fernando hasn’t moved in over an hour. In his lap, Lance’s head is resting, the tears still rolling down his cheeks but slower now. He’s digging the fingers of his right hand into Fernando’s thigh, like the moment could disappear from under his hand if he only let go.

<!-- bt-block:000079 -->
Fernando doesn’t mind.

<!-- bt-block:000080 -->
Well, *that’s a lie*. He *does* mind; his back is killing him, there are bruises surely forming where Lance is gripping him, and his legs have gone numb a long time ago. But he’s unwilling to move, scared to disturb the moment.

<!-- bt-block:000081 -->
“Did I ever tell you how Mark and I… we switched caps for a photoshoot?” Fernando asks suddenly, surprising them both. He looks down at Lance, searching his face.

<!-- bt-block:000082 -->
“*Non*,” Lance says with heavy French accent he hasn’t talked in since he was seven. “Don’t think you did.”

<!-- bt-block:000083 -->
Fernando nods. “We were doing this photoshoot,” he starts, looking towards the window with something like *dreamy* expression on his face. “It was me, Mark, Lewis, Jenson, Michael and Seb. And it was taking forever, like *hours* at this point of just ‘*do this pose, do this one next, Fernando, look more alive, Lewis, stop looking like we’re holding you hostage, Jenson, stop smiling so much.*’ It was so boring. And then Mark just casually grabbed my cap and his and switched them.”

<!-- bt-block:000084 -->
Lance is looking up at him, the tears still coming, but no longer with as painful of an expression. “Yeah?”

<!-- bt-block:000085 -->
*“So,* I’m standing there in my Ferrari overalls, he’s in his Red Bull, right? But now I’m wearing Red Bull hat, and he has my Ferrari one. Like it was funny, *yeah*, but very *obvious* a joke,” Fernando says. Meeting Lance’s eyes, he gives him a soft smile. “But the photos of us *doing* that, they got out. And someone made it a big deal. Like ‘*are they dating?*’ type of deal.”

<!-- bt-block:000086 -->
Nodding, Lance breathes out, “And were you?”

<!-- bt-block:000087 -->
Fernando pauses. “Were we*… dating?”* he asks with raised eyebrows.

<!-- bt-block:000088 -->
Lance nods again. “Yeah. Were you dating?”

<!-- bt-block:000089 -->
“No,” Fernando shakes his head. He holds Lance’s gaze when he adds, *“Cariño,* the closest Mark has come to *being* with a man is, well, holding Sebastian’s hand during that one podium. And even that was probably very *inappropriate* in his eyes.”

<!-- bt-block:000090 -->
Chuckling, Lance nods. “Makes sense,” he says, and for the first time in over an hour, the tears stop coming, “he doesn’t seem like he’d be into that. He’s too… *proper* for that.”

<!-- bt-block:000091 -->
Fernando laughs, too. “Well, you never know,” Fernando shrugs, “there are certainly a few drivers who — how to put this? — *fooled around* with one another for full seasons and still were straight as an arrow.”

<!-- bt-block:000092 -->
“Should I be asking who you mean?” Lance breathes out. He doesn’t admit it aloud, but he’s afraid Fernando might be talking about one driver in particular. *Himself*. “Probs not, yeah?”

<!-- bt-block:000093 -->
“People thought Lewis and Nico were a thing,” Fernando shrugs, avoiding the question with the expertise of someone with over two decades of experience in only partially answering unwanted questions. “*Sinceramente*, I did too. Still do.”

<!-- bt-block:000094 -->
*And you?*

<!-- bt-block:000095 -->
Lance nods. His head hurts slightly and so do his eyes. “It’d make sense,” he admits in hushed voice. Yawning, he screws his eyes shut for a moment before deciding to sit up. The world around Lance spins a little too fast for a second, only steadying when Fernando grabs his shoulders to hold him in place.

<!-- bt-block:000096 -->
“Okay?” Fernando asks, eyeing Lance suspiciously, like he might collapse back any second.

<!-- bt-block:000097 -->
“Yeah,” Lance says, even when it’s not entirely true. His head hurts, his eyes burn, and something in his chest ticks like a bomb ready to explode any second. He leans back, resting his back and head on the backrest. He doesn’t look at Fernando — actually, he closes his eyes to make sure he doesn’t look at him accidentally — when he lets out a sigh. He breathes out, “I’m sorry.”

<!-- bt-block:000098 -->
Fernando takes his hand into his, wrapping his fingers around it. Lance doesn’t see Fernando bring their joined hands to his lips, but he can feel Fernando kissing him on the back of his hand gently. His breath tickles as he says, “Don’t say sorry. You’ve done nothing wrong, *cariño.”*

<!-- bt-block:000099 -->
*Except for being gay.*

<!-- bt-block:000100 -->
Lance shakes his head, his eyes still closed. “A lot of people would disagree with you,” he breathes out quietly — not so Fernando doesn’t hear it, but so Lance himself doesn’t.

<!-- bt-block:000101 -->
Exhaling sharply, Fernando fights the need to say something like, *“Who cares?”* He knows Lance isn’t like him, knows *Lance* cares, knows it matters to him what others think of him. And it’s not like Fernando *doesn’t* care…

<!-- bt-block:000102 -->
“But a lot more people would agree with me,” Fernando says instead, his voice soft, as he squeezes Lance’s hand. “And don’t ask me *who*. You know well *who*. Sebastian. Checo. Lewis. Ocon. And outside of F1? The list is so fucking long that all the paper of the world would not be enough to contain it.”

<!-- bt-block:000103 -->
“Dramatic much?” Lance chuckles. “Sometimes I forget you’re Spanish,” he says, finally opening his eyes to look at his *teammate*, “but you always find a way to remind me.”

<!-- bt-block:000104 -->
“Should I speak more Spanish? Would you learn it for me?”

<!-- bt-block:000105 -->
Lance doesn’t hesitate before he nods. “Yeah,” he exhales, no longer able to look at Fernando again. “I would.”

<!-- bt-block:000106 -->
“I learn French for you, you learn Spanish for me. Deal?”

<!-- bt-block:000107 -->
Lance laughs again, and it’s a soft sound that makes goosebumps pop up on Fernando’s arms. “Sure,” he nods, “that sounds like a deal.”

Chapter 9: here with you

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
The apartment is too quiet and it’s making Max’s teeth itch.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
*You said you’d be right back.*

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
He walks from window to window, staring out into the sunny town below him, like a dog waiting for its owner to come back home. He opens one of them, allowing the outside noise to enter the warmth of a shared flat. Groaning, he moves to the window in the guest room that has slowly become his bedroom.

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
There are five posters on the walls, two of a hockey team, the rest of footballers, that Checo helped Max put up after asking him seventeen hundred times if he wanted something *special* for the room.

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
Max huffs, folding his arms on his chest, before he sits down on the bed.

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
*What if something happened?*

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
He rubs his face. It has been almost two hours since Checo left, and Max has already done *everything* he felt needed to be done — a load of laundry, ordered a new dryer because the old one broke, sorted Checo’s socks, had a coffee and tried not panicking about being alone for the first time in weeks.

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
*I should text him again.*

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
Biting his nails, Max gets up from the bed and walks back into the living room.

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
*He hates you. He doesn’t want to come back because he hates you.*

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
Max shakes his head to clear his mind. He thinks about having another cup of coffee before settling down on the sofa, with his legs folded under him. His phone makes a noise in the kitchen, forcing Max to jump up and run to check it with the excitement of a child on Christmas morning.

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
He rolls his eyes. “Oh, fuck *off*,” he groans before throwing his phone back onto the counter with a loud *bang*. Without thinking, he gets back to biting his nails.

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
A rustling of keys followed by a soft whine of the front door makes Max perk up.

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
*He’s home.*

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
Peeking into the hallway, Max tries to hide his smile behind his hand, still biting his nails. “Hi,” he breathes out with barely concealed shakiness. He looks Checo over, subconsciously checking if he’s alright.

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
“*Dios mío*, you won’t believe me what happened,” Checo says as he takes off his shoes. He picks up the tote bag he put on the ground and offers it to Max as he walks into the kitchen. “I got you a little something.”

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
Max’s voice is quiet when he states, “You said you’d be right back.”

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
Checo nods without pausing. “And I had the best intentions to do so.” He stops by the fridge, putting something inside, before looking back at Max who’s still turned into the hallway, his shoulders slumped. “What’s wrong, *cariño*?”

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
“*You said you’d be right back*,” Max repeats, his voice becoming shakier with each word. He turns to face Checo with tears slowly running down his cheeks. “I —”

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
“I’m sorry,” Checo hurries to him, opening his arms in a hug offering. “I’m so sorry.”

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
“I thought,” Max hiccups, his words slurred together, “I thought you left. Like *forever*.” With that, Max steps into Checo’s personal space.

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
“*Estoy aquí*.” Wrapping his arms around Max’s waist, Checo whispers, “I’m here, *cariño.* I’d never leave you like *that*.” He moves his right hand up, caressing Max’s back as he does so, and tangles it in Max’s hair. It’s the closest they’ve been since Max moved in. “I’m here,” Checo repeats. “I’m here.”

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
Max hides his face in Checo’s neck, pressing his wet cheeks into the warm safety. “I’m sorry,” he mutters, barely audible, as he grabs onto the back of Checo’s shirt.

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
Checo pets Max’s hair lightly, holding him in place with his other hand. “I’m so sorry,” he whispers close to Max’s ear. “Next time, we go together, *sí*?” he asks without expecting an answer — or waiting for it. “There was a big car crash,” Checo says as he combs through Max’s hair. “Someone *totaled* their Volkswagen and I had to drive all the way back, that’s why it took me so long.”

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
His tears keep coming as Max murmurs, “I’m sorry.”

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
“No, *leoncito*,” Checo pulls Max tighter to himself, “no apology. Is my fault, okay?”

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
“But it’s *not*,” Max whines, hiding his face deeper — as if he could disappear into the hollow of Checo’s neck completely. “I feel stupid.”

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
*Because you are.*

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
*You’re a fucking loser who can’t stay alone for couple of hours without having a meltdown.*

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
Checo pushes Max away — as far as his arms let him. He looks up, his dark eyes meeting Max’s, and shakes his head. His eyebrows knit together tightly. “Don’t say *that*,” he commands. “And don’t think it either,” he adds, his upset palpable around them. “Okay?”

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
Max hesitates for a moment. He doesn’t want to anger Checo, but at the same time, something deep inside him needs him to protest, needs to make Checo understand how terrible and stupid and weak he is. In the end, Max averts his red-rimmed eyes before breathing out a painful, “But I *am*.”

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
“No,” Checo says, sharper than before, “*we* don’t do that.” His hands tighten slightly on Max’s arms, holding him in place. “You don’t get to talk like that about yourself. Not with me.”

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
*The longer he takes to realize, the more disappointed he’ll be.*

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
Lowering his eyes, Max sighs a painful, “People who *aren’t* stupid don’t act like me,” fresh tears falling onto his cheeks as he does so.

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
Checo’s lets go of Max’s right arm, lifting his left hand to his face. He wipes Max’s tears with his thumb. “Look at me,” he asks, his voice gentle and low. “*Please*.”

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
Max obeys, meeting the intense gaze.

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
“We will talk about this later, okay?” he suggests, light and casual, though they both know there’s nothing casual about it.

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
Silently, Max nods.

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
“Now, let’s go sit down. I’ll make us some tea, okay?”

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
Another nod.

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
Checo gives him a soft smile before asking, “You wanna be in the kitchen with me, or will you sit in the lounge?”

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
Max shakes his head, his eyes wide with something *too close* to fear for Checo’s liking.

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
“Kitchen,” Checo says plainly before he lets go of Max’s other arm. He holds out his hand with a soft, “*Hm*?” He watches as Max grabs his hand with his and cannot help but smile at the way Max’s entire demeanor relaxes with the contact. “You can reach the top shelf, we can have the good tea.” Checo doesn’t add that he bought the tea specifically because Max mentioned liking it once; deep down he knows that he doesn’t have to.

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
Nodding, Max breathes out, “*Yeah*.” He looks at his phone and swallows loudly, suddenly very aware of his panic. “I… I might have bought a new dryer,” he admits, not daring to look towards Checo — whose hand he’s still gripping.

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
“Why?” Checo asks, confused but entirely soft.

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
Max shrugs. “I panicked,” he confesses, his face going even more red than it already has been from crying. “I thought you left.”

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
Checo closes his eyes fighting the need to burst out laughing. “Should I ask why that felt like the best… *course of action*?”

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
“It seemed reasonable in the moment.”

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
“I’ll say three things, and three things only,” Checo states, meeting Max’s eyes. He smiles, reassuring Max with the gesture, before counting on the fingers of his free hand, “*Uno*, you are the smartest person I know. *Dos*, I’d never leave you because you mean the world to me. *Tres*, I’ll probably never understand what goes on in your head. And I wouldn’t want it any other way.”

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->
Like he always does, Max seemingly picks up on only one of them. “You promise?”

<!-- bt-block:000056 -->
And Checo nods. “*Sí*,” he breathes out. He lifts their joined hands, maneuvering them so that Max’s is under his, and places them over his heart. “You feel it?”

<!-- bt-block:000057 -->
“Your… *heart*?” Max asks, his breath hitching in his throat.

<!-- bt-block:000058 -->
“*Sí*, my heart.”

<!-- bt-block:000059 -->
Max furrows his eyebrows, feeling for Checo’s heartbeat for a second, before responding. “It’s… *beating*,” he states, confused.

<!-- bt-block:000060 -->
“It’s beating,” Checo repeats, nodding. “As long as it’s beating, *I’m here*. Yeah?”

<!-- bt-block:000061 -->
And suddenly, *here* doesn’t mean an apartment too big for two people, it doesn’t mean Monaco, it doesn’t mean the planet Earth. In that moment, Max’s heart starts beating fast as it downs on him — *here* means, plain and simple, *with you*.

The clock above the door says it’s two in the morning when Checo wakes up.

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
He yawns, rubbing at his right eye with his fingers. He looks at the spot next to him, where Max is supposed to be, and stops. “*Max?*” he asks, barely above a whisper. The door into the bathroom is cracked open, but the light there isn’t on. Checo runs his hand over *Max’s* side of bed.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
It’s cold.

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
He stands up. His pajama pants are hanging low on his hips, and Checo pulls them up just enough for them not to slide down as he walks. On bare feet, he moves into the short hallway.

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
The door into the smaller bedroom they agreed not to use is cracked open, even when Checo knows they left it closed. He pushes it open with the tips of his fingers, hoping it doesn’t creak, and looks inside.

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
*Oh*.

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
With one hand on the doorframe, he exhales, painful and deep.

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
The twin-sized bed with powder blue sheets, that stands in the middle of the room under a way too flashy chandelier, looks too big around the man sleeping there. Max has a hand under his face; the other arm is wrapped around the pillow like all he wants is to be held and to hold another body close.

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
Checo steps into the room, careful not to make a sound as he moves closer to the bed. His hands are trembling, even if he’d love to *just* pretend to be unaffected by the sight. Unsteady feet carry him to the sleeping man, and he falls to his knees when he’s closest to Max’s head.

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
“I’m sorry,” he whispers as he reaches to touch the face scrunched up as if in thought even in sleep. His fingertips gently caress the smooth cheek, then up the forehead, before returning to the apple of Max’s cheek. Checo sighs, lowering his face so that his lips softly brush against Max’s forehead.

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
Max huffs, pouting his lips, before relaxing his face again. He makes a soft sound that’s somewhere between a moan and a whine, hugging the pillow tighter.

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
Checo hesitates for a second before caressing his cheek with a little more pressure. “Max?” he breathes out, quiet enough to be dismissed by the sleeping man, but loud enough to possibly get his attention. “*Maxie,* wake up,” he whispers, alarm and affection mixing in his voice.

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
Hiding his face in the pillow, Max groans.

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
“Wake up,” Checo says again, louder. He caresses Max’s cheek, his shoulder and back, his arm, any piece of his body he can touch. He lowers his face to Max’s again, asking him to wake up again. “*Please*.”

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
“*Maar dat wil ik niet*,” Max groans in Dutch, his voice quiet and sleepy. He hides his face deeper into the pillow before adding, muffled by the pillow, “*Ga weg, papa. Het is te vroeg.*”

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
Checo pauses, unsure of what to do. He should tell Max he’s not Jos. He should ask him to go back to *their* bed. He should—

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
*“Papa, hoe laat is het nu?”*

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
“I’m not your *papa*, Max,” Checo finally says, his voice the softest it can be.

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
Max looks up from the pillow, his eyes clouded with sleep. His mouth is open slightly, a bit of drool escaping past his lips. “What?” he asks, blinking away the exhaustion. “What’s going on?”

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
“Come to bed,” Checo exhales bluntly, suddenly way too aware of *everything*. “It’s two. Come to bed with me.”

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
*Fuck it.*

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
“*Nee,*” Max shakes his head — as much as the pillow allows him to do so. “I sleep here.”

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
Checo sighs. “Okay,” he exhales, standing up from the floor. He dusts off his knees before moving around the bed, behind Max’s back. Lifting the blanket off Max’s body, he climbs into the bed and lets the blanket cover them both. “*You* sleep here, *I* sleep here,” he says with a thick Spanish accent. He shoves his hand under Max’s neck, pulling him closer to his chest, while snaking the other arm around Max’s middle. “Good night. Don’t run away again.”

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
He can feel the stiffness in Max’s body, the way he’s clutching the pillow like a shield, the way something unspoken lingers between them. Checo sighs again before placing a soft kiss into Max’s hair. “We should sleep now, *baby*, but we’ll talk in the morning. Okay?”

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
Max shakes his head again. “No. Don’t wanna.”

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
Checo gives him another kiss, and then a few more, into his hair. “*Shh.* It’s sleep time now.”

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
The room falls quiet, even when sleep doesn’t visit either of them. Max exhales a shaky breath, something too close to a sob for Checo’s liking. The Mexican pulls him tighter to his chest, holding him as close as humanly possible. “I’m not upset at you. If… if you wanted to know,” he whispers. He pauses for a second, gathering up the courage to speak again. When he *does* speak, his voice is breathy. “Are *you* upset at me?”

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
“No,” Max says too fast. His words are muffled by the pillow, still, when he adds, “not at you. At *me*.”

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
“Do you wanna tell me why you’re upset at yourself?” Checo asks gently.

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
Max shakes his head again. “No. Is sleep time now.”

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
“It doesn’t *have to* be. We can… we can talk about it, *cariño*.”

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
Groaning, Max hides his face further. “*No*,” he says, barely audible. “Don’t wanna.”

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
Checo kisses the back of his head again. “That’s okay, *baby*,” he breathes out. “Good night.”

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
The room, surely intended to be a child’s bedroom, goes quiet again. Two sets of breath, so synchronized it hurts when Checo thinks about it for too long, echo through the silence. For maybe five minutes, Checo thinks Max has fallen asleep again and he’s content with just holding the man until the morning comes, when suddenly, Max shuffles in his arms.

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
“Here,” Max whispers, offering Checo the pillow he’s been hugging. He doesn’t turn to face Checo, but his back is pressed to Checo’s chest so tightly that if he tried hard enough, he could probably feel his heartbeat. He sighs, screwing his eyes shut. “Good night.”

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
“Good night, Max.”

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
“And Checo?” Max asks, his voice hushed and trembling.

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
“Hm?”

“Thank you.”

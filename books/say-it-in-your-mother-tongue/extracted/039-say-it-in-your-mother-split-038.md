Max also nods, too stunned to speak, before shaking his head. “I mean it. I’m gay,” he insists, his voice desperate and breathless. *“Ik ben verliefd op jou,”* Max admits, his voice breaking with the last word. “I… I think I have feelings,” Max tries again, stuttering into Checo’s neck. “For you. I think I have feelings for you.”

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
And Checo just laughs.

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
It’s not mocking, nor does it feel like the chuckle of someone utterly uncomfortable, but still — no matter how Max looks at it — Checo *is* laughing.

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
*Oh.*

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
Max pushes himself away, fighting his own face not to show any signs of just how hurt he actually is, putting on a media training approved smile instead — even if he knows it looks nothing like his genuine one. He takes a step back, away from the warmth of Checo’s body, taking a deep breath that has nothing to do with the way his eyes start watering again.

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
“I’m sorry,” Checo says, clearing his throat before adding, in a soft voice, “I just didn’t think you’d actually say that.”

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
Looking slightly down to meet Checo’s eyes, Max tilts his head to one side. “What do you mean?” he breathes out in genuine confusion.

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
Instead of an immediate answer, Checo offers him a soft smile as he steps into his personal space. His eyes are gentle when he raises his hand, placing it on Max’s cheek; they become even gentler when Max instinctively leans into the touch.

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
“What do you mean?” Max asks again, softer this time, as if his brain realized — even if really fucking unwillingly — that someone touching him *like this* cannot realistically be *pissed* at him for admitting such a thing.

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
Checo rubs Max’s cheek with his thumb, caressing the soft skin in slow circles, as he looks up into Max’s eyes. With his free hand, Checo reaches for Max’s right hand, placing it over his own chest. *“No puedo vivir sin ti,”* he tells him, gentle and full of adoration. “Is beating, no? And I promised — as long as it beats, I’ll be with you, no?”

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
Flexing his fingers slightly, Max feels the heartbeat under Checo’s shirt, under his muscles, under his ribs. He nods, *pretty sure* his voice would fail him had he tried to say *anything*.

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
Covering Max’s hand with his own, Checo nods. “I only make promises I want to *hold*.”

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
Max licks his lips without realizing he’s doing it. He’s looking into Checo’s eyes.

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
*How have I never noticed how pretty his eyes are?*

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
*“Leoncito,* what else did you think *this* was?” Checo asks, gentle but insistent. He doesn’t wait for an answer before he continues, “Thought I was just being nice or something?”

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
Max lowers his eyes, looking to the ground where a broken-off piece of his phone lies. *“Ja,”* he breathes out quietly. “Pretty much?”

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
An amused huff escapes Checo’s lips. *“Te adoro,”* he says, his voice low in his throat. “Only you could think *mi mamá* bought us matching pajamas because we’re *best friends*,” he jokes, snaking a hand around Max’s waist.

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
Max furrows his eyebrows. “But—”

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
“They’re a matching *pair*. Like for *couples* matching pair,” Checo chuckles. “And we got them for valentine’s day.”

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
*What?*

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
“Wait,” Max breathes out, looking back at Checo. “Is that why your sister asked me about *our anniversary?”*

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
Checo nods as he says, chuckling, *“Sí.”*

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
*Oh.*

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
“I told her I don’t remember,” Max admits, his eyes wide as the *mortifying* truth settles in. *“Verdomme.* She must think I’m a terrible boyfriend.”

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
“She did,” Checo agrees, still laughing. “Told me after it happened.”

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
Max screws his eyes shut with a groan. “Did you at least explain it to her?” he asks, opening his eyes only halfway.

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
Checo shakes his head. “No,” he admits with an affectionate smile, “that’d mean no more pretending that… that you want me too. It was easier to just… *not say the whole truth.”*

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
“Lie,” Max breathes out, “you mean *lie.”*

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
Checo smiles wider. *“Sí,”* he offers effortlessly. “But also… was it really a lie?”

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
Max bites his lip and shrugs, suddenly very aware of the way Checo’s heartbeat sped up. “A little. *You* didn’t know.”

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
“But now I know,” Checo licks his lips, stilling the movement of his thumb on Max’s cheek. “Is a truth now.”

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
With a nod, Max lets himself be pulled closer to Checo — their faces barely a single breath apart. He can feel the way their bodies collide, suddenly aware how every cell of his body yearns to be touched by Checo’s warm hands, and something inside him chest breaks with that.

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
With the dam holding *everything* back gone, there’s nothing telling Max how bad an idea *this* is — and he just presses their lips together.

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
It’s a hasty touch, barely a kiss, but Max melts into it anyways.

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
*Oh fuck.*

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
He can hear himself make a low growling noise, can feel the way goosebumps form all over his body, and suddenly, *everything* makes perfect sense again.

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
Checo pulls back first. “I’m not just being friendly now,” he says in a quiet voice, “do you know that?”

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
Max howls in laughter, shaking his head. “Yeah,” he says when his own laugh dies down, “I know.”

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
With a soft smile, Checo nods. “Good,” he breathes out. “You still… wanna do it again?”

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
And Max doesn’t wait for anything else, pressing their lips together once again.

“I promised you wine.”

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
Lance looks up from his phone — which he’s only staring at because he’s trying to *curate* a playlist that wouldn’t feel like a BuzzFeed list of *top twenty cheesiest love songs ever* — with a smile. “You did, yeah.”

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
“Can’t have wine *and* drive, right?” Fernando asks with a grin. Watching as Lance shakes his head, he continues, nonchalant as ever, “so I got us a place to stay at for the night.”

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
“What?” Lance breathes out, shoving the phone inside the pocket of his hoodie. “Why? We could’ve just stayed at my place.”

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->
Fernando shrugs, turning off the engine of the McLaren. “I told you,” he says, taking Lance’s hand into his own, bending down slightly to kiss his knuckles before adding, “you deserve the best.”

<!-- bt-block:000056 -->
“But—” Lance starts but gets stopped by Fernando raising his free hand.

<!-- bt-block:000057 -->
“No,” he objects, “is pleasure for me,” he continues with a wide smile that shows off his pearly white teeth. A beat goes by before Fernando unbuckles his seatbelt, pressing a button that opens the door on driver’s side. He’s already halfway out of the car when he turns to Lance again and commands, “Don’t move a muscle.”

<!-- bt-block:000058 -->
Lance just nods, taken back by… *everything*. He undoes his seatbelt as well, figuring that Fernando couldn’t be upset at him for that, as his eyes follow Fernando moving from the right side of the car to the left. He offers the Spaniard a smile over the window.

<!-- bt-block:000059 -->
The door of the passenger side *hiss* open. Fernando stands there, one hand outstretched towards Lance, with an expression that could only be described as soft. “May I?” he asks quietly, the palm of his hand facing up in an offering.

<!-- bt-block:000060 -->
“Gentleman,” Lance breathes out with a smile, letting his hand slip into Fernando’s before he shifts in his seat so both his legs face *out*. Lance places his right foot down first, then the left one, and then Fernando basically pulls him out.

<!-- bt-block:000061 -->
Lance staggers slightly, his feet not as steady as he’d like them to be, but Fernando is there, catching him with two strong arms.

<!-- bt-block:000062 -->
“Careful, *princesa,”* he breathes out, surprised but pleased, “you already fell for me. Don’t have to do it again.”

<!-- bt-block:000063 -->
And Lance giggles — actually giggles — at that, his cheeks going pink. Something about that sound makes him feel young, much younger than he actually is, which Fernando cannot help but notice.

<!-- bt-block:000064 -->
“Come,” Fernando says, pulling on Lance’s hand again. He leads him behind the small house they’ve stopped by, past stone wall and flowerpots full of not-yet-in-bloom flowers. Pausing for a second, Fernando breathes out, “Close your eyes.”

<!-- bt-block:000065 -->
Lance chuckles. “Sure,” he lets out, covering his eyes with his free hand. “But don’t let me break something.”

<!-- bt-block:000066 -->
“Don’t worry,” Fernando says, suddenly closer than before, his voice velvety and sweet, “I’ll keep you safe.”

<!-- bt-block:000067 -->
Lance nods, smiling, before telling his *teammate*, “I’m more worried about everything else. But yeah, don’t let me break my leg or something.”

<!-- bt-block:000068 -->
Fernando huffs out, amused, “Silly boy.”

<!-- bt-block:000069 -->
They don’t walk far, maybe a hundred meters, but with closed eyes it feels like four times as much. Fernando tells Lance to wait, turning him around as if to make sure he doesn’t peek even little bit at whatever the surprise is. Lance can hear a soft *click* followed by a set of noises he can’t quite place.

<!-- bt-block:000070 -->
“Don’t open,” Fernando commands again, his voice coming from further away than before.

<!-- bt-block:000071 -->
“I won’t.”

<!-- bt-block:000072 -->
As the first notes from the overture of Tchaikovsky’s *Romeo and Juliet* reach Lance’s ears, Fernando tells him to open his eyes.

<!-- bt-block:000073 -->
And Lance does, turning around to search for Fernando.

<!-- bt-block:000074 -->
*“Oh,”* he exhales. There, in front of him, stands a small white table set for two — two white chairs, two wine glasses, two plates with *some sort of salad* on them. In the middle of the table is a candle, tall and white, casting shadows over everything around it, including Fernando.

<!-- bt-block:000075 -->
Fernando stands with his hands behind his back, his suit perfect in a way Lance doesn’t quite grasp after the car-ride. He’s smiling, the lines around his eyes more prominent in this light — or lack there of — but to Lance, he is the single most handsome man on this planet, hell, in this universe.

<!-- bt-block:000076 -->
He steps closer, hesitant not out of anxiety but respect, his lips parted. They are somewhere on a hill, overlooking the city lights from above, and Lance’s breath hitches.

<!-- bt-block:000077 -->
“It’s so pretty,” he says, astonished, as he steps closer to the short stone wall surrounding the garden.

<!-- bt-block:000078 -->
“But still only a fraction as pretty as you,” Fernando tells him, suddenly standing next to him.

<!-- bt-block:000079 -->
Lance looks back at him, eyes wide and cheeks pink. “I should’ve changed,” he says, shaking his head. “I feel like a kid crashing his parents’ date night.”

<!-- bt-block:000080 -->
Fernando chuckles. “You don’t look like a kid,” he says with a soft smile. Looking into Lance’s eyes, Fernando brings out his hand from behind his back, a bouquet of flowers wrapped in dark paper held tight in his palm. “You look pretty.”

<!-- bt-block:000081 -->
With his eyes flicking from the bouquet to Fernando’s face, and back to the bouquet, Lance furrows his eyebrows as he breathes out a confused, “You… you’ve done all this for me. *Why?”*

<!-- bt-block:000082 -->
“Because I love you,” Fernando admits, still holding the flowers, offering them for Lance to take. “And I wanted to show you how much.”

<!-- bt-block:000083 -->
Lance swallows, finally taking the hint to accept the bouquet; and his eyes are no longer *just* wide, but also glossy. *“Oh,”* he exhales, looking down at the flowers in his hands before picking up his gaze slightly to meet Fernando’s eyes. “But I… I didn’t get you anything.”

<!-- bt-block:000084 -->
Fernando nods, smiling. He lifts his hand and touches Lance’s face with his fingertips, before caressing his cheek gently. “But you did,” he says, pulling their faces close together, letting their breathes mingles together. “You got me yourself.”

<!-- bt-block:000085 -->
And with that, he kisses Lance.

<!-- bt-block:000086 -->
It’s not a kiss like in the movies — all synchronized movements, with fireworks and *Take My Breath Away* playing in the background, and neither of them *pops their foot*. It’s so much better.

<!-- bt-block:000087 -->
Fernando’s left hand is on his waist, pulling him close, while his right hand slides from his cheek to the back of his neck, holding him in place.

<!-- bt-block:000088 -->
Closing his eyes, Lance melts into the kiss, aware that the bouquet is getting crushed between their bodies but unwilling to care. He hums into Fernando’s lips, which taste like a delicious mix of something undeniably *Fernando* and chocolate.

<!-- bt-block:000089 -->
When they part, Fernando caresses Lance’s cheek again with something that feels like *thank you* in his eyes. “I’ll get you a vase,” he says, nodding towards the flowers that Lance clutches to his chest. He doesn’t wait for a response before he walks inside the small, stone-brick house.

<!-- bt-block:000090 -->
The second the door *thuds* shut behind him, Lance pulls out his phone.

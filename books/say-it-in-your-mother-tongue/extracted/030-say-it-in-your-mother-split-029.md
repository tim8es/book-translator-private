Chapter 28: summer 2019

Chapter Notes

<!-- bt-block:000003 -->
**This is a Multi-POV ensemble story — chapters may focus on one pairing or move between multiple.**

<!-- bt-block:000004 -->
The words hang between them.

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
They are foreign and urgent and *so very light* they might as well be *kaleidoscope* of butterflies, flying around them in pretty curves.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
Lance breathes out, hoping it comes out *less* shaky than he feels. *It doesn’t*. “*Oh*,” he whispers, his voice tight in his throat. “I don’t… I’m not sure I understand Spanish enough for that.”

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
*Because you couldn’t have said what I think you just said.*

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
With a single nod, Fernando says, “That’s okay, *cariño.”* His smile is unnerving.

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
*Please, tell me that I understood you correctly.*

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
“What did… what does it mean?” Lance asks, his voice even tighter. His heart is beating fast — so fast, in fact, that it could be considered a medical emergency in any other context. He licks his lips. *“Please.”*

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
Fernando doesn’t look at him, his face turned away, his eyes partially closed. “I think you know, *cariño*,” he breathes out quietly, with an uncharacteristic edge to his voice.

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
*I don’t think I do.*

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
“I’m not sure,” Lance lets out. He bites his lower lip, gathering up his courage to speak again. He closes his eyes when he says, “because there is just *no way* that you *actually* said what it sounded like.”

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
Fernando turns at him, making intense eye contact. Without smiling, he breathes out, “And what did it sound like?”

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
*That you’ve been in love with me for years.*

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
“*Uh*,” Lance hesitates, no longer able to meet Fernando’s eyes. When he speaks again, it no longer sounds like a statement, but a question, “like… *uh*, something about being… *enamored*… for years?”

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
With a small nod, Fernando mutters, “Almost.” His voice is gentle, though his eyes are gentler, when he explains, “You *almost* got it, *cariño*.”

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
*Then say it. Please.*

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
“I think you already know what I said,” Fernando states, plain and simple. “You said the same, *no?”*

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
A beat passes between them.

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
Lance lowers his eyes, looking at the floor like it might give him all the answers — or maybe even *just the one*. His cheeks are a deep shade of pink, one that any rose could be jealous of, as he shrugs. “Did I?”

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
*“Quieres que te lo diga directamente?”* Fernando asks, but they both know it is not a question. He lifts his hand and brings it to Lance’s face, gently touching his face. He doesn’t force the Canadian to look at him, but — when Lance meets his eyes again — he holds his gaze gently. “I’m in love with you,” he says simply, like it’s the only logical thing to say, like the whole universe centers this truth as universal. “I’ve been in love with you for… for a long time, *I guess**?* Maybe longer than I should admit.”

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
“Since when?” Lance asks, his voice slightly shaky, almost like he didn’t mean to say it aloud. “I mean—”

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
While shaking his head, Fernando lets out a humorless chuckle. “You won’t like the answer.”

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
Lance furrows his eyebrows. “You… you *can’t* just say that and expect me to just… *not ask*.*”*

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
Without meeting his eyes, Fernando admits something he never told anyone before. *“Bien,* but remember that you wanted to know,” he mutters before exhaling deeply. “I, *uh*, I refused — *you really want me to say it, don’t you?* — I refused to sign with Racing Point. Because of you.”

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
“When?” Lance breathes out.

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
Fernando shakes his head again. “I shouldn’t—”

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
“No, you *have to* tell me now,” Lance grabs Fernando’s wrist, holding him in place. *“When?”*

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
Sighing, Fernando closes his eyes. “Summer of 2019. Thought that if I just… *left*, I could get you out of my head,” he admits with a pained exhale.

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
“That’s,” Lance stutters out, tightening his grip on Fernando’s wrist, “that’s not fair.”

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
“I know.”

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
This time, it’s Lance who shakes his head. He opens his mouth, closes it, then opens it again. “You… you don’t just get to decide to… to make decisions for both of us. Like… we could have had *something* since 2020 and you… *you* just decided to leave F1 instead? It’s just not fair.”

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
“Lance,” Fernando groans, looking anywhere but him, “listen. You were 21, *sí?* Do you know how old I was? Thirty-eight. Thirty. Eight. If I… if I even tried *looking at you*… Lance, I *had to* leave. You were still basically a rookie, and me… I was—”

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
Lance lets Fernando’s hand slip out of his, standing up. “I turned 21 before the 2019 season even ended,” he says, his voice high the same way it always is when he gets angry. “Do you know when *I* fell for you?” he doesn’t give Fernando time to respond, basically shouting, “when I was fifteen. You were still in Ferrari. Since then? It’s been you. *Only* *you*. And you get to call the shots? You get to decide to — *what?* Not tell me how you feel about me until *after* we hook up?” He’s pacing, now, throwing his arms into the air. “And… and… and this is *not fair*,” he whines, barely concealing a sob that escapes him. “You don’t get to decide for me. You don’t… *fuck*,” he groans with tears running down his cheeks for the third time that day. Lance wipes his face with the back of his right hand.

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
Fernando stands up.

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
There is still almost three meters of space between them.

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
“I’m sorry,” he whispers, his voice hoarse like he’s holding back tears as well. “I was scared.”

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
“Do you think I wasn’t?” Lance manages to push out, his voice close to failing. He swallows, and it makes his Adam’s apple bob. “I’m still fucking scared. All the fucking time.”

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
Fernando doesn’t argue, he just stands there, looking at Lance like he’s precious and terrifying at the same time. “I’m sorry. I thought… I thought I was protecting you.”

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
“From what?” Lance barks back, with tears streaming down his cheeks. “My father hates me for being a fag already, so what exactly were you protecting me from? Happiness?”

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
“*Lance*, I didn’t—”

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
“Everybody knew!” Lance shouts back, his voice breaking at the word *knew*. He lets a painful sob escape his lips, and then another. Clasping his hands over his eyes, Lance doesn’t try to fight it anymore. He’s shaking, painfully sobbing as he cries out, “everyone knows I’m in love with you. Don’t tell me *you* didn’t.”

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
Fernando swallows. There are a million things on his tongue he could say, but none of them feel correct. He sighs. “I didn’t… I didn’t think it was *like that*. I thought… *you know how paddock gossip gets.* I just thought you didn’t mean it like that.”

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
Huffing, Lance wipes his face. *“Bullshit.”*

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
“No, *really*,” Fernando insists. He *wants to* come closer — wants to hug Lance and kiss him and make everything *correct* — but he doesn’t. His shoulders hang low, defeated. “I thought,” he tries again, “it was just a *crush*. And that… one day, you’d outgrow me. Or that you *possibly* already did.”

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
Lance shakes his head with a pain-filled sob. *“C'est des conneries,”* he utters under his breath, barely audible.

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
*“Lancito,”* Fernando takes a step towards Lance. His palms are sweating as he thinks of what to say. “I mean it. I was… *terrified*, actually. Like — *dios mío* — you were so young back then. And just… *innocent*,” he breathes out, trying hard not to regret each new word more and more. “It felt… I felt like a creep thinking of you like that. Especially since *everyone* told me you… you were *into me*. Every time we talked, I just kept thinking that you’d… you’d maybe not tell me *no* if I asked you out. And that… that was scary.”

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
Pausing, Fernando waits for a response. It doesn’t come.

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
Lance has his arms wrapped around his torso, holding himself together *just enough* not to fall apart completely. His face is wet, new tears coming in thick drops and rolling down his cheeks and neck. He looks at Fernando with red-rimmed eyes and there’s nothing but pain in them.

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
“I didn’t wanna… I wanted to protect you,” Fernando exhales, no longer able to look at Lance directly. He’s staring at the floor when he continues, “There was… an article. Online. I don’t remember where I found it, or where — *no*, someone sent it to me. Who was it? Probably not Mark, I think. Maybe Nico, though… I’m not really sure. I can still look for the message, I think… *No.* I know who it was. *Sebastian*. He just sent me the link and went, *hey, your boy is in Swiss news*. And yeah, *you were*. There were photos and an interview, I guess? I didn’t read much. I just… there were photos of you. And of Ocon. You were laughing and just… I broke my phone that night. I threw it against the wall because, like… *that* was supposed to be me, you know? *I* was supposed to be making you laugh like that. Not some… *frog-eating imbécil*.”

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
*Is that why you hate Esteban so much?*

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
“I called, *ugh*,” he sighs, “I called Cyril the next day. Cyril Abiteboul. His people at Alpine was trying to, *um*, get me to sign with them for a while. And I did. I signed the contract without thinking. I just… I needed to be *there*. Cyril… I think he knew *something* but he didn’t… *say* anything. When I… when the 2021 season started, I was just… I enjoyed being back, of course. I love driving. But, *uh*, I enjoyed seeing you *more*. I think Ocon figured it out quickly.”

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
“He’s not stupid,” Lance says, his voice hoarse.

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->
Fernando chuckles without meaning to. *“Eh, ya no estoy seguro de eso,”* he exhales, “but yeah. He kept… he told me to stay away. Like he’s your brother scaring away your boyfriends. But I didn’t… I wasn’t going to *do* anything, *sí?* I just wanted to be close. Wanted to be *friends*.”

<!-- bt-block:000056 -->
Lance shakes his head again. “And you thought that was *better?”* he asks, his voice *too close* to breaking.

<!-- bt-block:000057 -->
“I don’t know,” Fernando admits. He’s told himself that it was better for both of them, but deep down… “I really don’t.”

<!-- bt-block:000058 -->
“It wasn’t,” Lance answers his own question. He pauses, wiping his face with the side of his hand, before his heartbeat steadies enough to continue. “You don’t get some *extra points* for suffering quietly. You don’t get to play the *bigger person* now just because you… *what?* Act so *noble* about it?” Looking straight into Fernando’s eyes, Lance says in a low voice, “No matter *how* you might put it, you lied to me. And now you expect me to say, *‘I love you’* back, and everything’s *bonita?”*

<!-- bt-block:000059 -->
“No,” Fernando breathes out, taking another step closer. Less than two meters divide them now. “I don’t expect anything. I know I’m lucky to be here with you.”

<!-- bt-block:000060 -->
Shaking his head, Lance turns on his heel. He closes his eyes, unable to stop the new wave of tears from spilling, sighing. “You are so fucking stupid,” he exhales, his voice low.

<!-- bt-block:000061 -->
Fernando nods. “Yeah,” he agrees, “I am.”

<!-- bt-block:000062 -->
Lance turns back, looking at Fernando with tears in his eyes (and wet traces all over his face). He searches the familiar face with intensity that sends shivers down Fernando’s spine. “Why did you sign with Aston?”

<!-- bt-block:000063 -->
Fernando’s breath hitches. “What?”

<!-- bt-block:000064 -->
“Why did you sign with Aston?” Lance repeats himself. He swallows, loudly, before he’s able to continue. “I mean… if being with me was so… *terrifying*,” he says, “why did you sign with my father? My contract doesn’t expire until *fuck knows when*, so you *had to* know I’d be there. I’d be *your teammate.*”

<!-- bt-block:000065 -->
“I *did* know that, *sí,”* Fernando nods.

<!-- bt-block:000066 -->
Lance also nods. “Then why did you sign it?”

<!-- bt-block:000067 -->
“Because I wanted to,” Fernando admits, quietly. His bravery is gone when he adds, “I was going *loco.* Like when… seeing you with Checo. I knew that either I’d get closer to you, or I’d have to leave again because… I couldn’t do it anymore. And Sebastian kept sending me photos of you — *how you say?* — random photos of you, *candid*. Just you, all the time. I hated him for it. But also… I got to see *you*. Like *real* you. Unguarded. Not knowing anyone is looking. I still have them. All of them,” he licks his lips, taking another step towards Lance. “Your father… he kept asking. My contract was ending anyways and I just… *yeah*, I went with it.”

<!-- bt-block:000068 -->
With only a breath of space separating them, Lance shakes his head again. He sniffles without meaning to. The expression on his face unreadable, he exhales, “You’re an idiot, *Alonso*.”

<!-- bt-block:000069 -->
Fernando nods, thin-lipped and defeated. *“Yeah.”*

<!-- bt-block:000070 -->
“And I am too,” Lance continues, taking the next step. Their chests brush as he does so.

<!-- bt-block:000071 -->
Fernando has to tilt his head up slightly to hold his gaze. He has wrinkles around his eyes, but Lance doesn’t quite see them. All he sees is the same molten amber that he’s looked up to at ten and wished to be, one day, as good of a driver as Fernando.

<!-- bt-block:000072 -->
Lance’s breath catches in his throat as he touches Fernando’s face with his right hand. “Because I’m still in love with you,” he exhales, leaning slightly down to connect their lips in an all-consuming kiss.

<!-- bt-block:000073 -->
Fernando can taste the salt on his lips as he gently touches Lance’s side. He whispers into the kiss, soft, almost broken, *“Sorry.”*

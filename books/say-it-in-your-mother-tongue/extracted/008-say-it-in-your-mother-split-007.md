Chapter 6: kebab, bad luck, and a single shoe

<!-- bt-block:000002 -->
Chapter Notes

<!-- bt-block:000003 -->
**This is a Multi-POV ensemble story — chapters may focus on one pairing or move between multiple.**

<!-- bt-block:000004 -->
Checo’s arm is hanging over the sofa’s back rest.

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
He’s watching intently as Max takes another bite of his kebab, a bit of the sauce dribbling onto his chin. Max looks up, his eyes still red but no longer *too* glossy. “What?” he asks with full mouth.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
Shaking his head, Checo gives him the softest smile. “Nothing,” he breathes out. He swallows hard, averting his eyes elsewhere. “I’m just happy you’re eating, is all.”

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
Max stops as he’s going for another bite. “*Oh*,” he lets out.

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
“I don’t even wanna ask when you last ate,” Checo adds, but it’s not judgmental. There is tenderness in his voice that both of them pretend not to notice.

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
“*Eerlijk waar*,” Max says with full mouth again, “I don’t even know.”

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
Checo’s eyes travel across the room, taking in the sight. Under a tiny radiator that cannot do anything for a room this big, there is a pair of socks, obviously taken off in a hurry and forgotten about. The blanket Max has undeniably been sleeping under is bunched up, half of it on the floor, the rest on the sofa’s arm rest, while his pillow rests behind Checo. The shoes Max was wearing when they last spoke in person are kicked around the room, one is by the front door, the other lays by a cabinet that’s supposed to be a TV stand but currently only houses Max’s keys.

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
“Move in with me,” Checo breathes out without thinking. His eyes go wide when he realizes that he *actually* said it, actually suggested what he only thought about in his wildest dreams.

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
“What?” Max asks, even though they both know he heard it.

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
Not yet daring to look back at his ex-teammate, Checo gestures with his head towards the room. “You don’t really… *live here* anyways.” The breath he takes is heavy, heavier than oxygen is supposed to feel like.

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
Max doesn’t move a muscle. His mouth hangs open, his eyebrows knitting together in surprise.

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
“And this way,” Checo continues after a long time, “I could at least make sure you actually eat something that has vegetables in it.”

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
“This had cabbage and cucumber,” Max offers, but not in the banter-like tone he usually uses when anyone accuses him of eating like a kid left home alone for too long. “I can take care of myself, you know?”

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
Checo’s face snaps to look at Max. “I know,” he says, way too fast for his own liking. “I know you can, Max. I just — *yeah*. I just wanted to offer… but like if you don’t want to, is fine. Just thought — *yeah,* no. I get it. Is okay.”

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
Max looks at his hands — or his kebab — and nods. “I don’t know,” he admits, his words shaky, entirely unsteady and unsure.

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
“No, is okay,” Checo repeats. His face is tight, making him look much older than he actually is, the exhaustion and desperation clouding his eyes. “Just wanted you to know it’s an option, is all.” And with that, Checo stands up. He doesn’t look at Max, not yet, as he walks to pick up Max’s worn hoodie. He folds it, kind of, and places it next to Max’s keys. “I should go.”

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
He finally turns to Max again, smiling at him — but the smile doesn’t reach his eyes anymore. “I’ll keep texting you,” he says plainly. “You know where to find me, if you need me.”

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
*“Or if you want me,*” he doesn’t add.

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
Max just nods, unable to form words.

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
Their eyes meet again, the bond strong but no longer full of expectations.

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
“No goodbye, brings bad luck,” Checo mutters as he opens the door. “Oh, and Max?” he breathes out, not looking at him, “you should shower.”

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
With that, the door *clicks* shut behind him, leaving each of them in their separate universes. Checo exhales deeply, almost like he’s willing tears away, before walking towards the unlit staircase in complete silence.

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
Every step he takes, each passing stair, his resolve to leave the man upstairs behind dissolves more and more.

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
*What if he does something stupid? What if he hurts himself?*

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
*No, he wouldn’t.*

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
*Would he?*

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
*No. No, he wouldn’t.*

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
*But what if —*

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
He only reaches about halfway down from the fifth floor when a loud, “Checo, wait!” stops him in his tracks.

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
Looking up, he sees a blurry mess of blue and black running towards him.

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
Max is only wearing one shoe, clutching the other shoe and the dirty hoodie to his chest. “I, *uh*, I wanna go with you,” he admits. And suddenly, he’s no longer twenty-eight, no longer a four-time world champion. He’s six, holding his *mama*’s hand, begging to go with her and Victoria. He’s eleven, crying at a gas station as his father drives away into the evening, scared and hungry and so angry at himself for not winning. He’s eighteen, scream-crying into his pillow as everyone calls him *Crashstappen* and the one person supposed to make him feel better is the one who yells at him for being a failure. He’s the little boy terrified of his father, and he’s the teenager who promised himself never to cry again, and he’s two seconds away from grabbing Checo’s hand to make him *stay*.

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
“You wanna —”

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
“*Ja*,” Max nods, taking another step closer. “I wanna stay with you. *Kan*… can I?”

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
Checo gives him a reassuring smile. “Of course,” he clasps a hand over his shoulder, “I offered you.”

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
“Okay, *yeah*,” Max nods again, smiling. There’s a faint blush on his cheeks, and a bit of the sauce still on his chin, when he adds, “but we have to go back,” he points up, “I might have locked my keys in there.”

Chapter 14: thirty-three seconds to understand

<!-- bt-block:000002 -->
Chapter Notes

<!-- bt-block:000003 -->
**This is a Multi-POV ensemble story — chapters may focus on one pairing or move between multiple.**

<!-- bt-block:000004 -->
It took less than two weeks for Max to get used to waking up with a set of arms wrapped around his waist.

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
By night three, he frantically searches for Checo’s heat every time he shifts just enough for their bodies to stop being in full-on contact.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
By morning on day seven, Max burrows his face into Checo’s chest with the *cutest* groan of, “Not yet, *please*, just five more minutes.” The *five minutes* last another hour, by which Checo has to get up with an urgent *call* from mother nature to run to the restroom. Max whines for at least twenty minutes afterwards, sulking with a blanket wrapped around his shoulders while Checo makes their morning cups of coffee.

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
Day eleven starts and ends the exact same way — with Checo kissing the top of Max’s head and Max whispering something *terrifyingly* close to, “*Mine*,” into the steady rhythm of Checo’s heart.

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
The morning of day nineteen starts the way all of their mornings do, with Max content and Checo needing to pee but refusing to move lest he wakes up the *precious* boy in his embrace.

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
Max’s phone, though, doesn’t seem to care as much.

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
With the first notes of his ringtone, Max just slaps his hand around the bed blindly. Failing to silence the most annoying piece of music ever produced, he opens his eyes. *“Wat is er?”* he asks Checo, as if he had a way of *magically* knowing. “Oh, *right*,” he says, reaching for his phone charging on *his* nightstand. “*Ja*?” he speaks into the microphone without greeting whoever is on the other side. “Yeah. I’m fine. Yeah, *really*. No. Already? That’s cool. Yeah. Okay. So not on the— *yeah*. No problem. I’ll be there. Yeah. See ya’. Bye.” He sets the phone display down onto the nightstand, sighing before he lies back down. Max pulls the blanket over himself again, his eyes already closed.

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
“Should I bother asking, or…?”

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
Max yawns as he sits back up. “Horner,” is all he says, rubbing the sleepiness from his eyes.

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
Checo gives him a questioning *look*. “Is he coming for lunch? Or like, what’s up?”

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
“No,” Max says like it’s the most obvious thing ever, “he wants me to go to the factory. Tomorrow. *Sim work*.”

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
Nodding, Checo gets out of bed. “Well, it *is* that time again, I guess,” he states as he opens the door of his wardrobe, pulling out a T-shirt that might have originally belonged to Max. He pulls it on before grabbing another one and throwing it to Max. “It’s gonna be weird not having you around,” he adds before walking out of *their* bedroom.

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
It takes Max and his brain exactly thirty-three seconds to understand what Checo just said.

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
“Wait,” Max breathes out as he throws on (definitely Checo’s) shirt and jumps from bed. “What do you mean?”

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
Checo walks out of the bathroom with a quiet yawn. “*Hm*?” he hums towards Max.

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
“What do you mean?”

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
“*Qué*,” Checo raises one eyebrow. He looks Max over, realizing that his confusion is entirely *too* *real*. “You’re going to Milton Keynes, *no*?”

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
Max nods. “Yeah, *tomorrow*.”

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
“Yeah,” Checo walks slowly into the kitchen, turning his head to add, “which mean you won’t be *here*.”

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
“Possibly, yeah,” Max agrees as he follows him, claiming his usual spot by the fridge — where he waits every morning for Checo to ask for milk. It’s a routine they’ve developed pretty fast (the first morning after they started *sleeping together*) and in the almost three weeks since managed not to break it a single morning.

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
Checo turns on the coffee machine that Max bought for *no reason* and stares at it intently until it makes a soft *beep*, at which he turns to look at Max again. “No, not *possibly*,” he groans. “Max, you won’t be driving there and back every day.”

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
“*Every* day?”

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
“Or are you expecting just one day sim work?”

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
Max furrows his eyebrows like he’s deciphering a coded message, when it clicks for him. “*Oh*,” he breathes out quietly, stumbling back until his back touches the fridge door. Leaning on it, he looks at Checo.

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
“Yeah.”

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
The flat goes quiet.

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
Max slides down the fridge door, dragging his knees into a tight hug when his butt touches the floor. “*But*,” he starts without knowing where to take the sentence next. “I could… ask to do remote—”

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
“And racing? How will you do racing?” Checo asks, not meaning to make his words sound *that* sharp. “Also remotely?”

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
Max sighs in response. There is no sign of anger in his voice, *just* resignation. “I thought,” he starts, his voice fragile and barely audible, “I thought you’d be going too. To the races. With me, I mean.”

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
“Do you want me to?”

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
“Well,” Max stares at a spot in front of his feet, “I thought maybe—”

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
“No, Max. No *thinking*. No *maybe*. Do you want me to?”

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
Max doesn’t trust himself to look up, to meet Checo’s eyes. He rests his chin between his knees as he admits, “*Yeah*.”

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
Checo looks down at him — only physically, though. In his chest, deep behind his ribs, the muscle Max has been holding in his palm every night starts beating fast, faster than it probably should, faster than it ever has for any of Checo’s teammates who weren’t Max. He descends to his knees, holding out one hand as he kneels like he’s letting a stray cat sniff it before it allows him to provide pets.

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
Max doesn’t sniff his hand, but he does lift his head, allowing Checo to caress his cheek. He leans into the touch with closed eyes, not trusting himself to meet Checo’s gaze. “I’m… *worried*. About sleeping, you know?” he breathes out, his voice shaky. He finally opens his eyes, and they are entirely too wide as he admits, “Without you.”

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
With the softest of smiles, Checo admits, “I don’t think I could fall asleep without you either, *mi mijo*.”

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
“Then let’s not,” Max suggests, his voice quiet but his eyes brave. “We… you can come with me. *Everywhere*.”

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
The hand on Max’s cheek stills, holding his face in place. “I *can* come with you,” Checo says, “if you let me.”

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
Their eyes meet, *finally*, and in that moment they both know. Neither of them is talking about sleeping together anymore.

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
The coffee machine’s *beep-beep-beep* breaks the spell around them, making both of their heads snap towards the sudden sound.

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
Checo is the first to laugh. “I think it’s done,” he says, letting his hand drag slowly away from Max’s face. Standing up, he picks up the mug Max claimed as his own and asks, “How do you want yours today?”

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
**=** **♡** **=**

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
“Could you explain to me why Geneva?”

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
Gabriel looks up from his text conversation with his mum. “What?” he asks, stretching his legs for the sixth time in the last fifteen minutes.

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
“Geneva,” Nico repeats, gripping the steering wheel tightly. They are stuck in traffic trying to get on the highway, and his body is way too aware of the person sitting next to him. “There’s an airport in Zürich. It’d take us less than thirty minutes from there. Forty with traffic.”

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
“I don’t know,” Gabriel shrugs. “Like someone bought me these tickets, not much I could do about it.”

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
*Someone bought him a layover flight.*

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
Nico shakes his head. “Haas would never,” he mutters under his breath, hoping Gabriel doesn’t hear it. “Did you sleep?”

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
Yawning, Gabriel shakes his head. “No, a *bebê* kept screaming. And from Brussels, I was too, *uh*, nervous? Was worried I’d somehow miss it.”

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
*I’d never leave without you.*

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
Humming, Nico reaches his hand out to turn on the car radio. “I have some classical music,” he states coldly — or at least that’s what he tries to go for. “You can sleep, if you want.”

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->
Gabriel grabs his wrist before his fingers manage to press the power button. “No,” he protests. He lets go of Nico’s hand, staring at him with big eyes. “I wanna be with you. Awake.”

<!-- bt-block:000056 -->
Nico doesn’t stop himself in time, and so he breathes out a restrained, “Why?”

<!-- bt-block:000057 -->
Frowning at the question like it doesn’t make sense, Gabriel shrugs again. “I missed you,” he says honestly. “If I fall asleep, you’ll just… *drive*. And then we’ll be *there* and you won’t— *oi*, I mean, *we* won’t have time to just… *hang out*.” He looks out of the window, lowering his voice just enough, “I like hanging out with you, *grandpa*.”

<!-- bt-block:000058 -->
*Why?*

<!-- bt-block:000059 -->
*He should hate me. He should dislike spending time with me. He should — why doesn’t he?*

<!-- bt-block:000060 -->
“But I can try,” Gabriel continues when Nico doesn’t react. “I know I’m not your favorite teammate. Is okay,” he says as he leans his head on the window, the same way children do on long road trips. There’s a pillow in his suitcase exactly for this purpose, but Gabriel doesn’t care. He can sleep without it. He *should* sleep. Tomorrow, they’ll need him to focus on his simulator training, and getting enough sleep is important. “If you want to switch, you can *awake* me.”

<!-- bt-block:000061 -->
*Say something. He needs you to say something.*

<!-- bt-block:000062 -->
“Why do you think that?”

<!-- bt-block:000063 -->
*Not that. You could have asked anything else. Why would you ask him this, you imbecile?*

<!-- bt-block:000064 -->
“Is not hard to see,” Gabriel shrugs without looking at Nico. He hides his hands into the sleeves of his hoodie before hugging himself. “But is really okay. I’m from Brazil, you’re from Germany. We’re too *diferente.*”

<!-- bt-block:000065 -->
“That’s bullshit,” Nico exhales sharply. He doesn’t look away from the traffic in front of him as he admits, “That’s not… *why*.”

<!-- bt-block:000066 -->
Gabriel huffs against the window, like he wants to cry and laugh at the same time, “*Sure*.”

<!-- bt-block:000067 -->
The car stops again.

<!-- bt-block:000068 -->
*He thinks you hate him. Good fucking job protecting him, you cretin.*

<!-- bt-block:000069 -->
Nico looks around the Volkswagen in front of them to check how bad the jam is before turning to look at Gabriel. His eyes are closed, his face angled slightly right so Nico doesn’t see him pout, and his arms are wrapped around him tighter than before. Nico sighs. He reaches over the center console, pulling on Gabriel’s sleeve to get his attention. “Next time, look at me when you say stuff like that,” he mutters before letting go of the fabric of Gabriel’s hoodie.

<!-- bt-block:000070 -->
“*Hm*,” Gabriel hums back. “I’ll keep that in mind.”

<!-- bt-block:000071 -->
As the cars start moving again, Nico feels his grip tighten on the steering wheel. He looks, out of the corner of his eye, towards Gabriel who is still pulled away from him. “I have a blanket,” he says, instantly regretting his decision to speak. Sighing, he adds, “It’s under your seat, if you want it.”

<!-- bt-block:000072 -->
“Not cold,” Gabriel bites back without moving. He looks over at Nico, the warmth and joy *gone* from his face, and says, “I don’t want some spare blanket from the company car.”

<!-- bt-block:000073 -->
Coughing, Nico tries to hide his laughter.

<!-- bt-block:000074 -->
*“Que?”*

<!-- bt-block:000075 -->
“Nothing,” Nico lies, clearing his throat. “Just… *this* is my car.”

<!-- bt-block:000076 -->
“*Huh*?” Gabriel’s eyebrows knit together as he tries to grasp those words. “Whatcha mean *your car*?”

<!-- bt-block:000077 -->
“I mean,” Nico glances at him before looking back at the road, “*yeah*. This is my car.” He shrugs, effortlessly overtaking the car in front of them when the road finally opens up enough. He glances at Gabriel again. “I’m not a monster. I’d not make you ride in the horrible thing we have as a company car.”

<!-- bt-block:000078 -->
“*Oh*,” Gabriel exhales shakily, letting his arms loosen around him a little. He’s no longer turned completely away when he asks, “Doesn’t *Audi* own Sauber?”

<!-- bt-block:000079 -->
“Technically,” Nico nods. “But don’t think I got RSQ8 on my roster just for wearing some green and knowing how to smile well in photos.”

<!-- bt-block:000080 -->
A blanket of silence falls over them, the only thing keeping it from being noiseless is the engine purring in front of them. Trying not to get noticed, Gabriel looks over at Nico, biting his lower lip in the process. His eyes are big and bright, and there is something in them that borders on adoration and admiration.

<!-- bt-block:000081 -->
*Why does he look at me like that?*

<!-- bt-block:000082 -->
*I should say something.*

<!-- bt-block:000083 -->
*Think, Hülkenberg, think. Du bist ein Professional, this shouldn’t be any different from giving Daniel a ride to the airport two days into being teammates.*

<!-- bt-block:000084 -->
“Do you want to listen to music? There should be an AUX cable in the glovebox,” Nico mentions casually, as if he didn’t buy it that morning just to offer it for Gabriel to use. He can see Gabriel reach into the compartment and take out the cable, from which Nico masterfully removed all signs of being brand new. Nico chuckles before speaking again. “I only listen to Karl Gott when I drive.”

<!-- bt-block:000085 -->
“Who’s that?” Gabriel asks as he connects his phone with the car’s radio. “And don’t say *a singer*.”

<!-- bt-block:000086 -->
“He *is* a singer, though,” Nico responds, before immediately correcting himself, “*was*. I think he died a while ago.”

<!-- bt-block:000087 -->
Gabriel lets out a chuckle. “So you *only* listen to some dead German guy while you drive. Nothing else? No — what do *old guys* even listen to? — Depeche Mode? No Enya? No Eminem?”

<!-- bt-block:000088 -->
“I will turn this car around and drive you back to the airport if you don’t stop,” Nico frowns at him, making sure to play it up enough not to seem serious. “And he’s Czech, thank you very much.”

<!-- bt-block:000089 -->
“Well, *lemme* show you some quality music then,” Gabriel pokes his arm as he turns on the first song. “And you better like it, *grandpa*.”

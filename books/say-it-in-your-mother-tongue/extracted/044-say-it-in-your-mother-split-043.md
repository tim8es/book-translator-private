On Thursday, however, it *all* happens a little differently.

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
Nico wakes up as always, entirely too early and with a groan that would put any retired man to shame. The second he gets up, he’s making coffee — something you *could* call an iced americano, but Nico would prefer you wouldn’t — shooting it back like a shot of vodka on his birthday before jumping into the shower.

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
Once clean and wet, without really bothering to dry himself, he pats down his bed, searching for his phone. It’s under his pillow, screen down, with only thirty percent of battery remaining. Nico rolls his eyes as the device struggles to recognize his face, forcing him to instead type in his pin code, and for the first time in weeks, he doesn’t immediately check his emails. He opens his conversation with *Gabi* — well, technically, it already opened the second his phone unlocked given that Nico fell asleep while chatting with his Brazilian teammate.

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
He drops his phone onto bed again and walks swiftly to his wardrobe, throwing on matching boxers and socks, both with logos of a company that sponsored him two seasons ago, before he pulls out his comfiest skinny jeans that he knows *Gabi* would make fun of him for and a plain gray T-shirt. To finish his *outfit,* he puts on a pair of black sneakers which he rarely wears.

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
With only his phone, wallet and two sets of keys, Nico leaves the apartment, taking stairs two at a time to save time. Once in the underground garage, Nico points his car keys at his Audi and unlocks it, throwing the wallet on the passenger seat. He plugs his phone in and lets the maps tell him where to go.

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
Once buckled in and ready to go, Nico sighs and begrudgingly opens Spotify in his car’s system. On Tuesday, *Gabi* walked him through setting it up step-by-step, unwilling to let go until Nico sends him a video of one of the playlists *Gabi* created for Nico playing in the background. He turns on the one titled 🎵*music i should know*🎧 with an eye roll as *Everywhere I Go* by Hollywood Undead starts playing.

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
“I know this one,” he protests but doesn’t turn it off or skip it as he puts the car into drive and pulls out of his parking space.

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
The very first time his phone’s GPS commands him to take a turn, he disobeys it, continuing straight instead. “This is faster,” he tells his phone, moving through back-roads and narrows one-way streets towards the highway.

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
Unsurprisingly, he arrives at the entry ramp with three minutes of advance, pulling into traffic with ease and confidence only achievable by years and years of practice. He speeds to the speed limit before overtaking the three *slower* cars in front of him, moving between vehicles effortlessly.

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
The sign above the highway tells him he’s 85 kilometers away from the first Braunschweig exit and Nico just nods at it as if it could see him.

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
His car moves through traffic like knife through soft butter, holding the speed limit *most* of the time, as the music still continues playing — with Juice WRLD’s *Lucid Dreams* turning into *The Enemy Between My Ears* by Sons of Charlie right as he overtakes a white Porsche with cracked windshield.

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
After being on the road for over two hours, Nico stops at a gas station to use their restroom, and buy a plastic cup of bland automat coffee. He scarfs down a snickers bar, buys a can of sugar-free energy drink from a brand he’s never heard of before, and returns to car just in time for a new message’s *ding* to steal his attention.

With a smile he can’t explain away as *just* friendly, Nico pulls out of the parking lot, following traffic signs telling him which way to drive to continue with his journey towards Dortmund. He yawns, cursing the coffee under his breath for not spelling away the tiredness from his eyes, and enters the highway again.

<!-- bt-block:000071 -->
The sign above ground tells him Dortmund is 185 kilometers away right as the first notes of *I WANNA BE YOUR SLAVE* by Måneskin come from the speakers. The moment Nico recognizes the song, he lets out a soft chuckle and speeds up a *little* over the speed limit to overtake a dozen or so cars *slowing* him down.

<!-- bt-block:000072 -->
The exit for Dortmund marks five hundred kilometers from Nico’s apartment, meaning that he’s been on the road for almost three and a half hours now. He doesn’t exit the highway, instead continues towards Moers and the border with the Netherlands that’s coming in one hundred kilometers.

<!-- bt-block:000073 -->
Once he enters the Netherlands, he doesn’t stay for long, moving past Venlo and Eindhoven just to enter the Kingdom of Belgium. The highway takes him all the way to Antwerpen, but instead of exiting there, he follows the signs leading to Brussel.

<!-- bt-block:000074 -->
From Joost’s *Europapa,* Spotify jumps into *Running Up That Hill* by Kate Bush, a song Nico wouldn’t expect *Gabi* to even know, unaware of just how many times the song plays in the TV show Stranger Things.

<!-- bt-block:000075 -->
His phone tells him that only about 40 kilometers remain and Nico lets out a content sigh.

<!-- bt-block:000076 -->
*Finally*.

<!-- bt-block:000077 -->
With his eyes *locked* onto his target, Nico hurries to park his car at the paid parking lot, paying for two hours of stay before he all but sprints inside the airport foyer.

<!-- bt-block:000078 -->
Searching for an *information* stand, he scans the place, actually running to pee before returning to speak with one of the workers.

<!-- bt-block:000079 -->
“Hello, how can I assist you today?” a woman in deep blue suit asks him, her accent heavy but her speech clear.

<!-- bt-block:000080 -->
“Hi, yes, hello, *um*, my boyfriend forgot his jacket on *this* flight from Sao Paolo,” he tells the woman, showing her his phone screen with the flight information. “It’s been a few weeks now, though. Is there a possibility to get it back?”

<!-- bt-block:000081 -->
She nods at him, writing something on her keyboard, before picking up the landline on her right and pressing a few numbers into the pad. She says something in Dutch, nods, says something else, nods again, and hangs up. *“Ja, so* you need to go with this man,” she tells Nico, pointing at a guy walking their way, “and he will take you to lost and found, okay?”

<!-- bt-block:000082 -->
*“Ja, danke schön,”* Nico tells her with a smile.

<!-- bt-block:000083 -->
She nods before looking away from him, already talking with the next *customer*.

<!-- bt-block:000084 -->
They walk through a hallway, or three, until the man stops in front of a locked door. “What we looking for?” he asks.

<!-- bt-block:000085 -->
Nico describes the jacket to him and adds, “well, it’s been like two or three weeks since he forgot it on the plane. Do you think it’ll be here?”

<!-- bt-block:000086 -->
Turning to look at Nico, the man raises one eyebrow as he unlocks the door. “I know where it is, even. I have a system.”

<!-- bt-block:000087 -->
And he really does, handing Nico the jacket in less than three minutes — of which most time was not spent searching, but moving stuff around so he could reach for the jacket.

<!-- bt-block:000088 -->
“Thank you so much,” Nico tells him, pulling out a five-euro banknote to offer as some sort of tip. “That was amazing.”

<!-- bt-block:000089 -->
The man shakes his head, refusing to take the money. “It’s okay,” he says, “we should go back now. You have your jacket and I have work to do.”

<!-- bt-block:000090 -->
Nico nods, noting mentally that the man is surely autistic, before they start walking back towards the front desk, jacket in one hand, phone in the other. He has five new messages from *Gabi.*

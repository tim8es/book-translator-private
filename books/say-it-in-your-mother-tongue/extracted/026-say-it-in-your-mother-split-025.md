Chapter 24: pinky promise

Chapter Notes

<!-- bt-block:000003 -->
**This is a Multi-POV ensemble story — chapters may focus on one pairing or move between multiple.**

<!-- bt-block:000004 -->
The website refuses to load.

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
Lance sighs, shaking his phone as if that could help somehow, and to his surprise, the site actually starts loading up. He scrolls through the list, making mental notes about the words and phrases he finds most useful. Number 241 catches his attention.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
*Mon coeur bat pour toi.* My heart beats for you.

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
He chuckles; not at the words themselves, or the implication that this is considered to be one of the 500 most important words and phrases in the French language. He chuckles simply because it’s true.

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
Nodding, he adds the phrase to *his* list.

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
It’s mental, really, that he has a crush on his coworker. That he hooked up with. And whom he sort of lives with, at this point. Lance shakes his head. It’s all so fucking stupid and embarrassing and amazing all at once.

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
*Mon cœur ne bat que pour toi.*

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
The tiny voice in his head corrects the phrase. His heart doesn’t *just* beat for Fernando. It beats *only* for Fernando.

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
Lance looks over at Fernando.

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
He’s sitting at the table, hunched over his laptop with a paper before him, scribbling down notes like he has a school project due tomorrow. His tongue peeks from his mouth as Fernando licks his lips, before he puts his pen into his mouth to bite it. There’s something so very adorable about him that Lance wants to both laugh and hide his face somewhere.

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
The stubborn ache that blooms in the middle of his chest dulls when Fernando looks up from the screen, a soft smile on his lips. He’s looking into Lance’s eyes, but he’s staring right into his soul. “I think I have it,” he says without looking away.

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
Lance nods. “Me too,” he breathes out.

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
Fernando stands up from the kitchen table, holding the sheet of printer paper in his hand, and walks towards the couch Lance’s sitting on. “You didn’t write anything down,” Fernando says, and it is not a question.

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
Lance answers it anyway. “No,” he shakes his head a little. “I keep the list here,” he points to his temple with a smile.

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
“Ah, young mind,” Fernando breathes out, chuckling. “Where’s the time when I could remember *everything*.”

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
*As long as you don’t forget me, it doesn't matter.*

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
“You still remember everything,” Lance says instead. He pats the couch, waiting for Fernando to sit down before he adds, honest and a little blushing, “I don’t think you know how to forget stuff.”

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
Smiling at Lance, Fernando puts a hand on his cheek. He caresses the soft skin under his eye gently, with so much affection it physically pains Lance. His voice is strained *just enough* when he says, “You flatter me too much, *cariño mío.”*

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
Lance swallows, suddenly too aware of the dryness of his mouth. “I, *uh*, I know what *that* means.”

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
*“Cariño mío*?” Fernando asks, pulling his hand back. He watches as Lance nods with a smile. “Good. And is… is it okay?”

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
Lance nods again. “*Oui.*”

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
“So,” Fernando breathes out, no longer meeting Lance’s eyes, “which one of us—”

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
“You can,” Lance doesn’t let him finish. “You wrote it down.”

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
Fernando raises his eyebrows. “Sure,” he nods once, “but paper lasts longer than thoughts, no?”

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
Shaking his head, Lance says, “I won’t forget. Pinky promise.”

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
Nodding, Fernando looks over his notes. “I went for… *practical,”* there’s a slight hesitation in his voice when he adds a breathy, *“mostly.* You probably know a lot of it,” he admits, his voice suddenly uncharacteristically small. *“Por favor. Gracias. Buenos días.”*

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
“Please, thank you, good morning,” Lance translates, genuinely proud of himself. “I even know how to say *good night.”*

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
“Go for it, *cariño,”* Fernando encourages him, a smile still strong on his lips.

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
*“Buenas noches,* no?”

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
Fernando reaches out, petting Lance’s hair in a way Lance images he’d pet a dog for performing a trick correctly. “Smart boy,” he says with his hand still in Lance’s hair. “Maybe you know *all* of these. You should take a look.” He hands the paper to Lance, finally letting his hand drop back into his lap.

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
The phrases all sound familiar to Lance. *Vamos. Lo siento. Cómo estás? Muy bien. Buen provecho.* As he goes down the list, nodding and mouthing the words, he reaches one he doesn’t know.

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
“What does this one mean?” he asks, his voice quiet but sure. His index finger is digging into the paper right next to the phrase.

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
“That’s, *oh*,” Fernando starts, biting his lower lip when he realizes what sentence Lance means. “*Uh*, that’s… *Yo tengo un novio.* It means, *uh,”* he doesn’t meet Lance’s eyes as he stutters out, “It means *‘I have a boyfriend’.”*

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
*Oh*.

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
“Why… why *that* one?” Lance asks when his brain finally turns back on. His heart is beating fast, he can feel — and *hear* — the blood rushing through his body.

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
Fernando still doesn’t look directly at him when he breathes out, “It’s useful.”

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
“Useful?” Lance repeats, his voice shaky, and his hands even shakier.

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
*“Sí,”* Fernando nods once. He finally looks at Lance when he says, “You should know how to say it. You know… in case you need it.”

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
“Need it?” Lance asks, not daring to look at Fernando out of shyness or maybe even fear. “Need it for what?”

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
Fernando lowers his eyes — *accidentally* looking at Lance’s lips as he does so — averting his gaze somewhere (*anywhere*) else before he allows himself to answer. His accent is strong when he admits, “I don’t know.” His eyes travel across the room, searching for anything to focus on, when he suggests, in the softest voice, “The future, *maybe?”*

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
“*Okay*,” Lance says, biting his lip. He takes the paper back, rereading the words over and over again. “Could you… could you say it again?”

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
With closed eyes, Fernando nods. *“Sí.* Is like, *uh*, soft. Like this: *Yo tengo un novio*.”

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
Lance nods, even if he knows Fernando isn’t looking at him. *“Yo tengo un novio,”* he repeats, gentle and oh so careful, as if saying it wrong would make it ruin *everything*. He swallows loudly, ignoring the way his fingers tremble, as he tries to say it again. *“Yo tengo un novio.”*

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
Fernando exhales all at once.

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
“Did I…” Lance starts, his eyes big and full of something dangerously close to expectation, “does it sound *right?”*

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
Giving the softest nod, Fernando breathes out like the air got knocked out of his lungs. “Yeah,” he whispers, moving to look at Lance again. Their eyes meet, and were it a movie, fireworks would certainly explode in the background. Fernando says, softer now, “It sounds *very right.”*

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
The gray smoke rises to the gray sky.

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
Nico exhales, watching as the ash falls down after tapping the cigarette on the metal rail. It’s after three, he knows that as surely as he knows there is a nose between his eyes. He should be sleeping. Should be unconscious, tucked under a blanket, way too close to the *boy* he cannot get out of his head. He should be enjoying the feeling of sharing a bed with him the only way he will ever be allowed to.

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
He puts the cigarette between his lips, inhaling deeply into his lungs. His chest aches, partially from the cold air around him, but mainly from the weight of *everything*.

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
Not for the first time, Nico considers *actually* jumping. It’d be so easy. All it takes is climbing over the short railing and letting go. It’d be over in a few seconds, maybe minutes if he were to be extremely unlucky. He breathes out, his breath full of cigarette smoke.

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->
“You shouldn’t smoke,” a voice — one that he really doesn’t want to hear — comes from somewhere behind him.

<!-- bt-block:000056 -->
Nico nods as he takes a drag. “You should be sleeping,” he says, ignoring the rising need to turn around to look at Gabriel.

<!-- bt-block:000057 -->
“So should you,” Gabriel tells him as he stands next to him, his elbows on the railing, back slightly bent. He’s staring forward — at something before them, or maybe nowhere at all.

<!-- bt-block:000058 -->
“That’s different,” Nico objects, but sounds weak even to his ears. The cigarette burns out between his fingers, and he flicks the butt away, before reaching into his pocket for a new one. He lights it, inhaling deeply.

<!-- bt-block:000059 -->
Nico sees, out of the corner of his eye, how Gabriel shakes his head. Gabriel reaches to Nico’s mouth, snatching the cigarette away from him without as much as a sound. He holds it, too skillful for it to be his first time, to his lips and inhales, closing his eyes as the smoke hits the back of his throat. He huffs out a cloud of smoky breath before he returns the cigarette back between Nico’s lips. “Smoking can kill you, *old man.”*

<!-- bt-block:000060 -->
*So could your presence.*

<!-- bt-block:000061 -->
“Something *has to* one day,” Nico says instead, trying hard not to think about the fact that Gabriel’s mouth touched the same filter that’s currently between his lips.

<!-- bt-block:000062 -->
Gabriel nods. He doesn’t say another word, leaning closer to Nico and stealing the cigarette again. He takes a long drag, breathing out into Nico’s face as he returns the cigarette. “I’m going back to bed,” he tells Nico, already moving away from the rails. He’s by the door when he speaks again, “and Nico?”

<!-- bt-block:000063 -->
The German turns to look at him. *“Ja?”*

<!-- bt-block:000064 -->
“I’ll wait for you,” Gabriel says. His eyes are shiny — Nico can see it all the way from across the room — reflecting stars and a small fire-like light from the end of Nico’s cigarette. He gives Nico a soft, way too gentle smile. “As long as you’ll need me to.”

<!-- bt-block:000065 -->
Nico doesn’t have time to ask what Gabriel means, the *boy* is already gone when he parts his lips to *finally* speak. His hands are shaking as he puts out the cigarette, throwing the butt over the rails, and before he can stop himself, he’s moving. Step by step, he follows in Gabriel’s direction.

<!-- bt-block:000066 -->
He stops at the door, with one hand on the handle, and he sighs. Hesitating, he leans his forehead on the door. The wood is cold under his skin as he lets out a shaky breath.

<!-- bt-block:000067 -->
*Why can’t you just tell me to fuck off?*

<!-- bt-block:000068 -->
Nico groans, quiet and long, only barely stopping himself from ramming his head against the door.

<!-- bt-block:000069 -->
*Why can’t you just hate my guts the same way I do?*

<!-- bt-block:000070 -->
He pushes the handle, opening the door with a *creak* so loud Gabriel would have to be deaf not to notice it. He steps into the room, shutting the door behind him with a *bang*, and moves to the bed, taking off his jeans before sitting down on the mattress. He pulls his sweater over his head, dropping it onto the floor right next to his discarded pants. With a quiet sigh, he lies down, dragging the blanket over his body, making sure to stay far away from Gabriel’s still form. Nico cannot help but look at the *boy*, the way his curls rest on his pillow, the way his back moves ever so slightly with each breath.

<!-- bt-block:000071 -->
And if he was a mad man, he’d swear he just felt Gabriel scoot ever so slightly closer to him.

<!-- bt-block:000072 -->
*How can you not see it, Gabriel?*

<!-- bt-block:000073 -->
Nico closes his eyes. Not to rest them, not to try and sleep, but to *stop looking.* He cannot afford to look *more*, to feel the warmth radiating from Gabriel, to smell the shower gel with Portuguese name all over his pillow (and himself). He cannot afford to fall even deeper into the hole he dug for himself with Gabriel standing *right there*, handing him the shovel.

<!-- bt-block:000074 -->
*Why can’t you see how madly in love I am with you?*

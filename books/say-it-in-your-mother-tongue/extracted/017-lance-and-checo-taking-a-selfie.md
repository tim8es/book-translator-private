Chapter 15: this one suits you better

<!-- bt-block:000002 -->
Chapter Notes

<!-- bt-block:000003 -->
**This is a Multi-POV ensemble story — chapters may focus on one pairing or move between multiple.**

<!-- bt-block:000004 -->
Christian runs a hand over his jacket before reaching for his thermal mug and taking a gulp of his coffee. “The older I get, the worse I do with these early mornings,” he groans as he wipes a drop of coffee from the corner of his mouth.

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
Not looking up from his phone, Liam mumbles something in response. It’s a well-known fact that he does even worse this early in the day than anyone else around this office. “*T’feels* like I slept like two hours. Why are we doing this so fucking early? It’s like midnight still, no?”

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
“Fuck if I know,” Christian shrugs. “I don’t set these up, I just come here.”

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
A beat of silence passes as Christian looks out of the window of his office, overlooking the parking lot.

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
A dark gray car, a shade that could pass for silver only if you’re severely colorblind, stops in the spot reserved exclusively for Max. A thought about the person’s inability to read the giant *Reserved for Max Verstappen* pops in Christian’s mind. “For fuck’s sake,” he mutters under his breath before he sees the driver’s door open.

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
It all happens fast, so fast actually that he doesn’t realize what’s going on at first. The door opens and out steps Sergio *fucking* Pérez, nonchalant and sure of every step he takes, as he walks around the front of the car and to the passenger’s door, which he opens. He stands there, hand on the door, as Max climbs out of the car. They pause for a moment, just looking at each other, before Sergio shuts the door and nods towards the building.

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
“Why did I just see Checo giving Max a ride here?”

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
Liam, finally getting through the longest email from his grandma, looks up with confusion written across his face. “How would I know?” he shrugs. “I’ve only ever spoken like four and a half sentences with him. And two of those were, ‘*Have you seen Max?*’”

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
Christian pretends to cough as he tries to hide his laughter. “Okay,” he says before taking another sip of his coffee. He can still see the two walking towards the office building. Max bumps into Checo’s shoulder, smiling from ear to ear. There is something soft about them, the way Checo looks at Max like he hung the moon and Max smiling back like Checo’s the center of his universe. He’s never seen either of them this relaxed.

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
*Eh, whatever. At least he’s not slamming doors again*.

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
The phone on his desk buzzes, forcing Christian to move from his spot by the window. “Horner here, you rang?”

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
“Pérez is here with Verstappen, do I let both of them in?” the receptionist asks quietly, almost like he’s whispering into the phone.

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
“Yeah, let them in. Give Pérez a guest pass, I guess.”

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
“Are we gonna be having a threesome?” Liam asks casually, only realizing a second too late what he just said. His face goes red in embarrassment, before he adds quickly, “Threeway? Trio. Are we gonna drive in trio instead of duo?”

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
Christian laughs. “I think Max just adopted an emotional support driver,” he suggests casually, like it’s the most normal thing ever. Sitting down, he signs into his work laptop. It *might* actually be the most normal thing ever, when he thinks about it.

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
“Oh, we can have those? Should’ve brought Jack with me, then,” Liam chuckles as he opens another email, this time from his Red Bull PR handler. “Or is this a Max exclusive thing?”

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
“Don’t push your luck, boy,” Christian shoots back without looking at him. “But don’t worry, I’ll tell you when it’s *bring your boyfriend to work* day.”

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
“Will you be bringing Toto, then?”

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
“What did I say about pushing your luck?”

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
The door swings open with a *bang*.

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
“Why does everyone know about my *very recent* and *very private* break up?”

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
Christian looks up at Max with an annoyed expression. “Good morning to you too, Max,” the words are dripping with irony. “It is good to see you too.”

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
“Shove it,” Max utters, his tone harsher than intended. “*Why* does the receptionist, that I’ve never seen before, *know*? She literally told me she’s *sorry for my loss*.” He makes air quotes with his fingers as he speaks.

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
“No, she said she’s sorry and hopes you’re doing alright,” Checo corrects him as he puts a hand on Max’s shoulder. “Good morning, Christian. It’s good to see you,” he adds, humoring his former boss a little.

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
“*Likewise*,” Christian nods towards him. “Missed us already?”

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
Liam yawns loudly, making the three men look at him. “What?” he asks before yawning again. “It’s too early.”

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
“It’s eight,” Checo says matter-of-factly. “I’ve been up since five, I think.”

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
“Four forty,” Max nods. “Woke me up, this guy.”

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
Christian opens his mouth to ask but decides against it. *Not worth the awkward conversation.* He can hear Liam yawn again. “You should have coffee or something,” he tells Liam, nodding as he speaks. “Do you know where the kitchen is? Checo could show you.”

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
Showing off his *guest pass,* Checo says, “I’m a guest, *actually*. I don’t—”

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
“I need to talk with Max,” Christian states, “and it’s private topic of discussion. So you two, *out*,” he points to the door with his thumb. “In the meantime, Mr. Pérez, could you show Mr. Lawson where the kitchen is so he could have his coffee and not fall asleep in my bloody office?”

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
Checo squeezes Max’s shoulder lightly, as if asking if it’s okay for him to leave. Max nods, once, and Checo sighs, “Let’s go. Since they don’t want us here.”

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
Yawning again, Liam gets up from his chair and follows the ex-Red Bull driver into the hallway, dragging his feet a bit.

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
The door *clicks* shut behind them, but Christian can still hear Liam asking, entirely too loudly, “Should we get Horner a cup?”

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
“No. He can make his own.”

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
Their voices get quieter and quieter as they walk down the hallway, and in no time, Christian can no longer hear anything aside from his laptop’s fan kicking on to cool the computer down.

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
Max laughs as he sits down across from Christian. “So, *boss*, what’s up? Need me to get us some more sponsors?”

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
“No,” Christian folds his arms over his chest. “I just wanted to talk. Man to man style.”

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
“*Yikes*,” Max mutters with an eyeroll. “Last time someone wanted to have *just* a conversation with me, I got dumped,” he chuckles, pulling one leg up onto the chair and folding it under him. “Are you also gonna break up with me?”

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
Christian laughs. “I’d never.”

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
Nodding, Max relaxes into the chair more. “That’s good, I guess,” he shrugs, “what did you want to talk about, though?”

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
“How are you doing?”

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
“*No*.”

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
Christian taps his fingers on the table. “Don’t give me details. I don’t need them, and frankly, I don’t *want* them. I just need to know, as your… *superior*, if you’re doing alright. That’s all.”

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
“You could’ve asked that in front of them, no?” Max tilts his head to one side, raising his eyebrows. “You know that Checo wouldn’t tell anyone and Liam… Liam is barely listening as it is. If it’s not car related, he doesn’t really care.”

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
“And are you?”

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
“Listening?”

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
“Max,” Christian says his name sternly, “are you okay?”

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
“I’ve been better, actually,” Max bites out, scrunching his nose. “Just between us two, my boss is like… *really* interrogating me right now.” He pauses for a moment, waiting to see if Christian says anything to that. When he doesn’t, Max sighs. “Yeah, honestly, it’s fine. You won’t even *remember* *it* when the season starts.”

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
Christian looks him over, suspicious and not actually believing anything Max said. He nods, “Good. You’d not be the first driver to, *uhm*, have performance issues on track due to *personal reasons*, but I’d prefer it if you… didn’t have them.”

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
“Same,” Max shoots back. He bites the nails on his right hand before adding, “Is it okay that I— that Checo’s here?”

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->
Tilting his head to the right, Christian eyes Max with intensity only someone who truly knows you can muster. He’s scanning Max’s face, letting his eyes roam over the familiar features that almost seem softer now than they did the last time they’ve seen each other in person. Christian reaches for his glasses but doesn’t put them on. They just rest in his hand as he thinks.

<!-- bt-block:000056 -->
*Why wouldn’t it be, Max? What are you hiding?*

<!-- bt-block:000057 -->
“All our former drivers have their door wide open,” Christian opts out for the neutral answer. He finally puts on his glasses, looking over the rim to meet Max’s eyes. “Or is there a reason why it shouldn’t be okay?”

<!-- bt-block:000058 -->
“No,” Max says *way too fast*. “Not that I’m aware of, no. He’s just… *here*.”

<!-- bt-block:000059 -->
*Ah. So* that’s *how it is.*

<!-- bt-block:000060 -->
“I see,” Christian smiles at him, “if having Checo here makes you *more* okay, then—”

<!-- bt-block:000061 -->
“It’s not like that! I mean,” Max stutters out, “he— I’m— we just—”

<!-- bt-block:000062 -->
*Got you.*

<!-- bt-block:000063 -->
“It’s okay, Max,” he says, giving him as supportive of a smile as he can, “you don’t need to explain. It’s perfectly fine. I don’t need to know anything.”

<!-- bt-block:000064 -->
“But—”

<!-- bt-block:000065 -->
An urgent *knock-knock-knock* steals the words right out of Max’s mouth.

<!-- bt-block:000066 -->
“Come in,” Christian calls towards the door.

<!-- bt-block:000067 -->
Liam walks in, a Red Bull-branded mug in his hands, and walks back to the spot he was sitting in before. “Pretend I’m not here,” he suggests, pulling out his phone and reading an article about something non-driving related.

<!-- bt-block:000068 -->
Checo hands Max *his* cup, smiling. “Made you a tea,” he tells him casually, like they’ve done this a million times before. He waits until Max takes the cup properly before letting go of it. “Hope you’re done,” he comments towards Christian, with no poison in his voice.

<!-- bt-block:000069 -->
“Thank you,” Max whispers. A soft blush creeps onto his cheeks. “Sugar?”

<!-- bt-block:000070 -->
Nodding, Checo takes the other chair, scooting it closer to Max than necessary. “And how have you been, Christian? How’s the *familia*?”

<!-- bt-block:000071 -->
“Can’t complain,” Christian winks at him with a bright smile. “Well,” he claps his hands together, “Rich and GP might be waiting for you two already. Can’t let your engineers wait too long, can you?”

<!-- bt-block:000072 -->
Checo chuckles. “Or as anyone else would say, *get out of my office*,” he laughs, standing up. He instinctively holds out his hand, wordlessly offering Max that he’ll hold his cup for him.

<!-- bt-block:000073 -->
Max smiles at him and hands over the tea. “Thanks,” he breathes out gently.

<!-- bt-block:000074 -->
The room goes quiet as the three of them shuffle out into the hallway. Christian breaks the silence. “Max?” he calls after him, almost like an afterthought.

<!-- bt-block:000075 -->
Turning to face him, Max lets out, *“Hm?”*

<!-- bt-block:000076 -->
And Christian just adds, “This one suits you better anyway.”

<!-- bt-block:000079 -->
He can hear Fernando in the kitchen cooking something. It smells amazing even through the closed doors.

<!-- bt-block:000080 -->
Lance reaches for another towel and rubs his hair with it, trying to dry it up. *Eh, that’s enough*, he thinks before throwing the towel over the sink. Pulling on a pair of gray sweats and dark shirt, he checks himself out in the mirror.

<!-- bt-block:000081 -->
On his neck, he can already see the outline of three hickeys, but who cares? They are from Fernando Alonso — which, Lance thinks, is something that would send his teenage self into a permanent state of insanity.

<!-- bt-block:000082 -->
“Are you done, *querido*?” Fernando’s voice cuts through. “Food’s almost done.”

<!-- bt-block:000083 -->
“Just a sec,” Lance shouts back. He fixes his hair one more time when his phone vibrates to announce a new message arriving.

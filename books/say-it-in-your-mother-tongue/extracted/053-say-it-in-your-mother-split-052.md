*Formula 1 Qatar Airways British Grand Prix 2025*, Silverstone Circuit, Silverstone, United Kingdom, race afternoon on Sunday the 6<!-- bt-inline:000001:start -->th<!-- bt-inline:000001:end --> of July

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
“Forty minutes until lights out,” someone announces in the garage, and Nico groans trying not to explode.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
It has been seven weeks since his very last cigarette, enough time to no longer search for a pack from time to time, but not enough for the craving to *fully* go away. He slaps his pockets, looking for the little box he knows is there somewhere, even willing to settle for a vape at this point.

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
“You promised you wouldn’t,” a familiar voice tells him, a little disappointment seeping into the words.

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
Nico turns on his heels, looking at Gabi with something dangerously close to anger in his eyes. “Don’t,” he barks, still searching the pockets of his race-suit. “Not right now.”

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
With a gentle smile, Gabi stretches his arm towards Nico.

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
The German groans again, but nods, letting his *boyfriend* pull him away from prying eyes of the Sauber team and more importantly the ever-present media personnel.

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
Gabi is leading them past mechanics and assistants and people who give them terribly insincere, all too positive smiles. They enter the *drivers’* room and Gabi shuts the door behind them, sealing the world outside with a loud *bang*. Turning around to face Nico, he doesn’t have time to say anything as the man in front of him wraps his arms around him, squishing him in a tight embrace.

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
“I’m sorry,” Nico breathes out shakily, “*ich weiß nicht* why I’m so nervous today.”

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
“Is alright,” Gabi tells him, caressing his back with one hand, the other stuck between their bodies. “You’ll be great today, trust me.”

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
Nico pulls away slightly, a soft smile on his lips as he asks, halfway between genuine and amused, “You think?”

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
“I know it,” Gabi nods before surging forward to give Nico a quick peck on the lips. “You could win today.”

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
With a chuckle, Nico brushes Gabi’s nose with his own, breathing out softly, “I already won, you know?”

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
“Did you?” Gabi asks, having to go a little cross-eyed to meet Nico’s eyes.

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
*“Ja,”* Nico says, nodding once. He presses their lips together once more, kissing Gabi gently, before he whispers into his lips, “I have you.”

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
Less than an hour later, Gabi is one of the first people out of the race, spinning out and crashing into the barriers. With a cup of overly sweet tea, he plops down next to Steven Petrik, watching as Nico moves from his terrible starting position into the top ten. Glued to the screens, his lips parted, Gabi follows Nico’s car, barely blinking lest he misses *anything*.

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
An hour and thirty-three minutes into the race, Gabi is barely sitting on the chair, too excited for his body to be able to remain stationary, as he watches Nico hold his pace in third place.

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
Checkered flag.

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
Lando wins.

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
Oscar follows close.

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
And then it happens.

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
Nico *fucking* Hülkenberg crosses the finish line third.

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
Steven offers him the headset and Gabriel grabs it with trembling hands, breathing a little too hard as he says, “Nico, it’s Gabi. You have no idea how happy I am for you! You’re a legend. Absolutely insane what you did today.”

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
He keeps the headset on his head, hearing Nico chuckle before he tells him, “Thank you, *Gabi,”* he pauses, laughing in disbelief. “Thank you very much, appreciate that from you.”

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
Everything moves fast after that.

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
The headset falls into Steven’s lap as Gabi rips it off his head. Throwing his phone onto the engineer’s table, he takes off — running past everyone, past Norris, past cameras, past everything that isn’t Nico.

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
He reaches his car right as Nico begins to climb out. Gabi holds out his hand and Nico grabs it, squeezing it while still wearing his racing gloves.

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
“You’ve done it!” Gabi shouts at him, looking up.

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
Even with the helmet still on, he can see Nico’s bright smile, the way a few stray tears roll down his cheeks. Nico lifts his visor up, and their eyes meet, no longer hidden behind the clear plastic shield.

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
Gabi shouts again, “You fucking did it!”

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
Laughing, Nico jumps down from where he’s standing on the formula, still holding Gabi’s hand. He lets go of it, wrapping his arms around Gabi’s waist instead and lifting him up. He spins them around, laughing, unable to hold back his tears anymore.

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
When he sets Gabi down again, the Brazilian leans forward, touching his forehead to Nico’s helmet. “You’re a fucking legend,” he tells him again. “I’m so proud of you.”

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
Nico just closes his eyes, with words he no longer cares if any camera catches dancing on his tongue. “I did it for you,” he says, opening his eyes again to look into Gabi’s warm browns before he adds, “I love you.”

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
“I love you too,” Gabi breathes out, soft smile on his lips. “I’d kiss you, but,” he taps the front of Nico’s helmet, “there’s something in the way.”

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
And Nico just laughs as he pulls the helmet off, holding it in one hand by the chinstrap, as Gabi grabs his face with both hands and presses their lips together.

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
“Do you think they’ll write more about this or the podium?” Gabi asks with a chuckle, leaning his forehead on Nico’s.

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
Shrugging, Nico admits, “I couldn’t care less,” before he kisses Gabi again.

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
*Formula 1 Heineken Las Vegas Grand Prix 2025*, Las Vegas Strip Circuit, Paradise, Nevada, United States, after-race morning on Sunday the 23<!-- bt-inline:000001:start -->rd<!-- bt-inline:000001:end --> of November

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
The hotel smells weirdly of bleach and lemons, a combination that’s making Lance’s skin crawl.

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
With his duffel bag over his shoulder, he’s trying to maneuver Fernando’s giant suitcase — that totally *has to* contain everything they own, for crying out loud, *why else would it need to be so huge?* Lance wheels it behind himself, pulling with one arm, trying to decipher what number the note scribbled in Fernando’s messy handwriting could possibly say.

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
He pauses, flicking his eyes between the note and the number on the door on his right. With a shrug, he knocks on the door with the hand still holding the wrinkly piece of paper he’s found taped to his phone screen just thirty minutes earlier.

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
“Coming, wait,” a familiar, little rougher than usual, voice calls to him from inside the room. With a *creak,* the door opens, revealing a shaggy-haired Fernando with a satisfied grin on his face. “You found me fast.”

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
Lance enters the room, letting the Spaniard to close the door again, before he says casually, like it’s *no biggie,* “You need to get better at hiding, *old man.* This was kind of lame scavenger hunt.” Lance drops the duffel bag on the floor before he turns to look at Fernando, smiling, his eyes soft.

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
“Ah, next time, promise I’ll hide better,” the man winks at him, stepping closer to his partner. “Don’t you want to ask me something, *princesa?”*

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
Tapping his chin, Lance looks around them for a moment before breathing out, “No, I don’t think I do.”

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
Fernando raises his eyebrows. “Really?”

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
*“Hm,”* Lance hums, looking around again, “maybe just one thing,” he pauses, meeting Fernando’s dark eyes. “Why are we here?”

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
“That’s a hard question,” Fernando says, his lips forming a tight line before he continues, “some people would tell you we’re *here* to have children so they could have children, but some—”

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
“I didn’t mean… *alright,* why are you and me in this hotel room?” Lance asks, putting stress on each word.

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
*“Ah,* that’s because I have a surprise for you,” Fernando states simply, moving away from Lance. He walks to the bed, which is covered in a suspiciously bumpy white sheet. Exhaling deeply, and inhaling even deeply-er, Fernando puts on a soft smile — one reserved for Lance only. “You know how it’s like *a thing* people do in movies and TV shows where they get *really drunk* in Vegas and marry someone without actually meaning to do so?”

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->
Narrowing his eyes, Lance breathes out, “Yeah?”

<!-- bt-block:000056 -->
“I was thinking,” Fernando nods, still smiling — now maybe even more, given Lance’s confusion — as he continues, “that we could maybe do something like that.”

<!-- bt-block:000057 -->
“What?” Lance asks, his voice higher than usual. “What do you mean?”

<!-- bt-block:000058 -->
Fernando chuckles as he pulls the sheet off the bed. A two sets of suits lie there, next to each other, with a dark red jewelry box placed intentionally between them. “I wanna marry you.”

<!-- bt-block:000059 -->
“Right now? In Vegas?” Lance breathes out, his voice shaking with confusion.

<!-- bt-block:000060 -->
*“Sí,”* Fernando nods, “right now, in Vegas. I even got a fake Elvis to marry us.”

<!-- bt-block:000061 -->
Lance blinks, flicking his eyes from Fernando’s honest face to the suits and back. “Like really?”

<!-- bt-block:000062 -->
*“Sí,* really,” Fernando nods again. “I love you. I would love to have a certificate that says we are legally married.”

<!-- bt-block:000063 -->
“I mean,” Lance breathes out, “aren’t you forgetting something?”

<!-- bt-block:000064 -->
Understanding immediately, Fernando laughs and falls to one knee. He gets up again to grab the little red box before falling to his right knee again. With a shit-eating grin, he says, “Everyone knows we come as a package deal anyways, so do you want to make it official in front of Elvis Presley? I won’t even force you to take my name, *princesa,* you can stay Lance Strulovitch if you want,” he *asks*, finally opening the little box to reveal two rings, each made of one white gold and one yellow gold band.

<!-- bt-block:000065 -->
“I don’t know,” Lance bites his lower lip, putting on a show of playfulness to hide his genuine surprise, “I have to ask my father if he’d even allow it!”

<!-- bt-block:000066 -->
Fernando stands up, holding the box in one hand still outstretched towards Lance, and his smile becomes much gentler than before. “I already did,” he admits, looking away for a breath, or several. When he looks at Lance again, he tells him, “I asked your father for his blessing.”

<!-- bt-block:000067 -->
Lance’s face drops, his eyes wide as he asks, “And? What did he say?”

<!-- bt-block:000068 -->
“That he’ll kill me if I ever fuck *this* up,” Fernando says gently. “And he told me to take care of you.”

<!-- bt-block:000069 -->
A beat of silence passes between them — and it’s the loudest thing either of them has ever experienced — before Lance nods, breathing out a quiet, “Do you mean it?”

<!-- bt-block:000070 -->
“Your father or,” he pauses, gesturing to the bed and the suits.

<!-- bt-block:000071 -->
*“That,”* Lance repeats the gesture, nodding towards the bed. “Are you really asking me to marry you?”

<!-- bt-block:000072 -->
“Yeah,” Fernando admits, stepping closer to Lance, only leaving enough space between them so their bodies don’t touch. “Will you marry me?” he asks softly.

<!-- bt-block:000073 -->
“Yeah,” Lance nods, smiling wide. There are tears in his eyes threatening to escape onto his cheeks before Fernando tells him,

<!-- bt-block:000074 -->
“Good,” he chuckles, “I already paid the Elvis guy.”

<!-- bt-block:000075 -->
Lance barks out in laughter, the tears spilling onto his cheeks as he screws his eyes shut. He clears his throat before telling Fernando, “But we don’t have witnesses. *Everyone* already left.”

<!-- bt-block:000076 -->
“Not everyone,” Fernando shakes his head. He walks to the door that *doesn’t* lead into the hallway and opens it, allowing a familiar man to step inside the room.

<!-- bt-block:000077 -->
Lance chuckles as he watches Mark Webber (to whom Fernando promised, years and years ago, that he could be his best man if he *ever* decided to get married again) move past the bed, grinning. “And where’s *my* witness?”

<!-- bt-block:000078 -->
“Oh no,” Fernando breathes out in surprise, looking inside the hotel bathroom as if he lost something, or *someone.* “He must have gotten out. Would *he* be enough?” he gestures somewhere past Lance.

<!-- bt-block:000079 -->
Lance follows the gesture and *shrieks*, actually shrieks, when he sees his best friend. He throws his arms around Esteban, pulling him into a tight hug.

<!-- bt-block:000080 -->
“Did you really think I’d miss this?” Esteban laughs into the embrace. “No way in hell. I need to make sure he actually signs everything and makes you forever *his* problem.”

<!-- bt-block:000082 -->
*time off between Miami GP 2026 and Canadian GP 2026,* Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya, Montmeló, Spain, midday on Friday the 15<!-- bt-inline:000001:start -->th<!-- bt-inline:000001:end --> of May

<!-- bt-block:000083 -->
Max looks over his shoulder for the seventeenth time in the last hour to check on Checo.

<!-- bt-block:000084 -->
Checo has been in a really weird mood since they woke up that morning, pacing around the Airbnb they’re staying in at the moment, fidgeting with his hands, hiding his phone screen anytime Max moves a little too close to it.

<!-- bt-block:000085 -->
Trying not to panic, Max keeps telling himself little reasons why Checo acts so… suspicious.

<!-- bt-block:000086 -->
*He’s just nervous about next week’s race.*

<!-- bt-block:000087 -->
*Maybe he’s not feeling well but doesn’t want to tell me.*

<!-- bt-block:000088 -->
*His sister is pregnant again and he can’t tell anyone, not even me.*

<!-- bt-block:000089 -->
Scratching his head, Max walks through the finish line of the circuit, lost in thought. He doesn’t notice when Checo stops, hands in his pockets nervously, until the man calls out to him,

<!-- bt-block:000090 -->
“Max?”

<!-- bt-block:000091 -->
“Hm?” Max breathes out as he looks back, noticing there’s quite a bit of space between them.

<!-- bt-block:000092 -->
Checo licks his lips before he asks, “Could you come back?”

<!-- bt-block:000093 -->
Without saying anything, Max struts back, his steps confident and fast. He stops maybe six feet away from Checo, shoving his hands into his pockets as well.

<!-- bt-block:000094 -->
“We should… I think we should talk,” Checo breathes out, his voice as shaky as his knees.

<!-- bt-block:000095 -->
Max nods, once, telling him, “Alright.”

<!-- bt-block:000096 -->
Swallowing loudly, Checo also nods as he asks, “Do you know what today is?”

<!-- bt-block:000097 -->
*Verdomme, is it his birthday?*

<!-- bt-block:000098 -->
Max cocks his head to one side, his eyebrows furrowed together.

<!-- bt-block:000099 -->
*No, that’s… he already had birthday. And it’s not our anniversary either.*

<!-- bt-block:000100 -->
“No, not really,” he admits after a moment, looking away from Checo as he adds, “I… I have no idea.”

<!-- bt-block:000101 -->
With a tight-lipped smile, Checo nods once more. “It’s the fifteenth of May,” he tells Max, who is still as confused as before. “Ten years ago,” Checo continues, licking his lips nervously, “you *won* your first ever grand prix and became the youngest driver to do that.”

<!-- bt-block:000102 -->
Max chuckles. “Oh yeah,” he breathes out, shaking his head. “Fuck, *ten years ago?* I’m getting old.”

<!-- bt-block:000103 -->
Checo tilts his head to one side, his smile gentler than before, his eyes softer. “I haven’t been by your side then,” he tells Max, fiddling with something in the right pocket of his pants, “I barely even knew *who* your name belonged to before *this.”* Looking up at the sky, Checo’s smile becomes even more affectionate, even gentler, even more familiar. “But a lot can change in ten years, *huh?”* he breathes out with a soft chuckle.

<!-- bt-block:000104 -->
“A metric shit ton,” Max adds, laughing.

<!-- bt-block:000105 -->
“Yeah,” Checo nods, “well… I haven’t been by your side *then*, but *uh…* I am now. And I will continue to be as long as you let me.” He pauses, looking into Max’s eyes with nothing but adoration. “So, *Max,”* his voice is shaking as he says his name, “will you let me be by your side forever?”

<!-- bt-block:000106 -->
And with those words, Checo pulls out a small velvet box from his pocket.

<!-- bt-block:000107 -->
Max scrunches his face, relaxes it and parts his lips, before closing his mouth again. His voice comes out a little ragged as he asks, “Have you just… proposed to me?”

<!-- bt-block:000108 -->
“Yeah,” Checo says, “I did.”

<!-- bt-block:000109 -->
Nodding, Max looks away, somewhere far on the track. He shakes his head a little as he lets out, a little breathless, “I should’ve known.”

<!-- bt-block:000110 -->
Checo chuckles, nervous and genuinely surprised by those words. “Known what?”

<!-- bt-block:000111 -->
“That you’d ask *that* here,” Max says, turning back to look at Checo. The smile on his lips is soft as he says, “Where else?”

<!-- bt-block:000112 -->
“I thought about asking you while you’re half asleep,” Checo jokes. “Just to mess with you. But this felt nicer.”

<!-- bt-block:000113 -->
Max nods, stepping closer to Checo. “So, what do I do now?”

<!-- bt-block:000114 -->
Cocking his head to one side, Checo responds, “Now you better answer the question, *baby.”*

<!-- bt-block:000115 -->
“Oh yeah,” he says, chuckling, “that makes sense.”

<!-- bt-block:000116 -->
Checo holds out the box a little closer to Max, grinning as he asks again, “So, will you marry me?” He opens the velvet box, showing Max a thick ring made of titanium.

<!-- bt-block:000117 -->
With eyes pinned to Checo’s face, not even noticing the ring, Max breathes out, *“Schatje,* I told you a year ago that I would.”

<!-- bt-block:000118 -->
“And now?” Checo asks. “Will you still?”

<!-- bt-block:000119 -->
“Of fucking course,” Max breathes out, laughing as he presses their lips together.

<!-- bt-block:000120 -->
Checo pushes Max away lightly, looking at him with silent apology in his eyes, and tells him, “I should put it on your finger now, no?”

<!-- bt-block:000121 -->
Max holds out his left hand with a chuckle. “Probably, *ja.”*

<!-- bt-block:000122 -->
Pulling the ring out of the box, Checo slides it down Max’s left ring finger. It fits him like a glove, like it was made specifically for him. “I love you,” Checo glances from the ring up, back to Max’s eyes, as he says, “Now we can kiss again.”

<!-- bt-block:000123 -->
Grinning, Max throws his arms around Checo’s neck, pulling their face close together again. He shakes his head lightly, whispering, “I’m so fucking crazy for you,” before he presses their lips together.

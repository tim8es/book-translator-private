<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
Max has been circling something for hours.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
Checo knows it. He can see it in the way his hands move a little too jerkily; can hear it in the way he says his name like it’s the only thing keeping him sane; can feel it in the way Max keeps grabbing his wrist to get his attention. He wants to ask — needs to, even — and yet, Checo *doesn’t*. He knows better than to probe at something Max isn’t willing to share just yet, knows better than to ask and ruin the fragile understanding of what it is that’s sitting between them.

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
And so… *he waits.*

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
He waits all the way through lunch. He waits when Max runs off to buy them smoothies that he swears are the best ones ever created. He waits as Max takes a midday shower without singing or talking to him through the locked bathroom door. He waits as Max paces around the room.

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
Checo is lounging on the sofa, one foot under him, the other on the floor, pretending he’s not observing Max.

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
Max plays with his hands, a stim he picked up years ago, tapping his fingertips together in a steady yet fast rhythm. He sighs, defeated, as he sits down next to him. “Checo?” he breathes out, grabbing Checo’s sleeve to make sure the man is looking at him.

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
Not saying anything, Checo just hums, a simple sound intended not to push but to encourage Max.

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
Sighing again while still tapping his fingers, Max nods to himself. “It’s stupid,” he admits, and Checo knows that whatever is on his mind has to be either *very good* or *extremely* *bad*. “I don’t even… *verdomme*, I don’t even know why I’m asking this.”

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
Checo gives him a soft smile. “Whatever it is, you *can* ask. I don’t mind.”

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
Max nods, more to himself than Checo, before breathing out, “I, *uh*, I just wanted to ask… *who’s-your-favorite-teammate*?” It comes out *all at once*, like it’s one (very long) word.

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
Blinking, Checo tilts his head slightly. “What?”

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
No longer able to look at Checo, Max repeats, much slower this time, “Who’s your favorite teammate?” He pauses for a moment before adding, “If you, *uh*, if you have one?”

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
“I do,” Checo admits without thinking, his tone genuine. “But it’s not *that* simple,” he continues, speaking in a quiet but unwavering manner.

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
“Of course,” Max breathes out, angry with himself for even asking. “You don’t… you don’t have to tell me. Maybe… I don’t even wanna know it.”

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
Checo pinches the bridge of his nose. “Do you want the *safe* option, or do I… do you want the safer option, or do I tell you the truth?”

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
Max shrugs. “Either. Both. *Neither*. Your choice,” he stutters out. “Completely up to you.”

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
And Checo nods, clapping a hand over Max’s knee. “You and Lance,” he states, plain and simple, before standing up. He moves maybe three meters away from the sofa — like he needs the distance now — before turning back, looking at the perplexed expression on Max’s face. “You said *both*. I gave you *both,”* he tells him with a smile on his lips that is closer to a smirk than anything else. He watches Max for a breath too long before adding, “now it’s for *you* to decide which is which.”

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
The second Checo exits the room, it goes quiet in a way Max hates.

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
*Safe option or the truth.*

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
Max keeps tapping his fingers, much faster than before, as the words play over and over in his head.

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
*You and Lance*.

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
*Safe and the truth.*

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
He runs a hand through his hair. There is no other way about it, no *other way* to ask.

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
*Which option am I?*

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
Before he even realizes he’s doing it, Max has his phone in his hands, typing out a message for *the other* man.

Max throws the phone onto the sofa, watching as it bounces to the side before landing screen down. He folds his arms on his chest, groaning, relaxing them again in a couple of seconds. His right foot is bouncing up and down, shaking the whole couch, when Max stands up. He doesn’t even have to think about it, his feet carry him to where he expects Checo to be.

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->
He doesn’t knock or clear his throat, sure that Checo already knows about him anyway.

<!-- bt-block:000056 -->
“Who’s *your* favorite teammate?” Checo asks, without turning back to look at Max. He’s staring at the glass kettle heating up water for two cups of tea.

<!-- bt-block:000057 -->
Max stands behind him, slightly over an armlength away, and he exhales. “What if I don’t have one?” he suggests, trying to go for casual but landing a bit tense.

<!-- bt-block:000058 -->
Checo laughs. “But you do,” he objects. As the kettle beeps, he pours the water into two mugs with teabags already prepared. “I’d say it’s Daniel, in your case.”

<!-- bt-block:000059 -->
Stepping closer, Max shakes his head silently. He knows Checo can’t see him, but that doesn’t matter, he could probably still feel the gesture *somehow*. “Wrong,” is all Max says before he lets his head drop low, leaning his forehead between Checo’s shoulder blades. Max allows his arms to snake around Checo’s waist, pulling them closer together.

<!-- bt-block:000060 -->
*“Wrong?”* Checo repeats, his voice low but steady. He feels Max nods into his back. “Well, I’m not saying myself, because I’m not an egoist, so… Alex?”

<!-- bt-block:000061 -->
“You,” Max mutters into his back quietly but sure. He tightens his hold of Checo before adding, “no one else is anywhere close to you.”

<!-- bt-block:000062 -->
“That sounds an awful lot like attachment,” Checo breathes out as he spins in Max’s embrace. Once eye-to-eye, he lifts his hand and places it on Max’s cheek.

<!-- bt-block:000063 -->
“I’m not a Jedi,” Max shrugs, “I’m allowed to be attached.”

<!-- bt-block:000064 -->
Checo’s thumb brushes over his cheek. “What a dangerous philosophy,” he murmurs with the softest smile.

It’s not an alarm that wakes him up.

<!-- bt-block:000067 -->
Nico stirs, groaning as too much light pours into the room. He rubs his face before stilling, suddenly very aware of *everything*. Nico opens his eyes, and they widen the second he sees the sleeping form next to him.

<!-- bt-block:000068 -->
*Scheiße.*

<!-- bt-block:000069 -->
Moving backwards ever so gently, Nico hisses.

<!-- bt-block:000070 -->
*Too close*.

<!-- bt-block:000071 -->
Gabriel is curled up on his side, face turned towards Nico, and his breathing is almost *too* steady. With a quiet huff, his lips move as if saying something, but no words come out — not at first.

<!-- bt-block:000072 -->
There, in the narrow space between their bodies, lies his hand. His fingers are twitching slightly, gentle movements caused by sleep, and Nico cannot help but stare. He watches as the fingers barely move before he does something unthinkable.

<!-- bt-block:000073 -->
Nico reaches with his hand to Gabriel’s. Lightly, lighter than a butterfly, he touches the tanned skin with the pad of his index finger. Gabriel’s skin is soft, softer than Nico remembers it being, and warm, and Nico can’t stop himself from touching *more*.

<!-- bt-block:000074 -->
With his fingertips, he caresses Gabriel’s inner wrist, feeling the steady heartbeat under his fingers. He breathes out, trying to pretend that his whole body isn’t trembling, when Gabriel huffs again.

<!-- bt-block:000075 -->
This time, he actually says something.

<!-- bt-block:000076 -->
Nico freezes, stuck mid-move, darting his eyes to Gabriel’s face. He’s still sleeping, but his lips part in a quiet moan, *maybe*, or just a sigh. Nico can’t tell.

<!-- bt-block:000077 -->
Whatever it is, it’s followed by a barely audible voice; Gabriel is more mouthing the word than actually speaking it aloud. *“Nico.”*

<!-- bt-block:000078 -->
*Fuck*.

<!-- bt-block:000079 -->
Groaning, Nico shuffles backwards until he feels the edge of the bed under his side. He slips from under the blanket, quietly standing up from the bed. On bare feet, he walks into the bathroom, grabbing the clothes thrown over the backrest of his chair as he does so. It is then, once the door closes behind him, that Nico lets out a long, heavy sigh. He leans his back on the door, the chill seeping into his skin instantly.

<!-- bt-block:000080 -->
*What the fuck are you doing?*

<!-- bt-block:000081 -->
He stands there for what feels like eternity — and yet, it couldn’t have been more than three minutes — when he decides to *finally* get dressed. Nico pulls on a pair of black joggers, a shirt he doesn’t remember packing and a gray hoodie with a logo of the Haas Formula 1 team.

<!-- bt-block:000082 -->
Without sparing himself a single look, Nico unlocks the door and walks into the bedroom with confidence he doesn’t *actually* have.

<!-- bt-block:000083 -->
*Right, I’ll just grab my phone and earbuds. Hopefully, Gabriel—*

<!-- bt-block:000084 -->
*“Oi, bom dia,”* Gabriel chirps, sleep still tugging at the edges of his voice. He yawns, stretching his arms *up*, and all Nico can focus on is the way Gabriel’s shirt rides up, revealing his toned stomach.

<!-- bt-block:000085 -->
*Pervert*.

<!-- bt-block:000086 -->

<!-- bt-block:000087 -->

*Verdammte* *Scheiße!*

<!-- bt-block:000091 -->
Gabriel just nods, an unreadable expression plastered on his face like a mask. “Sure,” he spits out as he lies back down. He pulls the blanket over his back, all the way up, covering the back of his head as well. Cocooned, he growls, “Don’t lock. I’ll be gone soon.”

<!-- bt-block:000092 -->
Nico watches him for a second before shoving the other earbud into his left ear. He turns to leave, glancing back one more time.

<!-- bt-block:000093 -->
*It’s for the best*.

<!-- bt-block:000094 -->
Leaving before he can change his mind, Nico sighs and walks towards the elevator.

*Maybe I should’ve said yes.*

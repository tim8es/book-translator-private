Chapter 49: my wingman and I

<!-- bt-block:000004 -->
Loading your whole childhood into a sportscar, it turns out, is not an easy task.

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
He groans, placing the box onto the concrete driveway before he *dives* back into the car. Lance is fighting tooth and nail with the collection of racing memorabilia he’s acquired over the years of driving fast cars around circuits, forcing his most valued possessions to fit into space reserved for a passenger and not dozen or so boxes.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
From the balcony above, with a cigar between his fingers, he knows Lawrence is *still* watching him. “Why don’t you take the G-Wagon?” he asks, his voice descending over Lance. “It’s yours anyway.”

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
Lance looks up, meeting his father’s face through his eyelashes. *“Mine,”* he scoffs, his nose crinkled up. *“Non, merci,”* he says while rolling his eyes, no longer looking up to his father — both physically and metaphorically, “I’m good.”

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
“Whatever, it’s your business,” Lawrence’s voice booms, entirely too loud for the distance between them. “You’ve never made good choices.”

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
Biting his tongue to prevent himself from saying something *too* stupid, Lance just nods, carefully placing one of the last boxes inside the car. He buckles the four-point harness seatbelt, that he’s extended to the maximum, with a soft *click,* holding the three boxes worth the most to him safely in place. Lance nods as he looks over his car, closing the door with way too loud of a *bang*.

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
Crunching of the gravel announces that another set of feet has joined him. He doesn’t need to turn around to see who it is.

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
“You can sell the rest,” Lance breathes out loud enough for his father to hear. “Or throw it out. I don’t care.”

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
Lawrence doesn’t respond, he doesn’t hum or clear his throat, he just stands there, looking past Lance at a spot in front of the car.

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
They stand there, side to side in a way, for a while before Lance clears his throat. “I should go.”

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
“How long?”

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
Lance finally turns to look at his father, expressionless. “What?”

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
“How long,” Lawrence tries again, gesturing around himself with his right hand, in which he’s still holding a cigar, “has it been going on? You and Fernando, I mean.”

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
“A while,” Lance responds, his face falling back into a thin-lipped expression.

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
*“A while,”* Lawrence repeats with a single nod. “How long of a while?”

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
Shrugging, Lance just breathes out, “Long enough to matter.”

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
Lawrence nods again, averting his eyes back to the spot in front of Lance’s car. He breathes in around the cigar, exhaling gray smoke before taking another drag, smoking the cigar more like a cigarette than an imported, entirely too expensive cigar. “Years?”

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
“You could say that,” Lance says, and even if it’s not exactly the truth, it’s not a lie either.

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
Lawrence closes his eyes, deep in thought. “He’s older than you,” he states, and for the first time in years, he sounds like his father and not a business manager Lance’s *just* sharing a house with. “A lot older.”

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
“Seventeen years, I know,” Lance tells him with a nod. “But it’s still less of a difference than between Raquel and you.”

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
“That’s different,” Lawrence shoots back fast, before he takes another drag of his cigar, buying himself the time to think. “He’s your teammate.”

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
“I know.”

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
Lawrence nods again, sighing. “Do you… does he make you happy?”

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
Not trying to suppress the soft smile from forming on his lips, Lance breathes out, “More than anything or anyone else. I don’t… words can’t even describe just how much.”

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
With his eyes still pinned somewhere safe, Lawrence nods, his lips in a tight straight line. He lets out a sigh, his shoulders dropping, before he tells his son, “You know what I think about… *this*. I really, *really* wish you weren’t a *queer,* I guess, but *uh*… I’m not going to tell you to *end it*.”

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
“End it?” Lance repeats after his father, finally looking at him again.

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
Lawrence nods. “Yeah,” he says, and it quite obviously takes everything in him to continue speaking, “I… your mother would kill me if I did.”

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
Lance doesn’t hold back the chuckle that escapes his lips. “She would, wouldn’t she?”

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
“And… I don’t think it’s right for me to expect that. Not as your father, not as your boss, not as *his* boss. But, *uh,* I *do* need to ask you,” he says with a nod, giving his son a forcefully empty look.

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
“Sure. Ask away, *papa.* Worst case scenario, I tell you to *fuck off,”* Lance says with way too wide of a smile given the situation, shrugging.

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
With their eyes locked onto one another, Lawrence takes a step closer to his son and breathes out, “Just… make sure you’re safe, alright?” He moves the cigar from right hand to the left, then from the left to the right again, rolling it between his fingers like a fidget toy before he gathers the courage to continue. “I don’t mean *scandal-free* type of safe; though, please, try to avoid the whole media circus thing… but *uh*, I mean physically… safe.”

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
“We both *have to* get tested regularly,” Lance objects with a shrug. “Not much more we could do to stay safe than that.”

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
“That’s not what I meant,” Lawrence tells him, briefly breaking eye contact to look *up,* to the sky, before meeting Lance’s eyes again.

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
Lance takes something like pity towards his father, nodding once their eyes meet again. He admits, soft and honest, “I know.”

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
“You can always… *uh,* it’s still… just keep the keys,” Lawrence stutters out, his voice a little shaky, his hands *even shakier.* “Alright?”

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
Lance nods. “Alright.”

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
Lawrence turns on his heel, then, walking towards the house again. He doesn’t say another word as he watches — hidden, peeking through heavy curtains — Lance climb behind the steering wheel, rolling the car slowly away from his childhood house, from the place he stopped calling *home* a long time ago.

*“Sí, mama.* I will ask him. *Sí,* I promise. *Sí.* Love you too, *mama. Hasta pronto.”*

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
Max is reading something on his laptop, sprawled out on the bed like a lazy cat, humming contently when Checo puts down his phone. He resumes the song they were listening to before *Marilú’s* call. “So,” he breathes out, not looking away from his screen.

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
*“So,”* Checo repeats as he sits down on their bed.

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
“What are you supposed to ask me, *schatje?”* Max asks, rolling over on the bed so that he could be closer to Checo, though he snakes *even closer*, laying his head in Checo’s lap.

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
*“Mi familia* decided,” he sighs, running his fingers through Max’s hair as he continues, “that we’ve not visited in too long.”

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
“You mean *you* haven’t visited in too long,” Max corrects him with a soft chuckle.

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
Checo shakes his head, however. “No, *we.* Got accused of keeping you *all to myself,”* he says, laughing at Max’s surprised expression. Still caressing his hair, Checo tells him, “*Mi mama* asked to bring you before Suzuka.”

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
“Me?” Max breathes out, sitting up straight. He immediately shuffles his legs so that they’re *under* him, leaning a little forward as he asks, “Why me?”

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
With a chuckle, Checo cocks his head to one side and breathes out, “Why do you think, *chiquitito?”* He doesn’t wait for Max to respond before answers his own question. “You’re my boyfriend *and* my family wants to spend time with you? That’s like… *a thing* people do sometimes, no?”

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
Max nods in agreement, but his face tells a different story, *still*.

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
“You don’t have to go with me,” Checo tells Max, reaching for his hand. “Is okay, you know? They’d get it.”

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
“I wanna come, it’s just…” he pauses, biting the inside of his cheek. “It feels different now, doesn’t it?”

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
Checo stops mid-move, meeting Max’s eyes with nothing but love in his. “Not to me,” he admits, adding, “you’re still the same Max as last time, just with one more podium under your sleeve.”

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->
Max just stares at Checo, wordlessly and with big eyes, for a few seconds.

<!-- bt-block:000056 -->
Unable to stop himself, Checo sighs and quotes a line from *Top Gun* he knows Max will immediately recognize, *“You are still dangerous.”* Pausing for a breath, or several — just until Max’s eyes light up as the movie scene pops up in his head — he adds, *“You can be my wingman anytime.”*

<!-- bt-block:000057 -->
*“Bullshit. You can be mine,”* Max shoots back, lightning fast, before hesitating for a few seconds, trying to gather up his thoughts. Finally, Max breathes out, “But… you know? Now we’re *really* dating.”

<!-- bt-block:000058 -->
With a shrug, Checo tells him, *“Mi familia* thought we’re together since at least September.” He squeezes Max’s hand lightly, before bringing it to his lips and giving it a gentle kiss. *“Mi mama* loves you, you know that.”

<!-- bt-block:000059 -->
“Your *mama* loves everyone. She’s too nice for her own sake,” Max shoots back with a chuckle. His eyes soften a bit right before he adds, “You’re like her in some ways.”

<!-- bt-block:000060 -->
“Am I also too nice?” Checo asks teasingly while squeezing Max’s hand again, gentle and familiar.

<!-- bt-block:000061 -->
*“Ja,”* Max nods once, “you are. Sometimes.”

<!-- bt-block:000062 -->
*All the time.*

<!-- bt-block:000063 -->
“Like when?” Checo asks lightly, not mentioning all the times Max explicitly called him an asshole over their time as teammates. “Say *one* time.”

<!-- bt-block:000064 -->
“Like to me.”

<!-- bt-block:000065 -->
*You’ve never been anything but nice to me.*

<!-- bt-block:000066 -->
Checo shakes his head, a *dumb* smile on his lips. “Max,” he breathes out with so much love in his voice it physically hurts a little, “I’m literally in love with you, Max. Of course, I wanna be nice to you.”

<!-- bt-block:000067 -->
A beat of silence passes. Max stares at Checo with wide eyes, as if he’s trying to figure him out, like he’s trying to waltz inside Checo’s brain and search for *the meaning of all of this.* He must have found what he was looking for in Checo’s eyes, breathing out a soft, “Alright, yeah. Let’s… let’s visit your parents.”

<!-- bt-block:000068 -->
“And my grandparents,” Checo adds quietly, offering Max a bright smile. His voice is barely audible when he tells him, “And maybe some other family members.”

<!-- bt-block:000069 -->
Max blinks wordlessly for a second before he asks, “This is going to be some big family gathering, right?”

<!-- bt-block:000070 -->
Shaking his head, Checo puts on his most innocent expression as he *lies,* “No, not really.”

<!-- bt-block:000071 -->
*“Schatje,* tell your mum I’m coming before I change my mind.”

<!-- bt-block:000072 -->
Checo laughs. “You got it, *baby,”* he winks at Max before unlocking his phone to send out a quick message to his mum. “She’s been trying to get your number for a while now, so be prepared that she *will* ask you for it directly this time.”

<!-- bt-block:000073 -->
“Why?” Max breathes out, his voice a little timid. “And don’t say it’s because we’re dating. I’ve never texted Kelly’s parents, not even once. Maybe, *maybe* once, I think. When *she* got too sick to call them herself.”

<!-- bt-block:000074 -->
*“Mi familia* does things a little differently than hers, then,” Checo shrugs with even bigger of a smile. He leans closer to Max, kissing him on the tip of his nose. *“Mi papa* asked me if you’d go fishing with him in summer, just you and him. And *mama* will bake you a cake for birthday, no matter what you tell her.”

<!-- bt-block:000075 -->
Max opens his mouth, then closes it, before opening it again to ask, “Is that why you asked me about my favorite sweet food?”

<!-- bt-block:000076 -->
*“Sí,”* Checo nods with a wide smile, “made me ask, *mi mama.* She kept texting me to ask. Like all the time, just *ask Max, ask Max, ask Max.* The whole *China*, it was just that.”

<!-- bt-block:000077 -->
“But my birthday is in half a year,” Max breathes out in something very close to horror. “Why is she asking now?”

<!-- bt-block:000078 -->
*Why is she asking at all? I fucking hate my birthday.*

<!-- bt-block:000079 -->
Shrugging, Checo offers, “To be prepared? I don’t know,” he chuckles. “All I know is *mi familia* is very ready to have you as a part of it.”

<!-- bt-block:000080 -->
“This *really* sounds like a proposal,” Max laughs a little, his eyes narrowing as he asks, “it isn’t, right?”

<!-- bt-block:000081 -->
“No. No, no, no. No proposing,” Checo assures him, playing up just how panicked he is, adding, “Not here.”

<!-- bt-block:000082 -->
*Why did he say, “Not here,” instead of, “Not now?”*

<!-- bt-block:000083 -->
Max nods as he breathes out, “Good. *My* mum still doesn’t know I’m dating you. Actually, she might still think I’m with Kelly, when I think about it… Well, I think she’d be pretty upset to learn we got engaged *before* telling her she *might* get a *son*-in-law.”

<!-- bt-block:000084 -->
“So,” Checo purses his lips in faux thought, huffing in amusement as he asks, “my family now, your family next? Like between Jeddah and Miami?”

<!-- bt-block:000085 -->
“Don’t think that’s necessary,” Max shrugs, putting up a show of nonchalance, “Vic… I don’t think she cares that much. I could probably just text her right now like, *hey, I’m dating Checo,* and she’d tell me that she knew or something. And grandma *just* wants me to have a baby finally so she could be *overgrootmoeder*. We *only* need to tell *m'n ma.”* He licks his lips, looking away from Checo before adding, “And we don’t have to tell *him. He* already knows anyways.”

Chapter 39: Tom Cruise in my head

<!-- bt-block:000002 -->
Chapter Notes

<!-- bt-block:000003 -->
**This is a Multi-POV ensemble story — chapters may focus on one pairing or move between multiple.**

<!-- bt-block:000004 -->
“You bought a record player just for this?”

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
Fernando shakes his head. “No,” he says, taking a sip of his wine, “not *just* for this. I bought it for you. No *just.”*

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
“That’s what I said,” Lance protests, swirling the wine in his glass before he also takes a sip. “You go through so much trouble for me.”

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
*“Oi,”* Fernando breathes out, his voice louder than intended, “where is this coming from?” He stands up from his chair, moving closer to Lance. “I wanted to do this. Is my pleasure.”

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
*But like… why?*

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
“That doesn’t even make sense,” Lance says with a frown. “Like if — *I don’t know!* — if I was some… *supermodel* or something, you know, at least you could like show me off to people. But I’m not,” he shakes his head slightly, “I’m just your teammate. You can’t even tell people we’re a thing.”

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
“You are not *just my teammate,”* Fernando tells him, stepping into Lance’s personal space. He runs his hand through Lance’s hair, then again, much gentler the second time. “You’re turning heads everywhere we go. And not because you’re ugly or infamous or *cualquier chorrada* you want to say. People look at you because you’re *hermoso* — you know — *pretty.”*

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
Lance huffs. “We’re not having this conversation again,” he rolls his eyes at Fernando. “No one other than you thinks I’m pretty. I certainly don’t.”

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
Fernando caresses his hair again, looking down to meet his eyes. “You’re wrong,” is all he says before he moves away, towards the source of music. He looks at Lance again as the piece decrescendos, giving him a gentle smile.

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
He stops the record player, lifting the hand away from the vinyl and *clicking* it into its stand. With gentle hands, Fernando takes off the record and slides it back into its paper cover before he takes out another one. He sets it down and carefully returns the hand to the edge of the new vinyl, turning the player on and waiting for the needle to find the right groove.

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
For the second time that evening, Foreigner’s *I Want to Know What Love Is* starts playing, soft and familiar.

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
*He really likes the song.*

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
Fernando looks down at Lance, furrowing his eyebrows. He licks his lips and smiles, holding out his right hand. “May I?”

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
*What?*

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
“What?” Lance breathes out. He’s looking up from where he’s seated, searching Fernando’s eyes. “May you what?”

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
“Dance. Will you,” Fernando *somehow* smiles even more, “dance with me?”

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
*Oh.*

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
Throwing his head back, Lance laughs. “You’re crazy,” he says, expecting Fernando do follow — but the man is dead serious. “Wait, like… *really?”*

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
Fernando nods. “Really, *cariño.”*

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
Not trusting himself to answer just yet, Lance looks over the city lights, searching for something to say to deflect. But his brain betrays him and *pushes* him to breathe out, “I’d like that.”

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
Fernando offers the hand to Lance again, and Lance takes it; he lets himself be *pulled up* onto his feet, steadying himself by grabbing onto Fernando’s other arm. He’s not tipsy, not really, but his head still spins a little. “You know I’m not a good dancer,” Lance mutters as Fernando rests his open palm over the small of his back.

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
“This is not a competition,” Fernando tells him in that reassuring tone of voice he reserves for *almost-crises* around the paddock. “No one watching but us.”

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
Lance nods, breathing out a hushed, *“Okay,”* as Fernando takes the lead, swaying them gently.

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
They *barely* move, their feet dragging on the stone-paved ground into the rhythm of the cheesiest and *romantic-est* song ever produced. Fernando’s hold is gentle — he’s not gripping Lance’s back, nor his hoodie — just lightly rests his hand there, the other still holding Lance’s hand.

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
Lance sighs, content and utterly in love, before letting his head *fall* forward. He lies his head onto Fernando’s shoulder, closing his eyes as he does so.

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
They keep moving slowly until the song becomes quieter and quieter before it finally fades out completely, followed immediately by another song. Lance giggles when he realizes what’s playing.

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
“What?” Fernando asks, his voice flat.

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
“Nothing,” Lance lies, giggling still. “I just didn’t expect you to go all *Top Gun* on me.”

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
Fernando stops mid-sway and pushes Lance away slightly. “What do you mean?”

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
Lance lifts one finger up, pointing at nothing (though gesturing at the song). “The song?” he breathes out. “It’s from *Top Gun.* Like this is *the* love song. If you tell someone to imagine an eighties rock love song, they’re going to think of this one.”

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
“If you don’t like, I can skip it,” Fernando suggests with a shrug.

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
“No, that’s… *look*, I didn’t mean it as a critique, okay?” Lance tilts his head to one side, offering Fernando a soft smile. “I like it. It’s… It’s good. Do I picture young Tom Cruise in my head when I hear it? Yeah, *maybe.* But that’s not like a bad thing or anything.”

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
Fernando stares at him for all of maybe three seconds before he chuckles, shaking his head in amusement.

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
“What?” Lance says in a *bit-too-offended* tone. “As if you don’t! *Maverick* was like such a crush for like everyone, gay or straight. And like *this* song? That’s like as much *his song* as like *Highway to the Danger Zone* is,” Lance continues, his voice tight in his throat. He can see Fernando bite his lips to suppress his laughter, and that’s what makes Lance *shut up*. “I'm rambling, aren't I?”

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
“A little,” Fernando nods, adding immediately, “but I don’t mind. Is cute.”

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
Lance looks away, but he doesn’t try to hide his smile. “Do you not think he was hot in that movie?”

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
*“Eh,”* Fernando shrugs, “I like other actors.”

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
“Like who?” Lance asks, ignoring the way a ping of jealousy goes off in his head.

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
“Oh, one of them has a movie coming out this year. I think is his first,” Fernando nods with a wide smile.

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
Lance cocks his head to one side. “What movie?”

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
“F1,” Fernando says, full-on grinning now. “Was talking about you, *cariño.”*

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
Rolling his eyes, Lance huffs out, in an obviously joking way, “If that’s your *best* attempt at flirting, you might want to give it another go.”

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
“Might suck at flirting,” Fernando says, pulling Lance close again, their chests touching, “but you still agreed to go on a date with me, so it can’t be *that* terrible.”

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
“Why wouldn’t I agree?” Lance licks his lips with a grin that *never bodes well*, “I’d have to be stupid if I said no to a two-time world champion basically begging to be my sugar daddy.”

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
Fernando barks out in laughter. “And yet you still won’t let me buy you nice things.”

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
“That’s unrelated,” Lance shoots back faster than necessary. “I can buy own *nice things,* you know?”

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
With a nod, Fernando tells him, “You don’t need a sugar daddy, then.”

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
“I don’t,” Lance agrees, surprised by the sudden seriousness of Fernando’s voice. He doesn’t get much time to think about it, though, before Fernando adds,

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
“That only leaves one option for me.”

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
Lance furrows his eyebrows as he breathes out, “And that is?”

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
“To ask to be your boyfriend,” he says with a smile.

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->
“Aren’t you already?” Lance asks, equally surprised and confused. “I mean… we’re literally on a date, no?”

<!-- bt-block:000056 -->
Fernando nods. *“Sí,* we are. But neither of us *said* that we’re dating,” he tells Lance, smiling still. “So, I’m going to.”

<!-- bt-block:000057 -->
“I—”

<!-- bt-block:000058 -->
*“No,”* Fernando cuts Lance off. “I *wanna* ask,” he continues, his voice softer now. He looks into Lance’s eyes, admiring them for the millionth time, as he breathes out, “I am so, *so* in love with you, Lance, and I can’t imagine my life without you. Would you do me *el honor,”* he says in heavy Spanish accent, “and be my boyfriend?”

<!-- bt-block:000059 -->
Lance blinks, his lips parted, as tears threaten to escape out of the corners of his eyes. “Of course,” he breathes out, shaking head in disbelief, “yeah. I do.” He leans forward, kissing Fernando lightly on the lips as *Alone* by Heart starts playing from the record player. Pulling away partially, he adds, giggling, “I have one condition, though.”

<!-- bt-block:000060 -->
Fernando raises one eyebrow questioningly. “And that is?”

<!-- bt-block:000061 -->
“I get to choose the vinyl next time.”

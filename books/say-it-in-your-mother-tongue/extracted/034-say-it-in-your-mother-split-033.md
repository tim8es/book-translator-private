Chapter 32: make me feel loved once more

<!-- bt-block:000002 -->
Chapter Notes

<!-- bt-block:000003 -->
**This is a Multi-POV ensemble story — chapters may focus on one pairing or move between multiple.**

<!-- bt-block:000004 -->
“What do you mean?”

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
Checo is still leaning in close, much closer than should feel *this* familiar. He’s looking into Max’s eyes with so much tenderness it pains them both — even if each one for a different reason. “Max,” he exhales, swallowing down every single *what-if* he forced onto himself over time, “how did you and Kelly start dating?”

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
*But…*

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
Without really trying to hide his annoyance, Max looks away with a sigh. “Why?”

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
*I don’t wanna talk about her.*

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
“Just… Just tell me,” Checo demands, touching Max’s cheek again, this time to make Max face him again. *“Please.”*

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
*No.*

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
Max furrows his eyebrows, his lips tight in a confused frown, and his defense walls — that Checo tore down over the months — built up high again. “We… *just* did. I don’t know what more to tell you,” he states bluntly. Jumping with his gaze from one of Checo’s eyes to the other, he sighs, wordlessly defeated by his ex-teammate. “She asked me out,” he offers with barely open eyes — as if holding the eye contact makes it not only more real, but also… harder to admit. *“Zeg maar,* she was there a few times, you know, when I went out with the team. After races and all that. Like of course, she *was* married to one of the drivers. *Natuurlijk,* she was there. When they broke it off, you know, *officially*… I’ve not seen her for a time. Six months, I think? Something like that. Then she asked me out. Just the two of us, *ja?”*

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
Nodding to show Max he’s still listening, Checo offers him a soft smile. He doesn’t say a single word out of worry that any disturbance might stop Max’s *speech*.

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
“It was fun,” Max pauses, biting his lower lip. “She’s… *nice,* I guess. Pretty. Made me feel important and *geliefd*. No one ever… I mean, it was new, *weet je wel?* All of it. Made me feel good. And I think she liked that I’m younger,” he admits as a soft blush creeps over his cheeks. “After some time, she said we should just go official. Since we’ve been exclusive for a while. And yeah, that… that was it. She asked, I said *yeah*, we moved in together. Been together for years before she kicked me out.”

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
“She kicked you out?” Checo breathes out in shock, his eyebrows high on his forehead.

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
Max huffs out in faux amusement. “Didn’t really mean to tell you that,” he scratches the top of his head with a grin, “but yeah. *Basically*. One day I came home and the keys didn’t—” he pauses in search for the right word, miming out with his hand a locking/unlocking gesture, “*work*. Changed the locks while I was gone. *Eigenlijk*, moved me out with her brother,” Max says, purposefully *forgetting* to mention that his father helped her get rid of him and his stuff as well. “But like… *eh,* we didn’t really… argue or anything. She just got rid of me.”

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
“That’s terrible,” Checo exhales, squeezing Max’s hand tighter. “I didn’t—”

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
*“Nou, ja,”* shrugging, Max admits plainly, “I didn’t wanna tell you. Or anyone, really.”

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
The room falls into uncomfortable silence for a second, only broken by two sets of breathing.

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
“Why did you even—”

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
*Why did you even ask about that?*

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
“I wanted to… *ah, pero ahora*, it feels *not right,”* Checo shakes his head.

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
*I just told you about mijn relatiebreuk. You can’t just… not tell me now.*

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
“Tell me,” Max doesn’t exactly ask, more demands than anything. “I just told you my ex kicked me out. There’s nothing you can say that’d… *you know.”*

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
Checo licks his lips. He’s not nervous, *not really,* but there is something all too obvious about the way his eyes become glossier the longer he looks at Max — like he’s the chosen one, the one who could do no wrong. “Move in with me,” he demands in the same way as he did while telling his parents they *need to* let him continue karting.

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
“Huh?” Max breathes out as his eyebrows knit together. “But… am I not… I already live here, *no?”*

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
Scrunching his face slightly, Checo nods, exasperated. *“Sí,* I meant — how do I say? — *como es debido*. Properly. On paper.”

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
*Oh.*

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
“We add your name on the lease,” Checo continues, ignoring the sudden heat in his cheeks, “and yeah. You live here. With me. Publicly.”

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
Max tilts his head to the right like a confused puppy, his hair falling out of its combed position slightly. “Like… for real?”

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
“Yeah,” Checo nods, “for real.”

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
Not bothering to hide his smile, Max nods too. “I’d… I’d like that.”

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
Checo parts his lips, readying up to say something else, to add *anything* that could make it even more obvious, when his phone rings out. He picks it up instantly, out of habit, with, “Listening, yes?” He waits a moment, hearing what the other person has to say, before adding, “I’ll buzz you in. Thanks. *See ya’.”* He hangs up sighing, before looking back at Max with an almost apologetic, “the food’s here.”

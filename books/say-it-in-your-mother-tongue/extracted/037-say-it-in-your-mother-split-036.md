<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
The van Checo rented is parked behind the building.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
Max clutches a box of trash bags and cleaning supplies to his chest, mumbling something under his breath as he walks up the *million* flights of stairs leading to a flat that he’s not been to in months, at this point. When he finally reaches *his* floor, he sets the box down onto the floor so he could fish out the keys from his pocket. With the first *click* of the key in the lock, he sighs — exhaling long and deep, like he’s been holding his breath for years just waiting for this moment.

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
He moves on autopilot, just barely noticing he’s opening the door and entering the place, before he places the box down onto an empty TV stand and swiftly moves to open the windows, allowing fresh air to envelop him.

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
*This is it, boys, this is —*

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
“What are we waiting for?”

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
Checo’s voice pulls him out of thoughts. Max looks over his shoulder at him, before turning completely. “Nothing,” he says with a smile.

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
They stand there, in an apartment that never really felt like *his*, and Max breathes out through his nose unable to force his lips to twist, to stop smiling.

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
Checo steps closer, leaving two meters of space between them, and offers Max his hand, nodding gently. He doesn’t say anything — whether because he’s not sure what to actually say or because none of his words feel big yet gentle enough for the situation, neither of them knows.

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
Looking away, Max lets his hand drop into Checo’s, watching from the corner of his eye as the man wraps his fingers around it.

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
“You okay?” Checo asks him, gentle and not expectant of anything.

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
Max nods. “Yeah,” he nods, “I think so.”

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
“Let’s finally move you out, then.”

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
The apartment is mostly empty — except for a few pieces of clothing forgotten here and there, and a couple of random *personal* objects he *totally still remembers owning*. Max is kneeling by a radiator in the living room, trying to fish out a dirty sock from behind it.

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
“How did you even get it there?” laughing, Checo asks as he wipes down one of the window panels. “Were you taking off your clothes like a stripper, throwing them,” he mimes out throwing a ball — only barely resisting the urge to add *‘Kobe!’* — with a wide grin, “away from yourself?”

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
“Fuck *off,”* Max groans, elbow deep between wall and radiator, “and can I not just,” he pauses, straining to fish it out, “keep it here?”

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
“Ew,” Checo laughs again, “stinky sock? *No way, Jose.”* Satisfied with the shine of the windowpane, he moves to another one, adding, “poor Irene would never get rid of this place again.”

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
“It doesn’t smell that bad,” Max spits out, his fingers *finally* grabbing something fabric-y. He pulls down and — with a metric ton of dust — yanks out a no-longer-white sock. “Hah! Got it!”

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
“Throw it out,” Checo commands, pointing his dry hand at one of the trash bags. “We ain’t taking that home.”

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
With a snigger, Max turns to look at Checo, his eyes wide, his lower lip pushed out in a pout. “Why not?” he asks in faux sadness.

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
“Stinky sock,” Checo shoots back without looking at Max, not giving him the satisfaction. “Dusty, dirty sock. Stinky.”

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
Max makes a noise low in his throat — partway between a whine and a hum — as he theatrically struts over to the bag with the sock laid in his hand like something holy. He lowers it into the trash bag with a sigh. “Goodbye, brave soldier,” he breathes out.

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
Rolling his eyes, Checo tells him, “Stop being dramatic, Max, and check if you left more socks around. Or underwear.”

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
“Now that would be mark to leave behind!” Max laughs, interrupted by Checo unamused voice.

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
“No, that’d be embarrassing.”

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
Thinking of what to say to that, Max shakes his head with a chuckle. “Irene could sell it,” he suggests, unable to finish his remark because his phone starts ringing. Max groans, but picks up the call with a faux cheerful, “Verstappen, speaking.”

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
“What a coincidence,” an all too familiar voice responds, full of poison and bitterness, “here as well.”

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
*Oh kut.*

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
Immediately stepping away from Checo, Max breathes out, *“Oh.”* His voice is trembling, and so are his hands as he steps inside the small kitchen (more a kitchenette than anything else). *“Hallo, pap.”*

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
*“Ah,* why so serious, boy? Not happy to hear your dad?”

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
*Not really*.

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
“No, that’s not… I’m in the middle of something,” Max stutters in Dutch, unable to *actually* oppose his father, let alone lie to him. “Do you need something?”

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
Max can hear Jos clear his throat, imaging the way the man is surely grinning, pleased that Max still expects to be scolded even at the ripe age of twenty-six.

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
“Do I have to have a reason to speak with my own son?”

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
*“Nee, pap,”* Max pushes out fast. “I meant… *why* are you calling? Why right now?”

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
Jos sighs into the phone. “Just wanted to hear you,” he claims — and Max knows it’s a lie. “Wanted to know how you’re doing.”

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
“I’m doing great,” Max shoots back, aware that it’s entirely too harsh but unable to really care. “No thanks to you.”

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
“Oh, that wasn’t very nice,” Jos tells him with fake sweetness in his voice. “I thought I taught you better than that.”

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
“Dad, *stop,”* Max cuts him off in English. He breathes in as much as he possibly can — to the point of chest ache — before asking, in Dutch again, “Why did you call me?”

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
There’s a pause.

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
Max knows Jos is still there — he can hear the way his father breathes into the microphone, the way he’s tapping into *something* with his other hand, can hear him swallow around a cigarette before taking a drag. His voice comes back like thunder, sharp and loud.

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
“Heard you’re a fag now.”

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
*Oh.*

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
Exhaling sharply, Max searches for words, unable to form even a single coherent thought with which he could shut his father up.

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
“Seems like the gossip is true, huh?” Jos continues once he realizes that Max isn’t going to respond fast enough. “Oh, how wonderful! My son is a fucking queer!”

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
With one hand on the counter, Max leans his head on the cabinet doors. He breathes against the wood — shallow breath in, shaky breath out. Going a million miles per hour, Max’s brain is trying to come up with *anything* to say, but nothing feels *right*. Claiming, *“I’m not gay,”* no matter how true feels like betrayal; avoiding the topic with, *“Where did you even hear that?”* won’t help; the mere idea of saying, *“You knew I’m bisexual,”* makes his fucking skin itch.

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
“So how is it? Is *he* fucking *you*, or are *you* fucking *him?* What am I even saying? Of course, you ain’t fucking him!” he barks in laughter into the phone. When Max’s side of the call stays quiet, Jos asks mockingly, “Got nothing to say to that?”

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
“What do you want me to say?” Max breathes out with tears running down his cheeks, hoping that his distress isn’t as audible in his voice as it feels.

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
“Honestly?”

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
Max sniffles. *“Ja.”*

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
“A good son would apologize and say he’s coming back home right this second,” Jos tells him in a flat tone, without any bitterness seeping through. “Even a shitty son would at least try to deny he’s turned into a poof.”

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->
Closing his eyes, Max thinks for a second. “And if I do neither?” he asks, pausing for a heartbeat, or several. “What does that make me?”

<!-- bt-block:000056 -->
“An idiot,” Jos responds. Max can picture the way he moves the phone from the left ear to the right, holding the cigarette between his lips as he does so, before he adds, “because only an idiot would give up his career to get some dick.”

<!-- bt-block:000057 -->
“Gay people can race in F1,” Max protests weakly, without realizing what he’s about to say. “Like Ralf—”

<!-- bt-block:000058 -->
“Maybe *some* gay guys can,” Jos growls, “but *you* won’t. I’ll make sure of that.” He waits for Max to say something, an apology or an excuse, even when he knows he’s not getting either. “So… which one will it be? Are you going to come to your senses, or will you rather say *bye-bye* to being a fucking driver?”

<!-- bt-block:000059 -->
Max squeezes the phone in his hand. His voice is barely there when he whispers, “I don’t need to come to my senses. I’ve never been more—”

<!-- bt-block:000060 -->
“No wonder Kelly broke things off,” Jos laughs into the microphone, cynical and angry, before adding, *“Max Vertappen* is a fucking fag!” His voice comes out loud and crunchy. “You brought shame on this family. I hope you’re proud of yourself.”

<!-- bt-block:000061 -->
Max inhales sharply. *“Papa—”*

<!-- bt-block:000062 -->
But before he can finish what he wanted to say, the call goes *out,* a soft *beep-beep-beep* informing Max that his father just hung up.

<!-- bt-block:000063 -->
He slams the phone down onto the countertop. “Fuck!” he screams, picking up the phone again. As tears surge down his face, Max throws the phone against a wall without flinching, watching as pieces of plastic fly all around. His voice louder, a slightly panicky, as he shouts again, “Fuck!”

<!-- bt-block:000064 -->
And then, Checo is there, standing in the door, his shoulders square and head high, an unreadable expression on his face. He doesn’t ask, *“Are you okay?”* nor does he step closer, wrapping his arms around Max no matter how much the small voice in his head tells him to do *just that*.

<!-- bt-block:000065 -->
He’s seen this version of Max before, after crashing in a race he was on his way to win, after someone stole his car, when Jos slammed the door in his face after a bad argument. He’s seen it before and knows what *not* to do.

<!-- bt-block:000066 -->
And so he just stands there, an immovable object against an unstoppable force, steady and firm in the way he watches Max’s every move.

<!-- bt-block:000067 -->
*How much of that did you hear?*

<!-- bt-block:000068 -->
Max turns away from him. He buries his face in his hands, whether to hide from Checo or himself, he doesn’t know.

<!-- bt-block:000069 -->
*I’m sorry. I’m so sorry. I’m so fucking sorry.*

<!-- bt-block:000070 -->
His sobs become louder, more intense, more painful with each passing second.

<!-- bt-block:000071 -->
*People are gossiping about you because of me. I’m so sorry for being—*

<!-- bt-block:000072 -->
“Max,” Checo speaks out quietly.

<!-- bt-block:000073 -->
Dropping his hands away from his face, Max doesn’t turn to look at him. “I’m sorry.”

<!-- bt-block:000074 -->
“Are you okay?” Checo asks, immediately following it with, “Did you get cut?”

<!-- bt-block:000075 -->
Max shakes his head. *“Nee.* Don’t think so.”

<!-- bt-block:000076 -->
“Good,” Checo says, still in the same spot as before. “Do you wanna sit down?”

<!-- bt-block:000077 -->
Shaking his head again, Max breathes out a simple, *“nee.”*

<!-- bt-block:000078 -->
A moment of silence passes between them before Checo asks, “Do you… I shouldn’t ask. Correct?”

<!-- bt-block:000079 -->
“Yeah,” Max’s voice cracks slightly, *“don’t.”*

<!-- bt-block:000080 -->
“I won’t,” confirming, Checo takes a first step forward. “We have some water in the car. I can go for it.”

<!-- bt-block:000081 -->
*“Nee.”*

<!-- bt-block:000082 -->
Checo takes another step forward. “Okay,” he says. He waits a second before he speaks again. “I have a tissue.”

<!-- bt-block:000083 -->
Max shakes his head again. *“Nee,”* he tells him, still turned away.

<!-- bt-block:000084 -->
As the apartment goes silent again, Checo shoves his hands into his pockets and says, in the calmest voice possible, “I’m still here. Not going anywhere, *baby.* I promise.”

<!-- bt-block:000085 -->
And with that, Max turns around.

<!-- bt-block:000086 -->
His eyes are half-lidded and glassy, and his tears are still coming, even if he begs them to stop, and he sniffles because snot threatens to make its escape, and his palms are sweaty — but none of that matters. Not when Checo’s looking at him *like that*, like he hung the moon and the stars and has never done anything wrong ever in the history of forever. Max swallows before he nods, not even entirely sure *why*.

<!-- bt-block:000087 -->
But Checo knows.

<!-- bt-block:000088 -->
He steps closer, his arms apart, as he pulls Max into an embrace. One of his hands finds its way into Max’s hair, his fingers tangling there with the familiarly soft strands; the other arm wraps around Max’s waist, holding him in their shared warmth.

<!-- bt-block:000089 -->
“He’s wrong,” Checo whispers as Max buries his face into the crook of Checo’s neck. “No matter what he said, he’s wrong.”

<!-- bt-block:000090 -->
*The thing is… he isn’t. Not really.*

<!-- bt-block:000091 -->
“And I’ll never not be proud of you, okay? I mean it. I’m proud of you, *leoncito*.”

<!-- bt-block:000092 -->
Max mumbles something into Checo’s neck, quiet and muffled.

<!-- bt-block:000093 -->
“Hm?” Checo hums.

<!-- bt-block:000094 -->
With a sigh, Max moves just enough to be able to speak properly. “I said,” he starts, grabbing onto Checo tighter, “he… isn’t. Not… not about this.”

<!-- bt-block:000095 -->
Checo pets his hair, combing through it with his fingers. “He is.”

<!-- bt-block:000096 -->
Max shakes his head slightly, as much as the position of his body allows him, as he repeats, “He isn’t.” No matter how hard he wishes for Checo to say anything, the Mexican stays quiet, and so Max stutters out, “I… I think… *I’m gay*. For you.”

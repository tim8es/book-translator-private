Chapter 38: fire, scabies, cough, and love

<!-- bt-block:000002 -->
Chapter Notes

<!-- bt-block:000003 -->
**This is a Multi-POV ensemble story — chapters may focus on one pairing or move between multiple.**

<!-- bt-block:000004 -->
“You’re upset.”

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
Placing down his mug with a loud *clank*, Max groans as few drops of coffee spill over the rim. His voice is low when he forces out, “Are you asking or informing me?”

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
Checo scans Max’s face, asking himself the same question. “What’s wrong?”

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
Instead of answering that, Max stands up from his spot at the kitchen table, making the chair slide backwards with a *screech*. He walks into the living room, moving barefoot past the sofa and coffee table towards *their* bedroom. He stops by one of the windows overlooking the sea, his hands deep in the pockets of his joggers, and breathes in deeply through his nose.

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
He doesn’t need to look over his shoulder to know that Checo is there, standing in the door with a puzzled look on his face — aware that something definitely *is* wrong.

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
“I’m upset,” Max admits, hushed.

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
He can hear the defeated smile in Checo’s voice when he says, “I see that.”

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
Max hums, nodding slightly, as he stares out. He follows a fast-moving boat with his eyes until it disappears behind a building obscuring his view. He exhales through his mouth, sighing as he does so, searching for *something* — be it outside, somewhere past the sea and past everything he knows, or *inside*, covered by his skull and layers of brain tissue.

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
“I’ve, *uhm,* I’ve been thinking,” Max speaks again, trying to sound casual and failing miserably. “About *this,”* he says, gesturing around himself with his right hand, “about racing and life and myself and… and *us.”*

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
Checo, still standing in the same spot to give Max space both metaphorically *and* literally, nods with a soft *hum*. He licks his lips before asking, “And… what did you *work* out?”

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
*“Niks,”* Max admits in Dutch. He turns around, meeting Checo’s gaze with wide eyes, as he breathes out, “Nothing. *Just…* nothing.”

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
Furrowing his eyebrows in confusion, Checo still nods. “And is that good?”

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
Max shrugs. “Wish I knew,” he says quietly. Biting his lower lip, Max looks down at the floor — as if it had any answers he didn’t think of already.

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
Checo takes a step towards Max, just one, moving barely half a meter closer. “I could help,” he offers gently. His hands itch with the need to pull Max into a tight hug. “If you want.”

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
Shrugging, Max follows Checo’s lead — moving away from the window, a breath closer to the only person that matters. He doesn’t look into Checo’s face, let alone his eyes, when he mutters, low and unsteady, “I’m not… I don’t *do* big speeches and ceremonies.”

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
With the softest of chuckles, Checo nods. “I know.”

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
Max nods as well. “But I thought about this a lot,” he continues, still in the same low tone, “like… for a *long* time. And I think… *this* might have to be a big speech.”

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
“I can make it then,” Checo offers, moving closer to Max again. “I don’t mind making it.”

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
Finally looking back into Checo’s eyes, Max’s breath hitches. He can feel his cheeks heating up, surely forming an *embarrassing* blush on his face, but *that* doesn’t matter now. All that matters is taking another step forward, shrinking the space between them to five feet, maybe even a mere meter. *“Ja,* you could.”

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
Checo has to look up slightly to keep looking into Max’s face as he inches even closer to his former teammate. “I think,” he starts, his eyes big and honest, “you want to say you never felt *this* before.”

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
Max nods, his blush deepening. *“Ja.”*

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
“And you are confused about why you feel like this now. Yeah?” Checo asks, pausing as he waits for Max’s responds. The Dutchman only nods, saying nothing, and so Checo continues, “You want to tell how *it* doesn’t have to change anything. That we can forget about the kiss and move on with living. Still correct?”

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
No longer able to hold the eye contact, Max looks away with, “Yeah, pretty much.”

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
Checo hums, pleased. “Okay,” he breathes out. “You are wrong.”

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
Flicking his gaze back, Max cocks his head to one side. “Why?”

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
“We can’t just… *forget* about it,” Checo says, swallowing before he’s able to continue. “I can’t.”

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
*“Oh,”* Max breathes out shakingly, his voice small and timid as he adds, “I can—”

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
“I can’t forget about it,” Checo doesn’t let him finish, instead lifting his hand and placing it on the warm skin of Max’s cheek. “Don’t want to. Ever,” he tells him, holding his gaze with as much affection as his face. “Why… I waited so long for it. I don’t want to forget it.”

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
Max furrows his eyebrows, raising them again, and his jaw muscles twitch as he’s trying to process what Checo just said. “You—”

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
There is no more space separating them; Checo’s chest brushes Max’s as he inhales deeply. “If you want… if… if *never again,”* Checo stutters out, his voice low but full of something Max doesn’t dare to name, “I understand. You don’t have to give me another one. But… leave me this one, *por favor.”*

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
Blinking slowly, Max searches Checo’s face, shaking his head ever so slightly. “I don’t understand.”

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
*“El amor es como la tos, no se puede reprimir,”* Checo says, his eyes painfully full and shiny as he holds Max’s gaze. “In English, I think is, *uh,* like, *love is like a cough, it’s hard to suppress.”*

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
Max nods at that. “In Dutch, we say, *‘Vier dingen laten zich niet verbergen: vuur, schurft, hoest en liefde.’* It means that only four things are hard to hide: fire, scabies, cough, and love.”

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
Checo offers him a smile. “Yeah,” he breathes out. “That… it’s true. That’s what I meant.”

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
Max flicks his gaze from one of Checo’s eyes to the other. He parts his lips slightly, breathing out his mouth, as his eyes widen in realization.

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
“I can’t pretend it never happened,” Checo tells him, his voice steady no matter how trembling his hands are, “because I waited so long for it. If you want to forget… I get it. But I *can’t.”*

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
Nodding, Max covers Checo’s hand on his cheek with his own. He licks his lips before saying, *“From the moment I met you, all those years ago, not a day has gone by when I haven’t thought of you. And now that I’m with you again, I’m in agony. The closer I get to you, the worse it gets.”*

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
“You stole my line,” Checo breathes out with a wide smile.

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
“I stole *Anakin’s* line,” Max says, leaning down to touch Checo’s forehead with his own. He closes his eyes as he whispers, “but you can say the rest.”

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
And that is exactly what Checo does. His breath is warm on Max’s face as he quotes, *“The thought of not being with you, I can’t breathe. I’m haunted by the kiss you should never have given me. My heart is beating, hoping that kiss will not become a scar. You are in my very soul, tormenting me. What can I do? I will do anything you ask. If you are suffering as much as I am, please tell me.”*

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
“I *am* suffering as much as you are,” Max breathes out, his voice shaky. “All I can think about is you. Only you.”

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
Max doesn’t wait for anything else, closing the distance between them — sealing their words with his lips.

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
Checo’s lips are softer than he remembers them being from the day before; softer and warmer and they taste like a brand of coffee Max doesn’t really like, but that doesn’t matter.

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
Checo’s hand is on Max’s waist, squeezing lightly as he pulls the man closer, as close as possible, maybe even closer.

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
Max moans into the kiss. He licks at Checo’s lips, begging for invitation, which he’s given immediately.

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
To Checo’s surprise, Max doesn’t kiss the way he drives.

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
He doesn’t fight for dominance, doesn’t rush, he lets Checo set the rhythm, meeting him with eagerness and quiet moans that Checo steals right from his mouth, swallowing them down.

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
They only part once the need for air becomes too much to handle.

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
Max’s lips are red, slightly swollen, and his cheeks burn from blushing hard. He inhales slowly, deep into his lungs, as he touches his lips with his fingertips. “Do… do I have to quote that every time I want to kiss you, or can I just… *ask?”*

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
“You don’t have to ask,” Checo tells him, caressing his cheek. “We can just kiss.”

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
Max nods. “Okay,” he breathes out into Checo’s face. His lips ghost Checo’s lightly, it’s barely a real touch, before repeating, “we can just kiss.”

The table shakes a little as Gabriel bounces his right leg.

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
He’s wearing headphones, hooded eyes barely open, tapping out a fast rhythm into the table with his fingertips. His tongue keeps peeking out of his mouth, and each time it does Nico cannot help but notice.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
*Behave, you old creep,* he scolds himself, averting his gaze to anything else he could pretend to be staring at. A potted plant in dire need of watering. Gabriel’s hands. His team issued jacket draped over the back of an empty chair. Gabriel’s hands. The telemetry data opened on his laptop that he’s pretending to review. *Gabriel’s hands*.

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
Nico stands up. His chair, pushed with the backs of his legs, almost falls over as he abruptly, without explanation, leaves the common area they’re supposed to share.

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
Desperate for any sense of sanity, he retreats into a tiny bathroom closest to him, locking the door. His hand lingers on the built-in key and he lets his head fall forward, hitting his forehead on the cold wood. “*Ernsthaft, was zum Teufel ist los mit mir*? Seriously, what the hell is wrong with me?” he breathes out, his voice shaky. It’s the ninth time he’s found himself asking that exact question in the past eight days.

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
He stands up straight again, running the palms of his hands over his black polo shirt as if smoothing the fabric out, and moves towards the sink. A mirror hangs above it, narrow and too truthful, which is why Nico keeps his eyes fixed on the white porcelain basin as he runs the cold water over his hands. He lets it collect in his palms before splashing it over his face.

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
Not bothering with drying his face, he unlocks the door again and steps into the hallway while shaking out his hands. The bitter taste lingers in his mouth, not allowing him to forget the thoughts he keeps having about his twenty-year-old teammate.

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
“Nico?”

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
*Fuck me*.

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
“You alright?”

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
Putting on the best neutral expression imaginable, Nico turns to face his *colleague*. “Oh, perfectly,” he says, his voice forcefully flat.

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
Gabriel smiles, a genuine pretty thing, nodding. “I got worried. You run away like *vroom* and gone,” he chuckles, carefree and happy.

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
The youthful joy radiating from him twists the knife in Nico’s stomach, pushing it even deeper into his core. *Just kill me, please*. He presses his lips into a thin line.

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
“Ah,” Gabriel breathes out, invading Nico’s personal space with the determination of a squirrel with a mission, his warmth and smell all encompassing.

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
*He has a new cologne.*

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
“You have a — *Como se diz em inglês?* — eye… hair. Eyelash! *Sim*. You have an eyelash on your cheek,” Gabriel says, chipper as ever. His hand is halfway to Nico’s face when he asks, gesturing with his hand, “Can I?”

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
And Nico — the poor sinner — just nods, not trusting his words not to betray him if he dared to respond.

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
His eyes follow the slender hand, and when a soft finger touches his face, he audibly sighs. *His hands are so soft,* he thinks as he watches Gabriel bring the eyelash into their shared space. *How are his hands so soft?*

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
“Blow,” Gabriel smiles with the eyelash on his finger, his accent more noticeable the happier he gets, “*do* a wish.”

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
Nico swallows and nods, again. “Okay,” he exhales. And then, something inside him — somewhere in his deep, depraved mind — makes him *touch* Gabriel. He wraps his fingers around Gabriel’s wrist and brings his hand closer to his mouth, pretending not to notice how easy it feels.

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
Their eyes meet, then, Gabriel’s chocolate meeting Nico’s steel in a characteristic eagerness.

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
Nico parts his lips, pursing them, without letting go of the eye contact. His thumb is resting on the inside of Gabriel’s wrist, over his veins, and he doesn’t stop himself in time from caressing the younger man’s *honey* skin. The heartbeat under his finger is fast. It stutters once when Nico’s thumb moves.

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
*Why did I notice that?*

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
He finally lowers his gaze before closing his eyes.

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
*Normal people don’t act like this*.

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
But no amount of self-chastising can stop the next thought from creeping through.

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
*Ich wünsche mir, dass he doesn’t regret meeting me.*

With screeching tires, the car comes to an abrupt halt.

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
The rain is falling in thick, heavy drops, wetting everything it touches, pooling in every little dimple, on every slightly uneven surface.

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
Lance lets out a shaky breath, fogging up the window; for the first time since Fernando kissed him, they have a chance to spend some time alone. *Completely alone*. On the outside, however, he’s wearing a suit of confidence and cockiness. “Are you going to hold your jacket over my head,” he shoots towards his teammate, smirking, “or do you honestly expect me to walk *in that* unprotected?”

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
“Don’t you worry your pretty head, *princesa*,” Fernando grins at him, more shark than a friend. Winking, he adds, “I won’t let you dissolve.”

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
And then he’s out, walking around the car to the passenger door, carrying something that looks suspiciously like an umbrella.

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
Lance meets his eyes through the window. His hands are trembling, sweating a little — maybe more than a little — which forces him to wipe his palms on his dark pants. Watching as Fernando swiftly opens up the umbrella, Lance can feel a blush creeping onto his cheeks.

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
Outside, Fernando pauses. He’s holding the umbrella in his left hand, but it is *not* above him. The rain is drenching him, wetting his hair, his clothes, *his face*, almost like he’s not even aware of it. Like the only thing that matters is that Lance stays dry.

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
With his right hand, he opens the passenger door. He’s looking at Lance — into his eyes, into his very soul — through the rain with a smile on his lips. “*Princesa*,” is all he says, holding out his right hand.

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
The words Lance carefully prepared to shoot while rolling his eyes, or maybe even laughing a little, are *not there*, and he just swallows. His fingers make contact with Fernando’s hand while his eyes refuse to meet the other pair. He’s stepping out of the car, the umbrella covering him like a shield from a single drop of water falling onto him.

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
Fernando pushes the car door closed with his knee, locking the vehicle with a key he produced from his pocket, before giving Lance a look that says way too much, yet answers no questions.

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
They are standing side by side, one dry, the other utterly drenched.

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
“Let’s get you inside, hm?” Fernando purrs with a wink. “You’re too pretty to catch a cold.”

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
“I know,” Lance smiles, his bravado back in full power as he winks at Fernando.

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
He’s been at this place hundreds of times, crashing here every now and then during off-season testing and factory work, but today, it feels different. The small, unassuming house twelve miles from Silverstone — Fernando’s off-season sanctuary — welcomes them almost too eagerly.

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
“After you, *princesa*,” Fernando says as he holds the main door open for him.

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
“Are you this *polite* to all your guests, or am I special?” Lance asks as he waltzes in, his steps steady even when his hands don’t want to stop shaking. He turns to look at Fernando again, licking his lips.

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
The Spaniard raises one eyebrow, smirking. “*Princesa*,” he growls as he steps inside, kicking the door closed behind him without looking, “you’re confusing me with someone else. *This* has nothing to do with politeness.”

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
Everything inside Lance is shouting at him to stop, to back out when he still has the chance. But Lance has never been one to obey those thoughts. “Oh, but you’ve always been so *polite* to me,” he breathes out in faux confusion.

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
The space between them — two, maybe three meters maximum — could swallow them both and never spit them out. It’s Fernando who steps closer. *“Was I, now?”* his voice is lower than before.

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
“Nice and polite,” Lance nods with a wide smile, certain that he’s pushing some sort of boundary, some unspoken rule, “like a well-trained guard dog.”

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
And that’s what *does it*.

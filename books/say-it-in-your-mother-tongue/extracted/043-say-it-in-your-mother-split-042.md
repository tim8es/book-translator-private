“It’s only three days.”

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
Max makes a rumbling noise somewhere deep in his chest, throwing another T-shirt into his Red Bull branded duffel bag before he turns to look at Checo, who is infuriatingly already done with packing. “I *know,”* he whines. Grabbing another shirt, he crumples it up into a ball and pushes it deep into the bag, surely messing up everything already in there.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
“Want some help?” Checo asks, walking to where Max is sitting on the floor.

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
“No,” Max shoots back angrily. He grabs the handles of the bag, flipping it upside down. They both watch as his clothes fall out onto the ground. Max looks up at Checo with big eyes, frowning a little as he mutters, “Yes.”

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
Checo offers him a smile as he kneels next to him. “How many shirts do you want?”

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
“I don’t know,” Max admits looking away, and something about his voice makes Checo pause. He sounds small and scared and so very un-Max-like.

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
Shaking his head, Checo stands up again with one hand stretched out in an offering. When Max looks up at him again, Checo gives him another smile. “Come,” he tells Max with a soft nod.

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
The Dutchman furrows his eyebrows but accepts the hand, letting Checo pull him up onto his feet. “I should pack,” he protests weakly, all too happy to abandon the worst part about getting to travel for work.

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
Checo hums in agreement. “Yeah, you should,” he says, pulling on Max’s hand as he himself backs out of *their* bedroom. “But doing it depressed won’t help you do it faster. And I think I have just the thing to make it better.”

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
“Unless you have a gun, I don’t think you have *the thing,”* Max groans, but he still follows Checo obediently out of *their* bedroom into the living room and then out of the living room as well, stopping only when they reach the front door. “Where’re we going?”

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
Watching as Max puts on his shoes, Checo rubs his face and says, “Out.”

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
“Bitch,” Max shoots back with a chuckle.

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
Checo laughs as he pulls Max into a one-armed hug. “You better watch your *fucking* language,” he says, kissing him on the right corner of his mouth.

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
“Sorry, *dad,”* Max sniggers, before grimacing and adding, “that was weird.”

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
“A little,” Checo laughs again. He reaches for the door handle with, “Now let’s go.”

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
“Wait,” Max breathes out. His hand is on the door, forcibly keeping it closed. *“Uh,* close your eyes,” his voice is a little breathless as he tells Checo.

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
Checo nods, closing his eyes with a word.

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
With a shaky breath, Max leans closer to Checo. There’s only centimeters separating their faces now, a fact of which Max is acutely aware when he moves even closer. He takes a deep breath and presses his lips to Checo’s.

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
They don’t make out, their lips *disconnect* after just a few seconds. The kiss is gentle, and sweet, and something that feels utterly normal and ultimately special at the same time.

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
When they part, Max’s face is flushed. He’s smiling down at Checo as he says, “now we can go.”

Before Fernando even types out his response, there’s a *knock-knock-knock* coming from the front door of Lance’s apartment. He sighs, getting up from his sofa with, “Yeah, coming.”

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
Checking the peephole, Lance barely resists sighing again. He unlocks the door as casually as possible, stepping out of the way for his father before either of them even says *hello*.

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
“Someone here has a terrible taste in music,” Lawrence complains, shutting the door behind himself with a loud *bang*. “You can hear it all the way from the fucking elevator. Disgusting.”

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
*Hello to you too, papa.*

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
Lance rolls his eyes with his back turned to his dad. “Do you want something to drink?” he asks, relaxing his face before he forces it into a fake smile reserved for Lawrence only. “I have a new coffee maker.”

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
*“Non,* you suck at making coffee,” Lawrence says as he walks through the apartment like it’s his own. “You should clean here more.”

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
*For fuck’s sake, how can I fucking suck at making coffee in a coffee maker?*

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
“It *is* clean here,” Lance groans as he turns on the machine to make himself a cup of something that would make Lawrence’s visit less annoying. “I vacuumed right before you came, *papa.”*

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
Lawrence gives him a *look* before he shakes his head and looks over the living room. “Sure,” he says as he sits down on the sofa.

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
Once the coffee finishes making, Lance grabs the mug, and while *already* sipping on the dark and deliciously hot liquid, he walks to the living room as well. He doesn’t sit on the sofa next to his father, choosing instead to plop down an old armchair that he knows his father dislikes with a burning passion. “How’s the factory, *papa?* Everything good?”

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
Tilting his head to one side, Lawrence huffs out in amusement. “Since when do you care about the factory?”

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
*Since you employed Fernando, papa. You know that better than anyone.*

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
“Of course I care about the factory,” Lance shoots back with a breath more force than intended. “Why would you think I don’t, *papa?”*

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
Lawrence eyes him suspiciously, raising his eyebrows slightly. He hums as if in thought before stating, “You seem… *comfortable.* I noticed you’ve been smiling a lot more lately.” His voice is low but so very sharp it makes Lance’s teeth itch.

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->
*No thanks to you, papa, that’s for sure.*

<!-- bt-block:000056 -->
“Life has been good to me lately, *papa,”* Lance responds, going for casual but landing defensive instead.

<!-- bt-block:000057 -->
*“Hm,”* Lawrence hums again, low in his throat, “I am glad to hear that.”

<!-- bt-block:000058 -->
*Are you, papa?*

<!-- bt-block:000059 -->
“But you should keep in mind that appearances matter,” Lawrence adds with a too stiff of a smile.

<!-- bt-block:000060 -->
“I *am* keeping that in mind, *papa,”* Lance says with a nod. “Though I doubt that seeing me smiling could bring any bad publicity to the team, *papa.”*

<!-- bt-block:000061 -->
Lawrence looks into Lance’s eyes, holding his gaze in a steel-jaw trap of eye contact, before telling his son, *“Smiling* might not,” he pauses for a breath, “but everyone loves noticing small things about drivers. And seeing that your priorities have… *shifted* recently, to say the least… *that* could invite people to gossip about the team. And we wouldn’t want that, would we now?”

<!-- bt-block:000062 -->
*“Non, papa,”* Lance breathes out shakily. “I’m sorry, *papa.”*

<!-- bt-block:000063 -->
With a smile that doesn’t quite reach his eyes, Lawrence gets up from the sofa. He walks past a small coffee table, past Lance’s armchair, past a photo of the team’s end-of-season celebration from 2023. He stops in the small hall leading to the front door. “Don’t get up,” he tells Lance, “I know where the exit is.” He pauses for a second before adding, “I’m not the one forgetting where I came from.”

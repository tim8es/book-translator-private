The pastries feel heavy in his hand.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
Nico doesn’t take the elevator, instead, he runs up the stairs, taking them three at a time.  The *pains au chocolat* bump the donuts, one of each flavor they had in the bakery that Nico changed his running route for.

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
The whole run, he couldn’t shake the guilt that itched its way *up,* from his stomach over his chest, crawling past his collarbones and over his neck until it plastered itself *fully* over him, wrapping him like the toughest clingwrap, hardening around him like a calloused second skin. And as he stood in the queue, looking over the baked goods, he couldn’t fight the feeling that this time, he might have fucked up for good.

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
“What’ll it be?” a middle-aged woman asked him as it became his turn.

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
Nico huffs. “What is the best?”

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
“Oh, that’s hard. The croissants are good, people like them a lot, but I personally love the peach pie the most.”

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
But Nico took neither, instead buying seven donuts and four pains au chocolat which he plans to use as a peace offering, or maybe a courting gift, he doesn’t really know. Finally on *their* floor, Nico forces his feet to slow down.

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
*Are you still upset with me? Yeah, makes sense. Got you pains au chocolat to apologize, though…*

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
He stops in front of the door of their suite, in one hand holding a bag with the pastries, in the other a keycard. Sighing, he closes his eyes.

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
*Gabriel, sorry I continue being an asshole. Care for a donut?*

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
He shakes his head.

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
*Hey, kid, what’s up? I have feelings for you and they’re driving me insane. Want some food?*

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
Nico sighs again. He lifts his hand and *beeps* the card onto the reader. The door opens with a soft *hush*, instantly clocking something’s wrong the second he’s in their suite. “Gabriel?” he calls out his name even if he feels, deep in his bones, that he’s not getting a response. “Gabriel?”

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
The suite is empty.

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
Not *completely* empty, the furniture still remains, but all signs of life are gone. On Nico’s pillow, there is a folded piece of printer paper with *NICO* written on the outside.

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
He drops the food onto the table as he steps towards the bed on shaky legs. *“Oh,”* he breathes out, reaching for the note with trembling hands.

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
» *Went out. I will come back, but don’t know when. No wait for me. GB* «

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
Sitting down on the bed, Nico rereads the note again. He scans the paper, as if he might have missed something — *anything*. He lets out a soft groan, dropping the paper onto the floor, right between his running shoes. *“Fuck,”* he breathes out as he pulls out his phone, still playing his running mix.

He doesn’t bother with changing his clothes — or even taking off his running shoes that are way too uncomfortable to drive in — before he darts out of the room. Nico grasps the bag with baked goods in his right hand, aside from his phone and car keys the only thing he brought out of the room, as he sprints down the hallway. The stairs feel longer going down than they did going up, but Nico doesn’t care. All he knows in the moment is how to put one foot in front of the other.

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
His night blue RSQ8 is waiting for him outside, like a trusty steed. Nico all but jumps behind the wheel. He quickly types out the address of Sebastian’s house, checking if there’s any issues on the road to Thurgau, before sighing. He stares at his phone screen, his hand trembling as he writes a quick message to Sebastian.

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
On the small laptop screen, Hayden Christensen and Natalie Portman share a pear-like fruit that they eat with knife and fork.

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
Checo braces himself for the next scene, waiting impatiently for the set to change to the much dark and softer one. He looks over at Max, who is sitting on the same side of the airplane table as Checo, which is highly unusual.

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
He’s leaning his elbow on his knee, bent over so he could be the closest to the screen without obscuring *too much* of Checo’s view. Max is biting the nails of his right hand, as if the movie — that he’s seen at least seventeen hundred times — could still surprise him with something new and unexpected.

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
The color scheme changes. Fire flickers on the screen. And Checo takes a deep breath.

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
Talking in unison with *Anakin*, he says, “*From the moment I met you, all those years ago, not a day has gone by when I haven’t thought of you. And now that I’m with you again,”* he pauses *just* long enough, *“I’m in agony. The closer I get to you, the worse it gets.”*

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
Max turns to him, lips parted, one of his nails still bitten between teeth.

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
Holding *strong* eye contact, Checo continues, *“The thought of not being with you… I can't breathe. I'm haunted by the kiss that you should never have given me. My heart is beating… hoping that kiss will not become a scar. You are in my very soul, tormenting me… what can I do?”* He takes a sharp breath. *“I will do anything you ask,”* Checo says with maybe a bit too much emotion behind it. *“If you are suffering as much as I am, please tell me.”*

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
Max licks his lower lip on instinct. *“Wow,”* he breathes out. *“Padme* is stronger than me, let me tell you. If someone just told *me* that? I’d fold in half like a lawn chair,” Max says with a soft blush high on his cheeks. He turns his face *back*, watching the movie as Natalie’s character rejects *Anakin’s* advances.

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
Checo exhales, closing his eyes with an exasperated expression. He reaches out to the laptop, pressing the spacebar with the tip of his middle finger to pause the movie. “Max,” he lets out, “I just *told you* and you didn’t fold.”

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
“Well, *yeah,”* Max nods, looking back at Checo, “but that’s different. I know you don’t mean it like *that*.” He turns the movie back on. Shrugging, he adds, *“He* wants to bang her. *We* are just friends.”

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
Running a hand over his face, Checo looks *up*, towards the ceiling of their plane — metaphorically searching for heaven. He sighs, shaking his head, before asking *anyone listening*, *“Dios mío,* you saw that, right?”

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
Max just hums, “*Hm*?” before adding, “Talking to me?”

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
“No,” Checo shakes his head again, *“just* thinking out loud.”

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
“Okay,” Max gives him a wide smile. “Did you know that in the flower field, George Lucas let Natalie and Hayden improvise their lines? They didn’t keep many of them, though, because — as both Natalie and Hayden admitted — the conversation turned flirty way too fast for Lucas’ liking,” he offers before turning back to watch the movie.

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
Checo sighs. *“You only tell me every time we watch it,”* he doesn’t say, no matter how much he’d love to. “That’s fun, *baby,”* he says instead, leaning back into the backrest of his seat. For only two people, the plane is entirely too big, but Checo doesn’t care. He’d rather *not* watch a movie at *this* volume with other passengers present; it’s enough for members of the flight crew to walk past them every now and then.

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
Max lets out a soft *hum*, lost in the movie. “I can’t imagine anyone else playing *Anakin,”* he breathes out. He glances at Checo as he says, “That’s like someone else being number 11. It just wouldn’t work.”

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->
“Yeah,” Checo breathes out, his voice trembling just enough to be noticeable. He blinks rapidly, willing away the tears that formed in his eyes, before he nudges Max’s arm with his. It’s brief contact, a fleeting friendly gesture, but the warmth of it lingers between them. Not trusting himself not to say something *stupid* and entirely too honest, Checo chooses to go for the safest option. “We’ll be landing soon, I think.”

Chapter 45: unconditionally and irrevocably

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
“Boring.”

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
Nico shoots Gabi a confused look, his eyebrows raised high into his forehead. “You put it on,” he reminds his teammate. “You *chose* this one.”

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
Gabriel skips to the next song before the previous one fully finishes, giggling as he admits, “Oh, this one is better!”

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
It turns out that the *better one* is a comedy song called *Half Horse Half Man* by OCT, a fact that Nico doesn’t comment on as much as one would expect — he only shrugs, nodding to the beat.

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
“How long still?” Gabi asks as he scrolls on his phone trying to pick the next track to play, ignoring all the songs he usually listens to while in car — given the fact that all of them feel a little too sad and *break up-y* for a drive to their *actual* date.

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
“I think,” Nico says while looking at the navigation’s on screen mini-map in the middle of the car’s dashboard, “like fifteen, maybe twenty minutes.”

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
Gabriel nods with way too mischievous of a smile, “Good.” He queues four more songs to play after the current one ends before hiding his phone in the pocket of his shorts. “What they asked you about?”

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
*“Hm?”* Nico hums, glancing at Gabi for barely a second. “Who?”

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
“The press,” Gabi says, rolling his eyes a little, even if he knows Nico cannot see it. “They ask about me?”

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
Nico chuckles, nodding weakly. “Sort of,” he tells his teammate, offering a genuine smile. “This one guy asked me if it feels weird that you’re *so much* younger than me. With extra stress on the *so much* part.”

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
“Rude.”

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
“That’s what Alex said,” Nico breathes out, pulling out of the wide speedway into a much narrower street. “And they wouldn’t let it go, either. Most questions for me were about mentorship or age. Or mentorship *and* age.”

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
“No one asked me like that,” Gabriel admits in hushed tone, looking at Nico through his dark eyelashes. Wordlessly, he blinks a few times before adding, “just if I’m excited and stuff.”

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
“That’s good,” Nico tells him softly, letting go of the steering wheel with his right hand and reaching over the central console to squeeze Gabi’s left thigh gently. “Hm?”

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
Gabriel’s cheeks grow pink, slowly turning redder and redder as he realizes Nico has no intention of removing his hand again. *“Yeah,”* he agrees quietly as the next song starts playing, this time The Weeknd’s *São Paulo.* He hums with the song, trying to focus on anything but the heat of Nico’s palm.

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
“That’s Portuguese, isn’t it?” Nico asks suddenly, pulling Gabi out of his attempt to ignore Nico’s hand on his thigh. Seeing Gabi nod out of the corner of his eye, Nico asks, “What is she singing about?”

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
Shaking his head, Gabriel just breathes out, “No.”

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
“No?” Nico repeats in a questioning tone. “What do you mean?”

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
“Is… *dirty,”* Gabi admits with a giggle. “She’s singing about her,” he gestures around his crotch vaguely, lowering his voice on the next word, *“pussy.”*

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
Nico bursts out laughing. “Ah,” he says with a nod, “got it. *Pretty* dirty.”

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
“Yeah,” Gabi breathes out, “my mum would be upset if she heard. *That* dirty.”

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
“You’re the one who put it on,” Nico protests weakly, shaking his head. “Don’t say it like it’s my fault.”

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
Gabriel mutters something under his breath, folding his arms on his chest with a played-up pout. “Will play *Baby Shark* next.”

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
*“Ah, Liebling,* don’t say that,” Nico says gently, squeezing Gabi’s thigh again to get the *boy* to look at him again. “I’ll win you anything you want.”

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
Angling his head towards Nico slightly, Gabi tells Nico, still pouting, “You can’t buy me with just *any* arcade prize.” He taps his chin, miming a thinking gesture, before adding, “But maybe will think of something, *hm.”*

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
Nico hums in agreement, checking the small map again. “We’ll be there soon,” he informs both of them, right as the next song starts playing, a cover of Lana Del Rey’s *Video Games* by Trixie Mattel.

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
*“They say that the world was built for two, only worth living if somebody is loving you,”* Trixie sings from the speakers as Nico pulls into the parking space in front of the arcade, ending the song with, *“And, baby, now you do.”*

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
They undo their seatbelts in sync, each of them stepping out of the car onto their respective sides. Nico looks nervously at his shoes, like a teenager before his first date, as Gabi walks up to him with a bright smile on his lips. Hesitating to meet his eyes only for a split second, Nico smiles back as he breathes out, “I *might’ve* rented out the place.”

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
“What?” Gabi laughs, expecting Nico to start laughing as well — but when he doesn’t, he pauses, asking, “Like… *really?”*

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
Nico nods, admitting a quiet, “Yeah. It seemed… *reasonable*. At the time.” He swallows loudly, raising his eyebrows higher than they’re already sitting as he thinks, *‘Well, might as well do it now and get it over with.’* He takes a deep breath, nodding again — not in agreement, but to push himself to admit *it*. “As did *this,”* he adds as he opens the fifth door of the Sauber-branded Audi car their team provided for them to travel around Australia in.

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
There, in the trunk, lies Gabriel’s jacket — the one forgotten in Belgium, the one retrieved by a determined Nico on a mission, the one that might *make or break* them.

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
“Is that— it *isn’t,”* Gabi breathes out, flicking his eyes between the jacket and Nico’s face repeatedly. “It is, isn’t it?”

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
Nico nods again, unable to look Gabriel in the face as he admits, “I thought you might want it back.”

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
*“How?”* Gabi asks, his voice higher than normally. “How did you *get* it?”

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
“I picked it up,” Nico says in a flat tone. His face is expressionless, which — in itself — is more telling than if he admitted it *straight up*. “At the airport.”

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
“In Brussels.”

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
Nico nods once. *“Ja.”*

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
“You drove to *Bélgica* for it,” Gabriel doesn’t ask, stating it instead. “From your house. You… *that* day— you weren’t texting me all day. Is this,” he points at the jacket (which is still lifelessly lying in the trunk), “why?”

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
*“Ja,”* Nico nods again. His eyes are half-lidded, looking somewhere past the car, and he fails to register that Gabriel *isn’t* upset. He swallows loudly, still not looking at Gabriel, when he breathes out, “Surprise?”

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
“You’re insane.”

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
Nico finally looks at Gabriel, the words pulling him back into the moment more directly. “What?”

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
“You’re insane,” Gabi repeats, his lips curled *up* in a comfortable smile. He *counts* on his fingers when he says, “Bonkers. Mental. Crazy. You’re all… *that.”*

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
“I mean—”

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
“And *you* told *me* to not get you something,” Gabriel continues, furrowing his eyebrows in faux anger. He giggles before shrugging and adding, “Good thing didn’t listen.”

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
They look at each other — still standing in the middle of a very public sidewalk, the hood of the car opened, people walking past them like it’s just a normal Thursday — and something about the whole situation makes them burst out in laughter.

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
Gabi shakes his head, comfortable and amused, with his curls jumping around lightly, and Nico realizes just how deeply *fucked* he is — no longer able to tell himself this is all just his utterly confused mind playing tricks on him. He knows, in that very moment, under the scorching Australian sun, as he grabs Gabi’s hand, that he is *unconditionally and irrevocably in love with him*.

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
He doesn’t say that out loud, though, choosing to ask instead, “Can I still win you a stuffed animal?”

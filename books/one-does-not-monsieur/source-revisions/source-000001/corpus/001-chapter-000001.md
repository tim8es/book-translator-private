# One Does Not, Monsieur

by [starlite_starbrite](https://archiveofourown.org/users/starlite_starbrite/pseuds/starlite_starbrite)

Summary

*A Triwizard AU: Durmstrang Arian × Beauxbatons Caleb.*

Rule one of the de Blanchemers: never trust a Conti.
Rule two: never let anyone close.
Rule three, which nobody thought to write down: never let a Durmstrang boy take a leaf out of your hair. He will only take it as encouragement.

Caleb de Blanchemer is charming to everyone and close to no one. Arian von Conti has no words for courting, no patience and no sense of personal space. What he has is a stare like a lit hearth, and a habit of leaving dead animals on doorsteps.

“One does not, monsieur,” Caleb tells him. Repeatedly.

Arian is not listening. He is watching Caleb’s mouth.

Notes

Season 2 is out today, and I’m posting this to celebrate! I won’t get to watch it until the weekend (work deadlines, alas). This was written before Season 2 aired, so apologies in advance for any discrepancies with canon.

This fic is dedicated to **Emo Universe**, for creating Caleb and Arian, and to **JINX**, who came up with the idea for this AU, without whom this fic would not exist in the first place!

A quick note on names:
In this AU, Caleb is French and goes to Beauxbatons, and Arian is Russian and goes to Durmstrang, to suit their schools.
Canon Caleb Whitmore is English, so he really belongs at Hogwarts, and since it bothered me far too much to send him to Beauxbatons with an English surname... I made him French.
Whitmore → White → *Blanche*, plus a little geography (the English Channel → the sea → *mer*), and *voilà*: Caleb de Blanchemer (Caleb of the White Sea).
It then only seemed fair that Arian became Russian and got a name change too, so he became Arian von Conti, lol.

**Who's who:**
The Whitmores are the de Blanchemers, a pureblood French family: Andrew, Matilda, Eleanor, and twins Cecily and Caleb (Cecily is older by eleven minutes).
Arian is the second son of the von Contis, a pureblood Russian family. His older brother Dario (OC) is the heir, and their cousins Rocco and Vittorio (also OCs) are Dario's minions at Durmstrang. Mirek, the first-year Arian looks out for, is borrowed from my other RBMK fic, [*Where the Shadow Leans*](https://archiveofourown.org/works/93434231).

A note on all the French: it has been a LONG while since I used it properly, so pardon my French!

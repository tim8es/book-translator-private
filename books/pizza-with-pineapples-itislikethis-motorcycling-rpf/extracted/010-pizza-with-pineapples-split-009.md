*2023, Le Mans*

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
Alex emerged from the medical center, wiping away the residual sweat on his forehead.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
His phone was silent. No new messages, no new calls.

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
Alex tried not to spiral.

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
He called Valentino. His phone rang. Once. Twice. Four times. Seven.

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
Then, on the tenth ring, the call was declined.

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
Alex took a deep breath. Placed his phone back in his pocket.

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
Valentino was likely busy. Likely worrying over Luca. He had no time for Alex, no time for their— mm, acquaintanceship.

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
Not, especially, after Alex had crashed into Luca.

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
In truth, Alex wanted to call back. But their system had rules, and when a call was declined, you could not call back. Not until the other person sent a message, or called on their own. So, Alex would have to wait.

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
Alex waited.

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
He waited a week.

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
A week of silence, of glancing at his phone, of trying his best not to let the nerves eat him alive. He focused on Marc, on his racing, on his training.

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
A week passed before Alex decided he had to do something.

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
The call rang. Once. Twice. Four times. Seven.

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
Alex pressed his eyes closed.

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
Then, on the tenth ring, the call went through.

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
Alex’s eyes sprang open, breath caught in his throat.

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
There was no sound from the other side. Not even of quiet breathing. Alex pulled the phone away from his ears. Connected.

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
He swallowed. Pressed the phone to his ear again.

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
“ . . . Ciao, Vale,” he said.

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
Silence. For one, two, three seconds—

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
“Ciao, Tola,” came Valentino’s voice, quiet and terse.

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
And Alex—

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
“I’m sorry,” he rushed out. “I’m sorry, Vale, I didn’t mean to crash into him, I— Bezzecchi was in the inside, and I’d been behind Zarco in the corner, so I couldn’t see what had happened ahead, and then Brad was trying to defend while Bezzecchi avoided Luca, so I didn’t have any space. There was nowhere I could go.” No response, and Alex pressed a hand to his face. “I’m sorry.”

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
Another second of silence, and then a long, heavy sigh came from the other side.

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
“I know,” Valentino said. “I know you did not mean to crash. I saw the video, I know you could not avoid him. Is not your fault.”

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
Alex felt somewhat relieved at those words— somewhat, but not entirely, because he could tell Valentino was still upset.

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
But, Alex couldn’t blame him.

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
He, too, took any crash involving Marc far more seriously than those he was involved in himself, and he knew the same went for Marc with him. He could easily imagine how scared Valentino had been, seeing Luca crash like that. It was different, when it wasn’t you. Worse. Much worse.

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
“Is he alright? I heard the injuries were minor, but . . . “

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
“He is fine. A small hand fracture, but he can still race.”

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
“I’m glad to hear that,” Alex said, making sure to put as much sincerity into his voice as he could.

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
Valentino hummed, a low, vague sound. “I have to go now,” he said.

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
Truthfully, Alex wanted to keep talking. But, that was not how their system worked, and Alex had already broken their rules once. Really, that Valentino had answered at all was already a bit of a miracle.

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
He had said Alex could bother him, though. Just a little was okay.

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
“Alright. Ciao, Vale.”

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
“Ciao,” Valentino said, and hung up.

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
Alex brought the phone away from his face, holding it in front of him as he considered the situation.

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
Valentino was still upset. Naturally, even though he’d said he knew it wasn’t Alex’s fault, but emotions were hard to control. Still, at least it meant that Valentino didn’t blame Alex. So, all Alex had to do was wait out Valentino’s emotions, and everything would be fine.

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
Everything would be fine. Right?

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
Alex closed his eyes, and tried to calm down. Truly, he wanted to believe that Valentino wouldn’t hold this against him, that they would be fine, that this wouldn’t affect them, but—

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
But Alex knew how quickly things could change. How quickly Valentino could change, when it came to racing, when it came to other people. Had watched it, live, front-row seats to the whole spectacle of it, the destruction left in the wake of Valentino’s disdain, vague and elusive and all the more painful for it. This was nothing like the situation back then, of course, but Alex hated the idea of Valentino holding any sort of resentment against him, even a minor amount.

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
After all, Alex himself was not immune to that kind of resentment, either.

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
It had taken him years to get past it fully, what had happened in Sepang. He hadn’t been angry at Valentino for that long, knowing even back then that it wasn’t something he would ever have wanted to happen, either— that sudden and unexpected escalation of aggressions, culminating in a rift within the world of MotoGP that no one could ever have predicted— but the fact that it had happened was impossible to ignore.

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
But Alex understood better, with every passing day, that that sort of thing just happened sometimes. Not often, and certainly not usually with that sort of cataclysmic fallout, but it happened. He could choose to hold on to that pain, on to that resentment, or he could not. And Alex had chosen, that day in Misano, to fully and completely let it go.

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
To let it go, like Marc and Valentino still refused to.

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
Alex tried not to think about it too much. Guilt simmered in his gut when he did, corroded into his chest, into his lungs. Marc and Valentino’s relationship had nothing to do with him, by their own admission, their own will. No one was allowed into their fight, into their game, not even Alex— Marc himself had made that very, very clear. So, Alex really shouldn’t feel guilty.

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
Still kind of did.

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
But, this wasn’t about them, and Alex wasn’t Marc.

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
Alex was going to make sure that whatever resentment Valentino held towards Alex, no matter how minor, he’d let go of it, too.

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->
*2023, Mugello*

<!-- bt-block:000056 -->
Luca was chatting with Pecco when Alex found him, propped against the VR46 garage wall and complaining about media obligations while Pecco fiddled with his water bottle and periodically made agreeing sounds. He didn’t really seem to be listening, from what Alex could see, but Luca also didn’t seem to mind.

<!-- bt-block:000057 -->
Alex made sure there were no cameras around before approaching. A VR46 mechanic passed by, giving him a slightly suspicious look, but seeing him approaching the other two riders, assumed it was a rider issue and went on his way.

<!-- bt-block:000058 -->
Luca and Pecco noticed him almost immediately, Luca falling silent as he turned towards Alex with a somewhat frosty look. Pecco, ever the polite one, nodded at Alex, though he also didn't look enthused by his presence.

<!-- bt-block:000059 -->
Alex nodded back at Pecco, then turned to Luca.

<!-- bt-block:000060 -->
“I wanted to talk to you for a second, if that’s okay?”

<!-- bt-block:000061 -->
Luca blinked at him. Glanced at Pecco, then back at Alex. “What about?” he asked. He didn’t sound happy, but he also wasn’t being actively antagonistic. Alex would take what he could get.

<!-- bt-block:000062 -->
“I just wanted to apologize again,” Alex said, a bit awkwardly. He watched as Pecco gave them both a look, shrugged, and then walked off. Very polite of him, Alex thought with some amusement, as though this were the kind of situation that required privacy. Alex would actually rather he had stayed. He and Pecco might have their differences, but he certainly got along with him better than with Luca.

<!-- bt-block:000063 -->
Speaking of Luca, he merely raised an eyebrow at Alex, aloof and questioning. “Sure,” he said.

<!-- bt-block:000064 -->
Alex bit back a sigh. He knew Luca was upset with him, but Alex was trying to be nice. Did he have to make it so painful? “How’s your hand?”

<!-- bt-block:000065 -->
Luca’s eyes narrowed slightly. “What about it?”

<!-- bt-block:000066 -->
“I, uh,” Alex stopped. Did other people not know that Luca had fractured his hand? Did Alex have insider knowledge? He looked down, saw the bandages. Looked back up, to find Luca watching him like a hawk. Alex tried not to look too suspicious. Wasn’t sure he was succeeding. “Is it . . . okay?”

<!-- bt-block:000067 -->
“I can race,” Luca replied, tight and PR-perfect. And, well. That was fine. Luca didn’t have to tell Alex anything, after all.

<!-- bt-block:000068 -->
“That’s good,” Alex said, stiff and uncomfortable. He was regretting a bit, his idea to check in on Luca. Maybe this would actually make Valentino more upset with him, rather than the opposite. Well, nothing to do about it now. “Um, anyways. I just wanted to say that. I’ll stop bothering you now.”

<!-- bt-block:000069 -->
“Sure,” Luca said again, still watching Alex with narrowed eyes. Alex nodded at him, a bit awkwardly, and then turned away, grimacing as soon as his face was out of Luca’s sight. That had been painful. Necessary, maybe, but painful. Now all Alex had to do was wait a few days, see if Vale called him, or maybe call Vale himself again. Not too soon, but soon enough that it would still be—

<!-- bt-block:000070 -->
“Tola.”

<!-- bt-block:000071 -->
Alex froze.

<!-- bt-block:000072 -->
Slowly, he turned to look back at Luca, who was watching him with icy eyes and an unreadable expression.

<!-- bt-block:000073 -->
“Uh,” Alex said, very eloquently. “Sorry, what was that?”

<!-- bt-block:000074 -->
The sounds of the paddock had dulled to a soft hum in the back of Alex’s head, almost like thunder in the distance. All Alex could focus on was the worry brewing in his stomach, the panic, the desperate hope that he’d just misheard Luca.

<!-- bt-block:000075 -->
Because how would Luca know that Alex was Tola?

<!-- bt-block:000076 -->
Unless Valentino had told him— but no, there was no way. They were both keeping each other secret from everyone else, even their own siblings. And Valentino wouldn’t have let the cat out of the bag without first warning Alex . . . not, at least, on purpose.

<!-- bt-block:000077 -->
Maybe, just maybe, Luca had figured some things out on his own.

<!-- bt-block:000078 -->
The silence hung between them, glass-sharp and just as fragile, for what must have only been a few seconds, but to Alex felt like hours. But, just as he was deciding whether he would have to be the one to break the ice— to beg Luca to keep the secret, maybe, or maybe just to immediately call Valentino in a panic— Luca spoke.

<!-- bt-block:000079 -->
“Nothing,” he said— tone genial, expression inscrutable, eyes piercing and icy. “See you in the race, Marquez. Just keep your distance this time.”

<!-- bt-block:000080 -->
“I’ll try my best,” Alex replied, feeling a shiver go down his spine. When he walked away that time, Luca didn’t call him back.

<!-- bt-block:000081 -->
Still, Alex could once again feel his eyes like knives on his neck, all the way back to his motorhome.

<!-- bt-block:000082 -->
Alex waited until after the race to call Valentino again. He’d crashed, but Luca had finished fourth. Hopefully, that would help soothe some of Valentino’s annoyance.

<!-- bt-block:000083 -->
He’d also waited to see if Valentino would call him first, if Luca had talked to his brother first. Apparently, he hadn’t, so Alex would have to take the initiative.

<!-- bt-block:000084 -->
He was worried, for a few seconds, that Valentino might again decline his call, like he had back when he and Luca had crashed. But Valentino answered on the third ring, and Alex breathed out a sigh of relief.

<!-- bt-block:000085 -->
“Ciao, Vale,” he said. He was in his motorhome, with the door locked and no guests. Marc might break in, but he’d crashed too, so he was almost certainly already sleeping, preparing for the ride back home the next day, bright and early.

<!-- bt-block:000086 -->
“Ciao, Alex,” Valentino replied. So, they were both alone.

<!-- bt-block:000087 -->
The thought made something warm curl in Alex’s chest as he settled into his bed. This would likely be a slightly uncomfortable conversation, so he wanted to at least be physically comfortable for it.

<!-- bt-block:000088 -->
“I think Luca knows,” he said, deciding there was no point in beating around the bush.

<!-- bt-block:000089 -->
“Luca?” Valentino asked, and Alex could hear a sound like pouring water over the receiver. Making himself coffee, maybe, a late-night tradition Valentino often partook in. He didn’t sound particularly worried, though, which helped soothe some of Alex’s own concerns. “How would he know?”

<!-- bt-block:000090 -->
“I talked to him two days ago. Just, checking in on him,” he said. Waited, but Valentino said nothing, so he continued: “But then, as I was leaving, he called me Tola.”

<!-- bt-block:000091 -->
“And?”

<!-- bt-block:000092 -->
“And?” Alex echoed, confused by the reply.

<!-- bt-block:000093 -->
“And? Did you react?”

<!-- bt-block:000094 -->
“I tried not to,” Alex said. Paused, as the dots connected in his head. “You mean, he was just testing me?”

<!-- bt-block:000095 -->
“Obviously,” Valentino muttered. “I mean, he’s heard me talk about Tola before. Of course I do not tell him who it is, but he’s figured out you are a rider, and he is always curious and observant. If you are checking in on him like this, he will guess it could be you.”

<!-- bt-block:000096 -->
Oh.

<!-- bt-block:000097 -->
“That’s a bit of a stretch, no?” Alex tried.

<!-- bt-block:000098 -->
“It is. But then you react, so now he really suspect. Don’t worry, though. Even if he did figure it out, he’d never tell anyone.” Valentino mostly sounded faintly amused, so Alex once again allowed himself to relax. That was, until Valentino sighed. “But, eh. You do not have to do this.”

<!-- bt-block:000099 -->
He sounded less amused now, and Alex drew himself up on the bed, to better focus. “Do what?”

<!-- bt-block:000100 -->
“Check in on him,” Valentino said, matter-of-factly. “I know you do it for me. You do not have to.”

<!-- bt-block:000101 -->
Alex winced. Felt glad Valentino couldn’t see him. “It’s not just for you. I also feel bad about what happened.”

<!-- bt-block:000102 -->
“Alex, please.”

<!-- bt-block:000103 -->
“It’s not, I also—” Alex now felt distinctly awkward. How had he thought this would go, really? Of course Valentino saw through him immediately. “I’d also want to. You know. Get along with him. At least a little bit.”

<!-- bt-block:000104 -->
“Because he is my brother.”

<!-- bt-block:000105 -->
“He’s also just another rider on the grid,” Alex tried one last time. Folded, as he realized it was useless. “But yes, because he’s your brother, too.”

<!-- bt-block:000106 -->
Valentino hummed. “He doesn’t like you.”

<!-- bt-block:000107 -->
Alex blinked, confused by the non sequitur. He’d thought Valentino would call him out on his little ploy, not . . .  “Excuse me?”

<!-- bt-block:000108 -->
“He doesn’t like you,” Valentino said, sounding amused. “I think he’s a little jealous.”

<!-- bt-block:000109 -->
Alex opened his mouth. Closed it. Opened it again. “What do you mean?”

<!-- bt-block:000110 -->
“I’ve mentioned you to him sometimes. Not as Tola, but as Alex. Similar age, similar situation, you know what I mean.” Alex could hear the laughter in his voice. “He always goes a bit unhappy when I do it. Is kind of funny.”

<!-- bt-block:000111 -->
Alex felt his face heating up. He abruptly remembered what he’d said, during the press conference he’d done with Luca a few years ago. Who would Valentino rather have as a brother? He’d meant it as a joke, a jab at the reporter more than anyone. But the thought that Valentino had brought Alex up to Luca— had been bringing him up, a few times, even— left something odd and niggling in his chest.

<!-- bt-block:000112 -->
“I wouldn’t want to be your brother,” he blurted out. From the other side of the line came silence, and Alex barely resisted the urge to groan. Why did he say that? “I mean, I like— you know, I like what we have. I wouldn’t want to be your brother, because I like— this. I like being this.”

<!-- bt-block:000113 -->
“My friend?”

<!-- bt-block:000114 -->
Alex froze. Friend? Friend. Friend?

<!-- bt-block:000115 -->
Yes. Yes, he supposed they were friends. After all, they’d been talking for . . . four years, now? It would make sense that they would be friends, after all that time. Not, you know. Taking into account everything else, maybe. But taking into account them, just them—

<!-- bt-block:000116 -->
“Yeah,” Alex said quietly. “Yeah. I like being your friend.”

<!-- bt-block:000117 -->
Valentino huffed out a soft laugh. “I like being your friend, too,” he said, and Alex tried not to cough in surprise. Why it surprised him so much, he couldn’t say. He’d just said it himself, after all. But Valentino was always something else, something different, something Alex could never— “And I would not want to be your brother either.”

<!-- bt-block:000118 -->
Alex was startled into a laugh. “No, you definitely wouldn’t.”

<!-- bt-block:000119 -->
He couldn’t even imagine what it would be like, having Valentino and Marc as brothers. Cousins, even. Anything at all related to each other. He didn’t think it would go very well. But then again, maybe the shared blood, the shared experiences, would actually help. He’d never know, regardless.

<!-- bt-block:000120 -->
“But, about Luca,” Valentino said, “do not worry too much about him. The injury is not too bad, and we are taking care of it. As for getting along with him . . . good luck, is maybe all I can say.”

<!-- bt-block:000121 -->
Alex groaned, bringing a hand up to his face. “What have you told him about me?”

<!-- bt-block:000122 -->
“All good things.”

<!-- bt-block:000123 -->
“That makes it worse!”

<!-- bt-block:000124 -->
“I know,” Valentino said, laughing as Alex groaned again. “But you are good motivation for him. Similar age, similar situation, but a bit better rider. So it gives him something to aim for.”

<!-- bt-block:000125 -->
“Just a bit better?” Alex joked.

<!-- bt-block:000126 -->
“Okay, okay, a lot better,” Valentino said with another laugh. “But do not tell him I said that.”

<!-- bt-block:000127 -->
Alex barely managed not to choke on the inhale. Tried, desperately, not to let those words get to his head. He didn’t have the results to show for it, after all, not since entering MotoGP.

<!-- bt-block:000128 -->
But Valentino’s respect had always been a unique sort of drug to Alex, a special source of pride, of confidence. Maybe now more than ever, when Alex was so doubtful of himself. Even Marc was faltering, Alex could tell, was losing his ever-present faith, his stalwart belief.

<!-- bt-block:000129 -->
But never Valentino. Valentino hadn’t hesitated once.

<!-- bt-block:000130 -->
“Thank you,” he said. Cleared his throat, upon hearing his own voice. “I mean, yeah, I won’t tell him.”

<!-- bt-block:000131 -->
He played with a corner of one of his pillows, pulling on the threads by the edge. They were coming apart at the seams, he noticed, even though they were only a few years old. He should get higher quality bedding, Alex thought. He certainly had the money for it.

<!-- bt-block:000132 -->
“You are a good rider, Alex. Do not let a few hard years make you believe you aren’t.” Valentino said, and Alex’s chest swarmed with something light and warm. “Even if you sometimes crash into other people.”

<!-- bt-block:000133 -->
. . . And there went the good feelings. “Va—”

<!-- bt-block:000134 -->
“Is joke, I am joking,” Valentino said. Paused. “Maybe half-joking.”

<!-- bt-block:000135 -->
Yeah, Alex didn’t think he was joking.

<!-- bt-block:000136 -->
“I swear I will do my best not to crash into Luca ever again,” he said, as sincerely as he could.

<!-- bt-block:000137 -->
“No other riders?” Valentino asked, faux-innocent.

<!-- bt-block:000138 -->
“Other riders can kiss the gravel, for all I care,” Alex threw back, hoping the joke would help improve Valentino’s mood. “On accident, of course.”

<!-- bt-block:000139 -->
“Of course, of course,” Valentino echoed. Was silent for another few seconds until he finally sighed. “Okay, fine. I forgive you.”

<!-- bt-block:000140 -->
Alex froze. Stared at the thread in his hand, at how far he’d pulled it out of the pillow.

<!-- bt-block:000141 -->
“Really?” he asked. He dared not hope, and yet . . .

<!-- bt-block:000142 -->
“Yes,” Valentino said, and for the first time, Alex believed him. “But if you do again . . . “

<!-- bt-block:000143 -->
“I’ll do my best not to,” Alex hurried to say, because he couldn’t let this chance get away from him.

<!-- bt-block:000144 -->
“Hmm.” Valentino grumbled, a bit annoyed, but finally he sighed again. “Fine, I’ll take it. All you can do is your best, after all.”

<!-- bt-block:000145 -->
“Yeah. Yeah, that’s true.” Alex collapsed onto the bed, relieved and relaxed as he hadn’t felt in weeks. Ever since that crash. He’d make sure that it never, ever happened again. But now that everything was okay again, he only wanted one thing. “Vale, can we meet?”

<!-- bt-block:000146 -->
He could hear the quiet sounds of dishes on the other side of the receiver pause for a moment. “Right now?”

<!-- bt-block:000147 -->
“Yeah. It’s not too late, right?”

<!-- bt-block:000148 -->
“ . . . I’m not at the paddock right now.”

<!-- bt-block:000149 -->
Alex stilled. Sat up on the bed. “You’re not?” Valentino was always around during the Italian races; that was why Alex looked forward to them so much. It was one of the few times in the year when the two of them could meet, now that Valentino had retired. And Valentino had always met with Alex when he was around, because he also knew there were few other chances. So, why . . . ?

<!-- bt-block:000150 -->
“I left immediately after the podium. Didn’t want to run into traffic.”

<!-- bt-block:000151 -->
“Oh.” Alex settled back down. Tried to ignore the tightness in his chest. “Yeah. That makes sense.”

<!-- bt-block:000152 -->
“I’ll be at Austria,” Valentino said. His voice had gone quiet, a bit apologetic. “We can meet then.”

<!-- bt-block:000153 -->
Austria. That was in two months.

<!-- bt-block:000154 -->
“Yeah, okay,” Alex said, trying his best not to sound as despondent as he felt. “I’ll see you in Austria.”

<!-- bt-block:000155 -->
"I'll call you before then," Valentino said, and was it Alex's imagination, or did he sound even more apologetic? "You know, next week I'll be travelling to Greece for a sponsor thing, but I'll also be going to the beach. I'll send you photos."

<!-- bt-block:000156 -->
It wasn't Alex's imagination. Valentino really did feel bad about sort-of-avoiding Alex.

<!-- bt-block:000157 -->
It wasn't quite as good as a meeting, but it did make Alex feel better.

<!-- bt-block:000158 -->
"Photos of you at the beach?" he joked.

<!-- bt-block:000159 -->
Valentino laughed. "I meant of the beach itself, is very beautiful. But I can send some of me, too, if you want." Alex could almost see him waggling his eyebrows in mock flirtation, and he laughed, ignoring the way his cheeks heated at the idea.

<!-- bt-block:000160 -->
Of course, Valentino wasn't actually flirting with Alex, just joking back with him. But, well. It wouldn't be the most terrible thing in the world if it weren't, you know. Entirely a joke.

<!-- bt-block:000161 -->
Alex shook his head. What was he thinking, really?

<!-- bt-block:000162 -->
"Yeah, send whatever you like. I'll send some, too. Not sure they'll be as beautiful as Greek beaches, but I'll try." Alex curled back into his bed. Since he wasn't going anywhere, might as well get comfortable. "What sponsorship thing are you doing, by the way?" he asked, hoping Valentino would take the bait and start talking.

<!-- bt-block:000163 -->
Valentino, ever-accommodating when it came to filling the silence, did.

<!-- bt-block:000164 -->
"Allora, do not tell anyone, is a secret for now," he said. "But in the last few months I have been doing a partnership with . . . "

<!-- bt-block:000165 -->
Alex melted into his sheets, warm and relaxed and comfortable as he listened to Valentino chat about nothing and everything. He'd missed this, so much more than he'd realized. And in that moment, Alex vowed he'd do everything he could to make sure he never lost it.

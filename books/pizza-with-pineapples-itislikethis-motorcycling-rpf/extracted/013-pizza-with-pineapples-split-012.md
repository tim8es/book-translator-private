*2025, Fuji*

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
Alex looked around carefully, winding his jacket more tightly around himself as he made his way across the paddock motorhomes as quickly and quietly as he could.

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
He’d never been to the Fuji circuit before, and sneaking in had been a bit of a stressful time. Not, of course, that he was there without permission. But having permission to enter and having permission to be there were two different things, and if anyone recognized him, even for an instant, Alex knew everything would quickly go downhill.

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
But, he’d begged Valentino to meet him, earlier that day. Had driven the three hours it took to go from Motegi to Fuji by himself, without alerting anyone as to his whereabouts. Everyone was busy celebrating Marc, anyway. If Alex wanted to go somewhere by himself, they could only let him. He was an adult; he could find his way back home when he was ready.

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
The only person he’d had to openly lie to was Marc, because Marc was the only one who’d insisted on knowing where Alex was going.

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
“Tokyo,” he’d told Marc, after some deliberation. “I’ve always wanted to visit. Might as well while we’re here, right?”

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
Tokyo was also conveniently on the way to where he was actually going, so if Marc asked for more details, it wouldn’t be too difficult to continue the farce. Still, Alex relied on the idea that Marc would be too distracted with his team and his celebrations to really think about Alex too much. He’d also leaned on the emotional aspect of the situation, how Alex felt a bit weird about celebrating Marc’s victory over, well. Himself.

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
That part wasn’t a lie. And that was why it worked so well.

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
It didn’t take much explaining for Marc to let him go, in the end. Alex knew he was pulling the guilt card, and he genuinely wished he could be there to properly celebrate Marc’s championship, but . . .

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
But he couldn’t.

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
Emotions were hard to control, weren’t they?

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
Alex wondered, all throughout the drive to Fuji, if this made him a bad brother. Marc had assured him it didn’t, that he understood Alex’s reluctance and didn’t blame him, but Alex could also see that Marc was hurt by it. Alex knew Marc would have loved to have him there, more than anyone else.

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
But emotions were hard to control, and Alex wouldn’t have been able to celebrate with Marc properly. So, he decided to leave his brother with his team, and travel three hours to Fuji.

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
Valentino, for his part, had been a bit confused about his request to meet.

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
“It’ll be difficult to get here,” he’d said, when Alex had asked. “And I won’t have a lot of time for you, either.”

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
“That’s fine,” Alex had said, already planning the logistics. “I’ll worry about myself. You just focus on your race.”

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
“Alright, then. If you’re sure,” Valentino said. Paused. “I’ll get a guest pass for you, no name or ID. I’ll send it to your phone.”

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
“Okay,” Alex said. “Thank you.”

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
“No problem, see you soon. Ciao.”

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
And that was it.

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
No questions about Marc, about why Alex wasn’t celebrating with him, about why Alex wanted to drive for three hours out of his way to see Valentino’s race rather than stay at his own. Just acceptance, and a bit of help.

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
So Alex took one of Gresini’s various nondescript rental vehicles, and off he went.

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
The ride was long but uneventful. It was late at night, so almost no one else was on the road. Alex put on some music, and let himself relax into the drive.

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
Arriving at the Fuji circuit was also relatively uneventful. There were cars going in and out, even this late at night, so Alex had no problem making his way inside without being noticed. Valentino’s pass had arrived an hour earlier, so he’d had no issues there, either. After parking his car in the lot, Alex carefully made his way inside. It was dark, and not many people were around, so he wasn’t too worried about anyone spotting him. And that was how he found himself, a few minutes later, making his way around the paddock motorhomes, trying to find Valentino’s private car.

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
Not, of course, his personal private car. That would be nearly impossible to get into without anyone noticing, Valentino being who he was. Even in WEC, his reputation preceded him, and Alex was fairly certain there would be fans scouting the area even this late at night. No, what Alex was looking for was the spare motorhome, one Valentino was using as his alternate meeting and guesthouse. Valentino had assured him it would be empty, and had told him where the secondary key was stored for Alex to get in without alerting anyone.

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
The nice thing was that Alex was, by that point, pretty used to sneaking around like this. The whole ordeal was a bit more dangerous in an unfamiliar paddock, of course, but Alex had always liked a bit of danger and chaos in his life. And Valentino was good at handling random situations like these. He clearly liked a bit of chaos and danger in his life, too.

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
He soon found the key, hidden under a rock jammed under the motorhome’s side. And after making sure no one was around to notice him, Alex went inside.

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
The motorhome was, in a word, plain. It was a spare motorhome, so of course, Alex shouldn’t have expected much of anything. But that also meant there was nothing much for him to do other than wait for Valentino’s current stint to be over.

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
Alex lay down on the living room couch and closed his eyes, letting the familiar sounds of an unfamiliar paddock wash over him while he dozed. Valentino would come find him when he could.

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
Alex smiled at the thought.

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
He might have lost the world championship, but this wasn’t so bad as a consolation prize.

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
A quiet knock came on the door a while later. Alex jerked up from the couch, slightly startled. He pulled out his phone, to see a message from Pipa telling him he was done with the stint debrief, and would be going over soon.

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
Alex made his way to the door. Knocked once, then stood to the side.

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
The door unlocked, and in came Valentino, quickly closing the door behind him. His eyes widened slightly when he finally turned around and actually caught sight of Alex.

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
“Allora, I can’t believe you are really here,” he said, and he sounded like he meant it.

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
“I can’t believe I’m really here, either,” Alex said, smiling. He could feel all the tension from the previous day already melting away.

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
Valentino smiled back. “You had no problem with getting here?”

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
“No, everything was good. The pass and the key, too.”

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
“Good, good.” Valentino paused, looking around the motorhome. “Ah, I’m being a bad host. Would you like something to drink? Water, soda?”

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
“Just water, thank you,” Alex said, amused by the politeness, but not minding it.

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
“Okay, one moment.”

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
They got their drinks and settled around the motorhome’s small kitchenette counters. Valentino had just been sitting for two hours in a car, and Alex for three, so neither of them wanted to sit.

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
“Where’s Marc?” Valentino asked as soon as the basic pleasantries were out of the way.

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
“Doing laundry down the road,” Alex replied. Added, at Valentino’s raised eyebrow: “Busy celebrating his ninth world championship back in Motegi, obviously.”

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
Valentino hummed. “I guess I meant, why aren’t you with him?”

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
Alex bit back a sigh. So, he wasn’t actually going to be able to escape that question.

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
“I don’t know.” He tried to shrug the issue off, dismissive. Didn’t succeed, judging by the look Valentino gave him. “I just feel . . . I don’t know.”

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
It really was hard to describe, what exactly Alex was feeling towards Marc lately. Joy, of course, pride, love, always. But there was also the tiniest bit of bitterness, of resentment, that Alex was finding increasingly hard to ignore. Today, of all days, he hadn’t been able to ignore it at all. That was why he’d left, why he’d driven three hours to go see Valentino instead.

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
If Alex couldn’t be a good brother to Marc, he didn’t want to be near him at all.

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
“Do you want to talk about it?” Valentino asked.

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->
“Not really.”

<!-- bt-block:000056 -->
“Okay,” Valentino replied, easy and permissive as always when it came to avoiding difficult topics. Now more than ever, Alex really appreciated that aspect of him. “Do you want to talk about this race?”

<!-- bt-block:000057 -->
“I’d love to.”

<!-- bt-block:000058 -->
“Allora, then let me tell you what’s been happening.”

<!-- bt-block:000059 -->
Alex settled against the counter, empty glass abandoned at his side, as he listened to Valentino regale him with tales of his exploits in Fuji over the past few days. He talked about their team’s strategy, their decisions, their calls, their ideas. He talked about their mistakes, their problems, their fights, their accidents. He talked about the car, the mechanics, the analysts, the other drivers.

<!-- bt-block:000060 -->
Valentino looked so animated, so happy through it all, that Alex couldn’t help but feel terribly, irrepressibly endeared.

<!-- bt-block:000061 -->
It’s beautiful seeing him like this, Alex thought. Then thought, shortly afterward, he’s beautiful.

<!-- bt-block:000062 -->
“It’s been a good race weekend,” Valentino concluded. Then he smiled at Alex, warm and soft. “And now I also get to have you here with me. So I’m very happy.”

<!-- bt-block:000063 -->
The sudden flood of yearning was overwhelming and absolute. Before he could stop himself, Alex found he’d taken one, two, three steps forward. Valentino froze, startled, staring at Alex as if asking what he thought he was doing by cornering him against the plastic counters of his private motorhome in the middle of a WEC race.

<!-- bt-block:000064 -->
As for Alex, he didn’t really know what he was doing either. But Valentino’s casual words had pierced into the depths of his chest, reached down into that yearning he’d tried so hard to keep quiet and hidden, repressed in its obvious implausibility, its clear impossibility.

<!-- bt-block:000065 -->
But Alex realized in that moment it was all for naught anyway. A ridiculously useless attempt from him, when all it took was Valentino saying that he was happy Alex was here for all his hard work to immediately go to waste, for all his repression to disappear like it had never existed— for that was all the yearning needed to take hold, to claw its way out of its dark corner, out of the small box Alex had so unceremoniously shoved it into ever since he’d realized what it actually was, all those months ago.

<!-- bt-block:000066 -->
It was a bit presumptuous, perhaps, to actually act on his feelings— to corner Valentino like this, physically and metaphorically. But Alex hadn’t approached Valentino all those years ago in Misano by being shy. Hadn’t called him that first time by being doubtful. Hadn’t become close to him by waiting for surety before acting. The door just needed to be opened the slightest bit for Alex to want.

<!-- bt-block:000067 -->
And, oh, Alex wanted.

<!-- bt-block:000068 -->
Alex watched as Valentino’s eyelashes fluttered, the way he bit his lower lip. Maybe it was his wishful thinking, making him see things that he wanted to see, but Alex was fairly sure there was some reciprocal want there, too. Attraction, in the way Valentino’s blue eyes flickered to Alex’s mouth, in the way his gaze darkened at their proximity.

<!-- bt-block:000069 -->
More to the point, Valentino hadn’t gotten upset at Alex’s actions, hadn’t tried to escape him. And Alex knew very well just how good Valentino was at escaping situations he didn’t want to be in.

<!-- bt-block:000070 -->
So Alex kept going. Leaning forward, ever so slowly.

<!-- bt-block:000071 -->
But, just before their lips were about to make contact, Alex felt Valentino’s hand press against his chest, pushing him gently away— gently, but firmly.

<!-- bt-block:000072 -->
Dread settled into Alex’s gut at the thought of what that could mean.

<!-- bt-block:000073 -->
“Vale?” he whispered, trying not to let his voice shake.

<!-- bt-block:000074 -->
“Alex,” Valentino said. He still didn’t look angry, but he didn’t look happy either. If anything, he looked a bit pained. “Allora. Your brother will kill me.”

<!-- bt-block:000075 -->
“I don’t care what Marc thinks,” Alex replied immediately. Paused. “Not about this.”

<!-- bt-block:000076 -->
And that really was true, Alex realized with some shock as he thought it over a bit more. Because while Alex was certain Marc would have a few. ahem, *opinions* on the situation, Alex wasn’t going to act upon them. And that was the crux of the matter: that sure, Alex might care, in some obscure, abstract way, about what Marc thought. Because he always cared, to some degree, about what Marc thought.

<!-- bt-block:000077 -->
And yet, when it came down to it— when it came down to Alex and Valentino—Alex would do nothing different. Would change nothing about what he’d done thus far, or what he planned on doing in the future, based on what Marc thought. And in that context, to say he ‘cared’ would not, as far as Alex was concerned, be true.

<!-- bt-block:000078 -->
Valentino merely sighed, looking, if anything, only more pained by his declaration. “You should care if he kills me.”

<!-- bt-block:000079 -->
Alex tried not to laugh. He didn’t actually think any of this was funny, really, but everything about this night just felt so surreal. He didn’t know how to react. “He won’t actually try to kill you.”

<!-- bt-block:000080 -->
“No, I think he will.”

<!-- bt-block:000081 -->
“He won’t.”

<!-- bt-block:000082 -->
“Agree to disagree.”

<!-- bt-block:000083 -->
“Valentino.” Alex tried to press closer. Was still held at bay by the hand on his chest, but at least he wasn’t pushed further away. He took what confidence he could from that, and slowly brought one of his hands to rest on Valentino’s waist. Valentino twitched slightly at the contact, but again, didn’t push him away. Again, Alex took confidence from that. Not too much, but just enough. “He likes you. No, listen to me. He does like you, I swear. And yeah, there’s some bad blood—”

<!-- bt-block:000084 -->
“A lot.”

<!-- bt-block:000085 -->
“Yes, yes, the point is, he likes you even with that. So he won’t actually try to kill you.” Again, Alex paused. Really tried to consider the situation realistically, though it felt impossible. And yet, it wasn’t. “He might try once.”

<!-- bt-block:000086 -->
Valentino winced, pressing a bit harder against Alex’s chest. “This is—”

<!-- bt-block:000087 -->
“Just, like, as a test. You know how he is.” Alex didn’t let himself get pushed. Not when the issue clearly wasn’t him, wasn’t them. Not when he might actually have a chance at something he never imagined he could get. “I won’t let him succeed, don’t worry.”

<!-- bt-block:000088 -->
“I worry,” Valentino said with a small smile, clearly trying to lighten the mood. A moment later, however, the humor fell from his expression. “Alex . . . I think this is maybe not the best idea.”

<!-- bt-block:000089 -->
“I’m telling you, he—”

<!-- bt-block:000090 -->
“Not because of that.” Valentino fell silent for a few seconds, then sighed, awkwardly looking at the floor. “Allora, Alex. You, eh, you have a crush on me, I can see that. And, you know, this is normal. I am Valentino Rossi, so I am used to it, I don’t mind. But, it will pass.” He paused. Then smiled, again, though this time with a slight edge to it, a mask of humor, rather than a genuine attempt at it. “Better not to make things complicated over something silly like this, no?”

<!-- bt-block:000091 -->
Alex, meanwhile, felt like he’d just been slapped. “Do you actually think that?”

<!-- bt-block:000092 -->
Valentino blinked at him. “Eh?”

<!-- bt-block:000093 -->
“Do you actually think I’m doing this because I have a ‘silly crush’ on ‘Valentino Rossi’? That I like you for, what, your money and fame, like some random fan? You actually think that’s what I’m after, what I care about? Really?”

<!-- bt-block:000094 -->
Alex tried to keep the hurt out of his voice. From the way Valentino flinched, he probably wasn’t doing a very good job.

<!-- bt-block:000095 -->
“Allora,” Valentino said, but said nothing else.

<!-- bt-block:000096 -->
“Look, if you want to reject me, just do it. You don’t have to come up with stupid excuses. I can take it.” Alex watched the emotions play across Valentino’s face. Felt dread once again settle in his stomach as he considered that, maybe, he really had been misreading everything. Maybe this really was about him, about them, after all. He realized, suddenly, he didn’t actually know if he could take it. “I know I’m not . . . whatever. It’s okay, I mean, if you aren’t interested.” He looked down, unable to hold Valentino’s gaze any longer. His eyes fell on the hand that was still perched on Valentino’s waist. Wondered if he’d have to move it away soon. “Would be stranger if you were, really.”

<!-- bt-block:000097 -->
Valentino might be attracted to Alex, but actually having feelings for him, actually wanting to be with him, was another thing entirely. And that was the thing; in spite of what Valentino had said, they both knew this wasn’t really about attraction, not really about a mere crush. They knew each other too well. They knew they both weren’t stupid enough to risk so much over so little.

<!-- bt-block:000098 -->
This— whatever this might end up being— would have to be taken far more seriously than that.

<!-- bt-block:000099 -->
“Allora,” Valentino repeated. Fell silent for another few moments. Then, he sighed again. “I think if I say yes, Marc really will kill me.”

<!-- bt-block:000100 -->
Alex froze. Was that . . . did that mean . . . ? Surely Valentino knew what he was implying by saying that, right? Surely. Right? “I wouldn’t let him.”

<!-- bt-block:000101 -->
“You think you can win a fight against him?”

<!-- bt-block:000102 -->
“I’m sure I can figure something out.” Alex looked back up, met Valentino’s gaze again. Tried not to hope too much at what he found there, just in case it killed him. “He’s broken a lot of bones. I should stand a chance.”

<!-- bt-block:000103 -->
Valentino snorted. The pressure on Alex’s chest eased. “He wouldn’t be able to hurt you, anyway. Loves you too much.”

<!-- bt-block:000104 -->
“Even more of an advantage.” Alex stared into Valentino’s eyes. Tried to press closer again. Was allowed, just barely. Want thrummed in his chest, growing stronger and more pronounced with every passing second of their closeness. “Are you actually thinking of saying yes?”

<!-- bt-block:000105 -->
Valentino’s eyelashes fluttered. “I am thinking many things.”

<!-- bt-block:000106 -->
“And one of them is to say yes?” The confidence was back now, too. Alex brought his other hand upwards, slowly, to rest it against Valentino’s nape. Giving the older man plenty of time to react, to pull away, to push him away.

<!-- bt-block:000107 -->
Valentino did neither. “Maybe,” he said, and as he spoke, he leaned into Alex’s hold. “I really do worry, though. I feel like Marc—”

<!-- bt-block:000108 -->
“Stop thinking so much about Marc,” Alex interrupted, quiet but sharp as his frustrations flared, fed upon by the strength of his want, by the depth of his yearning, “and think more about me.”

<!-- bt-block:000109 -->
With that, he leaned forward, and pressed their lips together.

<!-- bt-block:000110 -->
Alex’s first thought upon making contact was, I can’t believe I did that. His second thought was, I should have done it sooner. His third thought was significantly less coherent, as Valentino let out a small, hiccuping gasp, lips moving against his, warm and soft and just the tiniest bit dry. Alex didn’t mind, was sure his own lips weren’t particularly well-hydrated, either. And with his hand sliding into Valentino’s curls to tug him into a slightly more comfortable angle, teeth peeking out to softly bite, he couldn’t have minded less.

<!-- bt-block:000111 -->
Valentino made a low sound in the back of his throat, half surprise and half pleasure, and Alex barely managed to stop himself from biting down too hard.

<!-- bt-block:000112 -->
He pulled back, panting, blood rushing through his veins. The kiss hadn’t been particularly deep, barely more than a press of lips, but Alex felt dizzy. His only comfort was that Valentino looked similarly dazed. His lips were red, shiny. Alex couldn’t stop staring at them.

<!-- bt-block:000113 -->
Then, Valentino giggled.

<!-- bt-block:000114 -->
It was the most beautiful sound Alex had ever heard.

<!-- bt-block:000115 -->
“You are crazy,” Valentino said, still smiling. “I can’t believe you just did that.”

<!-- bt-block:000116 -->
“Sorry,” Alex said, though he wasn’t sorry at all. “I’ll ask for permission next time.”

<!-- bt-block:000117 -->
Valentino’s eyes narrowed coyly. “Next time?”

<!-- bt-block:000118 -->
“Mhm.” Alex couldn’t stop staring at Valentino’s mouth. Couldn’t stop thinking of how he’d felt, how he’s sounded. Wasn’t sure he’d ever be able to stop thinking about it. Wasn’t sure he wanted to. “Can I?”

<!-- bt-block:000119 -->
Valentino replied by being the one to lean in this time. Alex hummed in approval, once again reaching back to tug Valentino into place. Valentino went along with his request, even as he squirmed against Alex’s front. Alex’s grip on Valentino’s waist tightened in warning. Valentino squirmed again, letting out another giggle, and Alex smiled into the kiss.

<!-- bt-block:000120 -->
“Asshole,” he murmured, pulling away just enough to be able to speak. “You’re doing that on purpose.”

<!-- bt-block:000121 -->
Valentino’s eyelashes fluttered. Alex could feel them brushing against his cheeks, and his heart skipped a beat. “I don’t know what you’re talking about,” he said. Squirmed again.

<!-- bt-block:000122 -->
Alex groaned. “Come on, behave.” At the next movement, Alex tightened his hold on Valentino’s hair, pulling his head back in retaliation. Valentino let out a choked laugh, but again, went with the motion, agreeable and pliable like Alex had never dared imagine him to be.

<!-- bt-block:000123 -->
Valentino’s neck was left exposed. Alex stopped. Stared at it. Swallowed back a sudden swell of saliva.

<!-- bt-block:000124 -->
He could hear Valentino laughing again, as though through a curtain of water. He looked up, met knowing blue eyes.

<!-- bt-block:000125 -->
“You can bite, if you want,” Valentino said. His mouth curled into a grin. “Just don’t leave a mark.”

<!-- bt-block:000126 -->
Alex closed his eyes, tried to control himself. He did want to bite, but he didn’t trust himself not to leave a mark if he did. And if he did, that would be bad. Very, very bad.

<!-- bt-block:000127 -->
In the end, he just leaned forward, pressing his face into the crook of Valentino’s neck. Let himself settle into the warmth there, let his desires and his wants and his blood settle into something more manageable, something safer.

<!-- bt-block:000128 -->
Valentino hummed, bringing up a hand to rest against Alex’s nape, playing with the short hairs there.

<!-- bt-block:000129 -->
“Or, you can not bite,” he said after a minute passed in silence. “That’s okay, too.”

<!-- bt-block:000130 -->
It was Alex’s turn to giggle. A bit impulsively, he pressed a kiss to Valentino’s neck, chaste and soft. He drew back, then, his cheeks heating up.

<!-- bt-block:000131 -->
That had felt too intimate, somehow. Far more than the initial kiss, too much affection buried into one small motion. Alex wasn’t sure how to feel about that, the depth of his affection. It was a bit scary.

<!-- bt-block:000132 -->
But then Valentino brought a hand to rest against his cheek. Alex looked up, to find Valentino watching him with a soft, fond smile.

<!-- bt-block:000133 -->
And, in Valentino’s eyes, there was a genuine depth of affection too, along with its own uncurrent of fear.

<!-- bt-block:000134 -->
Alex’s chest felt suddenly tight.

<!-- bt-block:000135 -->
Valentino gazed at him for a few moments. “If we do this . . . “

<!-- bt-block:000136 -->
“We have to be extremely careful, I know,” Alex replied. “It could be dangerous, otherwise.”

<!-- bt-block:000137 -->
Valentino smiled, tentative. “Allora, what’s fun about a life without danger, anyway?”

<!-- bt-block:000138 -->
“Nothing,” Alex smiled back. “It’s why we do motorsports for a living.”

<!-- bt-block:000139 -->
“Exactly,” Valentino said. His voice softened, then, as he looked up at Alex through his lashes. “I really do like you.”

<!-- bt-block:000140 -->
“You do?” Alex felt a bit silly, with how strongly his emotions reacted to that statement. He should know it was true by now, and yet.

<!-- bt-block:000141 -->
“Mhm.” Valentino’s hand continued playing with the hairs on Alex’s nape. He shivered, feeling the touch all the way down his spine. “It is fun, spending time with you. You are relaxing and interesting. You like racing, but you are not obsessed with it. And you like me, but you are not obsessed with me. Is a nice balance.” Valentino paused. His smile grew warmer. “You are also very devoted. Very loyal. When you care about someone, you do it with all your heart. I like that a lot about you.”

<!-- bt-block:000142 -->
Alex tried not to look quite as affected as he actually was by those words. He felt a bit dizzy, drunk on the euphoria of Valentino’s affection. “I like you, too,” he murmured. Stroked his thumb along Valentino’s hipbone, feeling the warmth of his skin against his own. Valentino shivered at the motion, and Alex felt it like a punch to the gut. “Couldn’t say why, though.”

<!-- bt-block:000143 -->
Valentino pouted, eyebrows drawing together in mock dismay. “Too many reasons to list?”

<!-- bt-block:000144 -->
Alex swallowed thickly as he looked down into bottomless blue eyes. He knew Valentino was joking, the humor in his actions impossible to ignore. But his hand was curled into the base of Alex’s neck, the length of his body pressed into his, and Alex wasn’t laughing. “Exactly,” he said, voice low and breathy.

<!-- bt-block:000145 -->
Valentino smirked, visibly pleased by Alex’s reaction. “Eh, you are a bit embarrassing,” he said. He gently pulled Alex in by his nape, eyes going dark and half-lidded. “But, I do not mind.”

<!-- bt-block:000146 -->
And as their lips met once more, Alex found he definitely didn’t mind, either.

<!-- bt-block:000147 -->
He might have lost the world championship, but he might have just won something even better.

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
Valentino arrived five minutes later, closing the door carefully behind him as he made sure no one had realized where he was.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
“Alex. Are you alright?” he asked, warily making his way inside.

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
“Fine,” Alex called out. Waved Valentino over. “You remember how I promised I’d stop Marc from trying to kill you?”

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
Valentino blinked at him. “Did he . . . ?”

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
“Yep. Had to fight him to the floor and everything.”

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
“My hero.” Valentino paused by the kitchenette, glancing around the motorhome. “So he’s, uh. Outside?”

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
“Yep. Probably just walking around the paddock. Cooling his head.”

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
“And he will not try to kill me when he gets back . . . ?”

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
“No, I think he’s past that.” Alex chuckled faintly. “At least now I know I’d beat him in a fight, so. There’s that.”

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
“Good to know.” Valentino finally made his way over to the couch, giving it a slightly judgmental once-over before dropping onto it with a huff. “But, anyway, how are you?”

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
“Fine.”

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
Valentino sighed. “Alex, please. This is all very hard for you, I know. Is okay, if you are not fine.”

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
Alex took a deep breath. Looked up at the ceiling. “I just . . . I just wish he’d respect me.”

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
“He does.”

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
“Not entirely. Not always.” Alex looked back down, meeting Valentino’s eyes. He saw no pity there, only patience. It gave him the strength to continue, to try and explain. “He always loves me, but he doesn’t always respect me. Like how he doesn’t let me take responsibility for my mistakes. Or how he refuses to consider me a real rival. Or even yesterday, when he said all that stuff about how you’d never truly be my friend. I know he’s mostly just trying to protect me, but . . . “

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
Valentino hummed. “Did you tell him this?”

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
“Sort of.” Alex shrugged awkwardly. “It’s hard to be honest with him about things like this. I don’t want to hurt him.”

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
“But then he hurts you.”

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
“ . . . Yeah.” Alex let out a long breath, feeling his chest tighten at the admission. “Yeah, he does.”

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
“You know, you said he doesn’t always respect you, but at least he respects you more than anyone else.” Alex turned to Valentino, who gave him a shrug in return. “Is true. A bit sad, but true.”

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
“You think so?” Alex asked dubiously.

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
“He listens to you, when he would not listen to anyone else. He thinks about you, when he would not think about anyone else. He apologizes to you, when he would not apologize to anyone else.” Valentino grinned, a bit dryly. “Believe me, I know a bit about this.”

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
Alex watched Valentino. Wondered, as he thought about the conversation he’d just had with Marc.

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
He thought about what Marc had revealed. His fear of losing Alex. Ridiculous, in Alex’s opinion, but clearly not in Marc’s. But, Alex supposed, it did make some sense. After all, when it came to people, Marc had always lost to Valentino. It was nothing to be ashamed of, of course. Everyone lost to Valentino, when it came to people. Everyone would choose Valentino, given a choice. Everyone always had.

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
Not Alex, though. Alex would always choose Marc.

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
And Alex wondered, in that moment, if maybe that was precisely why Valentino had chosen him in turn. Well, probably not precisely why— but, thinking about what Valentino had told Alex when they’d first gotten together, about why he liked him, it did seem like it was, at least, a positive trait.

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
Alex then thought about what Marc had said about Valentino. Thought about how Marc had responded to Alex’s assertion that Valentino had no intention of ever getting along with him again.

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
‘I know’

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
Quiet, resigned. Like it was a foregone conclusion that Valentino would never want to talk with him, would never want to fix things. Would not even care to try.

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
“Vale,” Alex asked, “can I ask you something? And you answer honestly?”

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
Valentino gave him a suspicious look. “Maybe.”

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
Alex snorted. “Alright. I guess that was honest.”

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
Valentino shifted uncomfortably for a moment. Sighed. “Fine. I will try.”

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
“Thank you.” Alex smiled at him. “So, what I want to ask is . . . did you ever really like Marc? Genuinely, I mean. Did you ever actually care about him?”

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
Alex had, of course, his own opinions on the subject. His own observations, even, from back in those days. But, he wanted to hear what Valentino had to say on the matter.

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
“Allora,” Valentino said, and he genuinely looked like he wasn’t sure how to answer. His face had gone blank, unreadable, like it rarely ever did around Alex anymore. “This is a difficult question.”

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
“Is it?”

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
“This . . . “ Valentino paused. Grimaced, as he seemed to come to terms with something. “Yes. Yes, I did care about him.”

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
That answer, Alex was genuinely a bit surprised by. Not what Valentino had said, but that he’d said it at all. “You did?”

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
“Allora, yes, but this is—” Valentino huffed, arms crossing over his front defensively as his expression twisted into an uneasy frown. “This is a part of me that I do not like.”

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
“What do you mean? What part?”

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
“It is a part that is very soft. Too soft.” He looked at Alex. Sighed, at what he found there. “When I like someone, I want to care about them. I want to trust them, even when I know I shouldn’t, even when everyone else tells me I shouldn’t. And we were, you know, rivals. There is no place in being rivals for me to care about him. It was stupid of me, from the beginning. And I knew it was. It’s not like he was the first.” He paused, sinking further into the couch, nails digging into his arms. “But I cannot help it, it is how I am. I trust the people I like. And then I get hurt. I get hurt again and again and again.” He looked back down, voice falling to a murmur. “I never learn.”

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
“You also hurt him,” Alex said, because he couldn’t not say it. Gently, because he also wasn’t trying to hurt. He was just trying to understand. “You also hurt him a lot. Again and again and again.”

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
Valentino’s mouth pursed. “I did,” he said. “But that is the way it was always going to be. It is not, you know, the hurting that annoys me. That is what competition is, we all hurt each other. What annoys me is that I cared.”

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
Alex considered that.

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
“Do you regret it?” he finally asked.

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
Valentino huffed. “Caring, or hurting him?”

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
“Both, I guess.”

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
“Caring, definitely. Hurting him . . . “ Valentino was silent for a long moment. FInally he closed his eyes, tucking his arms even further into his chest. “No, I don’t regret it.”

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
“Really?” Alex asked, a bit shocked by that. A bit hurt, too. “Not at all?”

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->
“It is not—” Valentino let out a long hissed breath. “I don’t regret hurting him in the, you know, competitive sense. When I accused him of cheating. This is normal between riders, we are all always cheating, and we always accuse here and there to upset each other. It was about the competition, it was nothing personal.”

<!-- bt-block:000056 -->
“Alright,” Alex said, because though he didn’t entirely agree with that, he did recognize that media games were, in fact, a normal and natural part of their sport. All sports, really, being in the entertainment industry. And cheating was always happening everywhere, in the grey areas of the rules where teams and riders could gain even the slightest advantage. It was part of the competition. “But what about in the personal sense?”

<!-- bt-block:000057 -->
Valentino opened his eyes again. Glanced at Alex, then away. His nails scratched at his arms. “You know, this is why I thought us being together was a bad idea—”

<!-- bt-block:000058 -->
“Don’t.” Alex quickly reached out, placing his hands over Valentino’s arms. Held him, both in the comforting and restrictive sense, because he could tell Valentino was one uncomfortable comment from running away. “I don’t want to fight. I just want to understand.”

<!-- bt-block:000059 -->
“Of course we will fight,” Valentino said, glancing down at Alex’s hands, clearly aware he was being denied his freedom. But he didn’t struggle. Not yet. “You are on his side, how could we not fight.”

<!-- bt-block:000060 -->
“I am on his side,” Alex said, because there was no use in even trying to pretend otherwise, “but I am not only on his side.”

<!-- bt-block:000061 -->
Valentino sighed. “Alex—”

<!-- bt-block:000062 -->
“I’m not,” Alex insisted, because he needed Valentino to know that he meant this. “I am on his side, but I am on your side, too. I really do just want to understand.”

<!-- bt-block:000063 -->
Valentino’s expression dimmed. He looked exhausted all of a sudden, the look in his eyes heavy and sad. “This will not work,” he said, voice quiet, and Alex—

<!-- bt-block:000064 -->
“It will work. I will make it work.” He grabbed Valentino’s arms, pulling him in, forcing him to face Alex. “Look at me. We will make it work. Together. Do you understand?”

<!-- bt-block:000065 -->
Valentino blinked at him, startled. He licked his lips, then, a nervous tick, and Alex fought not to be distracted.

<!-- bt-block:000066 -->
“But—”

<!-- bt-block:000067 -->
“No ‘but’. Do you understand?”

<!-- bt-block:000068 -->
Valentino licked his lips again. “But what if I say,” he murmured, “that I do not regret it? Any of it?”

<!-- bt-block:000069 -->
Alex paused.

<!-- bt-block:000070 -->
That . . . well. It hurt. Alex couldn’t deny that.

<!-- bt-block:000071 -->
But, that wasn’t what this was about.

<!-- bt-block:000072 -->
“Then I would understand,” he replied. “I wouldn’t be, you know. Delighted. But I would understand.”

<!-- bt-block:000073 -->
Valentino looked doubtful. “Really?”

<!-- bt-block:000074 -->
“Yes,” Alex said, with a dry smile. “I’m not blind, Valentino. I know what kind of person you are. I care about you, a lot, but I’m also aware you can be . . . complicated.”

<!-- bt-block:000075 -->
“That’s a nice way to put it,” Valentino muttered.

<!-- bt-block:000076 -->
“I’m trying to be nice,” Alex retorted. “The point is, I would understand if you don’t regret any of it. You don’t like regretting things in general, do you? Even the negative things. You’d rather just learn from your mistakes and move on, than be sad thinking about what could’ve been. So it makes sense, that you wouldn’t regret this,”

<!-- bt-block:000077 -->
“Oh,” Valentino said, and now he really looked startled. “You do understand.”

<!-- bt-block:000078 -->
“Yes, you asshole. I do.” Alex leaned in a bit more, lowered his voice again. “So, are you going to tell me the truth now, or what else do I have to do?”

<!-- bt-block:000079 -->
Valentino stared at him, eyes wide. Then he laughed, relaxing finally from his defensive fortress of a pose.

<!-- bt-block:000080 -->
Alex leaned back, pleased his ploy had worked.

<!-- bt-block:000081 -->
“Fine,” Valentino said, and when he looked at Alex, there was an additional sense of belief, of trust, in his gaze. Alex tried not to feel too proud about that. “So. Yes, I regret a little bit.”

<!-- bt-block:000082 -->
“What part?” Alex asked.

<!-- bt-block:000083 -->
“I guess, just, the scale? You know, how big it all got. Even with the media, it should have stayed a thing within MotoGP. But so many people got so involved. It stopped being about rivals competing in a sport, and started being . . . I don’t even know. People took it way too fucking seriously.” Valentino shifted against the couch. “I think the thing I regret is not making an effort to control it in the beginning. I was angry and hurt, and so I let it explode too much. He also made it explode, but, you know. I also did. So that I regret a bit.”

<!-- bt-block:000084 -->
Alex tilted his head. Valentino did clearly trust Alex a bit more, but for Alex to believe that meant Valentino trusted him entirely was far too naïve for him at this point in their— mm, acquaintanceship. “Okay, but that’s media. What about the personal level?”

<!-- bt-block:000085 -->
“That’s what I mean. It got too big, too serious, and that hurt him a lot as a person. Both of you, actually. I know it was bad,” Valentino said, and he really did sound a bit contrite about it. He paused, then, and gave Alex a small, dry smile. “But if you are asking do I regret not being friendlier with him, then no. That I do not regret.”

<!-- bt-block:000086 -->
Alex narrowed his eyes at Valentino. “Really?”

<!-- bt-block:000087 -->
“Yes. I think I told you this, back when we first talked. How I liked him when I did not know him well, but when I know him better, it is . . . eh. I like him less, I will say it this way.”

<!-- bt-block:000088 -->
And sure, that might be true, but . . .

<!-- bt-block:000089 -->
“But you did still like him, even then,” Alex said, as he started to put some pieces together, as some things started to make more and more sense the more he thought about them. “And that’s why you refuse to talk to him now. Because you did still care about him, even after what happened, and so he still hurt you. And you don’t want to risk it happening again.”

<!-- bt-block:000090 -->
Alex knew, of course, that Valentino had hurt Marc deeply over the years. But what he’d never been sure of was how much Marc had hurt Valentino in turn. Of course, he’d seen some of it, but he’d never been entirely sure how much of that was circumstance, and how much of it was Marc himself.

<!-- bt-block:000091 -->
For Marc, it had all, always, been Valentino. It was never about circumstance, for Marc, it was never about anyone else. That made what had happened between them naturally painful, but that didn’t mean it was all inherently Valentino’s fault, either.

<!-- bt-block:000092 -->
Because Marc had obviously cared about Valentino, always had. Still did, Alex knew, in spite of everything. It was just that caring about someone had never stopped Marc from hurting them. Hurting them pretty badly, sometimes, though he might not want to, might not mean to. The pain he caused was still real.

<!-- bt-block:000093 -->
Alex would know. He would know really, really well.

<!-- bt-block:000094 -->
Valentino, for his part, didn’t look too enthused by Alex’s deductions. He didn’t look upset, exactly, but he had curled into himself once again, fingers rubbing against his arms.

<!-- bt-block:000095 -->
“What happened should not have happened at all, because I should never have cared about him in the first place,” he muttered sourly. “As I say, I do not like this part of me.”

<!-- bt-block:000096 -->
“I do,” Alex said. Added, at the narrow-eyed look Valentino gave him: “Not the you getting hurt part, obviously. But the part of you that cares. The part of you that cannot help but care. I like that part a lot.”

<!-- bt-block:000097 -->
Valentino huffed. “It is not so nice for me.”

<!-- bt-block:000098 -->
“No. I’m sure it’s not.” Alex could only imagine how frustrating it must be for Valentino. To work so hard to establish certain boundaries for himself, to try and protect himself while competing, only to have his own emotions betray him. To be unable to completely shut off that soft part of himself, no matter how much it hurt him. Alex was not someone who trusted so easily— he was, in fact, not a particularly trusting person in general— but the thought of leaving himself so vulnerable was daunting, let alone having it happen over and over without his control.

<!-- bt-block:000099 -->
And yet.

<!-- bt-block:000100 -->
And yet, Alex was also, perhaps, not above taking advantage of Valentino’s softness. Just a little bit. Just this once.

<!-- bt-block:000101 -->
“Vale, can I ask for something? A favor?”

<!-- bt-block:000102 -->
Valentino’s mouth pursed. “I think I know what you are going to say.”

<!-- bt-block:000103 -->
“I understand if you don’t want to. But I don’t think it’s better, to stay like this,” Alex tried to argue. “It still hurts, right? Even after all this time. So don’t you think it’s time to try something different, at least?” Seeing Valentino wasn’t budging, he added jokingly: “Like going to Yamaha instead of staying at Honda.”

<!-- bt-block:000104 -->
At that, Valentino let out a snort. “What kind of comparison is that?”

<!-- bt-block:000105 -->
“I’m just saying. You don’t like doing the same thing over and over again, right? Even if it’s safe, it’s boring. It’s not fun.”

<!-- bt-block:000106 -->
Valentino looked at Alex. Really looked at him, considering him with a cool, assessing gaze. “This will not be fun.”

<!-- bt-block:000107 -->
“But boring is still worse.” Alex held Valentino’s gaze. Tried to transmit his sincerity, his genuine desire for things to be better. For both of them, not just one. “Vale, please. Can you give him one chance? Just one?”

<!-- bt-block:000108 -->
Valentino’s expression grew tight, uneasy. His nails bit into his arm. “I have given him many chances,” he murmured. “Every time he hurts me, I ask him what he thinks he is doing. Over and over, I have asked him to explain, to give me reasons, to help me understand. He never does. He treats it like a joke, like it’s funny that he hurt me.”

<!-- bt-block:000109 -->
“Yeah, he does that,” Alex allowed, because it was true. Even Alex got hurt from time to time when Marc made a too-mean joke at his expense, but at least he knew Marc loved him while he did it. Valentino, meanwhile, had no such assurance.

<!-- bt-block:000110 -->
With that thought in mind, and seeing Valentino’s raised eyebrow, Alex added, very generously: “I’ll fight him for you, if he does something really bad.”

<!-- bt-block:000111 -->
Valentino snorted. “I can fight him myself.”

<!-- bt-block:000112 -->
“Absolutely not,” Alex replied, because he might be generous, but he wasn’t stupid.

<!-- bt-block:000113 -->
Valentino burst out laughing.

<!-- bt-block:000114 -->
“Fine,” he said finally, and Alex immediately perked up. “Just one chance, just this once. As a prize for equalling my nine championships.”

<!-- bt-block:000115 -->
“Sure, that’s why,” Alex said, a bit sarcastically, but he also reached over and wrapped his arm around Valentino’s shoulders, pulling him into Alex’s side. “Really, Vale, thank you. I really, really appreciate this.”

<!-- bt-block:000116 -->
“You better,” Valentino grumbled, but he settled against Alex easily enough. “What do I get as a reward?”

<!-- bt-block:000117 -->
“A reward?”

<!-- bt-block:000118 -->
“For giving him a chance.”

<!-- bt-block:000119 -->
Alex hummed in mock thoughtfulness. “The joy of forgiveness?”

<!-- bt-block:000120 -->
Valentino scoffed. “I never say I will forgive anything.”

<!-- bt-block:000121 -->
Yeah, fair enough. “The joy of . . . giving someone a chance?”

<!-- bt-block:000122 -->
“Overrated.” Valentino pouted at Alex, peering up at him through his lashes, eyes deep and dark and so, so blue. “Really? Nothing?”

<!-- bt-block:000123 -->
“I’ll think of something,” Alex replied, trying really hard not to get distracted by Valentino’s closeness. How was he supposed to stay focused, though, when he was looking at Alex like that? “How about a trip?”

<!-- bt-block:000124 -->
“A trip? Where?”

<!-- bt-block:000125 -->
“It’ll be a surprise.” Alex lowered his voice, leaning in closer to Valentino slowly, ever so slowly. “I’ll plan everything. The travel, the place, the food, the activities. You just have to sit there and look pretty.”

<!-- bt-block:000126 -->
Valentino’s expression turned coy, his lips curling into a faint grin as he watched Alex’s slow approach. “You just want to go on a trip.”

<!-- bt-block:000127 -->
“I also think you’re very pretty,” Alex murmured.

<!-- bt-block:000128 -->
“Eh, you really are embarrassing,” Valentino murmured back.

<!-- bt-block:000129 -->
“You don’t mind, do you?”

<!-- bt-block:000130 -->
“No, I don’t—”

<!-- bt-block:000131 -->
“Ahem!”

<!-- bt-block:000132 -->
The loud sound of a throat being cleared snapped Alex out of his Valentino-induced daydream, and they both sprang apart to stare at the person who was standing, wide-eyed and frozen, at the entrance to the motorhome side rooms.

<!-- bt-block:000133 -->
“What the fuck?” Valentino hissed.

<!-- bt-block:000134 -->
“Marc!” Alex blurted out, too shocked for a moment to say anything else. “What are you doing here?”

<!-- bt-block:000135 -->
“This my motorhome,” Marc replied, still looking somewhat shell-shocked.

<!-- bt-block:000136 -->
And, yeah, Alex knew that. “I thought you left!”

<!-- bt-block:000137 -->
“No, I just went to my room.” Oh, so the door Alex had heard had been the door to the side rooms, not the entrance. Well, then. Not a mistake he’d ever make again, Alex would make sure of that. “Happened to overhear some stuff. You don’t mind, do you?”

<!-- bt-block:000138 -->
Silence fell in the motorhome— as Alex struggled to come to terms with the awfulness of that joke, and even Marc looked a bit horrified with himself— when suddenly Valentino pushed off the couch.

<!-- bt-block:000139 -->
“You know what, I don’t think a trip is worth this.”

<!-- bt-block:000140 -->
Alex immediately moved to block him. “Vale—”

<!-- bt-block:000141 -->
“This counts as a chance, the chance is over—”

<!-- bt-block:000142 -->
“No, it’s not, sit back down—”

<!-- bt-block:000143 -->
“I do not—” Valentino yelped as he was yanked back onto the couch, falling on his back and being immediately held down by Alex. He quickly tried to struggle, testing the grip on his wrists, lightly, then more strongly, eyebrows rising as he realized he was well and truly pinned. He looked up at Alex with a frown. “You are surprisingly strong.”

<!-- bt-block:000144 -->
Alex snorted. Did Valentino really think his training was all for show? “Surprisingly?”

<!-- bt-block:000145 -->
There was another loud throat-clearing sound, this one a bit more strangled, and both of them looked up to find Marc watching them with an expression Alex had never seen on his face before and struggled to describe. It looked a bit like he was watching two trains crashing in slow motion, but also like he might be considering jumping in between them. The trains, not—

<!-- bt-block:000146 -->
Alex felt his own face heating up. Valentino, meanwhile, still looked like he was considering how to escape the situation as quickly as possible.

<!-- bt-block:000147 -->
“Don’t make that face, you can leave anytime,” he snapped at Marc. “In fact, please do.”

<!-- bt-block:000148 -->
“As I said, this is my motorhome,” Marc replied, slowly recovering from his . . . shock? Horror? Alex really couldn’t tell. He shook his head, then pointedly looked away from Valentino, and focused only on Alex. “Alex. I wanted to apologize.”

<!-- bt-block:000149 -->
Alex regarded him warily. “For what?”

<!-- bt-block:000150 -->
“For a lot of things,” Marc said, and he did sound genuinely contrite. “But, first of all, I’m sorry for what I said yesterday. I didn’t mean to imply that I don’t respect you, or that anyone wouldn’t want to be friends with you. I was just trying to insult Valentino—”

<!-- bt-block:000151 -->
“Fuck you?” Valentino piped up, indignant.

<!-- bt-block:000152 -->
“I’m joking,” Marc said, though they all knew he wasn’t. “No, but seriously, I’m sorry, Alex. And I also wanted to say that I know that, as much as the last few years have been awful for me, they’ve also been awful for you. So I am genuinely glad you had someone to help you through that, when I . . . when I couldn’t.” He finally dared to look at Valentino again, though it looked like the action pained him. “Even if it had to be him.”

<!-- bt-block:000153 -->
“Fuck you,” Valentino repeated, far more dryly.

<!-- bt-block:000154 -->
“Fuck you, too,” Marc returned, just as dry.

<!-- bt-block:000155 -->
“I accept your apology,” Alex interrupted, because he had the feeling that if he didn’t, Marc and Valentino were about to start punching each other. “I know you didn’t mean to be insulting. I know you were just angry. In shock, whatever. But still, thank you for apologizing.”

<!-- bt-block:000156 -->
“I really, really didn’t mean it.”

<!-- bt-block:000157 -->
“I know, it’s—” Alex startled as Valentino bucked below him, and he hurried to secure his grasp on the other man’s arms. “Will you behave?”

<!-- bt-block:000158 -->
“This is uncomfortable,” Valentino complained, and, yeah, he did look uncomfortable. However . . .

<!-- bt-block:000159 -->
“I’m not letting you go until you promise to behave.”

<!-- bt-block:000160 -->
“But my back,” Valentino said with a pout. And usually Alex would be easily swayed by that, but this was Marc on the line. He could be strong for Marc. Thankfully, Valentino quickly realized his strategy would be ineffective, so he sighed and dropped the pretense. “Okay, okay, I promise to behave.” He didn’t sound particularly sincere, but Alex would take his word for it. So, with one last warning glance, he let Valentino up from the couch, who, thankfully, settled back down in his previous spot without a fuss. Valentino then turned to Marc with crossed arms and a glare. “So. What now?”

<!-- bt-block:000161 -->
“You give me a chance?” Marc said, looking supremely unimpressed with the whole situation. Alex, of course, knew Marc was far more affected by what was going on than he appeared.

<!-- bt-block:000162 -->
“If I have to.” Valentino huffed. Paused. “So. What does giving you a chance mean?”

<!-- bt-block:000163 -->
“We apologize to each other and have an honest conversation for once in our lives?” For a second they just stared at each other, before Marc broke away with a snort, walking over to the nearby closet and pulling out a small box. “Just joking. I have cards.”

<!-- bt-block:000164 -->
“Oh, thank fuck.” Valentino sagged in relief, automatically taking the deck when Marc offered it to him and beginning to shuffle the cards. “Scopa?”

<!-- bt-block:000165 -->
“Of course,” Marc replied without hesitation, pulling up a chair so he could sit opposite the couch by the table. “You joining, Alex?”

<!-- bt-block:000166 -->
Alex blinked at him, then at the cards being delt before him. “I don’t know how to play.”

<!-- bt-block:000167 -->
Marc frowned, looking up at Valentino. “You haven’t taught him?”

<!-- bt-block:000168 -->
Valentino frowned right back at Marc. “You haven’t?”

<!-- bt-block:000169 -->
“You’ve been friends for years.”

<!-- bt-block:000170 -->
“You’ve been brothers your whole lives.”

<!-- bt-block:000171 -->
“It’s an Italian game.”

<!-- bt-block:000172 -->
“It’s your favorite game.”

<!-- bt-block:000173 -->
“It’s your favorite game?” Alex asked Marc, surprised. Marc blinked at him, then looked back towards Valentino, then back at the cards in his hand.

<!-- bt-block:000174 -->
“Used to be,” he said quietly.

<!-- bt-block:000175 -->
An awkward silence descended upon the motorhome living room.

<!-- bt-block:000176 -->
Then, Valentino coughed loudly.

<!-- bt-block:000177 -->
“So!” he said, and turned to Alex. “How you play Scopa is this . . . “

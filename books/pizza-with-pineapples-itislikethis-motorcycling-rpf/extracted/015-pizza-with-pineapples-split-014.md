<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
Tomorrow came far too soon.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
Alex snuck out of Valentino’s motorhome early in the morning and made his way back to his own. As they’d agreed, he and Marc didn’t speak about last night’s incident at all. All throughout the day, they only discussed the race: Alex’s management strategies, bike parameters, tyre options, braking points. Only after the race would they talk about anything else.

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
After the race also came far too soon. Sixth place wasn’t bad, but it wasn’t great, either. Especially given Alex had started second, especially given how he’d gotten passed by Fermin in the last corner. But although Alex wasn’t exactly proud of his race, given how much else was weighing on his mind, he was willing to forgive his sub-par performance.

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
After his debrief with the team, he and Marc made their way back to their motorhome. The feeling between them wasn’t tense, exactly, but there was a clear weightiness to it that Alex wasn’t yet sure how to address.

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
Truthfully, he still wasn’t sure how to address anything of what they’d soon talk about. But, it was for the best that they did talk about it. So, talk about it they would.

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
“I want you to explain how this happened,” Marc began, as soon as the motorhome’s door closed behind him.

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
“I can explain. But first, I want to tell you about something,” Alex said, as he made his way to the couch. “You’re, um. Going to want to sit down for this.”

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
“Sure,” Marc said, a little hesitantly, though he did take a seat next to Alex. “Can’t be worse than what I already figured out,” he then muttered under his breath.

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
Alex tried not to react to that. Took a deep breath, as he tried to steady himself.

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
Valentino and Alex had eventually agreed, late last night, that it was better to tell Marc the truth of their relationship rather than have him find out by himself. At least this way, Alex would have a far better chance of successfully dealing with the fallout. Or, barring that, he’d have a far better chance of warning Valentino sufficiently in advance.

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
It would still go terribly, Alex was sure of that. But, that didn’t mean it wasn’t the best way.

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
“Okay. So. There’s no good way to lead gently into this, so I’m just going to be blunt. Valentino and I aren’t just friends,” he said, as clearly as he could, because he didn’t want Marc to mishear him. It was best to break the news in one blow; to break the bone cleanly, so to speak, so there would be a better chance of it healing with minimal complications. “We’re together.”

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
Marc, unfortunately, still didn’t quite seem to hear him. Or, rather, he probably didn’t believe what he was hearing, if the suddenly dazed look in his eyes was any indication. “To . . . gether?”

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
“We’re dating,” Alex said, just in case his meaning wasn’t clear. Watched, as Marc’s entire body froze, his features constricting in a manner akin to how he looked when he was being asked an awful interview question, except worse. Much worse. “Please don’t freak out.”

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
“I’m trying really hard not to,” Marc said, voice toneless, expression frozen, and Alex believed him. “Is this a joke?”

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
“It’s not.”

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
“Can you just . . . say it’s a joke?”

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
“It’s not.”

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
Marc had blinked twice since the conversation started. Alex had counted. “So all those times I asked if you and . . . ‘Pipa’ were dating—”

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
“We weren’t, we really weren’t.”

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
“Since when, then?”

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
“Two months ago.”

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
For some reason, that statement was what seemed to snap the truth of the situation into place within Marc. The dazed look left his eyes and was immediately replaced with a ruthless, cold anger.

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
“I’m going to kill him,” he declared, and went to stand.

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
“No, you’re not!” Alex immediately pushed him back down, leaning his entire weight onto Marc as his brother did his best to buck him off. “Don’t— Stop!”

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
Marc seemed not to hear him, using his lower leverage to try and force Alex off him. “Get off—”

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
“Absolutely not!” Alex grabbed hold of both of Marc’s wrists, just as the other man swung his legs to try and destabilize them. With a slam, they fell onto the floor, and Alex immediately used his better position to hold Marc down, though not without some trepidation. “When I told Vale I’d fight you for him, I didn’t think it’d actually happen,” he muttered, somewhat thoughtlessly.

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
“You told him you’d fight me for him?” Marc said, peering up at Alex from the ground. He’d gone still, but Alex knew that just meant Marc was considering his options. He tightened his hold on Marc’s wrists, wary and worried.

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
“I was joking!” he tried to defend himself. He should have known Marc would take it the wrong way. “He was worried you were going to kill him.”

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
Sure enough, Marc’s eyes darkened. “He’s always been smart.”

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
“I told him I wouldn’t let you.” As Alex suspected, Marc took the chance to fling his body upwards, momentarily catching Alex off-balance. He barely managed to use his hold on Marc’s wrists to fight him back down, using his knee to press Marc’s legs to the side as he twisted his shoulder outward. He was trying, so hard, not to injure Marc, but Alex feared he’d be left with no options if Marc didn’t relent soon. As much as Alex wanted to believe Marc would not actually, literally try to kill Valentino, he also wasn’t sure that he wouldn’t. “Fuck, don’t— don’t make me break your arm.”

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
Marc looked up at him, eyes dark and cold and everything Alex never usually associated with his brother, not when it came to him, not when it came to them. “You wouldn’t.”

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
“I would to stop you from killing him,” Alex said, but even he wasn’t sure he was strong enough to do that. Emotionally, that was. Physically, it would be only too easy. “Marc, please,” he begged.

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
Whether he was strong enough or not, Alex never, ever wanted to find out.

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
Perhaps his thoughts were apparent, because finally, finally, something gave in Marc’s expression. “I’m just going to punch him.”

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
Alex relaxed slightly. He still didn’t fully trust Marc, but it seemed like the worse of his blind fury was over. “No.”

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
“Just once.”

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
“No.”

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
“Alex, you can’t be serious about this,” Marc said, and Alex immediately sensed that while Marc may have given up on killing Valentino, all he was actually doing was switching tactics. “What will Mom and Dad think?”

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
“They’ll think nothing because we are not going to tell them. Not for a long time,” Alex said. Tensed, as he realized what Marc was thinking. “If you say anything about this, to anyone, before we’re ready to tell people, I will never forgive you.” Marc still seemed to be considering it, so Alex added, as clearly as he could, “I’m serious, Marc. I will never forget this, and I will never forgive you.”

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
“Why? Because you’re scared of what people will say?” Marc bit back, sensing weakness. “Because people will ask you about him, about me, and begin questioning what—”

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
“This isn’t about him, or about you, or about anyone else! This is about me!” Alex snarled, because Marc just didn’t seem to get it— worse, he didn’t seem to want to get it. “*I* will never forgive you, because this is something *I* am trusting you with. And if you break *my* trust, *I* will never forgive you!”

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
At that, finally, Marc looked a little startled. A little like he hadn’t realized how serious Alex actually was about all this, a little like he was finally listening to what Alex was saying.

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
Still, he continued trying. “Please listen to me, Alex. You’re my brother. You can’t—”

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
“I don’t belong to you!” Alex yelled, and now he was really angry, and he didn’t want to be angry, but what Marc was saying was just— “Fucking listen to yourself, Marc! You talk like— like me being your brother means that I belong to you! Like I’m an object that you can do whatever you want with! Like I don’t have my own thoughts, and hopes, and wants, and beliefs, like I’m not my own person! But I am!” Seeing Marc’s wide-eyed shock, Alex lowered his voice, relaxing his hold on Marc’s arms. “I’m sorry if that hurts you, but— no, fuck that, I’m not sorry. I am my own person, and I do have my own thoughts and hopes and beliefs. And if they don’t always align with yours, that’s fine, that’s normal. Because I don’t belong to you. And I’m not sorry about that, I’m not.”

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
“I know you don’t belong to me,” Marc said quietly, as he drew back from Alex and sat up against the legs of the couch. Alex watched him warily, just in case he tried to bolt again, but Marc no longer seemed to be thinking about Valentino. At least, not in a murderous way. “I’m just— I’m just trying to protect you.”

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
“No, you’re not,” Alex stated. He could feel his eyes prickling, heating with the depth of his frustration, his worry, his anger. “You’re just trying to protect yourself.”

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
Marc stared at him, looking like Alex had just slapped him.

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
It was a bit ridiculous, Alex thought, how he could threaten to break Marc’s arm and Marc wouldn’t really care. But being critical of him, no matter how gently, always had a chance of ripping apart something tender.

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
Alex always tried so, so hard, not to rip into the tender parts of Marc. But it was impossible, in this situation, for some blood not to be spilt.

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->
“Marc, please,” he begged. “You know how long he and I have been talking to each other. Did it ever feel like I was being . . . pressured, or something, by him? Did it ever feel like he was, I don’t know, trying to manipulate me? Or turning me against you in some way? Did it ever feel like any of that? Be honest with me, please.”

<!-- bt-block:000056 -->
Marc’s jaw ticked. “The secrecy—”

<!-- bt-block:000057 -->
“You know exactly why the secrecy had to happen! That’s not an argument!” Alex took another deep breath. Tried to calm himself, to think of what else he could say, because they were just arguing in circles. If Alex wanted Marc to listen to him, to really listen . . .  “If you love me, you will not do anything to interfere with this.”

<!-- bt-block:000058 -->
It was a low blow, Alex knew that. But if that was what it took for Marc to listen to him, then that was what it would take.

<!-- bt-block:000059 -->
Marc stared at him. Slowly, his shoulders sagged. “If he hurts you . . . “

<!-- bt-block:000060 -->
“Then I will deal with it,” Alex stated. “I’m an adult, Marc. I can deal with the consequences of my choices.”

<!-- bt-block:000061 -->
“He can really make it hurt,” Marc said, and his voice had fallen to a whisper. “When he wants to. He’ll make it hurt.”

<!-- bt-block:000062 -->
“I know, I know he can,” Alex said, voice softening in response. “But so can I. So can you. If we want to— when we want to— we can also make it hurt.”

<!-- bt-block:000063 -->
So can I. So can you. A softening of the blow, as gentle a tear as Alex could make it. But Alex did want Marc to realize what he was saying— that it wasn’t all Valentino, it never had been, and Marc wouldn’t be able to convince Alex of it. Alex had, after all, watched it all happen, right from the beginning.

<!-- bt-block:000064 -->
“I just want you to accept that I’m not going into this with my eyes closed,” Alex said. “To accept that I’m smart enough to not be getting tricked. Because I’m not.”

<!-- bt-block:000065 -->
“But how?” Marc said, and for the first time, he really seemed to be listening to what Alex was saying. Actually trying to understand. “Being friends is one thing, but . . . dating him?”

<!-- bt-block:000066 -->
“It . . . took a bit to convince him,” Alex confessed, a bit awkwardly, because yes, he could see how that part might be a bit difficult to believe.

<!-- bt-block:000067 -->
Marc blinked. “Convince . . . him?”

<!-- bt-block:000068 -->
“Well, yeah.” Alex watched as Marc’s brows furrowed. A hilarious, ridiculous idea suddenly occurred to Alex. Really, after everything, did Marc really think . . . ?  “What? You really thought he was the one going after me?”

<!-- bt-block:000069 -->
“The way you talked about the idea of dating ‘Pipa’ in the past made it sound like it,” Marc muttered. Sighed, as he rubbed his face. “Then again, that’s not how you’d react if you actually didn’t want to. So. Makes sense, I guess.”

<!-- bt-block:000070 -->
Alex watched his brother. Wondered, not for the first time, just what Marc was actually thinking. What he was actually feeling, about all of this. It clearly wasn’t as obvious to him as he’d probably like it to be. Certain aspects of the situation were clearly contradicting themselves within him. “Marc . . . “

<!-- bt-block:000071 -->
“Just, does it have to be Valentino?” Marc asked. Pleaded, really. “Out of all people, does it have to be him?”

<!-- bt-block:000072 -->
Alex sighed. “It doesn’t have to be, no.”

<!-- bt-block:000073 -->
“Then—”

<!-- bt-block:000074 -->
“But I want it to be.” Alex needed Marc to understand that. Out of everything, if there was only one thing Marc could understand, Alex needed it to be this. “I want it more than anything.”

<!-- bt-block:000075 -->
Sure enough, Marc looked defeated by that one assertion. Everything else, he could fight against. Their difficulties, their past, their families, their situations— everything was, in many ways, positioned in Marc’s favor.

<!-- bt-block:000076 -->
But not Alex. Marc could not fight against Alex.

<!-- bt-block:000077 -->
“Look. I know it’s not . . . I know it’s not what you want. And you can be angry about it, I understand. I just . . . “ Alex said, softly because he knew there was no need to push so hard anymore. “I just hope you won’t stay angry forever.”

<!-- bt-block:000078 -->
Marc looked down at his hands. “I’m not angry at you.”

<!-- bt-block:000079 -->
“I want you to not be angry at him, either,” Alex insisted. “You don’t have to get along with him. I mean, he’s not . . . he doesn’t exactly want to get along with you, either. But he’s not angry at you. He’s just, you know.”

<!-- bt-block:000080 -->
“Yeah,” Marc murmured. His nails dug into the meat of his palms, leaving glaring red grooves behind. “I know.”

<!-- bt-block:000081 -->
The resignation in his tone was palpable. The old pain, the old wound, still there. Still festering, deep below the surface, no matter what Alex tried, no matter what Alex did. Alex no longer knew what he could do.

<!-- bt-block:000082 -->
Still, he reached over and gently took Marc’s hand in his. Marc didn’t look up, but he wrapped his hand around his in turn.

<!-- bt-block:000083 -->
“I just . . . “ Marc’s voice hitched, ever so slightly. “I don’t want to lose you.”

<!-- bt-block:000084 -->
“You won’t,” Alex said immediately, trying to hide his confusion, because, what?

<!-- bt-block:000085 -->
“It feels like I already have.” Marc still wasn’t looking at Alex. “Just a little bit.”

<!-- bt-block:000086 -->
Oh.

<!-- bt-block:000087 -->
“Marc. Listen to me. You’ll never lose me. You can’t.” Alex pulled on Marc’s hand, once, then again, more sharply, until Marc finally looked up at him. Alex stared into his eyes, willing him to understand. “Even if you wanted to, you wouldn’t be able to lose me.”

<!-- bt-block:000088 -->
Marc watched him. Didn’t blink. “What if Valentino wanted it?”

<!-- bt-block:000089 -->
“He doesn’t.” Alex tightened his grip on Marc’s hand. Held his gaze, as best he could. “But if he wanted it, the one who would lose me would be him. Not you. Never you.”

<!-- bt-block:000090 -->
Despite Marc’s worries, that was one concern Alex genuinely didn’t have.

<!-- bt-block:000091 -->
After all, Valentino knew, perhaps better than anyone, just how deep Alex’s loyalty towards Marc ran. He would never ask him to choose, not just because he knew he’d lose, but because he liked that aspect of Alex. He had praised him for his devotion, for his dedication, time and time again, had even made it clear to him that if it ever came down to it, Valentino would willingly be the one to step aside.

<!-- bt-block:000092 -->
Even though Valentino didn’t like Marc. Alex loved Marc, and that was all that mattered to him.

<!-- bt-block:000093 -->
Maybe Marc would one day understand that, too. But, for now, Alex’s reassurance would have to do.

<!-- bt-block:000094 -->
“Okay.” Marc looked back down at his hands. Let out a long breath, before moving to disentangle himself from Alex. “Okay.”

<!-- bt-block:000095 -->
Alex let him go, a bit reluctantly. “Marc . . . “

<!-- bt-block:000096 -->
“I need to be alone for a while.” Marc slowly stood from the couch. “I’ll talk to you later, okay?”

<!-- bt-block:000097 -->
“Okay,” Alex said. Watched, as Marc went over towards the living room. Alex then closed his eyes, leaning back into the couch. He heard the front door shutting as Marc left the motorhome, and finally allowed himself to release the deep breath he’d been holding.

<!-- bt-block:000098 -->
He waited one minute, then two.

<!-- bt-block:000099 -->
Then he pulled out his phone, and messaged Valentino.

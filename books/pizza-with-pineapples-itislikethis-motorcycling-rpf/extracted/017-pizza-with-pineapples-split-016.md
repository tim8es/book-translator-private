*2025, Tavullia*

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
“You are joking,” Luca said flatly.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
“No,” Valentino replied.

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
“I told you it wasn’t Miller!” Cele hissed from the couch, to a loud sigh from Franky and a passed over bill.

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
“At least it’s not Marc,” Pecco muttered, and both Cele and Franky nodded.

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
Bez, meanwhile, fidgeted with his sleeves. “I think Marc’s nice,” he murmured.

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
Valentino rolled his eyes, but he still reached out to pat Bez on the back. “Then you can have him,” he said, and Alex almost choked on his drink.

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
“You are joking,” Luca repeated, and, yeah, Alex could see what Valentino had meant when he said Luca would be an uphill battle. The uphill battle, in fact, according to Vale. And truly, no one else seemed too opposed to the idea, shockingly enough.

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
Alex and Valentino had talked it over and eventually decided that, much like Marc, it was best to break the news to the VR46 academy on their terms, rather than have them find out on their own. So, after the season had ended, and after Alex had had a few weeks alone with Marc to make sure they were fine— which, so long as the topic of Valentino was strategically and tactically tip-toed around, they really were— Alex had flown over to Italy and made his way to Tavullia. The plan was for him to stay for a week. How long he'd actually stay. Alex was prepared to find out.

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
He had seen the ranch before, of course, in media and pictures and videos. But the real sight of it— the heart of Valentino's passion, his determination, his dreams, pried open by old wood and dirt tracks and motorbike oil— did make something lodge in the base of Alex's throat.

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
And, well. The knowledge of just who was waiting inside the building above the hill did make him a bit nervous, too.

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
What Valentino had told them all in advance, Alex wasn’t entirely sure. But he’d been greeted at the door by Pecco, who had looked only slightly surprised by his presence— or rather, perhaps, his identity.

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
Alex wasn’t sure what Vale had told them.

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
But, the entire academy had been gathered at the ranch that afternoon. And, sitting on the couches in the living room, Alex had told them his side of the story. Shockingly enough, Alex realized the more he spoke without any major interruptions— or any attempts to bodily assault him— no one seemed too opposed to the situation.

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
No one, that was, apart from Luca.

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
“He’s mentioned Tola in front of us enough over the past years that we’re not, you know. Too shocked,” Pecco said, when Alex asked him about it a bit later, while they were alone in the living room while the rest of the academy got the bikes ready for an evening ride. “We didn’t know it was you, but we knew enough to be, uh. Mentally prepared for something like this.”

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
“Really?” Alex asked doubtfully, because it was one thing to know Tola existed, another to guess it was Alex, and another entirely to guess he and Valentino were together. And, no one from the Academy had seen him and Valentino during Misano. That, Alex was pretty sure of.

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
“It’s the whole, you know. Secret phone calls, hidden meetings, MotoGP rider combination. Could only be a few people, really.” Pecco paused. Sighed. “I’m not going to lie. With the how secretive Vale was being, a lot of us were worried it was going to be Marc.”

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
“I wasn’t!” Cele piped up as he ran through the room carrying a bunch of leathers.

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
Pecco waved him off. “Ignore him, he thought it was Fabio.”

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
“Fabio?” Alex repeated, because he was trying really hard not to think about the other thing Pecco had said.

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
“For the record, I really am happy it’s you and not Marc.” Yeah, Alex was pretty happy about that, too, though what he really felt was vaguely disturbed. “No offense, but your brother is, uh. Not the best influence on Vale. I’m sure you know what I mean.”

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
What a nice way to phrase it. But, Alex supposed, they were all trying to be nice. “No, I know. It’s mutual.”

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
“Yeah.” Pecco looked amused for a moment before a serious expression crossed his face. “But, eh, there is one thing. About Luca, he—”

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
“Alright, we’re ready! Everyone come out!” Valentino’s voice rang out from outside. Pecco turned towards the door, then back towards Alex.

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
“Just, good luck,” he ventured with a dry, but genuine enough smile, and then went to go outside.

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
Alex, after taking a moment to process that worrisome comment, followed him.

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
“So,” Luca said, tone accusing, expression tight and cold, “You really were Tola all along.”

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
At the very least, Alex comforted himself, Luca had waited for their evening ride to be over before he’d tried to come after Alex. He wasn’t sure he would have survived if Luca had decided to strike while they were still on their bikes.

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
Still. Following Alex to the dark side of the ranch house, where he’d gone to pick up the knee-pad he’d forgotten outside earlier, wasn’t giving Alex all that much reassurance that Luca wasn’t about to murder him in cold blood, either.

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
“I’m sorry, we had to keep it secret—”

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
“I know that, I’m not stupid,” Luca snapped, mouth pursed and hands gesticulating wildly in his irritation. “I just can’t believe it’s . . . you, of all people!”

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
He didn’t have to sound so insulted by that, Alex thought sourly. It wasn’t like Alex had done anything bad to Valentino. In fact, Luca should know that Alex hadn’t done anything bad to Valentino specifically, given how much Valentino had apparently been talking to Luca about Alex when it came to—

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
Oh. Right.

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
Alex took a deep breath, centered himself. He had dealt with Marc and that whole emotional fallout. Surely, he could deal with Luca’s little jealousy, too.

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
Except, Alex didn’t have the rapport with Luca that he did with Marc. In fact, they had no rapport with each other at all. So what leverage, exactly, did Alex have that he could use against Luca in this kind of situation?

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
Unfortunately, Alex really only had one option.

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
“If you kill me, Vale will be upset with you,” he said— trying for a bit of a joke, because the last thing he wanted was for Luca to think Alex was threatening him.

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
Thankfully— miraculously— Luca did look slightly amused by his reasoning. “He’d forgive me,” he then said, and his amusement suddenly felt distinctly less benevolent.

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
Alex thought back to all those times Valentino had expressed his concern over Marc trying to kill him, and how Alex had easily brushed him off. Perhaps, Alex thought, he owed Valentino an apology. Because this was genuinely kind of terrifying.

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
He raised his hands in front of himself, feeling a bit like a cornered animal. A lot, actually, especially with Luca’s piercing blue gaze nailed directly onto him. “Luca, listen, I’m—”

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
“No, you listen to me,” Luca said, and all the amusement abruptly fell from his expression. He took a step forward, and Alex immediately took one back. Luca’s eyes narrowed, and Alex began to wonder which one of them was the fastest runner. “I’ve already had to watch one Marquez hurt my brother for years. If I have to watch another—”

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
“Maro!” Relief flooded Alex as Valentino’s voice rang out into the night, the gangly shape of him appearing a few seconds later as he rounded the side of the house. Vale came to a stop between them, not quite directly in the middle, but making it clear no physical altercations were going to break out under his supervision. Still, when he looked at Luca, he mostly just looked amused. “Maro, stop scaring our guest.”

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
Luca, for his part, seemed suddenly a lot less intimidating, his righteous anger turning into something chastised and pouty as he looked at his brother. “I’m not scaring him, Vale.”

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
Valentino grinned at him, coddling and fond, before turning towards Alex. “Is he scaring you, Alex?”

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
Alex shifted on his feet nervously. “A little bit,” he admitted.

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
“Allora, a little is okay,” Valentino said with far too much levity, before turning back towards the house and waving them over. “Food is ready, come on. You can keep talking after.”

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
Alex had absolutely no intention of keeping talking after anything, and he hurried to follow Valentino back inside. He could hear the sound of gravel crunching behind him. It made the hairs on the back of his neck prickle.

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
“If Luca tried to kill me, would you fight him for me?” Alex asked quietly twenty minutes later, when everyone was huddled around the dinner table eating their post-workout meal. Alex had managed to snag the seat right next to Valentino, though both Franky and Luca had tried to herd him somewhere else.

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
Franky seemed to be doing it for benevolent reasons— wanting him to socialize, probably, to get along with the other members of the academy now that Alex was going to be spending more time with them.

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
Luca, not so much.

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->
Valentino’s face scrunched in surprise before he let out a huffed laugh. “What kind of question is this?”

<!-- bt-block:000056 -->
“An honest one,” Alex said, as he blew into his soup and brought it to his lips. It wasn’t bad. Quite good, in fact. “Would you?”

<!-- bt-block:000057 -->
Valentino hummed. “I do not think he will do something like this, don’t worry.”

<!-- bt-block:000058 -->
“I worry,” Alex parroted Valentino’s words back to him, and the older man snorted. “Come on, I fought Marc for you. And you know how fighting him sucks.”

<!-- bt-block:000059 -->
Valentino laughed, and Alex could see various pairs of eyes glancing in their direction. He fought not to fidget. He wasn’t feeling quite as nervous as he had earlier, but he still had the distinct sensation of being carefully observed; a bug beneath a microscope, an animal in a cage. He was there of his own volition, of course, but it didn’t make the experience any more pleasant.

<!-- bt-block:000060 -->
“Sadly, I do not think I would win in a fight against Maro,” Valentino murmured back after a moment. “He is stronger and taller. I have no leverage.”

<!-- bt-block:000061 -->
“You are smarter.”

<!-- bt-block:000062 -->
“Allora, no, he is—”

<!-- bt-block:000063 -->
“Don’t try that with me, asshole.” Valentino pouted at him, and Alex rolled his eyes. “Fine. Whatever. I’ll just fight him myself.”

<!-- bt-block:000064 -->
Valentino did fidget a bit at that. “Eh, this is also a bit—”

<!-- bt-block:000065 -->
“Well, what am I supposed to do? Just let him kill me?”

<!-- bt-block:000066 -->
“He will not—”

<!-- bt-block:000067 -->
“He will—”

<!-- bt-block:000068 -->
“Some more bread?” Alex startled, looking up to find Luca standing behind them with a basket filled with bread. The question was harmless, the bread even more so, but the look in his eyes was anything but innocent, and his smile was as sharp as glass. “Sorry. Am I interrupting?”

<!-- bt-block:000069 -->
“No, of course not,” Valentino said, and reached out to take a piece of bread. “Thank you, Maro, very nice.”

<!-- bt-block:000070 -->
“Of course,” Luca said, smile softening as it turned towards Valentino, only to immediately harden as it shifted back to Alex. “And you?”

<!-- bt-block:000071 -->
His tone wasn’t quite rude, but only just. Alex gave Luca a small, strained smile. “No, I’m fine, thanks.”

<!-- bt-block:000072 -->
Luca’s eyes narrowed, and he turned away from Alex without another word. Alex turned back towards the table and accidentally caught Franky’s eye. The other rider gave him a faint smile and a nod.

<!-- bt-block:000073 -->
Alex took a deep breath. Nodded back.

<!-- bt-block:000074 -->
He could do this.

<!-- bt-block:000075 -->
He could do this.

<!-- bt-block:000076 -->
“So,” Pecco said, leaning back into the lawn chair as he peered out into the surrounding darkness of the dirt track. “I was watching you two.”

<!-- bt-block:000077 -->
*Oh, I know,* Alex thought to himself, as he bit back a long sigh. *You and everyone else.*

<!-- bt-block:000078 -->
“And?” was what he actually said, because he could tell Pecco had certain observations he wanted to share. What those observations were, Alex wasn’t sure, but he hoped they weren’t too negative, at least.

<!-- bt-block:000079 -->
“It’s . . . well. It’s weird,” Pecco finally said, sounding like he’d been rolling the words around in his head for a while now. “You two really are friends.”

<!-- bt-block:000080 -->
“Yes?” Alex asked, confused now, because he’d thought that was well established by that point. “I promise, this is not a joke.”

<!-- bt-block:000081 -->
“No, I understand that. I meant, you two actually get along. Like, for real. You two genuinely, actually like each other.”

<!-- bt-block:000082 -->
“Yes?” Alex repeated, even more confused, as well as unsure of where Pecco was going with this.

<!-- bt-block:000083 -->
“No, it’s . . . “ Pecco paused for a moment, clearly gathering his thoughts yet again. “It’s just, when I heard you two had been talking for six years, I actually thought it was not that much. In the sense of how good friends you are, I mean. Like, you and I have known each other for, I don’t know. Almost twenty years, at this point?”

<!-- bt-block:000084 -->
Twenty years, huh. “I guess so.”

<!-- bt-block:000085 -->
“But we aren’t really friends,” Pecco continued, and Alex nodded. “So I thought it was a bit like that, really. As for the dating . . . I’m going to be honest, I thought it was just a hook-up at first.”

<!-- bt-block:000086 -->
Alex tried not to react to that. “But . . . ?”

<!-- bt-block:000087 -->
“But it isn’t that, is it? This is actually serious.” Pecco looked at Alex— really looked at him, focused and piercing, studying him as though he’d never seen him before. “I mean, it’d have to be, right? For both of you to risk telling us all the truth. You’re both smart enough to realize how risky this is. It wouldn’t be worth it for a hook-up, or even a fling.”

<!-- bt-block:000088 -->
“Yes,” Alex said, feeling like something was stuck in his throat. “It is.”

<!-- bt-block:000089 -->
Pecco continued watching him for a long moment. His hands curled into his sides, fingers tangling on the edge of his shirt as he glanced at the track, then back at Alex.

<!-- bt-block:000090 -->
“I love Vale,” he finally said, and beyond the fondness of his tone, there was a core of something hard and cold in the depths of his gaze. “We all do. And it’s not just about all he’s done for us. It’s about who he is. Who he is to us, who he is to all of us. He deserves to be happy.” Again, an outer layer of softness, covering a deceptively solid underneath. Pecco’s words weren’t a threat. But they were close. “Can you make him happy?”

<!-- bt-block:000091 -->
That was a very blunt question, Alex thought, a bit hysterically, to be asking someone who had been dating Valentino for . . . what, five months?

<!-- bt-block:000092 -->
Still. Alex had to admit that beyond the harshness of the not-threat, it was somehow heartening, too. Because it meant Pecco was taking him seriously— that he was taking them, Alex and Vale, seriously. And Alex did want to make Valentino happy.

<!-- bt-block:000093 -->
The question was: could he?

<!-- bt-block:000094 -->
“I’ll try,” Alex said, after a moment had passed. Added, after another moment, “I’ll try my best.”

<!-- bt-block:000095 -->
It wasn’t, in some ways, the answer that Alex knew Pecco expected. A resounding ‘yes’, to answer the gauntlet he’d so politely smashed into the ground before them.

<!-- bt-block:000096 -->
But Alex didn’t want to make a show of this. He had no intention of making a show of Valentino, as much as he knew many people probably expected it. Alex liked Valentino, the show, truly he did. But it was Valentino, the person, who Alex genuinely cared for. And it was Valentino, the person, who Alex would try his best for.

<!-- bt-block:000097 -->
It was, after all, the best he could do.

<!-- bt-block:000098 -->
Pecco’s eyes narrowed slightly. After a moment, however, he sighed and sagged into the chair. “Yeah. I guess that’s fair.”

<!-- bt-block:000099 -->
Alex wasn’t sure if Pecco had understood his reasoning. He didn’t seem disappointed, exactly, but he didn’t seem quite happy, either.

<!-- bt-block:000100 -->
Perhaps, then, there was something else Alex could do.

<!-- bt-block:000101 -->
“It’s not just about me, though.” Pecco turned back to Alex, who shrugged a bit awkwardly. “Like you said, you all love him. So, we’re all going to make him happy together. All of us. Right?”

<!-- bt-block:000102 -->
Just as Alex had Marc, Valentino had his academy. His family, in many ways, if not necessarily in blood. But, in many ways, blood was probably the least important aspect of family as far as Valentino was concerned.

<!-- bt-block:000103 -->
And, well, he had Luca. So there was some blood in there, too.

<!-- bt-block:000104 -->
Pecco’s expression flickered for a few moments, before settling on amusement as a wry smile graced his face. “Twenty years, huh. I guess it’s about time.”

<!-- bt-block:000105 -->
“ . . . Time for what?” Alex asked, confused by the switch in topic.

<!-- bt-block:000106 -->
“For us to be friends.” Pecco shrugged. “Try, at least.”

<!-- bt-block:000107 -->
Alex blinked at him. He hadn’t expected the conversation to turn in that direction . . . but then again, he had implied it, hadn’t he, with that comment? “Sure.”

<!-- bt-block:000108 -->
Pecco snorted. “Don't sound too enthusiastic.”

<!-- bt-block:000109 -->
“Sorry,” Alex said with a short laugh. Relaxed, then, as he too sank into his chair. “I would like that, actually.”

<!-- bt-block:000110 -->
Alex walked back into the ranch a few minutes later— Pecco having decided to stay outside— to find the rest of the academy surrounding the TV where a Switch had been set up.

<!-- bt-block:000111 -->
“What are you doing?” he asked Franky, who pointed to the screen just as it lit up to show a familiar image.

<!-- bt-block:000112 -->
“We were going to play Mario Kart.”

<!-- bt-block:000113 -->
Alex perked up. “Oh, nice. I love Mario Kart.”

<!-- bt-block:000114 -->
“Really?” Franky sounded . . . not quite doubtful, but a bit surprised.

<!-- bt-block:000115 -->
“Yeah, I play it all the time,” Alex assured him, looking around the room. An idea occurred to him, and before he could lose his nerve, he asked: “Can I join?”

<!-- bt-block:000116 -->
“Sure,” Franky said, and moved over slightly while handing Alex his controller. “Here, you go first.”

<!-- bt-block:000117 -->
“Is that alright?” Alex asked, sitting next to him, pressing the buttons a few times as he got used to the controller.

<!-- bt-block:000118 -->
“I’d rather not have to go against Luca.”

<!-- bt-block:000119 -->
Alex blinked at him, then looked backward to the person sitting on the couch next to Cele. Sure enough, Luca peered down at him with narrowed eyes, haughty and challenging.

<!-- bt-block:000120 -->
“If you’re worried you’re not good enough, you can always go next,” he said, with a shrug that was as casual as it was blatantly taunting.

<!-- bt-block:000121 -->
Oh. Oh, so it was like that.

<!-- bt-block:000122 -->
Alex barely managed to bite back a scoff as he raised an eyebrow at Luca. “Trust me, it’s not me I’m worried about.”

<!-- bt-block:000123 -->
Cele hooted, and beside him Franky also chuckled.

<!-- bt-block:000124 -->
“Yeah, yeah,” Luca said, gripping the controller tightly in his hand. “Alright, let’s see how good you are at this kind of racing.”

<!-- bt-block:000125 -->
“You’re cheating!”

<!-- bt-block:000126 -->
“Blue shells are part of the game—”

<!-- bt-block:000127 -->
“You inked me three times in a row!”

<!-- bt-block:000128 -->
“You pushed me off twice!”

<!-- bt-block:000129 -->
“You keep bumping into me!”

<!-- bt-block:000130 -->
“I’ll bump you into the gravel next time we—”

<!-- bt-block:000131 -->
“Allora, what is going on here?”

<!-- bt-block:000132 -->
Both Alex and Luca froze, peering up from where they’d somehow become tangled on the floor as they’d progressively gotten more and more involved in trying to hamper each other in their racing. Valentino was standing above them, hands on his hips and his eyebrows raised well up onto his forehead.

<!-- bt-block:000133 -->
Alex suddenly realized Cele and Franky were nowhere to be found. When they’d left, he had no idea, and he was willing to bet Luca didn’t, either.

<!-- bt-block:000134 -->
Alex bit his cheek, trying desperately to come up with some kind of excuse to get both of them peacefully off the hook—

<!-- bt-block:000135 -->
“He started it!” Luca called out.

<!-- bt-block:000136 -->
“No, I didn’t!” Alex retorted, all thoughts of peace immediately fleeing his mind. “Luca was cheating!”

<!-- bt-block:000137 -->
Luca glared at him, his hand coming down to smack at Alex’s controller, who immediately moved it out of the way. “I was not! You fell off by yourself, don’t blame your lousy driving skills on me!”

<!-- bt-block:000138 -->
Alex scoffed, kicking at Luca’s shins, who dodged and kicked at Alex’s hip. “Then don’t blame your losing on me—”

<!-- bt-block:000139 -->
“You blue-shelled me!”

<!-- bt-block:000140 -->
“You inked me!”

<!-- bt-block:000141 -->
“You—”

<!-- bt-block:000142 -->
“Ah,” Valentino said, crouching down between them, “so you two get along now! This is nice.”

<!-- bt-block:000143 -->
“We do not!” both of them exclaimed, shooting each other glares over Valentino’s shoulders.

<!-- bt-block:000144 -->
“This is nice,” Valentino repeated, smile curling at the edges as he looked over both of them fondly. “It makes me very happy.” Alex froze, and out of the corner of his eye, he could see Luca had done the same. Valentino’s head tilted, smile widening as he rested his chin on one of his hands. “Allora, why do you two look so surprised? My favorite people, getting along, of course I am happy.”

<!-- bt-block:000145 -->
Alright, so he was just going to be blatant about it, was he?

<!-- bt-block:000146 -->
Except it was still working, Alex admitted with some shame, his cheeks heating as his earlier conversation with Pecco flashed through his mind.

<!-- bt-block:000147 -->
At least he could take some comfort from the fact that Luca looked similarly stun-locked. He was probably as susceptible to Valentino as Alex was— probably more, if anything, though obviously not in the same way. The general motivation was similar enough, at least. Anything to make Vale happy, right?

<!-- bt-block:000148 -->
. . . and, alright, they had been having a bit of fun playing Mario Kart together. Just a bit. Even with all the cheating.

<!-- bt-block:000149 -->
Amazingly enough, Luca seemed to be sharing his thoughts, because when he next looked at Alex, some of the animosity had fallen from his gaze. Alex still didn’t think they’d leave this little trip as friends, but, perhaps, they could be . . . hmm, acquaintances . . . ?

<!-- bt-block:000150 -->
“Anyway, I just want to say thank you both for trying to get along. I know is difficult.” With that, Valentino leaned over and pressed a kiss to Luca’s forehead. Luca let out a small huff, as if pretending to be bothered, but he also shot Alex a very proud look, as if daring him to be upset with Luca being the chosen first to receive Valentino’s affection. Which was ridiculous, of course, Alex would never begrudge Valentino putting Luca over Alex, much like he’d never been begrudged—

<!-- bt-block:000151 -->
His thoughts came to a screeching halt as Valentino turned to Alex and pressed a kiss to his lips, pulling away after a second to grin at him mischievously.

<!-- bt-block:000152 -->
“Okay, that was all I was here to say,” he said, and moved to stand, brushing off his clothes as he did. “I will be going to sleep now. Alex, I will show you to our room?”

<!-- bt-block:000153 -->
Alex blinked up at him, frozen in shock for a moment. He didn’t dare to look at Luca. He had a feeling he might die if he did.

<!-- bt-block:000154 -->
The thought spurred him into action.

<!-- bt-block:000155 -->
“Oh. Uh. Yes. Yes, thanks.” He hurried to stand, quickly moving to put Valentino between himself and the still-unmoving figure sitting on the floor. Still stun-locked, it seemed. Thankfully. “Yes, let’s go.”

<!-- bt-block:000156 -->
Valentino laughed, pulling Alex out of the room by his hand. Alex went with him, clutching his hand like a lifeline, his face burning with embarrassment.

<!-- bt-block:000157 -->
“Why did you do that?” he hissed at Valentino as soon as the door to their bedroom was shut behind them.

<!-- bt-block:000158 -->
Valentino turned to grin at him, wry and delighted. “Eh? What do you mean?”

<!-- bt-block:000159 -->
“In front of Luca? Really?” Alex complained, and Valentino laughed again.

<!-- bt-block:000160 -->
“He has to get used to it. Best get him used to it early, no?”

<!-- bt-block:000161 -->
“That's—” Alex huffed, trying his best to ignore the way his stomach fluttered at that assertion; that they were something the others would have to get used to. Would get used to. “Still.”

<!-- bt-block:000162 -->
“Allora, do not be so upset. Next time, I'll make sure no one else is around,” Valentino said, pulling Alex over to him and then resting his arms on Alex's shoulders, eyes half-lidded and expression coy. "But it was a bit funny, no?"

<!-- bt-block:000163 -->
It really was a bit funny. But, Alex wasn’t going to let Valentino know that. He couldn't always let Valentino get his way, after all.

<!-- bt-block:000164 -->
“Next time,” Alex said, dropping his voice into a murmur as he leaned in closer, as Valentino's eyes darkened at their proximity, “you're going to come visit my house. And I'm going to make you eat pizza with pineapples.”

<!-- bt-block:000165 -->
Alex drew back with a laugh as Valentino made a sharp sound of disgust, face scrunching as he pushed on Alex's chest. Alex went with the motion, grabbing Valentino's wrist and pulling them both back so they fell on the bed. He huffed as Valentino dropped on top of him, gangly limbs entangling for a moment before they managed to sort themselves out.

<!-- bt-block:000166 -->
Making their way under the covers, Alex reached out and pulled Valentino into his side. Valentino curled into him, warm and soft and pliable— just as Alex had, by now, learned he could be.

<!-- bt-block:000167 -->
In the dark of their shared room, with the sounds of wind and insects and the glimmer of stars in the night sky outside their window, Alex thought his heart might burst from his chest, it felt so full.

<!-- bt-block:000168 -->
“Next time,” Valentino murmured into Alex’s neck, and his warm breath made Alex shiver, “we can do whatever you like.”

<!-- bt-block:000169 -->
Alex shivered again. He was too tired, far too tired to actually do anything— Still, he pulled Valentino even closer, trying to melt their bodies into each other as best he could.

<!-- bt-block:000170 -->
“Alright,” he murmured back, pressing a kiss to Valentino’s cheek, who let out a pleased, sleepy hum. “Next time.”

<!-- bt-block:000171 -->
In the distance, the stars glimmered through the night, the insects falling silent as their songs drew, eventually, to a close.

<!-- bt-block:000172 -->
As for Alex and Valentino, neither of them turned to look.

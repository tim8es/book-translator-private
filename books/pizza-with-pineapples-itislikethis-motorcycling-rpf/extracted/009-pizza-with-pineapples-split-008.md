*2022, Mugello*

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
“I don’t know if I’m cut out for MotoGP.”

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
Valentino paused, drink half-brought up to his mouth. He glanced at Alex, took a sip, before putting the glass back down onto the shelves beside them. The garage lighting, familiar and pale, washed over them both as Valentino finally turned to look at Alex fully. “What?”

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
Alex tried not to fidget. The VR46 chairs weren’t particularly uncomfortable— not any more than any other team’s garage chairs— but they felt particularly uncomfortable in that moment. “I said, I don’t know if I’m cut out for MotoGP.”

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
“No, I heard you the first time,” Valentino replied. “I just do not understand.”

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
“I just feel—” Alex let out a sharp breath, crossing his arms over his chest. He still wasn’t sure if it was the right thing to do, to discuss these thoughts with Valentino. But, he didn’t want to discuss them with Marc— couldn’t risk Marc feeling guilty about it, not when he had his own issues to worry about, issues far more serious than anything Alex was going through. But Alex had no one else to talk to. So Valentino it was, though Alex didn’t expect him to have any empathy for Alex’s situation, either. Sympathy, sure, but not empathy. “I don’t know what I’m doing wrong.”

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
Valentino’s brows furrowed. “What do you mean?”

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
“Vale, please. You know what I mean.” Alex turned toward the older rider. Caught his expression, genuinely confused. Alex fought not to feel humiliated. Valentino wasn’t doing it on purpose, surely, but surely he had to know exactly what Alex was talking about. Forcing him to verbalize it was a bit cruel, in that way. But Valentino still just looked politely quizzical, so eventually, Alex folded. “I’m doing terribly. This year, last year— 2020, whatever, I was a rookie, I understand why things were difficult, but now I’m in my third year. I can’t keep making excuses for why I’m so slow. And I feel like I’ve been trying everything, but nothing works. I don’t even know what to try at this point.” He leaned forward, digging his elbows into his knees. His gaze fell on the yellow Ducati in front of him, neon and bright and eye-catching. Just like its owner. “I feel so lost.”

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
Valentino was silent for a long moment.

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
“Do you remember,” he finally said, “your first podium?”

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
Alex raised an eyebrow at him. “You mean, my first podium, out of the many, many podiums I’ve had?”

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
“You start in the back of the grid— I think you were p18? — and you end up on the podium, in your first wet race,” Valentino continued as if Alex hadn’t spoken. “It was very impressive.”

<!-- bt-block:000016 -->
Alex suddenly had a bit of difficulty swallowing. His throat felt tight. “You remember that?”

<!-- bt-block:000017 -->
“Of course I remember,” Valentino replied, as if it were obvious. “As I said, was very impressive.”

<!-- bt-block:000018 -->
Alex closed his eyes. Opened them. “I remember you crashed.”

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
Valentino’s entire face spasmed. “Okay. Wow. Rude.”

<!-- bt-block:000020 -->
“No, I mean—” Alex closed his eyes again. Breathed. “You crashed. And Marc was injured. So I was alone on the podium.” Opened his eyes, again. Stared at a point above Valentino’s shoulder. “I was proud, but . . . it was a bit hard. To be happy.”

<!-- bt-block:000021 -->
Valentino was quiet for a few seconds.

<!-- bt-block:000022 -->
“I’m sorry,” he said.

<!-- bt-block:000023 -->
Alex frowned, turning back to him in confusion. “For crashing?”

<!-- bt-block:000024 -->
“No.” Valentino rolled his eyes. “For not calling. I was frustrated with my own race, I had been crashing a lot, and the situation with Yamaha was . . . anyway. I did not think to call. But I should have. You did really well, and you should not have been alone. Is horrible, I think, to do good in a race and not have people to be happy with.”

<!-- bt-block:000025 -->
Alex stared at him. Gnawed on his bottom lip. Felt it twinge with pain.

<!-- bt-block:000026 -->
“Actually, can I ask something?” Valentino said suddenly.

<!-- bt-block:000027 -->
“Sure,” Alex said.

<!-- bt-block:000028 -->
“Why didn’t you call?” Valentino’s voice had gone quiet, his expression softening. “If you did not want to be alone.”

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->
And, well. That was the question, wasn’t it?

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
Alex considered lying. Saying he hadn’t thought to. Saying he hadn’t wanted to.

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
But Valentino had asked. And Alex couldn’t deny him.

<!-- bt-block:000032 -->
“I didn’t think it was the right time,” he said, trying for nonchalant. “It would have been rude to call you to celebrate, after your crash.”

<!-- bt-block:000033 -->
Valentino frowned. “I don’t think it would be rude.”

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
“Insensitive, then,” Alex shrugged. Looked down, picked at his nails. “I just didn’t want to bother you.”

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->
They were both silent for a few seconds. Alex wondered if he’d messed up, in being honest. He hoped Valentino didn’t think he was trying to guilt him into anything, because Alex genuinely wasn’t. He was just trying to be honest, since Valentino had asked.

<!-- bt-block:000036 -->
He picked at his nails some more. Stopped, at seeing a tiny bead of blood swell at the edge of his cuticle. Pressed his hands onto his knees to try and prevent himself from causing them any more harm.

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
“You know, you can bother me a little,” Valentino said, and Alex startled out of his thoughts, looking up to meet Valentino’s eyes. Valentino grinned back at him, a bit dryly. “I know I said I block you if you bother me too much, but you never bother me at all. So, you know. A little is okay.”

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
Alex shrugged again, awkward and unsure. “I don’t want to bother you at all, though.”

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->
Valentino huffed quietly. “And I say, is okay if you do a little. You know. When you really, really want to.” He paused, seeing Alex’s frown. Reached out, suddenly, placing his hand on top of Alex’s, who froze in surprise. “You do not always have to make things easy for everyone else. It is okay to be a bother sometimes. And also, it is not a bother to want to be happy. Okay?”

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->
Alex stared at him. Stared and stared and stared.

<!-- bt-block:000041 -->
His eyes were prickling. It was a bit hard to breathe. The place where Valentino’s hand lay on top of Alex’s own burned.

<!-- bt-block:000042 -->
“I know that,” he finally managed to say. He felt a bit like he’d just been punched. “I know that. Of course I know that.”

<!-- bt-block:000043 -->
Valentino smiled. It looked a bit sad. “Okay. Is good you know.” He bent his fingers slightly, and Alex reflectively curled his hand around them. He was warm. They were both warm.

<!-- bt-block:000044 -->
Alex swallowed heavily though the lump in his throat.

<!-- bt-block:000045 -->
“I want to keep trying,” he whispered. “I just don’t know what to try.”

<!-- bt-block:000046 -->
Valentino nodded, humming thoughtfully.

<!-- bt-block:000047 -->
“I know this is not what you want to hear, but the truth is, at the end of the day, many of these things are not up to you,” he said. “You can be the best rider in the world, but if your bike sucks, there is nothing you can do.”

<!-- bt-block:000048 -->
Alex snorted, dry and sour. “Yes, because I’m definitely the best rider in the world. Oh, wait, no, wrong Marquez. Easy mistake to make, though, I understand the confusion.”

<!-- bt-block:000049 -->
Valentino rolled his eyes, not rising to Alex’s bait. “My point is, focus on what you can change, work to make that as good as possible, and don’t get too upset with the things you cannot change. Think about them, yes, try and see if there is a way to change them, but don’t let them drag you down. Believe that you are better, and do what you can to push for that.” He paused. Shrugged. “Just, you know. Try your best. It’s all you can do.”

<!-- bt-block:000050 -->
Alex blinked at him. “That’s . . . incredibly cheesy.”

<!-- bt-block:000051 -->
Valentino scoffed. “Rude.”

<!-- bt-block:000052 -->
“No, I mean, I like cheese.” Alex looked down. Realized, suddenly, he’d been stroking Valentino’s hand with his thumb. Sharply drew his hand back with a choked-off sound. “Sorry.”

<!-- bt-block:000053 -->
“Is okay, I do not mind,” Valentino said, with such genuine unconcern that Alex was immediately embarrassed over his own overreaction. And when Valentino drew back his hand, all Alex could feel was regret. “But, eh, one more thing.”

<!-- bt-block:000054 -->
Alex shook away those thoughts. Comfort was one thing, but he didn’t want to make Valentino uncomfortable. Though, the older rider was fairly touchy in general. Hopefully, he really hadn’t minded. “Yeah?”

<!-- bt-block:000055 -->
“Another thing you probably do not want to hear, but . . . “ Valentino paused. Sighed. “It is difficult to do your best when you cannot focus entirely on yourself. This is not your fault. Things are hard right now. But you are very strong. Okay?”

<!-- bt-block:000056 -->
Alex bit at his cheek. Pressed on his nails again. Watched the tiny bead of blood rising to the surface. “Okay.”

<!-- bt-block:000057 -->
“I’m sorry I cannot help more.”

<!-- bt-block:000058 -->
“No, I . . . “ Alex stopped. Shook his head. “Thank you. Really. I didn’t expect you to fix anything, you know? Just . . . “ He stopped again. Took a deep breath.

<!-- bt-block:000059 -->
You do not always have to make things easy for everyone else.

<!-- bt-block:000060 -->
It is okay to be a bother sometimes.

<!-- bt-block:000061 -->
And also, it is not a bother to want to be happy.

<!-- bt-block:000062 -->
Okay?

<!-- bt-block:000063 -->
Alex gathered his courage, his quiet regret. Shifted on his chair until his arm was pressed into Valentino’s side. Valentino pressed back, and Alex finally felt like he could breathe.

<!-- bt-block:000064 -->
“Thank you for listening to me,” he whispered. “Thank you for trying. I really do feel better.”

<!-- bt-block:000065 -->
Valentino hummed. “I’m glad I help, even a little.”

<!-- bt-block:000066 -->
“You did.” They sat in silence for a few seconds, Alex greedily taking in all the warmth he could. The chairs were still uncomfortable, but Alex would have stayed sitting there all night if it meant he could just have this easy warmth, this easy comfort, this easy this. Alex could be a bit of a bother. It was okay. He could have this. “Could you talk to me for a while? About anything. Whatever you want.”

<!-- bt-block:000067 -->
Valentino made a low noise of amusement. “Whatever I want? This is a dangerous request.”

<!-- bt-block:000068 -->
“I like dangerous things,” Alex said. Grew embarrassed, once more, as he realized the boldness of the statement. Valentino, however, didn’t seem to mind, as he merely chuckled in response.

<!-- bt-block:000069 -->
“If you say so.” Valentino paused, then shifted slightly in his seat. For a moment Alex thought— feared— he’d pull away. But he didn’t, instead settling along Alex’s side more fully, making himself at home against Alex’s ribs. Much like a cat, Alex thought, with a truly shocking amount of fondness. He barely managed to resist the urge to pet Valentino’s head, as that would definitely be too far. Just barely, though.

<!-- bt-block:000070 -->
“Allora, I will tell you a story,” Valentino declared, as soon as he was comfortable. Alex relaxed into him in turn. “It starts in Welkom. The year is two thousand and four, at the start of the season. I, after many months of fighting, with my team and my friends and my mechanics and my fans, have finally made the move from Honda to Yamaha . . . “

<!-- bt-block:000071 -->
Alex awoke the next morning feeling a little groggy, a little distracted, a little caught up in the memories of last night.

<!-- bt-block:000072 -->
He wasn’t sure how long Valentino had spent talking, in the end. He wasn’t even sure how he’d made his way back to his own motorhome from the garages. Alex had probably fallen asleep at some point. He wasn’t sure when Valentino had noticed, how long he’d spent talking before he realized his audience of one was no longer paying attention.

<!-- bt-block:000073 -->
The thought filled Alex with both amusement and affection.

<!-- bt-block:000074 -->
More importantly, however, he realized that the idea of going back out to practice that morning no longer filled him with a latent sense of dread. His inevitable failings no longer felt quite so inevitable, the endless disappointment no longer quite so endless.

<!-- bt-block:000075 -->
He felt, he realized as he got dressed, motivated anew.

<!-- bt-block:000076 -->
It was a bit ridiculous, he thought, as he left his motorhome and walked towards the garages— where he would surely find Marc, ready and waiting as he ever was— for a secret garage meeting and a nighttime story to have helped as much as it did.

<!-- bt-block:000077 -->
It was a bit ridiculous, Alex thought. But, he wouldn’t change a thing.

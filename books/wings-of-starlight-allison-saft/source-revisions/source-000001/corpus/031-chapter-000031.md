In some ways, I can hardly believe you’re reading this. Publishing my fifth book is a milestone I could not have fathomed reaching when I began writing “seriously” in 2016. And if you had told me back then that one day I would write something for Disney Fairies, I don’t think I would have believed you. Dreams really do come true.

<!-- bt-block:000002 -->
First, I want to express my immense gratitude for being trusted with such a beloved world. I hope this book felt like a homecoming for some of you. And if this is your entry point to Disney Fairies…To quote Queen Clarion herself: Happiness has brought you here. Welcome to Pixie Hollow!

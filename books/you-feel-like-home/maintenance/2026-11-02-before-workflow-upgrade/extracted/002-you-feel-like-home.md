# You Feel Like Home

by Hazellury

Summary

Prince Caleb Whitmore has survived assassins, scandals, and the suffocating expectations of the Crown. He has never survived Arian Conti.

When an assassination attempt erupts at an art auction in Paris, a mysterious man in black gets Caleb out alive, only to disappear before Caleb can learn his name.

Four months later, the stranger returns as his new protector.

Arian is the youngest godfather of the Conti family, feared by the underworld and trusted by the Crown. He was sent to protect the prince.

Falling for him was never part of the plan.

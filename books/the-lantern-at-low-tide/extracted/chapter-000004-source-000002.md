<!-- bt-block:000002 -->
When the Light Returned

<!-- bt-block:000003 -->
✦

<!-- bt-block:000004 -->
The key opened a narrow cupboard behind the lighthouse stairs. Inside lay a notebook, a tin of matches, and a photograph of a man holding a little girl by the sea. On the back, someone had written, *For Elin, when she is ready.*

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
Ada recognized the girl at once. It was her grandmother. She sat on the cold floor and opened the notebook. The keeper had not vanished at sea. He had left to repair the great glass lens after a fire, promising to return before winter. He never made it home, but he had arranged for the repaired lens to be delivered. No one had thought to open the cupboard.

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
A wooden crate stood behind a curtain. Inside it, wrapped in yellowing cloth, was a small replacement lamp. Ada carried it up the spiral stairs and set it beneath the glass. The matches were too damp to strike, so she used the lighter from her pocket. A flame caught, wavered, and grew steady.

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
Far below, a fishing boat answered with a brief flash of white. By morning, the whole town would see the light. Ada smiled, imagining her grandmother at the kitchen window, finally ready to tell the story from the beginning.

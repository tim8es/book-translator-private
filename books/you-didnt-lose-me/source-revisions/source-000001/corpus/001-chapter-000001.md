Mike Wheeler is going to die today.

He wakes up with a start, blinking his eyes a few times to process where he is, remembering that he had been knocked out only mere moments ago.

Flames burn bright through out the Mac-Z, and Mike's attention is quickly drawn to a demogorgon effortlessly crawling its way from a gate on the ground in front of him.

It darts over to him immediately, not daring to slow its pace as Mike lifts himself to his feet, having no time to run or hide or do anything to protect himself.

The demogorgon lunges at him, its arm outstretched and posed for attack. Its face opens up, exposing the many rows of teeth as thick strings of drool dangle from its mouth.

Mike throws his arms up in front of his face, shielding himself from both the impact of the incoming attack and the sight of it. He doesn't want the creature to be the last thing he sees.

In his final moments, he thinks of Will.

Will Byers, his best friend since he was five years old. Someone he's grown up with *and* grown to love.

He never got to tell him. He hopes Will knows.

Mike's story ends here, he knows there's no getting out of this. Any second now, he's going to feel the sharp claws of the demogorgon pierce through his skin and violently tear him apart.

He hopes death is quick.

With one final shriek from the creature, the piercing sound assaulting Mike's eardrums, he clenches his jaw, feet firmly planting on the ground. If he's going to die, it'll be with dignity. He won't fall to the floor in cower. Mike is going to be brave.

Suddenly, the shriek from the demogorgon changes from predatory to defensive, now sounding more like a pained cry.

With trembling hands and a heaving chest, Mike slowly lowers his arms, eyes still shut tight in fear of subjecting himself to the horrors in front of him. When the monster's cry doesn't stop, Mike opens his eyes, which go wide at the sight before him.

The demogorgon is floating in the air, frozen in the same position it had been in when it first lunged.

The tension in Mike's face releases, pure shock taking over as his jaw drops. He stares at the demogorgon suspended in front of him, vibrating against the unseen restraint.

Mike's arms fall to his sides as his brows furrow with confusion, jaw agape. Is this Vecna? No, Vecna doesn't give a shit about him, and certainly wouldn't stop him from being attacked. Could it be El? He knows she's somewhere in the Upside Down, but maybe her powers are stronger than anyone was led to believe.

He looks around, trying to make sense of the sight. His gaze drops, slowly scanning the area behind the monster. And that's when he sees it—sees *him*.

Standing in front of the fires, haloed by the blazing light behind him with his arm outstretched and eyes milky white, is *Will*.

He knew it, he fucking *knew* it.

*Sorcerer*.

Mike's widened eyes change from pure shock to something softer, more entranced. He's in complete awe.

Will's arm is shaking, his brows furrowed and jaw clenched with determination. Strands of his chestnut brown hair stick to his forehead, dirt caked around his face and neck.

His other hand shoots outward, both palms facing the sky as his head snaps up, his arms raising high above his shoulders. The flames rage on behind him, his eyes still rolled toward the back of his head, completely eliminating the view of his irises.

He looks like a God.

Just when Mike thinks he can't look away, the demogorgon in front of him rises in the air. It's limbs fall tight to its sides, bound by an invisible force—bound by *Will*.

The shrieking grows louder, the sound so high in pitch that Mike thinks his ears might bleed. He looks up at the monster, feeling lighter than he did before. He stares up at it with wonder as it quivers in pain. Suddenly, the demogorgon's arm snaps, the bone piercing through its slimy flesh as thick spurts of blood fly from the wound. The other arm breaks next, then both legs, and before he knows it, the monster is lying lifeless on the ground with a broken neck and a heap of contorted limbs.

Mike's eyes quickly trail back to Will, who falls to his knees with his arms at his sides, fists tightly clenched shut. His head drops low, and his shoulders hitch with each deep breath.

Mike breaks into a subtle smile, the headlights from the military vehicle behind him blink almost as fast as his heart rate. He's never been more mesmerized by anything—any*one*—in his life.

Will relaxes his hands, his fists falling open and revealing his dirt-covered palms. Time moves in slow motion as Will lifts his head, gaze set directly on Mike while his eyes roll back to reveal the beautiful hazel that immediately brings Mike home.

A trail of blood drips from Will's nose as he lifts his sleeve to his face, refusing to break eye contact with Mike as he wipes it away.

Mike forgets how to breathe.

"Do you know why I chose them? Why I chose them to reshape the world?"

Vecna's earlier words ring through Will's mind. His body strains with the after effects of using his siphoned powers for the first time.

"It's because they are weak."

Flashes of the children being taken away by the demogorgons play over and over in Will's head, their lifeless bodies dragged carelessly by the monsters as they disappear through the tear between dimensions.

"Easily broken. Easily reshaped. Controlled. The perfect vessels*."*

Is that the whole reason Will had been taken in the first place, too? Vecna just saw him as an easily broken vessel; something he could use to his advantage in any way he wanted. Something *weak* to control. And maybe he was right.

Will couldn't even save the kids.

A light touch on his shoulder snaps him from his thoughts, Will's head shoots up with a hitch of his breath, and he sees the concerned face of his mother staring down at him.

Without hesitation, she wraps her arms around his waist, helping to bring him to his feet. Will pulls her into a hug, ignoring the ache in his body as his arms cross tight around her shoulders.

"Mom." Will sighs, squeezing her tighter. The relief of her standing here with him floods Will's senses. She's real, she's here, she's *alive*.

"Oh, baby," Joyce coos, releasing her hold on him and stepping back to meet his eyes. "I don't understand, how did you…?"

"He's a sorcerer!" *Mike*. "A real life, honest-to-God *sorcerer*!"

Will doesn't have a chance to respond before he's pulled into the tightest, warmest embrace he's ever experienced. Arms wrap securely around his shoulders, Mike's face pressing into the crook of Will's neck. Underneath all of the dirt and grime from the day, Will can still smell the faint but familiar scent of Mike's jasmine shampoo.

"*Mike*," Will gasps, returning the gesture and squeezing his arms around Mike's shoulders. He grips the fabric of Mike's jacket, afraid of ever letting him go.

Seeing Mike standing there helpless, as a monster—from the place Will has so *desperately* tried to forget—was seconds away from ending his life, is something Will wants to permanently erase from his memory. It was all of his worst fears imaginable coming true before his eyes, and he knew he had to act fast.

In that moment, Will thought of Mike; their first time meeting on the swing set in kindergarten, their adventures in the woods together, their first DnD campaign. It all gave Will the sense of calmness, of *love*, that he needed to channel Vecna's powers from the hive mind.

Being connected to Vecna in that way is something Will never would have wished for, but when it comes to protecting Mike, there's not anything he wouldn't do. He'd stretch himself thin in more ways than one to assure his safety, and that's not something that will ever change.

Mike pulls away, his hands falling to Will's sides as Will's stay settled on his shoulders, neither of them wanting to completely break contact. He's looking at Will with an expression that makes Will's heart swell, like he's something to be admired. "You did it, you *really* did it!"

The smile on Will's face falls when he thinks of what almost was. If he had hesitated for even a second, he would have witnessed the person he loves most being brutally torn apart in front of him.

Will would never have survived that.

"I couldn't lose you." Will breathes, shaking his head. "I *can't* lose you."

"You didn't lose me," Mike says. "You saved me, Will. *You saved me*."

Tears begin to pool behind Will's waterline. He tries to blink them away, but feels them slowly break free and trail down his cheeks. He reaches his hand up to wipe them away, but Mike beats him to it, cupping his face with both hands before letting one slide down to the curve of his back.

"Will," Mike whispers. His brown eyes staring back at Will's give him a sense of security, despite everything that just happened. They're both gazing at each other, Will's breath hitching when he sees Mike's eyes travel down toward his mouth. Without a second to process the implications, he suddenly feels the warm press of Mike's lips against his own.

His eyes widen quickly before closing, the softness and warmth of Mike's lips encases him like a heated blanket. He allows his hands to slide down and settle on the dips of Mike's waist, fitting perfectly like they belong there. As he melts into the kiss, he feels relieved, desired, and above all else, he feels *safe*.

"It's okay, Will. *We're* okay," Mike whispers, breaking the kiss and inching back to lean his forehead against Will's. They wrap their arms around each other, pulling one another impossibly closer. This time, Will is the one to bury his face into the crook between Mike's neck and shoulder. The warm body heat radiating from him and the beat of his heart against Will's chest is grounding, a reminder that Mike is still here with him, that he's okay.

Will knows they're eventually going to have to discuss what this means for them, but all that matters now is that they have each other.

They stay wrapped tightly together, just two people afraid of letting go, only breaking apart when the sound of a throat clearing disturbs their moment.

With his hands still against Mike, Will looks over his shoulder to see his mom still standing beside them. Nervousness floods in, but it's quickly washed away when he sees the soft crease of her eyes that show nothing less than happiness and understanding.

"We should go." Joyce smiles subtly, a sense of urgency edged into in her voice.

"The kids," Will says, realization dawning on him as he faces Mike again. "Vecna… he's got the kids."

"I know," Mike responds, sounding remorseful. "Derek, Mary, Glenn…"

"No." Will shakes his head. "Not just them, Mike. The others didn't make it out of Hawkins. I- I could've helped them fight if I had only acted sooner." Will's voice cracks. "But I was too late, and now he's got *all* of them. All twelve."

Will begins to tear up again, his lower lip trembling. "We failed. *I* failed."

"Hey," Mike coos, bringing his palm up and gently rubbing his thumb back and forth below Will's eye. "You did more than anyone could have ever hoped to do. You're the strongest, *bravest* person I've ever met. We'll get them back, Will. I swear to God."

Mike's tone is soft, but firm. He takes Will's hand into his own, giving it a comforting squeeze. Will opens his mouth to respond, but is interrupted by the sound of helicopters in the distance, traveling closer and closer to their location with each passing second.

"The tunnels," Mike says, releasing Will's hand so the two can stand on either side of Joyce, supporting her in spite of her injuries as they make their way out of the Mac-Z toward the tunnels.

Mike and Will share a look over her head, both nodding at each other with mutual understanding that no matter what happens next, they're going to fight to get the kids back.

Together.

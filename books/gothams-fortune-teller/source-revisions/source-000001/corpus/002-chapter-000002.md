Summary

When Robin jumps in front of Batman to protect him from Darkseid’s Omega Beam, he expects death, to see the light and then nothing else. What he doesn’t expect is to wake up in his ten-year-old self. He especially doesn’t expect the power to time travel at will.

Cue shenanigans as he tries to shape the world into his liking, one saved life or relationship at a time. Gotham’s never had a more accurate fortune teller. They’re lucky Tim chooses not to be a supervillain.

In the six years since Darkseid sent him back in time, Tim had never run into Jack and Janet Drake.

Three months after Bruce had gained permanent custody and taken him in, Tim’s parents had been arrested upon arrival back in Gotham. They were so disconnected that they didn’t suspect a thing until Commissioner Gordon was waiting for them as they exited the plane.

Jack and Janet ended up only having to pay a fine for their criminal neglect and child endangerment (stupid corruption), but the damage to their reputation had been done. The two of them refused to spare a modicum of attention on Tim as revenge, instead continuing their

travels and hoping their silent treatment would reflect their disappointment and anger (it wasn’t anything new to Tim).

It is because of this that Tim thought they would never come back into his life. That they would live separate lives from him, the bridges burned.

But a month after his sixteenth birthday, he exits Gotham Academy and finds Jack Drake leaning against a bright red Porsche. Tim pauses, wondering if he can go back inside before he’s noticed. Jason has theater club today, so the two of them are usually picked up later. Tim either stays in the library or goes to the store across the street for a snack while waiting for him to finish.

It is one of the days where Tim is craving a frozen pizza with marshmallow fluff, gummy bears, and hot sauce, so he’s headed out to the store. Seeing his sperm donor with none of the Waynes to back him up has a pit forming in his stomach.

He tries to turn back, but it’s too late. “Timothy!” Jack calls out. Tim sucks in a breath. He hasn’t heard that voice so long, even longer than when he was sent to the past. He had been dead two years at that point.

“Hey, bud, how are you?” he asks, his voice getting closer. Tim’s eyes are stuck on his feet, which are rooted to the ground. He’s unable to escape, but trying to anyway.

Eventually, he sees fancy business shoes step up in his field of vision. He’s frozen, his hands clenched tightly in the straps of his backpack. He has no idea what to do. For the first time, Tim doesn’t feel like the adult he mentally is. He feels his body’s age, just a scared kid.

“Timothy,” Jack repeats, clearing his throat, “do you think your mom and I could talk to you for a moment? It’s important.”

Those are the first words his supposed dad has spoken to him after everything that happened between them. Tim knows they haven’t spoken to him for close to seven years, but Jack acts like that distance had never happened, like he’s just a normal dad who picks up his kid from school everyday.

Tim forces his head up to look over Jack’s shoulder. Janet Drake can be seen in the passenger seat of the Porsche, looking cold and uninterested (her normal resting bitch face), probably expecting Tim to come to her. Neither of his parents seem to acknowledge the pain they put Tim through. Tim snorts internally. They probably think he owes them an apology.

Tim debates screaming right then and there. He can just wait in the library and walk out of the school with Jason, straight to Alfred. He doesn’t even have to acknowledge them. He can force them to wait for him, like they had him do for the majority of his life.

He only hesitates because he’s curious what they want to talk to him about. They haven’t tried to contact him for years, so whatever it is, it must be important. He knows that once they set their mind on something, it’s impossible to set them off course. They’ll just keep coming after him until he hears them out.

“What?” he finally sighs, looking at Jack.

Jack frowns in disapproval, “That’s not very respectful, Timothy. You shouldn’t talk to your parents that way.” he scolds.

“I don’t,” Tim retorts.

Jack sighs, “I know we haven’t reached out to you in a while, Timothy, but that’s really your fault. If you hadn’t reported us, then we would have had the time to talk to you. As it is, Drake Industries isn’t doing very well right now because you couldn’t keep private matters private.”

Wow. Gaslight. Gatekeep. Girlboss.

“Um..I’d think you’re the one that broke the law. I had nothing to do with this. You should have known if you leave a ten year old on his own, people would eventually notice…” Tim reasons dubiously.

“Now, Timothy. We did that because we thought you were a responsible, well behaved little boy, but I can see now that we were wrong,” Jack accuses.

Tim sighs again. “If you’re just going to insult me, I’ll be leaving,” he says, moving to get around him.

“Wait!” Jack grabs his wrist tightly and Tim flinches.

“Just…come with us to a restaurant for a little bit. There’s just one small thing we need to talk about,” Jack pleads.

“Umm, I’m not letting you take me anywhere. You could kidnap me,” Tim deadpans, raising an eyebrow.

“We’re your parents , Timothy!” Jack emphasizes, raising his voice.

“Parents can still kidnap their kids. I’m not in your custody,” Tim replies.

Jack starts dragging Tim to the car, while Tim tries to fight his hand off. “Stop!” Tim yells.

“Timothy, stop being dramatic,” Janet’s voice sounds. She’s exited the car now and is crossing her arms disapprovingly.

“No! I will get a restraining order against you if you even try getting me in that car!” Tim threatens, on the verge of screaming.

Janet holds her hand up to Jack who stops dragging Tim, but doesn’t let go of his wrist.

“Timothy,” she starts, with a false sweetness in her voice, “we want to fight for custody of you again.”

Tim freezes his attempts to get free. “Wh-what do you mean?” he asks shakily.

“Drake Industries’ reputation has collapsed and it is struggling, in part because of your actions. We want you to take responsibility for that and ask to be put back with us. If you show forgiveness and we promise that we’ve changed and will arrange care for you, that would help all of us. You want your inheritance don’t you?” Janet commands more than requests.

This time Tim can’t hold in his snort. “I think I’ll have a bigger inheritance from Bruce Wayne, especially if you’re struggling financially,” he says wryly.

Janet sneers cruelly. “You think you’ll get any of Bruce Wayne’s inheritance? The only one of those brats that will get his inheritance is the biological one. You’re a burden, and I’m sure Brucie Wayne didn’t take you in because he loves you,” she snarls.

Jack guffaws, adding, “He probably wanted you for other reasons right?” He nudges Tim suggestively. It’s horrifying how he can joke about his own son getting sexually abused, but the Drakes were never known for their empathy (or any emotion really).

Tim tries shrinking away from them, but the blows keep coming. “You were okay on your own, right Timothy? We aren’t asking much from you. You just need to give the illusion that you’ve forgiven us and are in our custody again. You can go back to those Waynes when we’re gone, given you keep it secret, not that you’re any good at that,” Janet finishes.

Tim wants to protest that he is in fact very good at keeping secrets. He is keeping a big one from them right now even, but words are caught in his throat.

“C’mon bud, you’re old enough to be emancipated. You can take care of yourself just fine now. We’ll admit you might have been too immature at ten, but you’re practically an adult now!” Jack wheedles.

Tim just says, “I’ll think about it.” He knows he’s not really being given an option, but he makes to escape back into the school now that they’ve said what they needed to.

“Our lawyers will call you!” Jack calls after him.

Tim no longer feels hungry and he curls up in the library, fighting back tears. His time being a part of a loving family is over. He knew it would never last.

Tim has been so conflicted on his altercation with his parents. He had always planned on saving their lives even if he didn’t live with them. No matter how much they hurt him, they’re still his parents and he had grieved for them and what could have been (even if they didn’t deserve it). He can’t just stand by and let them die. Feeling empathy and love can be such a burden sometimes.

He arranges to have Batman and Robin investigate and arrest Obeah Man before he can kidnap Jack and Janet.

With the prevention of his parents and Robin’s deaths, Tim wonders if he should just listen to them. There’s no need for him at Wayne Manor anymore outside of Parakeet. Tim assumes that it’ll go back to how it was like in his past life. He’ll stay in Drake Manor and only go over to Wayne Manor for patrols.

Like Janet had said. He’s just burdening the Waynes, and he doesn’t need their pity. He’s fine just being their next door neighbor. The past few years were more than Tim could have ever hoped for. Jason and Damian regard him like a brother, he gets to patrol with the Bats once again, and he got to live in a house that wasn’t dark or empty.

Janet and Jack’s words start to bring out insecurities that Tim thought were long dormant. Maybe Bruce had only adopted him in the previous timeline because he felt responsible for Tim as Robin after his parents died. Bruce is also too responsible to have let ten-year-old Tim keep living alone. Jason’s refusal to be separated from him could have forced Bruce to foster him. Like his sperm donor mentioned, he’s now old enough to be emancipated, so Bruce should no longer feel this obligation.

Tim can still perform his duties as Parakeet and look after the Waynes from afar. He’ll make sure nothing happens to them. He’s starting to think he’s just better off going along with his parents’ plans.

With the amount of predications Tim makes daily on his Twitter account, it’s hard to believe that he couldn’t have predicted this.

Staring wide-eyed at his foster father and siblings as they lounge in a sitting room and look at him expectantly, he can only gasp out a “W-what?”

He glances down at the papers on the coffee table in front of him and can’t seem to connect to the lightbulb in his brain.

“Baby bird, we want you to be a part of the family in every way that counts. I kept doubting my place as B’s son when I was just his ward, and this is one of the ways he was able to cement that he loves me and wants to be my dad,” Dick explains, “I thought you would need the reassurance too.”

“But..my parents are still alive. I’m not an orphan,” Tim states confusedly.

Jason scoffs. “Like those assholes deserve to be called parents. You were the one that told me it wasn’t my biological parents that I reflect, but the people who chose to be my parents. You won’t need to deal with them ever again,” he says pointedly.

“Tim..” Bruce approaches tentatively, “I know that your parents have started contacting you again.”

Tim gasps, still in a state of shock, “What, how??”

“Your twitter posts got depressing, so I was concerned and went to B. We saw that their lawyers were calling you. They couldn’t even be bothered to call you themselves!” Jason explains, though his face grows more heated as he speaks.

Bruce continues the conversation, “Further, less legal, approaches showed that they’re trying to sue for custody back. I wanted to nip that in the bud. You’re my son, and I don’t think they’ll be able to care for you like I am. I..love you, Tim. Please let me adopt you?”

Tim exhales gustily, “Wouldn’t it just be easier for them to just get custody? They won’t be home very often so I could still stay with you guys most of the time and it won’t affect patrol. I don’t want to cause you trouble with a legal battle,” he questions, nervously.

“Tt. You’re worth it!” Damian speaks up finally. Tim doesn’t think he’s seen the baby bat this quiet or still. “I want you to be my brother legally too so no one can ever take you away!” he continues obstinately.

The nine-year-old is still as attached to Tim as ever. Tim’s kind of getting concerned it’s edging towards possessiveness, but honestly it’s nice to be coveted. Damian seems as spoiled as he was in his previous life, but it’s softened by his genuine care and love. He wants the best for both himself and everyone he loves.

“I have already worked out a custody agreement with your parents, Tim. As I am your guardian, they had no right to go to school and accost you like that. I can get a restraining order against them if that is what you want,” Bruce reveals.

Tim turns to face him surprised. “They’re okay with you adopting me?” he asks.

“Not necessarily okay with it, but more like they didn’t have a choice unless they wanted to be arrested for the artifacts they have stolen on their trips,” Bruce corrects.

“Yeah! They have a lot of shit they aren’t supposed to have. B threatened to expose them so they folded. Fuckers care more about their reputation than you, which is good for us because it’s their loss!” Jason rants.

“B just sent Selina a list of the artifacts so I don’t think they’ll be in custody of them for much longer,” Dick giggles, “you better sign the papers before they realize they’ve been had.”

Tim numbly picks up a pen, still processing his entire viewpoint being decimated.

A soft hand covers his own for a second, and he looks up into Bruce’s soft eyes. “We all love you Tim, but don’t feel forced to do this. What is it you really want? All we want is for you to be happy,” he expresses.

“I-” Tim licks his dry lips, “I want to be adopted,” he replies shakily, and a cheer erupts from his family.

Once Tim signs all the papers, Dick tackles them all into a group hug. Tim can’t help the bright smile that crosses his face. The doom he had been feeling for the past few days fades into oblivion, never to be seen again. He’s legally and permanently Bruce’s kid. Nothing can take him away from the Waynes anymore. In fact he is officially a Wayne now. Timothy Jackson Drake-Wayne.

God he never thought he would have this again when he changed the timeline. In fact, it’s even better now because he knows for sure it’s because he belongs and is wanted and not out of a sense of obligation.

“I’m finally cashing in that favor you owe me, baby bird! Daily hugs for the rest of your life! It doesn’t matter who it is, but I will be checking,” Dick announces.

Jason bursts out laughing while Tim groans. Jason had already had his favor called in when Bludhaven stopped selling Dick’s favorite cereal. Dick asked him to drive from Gotham to Bludhaven to drop off bags of that cereal until they started selling it again. Tim had thankfully not been allowed to drive at that time so he thought he had escaped. Unfortunately, that doesn’t seem to be the case.

“Why are your favors so much longer than what we asked of you? Our favor lasted just for the weekend!” Tim complains.

“That’s what you get for not setting limits, Timmy! Your favor took years off my life,” Dick responds sweetly.

“What favor are you speaking of?” Damian chimes in curiously.

Bruce just sighs.

Tim sinks into the group hug even more. He thinks back to the promise he had given Jason three years ago. Jason had been to see Dinah a few times, but Tim hadn’t found the courage to go. Maybe he should go talk to her and see what she says about his abandonment and self esteem issues. She can’t make them worse right?

Resolving to talk to Bruce about it later, Tim breaks away from the group and wipes his teary eyes. “I love you all too,” he chokes out. They all smile at him, and Dick is the only one not trying to hide that he’s crying too. Alfred enters with some comfort snacks, and smiles warmly at Tim when he sees the signed papers.

Without the fear of having to leave hanging over him, Tim finally feels completely at home.

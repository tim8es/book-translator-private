When they enter, they are greeted by Alfred whose eyebrows shoot up high on his forehead, the only evidence of his surprise.

“Young Masters. Who is our young guest?” he inquires.

“Hey Alfie!” Jason chirps, “This is Damian, B’s son!”

Alfred, who is the only one of the group to have known Bruce as a child and is never fazed, inspects Damian closely and nods, believing them right away. He looks pleased, and Tim exhales a sigh of relief. One down, one more to go (arguably the hardest one).

Damian is looking back at Alfred curiously from the safety of Tim’s arms, seemingly fascinated by his mustache. Alfred leads the group down to the Batcave where Bruce is hunched over the Batcomputer.

“Master Bruce,” Alfred calls, “Your sons are home. All four of them.”

Bruce turns around and waves blearily at them, his sleep deprivation causing a delay in processing. He turns back around, but then jolts when Alfred’s statement gets through to him.

He spins around, almost falling off his chair, suddenly alert. He scans the group until his eyes rest on Damian who is looking at him in awe. Looks like Damian will always look up to Bruce no matter the timeline.

Bruce stares for a long moment then sighs pinching his nose. “I was expecting an April Fools joke, but you have really gone above and beyond this year. Please send him back to his family,” he requests tiredly.

Wait, what?

Tim checks his phone for the date and trades a wide-eyed look with Jason. They both hadn’t realized it was April 1st. No wonder Dick believed the prank excuse so easily.

“It’s not an April Fools joke!” Dick says, “I thought it was too, but they’re being serious.”

“It’s just bad timing,” Tim hurries to explain, “We didn’t realize the date, but Damian is your son.”

“Nice try,” Bruce responds sardonically.

Tim groans. “Do a DNA test and you’ll believe us. He’s your kid with Talia al Ghul! This was the important thing in Nanda Parbat I was telling you about!” he insists.

Bruce sighs placatingly and pulls out a swab. “Alright, I’ll humor you,” he says.

Damian fights the swab at first, but submits to it reluctantly (and furiously) when Tim does it.

When the test result comes out, Bruce stares at it, stunned. He glares at Tim, “Did you hack the Batcomputer for this prank?” he accuses.

“ No! ” Tim replies, offended (okay that is something he would totally do).

“Denial isn’t just a river in Egypt, old man. You better believe it,” Jason interjects, annoyed.

“If I may, Master Bruce, the youngest master looks just like you as a toddler,” Alfred informs him.

Bruce examines Damian in a new light and tentatively brushes his fingers lightly against the three-year-old’s soft cheek. Damian yawns, eyelids drooping. He snuggles against Tim’s shoulder and everyone ’aww’s at the sweet sight. “Fuck,” Damian mumbles sleepily.

“ Jason! ” Dick whisper-shrieks. Jason groans and glares at Damian mutinously. “Really, Demon? Throwing me under the bus? You really don’t like me, and you know what? I don’t like you!” Jason whines.

“Shh” Tim hisses, not wanting to disturb the toddler. Alfred looks on disapprovingly while Bruce just pinches his nose again. Jason meekly (for him at least) apologises after that.

Alfred sets up a nursery at light speed (how he found a crib and rocking chair so fast, Tim doesn’t know), and Tim lays Damian down to sleep. The family spends the rest of the day recounting Tim and Jason’s adventure and getting the manor ready for Damian. The boys get plenty of scolding from all the adults for going off on their own as an eleven and thirteenyear-old. They’re grounded from patrol for a month and Dick says they owe him a favor for putting him through all that stress, but Tim thinks it’s worth it.

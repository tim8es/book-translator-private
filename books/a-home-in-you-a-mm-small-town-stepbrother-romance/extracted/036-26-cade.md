# 26. Cade

Finally, I was away from my fucking dad. It was like I could breathe fresh oxygen from the countryside after living in a polluted city. The University of Virginia wasn’t that far from home, about two hours away, but far enough away that I wouldn’t have to stay at home and could live in the dorms. Freedom was as sweet as any favorite dessert.

After my state championship win, I had more offers from other universities, but while I hated Dillon, I couldn’t go to another school. The tiny little thought that niggled in my mind wanted him to find me. Not to reconnect with him because fuck that. I wasn’t about to go through all that pain again. But to show him how I’d moved on. How I was just fine without his protective bullshit lies.

If he was here, he hadn’t bothered to find me, but I hadn’t exactly looked either. I’d been here for two weeks now with no sign of Dillon. Then again, there were twenty-five thousand students attending UVA. Maybe he went to another school because he was too cowardly to face me. Deep down, I knew that was bullshit. Dillon wasn’t afraid of anything.

I sat on my bed, reading my anatomy textbook, and making notes when my roommate walked in. He was a smart as fuck black dude, and pretty cool. I could have ended up with a real dickhead, so I got lucky.

He wasn’t much bigger than me with a shaved head and came here on an academic scholarship, being at the top of his class when he graduated high school. He was getting a BS in biology, while I was majoring in kinesiology, so I could be a physiotherapist to help athletes.

“What’s up, Cade?”

“Hey, Darius.”

“You studying? It’s Friday night, bro.”

“Yep. What else am I gonna do?”

“Uh, party. What else?”

I looked over at him. He worked as hard as I did, at least so far. Mine wasn’t so much to get good grades as it was a distraction, plus I had to keep good grades if I wanted to keep my scholarship. That, and Dad threatened to take away his tuition payments on a daily basis. Fucking control freak.

“I’m not really the partying type.”

“Dude, you’re in college now. We’re all officially the partying type.”

I blew out a laugh. “Is that a fact?”

“Yep. I’m pretty sure it’s in the student handbook.” He sat at his desk and scrolled through his phone. “There’s a frat party tonight. It’s gonna be dank, man. There could be a cute boy or three over there.”

I made a promise to myself that once I started college, I wouldn’t hide my sexuality. I was going to be open to anyone who asked, so I told my roommate the first day we moved into the dorms. But I never told him about Dillon. I didn’t want to talk about him because it still fucking hurt.

“I’m not really interested in dating right now.”

“Who says anything about dating? I’m not. Just give me a hot chick to wiggle under me for the night, and I’m good.”

“Oh? And if I brought a guy here, you wouldn’t care if we were banging in front of you?”

“Naw, dude. None of that shit bothers me.”

I’d thought about it. Meeting some guy to date or fuck. But neither appealed to me in the slightest. If I were being honest, it was because no one would ever match Dillon with his tenderness, protectiveness, love, and… loyalty. Until he wasn’t. No, even if I hated him now, I’d always compare some dude to him. I also didn’t want to get attached to anyone. Every time I did, it ended in disaster. Everyone always left me.

But maybe just a one-night stand wouldn’t be so bad. Shit, maybe it would. I’d only had sex once, so I wasn’t exactly experienced. But I did have a lot of practice in other things. Maybe.

“Fine, I guess I’ll come.”

“Yes!”

“Can I bring Raven?”

“The more, the merrier. Besides, she’s cute. I’ve been itching to ask her out.”

“If you say so.”

\* \* \*

We each had to pay to get in, but apparently it was worth it. The party was massive. I’d never seen so many kids in one place before. It was packed, and the living room was cleared out and turned into a dance floor, with bodies packed together and moving in unison to the beat. The air was full of booze, weed, and cheap perfume.

It was hot inside with all bodies as Raven, Darius, and I made our way toward the backyard where the alcohol was kept. I didn’t really drink, but since we walked here, I planned on getting shit-faced for the first time. It was the college experience, right? Maybe I could obliterate my pain for a while.

“Whoa, this party is epic!” Raven yelled over the music.

“You gonna save me a dance, Rave?” Darius asked.

“Anything for you, cutie.”

“Gross. I think I threw up a little in my mouth. It’s almost like you’re hitting on my sister.”

Darius laughed and led us to the back.

It wasn’t as loud outside, but it was still packed with people. We each got a beer, and I chugged mine, then poured another.

By our fourth beer, we were all feeling warm and toasty. At least I was. The three of us wandered to the dance floor and shoved our way inside the throng of bodies. Raven and Darius gyrated against each other, and I rolled my eyes at them. Whatever. If they hit it off, that was cool, as long as he didn’t fucking hurt her.

The room was spinning as I twisted and danced, letting the music take me away from all my hurt. A girl suddenly came up to me, gyrated against me, and wrapped her arms around my neck. I removed her hands, and turned my back on her, only to find another girl trying the same thing. Not bothering to tell them I was gay, I left the dance floor before someone grabbed my arm. I bit back a remark to leave me the fuck alone when I saw it was a guy. A really cute guy. His hair was a wavy dark brown, and a little longer than mine, with dark skin. He was shorter and thinner than me, with eyes I couldn’t see because of the darkened room with flashing colored lights. His clothing was more on the feminine side with a lacy, see-through, black top with skin-tight pants.

He leaned in and stood on his toes to reach my ear. “Dance?”

I chugged my beer and shrugged. I was feeling the alcohol as he pressed up against me and wrapped his arms around my neck as the girl did earlier. When I looked up, I saw her standing there annoyed, so I shrugged. It wasn’t my fault I didn’t like girls that way.

We swayed to the music, and he yelled in my ear that his name was Marco.

“Cade.”

“You’re really sexy.”

“Thanks.”

“Love your tats.”

“Thanks.”

“Those dimples are adorable, too.”

Marco was beautiful himself, but I couldn’t bring myself to compliment him. It felt like a betrayal somehow. Stupid. I was single and had every right to dance with a guy, compliment a guy, or fuck him if I wanted to.

But as we danced, my mood soured again, drifting off to Dillon. I swear to fuck, he owned my brain. There wasn’t a day that went by that I hadn’t thought about him, despite being separated for a year and a half.

Because of our taboo relationship, Dillon and I had never been allowed to dance like this in public. So there were no school dances, no kisses in public, no coming out to people, and no giving a shit what others thought. People wouldn’t have accepted us. Especially not the country folk.

Dancing with Marco was a reminder of all that I couldn’t have with Dillon.

Freedom.

Freedom with him. Freedom without him. Freedom from him. I was in a fucking Dillon prison.

The longer I danced, the more agitated I grew. My dancing partner must have sensed it because he grabbed my hand and dragged me away towards the kitchen where they were serving some sort of shots. He grabbed two, handing me one. We clinked cups and swallowed them down. They were fruity and gross, but I didn’t give a shit. Then we did another. By the third, I was pretty woozy.

Marco grabbed my hand again and with blurring eyes, I looked around for my friends as we walked somewhere, but I couldn’t find them. We headed up a flight of stairs, and he dragged me to a quiet corner of the hall. I leaned against the wall to keep from falling and took several breaths to stop the fucking spinning while Marco ran his hands up my chest.

“So strong.”

My heart beat faster with my drunkenness quickly forgotten. Then his hand threaded through my curly waves that had grown out. I closed my eyes to visions of Dillon taking care of me, running fingers through my hair as I rested my head on his lap. The ache stabbed me in the heart and I rubbed it where I had my first tattoo.

Marco stood taller on his toes as he got closer and leaned in towards my lips. Someone else was going to kiss me for the first time. Someone who wasn’t Dillon. Dillon, who had been my first kiss, first touch, first blow job, and first time having sex. We shared every sexual experience two people possibly could, and all those memories were about to be obliterated by another dude trying to kiss me.

I couldn’t. I couldn’t do it. God, and I fucking wanted to. Anything to break out of my Dillon prison.

My goddamn motherfucking eyes leaked because I couldn’t get my emotional shit together, and I eased Marco back.

“I… I can’t.”

I tried to walk away, but he grabbed me again. “Please just let me go. I’m sorry, okay? I… didn’t mean to lead you on.” My words were slurred, so I hoped he understood what I was trying to say.

Instead, he took my hand again and led me back downstairs, and I let him. Going down sure was a lot harder than going up. I slipped a couple of times on the steps, but I made it without falling on my ass.

“Let’s get you home, Cutie Cade. Where do you live?”

I pointed toward the general vicinity of the residential towers where my dorm was.

Marco never let go of my hand as we walked, but I hoped he wouldn’t try anything again.

“What’s going on, Cutie Cade? You were in the moment, and suddenly you were upset? Bad break up?”

I blew out a resigned sigh. “The worst break up.”

“Was it really that bad?”

“Yes.”

“Come on. I know of a place where we can get some coffee. Sorry I dumped so many shots down you. Had I known you were such a lightweight, I would’ve given you only one.” He winked at me as we strolled towards the coffee shop, still holding my hand in public like we were lovers. Like I was never allowed to with Dillon.

*Shut. Up.*

We ordered some strong coffee and sat outside at a table. Maybe it was all the drinks, but I ended up pouring my pathetic heart out to him. I told him every excruciating detail, and he just sat there and listened unless he had a question.

“Okay. You win. That’s a fucked up breakup, if you can even call it that. It’s more like you were yanked away from him.”

I rubbed my temples and nodded.

“Well, I’m definitely no rebound boy, but I would like to be friends if you want.”

I smiled at him. “I’d really like that. Thanks for listening to me drunkenly ramble.”

“You’re welcome.”

He walked me back to my dorm and gave me his number and a hug. “Call me anytime. And when you get to your room, drink lots of water and take some pain reliever.”

When I got to my room, I took a bottle of water from my mini-fridge and drank all of it. Then I crawled my way to my bed, and the beauty of the alcohol let me pass out before I could dwell anymore on Dillon.

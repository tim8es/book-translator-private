Mom gathered me in her arms and let me cry on her. “I’m so sorry, honey. I’m so sorry.”

“I love him so much,” I sobbed, gripping the back of her shirt in tight fists.

“I know,” she said. “Come sit in the living room. Let me make us some coffee, then we need to talk.”

She helped me stand and guided me to the sofa, then she handed me a box of tissues. After I blew my nose, she left to make coffee, and I curled up on the couch and softly cried. Tilly curled up behind my legs and rested her head on my hip, whining. I reached for her head and petted it.

Anyway, I tried but Jared kept putting me off, and he eventually told me no, that it wasn’t necessary. He never did give me a good reason. I kept pushing until I saw you boys in the loft. If you two wanted your relationship to survive this very difficult situation you were in, you didn’t need Cade as your legal brother, my adopted son, so I let it go.”

I shook my head. “I don’t think so, I… I think I’m a demisexual. Someone who needs a strong connection to feel… sexually attracted to them. Her smile was small as she continued the soothing strokes through my hair. “You came to me that summer, wondering about you being gay. I thought maybe you’d finally come to me and trust me with Cade, but you never did.”

“I trusted you with the sexuality stuff. But Cade and I are stepbrothers. I wasn’t sure how anyone would take it. Only Darren knows and only because he caught us kissing, but he’s been strangely supportive.”

I took a big sip of my coffee, set it down on the table, and ran my shaking hands through my hair.

“You didn’t fail, honey. There’s nothing you can do right now. I believe Jared’s threats. Let’s see if he’ll calm down after his initial shock and listen to us rationally. But I have no legal claim to Cade. Even if I had adopted him and Jared divorced me, he’d still end up with Cade.”

“He hit him, mom. Can’t we use that against him?”

She shook her head. “No, even if we could prove it, and they took Cade away, he’d be sent to his grandmother’s, not me. Hopefully, Jared will calm down. But I promise you, if I find out there’s been any more beating on Cade, I will call the police. He’d be better off with his grandmother at that point. But I think Jared was just reacting. I never pegged him as a physical abuser. Regardless, if we can’t do anything else, perhaps when Cade turns eighteen, he can make his own decisions.”

“But that’s like a year away!”

“I understand, honey, but I’m not sure what else I can do.”

“I don’t know what to do without him for that long. We’ve spent every waking moment together since he moved in. He’s my everything.”

God, the tears fucking came again. I couldn’t stop.

Mom pulled me to her and laid my head in her lap, just like I did for Cade so many times. She was the person I learned it from. Her fingers combed through my hair as I sobbed again.

“We’ll try to find a way, baby. I’m so, so sorry you’re hurting.”

“I’ll see him in school tomorrow. So maybe we can talk then, right?”

“Yes, I’m sure he’ll be at school. Though, I’m not sure that he’ll be there tomorrow. It’s been a rough day. In fact, if you need to stay home, you can.”

“No, I need to be there just in case.”

God, if I could see him and talk to him. Maybe we could run away or something. I’d take him far away from his dad. Shit, but it wasn’t like I had a job or money. I only helped out Mom at the store.

“I’m sorry about Jared. That you’re going to get a divorce now because of this.”

“Don’t you worry about it. This was inevitable. We hadn’t been getting along very well since last Christmas. Neither of us was happy.”

“Still.”

“He’s a homophobic piece of shit. I’m not going to miss him. But I will miss Cade. He’s such a good boy.”

“Yeah.”

I must have fallen asleep on the couch because Mom shook me awake. “Dillon, honey. I need you to go to your room. Now. Jared is here to get his and Cade’s things.”

That woke me up. I rushed upstairs to Cade’s room with Tilly never leaving my side and searched for the two pictures I gave to him. I found them in his desk drawer and pulled them out. Fuck if his dad was going to ruin them. I’d give them back when we met up again. When we could have a life together where we didn’t have to worry about anyone else.

There was no more time to look some more for anything else. I grabbed his pillow on the way out of his room and rushed back to my room. I shut my door and locked it behind me, and slid to the floor, clutching his pillow close to me and pressing my nose into it to inhale my Cade. My eyes leaked again as I heard yelling downstairs.

“Jared, please. Let’s be rational about this.”

“You call letting brothers fuck rational? Are you out of your mind?”

“They aren’t related. There’s no shared blood between them.”

“Oh, that makes it *so* much better! My son will not be fucking his brother or any other boy. Ever!”

There was stomping up the stairs, and the floorboards creaked under his feet as he headed to Cade’s room.

The thought of Cade gone, never seeing him every day, kissing him every day, telling him I loved him every day killed me. To find his room empty of his presence was going to destroy me.

“Jared, please don’t separate the boys. They need each other. I beg you.”

“I’m going to pack our things, and you’re going to stay away from me and my boy. If I see Dillon anywhere near him, I’m going to have him arrested. Starting tomorrow, I’m taking Cade out of school and putting him in another high school. Tell your faggot son to stay away from us.”

No, he couldn’t also take Cade out of our school. No. I couldn’t be prevented from ever seeing him ever again. Panic seized me and I struggled to breathe as I scrambled to stand. I unlocked my door and rushed to find Mom in tears.

“Don’t you dare call Dillon a—”

“No, please don’t take him, sir. I beg you.”

I stood my ground when he charged at me, but he didn’t dare touch me. If he did, I’d beat the shit out of him, and I’d enjoy it.

“He’s gone. As I told your mother, if I see you anywhere near him, I’m going to have you arrested for rape. Now, I saw his phone in your room this morning. Where is it?”

“You can’t have it.”

“Dillon, give him the phone. Let him get everything and get out of our lives. Let’s focus on what we talked about this morning.”

I walked over to my dresser and grabbed Cade’s phone sitting on top and handed it to him. I supposed if Cade had his phone, I could text him now and again if we were careful. But Jared instantly crushed that idea. Literally. He dropped it on the floor and smashed it under his cowboy boots.

“No!”

“If you think I’m gonna let you reach out to my son, you’re crazy.”

“I hate you!”

“I hate you back, you little punk!”

“Enough! Gather your shit and get the fuck out, Jared! Now! Stop talking to my son, or so help me, I’ll have you arrested myself for trespassing.”

He stormed off and started dumping Cade’s items into boxes he brought with him, and I stormed off to my room, slamming the door behind me.

I punched the door and screamed. “Fuck!”

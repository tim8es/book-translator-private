# 24. Dillon

School had been hell. The end of junior year, especially so. Once Cade was taken away and word got around of Mom’s divorce from his dad, the rumors began, thanks in part to Darcy and David’s big fucking mouths, both of whom were dating now. They spread around that Cade was forced to leave because I was fucking my brother. It wasn’t a lie, but no one was supposed to know that. They didn’t even know for sure, but that didn’t stop them from spreading the rumors. And every single fucking time I denied it felt like I was betraying Cade somehow. Like every lie pushed him further and further away from me.

To make matters worse, they’d been spreading the word that I was gay, too. So, while they didn’t care that Darren was with a guy, only because they were afraid of him, they ostracized me. Then the bullying started. It took all my self-control not to beat the fuck out of anyone who bad-mouthed Cade. The only ones who stood by me were Darren and his boyfriend Christian.

Did Cade have new friends? I hoped so. I hoped he wasn’t alone and no one was hurting him. Life was hard for me, but I knew Cade had it so much worse on top of his already struggling insecurity issues.

Once junior year ended, I worked at Mom’s shop full-time to keep myself busy. When I wasn’t working, I was creating art or hanging out with the animals. I didn’t go anywhere else unless Darren and Christian wanted to hang out, but even then, I shut myself out most of the time. Then, once a week, I’d get desperate bouts to find Cade, and I’d drive around to see if I could just stumble onto him. But Loudoun County had four hundred thousand people. People moved further and further out as they commuted to Washington, D.C. where it was more affordable.

I worried Cade felt I’d forgotten him. But I hadn’t. I’d been looking for him, though I had no idea where to start other than his high school, which I wasn’t allowed to set foot on.

When senior year began, apparently the rumors spread further over the summer and my teammates started to harass me and not listen to my orders despite me being the team captain. It was Darren who took charge of that shitshow with his threats of losing because we weren’t united until they finally backed off. Then I made it clear to them that there was nothing weird going on between Cade and me, and to fuck off. It was nothing more than a stupid rumor. More betrayal. Maybe it would’ve been easier just to admit the truth. At least the practices eventually went more smoothly thanks to Darren.

Then came the day we were to play against Heritage, I was pumped. At last, a reprieve from my pain. Cade went to school there, and I had my chance to finally see him. It had been fucking months and months since we separated, and I was goddamn losing my mind. I was irritable and angry, and Mom was getting frustrated with me despite her infinite patience.

And just when I thought I’d finally get to talk to him, I was called into the coach’s office.

“Have a seat, Dillon.”

“What’s going on, Coach?”

“We’ve been in a meeting with the Principal and Vice Principal over at Heritage. They stated that you’ve been warned away from their school due to bullying and stalking of another student who was forced to leave this high school. Is this true?”

“What? No. You know me, Coach. I’d never bully anyone. It’s a lie, and I know who it is. It’s my stepbrother. His father divorced my mom, which wasn’t amicable, and he took it out on us. He took Cade away and put him in another school. It’s all lies. I swear to you. Besides, even if I did, wouldn’t this school have informed you about it already when Cade left?”

“You’re right. I believe you. You haven’t steered me wrong, and you’ve always been a respectable young man and an excellent quarterback, but I just wanted to get that aired out. Regardless, when we go, you’ve been ordered to stay away from him. Do not even look at him if he’s there. Is that understood? You go in, play the game, and leave.”

My heart sank, and I wanted to rip it out. If Cade saw me there without even trying to reach out to him, he'd assume I gave up on us.

*Fuck, fuck, fuck.*

“But… why?”

The coach spread his hands. “They don’t care if it’s true or not. They just want to protect their students. If you reach out to him, you’ll be forcibly dragged out of their school, and I’ll have no choice but to kick you off the team.”

“I understand, Coach.” But I fucking didn’t. Goddamn Jared.

So I played and tried to focus on winning and not losing my ever-fucking-loving mind. It was like I could feel him there, watching, waiting, expecting. But there was nothing I could do. Again. My hands were constantly tied behind my back as I moved through life. One step forward meant five steps back.

We won the game—barely—but it no longer mattered to me. There was no love for the game anymore.

\* \* \*

They say time heals all wounds, but my depression only grew worse. My wounds festered. My strong bond with Cade never wavered despite it being completely severed. There was no one else for me. Only Cade. He was made to be mine. I was made to be his. No amount of time would ever change that. No matter how many girls threw themselves at me. No matter all the rumors that I was gay floated about. I didn’t care. Fuck it all. Without Cade, I was nothing. He literally owned half of my heart and soul. Those things just didn’t grow back on their own.

It was lunchtime, and I sat outside on the picnic bench alone on a freezing winter day. I wasn’t hungry, and I wasn’t in the mood to hang out with anyone, desperate for some silence away from noisy teens. I was back to my old introverted ways.

My notebook was open, but I was only doodling instead of studying, lost in thoughts about Cade. It was something I’d doodled often, and I used to draw it in Cade’s notebooks. I’d perfected it over time with the intention of turning it into a tattoo for both of us. It was simple, really. A continuous linear contour drawing of us kissing.

Would I ever feel whole or normal again without Cade? What if we never met up again? What if he resented me and never forgave me? I made a promise to him I couldn’t keep. My need to protect him and take care of him drove me insane because I could no longer do those things.

“It’s colder than a witch’s tit out here,” groused Darren.

“Expert are you?”

“What the fuck are you doing out here?”

“Go back inside, Dare. I need to be alone.”

“Yeah, not happening. I’m here because I’m fucking sick of you pouting.”

Fury filled me. I slammed my pen and notebook on the table. “Pouting?”

Darren being Darren didn’t even flinch at my wrath. “Yeah. Pouting.”

“I’m not being some fucking toddler, asshole. This is serious!”

Darren was so big that when he sat on the edge of the bench, I nearly went flying. “Jesus. Easy, you big oaf.”

He suddenly grabbed my face with his cold hands and made me look at him. “Enough is enough, Dil. I know you’re hurting, and it may never go away, but you’re not thinking clearly.”

“The hell I’m not. I’m surviving, playing football, winning, and getting good grades. And for what? What’s the point of it all?”

“Cade is the point of it all.”

“Do you see him here? Did he suddenly show up? Because I can’t see him anywhere! He owns half of me, and it’s fucking killing me!”

“What you’re going to do is keep up with the good grades, go to practice, win games, take us to the state championship and win it. Then we’re both going to get scholarships to the University of Virginia and find your boy.”

And there went the tears. Thank fuck, no one else was out here. “I’m trying so hard,” I said.

“I know you are.”

“He probably thinks I hate him or forgot about him.”

“Never.”

“What if he doesn’t go to UVA?”

“Then you’re going to find out where the fuck he went and go there. You’re going to do whatever it takes, right?”

I nodded and sniffed.

“But I need you focused and to stay on course. You *will *find Cade, and he *will* be back in your life.”

“I’m not whole, Dare.”

He put a meaty paw on my shoulder and gripped it. “You will be. We’ll find him together, okay?”

I nodded and wiped my nose with the back of my hand.

“What’s that?” he asked, looking down at my notebook.

“It’s something I doodle sometimes. It’s Cade and me kissing. I’d planned on both of us getting it tattooed.”

“Why don’t you?”

“Now?”

“Yeah, why not?”

“I was going to wait for Cade.”

“He can still get one. I think you should go do it. It’ll make you feel better. I’m sure your mom will allow it.”

He had a point. Cade could still get one later, and maybe it would make me feel better, though I doubted it. I wouldn't feel normal again until I had Cade back in my life.

Darren bumped my shoulder. “I’ll go with you and get one too. How’s that sound?”

“Okay.”

\* \* \*

The following weekend, Mom took time off from work and came with Darren and me to the tattoo shop. She signed off her consent for me to get ink, and we waited for my turn as I fiddled with the diamond studs in my hands. When I mentioned I wanted my ears pierced too, she dragged me to her room and pulled out the studs, telling me Dad bought them for her right after they were married and before I was born. I wished I remembered him more, but he died when I was too young.

“Thanks for letting me get a tat, Mom.”

“Ms. C is pretty cool,” Darren said.

“You’re welcome. I know it’s important to you. Are you getting one too, Darren?”

“Yup. I was going to get Christian’s name on my forehead spelled backward, but Dillon talked me out of it.”

My mom stared at him, blinked a couple of times, unable to speak, then she burst out in a laugh. “Good thing you have Dillon as a friend, then.”

He laughed too. “Nah, I’m getting the school letters on my arm. Not… high school because gross. But UVA. That’s going to be our school. My boy is going, too. College is my ticket out of here. Plus, I want to get numbnuts over there back with Cade, so he can quit moping.”

“That’s nice you all will have friends there,” Mom said, while I flicked him off, making him laugh again.

It would be great as long as Cade showed.

“Dillon?” A man called out.

I stood and handed him the drawing I wanted to be done. After going through the process and stenciling my upper right shoulder, he started. It hurt a little, but it wasn’t very big. Last went the earrings.

When I finished an hour later, I came out and Mom lifted my hair off my ears to look at the studs. “They look better on you than on me.” Then she looked at my new tat. “I love it and so will Cade.”

Two hours later, Darren finally came out grumbling. “That fucking hurt, man. Who knew getting color would be such a bitch.”

“Let me see, you big baby,” I said, lifting his shirt sleeve over his shoulder. The UVA letters were in orange and surrounded by dark blue lines, and fucking huge.

“Dude, most people stencil these on with face paint at tailgate parties. No wonder it hurt.”

“I’m not getting any more tattoos,” he groused.

The tattoo artist laughed. “Famous last words.”

# 8. Cade

Dillon chewed on a piece of hay, leaning against a tree trunk, sketching on his pad, looking cute as hell, while I tried to catch some fish. His thick black brows were furrowed, lost in concentration. He wore an old and worn gray tank top, showing off his growing muscles, and the summer sun had turned his pale skin lightly bronzed. God, why did he have to look so amazing all the fucking time?

Tilly sat next to him, panting but looking content with her eyes half closed.

In the past five months, Dillon grew three inches and was now taller than his mom, making him five feet ten inches, and four inches taller than me. He’d also broadened this summer. Part of me was envious, and the other part couldn’t stop looking at him. I’d grown two inches, but still, I was stupid short. Evelyn told me I was probably a late bloomer. She said Dillon had grown fast and hit all his milestones early when he was little.

I glanced at him one more time, and our eyes met. He looked over the top of his sketchpad, and his blue eyes turned paler from the bright sun. Lately, it had been harder and harder not to stare at him, but I turned away to focus on my fishing.

The day was hot, and the air was still, and slightly suffocating from the humidity. Dragonflies skidded across the smooth surface of the water, while the cicadas sounded like they were screaming their chorus through the trees and thick grass. Even the fish knew not to come too close to the surface of the water because they hadn’t been biting at all.

“What’cha drawing?” I asked.

“You.”

I looked at him again, wide-eyed. “Me? I thought you’d be drawing a horse again or the cows.”

“I’m drawing you fishing. Or trying to, anyway. I don’t do people often.”

Dillon was amazing at drawing, and I liked his animals. I mean, I couldn’t draw worth a shit, but I was pretty good at playing music. I knew that because I took a guitar class the past school year, although, Dad wouldn’t get me any musical instruments to practice with because he needed the quiet after driving for days. Maybe I could take it again this year in high school.

“Can I see it?”

“Not yet. Just stand there and fish.”

“Ugh, but it’s fucking hot and nothing’s biting.”

“Just give me ten more minutes, then we can jump in the water to cool off.”

“Fine.”

Secretly, I didn’t mind him watching me. Still, nothing bit as I stood there, suddenly feeling stupid. Posing was different from not knowing he was drawing me while fishing. Now it was all I could think about.

“Almost done,” he said.

Suddenly, something finally nibbled my bait. Once the fish tried to take off with it, I yanked my pole back to hook it properly and reeled the fish in. I pulled out the fish and looked at the scraggly thing hanging there, trying to wiggle away. What a tiny and pathetic thing. How sad. Ugh. So much for fishing.

I pulled the pliers out of the small tackle box and pried out the hook, then eased him back into the water, not even worth keeping.

“At least you caught something,” he said with humor in his voice, and when I looked at him, he kept his eyes on his sketch pad, but he wore a crooked smile.

“Don’t tell anyone how small it really was. And you better have fucking drawn me hauling out Jaws instead.”

He laughed and turned his sketchbook towards me. Wow, the drawing was pretty good. He got the right shape and stuff. He only did a quick sketch, but with more practice, Dillon could be really good.

He drew my profile as I held the rod over the pond, with one foot crossed over the other. Nah, it was amazing already, and perfect because Dillon drew it.

“That’s fucking awesome, Dil.”

“Thanks,” he said, suddenly blushing and looking away.

“Can I have it? I can like frame it or something.”

“You don’t want this. It was only practice.”

“Yes, I do. Please.”

He looked at me to make sure I wasn’t teasing him or lying, then he ripped out the page and handed it to me.

“Thanks.”

I rolled the paper and shoved the drawing into the backpack I brought. Then we took off our shirts and jumped into the water to splash around a bit. Even Tilly jumped in. We tossed a stick into the water and watched her fetch it before we grew bored. Tilly came out and shook water and mud all over the place, making us groan and laugh.

Finally, feeling dried off enough, we gathered our fishing gear and made our way back to the house a half-mile away.

“Have you ever been to the Loudoun County Fair?” I asked. “Dad took me once, and I had a really good time. But that had been like when I was ten. He hasn’t taken me since. Do you think maybe your mom and my dad would take us?”

“Yeah, Mom and I go every year. I’m sure she plans on taking us this year, too.”

“Hell, yeah. I’ve been dying to go back and see if it’s as good as I remembered.”

We walked side by side through the trail in the woods in silence. The more I hung out with Dillon, the less I talked. Not that I didn’t have things to say, but sometimes being in his presence and enjoying the silence was enough for me. Though he was introverted, he always had this strong and confident vibe about him. I only pretended to be confident, knowing my smiles disarmed people and hid what I was really feeling. Though Dillon seemed intuitive when it came to how I was feeling, like he could read my mind.

I usually walked behind Dillon when I wasn’t trying to touch him, so I could enjoy watching him without him noticing, especially on those days when he didn’t wear a shirt. The muscles on his back shifted and tensed as he walked.

Because of my current distraction, I didn’t see the tree root when my foot got caught, and I stumbled onto the rocky dirt path. I tried to catch myself as my hands waved and spun. Then I braced for impact. The pain shot straight from my knee and throughout my body. I rolled on the ground, holding my leg against my stomach, gasping for breath and trying not to cry from the pain, but some tears slipped out, anyway.

“Fuckfuckfuckfuck.”

“Cade! Are you alright?”

“Hurts…”

“Let me look at it,” he said, trying to pry my hands away.

“No. Hurts.”

“Dude, I need to check to see if you’re bleeding. If so, I have to clean it.”

I sucked in a breath as I sat up, trying not to whimper like a fucking baby.

Dillon sat on the ground and removed my hands from my knee. Sure enough, it was bleeding pretty badly.

“Damn, what the hell did you land on?”

“A rock, I think.”

He grabbed his shirt and poured some water from his water bottle on it, then pressed it to my knee, trying to stop the bleeding while cleaning out some of the dirt.

“I’m going to pour some water on it. We need to get that dirt out.”

I nodded and bit my bottom lip, knowing it was going to sting like shit. He dumped the bottle of water on my knee and I managed not to cry as Tilly sat next to me, whined, then licked my hand. I gave her a pet as Dillon used the dry part of his shirt to blot away the water and blood. It was still bleeding, but most of the dirt was gone.

Dillon stood and helped me to stand. “Let’s leave our gear here. No one’s going to take it. I’ll help you walk home, then I can come back to get it later.”

“Okay.”

He wrapped an arm around my waist and draped my arm around his shoulder, and helped me limp home. I would’ve been happy as hell to have him touching me like this if my knee didn’t hurt like shit.

“We’re almost home.”

Once we got inside, Evelyn came out to greet us and saw my bloody knee.

“Oh, my god. Cade! What’s happened? Oh, honey, you’re bleeding everywhere.”

“He’s fine, Mom. He landed on a rock when he tripped. I’ll get him cleaned up.”

She didn’t look so sure. “No, he needs a doctor? It looks terrible.”

“I’m fine, Evelyn. Trust me. Give me a band-aid and I’ll be good.”

“Are you sure, honey?”

“Definitely.”

“Okay, but I’ll have a bag of ice here when you come down. We need to keep the swelling down. But if it looks worse, I’m taking you to the doctor.”

“Yes, ma’am.”

Dillon helped me up the steps and into our bathroom. I sat on the closed toilet lid as he went through the cabinets, pulling out hydrogen peroxide and some bandages.

He sat on his knees on the floor and cleaned my wound properly. When it was dry, the wound finally looked like it had finished bleeding. He inspected it closely, then looked up at me.

“Does it still hurt?”

I nodded. “Yeah, a fucking lot.”

I suddenly held my breath and my heart stopped when he leaned in and gently pressed his lips to my knee, giving me a kiss on my wound. I think I died a little. And for the briefest moment, the pain went away before it came roaring back.

He blushed all the way to his ears and quickly glanced at me, then back to my knee. “Mom always kisses me when I get hurt. It’s… weird, I know, but I always feel better after. Not sure if a brother’s kiss is the same as a mom’s kiss, though.”

I died a little more.

It still hurt, but I wasn’t about to tell him that. “It really does work. It hurts less already. Thanks.”

He gave me a shy smile and my heart stopped once more. How many times can a heart stop before being declared officially dead?

Once he got me all cleaned up and dried off, he put some thick gauze on my knee and wrapped it with surgical tape. Then he stood as if nothing had just happened between us. Well, it was probably more one-sided, with me reading too much into it. Shit, maybe I should’ve gotten injured more often.

After I iced my knee, and we had dinner, we decided to hang out in the barn, and I felt good enough to climb the ladder to the hayloft. Now *our* hayloft. The miracle of Dillon kisses. I still had pain, but he had me so focused on him and not my knee, that I could ignore my wound.

I also almost forgot to breathe when he did it, just like that day when Dillon ran his fingers through my hair as I cried on his lap. Ever since then, I’d lay my head on his lap as he’d comb his fingers in my curls, and I wanted us to stay like that forever. When we were close like that, I quit caring that we were brothers and imagined we were just two guys who liked each other more than friends. How can something feel this good while knowing other people would see us as a bad thing?

Dillon sat on the hay-covered loft, resting back on his hands with his legs stretched out, inviting me to lay my head on him. As soon as my head was on his strong thighs, he ran his fingers through my hair, careful when he came to a tangle, and gently pried it apart.

We sat in silence as we did often now, not always needing to talk. The more time I spent with Dillon, the more I enjoyed just listening to the countryside, along with my heart drumming in my ears whenever we were close like this.

Soon those butterflies in my stomach moved around so much it almost hurt because I wanted to talk to him about something that was risky. Or at least to get a sense of what Dillon thought about it. I had this desperate need for him to know all of me, but I had to ask without giving myself away yet. While I trusted him, this was also a really big deal. He’d be the first person I’d ever told if I got the balls.

“Dil?”

“Yeah?”

“Have… have you ever known a gay person before?”

His body stiffened, making my hands get all clammy. Why did he react like that? What if he hated gay people? Fuck. If so, he’d hate me. The thought terrified me. I’d hate for this to be the one thing that took Dillon away from me, but I tried to trust him like I always did. To trust that our friendship was strong enough.

“Not that I remember,” he said, which made me instantly relax against him.

“Oh.”

“Why?”

“I dunno. Would you care if you had a gay friend?”

“No, I wouldn’t care. Would you?”

“Nah, it wouldn’t bother me.”

The next words out of Dillon’s mouth made my entire world turn upside down, and it changed everything. For the first time since we’d met, I had hope for us. Hope that we could be more.

“Sometimes I… I trust you not to say anything, okay?”

“I won’t. Ever.”

His fingers resumed their combing, helping me to relax.

“Sometimes I wonder if I’m gay. I… never really thought about it before. But I just… wonder is all.”

*Gay? Gay? Please let him be gay. Please, please, please*.

“H-how do you know?”

“I don’t. It’s just a feeling… but I’ve never been really into a guy before, so I dunno.”

The disappointment hit hard. If he was never into a guy before, then he wasn’t into me and probably never would be.

“But I’ve never been into a girl before, either. So… it’s pretty fucking confusing. Some kids last year were already dating, even if it was only for a couple of weeks. I only wonder because I don’t seem to be into anyone. Not sure if that makes me weird or not.”

“No way does that make you weird. But why do you think that makes you maybe gay?”

“I’m… not sure. It’s only a feeling I have.”

Should I tell him? I could trust him. Definitely. Especially after what he’d just told me.

“Dil?”

“Yeah?”

“I think I’m gay. No. I *know* I am. When I brought it up, I meant it to be about me, and to see how you’d react. Do you… do you still like me? You wouldn’t hate me for it, right?”

God, my heart hammering against my chest almost hurt.

Dillon helped me to sit up and forced me to look at him by pressing his hands on the sides of my face. His intense blue eyes stared at me and I died to know what he thought about it. But at least he didn’t seem angry or disgusted.

Still, my eyes watered because I shared my biggest secret and I came out for the first time to my best friend and my crush. It really was scary, even if I trusted Dillon.

“Fuck, Cade. I would never hate you or dislike you for being gay. You understand that, right? You’re perfect as you are.”

My entire body sagged with relief, and I almost cried. I hated crying so much and being so sensitive.

Dillon pulled me into a hug. “We’re best friends. Forever. You hear? Nothing you could do would make me hate you. Thank you for telling me and trusting me.”

“Thanks, Dil.” I held onto him as long as I could.

When we pulled away, he patted his lap. I smiled and lay back down, and he resumed playing with my hair.

“How did you figure it out?” he asked. “How did you realize you were gay?”

“I dunno. I just… I’d watch TV and think some male actors were good-looking, but not the women. The women were pretty too, but they didn’t interest me like the guys did.” I wasn’t ready to tell him I had a big crush on my brother. So fucking not ready for that. “Or having a crush on… some guys in school.” That wasn’t a total lie because I thought some guys in school were cute, but I hadn’t crushed on them like I had been crushing on Dillon, who was my entire world. He was everything to me.

“Oh.”

“Is that how you see things, Dil?”

“No. For me, it’s different. It’s just a feeling I have.”

“Talk to me, okay? When you figure it out or if you need help, I’ll be here for you.”

“Thanks, Cade.”

We went back to sitting in silence, staring out at the sun which had just set, casting the barn in shadows when we were called in for showers and bed.

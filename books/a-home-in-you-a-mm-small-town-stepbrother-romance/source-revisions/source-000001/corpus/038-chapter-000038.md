# 28. Cade

I walked back to my dorm with my hands shoved in my pockets in the rain. I’d forgotten my umbrella, but I didn’t care either. It felt good against my skin, sending chills through my body. The cloudy day matched my mood until my phone buzzed. When I pulled it out of my back pocket to see it was my dad, my mood turned thunderous. God, I didn’t want to talk to him. In fact, I’d be happy never to see him again. But I answered, otherwise, he’d be hounding me. Then the threats would start, always controlling everything in my fucking life.

“What?” I snapped.

“Hello to you, too.”

“I’m busy. What do you want?”

“You have a race in two weeks. I’m coming up.”

“You don’t need to be there. I don’t need the support.”

“But I want to be there for you.”

I sighed and raised my face to the rain, begging for lightning to strike me down and put me out of my fucking misery.

“Whatever.”

“Cade, I’m tired of the attitude. I’ve tried so hard to be a good father to you. I was neglectful before, I’ll admit. But I’ve been really trying to remedy that.”

I scoffed. “Trying?” Narcissistic piece of shit. “You only got involved in my life again to control me, not out of any sense of love. Trust me, I know where I stand with you.”

“Is that what you believe?”

“No, I don’t believe it. I know it.”

“If you want to keep being a shit even though I pay for everything, then I can take it all away. Shape up. I’m sick of it.”

Fuck! The only reason I hadn’t told him to fuck off with the tuition was that I didn’t want to leave, even though a lot of the money came from scholarships from my own achievements. I was still holding on to a minuscule of hope that Dillon would show. Maybe not to be with him again, but to at least tell him how I felt.

“Fine. I’ll see you in two weeks.”

“See, is it so hard to be nice to your old dad, who does so much for you?”

*Yes.*

“I gotta go.”

I hung up before we could talk anymore.

Once I sucked my dad dry of tuition and graduated, I’d move as far away as possible from him, never to see him again. He could die at this moment, and I wouldn’t give a shit, tuition or no tuition. That was how much hate I had for him. I kept telling myself that, but sometimes I doubted my level of hatred. I had been so tired of his suffocation.

I brought the storm with me inside my dorm room with my mood, along with wearing a rain cloud’s worth of water.

Darius tossed a towel at me. I caught it, I dried my hair and face and undressed to change into dry clothes.

“Rough day?”

“Isn’t it always?”

“What’s going on, dude? You’ve been moody as shit since that party last week.”

I climbed up to my bunk and melted into my bed. “Yeah, I almost had a hook-up with that twink and I couldn’t do it. Dillon refuses to get out of my fucking head. He fucking owns me and I want to let him go so bad.”

I’d told Darius about Dillon the next day after the party and why I left so early.

“Do you, though?”

“What’s that supposed to fucking mean?”

He shrugged, rolled onto his back in bed, and scrolled through his phone. “If you really wanted to move on, you would. Was this dude cute?”

“Yeah, he was beautiful.”

“You know what you need?”

“Drugs? Alcohol? Oblivion?”

He chuckled. “So dramatic. No. You need closure. Have you thought about finding him to see if he’s even here? If he is, then he’s been ignoring you. It would be a kick in the ass that he’s over you. Then you can finally tell him to fuck off, and you can fuck that cute dude.”

“I guess.”

And that had been my problem. I’d thought about finding Dillon and giving him a piece of my mind. But I was afraid to. What if I was wrong? What if something happened to keep him from finding me? Something out of his control?

Ugh! God, why did love have to be such a pain in the fucking ass?

“Going to the game Friday?” Darius asked, breaking me from my plummeting mood.

“No.”

“Come on. Everyone’s going. It’s the first game of the season and our first year here. What else are you going to do?”

*Study. Run. Sit in my room and mope.*

Football always reminded me of Dillon. Once I started watching, he’d be all I thought about.

*How is that any different from now, numbnuts?*

“Come on. You need some fun in your life, Cade. As someone who’s going to be a biologist, what type of biology notwithstanding, I need to have fun while I can. And clearly, so do you.”

“Fine.”

Darius snorted a laugh. “Is this how it’s going to be with you? Pulling teeth to get you out?”

I smiled and tossed a pillow at him. “Probably. You’ve got to work hard to be around my greatness.”

“Ha! There are the smiles. Who the fuck knew you had dimples, moody ass.”

\* \* \*

Rowdy and loud students filled the long lines at the stadium. Most of the people were students, but there were some adults and parents too. Temperatures rested around seventy degrees with not a cloud to be seen and no humidity, making the day absolutely perfect. A rare treat in Virginia.

Raven and Darius looped their arms together as we waited to show our tickets. I guess those two were an item now.

“So, are you all dating? I guess opposites attract.”

Not because Darius was black, but that he was so clean-cut and studious. While Raven was all emo black all the time and covered in tats and piercings. She was definitely smart and pretty, though. You had to be smart to get into this school.

“When you like ‘em, you like ‘em,” Darius said.

“Fair. Just… be good to my girl.”

“Will do.”

Raven kissed his cheek and laughed. She looked like a badass, but she was sweet as fuck. And persistent as hell. She had been the only one who pulled me out of my shell after Dillon, and I loved her for it. Her forcing herself into my life probably helped save me. It sounded dramatic, but I had been plummeting fast when I met her.

We sat pretty much in the middle of the field and not too far back. I had no idea how Darius scored such good seats, but I didn’t complain. We sat at the perfect vantage point to see everything.

God, the stadium was packed. I’d never seen so many people in one place before. Admittedly, it was exhilarating. The noise, the cheerleaders, the marching band music, and the school pride. Something I had never been into before. I wish we had this sort of support for running, but American football was everything in this country.

The teams came onto the field and when the kick-off started, the entire stadium erupted like thunder. It was hard not to get caught up in the chaos of the moment.

“Who are we playing again?” I asked Darius over the noise.

“The University of Illinois.”

It wasn’t a powerhouse school, but neither was UVA.

Darius tapped my shoulder and discretely handed me a flask of something. I opened it and took a long pull of something nauseatingly strong. I coughed, took another sip as the liquid burned straight down to my stomach, and handed it back.

By the end of the first quarter, the UVA Cavaliers led by fourteen points while I worked a comfortable buzz.

When the second quarter started, we all watched in horror as the quarterback got sacked and his leg bent in an unnatural direction. We all did a collective wince and groan as they showed the scene over and over in slow motion on the screen.

“Well, he’s out for good,” Darius said, handing me the flask again. “I guess we’re going to lose now.”

“It’s just a game,” Raven said.

He scoffed. “Where’s your team spirit, Rav?”

“Football isn’t my thing. I’m only here for you.”

“Aww, thanks, babe.”

Darius kissed her head, and she snuggled into him. I tried not to let the envious feelings take over, but it was hard. While my relationship with Dillon had been great, we also missed out on so much. No snuggles together during events, no dates, no kissing in public. Nothing. Everything had been a secret. Not once did he complain. Not once did he get upset when I fell into one of my insecure moods. All he did was try to keep me happy.

I shook my head as if I could shake the memories right out of it. It didn’t matter anymore. He forgot about me, probably realizing how high-maintenance I was after I left. He had another life now, as did I.

*Yeah, some fucking life*.

It took about fifteen minutes before they hauled the injured quarterback off to a roar of claps and cheers. After that, our team had to get reorganized.

Finally, our offense ran onto the field while they announced the replacer quarterback.

My mind took a moment to process the name, then I looked at the back of his jersey. Copeland.

*Dillon fucking Copeland.*

“No! No, no, no.”

“What? What’s wrong?” Raven asked.

“It’s… him.”

“Who?”

“Dillon! My Dillon!” Fuck, I practically screeched.

Raven and Darius looked back and forth between Dillon and me.

“Are you sure?”

“Yes, I’m sure! How many quarterbacks named Dillon Copeland are there who were supposed to come to UVA?”

She rested her hand on my shoulder as the feelings of betrayal festered once more. He’d been here all along and never tried to find me. He came here to *our* school and not once looked me up. What a fucking kick in the nuts.

Dillon now became the starting quarterback for the Cavaliers. And he wasn’t just only an ex-boyfriend now. He was my enemy. I fucking hated him even more at that moment. All that grief from missing him morphed into anger that he did not once try to find me after fucking everything we went through. Angry tears threatened as I tried fighting them back.

“He promised he’d never leave me. When we separated, he promised to find me, and he never did. Why did he come here, knowing I’d be here? To torture me? To shove it in my fucking face?”

Raven rubbed my shoulder. “Cade, we don’t know for sure what happened. Maybe he doesn’t even realize you’re here. Maybe he hasn’t had a chance yet.”

“Bullshit!”

I had to get out of there before I lost my mind and turned into a blubbering mess. I rubbed my chest with the tattoo, wishing I’d never gotten it now. So fucking stupid.

Choking back a sob, I stood and climbed over all the people until I reached the steps, and then I ran into the men’s restroom. I turned on the sink and splashed cold water on my face. My face reflecting in the mirror was wet with water and tears. My betraying lips quivered at my loss. It was like I lost him all over again. That I was this upset over Dillon after all this time showed how much I still loved him. How much I hated him.

“If we ever meet again, Dillon Copeland, you’re going to understand exactly what you did to me. You’re going to hurt like I fucking hurt.”

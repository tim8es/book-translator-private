# 13. Cade

Ever since moving into Dillon’s house, life had been fucking awesome, except for that little scare after being caught by Darren. I never expected high school to be so fun. No one picked on me as they used to in middle school, and I even made tons of friends. Sure, there was always that one prick, but nothing I couldn’t handle.

Even better? Dillon loved me.

Dillon and I were almost fifteen, so no more trick or treating for us on Halloween night. Instead, we got invited to a costume party by some friends. All the kids were going to be there, and our first party of the year, though we’d been invited to several. This was the first party our parents allowed us to go to. It was going to be epic.

Dillon dressed as Frankenstein’s monster since he was as tall as one, and I dressed up as the mad doctor. After Evelyn and Dad took tons of pictures of us, Dad put an arm around me and pulled me aside.

“Listen, son, I know I’m not always around, and we haven’t had ‘the talk’ yet, but since you’re going to party with girls, I thought I’d give a quick speech.”

“Ugh, Dad… please. No.”

He narrowed his eyes. “What? Don’t you like girls?”

That right there proved I’d never be able to tell my dad the truth about me. Especially about Dillon and me.

“I’m only fourteen, Dad.”

“Well, it’s better to be prepared.” He pulled out his wallet, opened it, and handed me a condom.

My face burned as I took it from him. “Always keep one on hand in your wallet. Make sure to always wrap it up.”

“Ugh, okay. Thanks.” *Fucking Gross*.

“I’m going to tell Dillon, too, since I’m his father figure now.”

He patted my back and walked off towards Dillon. And that was the extent of my sex talk with dad. *Good talk, Dad*. Jeez. Good thing we had sex education already, not that it would help me with loving a guy. But I couldn’t believe he’d assume I’d have sex with a girl already just because I was going to a party.

Dillon walked over to me looking green, even through his makeup, and thoroughly confused. Dad had that effect on people.

“Ugh, your dad handed me a condom like it was Halloween candy.”

“Yep. Me too.”

We both shuddered for effect.

“Okay, boys, one more picture. You two look so stinkin’ cute,” Evelyn said, missing the whole condom pass-off.

One more turned into twenty more with lots of eye-rolling and complaining until she finally drove us to the party.

The house belonged to Darcy Worthington. It was more of a mansion than a house. Her family was one of the wealthiest horse breeders in Loudoun County. She was also the head JV cheerleader and the most popular freshman girl. I guess money did that. I didn’t really know her or what she was like. Some rich kids could be dicks, and there was a lot of wealth around this county. Old money wealth.

“Nice place. I’ve had the Worthington’s in my shop a couple of times. They seem like nice people,” Evelyn said as we pulled up in front of the house.

There were kids everywhere in all sorts of costumes going in and out of the house. And the house looked awesome with all the Halloween decorations.

“I’ll pick you up at eleven,” she said. “No later. I’ll text you when I’m on my way.”

“Yes, ma’am,” we both said.

Dillon and I walked inside the house with dim lighting, music, and full of fake fog. There were so many kids, and I had that itch I always did to hold Dillon’s hand for comfort and to ease my nerves, but we couldn’t for obvious reasons.

In the massive living room, which had been cleared away of furniture, kids danced to a slow song that just came on. My heart ached because I wanted to do that with Dillon so much. To sway to the music as we held each other openly and unafraid. But that would never happen. Not during high school, anyway.

I looked up at him as he looked down at me. We shared a moment, and he probably thought the same thing as me. While I loved Dillon, it got to be lonely sometimes. I didn’t just have my secret of being gay, but being in love with my stepbrother.

There was a squeal from Darcy when she saw us. She was dressed like some dead prom queen, and bounced up and down, clapping. “Oh. My. God! You two look amazeballs!”

She grabbed Dillon’s hand, pulling him towards a group of kids sitting in a circle. A weird wave of jealousy hit me. I fisted my hands as she stood close to *my boyfriend*, still holding his fucking hand. It was also another reminder of the problems that could happen when you were in the closet. No one knew, so they assumed you were straight and free to date.

He looked back at me and mouthed, ‘help.’ That eased my growing anger, and I laughed.

When I stood next to him, I saw a lot of familiar faces, including Darren, who’d grown to be a decent friend of Dillon’s. He became mine because Dillon and I came as a pair, but I also wasn’t on the football team. It took me a long time to trust him not to give away our secret, even though I was the first to accept his truce.

Not surprisingly, he wore a football uniform, except he was also a zombie.

Dillon and I looked at the floor, and I may have gotten dizzy. They were playing spin the bottle. What were we? Twelve again? This is a fucking middle-school game. I didn’t want to play or kiss anyone other than Dillon. I’d never kissed anyone else. Neither had Dillon. I didn’t want to share him, even for a stupid game.

“Sit over there,” Darcy demanded. “It’s time for kissing.”

Dillon rubbed the back of his neck and shook his head. “Uh, I’m not sure. I haven’t played this before.”

“It’s easy, silly. Relax. It’s just a game. The only rule is to kiss on the lips. Now go sit across from me over there.” Darcy pointed to a floor cushion directly across from her.

I didn’t fucking like this at all. Dillon could only kiss me. But what else could we do? If we backed out, I knew kids well enough, they’d call us gay and shit. Apparently, calling others gay was an insult of insults. Everyone said it, which only put my secret deeper inside me. We’d definitely get made fun of if we backed out. Even worse, what if he kissed Darcy? I had a feeling she liked him.

Dillon’s face said the same thing my mind did. He didn’t want to kiss anyone, either.

“It’s no big deal,” she pushed.

Darren was looking right at us as if saying we better do it to keep our secret. Not that he would tell. He would’ve already said something if he’d planned to.

I blew out a sigh and sat down.

“Nope. You have to sit next to Darren, Cade.”

“Why?”

“It’s the only cushion left.”

That sounded weak to me, but I shrugged and sat down.

Darren leaned in close to me. “Don’t worry. I’ve got you,” he whispered.

I didn’t think there was much he could do if I had to kiss someone else. Even worse, what if I had to kiss Dillon in front of everyone? Would they figure us out? Fuck. No, I really didn’t like this. Couldn’t we get some punch or something instead?

Twelve of us sat around in a circle, taking turns spinning the bottle. The kisses were quick, with a lot of giggles. Not me, though. I just did a lot of mental eye rolls. God, if they only knew how Dillon and I kissed.

When it became my turn to spin, part of me wished I got to kiss Dillon, to have an excuse to kiss him for real in public, but I also didn’t want people to read too much into it. I spun it really fast, and everyone went quiet as the bottle slowed down, just narrowly passing Dillon and ending on a girl I didn’t know. She was dressed as a fairy and wore glasses. We both blushed and gave each other a quick peck on the lips, ending with lots of ‘oohs’ and ‘ah’s.’

Ugh. Humiliating. Why did this lame game even exist?

I quickly glanced at Dillon, who didn’t seem too happy. Welcome to the club.

And because someone up there hates me, when it was Darcy’s turn, the bottle landed directly on Dillon. My entire body grew stiff, and I wanted to yank him out of there and just go home. He could’ve kissed anyone playing, but why did it have to be her? I knew she liked him. Did she fucking plan this? Was that why she had been in such a rush to play as soon as we walked in? There were rumors about her liking him, and this only confirmed it.

She was on her hands and knees, leaning over the bottle with glossed lips, waiting for him. I swallowed hard, and almost stopped them before a big hand landed on my shoulder. I glanced up at Darren, who gave me a slight shake of his head.

He was right. I couldn’t give us away. I deflated as my heart clenched.

At least Dillon had the brains not to look at me first, but he didn’t even pretend he was having fun. He tried to give her a quick peck like I did with the fairy girl, but Darcy grabbed his face and forced her tongue into his mouth.

*Fucking bitch!*

I almost leaped in the middle to stop them, but Darren held me tight. My heart raced, and I was filled with so much jealousy and doubt that I wanted to lash out. What if he liked kissing her more? He admitted he didn’t know if he was gay or not, but he’d never been with a girl before. Maybe now he’d realize what he’d been missing and feel… normal. I tried not to let my eyes water as I waited for his reaction.

Dillon eased her back like the gentleman he always was and sat back after wiping his mouth. Good. Get her shiny, girly lip gloss off those lips. They’re mine.

He gave me a quick glance, frowning. What did that mean? Did he frown at what she did? Did he realize he didn’t like me anymore? That he only liked girls?

God, I wanted to go. I didn’t want to stay here anymore. This party sucked, but we had to keep playing.

Someone elbowed and teased Dillon, and he smiled, playing it all off. I looked over at Darcy, and she didn’t look happy either. Good. Fuck her. Dillon was mine.

And because whoever was up there *really, really* hated me, when it was Dillon’s turn, it landed right on me. Everyone chanted and teased because we were brothers, making me want to run or vomit. One of the two. But we had to keep playing along.

When Dillon and I met halfway, we quickly pecked each others cheeks instead of on the lips, breaking the only rule.

“Nope, you have to kiss on the lips,” Darcy said.

Someone coughed out, ‘gross, they’re brothers,’ making my heart sink more.

“It doesn’t matter if they’re brothers. Rules are rules.”

Dillon and I stared at each other, unmoving and uncertain about what to do.

“There should be a new rule. No siblings,” Dillon suddenly said. I wasn’t sure how that made me feel. Relieved? Upset with him saying that after kissing Darcy.

“Nope. You two have to kiss. Come on. We all know how close you two are.”

Oh, god. They knew. They all knew. Was that why she invited us to play that game? My heart hammered along with my need to run.

Darren stood with fisted hands. “Bullshit. What’s this really about, Darcy? What? Brothers aren’t allowed to be close, so suddenly they’re what? Dating? Give me a fucking break. Is that why you’re doing this? Or is it jealousy because Dillon doesn’t want you?”

Was it possible to love two people? Because I really loved Darren at that moment. We went from enemies to frenemies to now best buds.

Everyone went quiet because no one messed with Darren. Except for Darcy. “This is my party, Dare. My rules.”

“Again, I say bullshit.” Darren looked down at the two of us. “You don’t have to kiss. She’s just being mean right now.”

Darcy scoffed. “As if. Says the bully of the school.”

“Not anymore. Dillon here called me out on my shit. We’re buds, and you’re being mean to my best bud.”

“Give me a fucking break,” said that boy named Davi from the day at the funhouse. “You were there at the fair, Darren, seeing those two brothers holding hands. There’s something going on, and you know it.”

Oh, God. I had to go. I appreciated what Darren tried to do, but I needed to get out of there. How did you run without running?

Darren lifted the smaller boy up by his fake blood-stained t-shirt with meaty fists. “Is that fact? Is that what’s going on here? You spreadin’ rumors? Yeah, I was there, and it was established that they were not dating. Why would they? They’re brothers. Now leave them the fuck alone, or I’m going to have to start cracking skulls.”

“Whatever. This game is fucking stupid.” I had to go even more than I had to breathe. I climbed over the kids and stormed elsewhere in the house. Somewhere quiet. Getting upset would probably prove them right, but I couldn’t stay there any longer. I was literally suffocating.

And I wasn’t sure I wanted to know how Dillon felt about his kiss with Darcy. He was too quiet. Could I take it if he chose her over me? Hell fucking no. But why not? He wouldn’t be the first person to turn his back on me. Hell, even my own dad liked Dillon more.

God, get me out there, but I was trapped with no way home.

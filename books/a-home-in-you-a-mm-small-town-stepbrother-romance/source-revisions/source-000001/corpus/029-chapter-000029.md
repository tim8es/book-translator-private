# 19. Cade

The first thing that woke me up was Tilly barking. It wasn’t a normal bark, but it sounded agitated. Suddenly, a blast of air and coldness hit me across my naked skin.

“Get the fuck up,” said the voice.

My blurry eyes blinked open and widened when I recognized my dad’s voice. I panicked, looking for a lie or any excuse to reason away why I was sleeping naked next to Dillon… my stepbrother. My heart palpitated, and my stomach suddenly burned with acid, threatening to come up.

When I didn’t get out of bed fast enough, my dad grabbed me by my arm, dug his fingers and nails into my skin, and yanked me out of bed. I fell hard onto my knees as my mind spun to the point of dizziness. I blanked out, not knowing what to do, struggling to breathe and think.

Dad yanked me up to stand, and I glanced at Dillon, whose body shook as he put on pajama bottoms and a T-shirt.

“What the *fuck *is this?”

“Dad, I can—”

“Explain?! Explain that you’re naked in bed with a faggot and your stepbrother!”

He shoved me to the ground and let my arm go. “Get your fucking clothes on. Now!”

My hands trembled as I tried to stop the rising vomit from coming out. Tears welled, and I had no idea what to do. Dillon and I never talked about what we would do or say if we got caught. Only promises to find each other.

“We came home early because Evelyn hadn’t been feeling well, and when I came to wake you up to let you know I was home, Cade, I found you missing from your bed which hadn’t been slept in. So I hunted down Dillon here only to find you two fucking naked and snuggling. Are you fucking kidding me?”

“Sir, please, if you’d—” Dillon tried to talk, but Dad was on a rampage.

“No! You keep your fucking mouth shut. I don’t want to hear it, faggot. I should’ve known. You don’t date girls. You don’t talk about girls. How dare you corrupt my son! You fucking pervert! He’s your goddamned brother!”

Dillon looked pissed now, but was still shaking. “Hey! Enough with the bigotry! We aren’t related, and we love each other.”

Dad scoffed. “Love? It’s a perversion. Nothing more.”

He turned his ire back on me, scanning me up and down to make sure I was dressed, then out of the blue, he slapped me so hard I fell again.

“Hey! Don’t you dare fucking hit him!”

I sobbed as I touched my tender face and stood again.

“Don’t you tell me what I can and cannot do with my own son. You’ve fucking ruined him! No wonder he hadn’t been dating girls. You disgust me.”

Dillon folded his arms, now as angry as Dad. “Who the fuck are you to say? You’re useless. A waste of a father to Cade. All you do is hurt him. You’ve neglected him all these years, and *now* suddenly you care about him? Please.”

Shit, Dad looked like he was ready to beat the shit out of Dillon, but Dillon was way bigger. I didn’t think my dad was stupid enough to take him on.

“I’m about to remedy that real quick.” He faced me and pointed. “Your life is over. You’re not leaving my sight for a second. When I’m working, your grandma is going to keep a close eye on you.”

“Wait, what? No!”

“You don’t get to tell me no. You have no more say in your life from now on. I’m taking you away from here. Dillon, you stay away from my son.”

“No! Dad, please. Don’t take me away. I love him.”

“You’ve been perverted!”

“I’m gay, Dad! I’m gay! It’s me! All me! That doesn’t make Dillon or me perverted!”

“No! No son of mine chooses to be gay. Never!”

“What the hell is going on?!”

Evelyn arrived looking pale, yet angry. “Why are you yelling at the boys like that, Jared? What have they done now that’s got you so angry?”

“I caught them naked in bed together.”

She looked at Dillon, then at me, with sorrow in her eyes. When she said nothing, Dad narrowed his eyes. “Did you know, Evelyn? You don’t seem as upset or as surprised as I am. Did you fucking know?”

She wrapped her arms around her, looked down, and nodded. “I knew.”

All three of us looked at her, jaw dropped. So she *did* know. What gave us away?

“Dillon came to me three years ago, wondering if he might be gay. The boys just grew closer and closer, while neither ever showed any interest in girls, nor boys for that matter. I just put two and two together. But one day… I was giving the horses some carrots in the field and when I left, I looked up into the hay loft, and… they were kissing. This was two years ago.”

Dad sputtered and stammered before he could find his words. “Are you fucking kidding me? You never said anything. You never told me. Why didn’t you put a stop to it immediately? Why would you let that perversion continue?”

Dad’s rage was just simmering, about to blow despite his voice being calmer, like before a hurricane or a volcano.

“Because they aren’t related. Because I know love when I see it. Because they take care of each other. I wasn’t about to ruin that for them. All I could do was keep a careful watch and protect them. Protect them from people like you. Those who hate queers.”

“Oh, I bet it was your son who started all this. He’s a fucking faggot, and you’re going to keep him away from my son. Is that understood?”

“My son is not a damned faggot! He’s a good boy who loves unconditionally! So does Cade! You’re the one who’s the problem here. Not them, and not me. God, how did I not see the real you before? Your homophobia disgusts me.”

I was sobbing, appreciating Evelyn more than ever. Dillon was crying too, watching me. God, I needed to touch him and hold him, but Dad still had an iron grip on my arm that hurt.

“You and your son disgust me. What a fucking mistake it was to marry you. I’m going to take Cade away from your perversion, and protect him. Expect to hear from my divorce lawyer. We’re through.”

Dad pulled on me to take me away, but I dug in my heels. “No! Please… I don’t want to go! I love it here! Please… I’ve been so happy. Don’t ruin it. You ruin everything you touch! Why does everything have to be about you?”

“We’re leaving. Do not dare defy me, boy.”

Dillon and Evelyn blocked the bedroom door. “You’re not taking my Cade,” Dillon said, wiping the tears from his face. “We love each other, and there’s nothing you can do about it.”

“I love Cade. If you are so unhappy with him, leave him with us. He’s innocent. Divorce me if you must. Just leave Cade,” Evelyn pleaded.

“No fucking way in hell I’m leaving him to the likes of you two. Now get out of my way, or I’m going to call the cops. Then I’ll sue you for everything you’re worth.”

I finally yanked myself from my dad and rushed to Dillon. He wrapped his arms around me as we cried. “No, please. I can’t go. I can’t leave him.”

“You two make me sick. I’ll grab our shit later.”

Dad grabbed me again and tried to pull me away, but Dillon held on tight. My dad hissed at him. “If you don’t let him go right now, I will have you arrested for the rape of my son. Cade let this fucker go, or I will send you to military school and fucking wash my hands of you. Now!”

He pulled us apart, but we had no choice but to separate. I reached my hand out to him and grasped my fingers as Dad pulled me further and further away. He sobbed but didn’t chase after me. Evelyn was crying too, but she made no move for me.

“I’ll find you,” Dillon said. “Hold on for me, okay?”

I nodded and went with dad, sobbing. “Dillon!”

“Shut up!” Dad said, yanking me further and further from my Dillon.

Once we got outside and to his pickup truck, I tried again. “Please, Dad. I can’t help who I love. I’m gay. I’m gay. It’s okay if I am. It’s not wrong.”

“It’s *all* fucking wrong!”

He backhanded me and I fell into the gravel. I wiped my mouth and my hand came away with blood.

“Now get up, and into that car. If you fight me again, your life as you know it is over.”

My life as I knew it was already over, which hurt more than Dad’s beating.

Dad drove off once I got in the car, and I never took my eyes off of the house until I couldn’t see it any longer, watching Dillon standing there getting smaller and smaller. I rested my head on the window and quietly cried, wondering how such a perfect night turned into such a nightmare of a morning.

Dillon would come for me, right? But how? Dad wouldn’t ever let him near me.

“You’re going to your grandma’s, then I’m headed back to the house to move our things out. Say goodbye to that piece of shit and your horse. You’re never going back, do you hear? And you’re going to get that love nonsense right out of your head. That boy corrupted you. You were doing just fine until he got his perverted hands on you, making you believe you’re gay. Please. I know my own son.”

“You don’t know me at all. You’ve always hated me.”

He looked at me and softened, which strangely made me more afraid. “I never hated you, Cade. I… just hurt when your mama left me to raise you alone. Maybe this is partly my fault that you’ve turned towards a disgusting path, but I aim to remedy that. And we’ll start you in a new school immediately.”

Oh god, I couldn’t even see him at school. The hot tears continued to spill down my face, as my breath caught. “I hate you.”

“You’ll forgive me for this, and one day you’ll thank me for steering you back on the right path.”

“Never,” I said, and stared out the window again.

“Hey, you’re only confused, and that’s Dillon’s fault. I forgive you for being weak, but eventually, you’ll realize that only men and women belong together.”

“Are you kidding me? You can’t just make a gay man like women. It doesn’t work like that.”

“No one is born gay, Cade.”

“God, you’re so ignorant. How did I not see that before?”

“Enough! You will not talk back or so help me…”

“What? Are you going to hit me again?”

God, I was just asking to be beaten. Normally I shied away from confrontation because I feared they’d leave me, but I was too upset and angry. I’d never be happy without Dillon. And I meant what I said. I hated Dad.

“I’m… sorry about that. I just… reacted.”

“Whatever. I’ll never forgive you for any of this. You’re a lousy father.”

“I said fucking enough! Now, if you don’t want to be sent off to military school, I suggest you shape up real fast, young man. And you will do as you’re told.”

My lip quivered, and a sense of helplessness surrounded me as the prison walls slowly closed in on me. I felt suddenly trapped with no way out, doubting Dillon would find a way for us to be together again.

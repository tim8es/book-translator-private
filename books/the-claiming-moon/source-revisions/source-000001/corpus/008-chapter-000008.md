Sana's hand moved up to stroke his hair gently. "I guess the stories were true all along then."

Was he supposed to be happy about Jungkook living or supposed to be sad about him still not being out of danger yet?

Sana rubbed his back gently. "You hear that, Tae? He's going to make it through this. Just keep believing that with everything you have."

He nodded weakly, the words not fully sinking into his consciousness yet. He reached forward again, brushing Jungkook's cold fingers with his own, whispering under his breath so quietly only Jungkook might hear, "You better not leave me now, you fucking idiot. Not after everything we've been through. You’re my alpha."

"Omega, do you mind stepping out for a bit? I need to ask him something privately."

Sana blinked in surprise, clearly startled. "Ask him? Ask Taehyung something?" She glanced between the two of them, confusion clearly across her tired face.

"Yes," the healer said, his eyes not leaving Taehyung for even a second. He was definitely calculating something. "Just for a few minutes. It won't take long."

Taehyung looked up at Sana quickly, shaking his head slightly as if to say he had absolutely no idea what was happening either. Sana hesitated visibly, her eyes flickering first to Jungkook's still body on the bed, then back to Taehyung's confused face. "Okay," she finally murmured, standing up slowly and reluctantly. "Call me if you need anything at all, alright?"

Another few seconds passed in tense silence before he sighed. "I'm Hoseok From Red Moon."

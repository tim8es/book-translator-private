Chapter 1

Moon Village wasn't much to look at. It was just another pack settlement, surrounded by woods and hills that stretched as far as anyone could see. Maybe a thousand wolves called it home, though nobody bothered with exact counts anymore. People came and went, pups were born, elders passed on, and the numbers shifted so fast that no one counted it anymore.

The Clearwater Rivers ran across the territory, splitting the land apart. Jungkook knew the place like the back of his hand. He'd grown up there. The houses were simple, built from rough timber and fieldstone. People often sat in small groups, spread wherever the land gave them a decent place to stand. Come evening, smoke would curl up from a dozen chimneys at once, carrying the scent of whatever the hunters had managed to bring back that day. Venison, rabbit, and sometimes wild boar if the pack got lucky.

You couldn't walk anywhere without hearing life happening all around you. Pups shrieked with laughter as they rolled through the dirt, only to get scolded by mothers who'd given up on keeping them clean hours ago.

Hunters trudged home with animals slung across their shoulders, their boots caked in mud.. The elderly sat out on their front porches like it was their full-time job, keeping an eye on the village and silently judging every bit of drama that came their way. The conversations were always dramatic. People were friendly yet sarcastic at the same time.

"That boy's gonna eat us out of house and home," muttered Runjin from his usual spot on the porch steps. The old omega's joints creaked louder than his rocking chair now, but age hadn't dulled his tongue one bit.

Kayla didn’t bother looking up from his mending. The village tailor had heard this exact complaint before, probably about every single growing pup in Moon Village at one point or another.

“Growing boys are like that. Remember when my Jihoon shot up six inches in one summer? I thought I’d have to hunt elk every day just to keep him fed.”

Runjin huffed, rocking back harder. “Back in my day, pups knew better than to ask for seconds.”

“And back in your day, there wasn’t enough food for seconds,” Kayla said, already used to hearing the same complaint from her neighbor every day.

At the center of the village stood the packhouse. It wasn't just bigger than the other homes. Something about the place made people lower their voices. Only the Pack Alpha and his Luna lived there, even though there was enough space for twenty families. Now, most of it was empty. Hallways and room after room went unused.

The only person that lived there was Jeon Jungkook.

Jeon Jungkook was the Pack Alpha. Twenty-four years old, and somehow the youngest one Moon Village had ever ended up with. The elders hated that. They'd mutter under their breath whenever he walked away, talking about how young he was and how he didn't have enough experience. Never while he could hear them, though.

Nobody said it too loud, in fact, because they'd all seen what happened to the last person who'd been foolish enough to challenge him directly. Nobody wanted to make that mistake. Jungkook hadn’t come into power through bloodline or tradition. He hadn’t waited patiently for his turn nor earned it through years of loyalty to the pack. He’d claimed it the old way, with blood still fresh beneath his fingernails and death trailing close behind him.

Everyone had expected Jungwon to step into his father's role when old Alpha Hyungwon finally died.

That was how it usually worked. A son followed his father. The father would hand the reins to his son after a proper ceremony. Before that, though, anyone who believed they were strong enough could step forward and challenge the head alpha for the position instead of the chosen heir. Blood meant nothing when it came to the title. Even their own kin could challenge them. Victory meant taking the pack... and leaving no former Alpha behind.

But no one had challenged for years. Maybe even centuries.

The same pattern has been repeated for generations, but when Hyungwon called for challengers in his final days, it wasn’t his own blood who stepped forward first. The first to step forward was the boy he’d found in the forest twenty years ago. He was only four years old, crying on a flat rock where someone had placed him, with no family and no pack of his own. The one Hyungwon had raised alongside his own son, loved like his own blood, and treated like he belonged.

The same boy who'd grown up to rip his throat out in front of the entire pack.

The challenge lasted maybe three minutes, if you were being generous. It was long enough for half the pack to gather around the fighting circle but not long enough for anyone to believe it was already over. Jungkook's wolf was bigger than anybody had expected. So fast that half the crowd didn't even notice him move. When it was over, Hyungwon bled so much the stain stayed long after the rains.

It was just as simple as that. The strongest lead. It was wolf law, older than anything, older even than the village itself. But people remembered, and things meant more to them than old laws. Some still looked at Jungkook and saw the orphaned pup who had murdered his way to power and had betrayed the only father he had ever known.

Others whispered that Hyungwon had been the kindest Alpha they’d known in generations, and they couldn’t understand how his adopted son could have done something like that. The man had saved him from certain death and raised him from childhood and loved him like his own flesh and blood.

The conversation at morning tea was always the same old thing. Mostly the elders of the pack would shake their heads and tell him he was too young and too hot-headed and also way too dangerous to lead a pack.

Then there were those who said strength was exactly what the pack needed. Wolves required fire in their leaders, not gentle hearts that would get them killed by the next ambitious challenger that came along. Jungkook ignored it all with the same stone-faced silence he had gotten so good at over the years.

He’d learned early on that no matter what he did, people would find reasons to doubt him. however wisely he ruled, however righteous his decisions. Best to concentrate on keeping the peace and protecting the pack instead of wasting time fretting about gossip that would never end.

Keeping the peace tonight meant getting through another Claiming Moon Festival without letting anyone see how badly he wanted to leave.

Jungkook got there just after sunset. People had already packed into the clearing, and nobody seemed able to stand still. Bonfires burned in the middle of the clearing. Some faces were lit up with excitement, others with dread, depending on which side of the mating ritual you were on. A few people looked outright sick to their stomachs. The omegas, mostly.

Families spread blankets on the worn grass.

Mated couples sat close with their arms around each other, while pups bounced on their toes, barely able to hold in their excitement. For the children, it was better than any holiday they celebrated throughout the year. For the unmated wolves waiting at the edge of the clearing... well, that was something else entirely.

A group of pups stood near one of the bigger fires, talking so loud that people kept turning to look. They were a group of seven youngsters. "My dad says omegas always try to climb the really tall trees," a little girl announced. Dirt was spread across her cheek, leaves tangled in her braids; she'd clearly been playing for hours. "But the alphas can smell them anyway, no matter how high they go!"

"Nuh-uh!" A boy who couldn't have been more than six puffed his chest out like he had all the answers. "My brother Minho says the smart ones go to the river instead. The water washes their scent away so nobody can track them."

A third pup with two front teeth missing shook her head with all the confidence in the world. “You’re both wrong. The clever ones don’t run far. They hide right under the alphas’ noses, where nobody looks because it’s too obvious.” She giggled.

Their mothers smiled indulgently at the serious discussion of hunting strategy, finding it sweet rather than concerning.

Honestly, that should have been the most concerning thing about the entire pack.

An omega reached over to ruffle her son's hair affectionately. "You'll understand better when you're older, sweetheart." The boy wrinkled his nose in frustration as his mum kept grinning at him. "But I want to understand now. Why do I have to wait?"

The lady just rolled her eyes, already tired from answering the pups since morning.

The Claiming Moon came around once a year, following traditions so ancient that no one could remember who’d started them or why they’d seemed like a good idea at the time.

Every unmated omega would be sent into the forest with fifteen minutes to find whatever hiding spot they could manage. Then the unmated alphas would be released to hunt them down like prey. If an alpha caught an omega, they'd be dragged back to the circle and mated right there in front of the entire pack, whether they wanted it or not.

Claimed. Then knotted.

And then owned forever.

It was tradition, they said. It made Jungkook's skin crawl every single fucking time.

The ritual had been going on for centuries, maybe even longer, reaching back to a time when wolves were more animal than human. One man had tried to speak against it once, years before Jungkook was even born. He'd stood up in front of the council and called it barbaric and wrong.

He'd been banished from the pack within a week and never seen again.

People claimed he'd been killed by rogues while wandering alone. But Jungkook suspected the truth was uglier than that. The council didn't like being challenged and especially didn't like their traditions being questioned. These people called themselves civilized when it suited them. They taught their children to be kind, to share, and to help their neighbors in times of need.

Then once a year they'd gather around fires to watch terrified omegas get hunted down like rabbits while they cheered and celebrated. At least they had the decency to put the pups to bed before the actual mating began.

As if that somehow made it any less barbaric or any less wrong.

Jungkook wanted to burn the entire tradition to ash and salt the earth where it had grown. But being Pack Alpha didn't mean he could simply tear down centuries of custom without the council's approval, and those old bastards weren't ready to listen to him about anything yet, especially not about changing traditions they'd grown up with.

He stood there with his arms crossed and his jaw clenched tight, watching his pack work itself into a frenzy over something that disgusted him down to his bones. Near the edge of the crowd, some of the younger alphas had gathered in their usual group, already bragging about tonight's hunt like it was some kind of competition.

“This is my year; I can feel it,” Myul announced, flexing his arms like there had never been a doubt. “Been working out all season just for tonight. No omega’s outrunning me this time.” Jungkook glanced at Myul, probably the closest thing he had to a friend in the pack. That alone said enough. At least Myul talked to him like a person, not something dangerous that might bite if provoked.

Which most definitely was true.

Most of the pack still looked at him and saw the orphan who'd killed his adopted father in cold blood. These days they were afraid of him, but fear and respect weren't the same thing. Fear was just fear, and it made for a lonely existence.

"Yeah? Well, I've been tracking scents in the forest for months," Haejin shot back, not about to be outdone by anyone. He was younger to Jungkook by a year. Son of one of the council members. Way too cocky for his own good. “I know every inch of these woods. No omegas are getting past me this year.”

"You guys are dreaming," laughed a third alpha whose name Jungkook couldn't be bothered to remember. He was never good with people to begin with.

"I've got my eye on that sweet little omega with the dark hair. What's his name... Taehyung? He's been dodging everyone for six years running, but not anymore. Tonight he's mine." Jungkook's attention snapped to the conversation as if a wolf were catching the scent of blood on the wind.

"Good luck with that," Myul snorted, shaking his head. “Half the pack’s tried to catch him over the years. He’s like smoke in those woods, there one second and gone the next. Nobody even knows where he hides.”

"Well, he can't run forever," the nameless alpha replied with smug certainty that made Jungkook's hands curl slowly into fists at his sides. "And neither can he. Everyone's luck runs out eventually."

Jungkook forced himself to look away before he did something stupid that would require explanation later. Elder Namjin stepped into the center of the circle. His hair was white with age; his voice was worn smooth by years of ritual. He raised a hand, and the crowd slowly went silent.

"Tonight, the moon witnesses us," he began in his ancient tone he always used for festivals.

"Tonight, our unmated omegas will enter the forest. They will have fifteen minutes to conceal themselves as best they can. After that time passes, the alphas will hunt. Should an alpha find an omega, that omega will be claimed and mated here before us all. This is our way. This has been our way."

The pups clapped and cheered like they were watching a parade or some kind of celebration instead of what it really was. The young alphas grinned and rolled their shoulders, already half-drunk in anticipation and the promise of a mate. But at the edge of the circle, the omegas who'd be running tonight shifted nervously from foot to foot. Jungkook could smell the chemical tang of scent blockers thick in the air around them.

It was so strong it almost made his eyes water.

For some, this night meant joy, the chance to find their mate, their other half. For others, love didn’t always turn out the way the stories promised. Sometimes the alpha who loved you couldn't find you fast enough, and someone else would drag you back to the circle instead. Someone you didn't want but you'd spend the rest of your life bound to.

At the very back of the omega line stood Taehyung.

His head was bowed low, dark hair falling over his face. His hands were clasped in front of him like he was trying to make himself invisible through sheer force of will. Quiet as always.  Untouchable as always. For six years straight he'd stood in this exact spot, and for six years straight he'd vanished into those woods like he'd never existed at all.

Some people whispered that the moon goddess herself protected him, that there was something special about him. Others said he was cursed and too pure for any alpha to claim. They thought he was too perfect to ever be caught. Every year, more alphas set their sights on being the one to finally drag him back to the circle.

They wanted to be the one who succeeded where everyone else had failed. But it hasn’t happened in the past six years. Jungkook stared at him like a dying man staring at salvation, like Taehyung was water and he was dying of thirst.

Three years. That's how long he'd been chasing Taehyung through those trees, running himself ragged every Claiming Moon, following traces of scent that always led nowhere in the end. But sometimes, when Jungkook walked alone in the woods to clear his head and escape the suffocating weight of leadership, he'd catch glimpses of him.

A white wolf running its way through the bush, chasing rabbits with pure joy, splashing through shallow streams. Free as the wind. Beautiful as moonlight reflecting on water. He'd wanted Taehyung longer and more desperately than he'd ever wanted anything in his entire life.

"Alpha Jeon!" One of the younger alphas, Lucas maybe, turned to face him with a shit-eating grin. Jungkook didn’t bother remembering his name. That look meant trouble.

"Think you'll finally catch someone this year?" the kid asked with just enough mockery threaded through his tone to make it sting. "Or are you gonna come back empty-handed again like always?" A few others snickered quietly, trying not to be too obvious about it. Jungkook's eyes didn't move from Taehyung's bowed head.

Let them think he was incompetent at this.  Let them believe he couldn't catch an omega when the truth was he could probably track down any of them blindfolded and with one arm tied behind his back.

He’d had dozens of them practically throw themselves at his feet over the years. “Oh, Alpha, you found me. Guess I’m yours now.”

Every time, he’d just turn around and walk away without a word. They weren't what he wanted. Only one omega had ever been what he wanted, and that omega was standing thirty feet away, trying to disappear into the shadows.

"We'll see," Jungkook said quietly, his voice emotionless.

Namjin raised his stick again, holding it high above his head. The crowd fell silent.

"Run."

The omegas disappeared into the forest within seconds. The circle erupted into noise the moment the omegas vanished into the treeline. Pups squealed and clapped their hands while parents laughed and tried to hush them without much success.

To the kids, it was just a big game of hide-and-seek, a tradition older than their grandparents. They didn’t need to understand it to enjoy it. It was exciting and dramatic, and they got to stay up late watching it all happen. That was all they wanted.

The unmated alphas waited behind the central fire like caged animals pacing their enclosures.

Their shoulders were tense, and some were already letting their wolves creep closer to the surface, eyes beginning to glow with red light as their teeth slowly sharpened. The younger ones whispered among themselves, placing bets and making boasts about how easy tonight would be.

"I'm thinking Minji," someone muttered. "The girl's not that fast, and her scent's always been strong. Should be an easy catch."

"Nah, go for Seoyun," his friend whispered back eagerly. "Heard she's close to heat. Won't be thinking straight, probably won't even run that far. Easy fuck.”

"You're both aiming too low," a third voice joined in with poorly concealed arrogance. "Everyone knows the real prize is Taehyung. Tonight's the night someone finally gets him. Might as well be me." Just hearing the name spoken that way, like Taehyung was some kind of trophy to be won, made Jungkook's wolf pace restlessly under his skin. His hands clenched into fists at his sides, but he kept his expression carefully neutral, giving away nothing. At least he tried to.

On the other side of the fire, the elders were trying to settle the increasingly rowdy crowd. Namjin banged his walking stick against the ground hard enough to make the nearest pups jump in surprise. "Silence!" Slowly and reluctantly, the pack quieted down. Even the youngest children stopped giggling, but they pouted at that.

"The rules remain unchanged," Namjin announced, his gaze roaming across the gathered wolves with authority.

"The omegas run. Fifteen minutes to lose themselves in the forest. When the horns sound, the alphas give chase. Fail to catch one before sunrise, and you wait another year. Succeed..." his lips curved in a smile, "and the moon goddess herself has blessed your union."

The crowd roared their approval like this was something sacred instead of something sick, instead of something that should have been left behind centuries ago. Jungkook's stomach turned. Blessed. That's what they all loved to call it. He saw no blessing in dragging terrified omegas from their hiding spots and forcing them to submit in the dirt while everyone cheered and celebrated.

All he saw was cruelty dressed up in pretty words and ancient traditions that should have died long ago.

But he didn't have a choice. Not yet.

The council still saw him as too young and dangerous to trust with major changes to pack law. Most of them believed it was only a matter of time. That he'd grow into the role, and one day they'd respect him for more than the fear he put into people.

Until then, he had to play along with their games.

His gaze found the other alphas in the lineup. His brother Jungwon stood near the front. Tall, smug, already licking his lips like victory was guaranteed. His adoptive brother, though neither of them had called it that in years. They barely spoke anymore unless pack business demanded it.

The bastard had grown up expecting leadership to fall into his lap like ripe fruit.

He had spent his whole life preparing for a role that never came. Every time he looked at Jungkook now, resentment burned in his eyes like coals that refused to die. Let him burn. Jungkook had killed one Alpha to get where he was. He wasn't above doing it again if someone pushed him too far.

The horn was heard again. Five minutes had passed.

The crowd began to chant, voices rising in encouragement that sounded more like bloodlust than support. Mothers pulled their pups closer, telling them to watch carefully because someday they'd be running or chasing too. Someone started beating drums, joined by wooden flutes, and soon mated pairs were singing along with ancient chants that could be heard through the trees.

Jungkook tilted his head back, staring past the firelight into the black wall of forest that surrounded them on all sides. Somewhere out there, Taehyung was hiding.

Still as a tree.

Clever as a fox.

Probably already miles away from where anyone would think to look. "Ten minutes remaining!" Namjin called out.

Some of the younger alphas laughed, shifting their weight like their muscles couldn't hold still anymore. Several were already partway through their shift, teeth extending past their lips, claws pushing through their fingertips, eyes beginning to glow with a predatory light that made them look more animal than human.

Jungkook remained perfectly motionless. He was simply breathing, letting his wolf press against the cage of his ribs like a storm waiting to break loose. He despised this festival. Despised everything it represented. But if it brought him to Taehyung, if it gave him even a chance to find the omega he'd been dreaming about for years, he'd run until his feet bled and his lungs collapsed.

He'd chase Taehyung to the ends of the earth if that's what it took.

Taehyung had thought that maybe this year he could skip the festival, and maybe he could claim illness, fake an injury, or maybe find some excuse that would let him avoid this whole nightmare. But no. The council had made it clear years ago that all unmated omegas were required to participate. No exceptions. Not even for him, no matter how many times he had escaped. The forest swallowed the omegas like a hungry mouth every damn fucking time.

But Taehyung moved differently than others did. Six years of this ritual had taught him something important.

Panic got you caught.

He didn't run far because distance meant nothing if the alphas could still track your scent through the trees. Running fast just burned the energy you'd need later, and it made your breathing loud enough for ears to catch from half a mile away. Surviving this wasn't about speed or strength or even courage.

It was about becoming invisible.

So he walked instead of running. Barefoot, picking his way between the trees until he found what he was looking for. An ancient oak near the stream, its trunk thick enough to hide behind and its branches heavy with late-summer leaves. He climbed slowly, using old knowledge of which branches would hold his weight without snapping, pulling himself up until he reached a natural fork hidden deep in the canopy where the moonlight couldn't quite reach.

There, pressed against rough bark like he was trying to merge with the tree itself, Taehyung curled into the smallest shape possible.

The scent blockers burned his nose the moment he put them on. Their chemical smell covered everything else, even the damp earth beneath his feet, until his eyes started to water. They'd kept him safe for six years running. They'd work tonight too.

They had to.

But his hands wouldn't stop trembling no matter how tightly he pressed them together. Taehyung pulled his knees to his chest and squeezed his eyes shut, trying to calm the fast beating of his heart. God, how he hated this night.  He hated the way people looked at him all year long, like he was the quiet boy, the sweet one, the omega nobody had managed to claim yet.

He hated the way some alphas would smirk when they passed him on the village paths, their eyes trailing over him like he was just prey that hadn't been caught yet, like it was only a matter of time. He wasn't prey. He wasn't weak just because he was an omega.

But he also wasn't stupid enough to think he had any real choice in this. Every omega was required to take part in the Claiming Moon. There were no exceptions and no excuses. Refusal meant banishment from the pack, and banishment meant death in these mountains, whether it came from starvation or rogues or just the cold of winter.

Taehyung had nowhere else to go. This place was home, thanks to Alpha Hyungwon taking him in six years ago when he'd had nothing and no one.  Even if Jungkook had killed the man who saved him. Even if things were different now, more dangerous than they'd ever been before.

Fuck, Taehyung was terrified of their current Pack Alpha.

How could someone kill such a good man, someone who'd opened his home and his heart to strangers? Taehyung had actually liked Jungkook once, back when he was just the quiet boy who taught pups how to hunt and track, who never seemed to want anything from anyone. Now he was just ruthless and cold, a killer who'd taken everything he wanted by force.

When the second horn sounded and the drums grew louder in the distace, Taehyung curled even smaller against the bark, wrapping his arms around his knees as if he could disappear completely through sheer force of will. Taehyung had thought he'd be safe tonight, just like the other years. But something felt different this time. Everything seemed charged with danger, so weird and terrifying.

Back in the clearing, the alphas were getting restless.

Eyes glowing red, teeth sharpening into fangs, claws beginning to show through fingertips that were no longer quite human. The younger ones kept elbowing each other and laughing, placing bets on who'd drag back an omega first. Jungkook stood among them like a statue.

Every year he'd enter that forest, he'd returned empty-handed, defeated and expressionless while the other alphas celebrated their claims. It wasn’t because he lacked the strength or speed, everyone knew he had both in spades. It was because Taehyung was clever enough to vanish like morning mist, leaving no trace behind except the fading ghost of scent blockers which never gave away where he went.

This year would be different. He can feel it.

The third horn rang out for the night. Ten minutes gone, five remaining. The crowd went wild, stamping their feet and calling out the names of alphas they hoped would succeed. The music grew faster, drums pounding. Some elders swayed, lost in memories of their own claiming nights.

Jungkook closed his eyes and let himself imagine it. Taehyung's face was pale and beautiful in moonlight, with those dark eyes wide with something between fear and defiance. The way his white wolf looked when it ran free through the woods on ordinary days, chasing rabbits and splashing through streams with pure joy instead of terror. He'd watched Taehyung from afar more times than he could count, memorizing every detail like a prayer he couldn't stop repeating. Taehyung wasn't prey to be conquered, no matter what the ritual implied.

But he was Jungkook's.

Had been since the first moment Jungkook laid eyes on him years ago, even if Taehyung didn't know it yet. Even if Taehyung would probably hate him for it. Namjin raised his stick for one final time. The entire gathering held its breath, waiting for the moment that would change everything.

The last horn split.

Cheers broke out across the clearing. Birds burst from the trees as the alphas rushed forward together, driven by instinct more than thought.  Some changed before they'd gone ten steps, landing on all fours and disappearing into the trees. The rest kept running in human form, feet pounding against the dirt, red eyes searching the forest.

The hunt had begun.

Jungkook held back for just a heartbeat, watching the others disappear into the forest like demons unleashed from hell itself.

Then he gave in. Every bit of control he'd held onto for the past three years slipped away.

His body cracked and reformed, muscles expanding as his wolf burst free with a growl that rolled across the entire clearing. His paws hit the earth running, dirt spraying behind him in great arcs as he bounded toward the treeline.

The cheers faded away behind him. All he could hear now was his own rough breathing, the running of blood in his ears, and the thumping of earth beneath his claws with each stride.

Scents hit him immediately. Blockers and sweat and terror—every omega trail is distinct even through the tangle of forest smells. Other alphas howled, crashing through bushes without care, chasing whatever smell they could latch onto first. The sounds of pursuit echoed through the trees, accompanied by the occasional shriek of an omega who'd been found.

Jungkook ignored them all. He wasn't here for just anyone.

He ran deeper into the woods, leaping fallen logs and splashing through streams, his nose working constantly as he searched through the chemical stink for one specific scent. The one that haunted his dreams and drove him half-mad with wanting. He was coming for Taehyung, and this time, nothing would stop him from claiming what was his.

In his tree, Taehyung shifted uncomfortably, pulling his thin jacket tighter around his shoulders even though it did nothing against the cold that seemed to seep into his bones. His chest ached from how hard his heart was beating. Howls were echoing between the trees, claws were scraping against bark, and footsteps were crashing through undergrowth as alphas searched for omegas to claim. Somewhere not far away, he heard a scream cut short, followed by a triumphant howl that made his stomach turn.

He pressed his eyes shut and tried to control his breathing, counting slowly the way Alpha Hyungwon had taught him years ago. He could not be distracted by the thought of other omegas being caught. Just had to wait it out. Two hours, maybe less if the alphas gave up early or if all the other omegas got claimed first. He'd done this six times before. He could manage it again. He always managed it.

Then he heard something that made his blood turn to ice water in his veins.

Thunder was rumbling in from the west, and with it, the first drops of rain began to fall through the canopy above.

His eyes flew open in pure horror.

Rain.

No. No, no, no.

It soaked through the leaves above him, and worst of all, it began washing across his skin. The scent blockers began to wash off, running down his arms with the rain. Little by little, the protection they'd given him disappeared. It didn't take long before he caught it. His own scent was slipping through the blockers, growing stronger by the second. Terror grabbed him by the throat and squeezed until he couldn't breathe.

Taehyung had thought the blockers would last. He'd thought he'd be safe up here, hidden in his tree like all the other years, but the rain was ruining everything, stripping away his only defense and leaving him exposed.

Somewhere in the forest, Jungkook froze mid-stride.

There.

He lifted his massive wolf head, nostrils flaring wide, ears twitching toward something only he could detect. Under the storm, under everything else, something filled the…the air like honey mixed with lightning.

Sweet and unmistakably Taehyung.

The scent hit his system. His wolf's chest rumbled with a growl. He turned sharply toward the source, claws digging deeper into the muddy ground as he ran faster than he'd ever run in his life. Trees blurred past, rain soaking his dark fur until it was stuck to his body.

Found you, he thought, lips pulling back from razor-sharp teeth in something that wasn't quite a smile.

But then another howl rose behind him. Too close for comfort.

Jungwon.

His brother had caught the same trail; of course he had. Jungwon had always coveted everything Jungkook showed interest in, from childhood toys to hunting grounds to omegas. Even at thirty-four, the man couldn't develop an original thought if his life depended on it. He was a shadow, always following, always trying to take what wasn't his.

The rain had made everything wet and messy. Jungkook hardly registered it, as he pushed forward through the woods, his feet sinking into the earth before sending him forward once more. Each second that passed saw the scent intensify even further, until it was sweet, right on his tongue, almost close enough to taste it. But then there were footsteps closing in on him. Another howl cut through the storm, sharp with challenge.

Jungwon.

The bastard actually thought he could steal what Jungkook had been searching for all this time. Jungkook's wolf growled and pushed harder, crashing through the wet ferns until he skidded to a stop beneath a massive oak. He lifted his nose, sorting through the scents carried by the rain.

There he was. His pretty boy.

Taehyung.

Curled on a branch twenty feet above him, breathing hard. The rain wasn't enough to hide his scent anymore. For a second, Jungkook didn't move. Three years of searching and coming up empty. He'd finally found him. He was fucking emotional.

Then claws scraped against the bark behind him.

Jungwon appeared from the dark, being somewhere between human and wolf shape. Wide shoulders, muscles built, a smile on the lips, but no sign of happiness about it. Water was dripping from his skin, but his eyes were glowing with desire and victory.

"Back off," he growled, his voice rough and barely recognizable in his half-shifted state. "I found him first."

Jungkook's wolf turned slowly. His golden eyes locked onto the sound, his lips curling back over his fangs. A growl rolled through the forest, sending a few leaves fluttering from the branches overhead.

Mine.

In the tree, Taehyung hugged the trunk, his eyes flickering from one alpha to the other, fear plain on his face. He quivered like a leaf ready to fall, momentarily thinking of climbing up or running away, despite the fact that there was no place left for him to run anymore. He knew what would happen next. Every single member of the pack knew the laws of the Claiming Moon. When two alphas wanted the same omega, there was only one way to settle it.

A challenge to the death.

And there was no running from it now.

First move by Jungwon. He attacked Jungkook with his claw, slicing the rain-laden air in an attempt to cut Jungkook’s neck. But at the very last minute, Jungkook managed to dodge the attack, biting Jungwon on his shoulder so hard that his bone cracked. Blood was mingled with rain, streaming down their wet bodies. Jungwon cried in anger and slammed into Jungkook.

A cracking sound resounded in the forest when Jungkook fell from the impact of the blow. They went down together, rolling through mud and rotting leaves, snarls tearing from both throats like chainsaws through wood.

Every bite was meant to kill. Every claw swipe aimed for something vital, for arteries and organs and anything that would end this quickly. Jungkook's wolf fought with three years of frustrated longing burning in his chest like a furnace. He hadn't clawed his way to Alpha just to let this spoiled bastard take the one thing he'd ever truly wanted.

Not Taehyung.

He would do anything to prevent that from happening and, if necessary, would shred Jungwon to pieces with his bare hands. His teeth sunk into Jungwon’s neck and bit as hard as possible, shaking his head as if trying to break off a branch. Jungwon screamed as he stabbed Jungkook’s side with his claws. The pain only fed the fire inside him, made him bite harder, made him want to win more.

They broke apart and circled each other, both breathing hard as they both bled from multiple wounds. The storm kep pouring down.

"You don't deserve him," Jungwon spat, blood streaming from his mouth and dripping off his chin.  "You're nothing but a murderer who stole what wasn't yours. You killed our father, he saved your pathetic life when you had nothing. How can you do that fucker?" Jungkook's answer was just a loud growl.

Jungwon attacked once again, but this time Jungkook was prepared. He defended himself, clashing his teeth with those of the other dog, ripping away at him with his claws. Again they tumbled in the mud, but this time Jungkook used his physical strength to get the upper hand and hold Jungwon down under him by sheer weight. He bit Jungwon's neck and did not release him from that position.

Jungwon thrashed and clawed, fighting with everything he had left, tearing new wounds into Jungkook's sides and chest. But Jungkook was stronger. His wolf was bigger; his need ran deeper than anything Jungwon could understand.

He held on.

The thrashing grew weaker. The claws that had been tearing into him slowed, then stopped. For a moment, all he could hear was the rain... and the rough, choking breaths that finally faded into nothing.

Jungkook loosened his hold, and the body of Jungwon fell limply into the muddy ground. His bleeding throat became stained by blood that mixed with water from the rain to form a puddle of black under the darkness. The fight had ended. Whether his brother was alive or dead, Jungkook really did not care.

The Pack Alpha had won.

Jungkook shifted back to human form, chest heaving like he'd run a marathon. Blood covered his mouth and torso, streaming down his arms in red as the rain tried to wash it away. "Found you, sweetheart." His voice was strangely gentle when he looked up at the figure trembling in the branches above.

"Three years I've been chasing you through these woods. Three years of watching you run and hide like you could escape this forever. Now I've finally got you."

Taehyung's fingers dug into the bark hard enough to draw blood from his own palms. His whole body shook like he might simply fall apart, like his bones had turned to water. The sight of Jungwon's motionless form, all that blood, and the raw brutality of what he'd just witnessed right in front of him froze every rational thought in his head.

He won’t come down no matter what.

"Stay hidden, pup," Jungkook growls up at the trembling omega, his voice dropping lower, "and I'll fuck you bloody the moment I drag you down from there."

Taehyung's breath hitches, fingers gripping the branch so hard his knuckles turn white. "Y-you wouldn't dare—"

"Try me."

Jungkook didn’t bother waiting for an answer. He just began his climb, using muscles rippling under his skin to pull himself up into the tree, even though the rain slickened its bark into a hazardous challenge. Water droplets ran down from his dark hair, but his eyes never left Taehyung’s face, never faltered in their purpose.

Before Taehyung knew what was happening, Jungkook was perched on the same branch, so close that even through the chill of the rain, he could feel warmth emanating from Jungkook's body. So close that he could see how big Jungkook’s pupils were and how he was drawing in Taehyung’s scent with every breath he took.

"You can't hide anymore," Jungkook whispered, his voice soft but somehow more dangerous for its gentleness. His hand reached out slowly, fingers impossibly warm against Taehyung's ice-cold cheek.

"It's time to come with me, omega. It's time to stop running." Taehyung caught his breath.

Below, rain continued to fall over Jungwon's still body. Somewhere farther away, other alphas were still searching; their howls could be heard in the forest.

Jungkook had finally found him after three years.

And Taehyung had run out of places to hide. He had nowhere left to run.

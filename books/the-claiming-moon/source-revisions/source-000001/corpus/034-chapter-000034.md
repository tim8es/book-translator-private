Yoongi pushed another shovel of dirt into the grave and clenched his jaw. There was nothing he could do about it now except make sure she wasn't alone.

When the grave was finally filled, silence settled over the clearing. They didn’t know what to with so much sorrow.

Yoongi pressed the back of his wrist against his eyes quickly and stepped away before he could think too much about why.

A few seconds later, footsteps could be heard through the trees. Both of them turned immediately, knives ready in their hands.

Jungkook burst out at a run and nearly skidded in the mud trying to stop. He was breathing hard, his hair stuck to his forehead from rain and sweat.

"Why did you leave me behind?"

"No."

Namjoon shook his head. "We were wrong."

The confusion that crossed Jungkook's face was immediate. "What?"

His face kept changing.

Shock. Disbelief. Horror.

And then, the most scary expression – Hope.

"She's alive," Namjoon said.

Jungkook nodded like he was trying to convince himself he'd heard correctly.

"Can I come?" The question came so fast that neither of them had time to prepare.

"Yes." – Namjoon

"No." – Yoongi

"Jee came here for Jungkook. Not for us, not for Red Moon, not for revenge. Him." He nodded toward Jungkook. "He's the reason she's still standing outside that border. If what the books said is true, then putting Jungkook in front of her might be enough."

For a long moment Yoongi didn't answer.

His eyes moved toward the fresh grave behind them, contemplating everything happening around. Sara was lying under there with Kihyun's bones wrapped in her arms, and the image was still sitting fresh in his head no matter how much he wanted it gone.

He looked back at his baby brothers. Then toward the silver knife still hanging loosely from his fingers.

Without a word, he drew his arm back and hurled it into the darkness. The knife vanished between the trees.

"Fuck," he muttered, already turning away. "If this goes wrong, I'm haunting both of you."

Namjoon's mouth twitched slightly.

The anger hadn't gone anywhere. It was so uncomfortable tangled together with grief and exhaustion and the growing realization that none of them had any idea what they were doing anymore.

Jungkook and Namjoon were taking this lightly. They do not seem to understand the repercussions.

Behind him, Namjoon watched him disappear through the trees before quietly shaking his head. Then he squeezed Jungkook's shoulder once and guided him forward.

"Come on."

Jee was exactly where they'd left her.

She remained on her knees near the border, motionless except for the occasional twitch of her fingers. The moment they crossed back into view, her head lifted.

Her eyes locked onto Namjoon first. The hatred was immediate.

If looks could kill, Namjoon would've dropped dead on the spot. Even after all this time, even with her mind falling apart around her, she clearly remembered exactly who had forced her onto her knees.

Then her nose twitched.

Jee froze.

Something changed. She turned her head slowly as her eyes slowly moved towards Jungkook.

The entire forest seemed to go quiet.

Jungkook had spent the last ten minutes preparing himself for this. He'd listened to Namjoon's explanation. He'd heard every detail and kept telling himself repeatedly that whatever was standing out here wasn't really his sister anymore.

The preparation lasted exactly three seconds before it shattered.

Taehyung hadn't exaggerated a single thing. She was horrifying to look at.

The skin along her face sat so tightly against the bone beneath it that her features looked unfamiliar. Her jaw seemed sharper than it should've been. Her hands looked worse. Every finger bone, every knuckle, every joint stood out clearly beneath the skin.

Those hands had brushed his hair back when he was little. They had smacked his chest whenever he skipped meals.

Those hands had dragged him away from Hyungwon more times than he could count.

He wanted her gone, and that wasn't a lie. He admitted it to himself that he hated Jee and never wanted to see her again.

He'd never wanted her wandering forests with blood on her mouth and her eating raw animals to survive; he'd never wanted the last version of her to look like something trapped halfway between life and death.

Whatever anger he'd carried for months suddenly felt very small compared to the thing standing in front of him now.

Jee sniffed the air again. Then, slowly, she stood.

The movement looked awkward, as though her body no longer remembered how it was supposed to work. Jungkook's eyes briefly dropped to the wolf carcass beside her before immediately looking away again.

The remains were impossible to ignore. "You ate it." The words left his mouth before he could stop them.

Jee looked at him and nodded. That was apparently all the explanation she intended to provide.

Jungkook had absolutely no idea what to do with that information. So he did what any reasonable person would do. He would think about it later.

Her head tilted, and then she tilted it the other way round.

Then back again.

She kept studying his face from different angles as though she couldn't quite believe what she was seeing.

Neither Yoongi nor Namjoon spoke.

"Kook." Jungkook's breath caught. It wasn't just the word; it was the voice. Beneath the distortion, parasite, and wrongness, it was still her.

His sister, though, not by blood. Not that it had ever mattered. He knew that voice better than his own.

Hearing it come out of her now felt like somebody had reached into his chest and squeezed.

Slowly, Jungkook crouched down in front of her. He forced himself not to look away.

Whatever happened next, she deserved better than that. She deserved his presence, deserved him looking at her, listening to her, and giving her the attention she'd crossed all the distance trying to find.

"I'm here," he said softly. "I'm your brother, Jee."

Jee's hand came up slowly and settled against his face.

The bones pressed sharply into his cheek, every knuckle and joint distinct beneath the thin layer of skin covering them. Jungkook held perfectly still despite the discomfort. She was trying to be gentle.

This much was clear from the cautious way that she handled him, and when she saw the smear of blood on his skin, she looked at him with something approaching an apology.

The problem was that it kept coming back.

It was stuck between her teeth and seeped out slowly no matter how much he cleaned. After a while he gave up trying to make her look presentable and simply folded the cloth and pressed it gently against her mouth. The entire situation felt absurdly familiar in a way that made his chest ache.

"My baby brother," Jee said softly.

"Yeah," Jungkook answered.

A strange sound escaped her, mostly a sad sigh, and then she shifted forward until she was climbing directly into his lap. The movement happened so naturally that neither of them seemed to think about it.

One second she was sitting across from him, and the next she was curled against his chest exactly the way she used to when they were younger and exhausted after long days.

Jungkook's arms closed around her automatically.

The smell hit him immediately. The rotten smell coming from her body and her mouth was stinking so badly. It clung to her clothes and skin and hair; breathing through his nose became impossible. He buried his face against the top of her head anyway and tightened his hold around her.

None of it mattered.

It didn't matter to him what she smelled like or what she'd been eating. It didn't matter how much time had passed or how ugly the distance between them had become.

She was in his arms and she was his sister, and that truth felt bigger than every argument they'd ever had.

"What happened to you?" he asked quietly, his voice muffled against her hair.

Jee made a small unhappy sound and lifted her head. Her eyes moved across his face slowly before landing on the tears he'd failed to hide, and immediately both of her hands came up again. She touched his cheeks with careful fingers and shook her head.

"No cry. No cry. No cry." The urgency in her voice made something twist painfully inside him.

"Okay," Jungkook whispered. "Okay."

He pressed his lips together and nodded because she wanted him to, then looked over her shoulder toward Namjoon. The question was already written all over his face before he even spoke it aloud.

"Can we get her back?"

Namjoon didn't answer immediately.

The hesitation alone was enough to make Jungkook's stomach drop. By the time Namjoon slowly shook his head, he already knew what the answer was going to be.

"No."

"The pages were torn," Namjoon said quietly. "Whatever was on them is gone. We searched everything. There wasn't anything left."

Jungkook closed his eyes briefly.

For the first time since arriving, the reality of what was happening settled properly inside him. Sara had gotten her ending. Sara had found the person she'd been looking for and finally let go. Jee wasn't doing that.

Jee was here. Breathing. Curled up in his lap like she'd decided she belonged there. He lowered his head and pressed a kiss against her hair.

"I love you, Jee," he said softly. "You know I do. And if you're tired now, you can go. You don't have to keep holding on. I'm here. I'm right here, and I'll stay until the end."

The words hurt to say, every single one of them, but he needed to do this for her sake.

A selfish part of him wanted to take them back the second they left his mouth. He didn't want her to die in his arms or this night to end with another grave.

He didn't want the Moon Goddess deciding that this was how things were supposed to go after everything they'd already survived.

Jee went very still. Jungkook waited.

The seconds turned to minutes and minutes to more minutes. She stayed right there, her head comfortably placed on his chest with his heart beating against her ears. There was nothing but calmness in the air; no struggle, no fear, no indication of someone getting ready to depart.

She simply looked content. Ten minutes passed that way.

The rain softened into drizzle overhead while Jungkook sat in the mud holding her. Yoongi and Namjoon remained nearby without speaking, both of them watching carefully as though expecting something to change at any moment.

Nothing did. Jee stayed exactly where she was.

The longer it went on, the clearer it became that this wasn't Sara's story. Sara had followed a memory and found death waiting for her. Jee had followed a memory and found the person she loved still alive, still talking, still holding her together.

There was nothing here encouraging her to let go. If anything, she seemed more determined to stay.

Yoongi's eyes drifted toward Namjoon. Namjoon looked back at him. They did this too many times today.

Neither of them needed to speak. The realization was already there.

Jungkook saw it happen and felt his stomach sink. He knew what they were thinking because he had reached the same conclusion himself. Whatever remained of Jee wasn't weakening.

It was settling in.

Slowly, Yoongi bent down and picked up the silver knife he'd thrown away earlier. He turned it over once in his hand, rainwater sliding down the blade while the forest around them fell silent. No animals or birds made sound whatsoever.

The second Jungkook noticed Yoongi bending down toward the knife, Jungkook panicked.

His body reacted before his brain managed to catch up. One moment he was sitting there holding Jee, and the next he was scrambling backward across the wet ground, dragging her with him until his shoulders slammed against the nearest tree. His arms locked around her immediately, holding her against his chest as though somebody were trying to take her away.

Because his brothers were gonna do that.

Yoongi stopped moving.

For a moment he simply stared at Jungkook, and then his eyes shifted toward Namjoon. The anger that crossed his face wasn't subtle. It had been simmering for nearly an hour now, building every time Jungkook refused to let go, every time Namjoon chose patience over practicality.

"You bloody fucker," Yoongi snapped. "This is exactly why I said he shouldn't be here."

Namjoon closed his eyes briefly.

The worst part was that neither of them was surprised. Somewhere deep down, Yoongi knew this was how Jungkook would react. Namjoon knew it too. They'd raised him.

They'd watched him love people with a level of devotion that bordered on self-destruction. Expecting him to sit quietly while they killed Jee had always been a fantasy.

The movement had disturbed her sleep, and she shifted slightly against him before settling again. Instantly his hold tightened. The relief that flooded him when he felt her breathing was embarrassingly strong, but he couldn't help it.

She was still here, still warm and breathing in his lap.

"She's my sister," he said quietly.

Yoongi let out a harsh laugh that contained absolutely no amusement.

"She's also the same sister who grabbed your mate by the ankle three hours ago." His voice came out flat. "I honestly thought you'd be more protective of Taehyung than whatever the hell you're doing right now."

The words landed exactly where they were supposed to. Jungkook's jaw tightened immediately. His eyes burned.

For a second he couldn't even look at them. Because the worst part was that Yoongi wasn't wrong. Taehyung was terrified.

Taehyung nearly died.

The memory flashed through his head so vividly that his stomach twisted. He remembered Tae shaking afterward. Remembered carrying him. Remembered promising himself that nobody would ever get close enough to hurt him again.

But this was Jee.

And every time he looked down at her, every time she called him Kook or climbed into his lap or touched his face the way she used to when he was little, something inside him completely fell apart.

The strands were tangled and dirty and damp from the rain, but he kept smoothing them back anyway. The motion soothed him almost as much as it seemed to soothe her.

"But I can't do this."

The confession slipped out before he could stop it. His throat tightened. "I can't lose her too."

Silence settled over the clearing.

The rain continued falling softly through the branches overhead while nobody seemed entirely sure what to say next. Yoongi's grip tightened around the knife before loosening again.

The anger was still there. Jungkook could see it. But beneath it sat something else. Understanding.

Because out of everyone standing here, Yoongi knew exactly how much death Jungkook had already carried. He had watched his parents die.

He had killed the man who raised him.

He spent years hearing people spit the word "killer" at him as though it were his entire identity. Every time he'd managed to find something good and something worth holding onto, life had found a way to rip it away again.

Maybe this wasn't rational or smart. But for once, Jungkook wanted to keep something. Even if it was only for a little while.

"You're a pain in the ass," he muttered. Jungkook almost smiled.

The desperation in his own voice embarrassed him. He didn't care.

Maybe because it felt like helping. Maybe because he couldn't fix any of the things that actually mattered.

A breath.

"I know."

Something snapped inside Yoongi this time. His patience was worn thin. Jungkook saw it happen.

"Stop trying to make this gentle. Stop trying to make this more difficult than it is. Just end it." His voice cracked with anger.

"Stop pretending you're not capable of it when we both know you've done worse."

The silence afterward felt dangerous. Jungkook's eyes finally spilled over.

He pressed his mouth together so hard it hurt, trying to keep control of himself, but the tears came anyway. Yoongi just wounded him where it hurts the most.

The metal felt heavier than it should have, and when he looked down at Jee sleeping peacefully against his chest, he found himself nodding slightly as though he were trying to convince his own body to cooperate.

He could do this. He had done harder things than this.

He had managed to get through experiences that should have crippled him. He knew exactly where to place the knife and precisely how much force he would need. Slitting the throat was fast. No pain. No agony. The quickest way possible to die. And he would be done in a second so that afterwards, he could grieve for her instead of seeing her fall to pieces before his eyes.

She used to cook for him when he was still too little to cut up anything with a knife. She used to call him by name whenever she was happy to see him. She used to put herself between him and Hyungwon too many times for him to have ever started keeping count, accepting the punishment herself rather than letting it touch him. Before Jungkook even knew how to defend himself, Jee had been defending him.

His vision blurred, but the knife never moved.

"Jungkook-ah." Yoongi was so, so done for tonight.

"I'm t-trying, hyung," Jungkook whispered, and the crack in his voice made the words almost painful to hear. "I really am. Just give me a second. I'm trying."

He drew a breath, then brought everything within him into action — all his resolve, all his knowledge of pain and loss, all the vows he’d made himself about how he would hold fast. He gave the knife just a little pressure, and then did not move back.

It was when Jee moved that he reacted.

The movement was slight, just enough to jostle the blade, but Jungkook knew immediately what had happened, and his whole body froze as he watched those eyes slowly open. For a single, terrified heartbeat, he thought he was meeting her gaze.

"Jee," he said softly. Something flickered across her face.

She'd known him a second ago; he was sure of it, because she'd called him Kook, curled up in his lap, touched his face, and told him not to cry, and now she was looking at him like she had no idea who he was.

"Jee," he tried again, but she wasn't listening.

"Mate," she said, the word tumbling out, urgent and confused. "Mate. Mate. Where's mate? Where?"

Every trace of calm she'd shown while sitting with him was gone now, replaced by the same restless desperation she'd carried when they first found her.

He lifted a hand to the back of his head and stared at the blood coating his fingers before his attention snapped back to Jee. He sat back down immediately.

Yoongi was already moving.

The knife flashed as he charged forward, but Jee turned and shoved him too before he could reach her. The movement looked almost effortless, yet it sent Yoongi crashing to the ground hard enough that he stayed there for a second, clearly stunned.

Then she stopped.

Because she simply lost interest in all this drama. Jungkook watched her stare at Namjoon for several long seconds while Namjoon carefully backed away with his hands visible, and when she finally turned away from him as well, a horrible realization began settling inside Jungkook's chest.

She wasn't looking for any of them; she had forgotten them.

Slowly, Jee made her way toward the barrier and crouched near its edge. Her face lowered almost to the ground as she followed the scent trail back and forth through the wet grass, sniffing desperately for her mate.

But Sara was dead and was buried with Kihyun.

All he could do was watching her search and understand, with a sick feeling settling deeper and deeper into his chest, that for a few brief minutes he'd had his sister back.

The second he saw an opening, he took it. He came in low and fast from her blind side while she was still crouched near the barrier, her attention fixed entirely on the scent trail she was desperately trying to follow.

His breath got knocked out of him before he even hit the floor.

Unlike Yoongi, Namjoon didn't move straight away. He waited until she turned, then crossed the distance in a few quick steps with the second silver knife already in his hand. She barely had time to look at him before he drove it into her stomach.

Her whole body jerked.

The sound that came out of her was not sounding human anymore. She looked down at the knife sticking out of her stomach, then slowly lifted both hands and pressed them over the wound.

The expression that crossed her face made Namjoon step back. Confusion came first. Then recognition. Then something frighteningly close to horror.

For one brief moment, she looked like somebody waking from a nightmare only to realize the nightmare was real. Slowly, she sank to the ground.

Her attention drifted back toward the barrier almost immediately. The silver was already working through her system, dark veins spreading outward from the wounds, yet she continued breathing through the pain as though sheer stubbornness were refusing to let her die.

The fight seemed to drain everything out of all of them. Yoongi eventually managed to sit upright, one arm wrapped protectively around his ribs while he focused on breathing without passing out.

Jungkook remained where he'd fallen beneath the tree, blood dried along the side of his neck, while Namjoon stood somewhere between them.

The waiting began. They waited for her to die. Time passed strangely after that.

Despite two silver wounds slowly poisoning her from the inside, Jee simply refused to die. Every now and then she made a small sound of discomfort or shifted slightly against the ground, but the steady rise and fall of her chest never stopped.

An hour passed, or maybe longer; they couldn’t even fathom it any longer.

Yoongi had spent most of that time staring at her with growing irritation because she was making a mockery of everything he knew about silver. Any normal person would've died long ago. Any normal wolf would've died long ago, yet somehow she continued clinging stubbornly to life.

Then they heard footsteps.

Yoongi's hand dropped toward the knife at his side while Namjoon straightened, already preparing for another problem. Even Jungkook pushed himself more upright against the tree, his body tensing despite the dizziness still lingering from the blow to his head.

Then Hoseok emerged from the treeline. Yoongi immediately let out a long breath and slumped backward.

The color drained from his face. "What happened?" The question came out instantly.

Then his attention shifted and he saw her.

Yoongi caught the reaction.

Hoseok flinched for a split second before he hid it again. Yoongi had never seen him react like that before. He'd treated countless injuries over the years, but he still hadn't been ready for this. Jee heard the voices. And her eyes opened.

Yoongi looked over at her and felt something cold crawl down his spine because there was absolutely no fucking way she should still be conscious.

The force of it actually made him stagger. Then she wrapped herself around him.

Her hands clutched desperately at his shirt while she buried her face against his chest. Her entire body shook with relief, small tremors running through her shoulders as though she'd finally stopped holding herself together.

"Mate," she whispered into the fabric. The word came out broken, small, and relieved.

"Mate. Where were you?"

Her grip tightened. "Missed you. Missed you. Missed you."

Their jaws dropped down. Because every single person standing there understood the same thing at once. What the fuck did she just say?

Yoongi went completely still. For a second he genuinely thought he'd misheard her. His eyes immediately found Namjoon, searching for some sign that somebody else had heard something different, but Namjoon looked just as stunned as he felt.

Jungkook had gone utterly blank too, his expression frozen somewhere between confusion and disbelief, and none of them seemed capable of putting words together fast enough to catch up with what had just happened.

It spread slowly through all of them as their brains tried to process the sight in front of them.

Because while they were still stuck on the word she'd used, Hoseok had already reacted.

The second Jee collided with him, his arms came around her automatically, one hand pressing firmly against the back of her head while the other wrapped around her shoulders, holding her against him as though letting go wasn't even an option.

Yoongi had known Hoseok for most of his life. He was his brother for fuck’s sake.

He had seen him furious enough to tear through walls, exhausted enough to collapse where he stood, and devastated enough to lock himself away for days.

What crossed Hoseok's face now didn't resemble any of those things. It was something rawer than grief and deeper than shock, something so nakedly painful that Yoongi almost felt uncomfortable looking at it.

Then Hoseok whimpered.

It was small enough that Yoongi almost missed it beneath the rain, but it was there. Not a word. Not even close to one.

It sounded frighteningly close to a whimper, and the realization hit Yoongi so hard that he forgot about his ribs for a second. He had never heard a sound like that come out of Hoseok before. Not even once.

His gaze dropped to Jee again and then immediately returned to Hoseok. Nothing about this made sense. Jee was Sara's mate.

Everything they'd learned tonight had reinforced that understanding, so seeing Jee wrapped around Hoseok while calling him mate felt like somebody had picked up reality and twisted it into something they couldn’t quite understand.

"What did you do to yourself?" Hoseok asked quietly. The words sounded dragged out of him against his will. When he pressed his mouth against Jee's forehead, Yoongi immediately looked away, his jaw tightening as though he'd accidentally witnessed something private.

Yoongi glanced over and discovered that Namjoon looked just as disturbed as he felt. Neither of them understood what they were seeing. It wasn't simply the affection. It was the instinct behind it. The complete absence of hesitation.

Hoseok wasn't behaving like he usually did around them. He was behaving like a man watching his entire world bleed out in front of him.

"Sara was her mate," Namjoon finally voiced out.

"Sara was her mate. We all know Sara was her mate. So how is she..." His voice faltered. "How?"

Hoseok finally lifted his head, and tears were already streaming down his face.

That sight alone was enough to make Jungkook stare openly. Even Yoongi felt something strange twist in his stomach because Hoseok didn't cry. Almost never seen him cry. Even while their parents were dying.

"Fated mate," Hoseok said quietly. The answer somehow made everything worse.

Nobody spoke for several seconds because there was too much wrong with that sentence.

They found themselves staring at Hoseok like he was a stranger because the words refused to fit into anything he knew.

Fated mate.

Jee's fated mate.

Hoseok.

Their Hoseok.

The man nobody had ever once associated with Jee in that way. The idea felt absurd. Impossible. Completely insane.

"What the actual fuck are you talking about?" Yoongi asked.

Hoseok didn't answer immediately. Instead, he lowered himself carefully into the wet grass with Jee still held against his chest, keeping one arm around her the entire time. that only made the three of them stare harder.

When he reached behind her and found the knife still lodged in her back, his face pained visibly, and he worked it free with care before pressing part of his cloak against the wound.

Jee made a broken sound and immediately buried herself deeper against him. The reaction was instant. Like she trusted him without question. So she'd been looking for him all along.

"It's okay," Hoseok murmured. "I've got you. You're safe. Just keep breathing." His hand never stopped moving over her back.

The words seemed to calm her almost immediately. The pained sounds gradually faded, the tension left her shoulders, and after several minutes her breathing finally began to even out.

Hoseok checked it automatically with one hand against her chest and then rested his cheek on top of her head, closing his eyes for a moment as though simply feeling her breathe was keeping him upright.

Yoongi watched the entire thing in horrified silence.

The longer he looked, the worse it became. Every instinct Hoseok showed was wrong. Every touch was wrong. Every reaction was wrong.

Because it was genuine. There wasn't a trace of uncertainty anywhere in him. He wasn't acting confused or questioning it. What the actual fuck? They were all so sure that Hosoek hadn't found his mate yet.

"You're a fucking traitor," Yoongi said at last.

The accusation came out because he genuinely didn't know what else to call this. He felt betrayed by information itself. Betrayed by reality. Betrayed by the fact that somehow his own brother would hide something this big for years.

Hoseok didn't even look up. He simply tightened his arm around Jee slightly and kept his attention on her.

"Call me whatever you want," he said quietly. "I don't care."

"What the actual hell just happened, Hobi. From the beginning."

Hoseok was quiet for several seconds. He pushed damp strands of hair away from Jee's face with two fingers and left his hand there afterward, his palm resting against her cheek while he looked at her. "The first time she came here with Jungkook," he said eventually.

"When Jungkook was fifteen, maybe sixteen. She came with him, and I met her, and I knew immediately. I was the first one out of all of us to find a mate."

Nobody interrupted him. Jungkook was still staring, trying and failing to fit that information into anything he knew about the last several years.

Looking back, he couldn't remember a single moment that had hinted at this. Not one conversation or look. Not one thing that should have prepared him for hearing it now.

"I told her," Hoseok continued quietly. "Told her what she was to me and what I wanted. Asked her if she'd stay here, and if she didn't want that, I was willing to go with her instead. Moon Village, Red Moon, I didn't care where we lived. I just..." His voice caught briefly before he continued. "She told me she only loved women, so I let her go."

"Just like that?" Namjoon asked before he could stop himself. The answer felt impossible because people didn't usually survive things like that with their hearts intact.

"Just like that," Hoseok said. He finally looked up, and there wasn't a trace of bitterness on his face.

"If you love somebody enough, you let them live the life they actually want, Namjoon. Even when it costs you."

Jungkook felt something twist painfully inside his chest. It wasn't even the revelation itself anymore. It was the realization that Hoseok had apparently carried this alone for years.

The fact that he kept chasing Taehyung until finally he liked him back and hearing Hoseok give up this easily, he didn’t know what to feel.

"And you never said anything?" Jungkook asked.

Hoseok's eyes shifted toward him. "What was there to say? She made her choice. She wasn't mine in any way that mattered."

Yoongi let out a low, irritated sound from where he was sitting with one arm wrapped around his ribs. "And now all of a sudden you care? Didn't give a damn all these years, but now that she's dying, you want to play mate?"

Hoseok didn't even look in his direction. His attention stayed fixed on Jee while his fingers carefully threaded through hers, the bones in her hand looking disturbingly fragile against his own.

"You'd burn the world down for your mate, Yoongi. You've been ready to burn it down since the day you found Jimin, and everybody knows it. Nobody calls you selfish for that." His thumb moved slowly across Jee's knuckles.

"But when it's me, suddenly mates don't matter. So who's actually being selfish here?"

The words landed harder than Yoongi expected them to. He opened his mouth, ready with the argument he'd been building but he didn't actually have one. After a few awkward seconds he cleared his throat and looked away.

"I didn't mean it like that."

Hoseok didn't answer. He simply looked down at Jee again, watching the shallow rise and fall of her chest with an attention that bordered on obsessive, and for the first time since this entire disaster started, Yoongi felt the last of his anger begin to drain away.

"Can we not kill her?" Hoseok asked quietly after a while. When he finally looked up, there was nothing defensive left in him. "Please. Is there any way?"

"Yes," Jungkook said immediately.

Namjoon looked from Hoseok to Jungkook and then down at Jee. Beyond them, the barrier shimmered faintly through the trees, and with it came the reminder of what actually rested on his shoulders. An entire pack. Hundreds of lives. Hundreds of people depending on him to make the correct choice.

"I have to think about the pack first, Hobi," he said eventually. "You know that. Pack comes first."

"I know." The answer came immediately because of course he knew. That was the worst part.

Hoseok understood exactly why Namjoon couldn't promise him anything, and somehow that made the silence afterward feel even heavier.

Yoongi leaned his head back against the tree and stared up through the branches above him. His ribs hurt. Jungkook was bleeding from the head. Jee had survived silver for far longer than she should have.

Apparently Hoseok had found his fated mate years ago and told absolutely nobody. At this point, figuring out what was wrong with Jungkook's head felt significantly easier than figuring out what the hell they were supposed to do next.

"She's not coming into the palace," Yoongi said. "That's final."

The pain in his ribs, the blood on Jungkook's head, the fact that his entire understanding of the last several years had just been flipped upside down. none of that mattered right now.

There was only one thing sitting at the center of his mind. Jimin was pregnant. Jimin was carrying their child. As far as Yoongi was concerned, every other problem in the world could wait behind that.

Hoseok looked up at him without anger.

"The Hollowed doesn't spread that way, Yoongi. It only passes from wolf to werewolf through a specific transmission. It isn't sitting in the air waiting to infect people. It doesn't work like that."

"I don't care how it works," Yoongi replied immediately. "My mate is pregnant. No."

For a second it looked like Hoseok might argue. The words reached his mouth and stopped there. Then he glanced down at Jee curled against him and simply nodded once.

"Okay." The acceptance caught all of them slightly off guard.

There was no fight in it. No attempt to convince anybody. Hoseok understood exactly where Yoongi was coming from, probably because if their positions had been reversed, he would've done the exact same thing. Instead, he shifted his attention toward Namjoon.

"Is the jail empty right now?"

Namjoon blinked. "Why?"

"Is it empty?"

"Yes, but—"

"Then I'm taking her there."

"I'm a healer. That's not an accident. I didn't choose it. The Moon Goddess gave me those abilities for a reason, and if there's any way to bring her back, any way at all, then I'm going to find it." His arm tightened slightly around Jee. "I need somewhere to work from. Let me try."

Namjoon looked at him for a long time.

His gaze moved from Hoseok to Jee and then back again. He took in the knife wound in her back, the silver that should have killed her hours ago, and the way Hoseok was holding her like letting go wasn't an option. Beyond all of that sat a much larger problem. He wasn't just a brother right now. He was an Alpha.

"I can allow that," he said eventually. "Lock it down properly. Nobody goes in without permission. I'll send food, blankets, supplies, or whatever you need. You go to the other side of the jail. I just got it cleaned yesterday so It’s all nice and ready already."

"You don't leave until there's a resolution," Namjoon said.

Relief flashed across Hoseok's face so quickly that most people would've missed it. "Thank you," he said. His voice came out very thankful. "Thank you, Namjoon-ah."

"And you don't set foot in that palace," Yoongi added immediately.

The older alpha was still sitting against the tree with one arm wrapped around his ribs, but there wasn't a trace of hesitation in his voice. "Not until she's cured or she's dead. Those are the terms."

Hoseok met his eyes and kept staring at his brother for a moment before he eventually nodded.

"I won't. I give you my word."

Yoongi held the stare a moment longer before finally looking away. That was enough for him. Hoseok didn't make promises lightly, and everybody standing here knew it.

The entire discussion had gone completely unnoticed by Jee.

She had apparently decided that none of it was her problem. While the rest of them argued about all the matters, she was lying with her head in Hoseok's lap and slowly swinging her feet back and forth through the wet grass.

Her attention seemed completely absorbed by the movement of her own ankles, as though she'd just discovered they existed and found the whole thing fascinating.

The sight was unsettling: two stab wounds, hours of silver poisoning.

She should have been long dead. And she was sitting there entertaining herself with her own feet.

Hoseok looked down at her and shook his head slightly.

"Wolf," she announced suddenly.

Her head tipped back so she could look up at him upside down. Then she pressed two fingers against the side of her throat and made an exaggerated biting motion. "Bite bite."

"Yeah," Hoseok said. "I can see that."

She seemed pleased that he'd understood.

With a series of slow movements accompanied by enough creaking joints to make Jungkook visibly uncomfortable, she pushed herself upright and climbed toward him. Before anybody could react, she pressed her mouth against the side of his neck.

"Missed you," she mumbled against his skin. "Missed you. Missed you. Mate mate mate mate."

Then she giggled. The sound was bizarrely cheerful given the circumstances. Hoseok closed his eyes.

For a second his entire face tightened, and Yoongi had the uncomfortable feeling he was watching somebody hold back years worth of grief.

"I missed you so much more," Hoseok whispered. "You have absolutely no idea."

Apparently satisfied with that answer, Jee settled herself against his chest again.

Hoseok carefully got to his feet, adjusting her weight in his arms until he had a secure hold on her. Even exhausted and injured, she immediately curled toward him, pressing her face against his shoulder as though she'd found the only place she wanted to be.

Without another word, he started walking toward the treeline.

Namjoon followed after him at a quick pace, already slipping back into Alpha mode as he called instructions ahead.

For the first time all night, it was just Yoongi and Jungkook left behind with the rain, the silence, and far too many things to think about.

Jungkook was still sitting with his back against the tree, one hand pressed to the back of his head where the blood had mostly stopped but definitely hadn't healed, and the second Hoseok disappeared into the trees, Yoongi pushed himself upright and crossed the clearing toward him.

His ribs immediately complained and hurt like a bitch, but he ignored them. He had bigger problems. Jee was gone. Hoseok was gone. The adrenaline was wearing off.

Now he could finally pay attention to the fact that Jungkook had taken a hit to the head hard enough to leave him bleeding in the dirt.

"Kook-ah. Look at me." Jungkook's eyes lifted slowly.

For a second Yoongi tracked them carefully, watching for confusion, watching for that distant look people got after bad head injuries. Concussion as Hoseok says.

"You called me a murderer, hyung."

Yoongi immediately closed his eyes.

Of all the things.

Out of everything that had happened tonight, out of the Hollowed, the books, the mates, the silver, the blood, somehow Jungkook's brain had grabbed hold of that one sentence and refused to let it go.

"You spent years telling me I wasn't one," Jungkook continued. His voice wasn't angry. If anything, it sounded exhausted. "Every time somebody said it, you argued with them. Every time I said it, you argued with me. You spent years telling me it wasn't true."

His eyes dropped to the ground. "Then tonight you said it yourself." The guilt hit so hard that Yoongi physically felt it.

"I know." The words sounded pathetic the second they left his mouth.

For once there wasn't a defense waiting behind them. No clever explanation. No excuse. Just guilt.

"I know, Kook-ah."

Jungkook let out a short laugh that wasn't remotely amused and tipped his head back against the tree.

"You're being a hypocrite." That actually made Yoongi stare at him.

"I am being a hypocrite?" he repeated. "All I've done tonight is run around making sure everybody survives, and somehow I'm the hypocrite?"

The argument would've had more force behind it if Jungkook hadn't chosen that exact moment to stand up.

He stood up all alone, stubbornly. For one glorious second it looked like it was going to work. Then the world tilted underneath him.

Yoongi was moving before he could think, grabbing his arm to steady him, but Jungkook immediately shook him off and continued walking as though nothing had happened.

The rejection wasn't violent, but everything shattered in Yoongi’s heart.

If Jungkook had yelled at him, Yoongi would've known how to handle it. If he'd punched him, they'd have been fine by breakfast. Instead, he simply removed Yoongi's hand and kept moving, and somehow that hurt worse.

They started the walk back toward the palace together.

The rain had mostly stopped by now; mud pulled at their boots with every step, and the silence between them seemed to grow heavier the closer they got to home.

The bleeding worried Yoongi.

He could see it every time Jungkook turned his head. Dried blood around his neck and collar was way more than it should be.

He wanted to say something about it. Wanted to check the wound again. After getting his hand knocked away, he wasn't sure he was allowed.

"Hyung's sorry." The apology slipped out quietly. There was no performance behind it. No expectation that it would magically fix anything. Yoongi simply needed Jungkook to hear it because the guilt had become unbearable.

Jungkook kept walking. "I don't want your sorry."

The answer landed like a punch.

Yoongi reached out automatically when Jungkook nearly slipped on a patch of uneven ground, and once again Jungkook knocked his hand away Itw as clear he was avoiiding to talk to Yoongi..

Something inside Yoongi cracked. It had nothing to do with his ribs "Don't do that," Yoongi literally sobbed out. "Don't push me away."

The words sounded far more desperate than he'd intended. "I said I was sorry." He wiped at his eyes aggressively. He can’t remember the last time he had cried.

Jungkook didn't answer. That somehow made it worse. Yoongi swallowed hard and wiped angrily at his face before the tears could become obvious.

"You're my kid, Jungkook-ah." The confession slipped out before he could stop it. "You don't get to push me away."

Because that was the truth of it. Jungkook was his baby. Everybody knew that. It didn't matter. Jungkook stayed silent for the rest of the walk.

The palace eventually appeared through the trees. They climbed the stairs without speaking.

Jungkook headed directly toward his room while Yoongi followed several steps behind, refusing to leave him alone until somebody looked at that head wound properly. At this point Jungkook could complain all he wanted; Yoongi wasn't going anywhere.

Taehyung and Jimin were sitting by the window with tea when the door opened. They had been talking quietly while waiting for everyone to come back, but both of them looked up immediately at the sound, and Taehyung's entire expression changed the second his eyes landed on Jungkook.

He was already standing before he'd fully processed what he was seeing, setting his cup down too quickly as his gaze swept over the dried blood, the exhaustion written all over his face, and the way he seemed to be holding himself together through sheer stubbornness.

"Kook?" The word came out more like a question.

Taehyung took two quick steps forward, searching his face for answers and finding none of the ones he wanted. "What happened?" Jungkook opened his mouth and gave him the most predictable answer possible.

"I'm fine, baby."

The lie was so obvious that neither Taehyung nor Jimin bothered acknowledging it. Taehyung's eyes immediately shifted to Yoongi instead, silently demanding an explanation, but all he got in return was a tired look and a quiet, "Long night."

Whatever had happened out there clearly wasn't something that could be explained in a sentence.

Without wasting another second, Taehyung moved to Jungkook's side and placed a careful hand on his elbow, guiding him toward the bed.

What made Yoongi's chest tighten painfully was how easily Jungkook allowed it. He had spent half the night fighting everyone who tried to help him, shrugging off hands and pretending he was fine, but he followed Taehyung without resistance and practically dropped onto the mattress the moment he reached it.

Taehyung turned his head gently to inspect the injury and immediately went quiet. The wound wasn't life-threatening, but there was enough blood to make his stomach drop, and for a second he simply stared before forcing himself to breathe normally.

Jungkook knew that silence. It was the one Taehyung always fell into when he was actively choosing not to panic.

He reached up and pressed a kiss against Taehyung's forehead. "You okay, pup?"

Taehyung nearly laughed from sheer disbelief. Jungkook was sitting there covered in blood and looking like he'd collapse if somebody breathed on him too hard, and somehow he was still checking on everybody else first.

"I'm fine," he said firmly, already moving toward the shelf. "Stop talking and sit still."

For once, Taehyung shoved every emotional question to the side and focused on something practical. He grabbed the sewing kit, brought it back to the bed, and immediately started cleaning the wound with careful hands.

The methodical process gave him something to focus on besides the growing dread in his chest, and Jungkook sat quietly through all of it, allowing himself to be cared for in a way that made Taehyung even more worried.

After a minute Jungkook pointed toward Yoongi without even looking up. "Tell him to leave."

Taehyung paused mid-motion and looked between them. Suddenly the tension in the room made a lot more sense.

Yoongi looked devastated, just completely wrecked. Taehyung had almost never seen him like this before. For a second he genuinely thought he'd turn around and walk out.

Yoongi moved across the room, however, and sank down to the floor next to the bed. Each move seemed to cause pain, either from his broken ribs or some deep-seated source. But before anyone could do anything about it, he bent down and rested his cheek on Jungkook’s.

He pressed a few kisses over Jungkook’s cheek. He rested his forehead on the side of Jungkook’s face, and when he finally backed away, he had tears in his eyes.

"Hyung loves you," he whispered quietly. "I love you so much. I'm sorry, baby."

His voice cracked badly on the last few words, but he kept going anyway, pressing another brief kiss against Jungkook's cheek as though apologizing enough times might somehow undo whatever damage had already been done.

Jungkook didn't answer. He didn't forgive him, didn't comfort him, and didn't say a single word.

But he also didn't pull away this time, and judging by the way Yoongi's shoulders loosened afterward, that tiny mercy was enough. It wasn't forgiveness, but didn’t feel like rejection either.

After a moment Yoongi pushed himself back to his feet and went to Jimin. His entire demeanor softened the second he reached him, one hand settling carefully against his back as he helped him stand.

Jimin looked up at him with tired eyes, clearly full of questions, but he didn't ask a single one of them.

Yoongi pressed a quick kiss against his temple and guided him toward the door. Behind him, he could already hear Taehyung asking what happened in a quieter voice and Jungkook answering that it was a long story, that everybody was safe, and he’d explain everything later.

A second later there was the unmistakable pain of the needle going in, followed immediately by Jungkook hissing through his teeth.

"There he is," Taehyung muttered. "Big scary alpha."

Outside the room, Yoongi kept one hand pressed against his ribs as he walked slowly beside Jimin. The injury on Jungkook's head wasn't silver. He repeated that fact to himself over and over again because it mattered.

By morning it would already be healing, and by tomorrow Jungkook would be back to pretending nothing had happened. He would be fine. They would all be fine.

At least he hopes so…

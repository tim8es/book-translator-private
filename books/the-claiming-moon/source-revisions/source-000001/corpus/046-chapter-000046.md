He sat back on his heels and gave Jungkook space. He smiled, even though his eyes were wet. He can be mature for his alpha.

"Alpha," he said again, very softly.

Jungkook stared at him, and the silence stretched until Taehyung was sure he would never answer. Then Jungkook looked away from him and up at Yoongi, who was standing near the door.

"Why did you switch off the lights?" His voice was hoarse and small. "And why did you leave me alone? You said you wouldn't leave me alone."

He sounded hurt, and that hurt Yoongi more than any of the screams had. Yoongi opened his mouth, but he didn't know what to say. He had only turned off the light so Jungkook could sleep better. He should have known that darkness would haunt him for a while now.

Out of the corner of his eye, Yoongi saw Jin hovering at the gate, trying to peek in. Yoongi gave him a hard look, and Jin stepped back at once and disappeared down the hall.

Yoongi crouched and pulled a thick rug from the chair near the wall. He spread it on the floor close to the corner.

"Sit on this," he said gently. "It's too cold down there for you."

Jungkook looked at the rug for a second. Then he crawled out of the corner and sat down on it. He still listened to his hyung, even now.

Taehyung moved closer and sat on the other side of the rug. He didn't reach for him. He didn't want to overwhelm him. He just rested his hands over his bump and sat quietly, and for the first time in a month he felt like he could breathe properly.

Jungkook kept staring at his face. He stared for so long that Taehyung started to feel nervous, and Yoongi could not tell what was going on behind those eyes.

Yoongi knelt beside Jungkook and combed his hair back from his face with his fingers. He kissed his cheek.

"That's your mate, baby," he said quietly.

Jungkook turned his head toward Yoongi slowly. "I have a mate?"

Taehyung nodded right away. Tears spilled down his cheeks, and he didn't bother to wipe them. He had imagined this moment a hundred times in the past month, and he had never once imagined that Jungkook would not know his name.

"You love me the most. And I love you the most."

Taehyung broke down completely. He sobbed into Jungkook's hair and held him tighter, and Jungkook held on just as hard. Jungkook pulled back a little and looked at him with uncertain eyes.

"You're my pup?"

"I'm your pup," Taehyung said, laughing through his tears. "I'm your pup. And you’re my baby alpha."

Jungkook let out a shaky laugh of his own, and then he cried a little more. "I remember you," he whispered. "Only a little. But I remember you."

Taehyung pulled him back into his arms and held him there. Jungkook was almost as light as Taehyung was now, and it broke his heart all over again. His alpha used to be so strong and so well cared for, and now he felt like something that might blow away if the wind changed.

Taehyung pressed his face into Jungkook's neck and held on. He was not going to let go, not now and not ever. He was going to heal Jungkook. Jungkook's eyes landed on the mating mark on Taehyung’s neck. He reached up and touched it with two fingers, very lightly, as if it might bruise.

"I gave this?"

Taehyung nodded and tilted his head to make it easier for him. Jungkook pressed his fingertips against the mark again, and each time he seemed a little more sure of it.

Then his eyes moved down. The chain of the necklace lay against Taehyung's collarbone, and the blue stone sat in the hollow of his throat. Jungkook gasped.

"Match," he said. He touched his own chest, where the same necklace had left its marks on his skin, and then he touched Taehyung's. "We match."

Something shifted in his face, and Taehyung saw the memory come up behind his eyes. It was the only thing Jungkook had held onto since waking up.

He remembered someone pressing a seashell bracelet into his palm and someone fastening this necklace around his neck. He had clung to those two things the whole time he was in the storage room, and they were the reason he had not given up completely.

Jungkook let Taehyung kiss his cheek this time, and then he picked up the blue stone and put it in his mouth. He sucked on it quietly, his eyes half closed, the way a child does with a comfort toy. Taehyung wiped his own tears away with his sleeve and kissed the top of Jungkook's head.

He didn't say anything. He just let him have it. After a while Jungkook took the stone out of his mouth and spoke without looking up. "I feel so shitty."

"Why, my love?" Taehyung asked softly.

"Because I don't remember you." Jungkook's voice cracked. "And I want to remember you. I really want to."

Taehyung bent his face down and kissed his cheek. "Then I'll remind you every single day for the rest of our lives. Maybe it will help, and maybe it won't. But I'll tell you everything, alpha. Every little thing about us."

Jungkook lifted his head. His eyes were full again. "Are you not disgusted by me?" He looked down at his own arms, at the cuts and the bruises and the chain marks. "Look at me. I'm covered in scars."

Taehyung shook his head before Jungkook had even finished. "No. You're my sweet mate. I could never hate you, not for anything. We'll heal together, okay?"

Jungkook seemed to think about it for a long moment. Then he nodded.

Yoongi came back into the room carrying a thick duvet and shook it out over the bed, then glanced at the two of them on the floor. It was freezing in the room, and he did not want Jungkook's pneumonia getting any worse. He picked up the duvet and put it on the floor instead.

Jungkook started to lower himself down onto the rug, then stopped with a hiss as his back pulled. He looked up at Taehyung.

"Lie with me?" he asked.

Taehyung lay down beside him at once. Jungkook tried to curl into his side, but the movement pulled at his marks, and he hissed again and froze. Taehyung didn't rush him. He stayed where he was and waited, one hand resting lightly on Jungkook's back, until Jungkook let out a breath and settled his head on Taehyung's chest.

Jungkook picked the necklace up again and chewed on the blue stone while he hummed quietly.

"Why didn't you come and save me earlier?" Jungkook asked after a while. He lifted his head to look up at Taehyung. His voice had gone small and insecure. "Did you give up on me? I've been here for so many years."

Taehyung's hand found his and held it. "No, baby. It's only been a month. We didn't know where you were. Nobody could find you. You sent me back home. You protected me." He swallowed. "You also closed the mating bond, so I couldn't find you."

Jungkook looked at him for a long moment, and then he nodded slowly. "Good," he said. "I protected you." He sounded proud, and Taehyung saw something in his face that had not been there since he woke up. It was the first thing that felt like the old Jungkook.

"You love me?" Jungkook asked.

"I love you so much," Taehyung said.

Jungkook started crying again, and this time he pressed his face into his own hands. Taehyung pulled him closer against his chest. "Don't cry, Jungkook. You're alive, and you're safe now."

Jungkook nodded but kept crying into his palms. "I thought I was going to die," he whispered.

Taehyung shook his head and held him tighter. "You're not going to die. So many people love you. You have brothers, and you have a mate. You're never going to be alone again."

"I thought I had no one who loved me," Jungkook said, and his voice broke on the last word.

Taehyung sobbed. His alpha had been through hell and back, and he was still able to hurt over being forgotten. Then Jungkook reached up with a shaking hand and wiped a tear off Taehyung's cheek.

"Don't cry, omega," he said.

Taehyung gave him a sad little smile. "There's one more person who loves you," he said, and he took Jungkook's hand and placed it gently on his belly.

Jungkook's head came up fast. "Who?" he asked.

"Our pup."

Jungkook stared up at him with wide eyes, and his lips parted. "You're... you're pregnant? You're pupped?"

Taehyung nodded, not trusting his voice.

"Whose baby is it?" Jungkook asked, then looked at him again, as if he needed to hear the answer twice.

Taehyung wanted to laugh and cry at once. He pressed Jungkook's hand flat against his stomach and held it there. "It's yours, alpha. You gave me this. You made me pregnant."

Jungkook's face broke open into a smile, the first real one Taehyung had seen since he came into the room. He realized it was his own baby, and he kept his palm right where it was.

"Am I allowed to be there for you?" Jungkook asked.

Taehyung laughed. Jungkook was so sweet, even when he didn't remember much of anything. "Yes, alpha. You're allowed. Who's going to hold me when I get big like a balloon?"

"I will," Jungkook said at once, sounding very pleased with himself. Taehyung smiled and leaned his forehead against his.

Then Jungkook pulled back and looked up at him. "I'm hungry. Can I have some food?" Taehyung looked up at Yoongi right away.

"Food's coming in five minutes, Kook-ah," Yoongi said. His voice was rough from the day, but he kept it gentle.

Jungkook nodded and put the blue stone back in his mouth. Taehyung let him keep it there. He held on to him and looked at his face in the dim light. There was a small gash across the bridge of Jungkook's nose, and it was still open.

Taehyung bent down and licked along it once, carefully. The cut closed up in front of him, and the skin went smooth again within a second.

Taehyung let out a shaky breath. He had not known he could do that until now, and he was a little scared of how easily it had come. But he was not going to sit still and watch his alpha fall apart. He would heal him for as long as it took. He would also have to heal himself, because the baby was growing and his body was tired and empty, and that could not be ignored. He was determined about that too.

Jungkook's eyes slid shut again. His breathing slowed, and his fingers went loose around the stone. Taehyung kept holding him and did not move, even when his own back started to ache from sitting on the hard floor.

Yoongi tried to be strong for as long as he could. But exhaustion won as he fainted. He almost fell down if it were not for Hobi and Jimin running to hold him upright. Jimin knelt next to him right away and sprinkled some water on his face. Then he wet a cloth and wiped Yoongi's face.

Yoongi blinked back and just smiled as he kissed Jimin’s cheek. Jimin hit his chest again, and Yoongi just laughed as he closed his eyes again.

Jin looked back over his shoulder at Hoseok. "Give him some sleep meds, Hobi. He needs it. I don't think he has slept properly since this whole mess started. I can't even remember the last time."

Hoseok nodded and glanced at Yoongi, then at the bed. "He'll sleep when we're moving. Not before." He was already crouched by his pouch, pulling out bundles of dried herbs and sorting them into little cloth packets. "I want us to travel to Red Moon first. Then we can rest there."

"Are you going ahead?" Jin asked.

"Yes. I'll go first and get everything ready. You all come after, carefully. I'll send some soldiers out to meet you on the way with horses." Hoseok tied off one packet and dropped it into his bag. "We have fewer horses than we need, so you'll have to share. Don't make a fuss about it."

Jin gave a tired nod. "Yeah, okay."

Taehyung looked up from the rug. He was still stroking Jungkook's hair with his fingers. "You're going already?"

Hoseok stopped packing and looked at him. "I want to be prepared for everything when you arrive. Things don't work if one of us brothers isn't there to organize them."

Taehyung was quiet for a second. Then he lifted his chin as he said, "Then tell the soldiers that the crown prince is coming home," he said. "Have them prepare a feast for their royal alpha. Make everything to his liking. Get his chambers ready, too. Tell the entire pack that their alpha is coming back to them."

Hoseok straightened and looked at him for a long moment. He had not heard that tone from Taehyung before. Then he lowered himself into a bow, one hand on his chest.

"Your wish is my command, Luna."

## Four blocking findings

1. Paragraph **48**, Namjoon to vomiting Jungkook:
   - English `It's okay. Let it all out in hyung's hands.`
   - Russian `Всё хорошо. Выпусти всё в руки хёна.`
   - The literal imperative `выпусти` is unnatural/misleading in the physical context. Use `Всё хорошо. Не сдерживайся, даже если вырвет прямо хёну в ладони.` It keeps the caring self-address as `хён` and the literal fact that he is ready to catch vomit.
2. Paragraph **254**, Jungwon shifting from wolf to human:
   - English `shifted back into his human form, panting on the ground`.
   - Russian `возвращаясь в человеческий облик, и задыхался, стоя на полу`.
   - Source has him panting *on the ground*, not explicitly standing upright. Replace `и задыхался, стоя на полу` with `и пытался отдышаться, осев на пол`.
3. Paragraph **289**, Myul learns he was used:
   - English `His face, already wet with tears and blood, twisted into something close to betrayal`.
   - Russian `Лицо, уже мокрое от слёз и крови, исказилось почти предательским выражением`.
   - Current text implies Myul himself is treacherous; the narrative conveys a feeling of **being betrayed**. Replace the second sentence with `Лицо, уже мокрое от слёз и крови, исказилось так, словно его предали.`
4. Paragraph **298**, Yoongi instructs Jungwon:
   - English `Give these both the slowest death you can` (two).
   - Russian `Устрой всем троим самую медленную смерть` (three).
   - The source specifically says **both**, even if three potential perpetrators exist in the scene. Change `всем троим` to `этим двоим`; do not silently fix the original's referent/number.

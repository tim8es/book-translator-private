# Style Guide

Evidence-based literary style decisions for *The Claiming Moon* (English → Russian).

Workflow revision: `5e0731da8b7ca81ada5316ed536a8a954008b789`. These are source-supported editorial guidelines, **not** independent Reviewer PASS evidence. Check each chapter against its own original; do not mechanically impose observations from one scene.

## Narration

- **Point of view:** often close third person, with viewpoint changing by scene or chapter. Chapter 4 follows Jungkook; chapters 5 and 7 center Taehyung; chapter 31 opens close to Hoseok. Check focalization locally before choosing Russian pronouns and mental-state wording.
- **Narrative distance:** characters' fears, doubts, rhetorical questions and self-accusations often enter narration directly (chapters 5, 15, 17). Do not convert subjective impressions into narrator-certified facts.
- **Register:** conversational, at times emphatic, rough or explicitly profane. Keep strong/crude language proportional to source; neither censor nor intensify.
- **Sentence movement:** extended descriptions alternate with short fragments, questions and repetition. Preserve purposeful abruptness and semantic paragraph divisions.
- **Verb tense:** source passages mix present and past (e.g., chapters 4, 15, 17). Assess the source context before regularizing tense in Russian.
- **Emotion:** inner narration is frequently immediate and intense, but intensity varies. Maintain the source's degree of certainty and urgency.
- **Humor / sarcasm:** everyday irony in the Moon Village opening (chapter 1) and playful author's notes (chapters 4, 9, 45) need different Russian registers from tragic scenes.
- **Dialogue:** use natural Russian dialogue punctuation while preserving who speaks, fragments, hesitation, obscenities and distinct attitudes.

## Prose tendencies

- Concrete daily detail (food, weather, rooms, bodily sensations) matters alongside dramatic events: retain such texture instead of abridging it (chapters 1, 4, 5).
- Pace shifts from slow domestic sequence to rapid emotional beats (chapters 4, 15). Do not smooth all passages into the same cadence.
- Meaningful repetition is deliberate. The three separate `Please.` lines in chapter 15 should remain three separate `Пожалуйста.` lines, not one prose sentence.
- Chapter notes, summaries and afterwords are source content; preserve them when present. **Translator/service warnings** (e.g. `Черновой перевод`, `Статус`, text block coverage) are not authorial and must not appear in a finished book.

## Character voices — bounded observations

### Jeon Jungkook / Чон Чонгук
- Chapter 4 presents practical, detail-focused behavior; this alone is not a blanket rule for his dialogue.
- In chapter 15, inner speech uses panic, fragmentary self-reproach, and repeated pleas. Preserve repetitions and terms of address without embellishment.

### Kim Taehyung / Ким Тэхён
- Chapter 5 juxtaposes caution, uncertainty and emerging trust; preserve hesitation rather than upgrading inference into certainty.
- Chapter 17 uses fear-driven causal reasoning. Maintain specific threat references and the emotional pressure of the scene.

### Jung Hoseok / Чон Хосок
- Chapter 31 opens with bodily fatigue and informal inner narration. Insufficient evidence to assign a fixed speech register across all chapters.

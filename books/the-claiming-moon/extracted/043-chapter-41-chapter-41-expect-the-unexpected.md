They left before the sun even came up.

Jungkook and Taehyung were already mounted on their horses when more than half of the Deliah packs were sleeping; they just wanted to leave in silence and not cause commotion. The soldiers were around them as they circled around their luna. They already had travelled three miles.

The ride that should have taken three hours was cut down to less than two. Jungkook kept pushing Athen faster, and Taehyung matched his pace on Apollo without complaint. The soldiers had no other option but to match their pace with the royal couple.

Taehyung's hands gripped the reins tighter than necessary, his jaw clenched. His eyes kept drifting toward the horizon where Hefter's pack supposedly lay. He didn't know if his mother was even alive.

A part of him hoped she wasn't, because then he could just turn around, forget this place existed, and go back to Red Moon and figure out his life with his alpha. But another part of him, the part that had spent eighteen years in that pack, needed to know. He needed to see her face and ask her why.

Why would she let that happen to him? Why would she choose to look away?

The thought made his stomach twist. His mother would be furious when she found out he was mated. She would be even more mad if she figured out he was knocked up. The rage she'd feel would probably eclipse the rage Taehyung himself was trying to hold back.

His eyes started burning, tears gathering at the corners. Taehyung blinked hard, forcing them away. Not yet. He couldn't fall apart yet. He was so strong; this couldn't be the thing breaking him down.

Jungkook looked at him every now and then. Not in an obvious way, just enough to catch the little things. The way Taehyung's shoulders pulled in and how his breathing changed whenever he was sad.

He noticed.

He didn't say anything, though. There wasn't any point asking what was wrong. Taehyung would've spoken if he was ready, and pushing never worked. He didn’t want Taehyung to feel weak in front of their soldiers.

By the time they reached the pack borders, Taehyung felt completely unsettled. Like his skin didn't fit right anymore. The omegas inside him were way more nervous than he was. He didn't know if he even belonged here or fit in with his own people.

The Hefter soldiers stopped them immediately when they spotted them, stepping forward with hands raised. "State your business," one of them commanded, looking unimpressed.

Jungkook shifted in his saddle, drawing all the authority he needed to radiate. "Tell your alpha that Red Moon's crown prince has come for personal matters."

The soldier's entire demeanor changed. His eyes went wide for just a second before he recovered. "Y-yes, your majesty. You'll need to wait here. We'll fetch our alpha immediately to grant you access."

Jungkook nodded once, and two soldiers shifted into their wolf forms as they bolted back toward the palace.

While they were waiting, Jungkook took a look around. The first thing that caught his eye was just how weird this place looked. It looked as if it was a dessert or something.

Unlike the rich and fertile forests and fields of Red Moon, which had been carefully tended so that they might thrive, the land here was all sand, sun-bleached, and buildings left to decay. It was all a graveyard of abandoned homes. There was no vegetation to speak of, except where the land had been overgrazed and overworked until it died.

Greed. That was what he saw. Exploitation. Lack of foresight and poor planning. These things destroy the pack, and clearly, Hefter has been destroyed.

Taehyung didn't have to live like this anymore. That thought gave Jungkook a little satisfaction. He was happy that Taehyung managed to run out of this hellhole. He was so much stronger than he realized.

He turned to look at his mate. Taehyung was still on Apollo, staring at the pack entrance with an expression Jungkook couldn't quite read. Lost in thought. Drowning in it, more like. "Everything's going to be okay, love," Jungkook said quietly, reaching over to squeeze Taehyung's hand. Their horses were close enough that it was easy.

Taehyung nodded, but he didn't speak. His fingers squeezed back, though, holding on because he didn’t want to let go.

"Are you hungry?" Jungkook asked. Taheyung nodded, a little embarrassed, but they hadn't had anything to eat in the morning, and they ate in the night around 7pm. Taehyung doesn't like to stay hungry for more than twelve hours, especially when he’s pregnant.

Taehyung took it gratefully, his fingers brushing Jungkook's as he accepted it. "Thank you, alpha," he said, already peeling it open as his gaze drifted back to the pack.

"You're very welcome, my love," Jungkook replied.

A few of the soldiers around them snickered. Jungkook's head snapped toward them immediately. "What?" His voice was sharp enough to draw blood. "Do you not address your mate with respect?"

One of the soldiers went pale, his mouth opening and closing. "I'm s-sorry, your majesty, I didn't—"

"Stop terrorizing everyone, Jungkook," Taehyung said, shaking his head with a sigh.

Jungkook glared at his mate for a second, then let it go. He turned back to the road ahead, jaw clenched every time he thought about the people of this pack and poor pups growing here.

Taehyung finished the banana and handed the peel back to Jungkook, who put it away neatly in the bag. These were small gestures, but they describe their relationship as a whole. The soldiers returned within ten minutes, followed by another wolf form who quickly shifted back into human form.

"Your majesty," the man said, bowing quickly. "You're welcome to enter the pack grounds. However, your horses and soldiers will need to remain here outside the territory. We have accommodations for them."

Jungkook was confident enough in himself to handle whatever came. He could protect Taehyung if anything went wrong.

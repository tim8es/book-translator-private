Chapter 5

Taehyung’s nose turned red from the cool air as he woke up from his slumber.

He just lay there for a while, staring at the ceiling, trying to listen if Jungkook could be heard. Maybe he was there in the kitchen, or was he gone?

No, definitely not. Taheyung smelled something amazing being cooked, and there are only two people living, so it was definitely Jungkook cooking.

It had become routine now. Breakfast was waiting on the table; the smell of whatever had been cooked was the first thing penetrating his nose, and that cozy feeling of knowing Jungkook cooked for him.

A week had passed since that night at the pond where they had made the pigments and where they laughed together, that peculiar time when he saw Jungkook not as the dangerous alpha he was rumored to be but as… someone steadfast.

Thoughtful. Human. He had made him pigments from rocks. Taehyung still couldn't wrap his head around it. What kind of person even thinks to do something like that?

Something had changed since then.

Taehyung had spent nights thinking about it, thinking about it over and over again.

But no, he wasn’t ready for bonding after all that had happened. However, he wasn’t bothered by sharing a roof with Jungkook anymore either.

In truth, he was never bothered by being in the company of someone who just wants to share a roof and nothing else for now.

It somehow made everything seem easier and more colorful to him. Now he finally had someone living with him. Someone to talk to despite the fact that he is an alpha.

He rubbed his eyes and yawned, feet padding against the floor as he made his way to the kitchen. His night clothes hung loose on him, practically swimming over his frame, because he preferred to steal Jungkook's clothes. They were more comfortable anyway, and they smelled like cedar all the time.

Jungkook was already there, sleeves rolled up, hair a messy black mop, focused on plating food.

Taheyung started looking at his arms and the scars from the battles, thinking about how Jungkook got them in the first place. Stories Taehyung had started to wonder about more than he probably should.

Taehyung couldn't help but smile, quietly leaning against the doorway for a moment longer than necessary.

"Good morning, Jungkook," he greeted, his voice still soft from sleep.

Jungkook glanced up, and that teasing little half-smile found its way to his lips. "Morning, pup. You slept like a rock again."

Taehyung snorted, walking over to sit by the table. "Maybe because someone keeps cooking too good. You're secretly trying to make me fat, aren't you?"

Jungkook chuckled under his breath, setting down a plate in front of him. The menu today was warm rice, apple slices, steak, and tea steaming beside it.

"Guess I'll take that as a compliment. Now go brush before you sit down. I'm not feeding unbrushed pups."

Taehyung rolled his eyes, though his lips quirked upward. "You sound like a dad."

"I value hygiene," Jungkook shot back easily, grabbing another plate.

They had this easy camaraderie now that hadn’t existed before. It was just a week ago when they spent their morning glaring at each other and snapping at everything. But now, they joked and laughed, and it was peaceful.

They were good friends now. Jungkook is a great friend, he realizes. Better than he had ever anticipated that any alpha would be.

Taehyung had made a deal with Jungkook that they be friends. He had nothing else to do for the foreseeable future, and as much as he wanted to hate Jungkook for what he had done, he was going to try his best to get along with him.

Taehyung realized that he might have never thought about forgiving Jungkook, but now, each day, it became easier to accept him as Jungkook, nothing more. The same Jungkook who killed Hyungwon but also the same Jungkook who ground colors by hand for him.

He didn't even think about it anymore. Or at least, he tried not to.

Taehyung lingered for a second before turning toward the washroom, tossing over his shoulder, "You didn't leave early today?"

Jungkook shook his head, a small grin tugging at his lips. "Surprise. I've got a full day off from pack duties."

That made Taehyung pause mid-step, toothbrush forgotten in his hand. "A whole day? Don't they need the pack alpha or something?"

"Apparently, even the pack's best hunter needs to rest." Jungkook shrugged, leaning against the counter, eyes briefly softening as he looked at Taehyung. "Figured I'd spend it here. With you."

Taehyung blinked, caught off guard. "With me?"

"Well, unless you'd rather have me gone," Jungkook teased, raising a brow in that infuriating way of his.

Taehyung quickly looked away. "I didn't say that," and he could feel his ears warming up a little.

Jungkook's low laugh followed him as he went to brush his teeth. The voice even followed till the gate was shut, and it warmed Taheyung’s heart way too much. He needed to get a grip.

The table was laid when he returned, and the tea was already prepared by Jungkook. The scarf he had been gifted by Jungkook a week ago loosely hung on his neck, and the aroma of cedar remained attached to it.

And for reasons unknown to himself, he did not remove the scarf. Perhaps it was because of the comfort factor; perhaps it was because of familiarity; or perhaps it was because mornings no longer seemed lonely for him.

There could be other reasons as well, and he was not ready to analyze them yet.

Taehyung was seated in a lotus position in his chair, having a half-filled plate before him while giving a glare to the large amount of food Jungkook had just put there. And as was usual for Jungkook, he was going to keep putting chicken and steak from his plate into Taehyung’s plate.

"Stop that," Taehyung muttered, stabbing a piece of meat like it had personally offended him. "I'll grow like a fat pig if you keep feeding me like this."

Jungkook, completely unfazed, scooped another chunk of meat onto his own plate and shrugged. "That's okay. You can be a fat pig. I'll still be your friend, I swear."

Taehyung shot him a flat look, his chopsticks hovering mid-air. "You're not funny."

"I wasn't trying to be. It's a promise. Friends don't ditch friends even if they turn into livestock." Jungkook grinned, chewing slowly like he had all the time in the world.

"Wow," Taehyung deadpanned, setting his chopsticks down. "Touching. Really heartfelt."

Jungkook chuckled under his breath, clearly pleased at how easily he could get under Taehyung's skin. "You're eating well. That's good enough for me."

Taehyung rolled his eyes, sipping from his tea to hide the small smile threatening to break through. "You sound like an aunt."

"I sound like a responsible adult."

"You sound like a nag," Taehyung countered immediately, and Jungkook laughed again, irritating Taehyung even more.

The kitchen was small compared to the other rooms in the pack house, but it was still big enough for Taehyung. It’s not like he has to cook something. But still, Taheyung loved observing such things about the house.

They ate quietly for a moment before Jungkook leaned back in his chair, stretching his arms above his head. "So, what's your grand plan for today, pup?"

Taehyung didn't even look up from his rice. "Eat, sleep, poop, crochet, paint, and repeat."

Jungkook snorted, shaking his head with something that looked like fond exasperation. "Wow. Productive life."

"It's called peace, something you wouldn't understand," Taehyung said, smirking a little as he popped a piece of apple into his mouth, savoring the sweet fruit.

"Oh, I understand peace," Jungkook said, pretending to think deeply about it. "It's what I have when you're asleep."

Taehyung's head snapped up, eyes wide. "Excuse me?"

"Nothing," Jungkook said quickly, though his eyes were sparkling with barely contained laughter. "Eat your rice, pig."

Taehyung grabbed the nearest napkin and threw it at his face.

"Pup!" Jungkook barked a laugh, dodging it barely in time, his reflexes still excellent even in domestic moments like these.

"Serves you right," Taehyung grumbled, pretending to focus on his plate with concentration, though the corners of his lips twitched upward despite his best efforts.

Jungkook only shook his head, still smiling as he wiped his hands on a cloth. Then suddenly, his eyes lit up, as if he'd just remembered something important.

He reached for his hunting bag that was lying near the door and rummaged through it, the sound of clinking glass making his anxiety bad. He made something for Tae, and he just wished that the bottles hadn't broken.

"Wait," he said, his voice slightly muffled as he dug deeper, "I made something yesterday."

Taehyung frowned, suspicion creeping into his voice. "If it's some kind of dead animal again, I swear—"

"It's not food," Jungkook cut in, and when he turned around, he was holding a small glass jar carefully between his palms.

The inside on the glass shimmered a soft and deep blue, like dusk, sprinkled with touches of light colors resembling the sparkle of seafoam under the sun's rays.

Taehyung blinked and held his breath for a moment. "What is it?" It was clear to him that it was some sort of pigment by the way it shone inside the jar, but still he needed to know to make sure of what he had seen with his own eyes.

Jungkook gave him that proud look, slightly smug because of the very thing Taehyung liked the most. "It is a new pigment. I found a better stone to grind near the ridge; crushed it and added linseed oil. It took me hours to get it ready, but it turned out well."

Taehyung turned the jar in his hand slowly, eyes widening just slightly at how beautiful it looked. The color seemed to shift in the light, revealing depths he hadn't noticed at first glance. "It's… really blue."

"Thank you, color expert," Jungkook said dryly.

"I mean – it's… it's a nice blue," Taehyung said, still staring at the jar like it might disappear if he looked away. His fingers traced the smooth glass, feeling the slight warmth from where Jungkook had been holding it. "You really made this one too?"

Jungkook's grin softened into something gentler, something that made Taehyung's chest feel oddly tight. "Yeah. Figured you could use it. Thought it might match the sky you're always trying to paint."

Something warm flickered in Taehyung's chest, but he buried it quickly, clearing his throat. "Well… it's not terrible."

"I'll take that as high praise coming from you," Jungkook said, settling back into his chair with  satisfaction.

They cleaned up after breakfast, working in comfortable silence, Taehyung still stealing glances at the jar every few seconds as though the color might somehow fade or vanish if he blinked too long.

When they finally moved to the couch, Jungkook brought out a few other jars and a rough palette, spreading them across the low table between them.

"Alright, artist," he said, arranging things nicely for Tae. "Today I'm teaching you how to layer colors properly."

Taehyung groaned, half-playing, as he flopped back against the cushions. "Oh no, here comes Professor Jungkook again."

"Be grateful," Jungkook teased, dipping his brush into a light wash.

"You've been painting flat like you're coloring a kid's book. Layering gives depth. Makes it look real."

"Depth," Taehyung repeated mockingly, though he was already leaning forward to watch. "Right. Big words, alpha."

Jungkook ignored the jab, sliding a page toward him across the table. "Watch carefully. You start light. Then you go darker on the edges, building it up gradually."

And he did it with care, making sure to perform each slowly and steadily. Taehyung watched with his eyebrows knitted in intense concentration while Jungkook gradually built the shade one by one until the simple circle of blue on paper appeared almost like a real object.

"Now you," Jungkook said, holding out the brush.

Taehyung took it, his tongue poking out just a little in concentration the way it always did when he was focused. He made one stroke; shit, it was too heavy. The pigment bled into the paper immediately, leaving a dark blob instead of the soft wash Jungkook had created.

Jungkook snorted, trying and failing to hold back his amusement. "Well, that's one way to do it."

Taehyung scowled at him, his ears flushing pink. "Shut up, I wasn't ready."

"Try again, gentler this time," Jungkook said, encouraging.

Taehyung exhaled dramatically and tried again, this time pressing too lightly, the brush barely leaving any color at all on the paper.

"Are you painting or tickling the paper?" Jungkook asked, and he couldn't help the laugh that escaped.

"Oh my god, I hate you," Taehyung groaned, throwing his head back against the couch in defeat.

After the fifth attempt, Taehyung had paint smudged on his fingers and a streak across his wrist, and Jungkook had tears in his eyes from laughing so much.

"Okay, okay," Jungkook wheezed, reaching out to grab Taehyung's wrist gently to stop him from overloading the brush yet again.

"Like this, see? You glide it, not stab it into submission."

"I was gliding!" Taehyung protested, his voice pitching higher.

"That was a stabbing motion if I've ever seen one," Jungkook said, trying not to laugh again but failing. "Here, light hand, pup. Think of it like... like petting a cat."

He guided Taehyung's hand over the page, his fingers warm over Taehyung's, the brush moving in smoother strokes this time. The color bloomed softly across the paper, the blue fading into a lighter edge just like Jungkook had shown him, gentle and gradual.

Taehyung's face lit up like sunrise breaking. "Wait –  wait, that actually looks good!"

Jungkook nodded, looking genuinely pleased. "Told you. It's all about the pressure and patience."

"Guess you're not totally useless after all," Taehyung said, grinning now, his earlier frustration completely forgotten.

"Wow," Jungkook huffed, sitting back with mock offense. "That's how you thank your art teacher?"

"By calling him mostly useless? Yeah, that sounds about right," Taehyung said, and he was smiling now.

They both laughed for a while. Paint covered their hands in various shades of blue; the table was a mess of papers and brushes and water cups, but neither seemed to care. This was such a sweet moment.

Taheyung hadn’t felt like a human in a while, and these types of days with Jungkook were definitely healing something in him.

When Taehyung finally leaned back, stretching his arms above his head with a satisfied sound, Jungkook pointed to his cheek. "You've got paint."

Taehyung frowned, rubbing at his cheek only to smear the blue worse, spreading it across his skin. "Better?"

Jungkook grinned, something fond and warm settling in his chest. "Perfect."

This was the kind of perfect morning Jungkook had always dreamed about. To spend time with his omega like this, teaching him something, hearing him laugh, watching his face light up over something as simple as paint on paper.

He loved every second of it. Every single moment felt precious, like he was collecting memories he'd keep forever.

Sometimes Jungkook found himself wishing badly for Taehyung to look at him as his mate, to see him the way Jungkook had been seeing him for months now.

But for now, this was okay. Being his friend was more than enough. It had to be enough. He'd still managed to live when Taehyung screamed at his face that he hated him, when those words had felt like claws raking across his heart.

He'd survived that, so he'd manage this friendship phase too. He'd take whatever Taehyung was willing to give him and be grateful for it.

Just a morning, a few pigments, and two people who were slowly and unknowingly building something that felt like home, like something neither of them had quite found the words for yet.

They will eventually, Jungkook had hope.

Jungkook stood up suddenly, brushing paint dust from his hands. "Come on, let's go out."

Taehyung looked up at Jungkook like he'd grown a second head. "You crazy?" he said, groaning as he stretched, his bones cracking here and there in protest.

Jungkook looked back at him with that determined expression of his. "No. Get your ass up and wear a scarf. I'm taking you out."

And Taehyung did as asked, because for once in his life, he'd found himself trusting an alpha more than he did himself. It was strange and new and a little terrifying, but he grabbed the scarf anyway.

They stepped out.

The forest was quiet with birds singing and dew still resting on the leaves. Everything smelled fresh.

Taehyung padded behind Jungkook, complaining, the hem of his loose shirt fluttering against his thighs with each step. "Seriously," he muttered, squinting at the path ahead, "if you're going to kill me in the forest, at least make it quick. I don't want to die sweating."

Jungkook just laughed, his hand reaching out to brush past a low branch so it wouldn't slap Taehyung in the face. "You talk too much for someone supposedly being murdered."

"That's exactly what murderers say to make you comfortable," Taehyung shot back, stepping carefully over a root, his brows furrowed in concentration, though his lips were twitching upward despite himself.

They walked a little farther until Taehyung recognized the path. Jungkook was taking him to the pond. He glanced around as they walked. The forest looked brighter in the daylight, and for the first time in a long while, he wasn't looking over his shoulder.

When they reached the clearing, the pond sparkled in the morning sun. Taehyung stopped where he was and couldn't help smiling.

Wow," he breathed, the word escaping before he could stop it. "It's so… alive."

Jungkook turned toward him, and there was something soft in his expression. "That's kind of the point, genius. I told you to shut up and see."

"Yeah, yeah," Taehyung grumbled, but his gaze never left the pond and the water flowing so beautifully, the way it seemed to move with its own heartbeat.

The butterflies were hovering lazily around them, and their wings caught the light of the sun, while the rabbits were darting in and out of the bushes in rapid, fidgety movements.

The air was filled with the fragrance of moss, sunlight, and fresh vegetation. For an instant, Taehyung’s inner wolf was awakened inside him, impatient and anxious to be released.

He always, always used to come here before the claiming moon night and enjoy his time in this exact spot. Chasing the rabbits, feeling the grass under his paws, being free.

So his wolf craved it again as soon as they reached the clearing, the memory stirring something hungry and wild in his chest.

Jungkook noticed the way Taehyung's breathing had changed and the tension in his shoulders. He chuckled under his breath, dropping his bag on the grass with a soft thud. "Shift," he said simply, his voice low but coaxing. "Go run, pup. I'll guard you. I promise."

Taehyung blinked at him, surprise clear on his face. "You sure?"

Jungkook's eyes softened even more, something protective settling in his expression. "I wouldn't say it if I weren't. Go on. Be free."

Taehyung's lips curved up as he got so excited. He darted behind a tree, his fingers working quickly to unbutton his shirt.

There was something different about shifting in daylight. Everything was out in the open. A moment later, his white wolf came out from behind the tree, sunlight catching on its white fur.

He carried his folded clothes carefully in his teeth, padded up to Jungkook on his paws, and dropped them gently into his lap before shaking his coat free, the movement sending ripples through his fur.

Jungkook froze completely.

For a second, he forgot how to breathe, forgot how to do anything except stare. He'd seen Taehyung's wolf before, sure. Far away during pack hunts or guard duties, just a flash of white in the distance. Because Taheyung’s wolf was that white. You would catch sight of him even when not looking for him.

But Jungkook has never seen Taheyungs’ wolf up this close. He was never so close that he could see how soft the fur looked, how the sunlight caught hints of silver under the white, or how his eyes were the most stunning shade of ice blue.

"You're… beautiful," Jungkook murmured, the words slipping out before he could stop them.

Taehyung's wolf tilted his head, huffing softly, his ears flattening against his head in what looked suspiciously like embarrassment.

Jungkook laughed. He reached out hesitantly, giving Taehyung time to pull away if he wanted, letting his fingers brush through the wolf's fur.

It was way, way softer than he'd imagined, softer than he'd dreamed about during all those lonely nights, almost silky, warm beneath his touch. "Aren't you so pretty, pup?" he whispered with a grin that was equal parts teasing and genuine awe.

Taehyung's wolf leaned into the touch immediately, pressing his cold nose against Jungkook's chest. His tail flicked before he rubbed his face along Jungkook's neck, leaving faint traces of his scent there.

Jungkook went completely still, his heart thumping hard against his ribs as the realization struck him. Fuck, Taehyung's wolf was scenting him. Marking him.

Marking him in the way wolves did only when they felt safe, when they felt like someone was theirs and they needed others to know that.

A quiet laugh broke from Jungkook's chest, overwhelmed and delighted all at once. He let the wolf nuzzle freely, running his fingers through the fur at the scruff of his neck.

"You really are something else," he said softly, the teasing gentled by a tone that Taheyung’s wolf made out to be devotion.

Then Taehyung pulled back, his blue eyes shining as he turned and bolted toward the open meadow in a burst of speed.

"Yah!" Jungkook called after him, grinning wide now, his heart feeling too full for his chest. "At least warn me if you're gonna run!"

The wolf barked smugly and disappeared into the tall grass like a ghost.

Jungkook sat down on the grass with Taehyung's clothes in his lap and watched the white wolf race through the field. He could hear paws rustling through the grass and the occasional happy bark. Taehyung looked happier than he had in days.

When Taehyung returned finally, panting lightly, tongue lolling slightly from his mouth, Jungkook patted the grass beside him. "Done chasing butterflies?"

The wolf huffed indignantly, like the very suggestion was offensive.

"I'll take that as a no." Jungkook laughed again, leaning back on his palms. "You're impossible, you know that? Even as a wolf, you don't listen to anyone."

Taehyung's wolf gave him a playful growl before settling beside him. His head came to rest on Jungkook's thigh, and the alpha smiles at that.

They sat like that for a good while in silence. It felt like meditating. Jungkook absently stroked Taheyung’s wolf’s fur, his thumb tracing idle circles near his ear. He didn't say anything; he didn't need to. The silence was easy and exactly what they need right now.

Close to each other, but not speaking.

Then. A hop.

A rabbit wandered out near the pond, stopping every few steps to sniff the ground.

Before Jungkook could even react, Taehyung's wolf spotted it and took off.

Jungkook laughed as Taehyung charged after the rabbit, only to nearly trip over his own paws. The rabbit easily dodged him and kept hopping away while Taehyung bounced after it with far more enthusiasm than skill.

His tail wagged so hard it was almost embarrassing. He looked less like a wolf hunting and more like an oversized puppy who'd just discovered rabbits existed.

Then, as if that wasn't enough to completely melt Jungkook's entire chest, Taehyung discovered the butterflies.

Tiny golden insects hovered over the pond, skimming the water now and then. Taehyung's wolf noticed them right away and started bouncing after them, pawing at the air whenever one flew too close. He never caught a single one, but that didn't seem to bother him.

Jungkook leaned back on his hands and smiled. Taehyung looked completely caught up in it, running after anything that moved and stopping every few seconds to stare at something new. It was hard to believe this was the same omega who had spent so long afraid of everything around him.

Jungkook couldn't take his eyes off him. Seeing Taehyung like this, happy and completely at ease, was enough.

Then one butterfly had the audacity to land right on Taehyung's nose.

For a second, the white wolf went perfectly still, his entire body freezing, eyes crossing comically to stare at the delicate creature perched on his snout.

Then, “achoo!”

A high-pitched, helpless sneeze sent petals flying in every direction, and the butterfly fluttered away in startled panic.

Jungkook laughed so hard his stomach hurt. "You idiot," he breathed between chuckles, wiping at his eyes. "Oh my god, so cute. You're so cute I can't stand it."

Taehyung turned toward him, his ears flattening against his head in mock offense before his tail started wagging again, unable to maintain the annoyed act.

He padded over slowly and shyly, paws dipping into the muddy grass near the water's edge, then climbed right into Jungkook's lap without any warning or hesitation.

"Whoa, hey," Jungkook chuckled as Taehyung curled up small and warm, pressing that impossibly soft fur against his chest. "You're seriously just making yourself at home, huh?"

The only reply was a soft, content purr that he could feel against his chest.

Jungkook's hands moved on their own, fingers playing into that white fluff, rubbing slow circles over his side. The fur was impossibly soft, like touching clouds or fresh snow or something like that.

Taehyung settled into Jungkook's lap without a hint of hesitation. After a moment, he tipped his head back to look at him. His blue eyes stood out against his white fur, bright in the morning sun. Neither of them looked away. Taehyung couldn't speak like this, but somehow Jungkook felt like he understood anyway.

Taehyung was feeling things he probably shouldn’t. This alpha adored him.

"What now, you cute little puppy?" Jungkook teased softly, brushing a bit of fur from Taehyung's forehead with gentle fingers.

Taehyung's tail gave a slow wag, those bright blue eyes begging, no, pleading with an intensity that was impossible to ignore.

Jungkook tilted his head, already knowing but asking anyway. "You want me to shift too, huh?"

He let out a bark, as if agreeing.

"Alright, alright," he laughed, carefully pushing Taehyung off his lap and onto the grass. "Don't give me those eyes; I'm going. You're too powerful with those eyes."

He quickly pulled off his clothes and folded them beside Taehyung's before closing his eyes and shifting.

The feeling never really changed. His bones shifted into place with a series of familiar cracks, warmth spread through his body, and a few seconds later his black fur had taken over.

When he opened his eyes again, the world looked different. Every scent carried farther, every sound stood out, and even the breeze felt easier to notice. He padded across the grass toward Taehyung, his large black wolf stopping beside the smaller white one.

Taehyung tilted his head up, and his tail started wagging wildly, so fast it was almost a blur. Before Jungkook could even process it, Taehyung leapt forward and nuzzled his muzzle against Jungkook's broad chest, making a happy sound.

They stayed by the pond a while longer, neither of them in any hurry to leave. Everything else could wait. For now, it was just the two of them, the quiet forest, and a peaceful morning neither of them wanted to end.

They didn't need words. Jungkook understood Taehyung well enough, and Taehyung seemed to know that.

Jungkook's big black form lay still except for the rise and fall of his breathing. His fur glowed like polished ink under sunlight, and next to him, Taehyung's smaller, snow-pale wolf curled close, his head tucked perfectly under Jungkook's chin like it belonged there, like they'd been doing this for years instead of just today.

Every so often, Jungkook would nuzzle against the soft fur at Taehyung's neck, brushing his muzzle over it tenderly, breathing in that sweet scent that felt like home, like everything good he'd ever wanted.

Taehyung would respond with a tiny whine, shifting even closer until there was no space left between them, burying his face deeper into Jungkook's thick chest fur.

Just two wolves under the golden sky, their shadows touching and blending on the grass beneath them, becoming one dark shape in the bright afternoon.

They said they were "just friends" when they were human, when they had words and walls and all the reasons to keep distance between them.

But their wolves. Oh, their wolves already knew better. Their wolves had always known.

Because wolves didn't lie to themselves. Wolves felt things purely, without all the human complications and fears. Wolves understood what the heart wanted before the mind could catch up.

But to Jungkook's inner wolf, Taehyung was something to protect and care for, something that makes the world worthwhile. To Taehyung's wolf, Jungkook was gravity, the force that made him run towards him, the safety and security that made it okay for him to fall, and made him feel like he could fly since there was someone below him ready to catch him.

For a while, they just lay there, basking in the silence and the warmth. Taehyung's tail twitched lazily, brushing against Jungkook's leg in an affectionate gesture, and Jungkook's nose nudged under his chin, drawing a sleepy rumble from the smaller wolf that sounded almost like a purr.

But then, something shifted. Something flickered in Taehyung's bright blue eyes: a sudden spark in his eyes, a sudden wild energy.

Before Jungkook could even process what was happening, Taehyung leapt to his feet, paws kicking up bits of dirt and grass as he darted toward the treeline. His snow-white fur flashed between sunbeams, weaving through shadows, and Jungkook could see the playful challenge in every movement.

Jungkook's head shot up immediately, all his senses suddenly alert. Oh, he wanted to play. More than that, he wanted to be chased.

A low growl rolled from deep in Jungkook's throat as he stood, muscles tightening under his black fur. His gaze tracked that darting white figure running through trees, and he couldn't help the thrill that shot through him. He couldn't help the way his wolf responded instantly, eagerly, to the invitation.

You wanna run? Fine, run then. Let's see how far you get.

Because every omega knew instinctively how to tempt their alpha, how to make them chase, how to draw them in with the oldest game in the world.

And every alpha knew exactly what it meant when an omega ran like that with their tail high, movements graceful, looking back with those bright eyes. It meant Catch me if you can. I want you to find me.

It meant something neither of them would say out loud but both of them felt in their bones.

So Jungkook chased.

He rushed ahead, his muscular legs making him rush ahead in the clearing, digging his claws into the soft soil while rushing after that flash of white through the woods. The wind brushing by him carried along Taehyung's scent, which was sweet, wild, and incredibly tempting.

All he could hear was the sound of leaves fluttering and the sound of his heart beating and something that resembled Taehyung laughing somewhere through their bond that they rarely use.

Taehyung was incredibly swift. He barely touched the ground with his paws, and the way he moved was graceful and effortless at the same time. His tail was flying behind him like silk blown by the wind.

He glanced behind himself only once, just once, and his blue eyes sparkled with mischief and some other emotions.

A deep rumbling sound emanated from Jungkook’s chest that sounded both like growls and laughter, both amused and excited at the same time. All of his animal instincts are buzzing within him to the rhythm of a timeless hunt and claiming.

The wind brought him every hint of Taehyung’s whereabouts, and he could easily find his omega even blindfolded, since his wolf was able to recognize his scent just like air or breathing.

And then he spotted movement behind a mighty oak tree. The scent of Taehyung was suddenly much more intense, intentionally laid for Jungkook.

Jungkook stopped and crouched down, readying himself. His eyes were fixed on the flash of white fur that appeared in front of him, barely peeking out from the bushes. He took one step… another step… and his muscles tightened… and then…

Pounce.

A surprised yelp rang out through the air as Jungkook’s bigger body took down Taehyung’s smaller one into the plush greenery of the field. They wrestled each other around, spinning around in a frenzy of black and white fur, before finally settling down when Jungkook ended up on top, holding Taehyung softly below him

With one swift movement of his mouth, Jungkook bit the scruff on the back of Taehyung’s neck, carefully, never hurting, only showing Taehyung exactly what he meant.

Mine. You’re mine. I got you.

For a beat, Taehyung didn’t move, and then he made a sound of happiness, and Jungkook felt Taehyung give a soft wag with his tail. Jungkook released the scruff after a moment, his grip gentling until he was just resting his head against Taehyung's shoulder, breathing heavy from the exertion of the run.

His heart was pounding, adrenaline still singing through his veins, but underneath it all was something right and perfect. He licked gently at Taehyung's ear, the gesture both fond and claiming, tender and possessive all at once.

You ran, and I caught you. Just like it's supposed to be.

They lay entangled, panting and warm, amidst the leaves scattered on the floor and the light streaming through the leaves on top. Jungkook could sense each breath that Taehyung took, each movement, and how well he fit under him, both content in each other’s warmth.

Taehyung shifted a little and nuzzled his chilly nose against Jungkook’s cheek in response, an act that spoke volumes of forgiveness and acceptance and perhaps a whole lot more that neither had the courage to delve into.

They stayed like this for a while, in silence with only the sound of the trees and the breeze. Finally, with reluctance, Jungkook rose up, his dark fur shaking off the leaves from his body. With a nudge from his nose, he urged Taehyung to get up, and they headed towards the clearing where their clothes waited for them.

The chase was over; the game finished for now.

But the affection between them – the connection that had only grown stronger day by day, moment by moment, touch by touch, gaze by gaze, feeling by feeling – was not about to disappear.

It was taking hold, becoming rooted in parts of himself that he did not even realize yet, and it would not be denied much longer.

Taehyung had considered himself only putting up with Jungkook, only being polite to him, only doing his best with an unfortunate situation. He had considered it enough that he could be friends, but no more than that.

But his wolf knew better. His wolf had already made its choice and understood that which Taehyung was still too afraid to admit to his human self.

And Jungkook, Jungkook had been patient, had been waiting, and had been quietly loving Taehyung from afar for so long that having him this close felt like a dream he was afraid to wake from.

Maybe they were just friends when they had words and walls between them.

But out here, under the sun with dirt on their paws and each other's scents mingled together? Out here, they were something else entirely. Something neither had a name for yet, but both could feel growing stronger with every heartbeat.

Something that felt a lot like coming home. They shifted back and changed into their clothes to go back home.

Taehyung was still skipping a little, his boots scuffing against the earth, the ends of his scarf fluttering behind him with each bounce. He looked lighter somehow.

Letting Taehyung chase butterflies did help.

Jungkook watched him from a step behind, his chest filling up with something warm and unfamiliar that he didn't really want to put a name to. "Did you enjoy today, pup?" he asked quietly, reaching up to adjust the strap of his bag where it was digging into his shoulder.

Taehyung spun around, his grin stretching wide across his face, eyes shining. "Mm-hm. It was perfect," he said, and his voice came out almost like a song.

Jungkook found himself smiling back without meaning to. That's what he'd wanted all along, to see that exact expression on Taehyung's face. No fear lurking behind his eyes. No walls built up between them. Just Taehyung being himself, unguarded and free.

As they walked hand in hand through the narrow path, the conversation turned naturally towards what they would prepare for dinner.

Taehyung was being entirely silly and demanded that they prepare rice packed in fried leaves since he had once read about them in a very old book. Jungkook was just starting to laugh at his idea of burning the entire kitchen down when the atmosphere around them changed.

It was a subtle change, a rustling of sorts.

Jungkook's arm moved before his brain could even catch up, pulling Taehyung back slightly behind him as a figure stepped out from between two birch trees up ahead.

"Myul?"

His friend came to a stop, a grin spreading wide across his face. "Oh, Jungkook! Didn't think I'd bump into you out here."

Jungkook's brows pulled together. "What are you doing out here? I thought you were supposed to be home with Sana."

Myul let out a laugh, reaching up to scratch the back of his neck. "No, no. I've got patrol duties this week, remember? You probably forgot again."

Jungkook's eyes narrowed; an alert edge slid back into his posture. Was he really that forgetful these days? He didn't get the chance to answer, though, because Myul's gaze had already shifted, landing on the boy who was half-hidden behind Jungkook's shoulder.

"Taehyung," he greeted, and his voice warmed up considerably. "It's been a while. You look well." Myul's smile was genuine as he looked at Taehyung.

Taehyung took a small step forward, offering back a polite smile that didn't quite reach his eyes. "Yeah... it's been some time. How's Sana doing?"

Myul's whole expression softened at the mention of his mate. "She's wonderful. Happier than ever to be mated with me."

Then, maybe out of genuine curiosity or maybe just to make conversation, he added with another grin, "And you? Are you happy with Jungkook?"

The forest around them seemed to go completely still for a second.

Jungkook's hand loosened just slightly on Taehyung's wrist, as if he were bracing himself for an answer he wasn't sure he wanted to hear. His jaw set into something unreadable, but his eyes flicked sideways to Taehyung.

He wasn't demanding anything. He wasn't pleading either. He was just waiting, holding his breath without meaning to.

Taehyung caught that flicker in Jungkook's expression. He didn't even know why he did it, but his fingers tightened their grip around Jungkook's hand, holding on like he needed to ground them both.

"Yes," he said, looking up at Myul with a smile that felt surprisingly real. "I'm... very happy with my alpha."

Jungkook looked as if he were about to cry, something that mixed pride with a hint of disbelief before Myul finally nodded, letting out a half-laugh. "That's good to hear. The rumors going around weren't exactly painting the kindest picture of things. I'm glad they're wrong."

Jungkook's expression didn't really change, but his thumb brushed once across Taehyung's knuckles. It was brief and grounding and said everything he couldn't put into words.

Myul said a few more pleasantries, mentioned something about seeing them around, and then stepped away.

Taehyung finally let out a breath he'd been holding. "You really do scare people, you know that?"

Jungkook let out a huff of laughter. "Yeah. I've heard that once or twice."

They started walking again, the forest gradually opening up toward the path that would lead them back home. Jungkook's hand still rested lightly against Taehyung's wrist, and Taehyung found that he didn't want to pull away. Not even once.

Jungkook's hand stayed firm around Taehyung's as they made their way back through the village.

Some of the villagers glanced over at them with open curiosity in their eyes. A few offered polite nods as they passed. A couple of the omegas leaned forward slightly from where they stood chatting, peeking at the scarf wrapped around Taehyung's neck with poorly hidden interest.

Taehyung kept his head held high as they walked, even though the scarf smelled faintly of Jungkook's cedar scent and made something warm bloom in his chest. He wasn't about to admit that to anyone, least of all himself.

An elderly omega with graying hair stepped closer as they passed her doorstep. "How's our Luna doing today?" she asked, her tone soft and genuinely concerned.

Taehyung's shoulders lifted with a quiet kind of pride. "I'm doing amazing," he said clearly, making sure to meet her eyes when he spoke. "No complaints at all. Really."

A few of the other villagers nearby gave him those looks, some of the sympathetic ones they always gave to omegas who'd been "rescued" by an alpha, like he was some fragile thing to be pitied.

Taehyung had to resist the urge to roll his eyes so hard they'd hear it somehow. He didn't need anyone's pity. He definitely didn't need their silent commentary on his life.

Then there was another omega, younger than the others and far more inquisitive than she was polite. She leaned forward from where she'd been standing with a group of friends, her eyes darting toward his scarf with barely disguised curiosity. "That's... new," she said, and her voice was practically dripping with interest. "Why are you wearing that, though?"

Taehyung's mind immediately started scrambling for an answer. What was he supposed to say? Should he make up some story? Should he claim it was his own scarf that he'd always had?

He froze for a second, his mouth opening to stammer out some kind of lie, but then Jungkook's fingers tightened around his hand.

"My omega tends to run a little cold," he said smoothly, like it was the most obvious thing in the world. "Does it bother you that I make pretty scarves for him? Or is it that your alpha can't even provide for your basic needs, hmm?"

Taehyung blinked, caught somewhere between complete shock and absolute awe as the omega's face went bright crimson. She started stammering, "N-no, alpha! I didn't mean that," and then she basically bolted away down the street, muttering under her breath to her equally embarrassed friends.

Taehyung couldn't help the snicker that escaped him, and Jungkook's own quiet chuckle mixed right in with it.

They'd barely made it a few more steps when an older man called out from where he was stacking crates outside what looked like a small shop. "Pack Alpha! Luna!" He wiped his hands on his apron and walked over with a warm smile. "Good to see you both out and about."

Jungkook nodded respectfully. "Jihoon. How's business?"

"Can't complain, can't complain," Jihoon said, and then his attention shifted to Taehyung with genuine friendliness in his weathered face. "And how are you settling in, young Luna? The pack treating you well?"

Taehyung felt himself relax a little at the man's easy tone. "I'm settling in just fine, thank you. Everyone's been... kind." It wasn't entirely a lie, even if kind came with a hefty side of curiosity and gossip.

A woman emerged from the shop behind Jihoon, wiping flour from her hands. "Oh, is that our new luna?" She beamed at Taehyung.

"I'm Miyeon, Jihoon's mate. We've been meaning to bring by some fresh bread as a welcoming gift, but this one keeps forgetting." She swatted her mate's arm playfully.

"We've just been busy," Jihoon started to protest.

"Busy, he says." Miyeon laughed, then looked at Taehyung warmly. "You're looking healthy. That's good to see. The pack alpha taking good care of you?"

Taehyung felt Jungkook's thumb brush against his hand, just once. "He is," Taehyung said, and meant it more than he'd expected to. "Very good care."

"That's what we like to hear," Jihoon said with an approving nod. "You need anything at all, Luna, you just let us know. The pack looks after its own."

"We'll bring that bread by tomorrow," Miyeon promised, already shooing them along with a warm smile. "You two get home before it gets dark. And alpha, make sure he eats enough! He's too thin!"

Jungkook actually cracked a smile at that. "I'm working on it."

They continued down the street, and Taehyung noticed how differently people looked at them compared to before. There was still curiosity, sure, but there was something else too. Acceptance, maybe. Or at least the beginning of it.

By the time they finally reached the house, Taehyung's hands had loosened from their nervous grip, and he immediately tugged at the scarf, his face scrunching up into an exaggerated pout. "You blamed me for feeling cold?" he muttered, crossing his arms over his chest in a way that was far too dramatic to be truly angry.

Jungkook let out a laugh. "You always run cold, pup. Your hands, your feet... all of it. What else am I supposed to do?"

Taehyung's jaw tightened, though his eyes were sparkling with something that looked suspiciously like amusement. "You alphas are all hot-blooded," he argued, gesturing vaguely at Jungkook like he was exhibit A. "That's exactly why you don't feel the cold like I do!"

Jungkook just grinned and shook his head. "It's only twenty degrees outside, and you're still complaining about being cold?"

Taehyung made a show of chattering his teeth together, bringing his hands up to rub at his arms. "It's still very cold!"

"And that's exactly why I keep making sure you eat more meat," Jungkook said, reaching out to tug Taehyung inside the house by his hand. "It helps build up your blood. You probably run low on iron or something."

Taehyung's lips twitched, fighting against a smile that was trying very hard to break free. "I... might appreciate that," he muttered under his breath, though he absolutely refused to admit it any louder than that.

Jungkook's smirk was knowing, his eyes glinting with quiet amusement as he guided Taehyung through the doorway and into the warmth of the kitchen. "See? I do care, pup. I take care of my friends whether you want to admit it or not."

"You alphas are completely impossible," he said, brushing past Jungkook to set his scarf down on the back of a chair. "But... fine. I guess this particular one isn't completely terrible."

"Not terrible, huh?" Jungkook teased, nudging him lightly with his elbow. "I'll take that as progress."

Taehyung rolled his eyes, but the small smile tugging at the corners of his lips completely betrayed him.

For the first time that entire afternoon, walking through the village with all its nosy questions and curious stares, he actually felt something unexpected. Safe. And oddly content with the idea of walking through life with Jungkook right there beside him.

Taehyung had thought he'd just be tolerating this arrangement, surviving it day by day. But somehow, without him even noticing when it happened, things had started to feel different.

And that scared him almost as much as it comforted him.

Jungkook sat sprawled across the couch, looking half-bored and half-sulky, with a book lying closed beside him that he definitely hadn't actually bothered to read. "Staying home is so boring," he muttered, throwing his head back dramatically against the cushions.

"Like, I'm pretty sure I'm actually losing brain cells just sitting here."

Across the room, Taehyung glanced up from where he'd curled himself near the window, his chin resting on his knee as he stared out at nothing in particular.

"Tell me about it, Jungkook. That's basically what I've been doing for the past few weeks straight."

That made Jungkook go quiet for a moment. He turned his head slowly, his gaze settling on Taehyung's profile; the soft strands of his hair caught the sunlight and the eye bags that lingered under his eyes like he hadn't been sleeping well. Something in Jungkook's chest ached at the sight.

"Pup, I promise I'm trying. I really am trying here. I've been racking my brain trying to find some way for you to go out safely... but there's nothing yet. Not one damn option that's actually safe enough." He sat up a little, leaning his elbows on his knees. "I don't want you stuck in here forever like some prisoner. I want you to be able to be independent too, you know."

Taehyung's eyes dropped down to the floor. He didn't really understand why his chest was suddenly hurting so much or why his throat felt like it was closing up on him.

His hands curled a little tighter against his legs, and when he finally spoke, his voice came out smaller than he'd intended. "Yeah... I know you are."

He blinked rapidly, like that might somehow stop the burning sensation building up behind his eyes. Why am I getting so emotional over this? he wondered. It was stupid. The whole thing was stupid.

He cleared his throat roughly, trying his best to sound normal and unaffected. "Damn... the air's really rusty today or something," he muttered.

Jungkook's lips twitched into a small smile. He could see right through Taehyung's act, all those little defenses he put up, and the shaky way he tried to pretend everything was fine.

Jungkook pushed himself up from the couch slowly, crossing the room until he was standing right in front of where Taehyung sat curled up.

Taehyung's head jerked up, startled. "What?"

Jungkook smiled, "Nothing." He tilted his head slightly to one side. "Push me away if you want to."

Taehyung completely froze. His breath caught somewhere in his throat.

Jungkook's hand moved carefully until it came to rest lightly against Taehyung's waist. He didn't move any closer yet.

He just waited there, his gaze steady and open, giving Taehyung every single second he needed to make a decision.

The most logical, rational part of Taehyung's brain was absolutely screaming at him. Do it. Push him away right now. Take a step back. Don't let this line get blurred. Don't make whatever this is become weird and complicated.

But then there was that other part of him, the quiet part that had been growing stronger lately, and it was whispering something completely different. Stay. Just stay.

Taehyung didn't move. He didn't push Jungkook away.

He just stood there, his body tense but his heart racing like it was trying to escape his chest, his eyes wide and already getting glassy with emotions he couldn't quite name.

Jungkook leaned in closer, moving slowly and carefully like Taehyung was something precious that might break. His breath ghosted warm against Taehyung's skin. And then, finally, he pressed a soft kiss against Taehyung's forehead. It was just a gentle touch. Barely even there. But somehow it made absolutely everything inside Taehyung go completely still.

Jungkook didn't pull back right away. His lips lingered there against Taehyung's skin.

"I swear," he whispered against Taehyung's forehead, "I'll protect you. Always."

Taehyung's breath came out trembling and uneven. His hands moved up without him really deciding to do it, grabbing onto the front of Jungkook's shirt and holding on.

Their foreheads touched now, close enough that their noses were brushing faintly against each other. The entire world felt impossibly small and quiet, like nothing else existed outside this moment.

And suddenly, Taehyung had absolutely no idea what to do with himself. His thoughts scattered in every direction like leaves caught in a strong wind.

He could hear his mother's voice echoing somewhere deep inside his head, words she'd drilled into him over and over again. Alphas can never be good, Taehyung. They all want the same thing in the end. They'll use you and hurt you and leave you feeling hollow inside.

But... this didn't feel anything like that.

Jungkook wasn't touching him like he thought he owned him. He wasn't holding onto him like he wanted to take something away. He was holding Taehyung so carefully, like he was something fragile and valuable, something that deserved to be protected instead of possessed.

There was no lust in his touch. No pressure or expectation. Just warmth. Just care.

Taehyung's throat hurt again, but it was for a completely different reason this time. He felt small in Jungkook's arms, but not in a bad way. He felt safe. He felt completely seen in a way that was terrifying and comforting all at once.

His heart was beating so fast against his ribs, and it wasn't because of fear. It was because of something that actually scared him even more than fear ever had, trust.

Jungkook's thumb moved in small, soothing circles against Taehyung's side, right through the fabric of his shirt. He didn't say anything else. He didn't need to.

Taehyung finally managed to let out a small, shaky breath and whispered, "You're really warm."

Jungkook smiled, still keeping his forehead pressed gently against Taehyung's. "And you're absolutely freezing."

Taehyung let out a soft huff.  "Then don't move," he said quietly, his voice barely above a whisper. "You'll fix it if you stay."

Jungkook didn't move. Not even an inch.

For the first time in what felt like forever, Taehyung actually let himself believe something he'd thought was impossible.

Maybe, just maybe, an alpha could actually be good after all. Maybe they weren't all like his mother had warned him. Maybe Jungkook was different.

Maybe he'd been wrong about everything.

The warmth between them doesn't get to last forever, though. It never does. There’s a knock on the door.

Jungkook instantly goes tense, his whole body shifting into alert mode. His hand reaches out for the scarf and wraps it carefully around Taehyung's neck, making sure to hide the unmarked skin there. "Keep it on," he murmurs.

Taehyung barely manages to get a nod out before Jungkook turns away and heads toward the door.

When he pulls it open, there are four men standing on the other side, villagers, judging by their clothes, with stiff shoulders and uneasy expressions painted across their faces. "What's the problem?" Jungkook asks, his eyes sharp as they scan over each of them.

"Pack Alpha," one of them says, bowing his head slightly in respect. "Some people came from another village. They said they need to meet with you. It sounds urgent."

Jungkook's jaw tightens visibly. "Take them to the meeting house. Tell them I'll be there soon."

Jungkook turns back toward Taehyung, and for just one fragile moment, his eyes soften considerably.

"I'll come home soon, pup."

Taehyung manages a faint smile, even though his heart is stuttering oddly in his chest. "Go, Alpha. Do what you need to do."

Taehyung sits down for maybe half a second, but his heart is beating way too loud in his ears. He can't manage to stay still. So he gets up and starts cleaning instead, washing the dishes, sweeping the floor, and straightening anything and everything he can reach.

His hands move fast and restlessly, his mind trying desperately to catch up with everything that just happened between them.

Jungkook had kissed him.

Just on the forehead, sure, but still. It had made his chest ache in that stupid, almost teenage kind of way that didn't make any logical sense.

He keeps catching himself smiling without even realizing he's doing it. His cheeks are actually starting to hurt from it.

Hours drag by like that, slow and endless. The sunlight fades from the windows, the sky darkening into deep blues, and he's about halfway through folding up a blanket when he hears another knock at the door. His stomach drops instantly.

He runs. Jungkook never knocks.

When he yanks the door open, Jungkook basically collapses right into him.

"Alpha-!" Taehyung gasps out, catching as much of Jungkook's weight as his body can possibly handle. Jungkook's shirt is completely gone, blood smeared down his bare chest and arms.

There's a deep gash torn through his side that's still bleeding heavily. Blood is dripping from his mouth, running down his chin, and his eyes look wild and unfocused and completely dazed.

"Fuck-what-what the hell happened to you?" Taehyung stammers, his hands already shaking as he tries to drag Jungkook's much larger body inside the house.

Jungkook barely manages to force the words out, his breathing harsh and labored. "Lock... lock all the windows... and the door."

"Y-yeah, okay, okay." Taehyung rushes to do exactly that, his heart hammering so violently in his chest that it actually hurts. Terrifying.

He comes rushing back and drops to his knees beside Jungkook, carefully lifting his head up into his lap.

Blood is staining absolutely everything now, coating his hands, soaking into his clothes, and spreading across the floor beneath them in a growing pool.

"Alpha... please, you have to stay awake," Taehyung whispers desperately, his voice cracking and breaking as he tries to clean away some of the blood with trembling, useless fingers.

He can see bruises forming in dark purple patches. There are claw marks raked across Jungkook's ribs. And there's a burn mark across Jungkook's hip that makes Taehyung freeze completely when he spots it.

It looks strangely familiar somehow, but his brain absolutely refuses to place where he's seen it before, not right now, not when his alpha is lying here, barely breathing in his arms.

The burn looks pretty old too, which doesn't make any sense at all. How was it even possible for an alpha to have a years-old scar like that, especially when werewolves could heal completely in just a few days, sometimes even hours?

But Taehyung's brain is way too panicked and scattered to make any sort of sense out of anything right now.

Jungkook's hand moves weakly, reaching up with obvious effort to touch Taehyung's face. His palm is wet and sticky with blood, smearing across Taehyung's cheek as he cups it with surprising gentleness despite everything.

"They're here to get you, Luna."

Taehyung's breath catches hard in his throat. "W-what? Who?"

"They're going to take you away from me."

"No," Taehyung immediately shakes his head, tears already spilling down his cheeks before he even realizes he's crying. "No, I don't want to go anywhere. who the hell came to take me?"

Jungkook's eyes are getting glassy now, the focus fading out of them bit by bit. His bloodied lips part just barely enough for one last whisper.

"Your old pack," he breathes out, and each word sounds like it's taking everything he has left. "The Hefter pack. Their Alpha... he said he's come to take his Luna back."

And then Jungkook's entire body goes completely limp in Taehyung's arms.

Taehyung freezes. His blood turns to ice in his veins.

No. No, no, no, no.

He shakes his head again and again, clutching Jungkook closer against his chest, his tears soaking into Jungkook's blood-stained skin. "No... no... please no. Please don't do this."

And for the first time in years, Taehyung is terrified.

His old pack had found him. After all this time, after everything, they'd finally tracked him down.

And they wanted to drag him back to hell.

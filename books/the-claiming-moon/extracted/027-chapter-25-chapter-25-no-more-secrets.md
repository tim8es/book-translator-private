Little brother.

Taehyung needed a whole damn fucking minute. He needed a full minute to stand there, breathe through his nose, and let his brain catch up to what his ears had just heard, because his entire world had just rotated on its axis without his permission. He was still standing in the same spot, but everything around him felt completely different from two seconds ago.

Little brother.

Jungkook was Yoongi's little brother. Which meant he was Namjoon's little brother and Hoseok’s too, apparently. Not even then.

No. No, no, no.

What the actual fuck.

Jungkook looked terrified.

And Taehyung stood there looking at him, thinking, Why is Jungkook the one who looks terrified right now? It’s Taehyung who should be terrified.

I am the one who has been living with a man who lied to my face. I am the one who is mated to that man and has been sleeping beside him and trusting him completely without knowing that the place he kept pretending was foreign to him was actually his own home.

Taehyung breathed through his nose again, slowly, and looked around the room. Today had been a heavy day from the moment he had opened his eyes. He had woken up nauseous and been told he could get pregnant when he had spent his entire life believing he was infertile. He had cried until he had nothing left in him, and then he had gone to visit a sweet old woman and eaten venison and received a handkerchief and felt genuinely peaceful for the first time in a very long time, and now this.

Now this. All in one day.

Betrayal came from the people closest to you.

He knew that ever since his mother handed him a pill and told him it was for his own good. Betrayal required closeness. You could not be betrayed by someone who meant nothing to you.

He rubbed a hand over his face and felt the dizziness and the nausea hit him, and this time it had nothing to do with withdrawal symptoms, leaving his medications, or any physical thing at all. It was everything else. It was the weight of everything that had happened to him in a single day landing on him all at once. His whole life had been coming apart and stitching itself back together into something new ever since he woke up. He was tired.

But then there was that voice in his mind, screaming at him to just go on. To move, to run, to not even look back. To get lost in the woods and stay lost until things finally made some sense. But there was another part in him that had learned throughout the last year the meaning of being listened to and being important to someone else.

To breathe and to listen because Jungkook had never once put anyone above Taehyung. Not a single time.

If you were so scared, why the hell didn’t you come and take care of it yourself? Should I assume you don’t love him like I do?”

Namjoon’s only little brother.

He turned his head toward the window and kept his hand wrapped around Jungkook's, feeling the cold fingers that didn't squeeze back.

A little pile of matted black hair fell onto the towel beside the bed.

Within a few breaths of putting his ear against Jungkook's chest, he knew it was pneumonia. The way he had difficulty breathing was too prominent to miss. So he put in extra nutritional herbs for that and then for his rut, his recovery, and also to heal his gashes. He put the bowl down on the table.

“Feed him when he wakes up.” He instructs.

Hosoek can know the story later. Jungkook has to be healed now, though.

And the main thing is that we need to get Jungkook back to Red Moon, and that's going to be a task in itself." He looked down at Jungkook.

"Look at him. I don't even know how we're going to move him."

Nothing internal looks hurt, so I'm hoping this is just short-term memory loss. I'll give him the quick healing herbs for his head first, and then we'll see."

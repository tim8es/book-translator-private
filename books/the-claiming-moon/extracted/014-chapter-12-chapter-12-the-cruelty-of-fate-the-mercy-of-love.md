It was the way he talked when words failed him. It was how he processed the world when the world got too loud. And Jungkook remembered that, even now, even when everything was falling apart.

Jungkook packed his own weapons. Knives. A bow. Anything that would help them survive the first few days. He grabbed a piece of paper from the desk and wrote a letter. Taehyung hovered nearby.

After folding the letter, Jungkook looked strangely calm and almost relieved. "I want Myul to become the next pack leader. I don't trust anyone else. At least someone should take care of the pack if I'm not here."

Taehyung expected him to look upset or to hesitate or To show even a little fear about leaving. But Jungkook didn't. He seemed almost done with this place. Like he'd been waiting for a reason to leave, and now he finally had one.

Maybe that should've scared Taehyung. But instead, it made him feel something close to hope. If Jungkook could let go of everything he'd known, everything he'd built, then maybe they really would be okay. Maybe they could build something new together.

Jungkook zipped the last bag and finally walked toward Taehyung. He pulled him into a long hug that Taehyung didn't know he needed so badly. He held him from the waist and pressed him close. "Everything's going to be okay, pup."

Taehyung closed his eyes and breathed in slowly because it felt like the world was tilting under his feet once again. He didn't know where they were going or if they'd survive on their own. He didn't know what kind of life waited for them. He didn't know anything.

Except Jungkook. And maybe that was enough.

They went out once the sky grew dark. They carried heavy luggage; however, the silence that enveloped them was heavier. Behind them stood the packhouse silently, the building full of memories that suddenly became meaningless.

Taehyung stared at Jungkook with a million questions on his mind, but Jungkook held his hand tightly. "We're leaving," Jungkook said softly. "We're not looking back."

Taehyung nodded even though the fear still sat in his chest. But walking beside Jungkook made the fear feel smaller. He kept thinking the same thing over and over.

As long as I'm with him, I'll be okay.

Even if he wasn't sure he believed it fully yet, he wanted to. And wanting to believe was a start. It was something to hold onto when everything else had been ripped away.

And for now, that was enough.

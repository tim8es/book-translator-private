Chapter 3

Taehyung yawned, rubbed his eyes, and blinked several times trying to push the sleepiness away. His head was still heavy, and he felt tired, but somehow he managed to sleep. He did not think it would happen, that he would be able to fall asleep even after all that happened.

Not after he was found by pack alpha Jeon Jungkook, who had almost killed his own brother just to claim Taehyung as his. Not after he and Jungkook had faked the entire knotting and claiming ceremony, fooling everyone who watched.

Taehyung had thought the weight of it all would keep him awake, staring at the ceiling until dawn broke, but his body had other plans.

His body had betrayed him in the end, sinking into the mattress like it was the first time in years he'd been allowed to truly rest.

He sank back on the mattress as if it were the best sensation in the world that he had missed for years, but he was wrong to do so. The sheets were a nice touch, softer than anything he remembered, and the pillow was cold, something he had not felt since he was a child.

His head throbbed with the aftereffects of the panic attack that he had last night, but the sleep was good, too good for his liking, which made him feel uncomfortable.

He shook himself off, shoved his legs over the bed’s edge, and winced in pain. His legs were sore, his thighs tight from last night’s race through the woods, and his calves, oh his poor calves, were aching from the stress.

Not to mention his feet, which he had beaten against the trees and rocks in the forest while Jungkook was following him. It all came back to him in bits and pieces, how Jungkook had caught him like an animal, dragging him back over the ground by his side like he weighed nothing. Taehyung groaned in discomfort as he made his way to the loo.

The cold tile made his toes curl as he bent over the sink to wet his face, and the cold water ran down his back, bringing him back to reality.

For a second, he was certain that he was back in his cottage, alone, and that all of this was a vivid nightmare. He stood motionless with a few droplets of water running down his chest before shaking his head and walking to the sink.

He stared at his reflection, and for a brief moment, he forgot what he was looking at. His cheeks were pale, as usual, though he never used to have such pallor over his bones. His eyes were bloodshot and puffy, even though he had not cried since last night.

His lips were dry, and he looked like his old self, the only difference was that he was not alone anymore.

His jaw tensed, and he looked at his reflection again, this time narrowing his eyes. The word mate echoed in his head, and he stopped brushing his teeth.

His heart was hammering in his throatt so hard that it physically hurt to swallow, and he clenched his jaw. His mouth twisted in a pained expression as he stared at the mirror, and the word mate was still there, burning at the edges of his vision and senses.

It was Jungkook.

Thinking about him made his throat tighten, and it had been months since he had avoided looking at the alpha. Months of pretending that Jungkook’s eyes did not trail him when he walked through the village and that his wolf did not react to the alpha’s presence in such a way.

Now, there was no more pretending. Now, the whole pack thought that they were mates.

That Jungkook claimed him properly with blood and the hunt. It would happen eventually; he knew it would, but it would not be soon enough. He hopes so.

But then, Jungkook was just another alpha. Jungkook would not wait for his answer but rather take what he wanted as he always did.

Somewhere deep inside, he knew that one day, that would be the truth. That the name "mate" would be etched in his bones forever. The brush fell from his hand as he rinsed his mouth, spitting the foamy water in the sink, and he coughed as he choked on the aftertaste.

His hands were shaking as he wiped his mouth with the back of his hand, and he hated that he could not control them.

He leaned against the mirror for support as he looked at himself in the eyes, steadying his breathing. “Pathetic,” he muttered to himself, and there was nothing else he could call himself at this point.

What else could he say to the man who had tied him up and thrown him in another person’s house like a trophy? He walked back into the main room and took a deep breath, his decision finalizing.

He looked around the room, waiting for his eyes to lock on the familiar shape of the alpha. Maybe in the corner of the room, legs spread wide, arms crossed over that broad chest. Waiting for him.

Watching him with those dark, unreadable eyes. Maybe Jungkook would even have that cocky look on his face, that subtle smirk that said he was proud of himself for being the one who found Taehyung, for being the one who got to claim him.

For making Taehyung his, whether Taehyung wanted it or no

But instead, he saw an empty room, and for a moment, he could not breathe. He was not there. Not waiting for him in his own house. Did he just leave him all alone on his first morning there? Was he so eager to get rid of him in the new place that he did not even say goodbye?

Taehyung laughed at the irony of the situation because it was ridiculous. The alpha of the pack, the man who claimed to be the strongest and proudest, could not even stay in the same house with his mate for one night.

But the laugh died in his throat as the next thought made him physically ill. His hands clenched into fists at his sides as his throat burned, his eyes stinging from the tears that had not fallen yet. He wanted to curse the man out, to tell him to go to hell, but he could not do it. He could not do it because his heart was breaking, and he hated himself for it.

The wood at the gate creaked as the alpha stepped through, and Taehyung tensed. His muscles coiled, and his whole body went stiff at the sound of his footsteps. He could feel them before he saw him, and he looked up as he heard the man’s voice.

Jungkook stepped through the door, his bare chest damp from the cool morning air. His dark hair was slicked back from the sweat on his forehead, and he wore his usual gray sweatpants, hanging low on his hips.

Taehyung’s stomach twisted painfully at the sight, a warmth pooling in his gut that he did not want to think about. Of course, he looked like that. It was unfair that the man was so good-looking and that Jungkook had this effect on him even after everything that happened last night.

In his hands, the alpha held a bundle of fresh herbs, their green stems and leaves spilling out from between his fingers, and he looked at Taehyung with his usual unreadable expression when he was annoyed.

“Morning,” he muttered, his voice a deep rumble in his chest. Taehyung rolled his eyes in response, just as he always did, but this time, it hurt. He did not say anything in reply, only stared at him with such daggers in his eyes that he hoped that the man would know how he felt.

Like he was disgusted, angry, and in so much pain that he could barely stand being in the same room with him. But Jungkook did not seem to notice it, or if he did, then it did not affect him in the slightest.

He walked past Taehyung, towards the kitchen, and pulled a large stone mortar from one of the cabinets. He dropped the bundle of herbs on the counter and began grinding them with a pestle, crushing the leaves and stems.

The fresh, bitter scent filled the room within seconds, and Taehyung watched as the alpha’s broad shoulders rolled. His muscles tightened as he continued with the movement, flexing his biceps as he pushed the pestle down with his palms.

Taehyung watched silently as Jungkook worked. His hands moved skillfully and calmly, crushing the herbs to a rough green paste.

Confusion prickled at the anger in Taehyung's chest. What was this? Why was he doing it? Wasn't he supposed to be possessive and demanding? Supposed to treat him like the alpha male he was?

When Jungkook finally brought the wooden bowl over to him, Taehyung narrowed his eyes at the alpha's face. "What do you want?"

Jungkook said nothing as he lowered himself onto his knees in front of him, setting the bowl on the floor between them. Large hands took hold of his legs. "What... are you doing?"

Taehyung tried to pull his legs back, away from the man, but a sharp pain lanced up his ankle as Jungkook's grip tightened. The alpha had a good hold on his ankle; he had taken it gently but firmly, lifting it into his lap to press the cooling paste into the torn skin.

All of his muscles tensed up at once, his body fighting against the touch like he was afraid to be touched. Even as his skin burned where the herbs met the damage, he felt the dull pressure against his skin help soothe some of the pain away.

Then the sting of the pain was gone, smothered underneath the firm pressure of Jungkook's hold on his ankles as he kept the alpha from moving.

The angry patch of skin on the ball of his foot was gone where it had been scraped raw the other day.

Taehyung barely believed it as his heartbeat pounded in his ears from the dull ache that still remained. It seemed like forever since he had gotten the injury, and the pain had made every step a horrible ordeal. He remembered walking as if he had bare feet on broken glass.

And yet now, it was practically nothing.

He swallowed hard, working to steady his voice when he spoke again. "Why…are you doing this?"

Jungkook didn't move as he worked the ointment into the skin along the arch of his foot, his fingers pressing slow circles into the muscle. He closed his eyes as he worked. "Because. It hurts."

Taehyung scoffed, shaking his head. "You think this makes any difference? You toss me like a dog over the dirt and now you want to take care of me? You're just like the rest of them, Jungkook. Just as controlling and smothering. You're even more selfish than the rest."

Jungkook's hand stilled on his foot for a moment, and Taehyung tensed up, expecting an angry response.

Instead, the alpha simply worked more ointment along the side of his heel, his jaw tight with something that looked an awful lot like his own brand of frustration. His eyes never left the skin of Taehyung's foot.

It wasn't until now that Taehyung noticed the dark bite mark along the inside of Jungkook's wrist.

His stomach turned at the sight of it. The bite was deep, but it looked worse on him. It curved in a crescent shape, a clear sign of the alpha's injury.

It would leave a nasty mark if he didn't take better care of it, and it was very obviously self-inflicted.

It was the same wound that Jungkook had made as a show-off to the rest of the pack last night.

The one that he had pretended was from Taehyung, which he used as 'proof' of their bonding. Because, of course, Jungkook hadn't bitten him. He had bitten himself, tearing open his own skin to lie to everyone in the pack. To protect Taehyung's lies. To cover up Taehyung's stupidity.

Why did he do that for Taehyung?

"Why did you lie for me?" Taehyung asked in a hushed voice, and he wasn't sure what he hated more: hearing the hurt in his voice or the fact that he was asking the question in the first place.

Jungkook looked up at him then, his expression unreadable. "Not for you."

"Not for me?"

Jungkook's jaw was set as he pressed another bit of ointment into the skin above his heel. "For us."

It took a moment for the weight of his words to fully settle in. When it did, Taehyung had to look away, his stomach roiling in protest. He clenched his jaw, tightening his fists until his nails dug into his palms

. "Us," he muttered darkly. "Of course."

Jungkook's fingers curled for a moment on his skin before he withdrew them, standing up to his full height again. He didn't seem to notice that Taehyung's expression had soured, or maybe he just didn't care.

"Do not go outside. Not at all. For any reason. Wait until the pack settles down, and then I'll deal with it. If anyone sees you outside, they will sense you, and they will know something is wrong. They will come after us. We'll both be killed, or worse yet, exiled."

Taehyung frowned deeply, his expression incredulous. "So, I'm to stay here like some kind of prisoner?"

"You're not a prisoner," Jungkook muttered, pacing away from him and stopping a few feet away. He exhaled sharply before finally speaking again.

"You can do whatever you want in this house. Call Irene for company if you want. Sleep whenever you want. Eat whatever you want. Scream into the walls if that'll help. It's all fine by me. As long as you stay inside this house until I sort some things out."

Taehyung bristled at his words, his frustration bubbling over. "Wow. So incredibly generous of you, bitchy Alpha. Letting me breathe inside your big scary house. Should I get down on my knees and thank you for your amazing generosity?"

Jungkook's jaw tightened at his words, a muscle twitching. "You think this is about you? About what you want?"

"Of course it's about you," Taehyung snapped, his voice rising.

"You parade around this village like some kind of noble hero, playing house and pretending to care. But you don't care about me. Not really. You only care about your precious power and your precious fucking pack. You're just like all the other alphas. You'd toss me in a cell if you thought it would make your precious alpha crown shine a little brighter." He spat the words out in a heated stream, not noticing the pained expression on Jungkook's face until he caught sight of the fists at the alpha's sides.

"Careful with that tongue of yours, pup," he warned.

Taehyung leaned forward, hissing quietly. "Fuck you. Fuck your pack and their fucking crazy rituals. And fuck this bond while you're at it. You're not anything special, Jungkook. Just another arrogant alpha with a bad attitude who thinks he owns everything he touches."

Something in Jungkook snapped at his words, and his nostrils flared as he clenched his fists tighter. "I already warned you once. Keep your tongue fucking clean," he growled.

Taehyung was prepared to snap back at him again, but the words died in his throat when Jungkook turned away. The alpha stormed towards the front door.

He left with a slam of the door, leaving Taehyung completely alone in the house. For a long moment, Taehyung simply sat in the dining room, his chest tight with something he wasn't quite ready to identify.

A sharp pain crawled up his throat, making him bite his lip until he could taste blood. "Fucking bitchy alpha," he muttered under his breath, standing and pacing back and forth through the room.

The house was large, it was quiet, and it was very much not his.

It was strange. He didn't feel quite right walking the long hallways. The house was massive. He had passed through the front doors and into the house proper, and it wasn't until he wandered through the different rooms that he realized how large the place actually was.

It was more like a mansion, really. It can accommodate half a pack of wolves at one point.

It was apparently the residence of Hyungwon's father. Taehyung had heard from Irene that it had been built by him.

And yet, even with how cold the place felt, even though Taehyung knew he was nothing more than a prisoner here, he couldn't help thinking it was... nice.

It didn't feel lived in, and it definitely didn't have that warm feeling a home was supposed to have, but it also wasn't as empty or lifeless as he'd expected. Most of the furniture was made of wood, giving the whole place a warm, cozy look, almost like a cabin.

Just a much bigger one, and a lot more modern. His own cabin wasn't anything like this. It was tiny, didn't even have a proper bathroom, and could probably fit inside this place more than a hundred times over.

But he was homesick. So, so very homesick. He had left almost all of his things behind at the house he had grown up in the past six years.

He didn't have a single personal item left that made it feel like a real home. He had no one to greet him here either, no one that would really miss him if he left, not in the way that someone should.

Well, no, not entirely true. He had Irene. His best friend, his omega best friend, was one of the few people that actually mattered to him.

She was special, really. She was a one-of-a-kind girl, and Taehyung was so very lucky to have met her.

But now he was trapped in the house that Jungkook considered his childhood home.

He ran his hand over the wall as he wandered through the rooms, a bitter laugh escaping his lips as he thought over everything that had happened. "Luna," he muttered, shaking his head.

The name was strange coming from his mouth. Luna. He felt ridiculous even saying it. Him, of all people, running from a pack and having to live in a mansion built for wolves. He couldn't even control his own moods half the time, much less the well-being of an entire pack. Even as a wolf, he was a disaster.

"Can't even take care of myself properly," he muttered, staring up at the windows in the front hallway. "And they want me to take care of all of them?"

He wandered through the house again, his bare feet padding against the wood. He muttered curses under his breath as he paced, hoping it would somehow help clear the silence. Taheyung did not like silence.

His throat was still burning from the words he had shouted at him, even if he didn't believe half of them.

He walked through the halls without any real destination until his steps eventually slowed before a door that had been left half open almost like an invitation. Jungkook's room.

He should not go in there; he knew that. It was a clear invasion of privacy. But his hand was already pushing the door open wider before his brain could catch up with what he was doing.

There was a light smell of cedar and woodsmoke in the house. Jungkook smelled like that.

Taehyung felt something twist in his chest the second he noticed it, and he shoved the feeling away before his mind could wander anywhere near it.

The room was not at all what he had expected it to be. It wasn’t decorated like an alpha's room should be with trophies and weapons displayed.

It looked…just lived-in. Comfortable. There was a messy stack of clothes thrown carelessly over the back of a chair in the corner. A simple desk pushed up against the far wall.

And that is where his eyes finally landed and stuck on a worn, leather-bound file placed there. Its edges looked worn out from what looked like years of constant use.

Curiosity tugged at him harder than guilt could push back. He crossed the room and sat carefully on the edge of Jungkook's bed. He pulled the heavy file into his lap and flipped it open with his trembling fingers.

His breath got caught in his throat.

Drawings. Page after page of detailed sketches. Some were rough and unfinished, just quick gestures capturing movement. Others were incredibly detailed.

Some pages were filled with wolves caught in the middle of a run, their teeth bared and fur drawn in quick, rough strokes. Others were with little pups sleeping in a pile together.

The more he looked, the more familiar the faces became. They were people from the pack, sketched while they were busy with their own lives, completely unaware someone had been drawing them.

Their pack alpha had been drawing them.

Taehyung blinked in genuine surprise. His fingers brushed lightly over the graphite lines as if touching them might help him understand better.

He'd never imagined Jungkook could make something like this. Those same big, rough hands had broken bones in fights, chopped wood without slowing down, and ripped into raw meat like it was nothing.

Seeing them create drawings this careful and gentle just didn't make sense. And yet somehow those same hands could also hold a pencil gently enough to create something this delicate.

He kept flipping through the pages. His heart was racing now. There were more drawings, and they just kept getting softer. Trees full of leaves.

Rivers that looked as if they were actually flowing. The moon was drawn over and over as it changed through the month. Then he turned another page. Everything in him just... stopped. His throat went tight.

His own face stared back at him from the paper. Not his face as it was now, though. This was old, from years ago.

The boy he used to be before everything got so complicated. Bright-eyed and innocent, thinner than he was now.

His hair was a complete mess from running wild through the forest like he always used to do. Jungkook had drawn him like he was something precious, something worth remembering and preserving on paper.

Taehyung slammed the file shut abruptly. His heart stuttered painfully in his chest. "Fuck," he whispered harshly.

He pressed the leather cover against his chest for a moment before shoving it aside and away from him suddenly, too scared of what else he might find hidden in those pages.

He turned his attention instead to a small wooden box sitting beside the desk, desperate for a distraction from the emotions churning in his gut. Inside the box he found brushes, dozens of them in various sizes.

Colorful pigment powders in small containers. Some of the brushes looked uneven, clearly handmade rather than purchased.

He knew these supplies. He actually remembered them from years ago. He remembered those long afternoons when Jungkook would sit outside in the sun, carefully carving brush handles from wood, binding the bristles with thin thread, and testing each one to see how it worked.

He remembered watching Jungkook work, asking endless questions the way he always did, being taught with more patience than he probably deserved.

A small smile tugged unbidden at Taehyung's mouth, aching with old memories he had tried to forget. Well, it looks like he just found something productive to do with his time trapped in this house.

They used to be friends before Jungkook killed his dad. He tries to not get emotional over it.

These might actually keep him sane and distracted. Something to fill the long empty hours ahead of him.

And maybe just maybe, something that might help him remember who Jungkook used to be before everything between them got so twisted and complicated.

Taehyung shifted back to the living room after a while. The leather file and containers of pigments stayed carefully under his arm like stolen treasure he had no right to take.

He dropped himself onto the couch still wearing the same loose clothes Jungkook had given him to sleep in yesterday, which were way too big on his smaller frame. The sleeves kept sliding down past his wrists, and the soft cotton felt warm from being worn.

He should have hated wearing Jungkook's clothes. He should have stripped them off immediately in defiance. But the truth was they were comfortable and he was honestly tired of fighting against every single little thing.

He spread the various pigments across the table in front of him as he grabbed a sheet of rough paper from the stack and settled in cross-legged on the couch, balancing awkwardly as he dipped one of the brushes into water from a cup he had filled.

His tongue slipped out unconsciously to press against his bottom lip as he tried to mimic what he had seen in Jungkook's book.

It looked so easy when Jungkook did it. The colors mixed together, and somehow everything looked right. Taehyung tried the same thing. It looked awful.

"Shit," Taehyung muttered, pulling the brush back too fast. A big drop of water fell right onto the corner of the page and ruined it even more. He groaned and dropped his head against the couch.

"Of course." Still, he did not let himself stop and give up. He tried again with determination. He wasn’t about to stop the only thing he had to do right now.

He mixed the blue and yellow together, trying to make a pretty sunset. All he got was a weird green that made him want to throw the paper away.

He frowned at the page and scrubbed at it harder with the brush. His tongue stuck out without him noticing. It was something it always did when he was trying really hard.

His hands had never been steady. They weren't when he was a kid, and they aren't now.

Still, he kept going. Every time a line came out wonky or the colors mixed into something they weren't supposed to, he'd bite his lip and mumble another curse under his breath.

Time passed. Maybe a few minutes, maybe a couple of hours. He honestly couldn't tell anymore. The house stayed quiet around him, leaving him alone with the mess he'd made.

He sucked at this. Seriously, he was awful at painting. Nothing looked right. Not one thing looked the way he'd pictured it. It wasn't even close to Jungkook's drawings.

His sunsets looked awful, and the rivers looked even worse. He got so frustrated he almost gave up right there.

But he didn't stop. He just kept going, adding more paint here, fixing something there, and hoping the next brush stroke would look better than the last one.

After a while it actually started looking... okay. Not amazing or anything, but okay. It still looked nothing like Jungkook's paintings, not even close, but it looked like something he'd made with his own hands and that felt good enough for now.

By the time he decided to stop, he collapsed on the couch in exhaustion. The brush fell from his tired hand. All he could do is stare at the page right in front of him, not sure whether or not to believe what he was seeing.

A sloppy sunset was smeared on the canvas. Orange colors merged awkwardly into the red ones. A fight for space between the yellow ones. A black line on the bottom where the paint accumulated and created an unwanted shadow.

The picture was not beautiful, definitely not even by a stretch of the imagination. But then, wasn’t it true that the painting was not ugly? At least partially. His lips curled up into a small smile, a sincere one.

“Ha. Not so bad,” he whispered to himself, turning the page in the direction of the sun shining through the window in order to get a better look. For a person with such talent, or lack of it, for someone whose hands were shaky and inexperienced, he managed to create something decent. Something of his own.

He relaxed back into the cushioned sofa. He held the paper on his lap in a manner that allowed him to study it. For the first time in his whole stay in this home, which did not belong to him, Taehyung found himself having a feeling of satisfaction.

Not because he was proud of Jungkook and all that Jungkook could do. Not because of the pack and his position in it. It was just a simple feeling of satisfaction of what he had done by himself.

Taehyung had been working on it for what seemed like several hours, although time had lost all meaning for him. His tongue was as parched as paper. He had stained his right hand with different pigments that would most likely take days to come out.

But yet, he had not realized how the sky outside had already started darkening in the evening. His back throbbed because of sitting bent on the low table, but he was too stubborn to give up.

He went from one page to the next without pause. He got angry at himself for being so terrible at this, then laughed bitterly at his own failures, then tried again anyway because he could not help himself.

Always trying never quite succeeding. But he’ll figure it out on his own. He always did.

He did not want to think about Jungkook right now. He did not want to think about packs or responsibilities or feeling like a bird trapped in a cage. He just wanted the brush to bleed color the way he wanted it to. He wanted to make something that belonged entirely to him and nobody else.

It was only when the sound of the front gate screeched open loudly that he startled badly. His brush jerked hard across the paper and left a long, ugly stroke that ruined what he had been working on.

His head snapped up fast as his heart jumped into his throat. His stomach twisted itself into immediate knots.

And here was Jungkook, finally returning. It was already getting late. The sun was about to set at the same time he did when he had departed earlier that day. His entire upper body remained unclothed and dripping wet.

Those grey sweatpants had still remained low on his slim waist, but now he had pulled back his dark hair to form a shaggy bun. Strands of hair stuck to his sweating neck and temples. His golden skin was drenched in a fresh film of sweat, and Taehyung could see fresh cuts and scars covering his body. Some of them looked red and angry.

For an instant, Taehyung forgot how to breathe. But then his gaze drifted downwards and he stopped dead.

In the background, Jungkook dragged what appeared to be a full goat by one arm as if it were light as a feather. The animal was simply too large. Its head hung at an ugly angle, making Taehyung's stomach churn. Its legs appeared stiff and unnatural.

Taehyung's jaw unhinged as his scream broke from him. "What the hell is this?!"

Jungkook didn't respond. He showed no sign of concern about how Taehyung was handling the situation. Instead, he entered and shut the door behind him.

"What, what did you do?" Taehyung could not get the words out properly. He scrambled to his feet so fast his painting was forgotten and the paper slipped to the floor.

"Are you insane? You just dragged that thing inside like it is a rug or something."

Nonetheless, Jungkook remained silent. He simply tightened his grip, and Taehyung observed the way his muscles moved through all that scarred skin as Jungkook dragged the goat inside the door frame. There was a thin trace of blood left there on the wood.

Taehyung panted as he tried to draw in air. He was shaking, not knowing whether what he was feeling was disgust or fear or maybe something else, which was shamefully hot under his skin, watching Jungkook moving around as if nothing were unusual about today.

"Answer me, damn it!"

But Jungkook continued to be silent. He simply bent down and set the goat right on the mat as if everything were perfectly normal, as if nothing were off here.

The smell hit Taehyung first. It was so sharp and so metallic that it burned his nose.

He choked, putting a hand to cover his nose as Jungkook bent over the goat, firm and calm as though it were just another job of cutting the vegetables for their meals.

The cut came easily, and his hands were completely still and steady. There was no pause. He peeled off the skin easily as though he had done this a thousand times over. The soft, slick tearing of flesh made Taehyung's stomach churn sickeningly.

His eyes were wide, but there was no turning away no matter how hard he wished he could. His heart was racing. His breaths came out rough, and his face twisted in disgust but also something else that he simply couldn’t give a name to. And he just kept staring.

Finally, Jungkook looked up. Blood dripped down from the knife in his hands. He lifted one brow in a questioning manner. "What is it, pup? Never seen a goat before?"

Taehyung's voice cracked when he spoke. "Did you kill it?"

Jungkook blinked at him, and there was confusion written all over his face. He looked down at the hard body on the floor and then back at Taehyung as though he did not really think this could have been the question. "Well, he sure isn’t alive, so I am going to assume, yes, I killed him."

Taehyung's mouth dropped further open. "Why?!"

But this actually caused Jungkook to stop. The blade was paused partway through its slash, and he tilted his head, narrowing his eyes as though he actually wasn't sure whether Taehyung was being serious or not. "What do you mean why?"

"Well, I mean, why would you?"

"Fucking forget it," Jungkook said with a shake of his head as he sliced through the leg. "Do not tell me you are one of those vegetarian omegas."

"What the hell? No!" Taehyung shrieked, clutching the back of the chair like it might be the only thing keeping him steady. "But I do not go around killing animals with my bare hands!"

Jungkook's lips curled into something halfway between disbelief and a smirk that did not quite reach his eyes. He dragged the knife clean through the goat's flank, and red spilled across the wooden board beneath.

"Yeah, because you are a princess," he said flatly, meeting Taehyung's gaze dead on without blinking.

"You do not have to do any of this. You just sit there, wrinkle your nose, and complain about how terrible it all is. Then you eat whatever gets put in front of you. Congratulations, pup. But let me break it to you: meat does not just drop from heaven on a silver platter. Some of us actually have to go out there and kill so princesses like you can sit around and whine about it later."

Taheyung fumed. His cheeks went hot and his fists curled tight at his sides. He wanted to scream something back to spit out something twice as cruel and cutting. But his tongue felt stuck in his mouth.

Taehyung could not deny that something about watching Jungkook in this way was having an effect on him. It was getting harder and harder to maintain the façade that Jungkook was just an overbearing alpha, throwing his weight around. He looked alive. He looked necessary. And Taehyung hated how small this made him feel, how insignificant.

By the time Jungkook was done butchering, the floor was once again immaculate. There wasn't even a drop of blood left on it. The meat was artfully arranged inside of bowls, fat removed delicately, bones to the side.

Jungkook was good at whatever he did,, and it pissed the fuck off of Taehyung. Why did he have to kill Hyungwon Alpha?

The fragrance of herbs began to fill the room as he got hold of glass jars from the shelf while sprinkling salt over them using his nimble fingers and rubbing spices into the cuts.

"Never in a million years," Jungkook replied after a moment, "would I ask my mate to perform traditional roles if they don't wish to do it." He continued working while talking, marinading the meat. "It is quite easy for me to provide for both of us. To cook. And to do everything that my mate requires me to do."

Taehyung raised his eyebrows, taken aback for a brief while, but soon brought back his frown. He raised his legs to his chest, hugging them as he stared at Jungkook from across the room. "Yeah. That's what all alphas say." His lip curled and his voice became bitter and sarcastic.

"That's what all alphas lie about."

The words were so, so gentle, but somehow they cut deeper than any knife could have. "Because I sure as hell did not do anything to you, not for you to hate my existence this much."

Taehyung went completely still. His mouth opened then closed again without sound. He chewed at his bottom lip, eyes darting away to the corner of the room, anywhere but Jungkook's face.

His throat worked for a moment before he finally whispered so quiet it was barely audible. "You are a killer."

The silence that followed was heavy.

Jungkook's jaw ticked. His hands were still slick with spice and oil. He did not look offended by the accusation, though. He did not even look surprised. He just held Taehyung in that dark, unwavering gaze of his. "And you," Jungkook said quietly, sounding almost tired, "are alive because of it."

"Then tell me, is Jungwon even alive?" Taheyung tried to fight.

Jungkook's hand stopped moving mid-stir. He turned towards Taehyung as something cruel happened on Jungkook’s face. "You do not have to know that."

The growl that ripped from Jungkook's chest was not even remotely human. It was a sound that shook the floorboards under their feet, guttural and wild and terrifying.

His eyes bled into darkness, pupils blown wide and dark as his anger took over. "I’m your fucking mate," Jungkook snarled, and his voice vibrated with a fury that made the air feel too thick to breathe.

"That piece of shit is nothing to you."

Taehyung's breath caught hard in his throat, and his knees nearly buckled under the sheer weight of that alpha presence pressing down on him. Fear shot straight through his body, impossible to fight.

The room went dead silent again. Jungkook blinked hard. His chest rose and fell in heavy pulls before he forced the growl back down into his chest. His eyes faded back to that deep brown and his expression softened like the storm had never even been there at all.

“I’m sorry,” he said quietly, turning and placing the pot down on the stove. “But never, ever, mention another man as your mate around me. Not if you want peace in this household.”

Taehyung nodded and swallowed hard through the knot in his throat. His heart was beating against his chest like it was about to burst, but he did not protest this time. No words came out of him.

Instead, he got off the couch and returned to his sketchpad, holding it with shaking hands, seeking solace in empty papers and colors, which made more sense than anything else now.

Jungkook let the silence stretch between them for some time, keeping busy with his cooking. Jungkook casually asked from behind, “What were you drawing, anyway?”

Taehyung snapped his head up immediately, defensive again. "Why should I tell you that?"

"I don’t mind it one bit, pup. But at least you could try to be just a little civil with me, don't you think?" His eyes flicked up, teasing but dangerous in that playful way of his. It did. It hurt him to see Taheyung being so rude with him. But he also understands where Taehyung's coming from.

By the time Jungkook was done cooking, the whole house smelled of spices and smoke. The meat sizzled with a crisp edge that made Taehyung's stomach growl without permission.

Jungkook plated two portions, piling them heavy with rice and slices of fresh tomato and cucumber on the side.

And then, without thinking twice about it, he put all the meat without any bones into one plate, while the bony pieces found their way into his own plate.

Taehyung thought he had been very discreet when he despised bones during pack meals. Never did he indulge in gnawing on bones, and whenever someone offered him a chunk with lots of gristle, he wrinkled his nose in disdain. Evidently he had not been discrete enough.

Jungkook brought both plates and put one down on the dining table. “Eat pup.”

Taehyung walked to the dining table. The sketchbook was clutched to his side as if it were some precious thing he needed to guard. Then he put it down on the table and seated himself opposite to Jungkook.

His gaze drifted for a second towards the plates, and he saw, of course, that he saw the arrangement of foodstuffs, but he did not say anything. He just grabbed his fork and ate right away, cramming his cheeks as if he had never eaten properly in ages and ages.

And it was probably true. He hadn’t had meat in a while.

Jungkook leaned back in his chair, watching him with something warm and odd pooling in his chest.

Taehyung chewed so fast the grain of rice stuck to the corner of his lip, and that little huff he let out when the food turned out hotter than he expected. It was endearing in a way Jungkook had no right to find this charming.

More endearing than he wanted to admit out loud anyway. "Eat well, pup," Jungkook murmured.

Taehyung did not even look up at him, too busy shoveling another spoonful into his mouth. For a while the only sounds in the room were from them chewing on meat.

Jungkook's lips curved into a rueful smile. He tried again. "So what is in the sketchbook?"

"None of your business."

Jungkook loves him so much.

If it keeps them off our backs, if it makes them pity you instead of asking you uncomfortable questions, then it helps us in the long run. That is all that really matters here."

"Did not tear into you like some animal? Did not force you down onto the ground? Did not make you scream the way they all thought I would?"

Taehyung's throat bobbed, and the words caught somewhere between his chest and his mouth. He hated the way his chest clenched up; he hated the way Jungkook's voice seemed to pierce straight through him.

Jungkook finally turned around, wiping his damp hands off on a towel, and his expression was as unreadable as stone. "I do not give a fuck what they think about me, pup. Let them talk. Gashes from scars that had been etched all over his chest. His newly acquired wounds are going all up and down both of his arms. That severe bite mark, red and infected that is embedded in his own wrist.

Sit down," he said curtly.

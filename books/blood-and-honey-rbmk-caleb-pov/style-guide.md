# Style Guide — Blood and Honey - RB&MK Caleb's POV

## Scope

This guide records source-supported literary tendencies for the Russian translation and should be revised only when later chapters provide durable evidence.

## Narration

- Third-person close narration centered primarily on Caleb Whitmore. Preserve his perceptions, bodily reactions, jokes, and shifts between public composure and private uncertainty.
- Contemporary romance/crime prose combining royal spectacle, mafia danger, banter, attraction, and action. Do not inflate either the royal or mafia register into caricature.
- Rhythm alternates between flowing descriptive paragraphs and short emphatic sentences/fragments. Preserve intentional abruptness and repetition.
- The source uses internal separators `+++`; preserve them exactly as separate blocks.
- Author chapter notes are part of the supplied text and must be translated rather than omitted.

## Character voice

### Caleb Whitmore
- Witty, theatrical, flirtatious, impulsive, and observant. His humor often masks uncertainty or danger.
- Publicly polished when needed, but private dialogue is contemporary and quick. Avoid making him stiff or archaic in Russian.

### Arian Conti
- Controlled, economical, physically self-possessed, and difficult to read. Keep his speech concise and avoid adding theatrical menace or sentimentality.

### Paul Walker
- Anxious and deferential around Caleb in the opening. Preserve the contrast with Caleb's ease and showmanship.

## Russian prose and dialogue

- Use natural literary Russian while preserving meaning, emotional force, ambiguity, pacing, and character distinctions.
- Format spoken dialogue with an em dash (`—`) following standard Russian literary dialogue punctuation rather than retaining English quotation marks.
- Preserve italics/bold and written headlines or quoted documents according to Russian conventions.
- Do not sanitize explicit language, sexual content, violence, or profanity; match the source's register and intensity.

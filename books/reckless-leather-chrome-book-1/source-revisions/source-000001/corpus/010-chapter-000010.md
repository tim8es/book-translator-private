*Dear Gavin,*

<!-- bt-block:000003 -->

<!-- bt-block:000004 -->
*Anyway, what I mean is—this whole prison pen pal thing isn’t my idea of a good time or whatever. I don’t want you to think I’m some kind of weirdo or religious freak or anything. My friend Anna—the one who started the program—recruited me, and I said yes to help her.*

<!-- bt-block:000005 -->
*And maybe because she threatened me.*

<!-- bt-block:000006 -->
*Sort of.*

<!-- bt-block:000007 -->
*But don’t worry! I’m totally committed. You can’t see it, but I have my committed face on. Anything you want to talk about or share, I’m here for you. Consider me a vault if you have any secrets you want to share. Well, I’m pretty sure the prison reads these, so don’t share, like, crimes or anything.*

<!-- bt-block:000008 -->
*Um, okay, this has gotten off track.*

<!-- bt-block:000009 -->
*My point is, you tell me what you need and I’ll do my best to do it. You want me to tell you about my boring life? Done. Want to talk about books or TV shows? I’m here for it. Have emotional trauma you want to work through? Well… okay, I’m not really qualified, but I’ll listen and offer what meager advice I can.*

<!-- bt-block:000010 -->
*When I was assigned to you, I got a copy of the little bit of info from your application, so it’s only fair I share some things about me. I’m twenty-four, and I still live in the same town I was born in. I have an older brother, and I’ve never been arrested. Though I did get a parking ticket once when I was in college! I know that’s not the same, sorry. I’m kind of boring.*

<!-- bt-block:000011 -->
*The most interesting thing to ever happen to me was when my parents found out I was gay, kicked me out, and I had to drop out of college.*

<!-- bt-block:000012 -->
*Oh, um, and I’m gay. If that’s a problem, I’m sure Anna could find you someone else.*

<!-- bt-block:000013 -->
*Crap. I should probably just start this over, but really? This messiness is the real me.*

<!-- bt-block:000014 -->
*I guess… Well, I guess that’s it. I hope I haven’t scared you off.*

<!-- bt-block:000015 -->
*Is there anything about me that you want to know?*

*CJ*

Prison is… A lot of times it’s boring, but then in a split second you can be fighting for your life or getting your cell tossed for a random search and you remember that it’s not safe. It’s not anything other than what it is: a steel cage for dangerous people.*

“Chuckie! Get us another round! And we still haven’t gotten our apps!”

Twenty minutes later, he’d brought Deacon’s table their refills and appetizers, put in the order for the new table, settled the bills for two other groups, and was covering the tables of one of the other servers while she was on break.

One was Deacon’s table, and they’d all gotten louder as the night had progressed.

<!-- bt-block:000019 -->
One of the other tables—a group of thirty-something women who’d switched to pop about an hour ago but showed no signs of leaving—was flagging him down. <!-- bt-block:000028 -->
CJ sort of hated how much he liked it.

<!-- bt-block:000029 -->

No one had ever looked at him the way the stranger did. CJ used to imagine that was how Tank would watch him, when he’d let himself think about the possibility of meeting him in person.

<!-- bt-block:000030 -->
Despite the sweet words and promises made in their letters, Tank had always been vague about when he’d be released or if he’d continue writing when it happened. CJ hadn’t worried, assuming they’d have years before that happened, but he’d obviously been wrong. Or at least mistaken that Tank had felt the same way CJ had been feeling.

<!-- bt-block:000031 -->
Maybe it really had all been an elaborate plot to trick him into… what? Sending dirty pictures and his underwear? It wasn’t like Tank or anyone else had ever asked him to send money or anything. So what could the game have been if it had in fact been one?

<!-- bt-block:000034 -->
She said it with a laugh as she easily took the tray of drinks and started away, but he couldn’t help but make a flustered guffawing noise. “He’s not… That isn’t…”

<!-- bt-block:000035 -->

<!-- bt-block:000037 -->
“Hey there,” he said as cheerfully as he could manage, but it still sounded too loud and awkward to him. Unable to stop himself, he ran his eyes over the impossibly wide shoulders encased in black leather, up over the man’s short, well-kept beard, to his expensive haircut. When he finally got the nerve to meet the man’s gaze, his dark brown eyes were practically molten as a small smile formed little wrinkles on either side.

<!-- bt-block:000038 -->
Licking his lips, CJ rasped out, “What can I get you?”

<!-- bt-block:000039 -->

<!-- bt-block:000040 -->

## About The Book

**I obsess over her. Protect her. And when she needs me, I’d kill for her. Soon she’ll see that. So will her enemies…**
My goddaughter is *everything* to me.
I was her dad’s best friend, which means I have *no* business wanting her the way I do. She’s too sweet, too innocent for someone like me. So, I walked away after her parents died.
But I didn’t go far.
I stalked her from the shadows, and it’s a good thing I did. That’s how I found out her so-called friends plan to rob her—of her virginity *and* her inheritance—before killing her.
They won’t get the chance.
Every one of them is about to face this masked serial killer for the first—and last—time. I’ll take them down one by one.
Because *no one* touches what’s mine. And make no mistake…
Darlene Pierce is *mine*.

# Style Guide

## Narration
- First-person present with switches in viewpoint (Vaughn, Darlene). Preserve explicit sexual vocabulary, dark humor and obsession.
- Do not embellish or sanitize. Russian dialogue uses em dashes.

## Prose tendencies
- Direct, colloquial, dark erotic horror; abrupt sentences.

## Review notes
- Maintain chronology (prologue two and a half years ago; chapter one today).
- Keep original paragraph segmentation as far as possible.

End Notes

**What every Alorian child is taught**

- **Aether-crystals** hold true sunlight. Legend says they are the hearts of the old dragons. The best grade is mined only in Vesperia's deep trenches, and that is the whole reason the two realms bother trading.

- **The Twilight Accord** is the peace treaty between Aloria and Vesperia. Article Nine is the one that matters here. A wronged house can claim a hostage of equal rank, and the hostage then belongs to them entirely.

- **The Long Eclipse** happens every twenty years. For one day every crystal goes dark, and the two peoples can meet as equals. The Prologue takes place during the last one.

- **Star-Grace** is the healing gift of the Solar royal family. You'll see it as gold under Caleb's skin and amber in his eyes. Elves kept from the sun too long sicken with the **Solar Blight**.

- **Vampires** drink blood-wine and burn in sunlight, and that includes the light of an Aether-crystal, because it is the real thing. Elven blood carries the same light, which is why it is poison to them.

- **Silver.** Ordinary silver is harmless to vampires. **Sun-forged silver**, worked in the Solar forges under Aloria's noon, burns them like the sun itself.

- **The Leaning.** When a Sovereign dies, the heads of the great houses stand in the great hall and the shadows choose the next one. They have chosen House Conti for a thousand years. House Caruso held the throne before that, and is still bitter about it.

**The houses and their households**

- **Caleb** is the youngest of King George and Queen Philippa's five children. His siblings, from eldest to youngest, are **Andrew** (Crown Prince), **Matilda** (High Paladin of the Radiant Guard), **Eleanor** (Arch-Chancellor, who runs the Treasury) and **Cecily** (Grand Alchemist). **Paul Walker**, a lycan, is his attendant.

- **Arian Conti** is Sovereign of Vesperia. **Enzo** is his uncle and Consigliere. **Vesna** runs his kitchen, and **Mirek** is her son. **Don Salvatore Caruso** heads House Caruso and sits on the Council of Nine.

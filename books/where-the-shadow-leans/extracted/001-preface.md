# Where the Shadow Leans

by [starlite_starbrite](https://archiveofourown.org/users/starlite_starbrite/pseuds/starlite_starbrite)

Summary

*A Vampire King × Elven Prince AU.*

Every elf knows never to touch a vampire. Every vampire knows that elven blood is lethal.

The Sovereign of the Coast takes the sun prince of Aloria as his hostage, and then as his.

Anyone who tries to take him back will learn why the shadows lean away from Arian Conti, and towards nothing else but Caleb.

Notes

My deepest thanks to Emo Universe, who created the most perfect male lead in Caleb Whitmore, matched only by his devoted future husband, Arian Conti.

Caleb, I love you. You deserve nothing but goodness, perfection, and Arian Conti.

A little background, for the curious. I've been building this world for many, many years, inspired by the worlds of Robert Jordan, Brandon Sanderson and J.R.R. Tolkien. The realms, the Accord, the crystals and the Long Eclipse have lived in my notebooks all this while, waiting for the right characters. Every choice I tried felt wrong, and I never managed to create any original characters who satisfied me enough to write a story about. And then along came Caleb and Arian, and FINALLY, at long last, this world had its main characters, and a story.

While I write purely for my own self-indulgence, I am very grateful to everyone reading.

**A short guide to the world** - Each realm draws on the culture and geography of a real-world country.

**The realms**

- **Aloria, the Solar Dominion** *(elves, loosely Britain)* - Caleb's home. Great Aether-crystals on the spires of the Citadel keep it noon around the clock, so an elf who wants a night has to close the shutters. The Radiant Guard, the temple-schools of the Harp Vales and the Highland March are all here.

- **Vesperia, the Umbral Syndicate** *(vampires, loosely Italy)* - Arian's realm, under volcanic ash and a violet twilight that never lifts. He rules from Castel Nerezza on the Obsidian Coast, and the great houses sit on the Council of Nine. Vampires are a quiet, secretive people, even by the standards of the other realms.

- **The Court of Lys** *(fae, loosely France)* - the fae cannot lie, so they make good diplomats and witnesses, and nobody sensible tells them a secret. Their broadsheet, the *Lantern of Lutèce*, turns up everywhere.

- **The Deep Holds** *(dwarves, loosely Austria, Germany and Switzerland)* - engineers and bankers. Their vault-city, Durnhollow, takes no sides.

- **Lowmere** *(gnomes, the Low Countries)* runs the world's trade. **Thalassa** *(merfolk, Greece)* has the seas and the harbour of Nauplion, and the **Jötun North** *(giants, Scandinavia)* mostly stays out of it.

- **Lycans** don't have a realm of their own. Their hill towns are in the **Wildmarch**, high in the Eclipse Marches, but they live all over and serve in both courts. Unlike elves and vampires, who stop ageing once they reach their prime, they age, and they turn grey early (like us humans!!!)

- **Hedge-witches** belong to no realm and no court. They keep to the back streets of other people's cities, and grow and brew the things that great houses would rather not be seen buying.

- **The Eclipse Marches** are the neutral mountains in between, where day and night still behave normally.
